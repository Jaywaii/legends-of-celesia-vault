# 10,105 4A / 0 5A
The 4th Age came to an end with the [[Fiend Soul War]], a war that consumed all of Celesia and the Heavens. [[The Exalted Tyrant|Zhao-Khan, the Exalted Tyant]], devised a means to rapidly create armies of demonically-enhanced mortals. The war came to an end when Zhao-Khan's avatar on Celesia was slain by Kaï-den's champion, an elf woman named Ama'tae. This allowed [[The Lightbringer|Kaï-den, the Lightbringer]], to deliver a fatal blow to Zhao-Khan in the Heavens, slaying the creator deity.

Zhao-Khan, however, had contingencies in place in case of his defeat. One of these involved a minute piece of his essence in the form of an undying black flame. An order of fanatical followers formed around this essence, becoming the [[Order of the Black Flame]]. Throughout the 5th Age, for over five thousand years, the Order worked to remain hidden from the Tyrant's enemies while they worked to fulfill Zhao-Khan's plan. 

The first part was to keep the Black Flame fueled through sacrifices and acts of hate and tyranny. Next was to create a body magnificent enough for their god, made from the flesh of sacrificed powerful tyrants. They must then assemble three of the Tyrant's greatest relics: the Sceptre of Power, the Crown of Tyranny, and the Tome of Hate. Once all of the pieces have come together, the Order would be able to perform a great ritual to resurrect their lord.

However, the Tyrant's foes were not ignorant of the fallen god's machinations. In an attempt to stop the Order, Ama'tae committed a final sacrifice to block their ritual. However, the magic tied this restriction to Ama'tae's bloodline. The Order's ritual now required the sacrifice of one of Ama'tae's descendents, but not just any family member would do. It had to be one whose fate is embroiled with greatness. The Order would have to use powerful divinations to determine which Ama'tae descendent to target, and they would make many attempts to capture them. However, the Ama'tae family was a force to reckon with unto itself, with many allies to call upon. The Argent Rose Society and the Church of the Lightbringer worked together to keep the artifacts out of the Order's hand, moving them from one hidden cache to another, trying to stay one step ahead from the Order.

The war between the Order and their foes would go back and forth for millenia. The Order would gain the relics only to lose them again. The body they crafted would be destroyed. The Ama'tae descendents they captured would be saved. Nonetheless, the Order was able to survive, and most importantly, preserve the Black Flame. Their greatest success, however, was to fool their foes that they had finally extinguished, allowing them to act in secret. The Order passed on in their foes' minds as a vanquished threat that no longer needed to be heeded. Even the Ama'tae family came to barely remember their role in this shadow war.

The secrecy did slow the Order's progress towards their goal, but progress was still made, even if it took several thousand years. Their patience was rewarded when events set in motion long ago finally resulted in all three relics to have found their way in close proximity to one another in the eastern half of Asteropa.
