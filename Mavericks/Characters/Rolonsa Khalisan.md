**Physical description:** Six feet tall. Muscular build with dark brown skin. Almond eyes with brown irises. Long, curly black hair tied back in a ponytail. Trimmed muttonchops sideburns. Thin moustache-goatee.

**Origin:**
- **5117 5A:** Born to Maati and Dalwani of House Khalisan, a border barony of Hynaeria. Rolonsa is the first son and eldest child of the family. He would have a younger sister, Nahiti, born two years after him.
- **5123 5A:** Rolonsa began his spiritual training under the Kaïdenite priest Father Chauncibald as part of a class of 20 students. Among these students were his cousin [[Aransi Daravu]] and Theodric Merwyn with whom he became close friends. Father Chauncibald was particularly impressed by the insight and arguments of the trio, and would personally oversee their teaching, becoming a close mentor to them. Rolonsa was the least argumentative and most willing to go along with the traditional teachings, but his moral compass was unwavering. Whenever the teachings became incompatible with his sense of morals and ethics, Rolonsa could be very defiant, but was also very eloquent and persuasive in his arguments and interpretations of the teachings.
- **5127 5A:** Rolonsa proved to be strong as a boy, displaying great potential in martial skills to his teachers. He was selected to squire under the paladin and knight of the realm Dame Elicia Merwyn, mother of Theodric.
- **5132 5A:** Chauncibald becomes the next bishop of Solaris, and the personal spiritual mentor of [[Melisande Luskarn|Princess Melisande]]. He brings with him his personal students Rolonsa, Aransi, and Theodric. Melisande quickly becomes friends with the three teenagers, ecstatic to spend time with other people of the same age. Rolonsa is the most distant of the group, as his status as a noble heir and knight-in-training urge him to remain respectful of her royal status, despite her attempts to be more familiar.
- **5134 5A:** Rolonsa was anointed as a paladin of Kaï-den, although he remained a squire for a time being. He joined his knight as part of Princess Melisande's escort to the Valkirk Republic during Xaos' western invasion.
