**Description:** Hulking frame over seven feet tall. Completely encased with black, rough plate armour including a closed helm with thin slits for eyes that reveal burning bright red orbs pierce through the darkness. The top of the helm is shaped as an impressive crown. Any visible gaps in the armour reveal wispy clouds of absolute black smoke.

**Origin:**
- **4880 5A:** Eadred was born as the heir of the county of Somerstark in the Aerdwish colonial holding of Caerwedd (modern day Valkirk Republic).
- **4900 5A:** Eadred Somerstark grew up to be a noble paladin lord of Kaï-den.
- **4901 5A:** Eadred was needed to continuously lead expeditions to put down rebellions and defeat monsters. He formed a company of close companions across his adventures. Among them was the adventuring elf cleric of Kaï-den [[Mysellia Ama'tae|Mysellia of House Ama'tae]]. Mysellia schemed at home, both to strengthen Somerstark, but also her own powerbase.
- **4902 5A:** With rebellious elements growing more disruptive to Aerdwish imperial rule, Eadred was forced into political marriage to secure military aid and economic resources for Somerstark. His partner was Maeryn of Duncarl, a beautiful, cunning, and ambitious woman. Neither she and Eadred held any affection for each other. She found him too naive and simple minded. He thought of her as a devious snake. Nonetheless, the two had agreed to work together for their mutual goals. However, Eadred never became intimate with her, and the lack of an heir became a point of concern, and an insult to Maeryn.
- **4903 5A:** Eadred and Mysellia both realised they had fallen in love with each other, however they refused to do anything due to Eadred's marriage.
- **4904 5A:** Political instability and growing monster threats forced Eadred to enact more militant policies to preserve the safety and stability of his domain. He recruited a mercenary company, the Ironfist Company, to bolster his numbers. The Ironfist was one of several companies secretly operated by the [[Order of the Black Flame]] to earn wealth and recruit acolytes.
- **4905 5A:** The leaders of the IFC grew closer to the Somerstarks. Its captain, Danfric, befriended Eadred and Mysellia. The older veteran became a much needed mentor to the younger paladin in the ways of leadership and warfare. On the other hand, the IFC's second-in-command, Karellia, became an ally of Maeryn, advising her in intrigue and plots to increase her power. All the while, the IFC leaders informed the rest of the Order that Somerstark was looking to be an excellent location to build their strength.
- **4906 5A:** Inspired by some of Karellia's advice, Maeryn secretly drugged Eadred one night in order to consummate their marriage. However, Eadred did not react to the drug as expected, so Maeryn slept with Eadred's cousin Royfred, whom she had seduced into her camp. Eadred is confused, but imagery inflicted upon him during his drugged state, and the poisonous words of Maeryn and her allies, made him believe he had indeed slept with his wife.
- **4907 5A:** Maeryn gave birth to her first child, a son named Marthstan. Eadred accepted the boy as his son, but silently kept his doubts about that night he and his wife consummated their marriage.
- **4908 5A:** Maeryn's continuing unscrupulous plotting drove Eadred away from her more and more. Meanwhile, the time he spent with Mysellia adventuring around his realm increased, as did their closeness. Rumours of an illicit affair between them soon spread.
- Danfric's advice to Eadred was to follow his heart, teaching him that if he was powerful enough, he would not need the alliance that Maeryn brought. Meanwhile, Karellia subtly encouraged Maeryn's suspicions of her husband's infidelity, and advised her on taking steps at consolidating her own powerbase.
- **4909 5A:** Eadred and Mysellia finally admitted their love for each other after another harrowing adventure. With his relationship with his wife deteriorating further and further, Eadred resolved to divorce Maeryn.
- **4910 5A:** Eadred reached out to the Kaïdenite church to divorce Maeryn, but the bishop refused. The bishop was a cousin of Maeryn's family, and was on her side.
- **4911 5A:** Eadred and Mysellia met with the Ama'tae family to gain their blessing on their courtship and support in overturning the bishop's decree. However, the Ama'tae matriarch was conservative, and denied the couple. She also intended an arranged political marriage for Mysellia. Mysellia was particularly hurt by her cousin's refusal of support, the heir of the family Azariah.
- **4912 5A:** Eadred and Mysellia continued their courtship with each other in secret, but kept it chaste as both of them did not wish to compromise Eadred's honour and integrity any further.
- **4913 5A:** The threats to Caerwedd grew more numerous and more frequent. Eadred and Mysellia sought out aid from neighbouring Solonaith, but House Ama'tae was instrumental in blocking any aid unless Mysellia ceased her relationship with Eadred. Mysellia refused.
- **4914 5A:** Maeryn made efforts to befriend Mysellia, claiming that they both want what is best for Eadred, and that it behoved them both to work together. Mysellia accepted as she felt more isolated than ever after her family cut her off for choosing to stay with Eadred Over the next several years, the two women formed an alliance with a bit of friendship between them mixed in. Working together, they bested Eadred's political and martial enemies several times. They even negotiated Maeryn's willingness to allow Eadred's dalliance with Mysellia, and promised to divorce him when Marthstan had full claim to Caerwedd's succession. Unbeknown to Mysellia, Maeryn had every intention of betraying the elf and Eadred, her deceptions hidden by magical enchantments provided by Karellia.
- **4921 5A:** With Marthstan's coming of age, Maeryn lured Mysellia to Caerwedd Keep under false pretences of friendship while Eadred was lured away on an emergency mission by Danfric.
- Taking Mysellia to a solitary room, Maeryn poisoned her with a potent magical poison, one designed to take time to painfully, but surely, kill its victim. As Mysellia was dying, Maeryn finally let loose, ranting to her about her ambitions, and her hatred of the elf and Eadred. And after Mysellia died, Eadred would be next.
- However, Eadred arrived home early. Danfric had subtly raised Eadred's suspicions and worries. Eadred rushed home, and barged into the room to see his wife killing his love. He had heard the admission of her plotting as well.
- Overcome with rage, Eadred aggressively confronted Maeryn, who used her own words and powers to defend herself. Finally snapping, Eadred swung his sword at his wife, but Marthstan ran in to defend his mother and took the blow, dying instantly.
- Overwhelmed with grief, Maeryn gained a surge of power, and brought a shocked Eadred to his knees. Maeryn was about to deliver the killing blow to her husband, but Mysellia, with her last ounce of strength, distracted her. The elf called out to Eadred, and the knight killed Maeryn with one stab.
- Eadred lost his paladinhood at that moment, but he did not care. The love of his life was dying in his arms. Danfric and Karellia appeared, offering a means to burn away the poison, a flicker of black flame. However, they told Eadred it would only work if he was the one to use it on Mysellia, and if he gained the flame's approval.
- Eadred took the flame, and both he and Mysellia became engulfed in it. They were drawn into a mental realm formed by the black flame. They were both put through separate trials that broke their spirits, and convinced to abandon their morals and embrace power in order to fulfil their love for each other. When the flame burned out, Mysellia was cured, and both pledged themselves to Zhao-Khan.
- **4922 5A:** Eadred and Mysellia ruled Caerwedd together, and destroyed all of their enemies. However, Maeryn's family declared war upon them. For a time, Eadred and Mysellia held them off, but Maeryn's family held enormous power, and were able to bring greater and greater forces to bear upon them. The Ama'tae had long abandoned Mysellia as well.
- In the final siege, Eadred sacrificed his life to save Mysellia. Mysellia, overwhelmed with grief, begged the Black Flame to save him. Eadred came back, but as a skull knight. With Eadred's newfound power, the pair laid waste to their foes. Danfric and Karellia both willingly submitted the leadership of the Black Flame Order to the powerful pair. After stripping Caerwedd of anything of value, Thanatos embarked the Order onto their true mission, the revival of Zhao-Khan.
- **5121 5A:** First encounter between GC and OBF.
- **5126 5A:** [[Yinhun the Gallant|Yinhun]] was believed to be killed in battle against the skull knight Thanatos. The GC halted a magical ritual, and Yinhun and Thanatos were caught in the ensuing magical explosion, with both of their bodies disappearing. Only Yinhun's dismembered arm remained.
- The battle crippled Thanatos, draining much of his power, but he survived. Mysellia located him despite his shroud to divination thanks to their spiritual bond. While his physical form would need time to recover, his mind remained sharp as ever, and he continued leading the order while he recovered in hiding.
- **5134 5A:** Thanatos recovered enough to lead in person the ambush on Eldebran's Outriders. His goal was to capture him, and he found himself blessed by the presence of [[Sylvanna Ama'tae|Sylvanna]], the other candidate for sacrifice. However, Eldebran sacrificed his life to stop Thanatos by using a sun bomb (last artifact from the 4th age found during ruin delving with [[Mira Greenleaf|Mira]]). The sun bomb once again crippled Thanatos, although not as badly as last time.
	- Thanatos had to lead the ambush himself as Mysellia and [[Zeia kar Dasturm|Zeia]] were assaulting Wyrstadt, and nor did he trust [[Valestria Meciana|Valestria]] on her own.
