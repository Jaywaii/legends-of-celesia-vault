**Description:** Six feet tall with pale white skin. Long, dark red flowing hair.

**Origin:**
- **4802 5A:** Valestria was born into a noble family in the Venicar Empire.
- **4834 5A:** On the run for her crimes, Valestria accepted being turned into a vampire.
- **4867 5A:** Valestria's plot succeeded in the destruction of her sire, freeing her to have complete control over herself.
- **4993 5A:** [[Thanatos]] sought out Valestria upon a vision from the Black Flame as the Order needed her expertise to finalise the ritual.
