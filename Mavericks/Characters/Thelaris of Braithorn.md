**Description:** Five and a half feet tall. Petite, slender build with light tan skin. Short, pixie cut auburn hair. Almond eyes with hazel irises. Soft, boyish features.

**Origin:**
- **5118 5A:** Thelaris is born to a family of caravan traders.
- **5119 5A:** Thelaris' family was travelling through the Forest of Braithorn when they were ambushed and killed by monsters. The Circle of Braithorn intervened too late, only in time to rescue Thelaris as a baby. With no other family to turn to, the Circle raised her as one of their own.
- Thelaris considered the Circle as her family. They were closest to Elder Almera. They had a happy childhood learning the druidic ways, surrounded by friends they considered family, being the youngest of the Circle.
- **5134 5A:** The Circle discovered the presence of the Lysenthiir dark elves underneath the forest. The Lysenthiir are setting up an outpost both for themselves and for their Black Flame allies as part of their plans to invade Valkirk and Hynaeria. The dark elves killed the Circle for their discovery to keep their presence secret. With her last actions, Almera succeeded in get Thelaris to escape.

**Arc:** Thelaris had the most happy childhood despite the loss of their two families. They will struggle to learn to love a new family, this time with the Mavericks, but by facing the horrors of the world alongside them, they learn that they can still live happily in face of those dangers.
