**Physical description:** Slender, athletic build with snow white skin. Long silver hair; splits into two long bangs on the side in the front, tied in a top ponytail in the back. Long, sharp eyebrows. Upturned, slender eyes with sky blue irises. Long, pointed ears. Slender, angular facial features.

**Qualities:** careful thinker, protective, responsible.

**Flaws:** emotionally distant, low confidence.

**Arc:** you must find a way to deal with your nightmares in order to grow.

**Origin:**
- **5057 5A:** Cyrus was born to Andellar and Eversil Ari'luth as their only child.
- **5109 5A:** Cyrus was thought to be killed by House Lysenthiir in a surprise attack, but was actually kidnapped by House Lysenthiir. The matron mother Ghanathis subjected Cyrus to horrific magical experimentation to turn him into a drow. She succeeded, but Noué's Madness took hold of him, and Cyrus became a spear-wielding berserker. Over the next 5 years, the Lysenthiir took delight in turning the boy into a crazed killer, letting him loose on slaves and captives.
- **5111 5A:** Andellar and Eversil discovered that the body left behind by the Lysenthiir's attack was not Cyrus, and that their son was actually alive. The Greenleaf Company devote all their time searching for him, but the Lysenthiir continuously move their location and erect obstacles in their way.
- **5114 5A:** After several  years of searching, the Greenleaf Company finally discovered Cyrus' location, and assaulted the Lysenthiir. Under Noué's Madness, Cyrus attacked his parents. His mother sacrificed her life to free him from the curse and purge him from the Lysenthiir's control by letting him kill her. With his mother's dying body in his hands, Cyrus was overcome with immense rage. With the power imbued into him by the Lysenthiir's experimentation, he killed the matron mother in a surprise attack, though he nearly died in the process. When he woke later, he became overwhelmed with grief, and became emotionally closed off.
- **5116 5A:** Cyrus' showed the first signs of emerging from his vegetative melancholy when a baby [[Kain Gallant|Kain]] crawled up to him with joy and innocence in his eyes. Over time, the half-elf's exuberance and drive infected Cyrus that he gradually opened up, although he retained a very cool demeanour and control on his emotions.
- Cyrus would become Kain's, and later [[Kyle Windswood|Kyle]]'s, effective older brother. Kain admired Cyrus' martial skills. He also loved to try and make Cyrus break out a smile or a laugh. Cyrus would try to act responsible, and keep Kain out of trouble, but Kain somehow always managed to get him to go along with his adventures and antics.
- Ghanathis' experimentation on Cyrus never went away. Cursed with a mutated form of Noué's Madness, Cyrus could tap into its power to give himself incredible speed and strength. However, in order to do so, he must embrace hatred and despair, and each time he does so, it becomes harder and harder to regain his sanity. Cyrus would also involuntarily succumb to the curse when he experiences great emotional distress. This happened only once after experiencing a nightmare of his ordeal soon after his rescue. Any mention of dark elf also risked triggering a relapse.

**Skills:**
- Trained in the Ari'luth Art of the Spear.
- *Quarter Moon Crescent:* The practitioner swings the spear horizontally to hit two adjacent opponents in one slash with such speed that few can react before the swing completes.
	- Improves to *Half Moon Crescent* where the swing can slash against all opponents within immediate sight in front of the wielder.
	- Improves to *Full Moon Crescent* where the swing can slash against all opponents around the wielder.
- *Moonlight Strike:* The practitioner charges and delivers a thrust with their spear in a single moment. The practitioner's reach is extended thanks to the spear's length, and the attack can pierce through armour.
