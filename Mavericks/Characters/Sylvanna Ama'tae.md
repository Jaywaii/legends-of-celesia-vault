**Description:** Slender, dancer build with light skin tone. Long, flowing platinum blonde hair. Wide, upturned eyes with bright green irises. Thin, long, curved eyebrows. Soft, gentle lips. Long pointed ears.

**Qualities:** learned, agile, politically astute, diplomatic, charismatic, artistic.

**Flaws:** naive, haughty, vain, spoiled.

**Origin:**
- **5060 5A:** Sylvanna was born to Azariah and Lorendal Ama'tae in Solonaith. She was their sixth child. It was obvious early on that she was born with physical charm and beauty exceptional even among elves.
- Azariah was over 250 years old (born 4805 5A), and was the matriarch of the family for the past 100 years (starting 4958 5A). The Ama'tae clan had a long history, experiencing many ups and downs; Azariah single handedly brought the family back to peak prominence through sheer stubborn hard work. She now sits atop a powerful merchant empire and on the ruling council of Solonaith. The hardships she went through forged her into a strong, stubborn, but extremely intelligent and politically adept woman who knows how to get what she wants, and won't take no for an answer.
- Sylvanna grew up becoming enamoured with stories of exploration and adventure, spending time in Solonaith's many libraries.
- Sylvanna demonstrated at an early age to be adept at many artistic talents: singing, dancing, playing the harp. She also had a silver tongue, able to accurately gauge the mood of others and pick the right words to make them charmed by her.
- Because of her beauty and charming personality, many flocked around her and spoiled her. Sylvanna grew up used to getting what she wanted with the right words and subtle gestures. The exception was her mother, who had high expectations from her. The Ama'tae matriarch was strict with Sylvanna, expecting her to excel in all fields needed to become a powerfully influential figure in Solonaith's political and aristocratic landscape.
- Azariah hoped Sylvanna would become a powerful wizard like herself, but Sylvanna gravitated towards the bardic arts, much to her mother's disapproval. While the cultural arts were important to elven life and a noble pursuit, Azariah held it in lower regard than mastering the magical and political arts.
- Sylvanna grew up admiring her adventurous eldest brother Eldebran. Eldebran, in turn, loved his sister the most of their siblings, and spoiled her on tales of his adventures. 
- Her eldest sister Illendria shared their mother's disapproval, stemming from a tinge of jealousy that their mother held the family's youngest with such high regard despite her flighty attitude, and their father spoiling her more.
- **5110 5A:** Sylvanna began studying at the St. Ilythrasil Academy for the scions of Solonaith's aristocracy. She naturally excelled at all of her artistic classes, but also of her intellectual courses. The only area where she struggled was in her physical classes. While she was a talented fencer, she simply did not have the endurance to do well.
- Sylvanna easily became the most popular student at the school. She gained a large following, many admirers, and a close circle of true friends (the bookish Nuali Miralei and the boisterous Prisspira Kellenis). She also gained a rivalry with the second more influential student at the Academy, Anauria Fillien (and her own circle).
	- [[Nuali Miralei]]: House Miralei is a branch of House Ama'tae, and had always held a close relationship with them. They have a tradition of their members serving as close aides to the members of their senior house. Nuali was a few years younger than Sylvanna, and grew up at her side. Her parents groomed her to be Sylvanna's primary aide, but Sylvanna always treated her more as her best friend than assistant. Nuali was forever grateful for Sylvanna's friendship, but that only fueled her dedication to perfecting her expected duties.
	- [[Prisspira Kellenis]]: Prisspira is the first heir of the newly elevated House Kellenis, thanks to House Ama'tae's sponsorship. A few years older than Sylvanna, she entered the Academy late due to her status and lack of experience in the ways of the nobility. She encountered many conflicts with the more traditional nobility, and Prisspira is quick to get physical. Sylvanna, however, admired her rough and unrestrained attitude, being so different from what she was used to. She quickly roped her former commoner girl into her circle, and appreciated how independent and un-sycophantic she was in contrast to everyone else trying to curry her favour.
	- [[Anauria Fillien]]: Anauria was the heir to her house which was already a rival clan to House Ama'tae (on the council of Solonaith as well). She was beautiful and very talented, well-rounded in nearly every field. She easily became the second most popular student at the Academy. Her jealousy of Sylvanna stems from the fact that Sylvanna proved superior to her in certain fields she considered her domain, and her greater popularity despite the fact she was a sixth child and was inferior to her in other fields (mainly the physical).
	- [[Kethrendil Symbaeryn]]: Kethrendil was the most popular male bachelor of Sylvanna's generation. While not the heir of his family's fortunes, Kethrendil was a rising star, excelling in arms and studies. He was extremely charismatic, and a natural leader. Both Sylvanna and Anauria had an interest in him which fueled their rivalry with each other. Kethrendil, for his part, was gentlemanly with each, and officially remained non committed to either, but he was more intrigued with Sylvanna.
- **5134 5A:** Eldebran led his free company, the Solonaith Outriders, to join the combined armies of Valkirk's states. Sylvanna was planning on joining him, as Eldebran had previously promised her that he would let her accompany him on his next expedition. However, Eldebran had intended to take her just on an exercise trip, not to a warfront. He regrettably denied her from joining, so she snuck into the cargo train after asking her friends to fake her presence elsewhere. She was eventually discovered, and Eldebran was planning on turning back or having her get teleported, but the Outriders were ambushed by Thanatos and the Black Flame. Eldebran and the Outriders ferociously fought back, and Sylvanna was traumatised by her first experience of fighting to the death, killing a Black Flame man. Eldebran was horrified when Thanatos revealed himself. After some back-and-forth taunting, Thanatos let slip that not only was his intention to capture Eldebran alive, but that it was fortuitous that Sylvanna was unexpectedly also here so he did not have to chase her in Solonaith. Seeing his Outriders and himself not winning against Thanatos' more powerful forces, Eldebran ordered his chief wizard Galathea to teleport Sylvanna back to Solonaith. He then purposefully impaled himself on Thanatos' blade so he could catch the undead knight in the explosion of a sun bomb. As Eldebran clung to Thanatos, he told Sylvanna to find Mira, and that he loved her. The last thing Sylvanna saw of her brother was him and Thanatos being engulfed in the light of the sun.
- Sylvanna was in shock upon her return to Solonaith. Grief overwhelmed her as the shock passed and the reality that her beloved brother was dead. Her family had also immediately learned of the fate of Eldebran and the Outriders. Sylvanna's mother let down her strict demeanour, and gently shared their grief over Eldebran together, an act which comforted her. It helped her remember Eldebran's last request for her to go find Mira Greenleaf, but Azariah forbade it; Sylvanna was safest here in Solonaith, and that it was Mira's fault for leading Eldebran into that dangerous life. Sylvanna tried to protect, but Azariah remained firm. In their arguing, Azariah let slip of her plan to go ahead with Sylvanna's engagement to Kethrendil Symbaeryn, much to Sylvanna's shock. Azariah explained that she and Kethrendil's mother had been negotiating the pairing for over a year now, and while any marriage would be years off, now would be a good time to make the engagement official. Azariah knew of Sylvanna's interest in the boy, and expected that she would be happy for this news. But Sylvanna was more concerned about this Thanatos and his intended plans for Eldebran and herself, and that she absolutely needed to go find her brother's teacher. Azariah absolutely refused, wanting to protect her daughter, but Sylvanna believed it to be just her mother trying to control her life.
- Sylvanna schemed to escape and fulfil her brother's last request. However, all she knew was that Mira Greenleaf, Eldebran's mentor, lived in the great forest of Navaraith, a region so vast it was going to be searching for a needle in a haystack. Galathea was only able to help a little. While the wizard wanted to help Eldebran's little sister, she was bound by oaths to obey Azariah's wishes. She did, however, share Eldebran's journals in which he talked about Mira's last known location, a forest village named Feywood in the southeastern part of Navaraith.
- Sylvanna convinced her childhood friends to help fake her presence elsewhere in Solonaith. Meanwhile, she would actually sneak out of the city. Having sold off many of her possessions, Sylvanna was able to bribe her way onto a series of transports and caravans, sneaking onboard when necessary. She made her way to the nearest town outside of the southeastern border of Navaraith. She ventured into the forest on her own with only vague information on how to find Feywood from travelling merchants who sometimes made stops there.
- Both Azariah and Thanatos tried to search for Sylvanna. However, little did they know that Eldebran's Final Sacrifice blanketed his little sister with an enchantment that cloaked her from magical divinations until she was safe from Thanatos. This forced her mother and her pursuer to track her down through mundane means.
- Azariah recruited a posse to trackers to find her daughter. Galathea was included.
- Thanatos alerted all of his followers in the region to find and capture the girl unharmed and unspoiled.  

**Skills:**
* Knowledge of history, songs, legends and stories.
* Combat: Fencing, archery, acrobatics.
* Social: Dancing, negotiating, singing.
