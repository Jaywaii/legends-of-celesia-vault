**Physical description:** Six feet tall. Athletic build with dark brown skin. Almond eyes with brown irises. Shoulder-length, curly loose black hair growing upward before flowing down like a fountain.

**Origin:**
- **5117 5A:** Born to Faarsa and Timara Daravee. The Daravee family was a minor branch of the Khalisan family with no status of nobility, but have traditionally served as priests of Kaï-den for many generations.
- **5123 5A:** Aransi began her spiritual training under the Kaï-denite priest Father Chauncibald as part of a class of 20 students. Among these students were her cousin [[Rolonsa Khalisan]] and Theodric Merwyn with whom she became close friends. Father Chauncibald was particularly impressed by the insight and arguments of the trio, and would personally oversee their teaching, becoming a close mentor to them.
- Although the Daravee could have taught Aransi themselves, her parents thought it might be good for her to learn the Hynaerian ways of Kaï-den in addition to their own traditions. It was also a way to form connections with other families. Aransi's parents also hoped that more exposure to the world outside would kick Aransi out of her laid-back attitude towards life.
- Aransi always displayed interest in simply taking over her parents' little congregation outside of the politics of the settlement and wider region. She seemed to be the lazy student of the class, so Father Chauncibald called on her to answer many questions. At first, it was to jolt her into paying attention to the class, but upon hearing her answers, the priest became intrigued, and kept regularly asking her questions more to hear her answers. Contrary to her attitude, Aransi's responses were deeply insightful and revealing, even if they go against orthodox doctrine, nor did they adhere to Obori traditions. Moreover, these insights came naturally to her, as if it was completely obvious. She and Chauncibald would verbally spar many times, and while there was never a clear winner, Chauncibald came away from the debates with new ideas. More impressively, Aransi was still just a child.
- **5132 5A:** Chauncibald became the next bishop of Solaris, and the personal spiritual mentor of [[Melisande Luskarn|Princess Melisande]]. He brought with him his personal students Rolonsa, Aransi, and Theodric. Melisande quickly became friends with the three teenagers, ecstatic to spend time with other people of the same age. Melisande loved to hang out with Aransi because the other girl did not care for formalities, treated Melisande as an equal, and had very insightful conversations that went beyond what the princess was used to.
- **5134 5A:** Much to her reluctance, Aransi and Theodric joined Melisande on her diplomatic mission to the Valkirk Republic as one of close advisors.
