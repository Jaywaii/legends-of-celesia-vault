**Description:** Muscular, athletic woman standing six feet tall with pearly skin. Long, swept back straight black hair with a slight widow's peak in front. Angular eyes with amber irises. Purple tinted lips.

**Origin:**
- **5116 5A:** Zeia was born to [[Karse kar Dasturm|Karse]] and Hildwind, ducal rulers of Dasturm.
- **5115 5A:** Karse handed over Zeia over to his teacher [[Thanatos]] and [[Mysellia Ama'tae|Mysellia]] to be their student in the ways of war, tyranny, and hatred.
- **5131 5A:** Thanatos sent Zeia to Wyrstadt to establish a Black Flame cell. Zeia would have to learn how to wield political power and cunning. Part of her mission was to weaken Wyrstadt defences and provide the groundwork for the invasion. However, her main mission was to follow up on clues that indicated that the Tome of Hatred was last recorded in this city. The dark elf assassin [[Feydarrin Lysenthiir]] was assigned as her shadow. Feydarrin would teach her intrigue, watch her back, and act as her blade in the shadows.
