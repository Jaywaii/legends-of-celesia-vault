**Alias:** "Swiftblade"

**Physical description:** Six feet tall. Muscular, athletic build with light skin. Short, swept-back blonde hair. Almond eyes with blue irises.

**Flaw:** thrillseeker, quick to violence.

**Arc:** violence is not always the answer, thrills are not the only things that can make life worth living.

**Origin:**
- **5117 5A:** Kyle was born to Marek and Terra Windswood as their eldest child in the Valkirk republic. Both parents were retired soldiers, and Marek had developed his own sword style.
- **5122 5A:** Terra died giving birth to Kyle's younger sister Sera due to an old wound.
- Marek fell into depression and became an alcoholic. While he was never abusive, he neglected his children, and became unable to hold a job to provide for his family. At just five years old, Kyle became responsible for raising his sister and himself. Fortunately, Terra's old loyal attendant from her army days and now her midwife, Fiona, was able to use her meagre resources to help a bit.
- Kyle grew up scrounging and fighting for every bit of scrap he could so his family didn't starve. He became ashamed of his father's behaviour, but also fought anyone who spoke ill of him or his family.
- **5126 5A:** Kyle learned of a big score through rumours. A large amount of gold was going to pass through his hometown via a Crown Consortium merchant group. If he could just grab a handful of that loot, it would provide for his family for years. However, the loot was part of a greater plot, and security was extraordinarily high. Kyle got captured and tortured, with the Crown thugs thinking he was working for their enemies. Kyle kept his mouth shut so that his family would not be targeted, but the Consortium was going to use magic to extract his mind. However, before he was going to be killed, the Greenleaf Company attacked the Consortium base, having followed the trail of their atrocities.
- [[Yinhun the Gallant|Yinhun]] saved Kyle's life. Kyle saved Yinhun's life in return by picking up a sword and rushing into the middle of the fight to kill the main Consortium agent who was about to stab Yinhun from behind.
- However, at least one Consortium agent who tortured Kyle escaped. The GC knew that Kyle was now in danger. Kyle begged them to take him with them, for he came to admire Yinhun and wanted to learn from him. The GC decided this was the best thing to do for Kyle and his family's safety.
- Kyle was unable to say goodbye to his family to avoid risking anyone making a connection between him and them. However, the GC was able to funnel gold to Fiona from their own funds (replaced with the loot they took from the Consortium) so that she could support the Windswoods and herself for many years, pretending to be from extended, long-distanced family. Fiona was suspicious, but she was experienced enough to get the hint. The family would never know what happened to Kyle for their own safety.
- The GC brought Kyle to Feywood. [[Kain Gallant|Kain]] was unhappy at first that his other boy was trying to learn from his father, and they got into some fights. However, both boys came to respect each other after exchanging blows and surviving a monster encounter together. They quickly became best friends thanks to their similar attitudes and love for adventure and action. Both of them were also finally happy to have a friend of the same age and same interests.
- Kyle would only study under Yinhun for a few months. Yinhun would die in an adventure later that year. Kyle was devastated. The man had become a second father to him in that short time. He and Kain would continue their training, and he would eagerly embark in getting into trouble with him.
- **5132 5A:** Although Kyle was an expert in getting into trouble, unlike Kain, he lacked the judgement of when to pull back. In the last 2 years before the start, Kyle's libido became very energetic, and he flirted heavily with the girls of Feywood, no matter how much trouble it got him into. He would be the first to lose his virginity in a one-night encounter with one of the more daring girls (Daria Fairtree) after the midsummer celebration. This got him into a fight with Esrick Welbrook and his cronies, as Daria was considered to be attached to the other boy at the time. Even after, Kyle would have the occasional intimate encounter with Daria, usually after she got into a fight with Esrick.
- **5133 5A:** Kain and Kyle had their first sexual encounter with each other after Kyle stole a large amount of ale from Feywood's midsummer celebration. Kyle's advances were rejected by Daria Fairtree (unlike last year), and Kain kept him company to cheer him up. They both got drunk off the ale, and Kain suggested they spar as usual to blow off steam. With adrenaline pumping through their veins after an intense sparring session and still under the influence of alcohol, the two boys became physically intimate with each other that night. The morning after, both boys agreed that they enjoyed it a lot, but also agreed that there was no way they were going to get into a romantic relationship with each, knowing full well each other's personality. That being said, both were happy for the occasional tryst and experimentation any time they needed to work their aggression or frustrations, something that would occur every month or so.
