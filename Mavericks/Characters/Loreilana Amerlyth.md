**Physical description:** Five and a half feet tall. Slender, muscular build with light skin. Big, upturned eyes with blue irises. Long, straight red hair tied back into a braided ponytail except for a long lock hanging forward from the centre brow, and two smaller locks in front of the ears.

**Flaw:** aimless, torn between familial duty and her own dreams.

**Arc:** getting everything you want requires patience and hard work, may come with unexpected sacrifices, and may end up not being what you wanted in the first place.

**Origin:**
- **5059 5A:** Loreilana was born to Fanalorn and Tomilyth as their only child, on the continent of Zhoulong. Fanalorn was the patriarch of the Amerlyth martial clan, and Loreilana was his heir. Although all members of the clan learned the Amerlyth school of martial arts, only Loreilana would learn all of its secrets. One such secret was how the Amerlyth passed down the knowledge to their heir. Through secret rituals, the Amerlyth secret arts were planted and locked away into Loreilana's mind. She would be unable to access any of that knowledge until she attained certain stages of physical and mental proficiencies that would require decades, if not centuries, of training.
- Fanalorn and Tomilyth traveled widely across the world before settling and assuming the leadership of the clan. Fanalorn in particular brought new ideas that stirred up controversy among the generally conservative elf martial world. However, Fanalorn and Tomilyth were very persuasive, and respected masters, so many listened to them. It was only when Fanalorn suggested they exchange techniques with non-elf martial sects that the Amerlyth clan was targeted for destruction. Clan Kirthendil led the coalition against Amerlyth, joining the Twilight Houses as a result.
- **5070 5A:** Clan Amerlyth is betrayed by Kirthendil's alliance, framed for crimes against the elven martial world, and the entire sect is massacred. Only Loreilana and her guardian Minsaiel escaped. Clan Kirthendil chased after them. They needed Loreilana alive to marry her to their heir, thus gaining the Amerlyth secret arts, and a legitimate claim over the clan's assets. The surviving pair flee all the way west into Asteropa.
- **5071 5A:** Loreilana and Minsaiel come across the elf village of Lerucian in Suldeinor. Slipping away from her guardian while he acquired more traveling supplies, Loreilana witnessed a weak elf boy being beaten by other children. Loreilana intervened, defending the boy with her skills and the ideals of her clan. She chased off the bullies, and helped the purple-haired boy to his feet. Before she could learn his name or give her own, her guardian appeared and dragged her away, chastising her for bringing attention to themselves.
- **5115 5A:** Loreilana and Minsaiel continued wandering Asteropa for the next several decades, trying to stay one step ahead of Kirthendil's hunters. In that time, Minsaiel taught Loreilana the basics of their martial arts. However, Kirthendil had used contacts among the Twilight Houses to recruit the New Dawn Knights to hunt the fugitives. The NDK were successful.
- Minsaiel attempted to hold the NDK back to allow Loreilana to flee. As she fled, a purple-haired boy of the NDK found her, but instead of capturing her, tried to help her escape. There was hesitation in trusting what seemed to be an enemy, and that delay allowed the rest of the NDK to surround her. Minsaiel, not being the true target, was killed by the NDK, his body reduced to ash.
- While in the NDK's custody, the boy that tried to help her introduced himself as [[Jannilus]]. He was the boy she defended that one time many years ago. He tried to get to know Loreilana, but she rebuffed his attempts, still grieving over the death of her guardian and fearful of her custody.
- The NDK were tasked not to harm Loreilana, but also to use their skills to brainwash her into being compliant with the Kirthendil's plans. When Jannilus learned of this, he went into action and helped Loreilana escape. Loreilana, seeing no other choice, went with him.
- The pair of young elves helped each other overcome the obstacles to escaping the NDK's lair. However, once they were free, Loreilana tried to flee from Jannilus. Unfortunately, the NDK soon found her. Jannilus jumped into the fray to shield Loreilana, and give her time to escape. The NDK blasted Jannilus for his defiance. Loreilana quickly decided to grab the dying Jannilus and flee with him, but that cost her the chance to escape.
- The Greenleaf Company suddenly arrived and battled the NDK. The GC were following the trail of the NDK's crimes, found their lair, and chased after them. After defeating the NDK, they healed Jannilus, and offered to bring them back to safety in Feywood.
- In Feywood, Loreilana first got to know [[Cyrussilar Ari'luth|Cyrus]], then helped raise [[Kain Gallant|Kain]], followed by [[Kyle Windswood|Kyle]] years later. Because of her natural compassion and the sense of responsibility taught to her, Loreilana became the mother of the group of friends. However, she was not above having getting into fun trouble with them. She was still young and full of energy and curiosity. She justified her participation in their antics by being the one to pull them back when they went too far, and for the most part, this was true.
- Loreilana taught Kain and Kyle how to fight unarmed. She and Cyrus became the older siblings of the group. She and Jannilus also grew closer to each other. Loreilana was the only reason that Jannilus tolerated being with the group.
