**Physical description:** Slender, athletic build with lightly tan skin. Black hair cut short and kept messy. Slender, almond eyes with brown irises. Sharp black eyebrows. Short, pointed ears.

**Qualities:** brave, loyal, adventurous, quick-witted, sharp instincts.

**Flaws:** reckless, cocky.

**Origin:**
- 5116 5A: Kain was born to the elf Mira Greenleaf and the human Yin-hun the Gallant in the village of Feywood.
- Growing up, Kain relished listening to his parents' tales of adventure, and it was obvious at a young age that he had an adventurous spirit and drive to explore and overcome incredible challenges. He loved to learn both of his parents' skills.
- Because of his father's reputation, Kain did not get along with the other children in the village. The other children, led by Esrick Welbrook, inherited their parents' dislike of Yulong for being a disgraced and exiled knight of Hynaeria, and they made fun of Kain for that. Kain's recklessness only added fuel to the fire.
- 5126 5A: Yinhun was believed to be killed in battle against the skull knight Thanatos. The GC halted a magical ritual, and Yinhun and Thanatos were caught in the ensuing magical explosion, with both of their bodies disappearing. Only Yinhun's dismembered arm remained. Unable to resurrect Yulong, and all investigation pointing towards his death, Kain accepted his father's death.
- Kain expressed his anger and grief at his father's death by doubling his training and efforts into becoming an adventurer like his parents. He also became more aggressive in general, but was especially quick to get into fights with anybody who spoke ill of his father.
- Kain would often explore the surrounding forest as his "adventures". Eager to push his skills, he was quick to get into fights with Blacktusk orcs and Bloodfang goblins that strayed into the agreed Feywood lands (instead of peacefully warning them off). He never fought to kill, however; Kain only sought a challenge, and had no stomach for useless killing. These unnecessary fights, even though no one would die, still got him into trouble with the village, as they had no desire to provoke their neighbours.
- Kain had the uncanny knack of roping his more responsible companions into his adventures, except for Kyle who was always willing to get into trouble. It was Kain's complete willingness to share fun and his love for adventure with others without prejudice that inevitably broke their resolve.
- 5133 5A: Kain and Kyle had their first sexual encounter with each other after Kyle stole a large amount of ale from Feywood's midsummer celebration. Kyle's advances were rejected by Daria Fairtree (unlike last year), and Kain kept him company to cheer him up. They both got drunk off the ale, and Kain suggested they spar as usual to blow off steam. With adrenaline pumping through their veins after an intense sparring session and still under the influence of alcohol, the two boys became physically intimate with each other that night. The morning after, both boys agreed that they enjoyed it a lot, but also agreed that there was no way they were going to get into a romantic relationship with each, knowing full well each other's personality. That being said, both were happy for the occasional tryst and experimentation any time they needed to work their aggression or frustrations, something that would occur every month or so.
