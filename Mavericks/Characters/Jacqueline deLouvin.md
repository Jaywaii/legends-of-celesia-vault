**Physical description:** Three and a half feet tall. Slender, athletic build with light brown skin. Shoulder-length wavy dark brown hair. Big, round eyes with brown irises. Button nose. Freckles horizontally across the nose and under the eyes.

**Origin:**
- **5117 5A:** Jacqueline was found as a newborn on the doorstep of the poor Coeur-d'Espoir orphanage in the city of Louvin. Jacqueline would grow up mostly on the streets, stealing whatever she could to survive and support her fellow orphans.
- In her middle teens, Jacqueline fell in love with fellow orphan Katrine, a human girl. Together, they would form and lead the Velvet Hands, a gang of thieves composed of their fellow orphans, targeting the wealthy of Louvin for the next couple of years.
- **5132 5A:** the Velvet Hands striked a big score that turned out to belong to the Crown Consortium. The CC killed many of the VH, including Katrine who gave up her life to give Jacqueline a chance to escape capture. Jacqueline was wounded, but managed to hide herself aboard a departing airship. While hiding, she fell unconscious, and only awoke when the vessel reached its destination: Thunderstrike Hall. She had to sneak off the ship before being caught by disguising herself as one of the prisoner-slaves.
- Jacqueline came to the attention of the duerghendar guards of Clan Ulgendarth, and they were going to have some vile sport with her. Their attempt was interrupted by a young dwarf prisoner by the name of [[Baroth Strikinghammer|Baroth]]. The dwarf was beaten by the guards, allowing Jacqueline to sneak away from their attention. When the guards were satisfied and left, Jacqueline quickly returned and nursed Baroth back to health. The two quickly became friends.
- With Jacqueline's skills and Baroth's strength and knowledge of the Hall's layout, escape became possible. After coming up with a plan, they made their attempt. However, the CU proved to be more capable. Although the pair tried to bring as many of the prisoners with them, the prisoners opted to stay behind and prevent the guards from capturing Jacqueline and Baroth, allowing them to escape the Hall.
- Jacqueline and Baroth were swept away along a river, but managed to stay together by holding onto each other's hand, even after passing out from the current and their wounds. When they recovered, the pair found themselves lost in the wilderness with no way of tracing their way back to the Hall.
- **5133 5A:** For the next several months, Jacqueline and Baroth eventually succeeded in stealing what they needed to survive. They gained weapons, and began attacking CC caravans and assets. For Baroth, it was in hope of finding clues of where TH was located. For Jacqueline, it was revenge for the loss of her adopted family.
- One night, both Jacqueline and Baroth got tipsy from the ale they stole. Jacqueline grew sorrowful as they reminisced about the loss of the people they loved, Katrine in particular. Jacqueline nearly sought comfort in Baroth's arms, but Baroth helped her fall asleep without anything happening as he knew her heart still pined for Katrine. Jacqueline thanked him the next morning for that. A week later, after raiding another CC caravan, both had a drink of ale again, and Jacqueline offered to spend the night with Baroth, but this time, it was out of the camaraderie the two held for each. Neither were looking to start a relationship, both understood that Jacqueline's heart was not ready for it and Baroth knew too little of the world, but that night, the two of them together felt right. In the morning after that night of intimacy, both agreed that they were not lovers, but their bond was certainly stronger, and it was a fun way to work off their frustrations.
- In a failed attack, Baroth was captured by Shinkiba's Raiders, a band of mercenaries that pillage for loot and slaves, secretly supported by the CC. SR took Baroth and forced him to participate in their underground gladiator ring in the city of Spiergans where he went up against the slave gladiator [[Klash]]. Jacqueline stealthily followed, and managed to sneak in to get food and healing to Baroth who shared them with Klash. The three formed a friendship. Jacqueline was able to find out how to free them from their magical shackles, but Shinkiba was too well protected for her to do it alone.
- Baroth and Klash had a very deadly gladiatorial match that nearly killed them, surviving only by some very desperate actions both of them did to save each other. That night, hidden away from the guards (who were celebrating their new gains), and their wounds tended to by Jacqueline, Baroth, Klash, and Jacqueline were overwhelmed with adrenaline and the fear of their lives. The three of them together engaged in a night of intimacy and comfort, cementing their bond together.
- All that changed when [[Kain Gallant|Kain]] and [[Kyle Windswood|Kyle]] were captured by the SR, and Jacqueline teamed up with their friends.
