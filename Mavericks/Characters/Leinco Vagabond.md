**Description:** slender muscular build, long night blue hair swept back with only a pair of strands bent forward from a single point at the centre of his forehead, pale skin, long sharp face, narrow golden eyes, long ears.

**Origin:**
- **5045 5A:** Leinco was born to Ellessia Arialune, Princess of Coraithess, and Daeroleth Kethrym, a disgraced knight. Leinco's parents grew up together on Coraithess, and Daeroleth became Ellessia's personal knight, then betrothed. The two loved each other, and Daeroleth supported Ellessia's adventures. However, Daeroleth had grown fanatically loyal to the royal family and the elven ideals they represented. In his service, he had secretly committed many atrocities against enemies of the crown without the royal family's knowledge or approval. When his actions were uncovered, Ellessia became horrified by him. The betrothal was broken and Daeroleth was exiled from Coraithess, but not before Ellessia had become pregnant with his child, though the conception was so recent that no one knew at the time. When Ellessia did realise she was pregnant, she kept it a secret from even her family. She was unwilling to abort the child, but neither could she have the child of a criminal as a prince. With the aid of her youngest brother Illystiel, she travelled in secret to a hidden location on Asteropa to give birth under her adventurer's identity. She entrusted the child to a childless elf couple farmers who she had befriended in the past, then never saw the child again, with only Illystiel knowing the whole truth.
- **5050 5A:** Unfortunately, several years later, the farm would be attacked by the mercenary company Vallon's Tigers as collateral damage of a war in the region. Leinco's foster parents were killed, but the elf child was spared as Vallon was impressed by this small child displaying psychic power in an attempt to attack the mercenaries. Vallon would take in the child, and for the next four decades, he would raise him as his son. He trained him in the mercenary ways, instilling in him a strict mercenary code. Even though he was still technically an elf adolescent, Leinco developed his psychic powers and sword skills. He became the Tigers' deadliest fighter, with more room to grow. Vallon was strict in his training, and although he was proud of Leinco, never showed him outward affection. This upbringing, and with all the killing he participated in, hardened Leinco emotionally, and he grew callous to the feelings of others. However, he nonetheless developed a strong bond with his company, especially Vallon, even if there were no outward expressions of feelings involved.
- **5080 5A:** On one job, the Tigers were betrayed by their employers that left them hunted and depleted in resources. The company came into the employ of Saeleron Lamilryth. Saeleron was the heir of his house, and a member of the Houses of Twilight. He needed some disposable fighters that no one would miss, so he employed the Tigers. However, thanks to Leinco, the Tigers succeeded in their jobs, and Saeleron paid close attention to this young elf prodigy. Secretly, Saeleron was offended that such an elf was working as a merc among non-elves. However, the more he looked into Leinco, he was surprised that this might be a member of the royal family.
- Saeleron convinced Vallon and the Tigers that Leinco was the last heir to an enemy against the state of all elves. His death would see the Tigers receive a great reward, and guarantee many years of fruitful employment from elven nobles, in particular his own. With a bit of magical coercion from Saeleron's own skills, Vallon and the Tigers attempted to kill Leinco in an ambush. However, Leinco was skilled enough to kill many of them. He was forced to kill Vallon as well, but Vallon mortally wounded him in exchange. Saeleron timely appeared in time to finish off the rest of the Tigers and rescue Leinco.
- Saeleron told Leinco that he had overheard some of the Tigers expressing jealousy of Leinco's skills and survival in their harsh times, and that they viewed him as a curse. Saeleron, on the other hand and having gained Leinco's confidence, offered him a place in his retinue. Leinco, freshly scarred from the betrayal of the only family he knows, agreed. Part of him still adheres to the mercenary code, and by rescuing him, Saeleron has bought his service for life.
- For the next several decades, Leinco became Saeleron's personal enforcer and agent. Saeleron was careful to never directly involve him in Twilight House operations, but he did have him do a lot of clean up and errands that would support those activities. Many of those clean ups involved the elimination of hired goons who committed wetwork for Saeleron, but Saeleron would claim them to Leinco as deadly threats that needed to be eliminated so as to not let Leinco think that Saeleron was involved in nefarious activities. Saeleron also tried to cultivate the ideals of Elven superiority in Leinco, though to little success; Leinco simply didn't care anymore.
- **5134 5A:** Leinco became numb to his service to Saeleron, though he continued to excel in his tasks. He followed orders, accompanied Saeleron when he needed to, and honed his skills, but he had no friends, no hobbies, no dreams of the future. Used to working alone and knowing he is being exploited, Leinco was cold and silent to everyone.

**Skills:** Leinco wields two straight swords. He can manifest a psionic armour. His only non-combat skill is smithing weapons and armour.
