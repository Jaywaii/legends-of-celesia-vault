**Physical description:** Four feet tall. Bulky, broad, muscular build with light skin. Thick arms and legs, broad shoulders. Shoulder-length dark orange hair pulled back and tied in a top-knot. Braided beard and moustache. Bushy brows. Round eyes with amber irises.

**Origin:**
- **5090 5A:** Rogard Strikinghammer, patriarch of the clan, was tricked by the Crown Consortium. The CC's newest association member, the duerghendar clan Ulgendarth, infiltrated Thunderstrike Hall as a result of the bad deal, and conducted a violent takeover, killing Rogard. The surviving clan members were enslaved, and imprisoned forever within the Hall. In addition to plundering the mines of Thunderstrike, the CC used the Hall as a secret prison for their enemies.
- **5100 5A:** Baroth was born to Rogard and Hartha Strikinghammer in Thunderstrike Hall while in captivity by the Clan Ulgendarth. Hartha died in childbirth due to being overworked in the mines. Baroth's maternal uncle Cornelius raised him.
- **5132 5A:** [[Jacqueline deLouvin]] stowed away onboard a CC airship heading to TH. She encountered Baroth when the young dwarf took the abuse from the CU meant for her.
- Baroth and Jacqueline quickly became friends. With her skills and his strength, they came up with a plan to escape. Baroth wanted to get more of his clan out, but the CU caught on fast. Cornelius and other members of the clan forced Baroth and Jacqueline to flee while they held the CU back. In the process of escaping, Baroth and Jacqueline were swept away by a river. Half-conscious, the pair became lost as they continued to flee.
- **5133 5A:** Baroth and Jacqueline were unable to trace their steps back to Thunderstrike Hall. After recovering, they stole weapons and supplies and began attacking CC caravans and other assets, for both revenge and trying to find the way back to the Hall.
- One night, both Jacqueline and Baroth got tipsy from the ale they stole. Jacqueline grew sorrowful as they reminisced about the loss of the people they loved, Katrine in particular. Jacqueline nearly sought comfort in Baroth's arms, but Baroth helped her fall asleep without anything happening as he knew her heart still pined for Katrine. Jacqueline thanked him the next morning for that. A week later, after raiding another CC caravan, both had a drink of ale again, and Jacqueline offered to spend the night with Baroth, but this time, it was out of the camaraderie the two held for each. Neither were looking to start a relationship, both understood that Jacqueline's heart was not ready for it and Baroth knew too little of the world, but that night, the two of them together felt right. In the morning after that night of intimacy, both agreed that they were not lovers, but their bond was certainly stronger, and it was a fun way to work off their frustrations.
- In a failed attack, Baroth was captured by Shinkiba's Raiders, a band of mercenaries that raid for loot and slaves secretly employed by the CC. Jacqueline managed to evade capture, and stealthily followed the SR. The SR run an underground gladiator slave ring, and forced Baroth to participate. Baroth fought against the urokhan [[Klashi'ra]] multiple times, and they earned each other's respect. Jacqueline joined their growing friendship by sneaking in food and supplies for their wounds. Jacqueline figured out how to help them escape their magical bondage, but she could not do it on her own. Shinkiba the ogre mage had too many protections guarding her.
- Baroth and Klash had a very deadly gladiatorial match that nearly killed them, surviving only by some very desperate actions both of them did to save each other. That night, hidden away from the guards (who were celebrating their new gains), and their wounds tended to by Jacqueline, Baroth, Klash, and Jacqueline were overwhelmed with adrenaline and the fear of their lives. The three of them together engaged in a night of intimacy and comfort, cementing their bond together.
- The plan becomes possible when the SR capture [[Kain Gallant|Kain]] and [[Kyle Windswood|Kyle]] and force them to fight with Baroth and Klash.
