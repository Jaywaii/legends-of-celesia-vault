**Origin:**
- **5075 5A:** Karse was born as an orphan in the Valkirk Republic, his parents killed by corrupt officials.
- **5080 5A:** Karse was a strong and eager fighter even at 5 years old, and came to [[Thanatos]]' attention during one of the skull knight's child soldier recruiting. Thanatos saw Karse's potential, and chose to be the boys teacher.
- **5091 5A:** Thanatos ordered Karse to go and grow his strength out in the world on his own.
- **5093 5A:** Karse first encountered Abrihm as a new mercenary protecting an Aerdwish merchant noble.
- **5094 5A:** Abrihm convinced Karse to join him in rebellion against the Aerdwish Empire. Karse agreed, but still demanded a pay.
- **5111 5A:** Karse married into the noble Dasturm duchy who were secret Zhaokhanites, arranged by Thanatos.
- **5116 5A:** Karse fathers a daughter, [[Zeia kar Dasturm|Zeia]].
