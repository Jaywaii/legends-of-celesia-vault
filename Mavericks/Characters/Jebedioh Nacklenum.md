**Origin:**
- **5089 5A:** Jebedioh was born to Clan Nacklenum in the village of Gleamstadle, an independent settlement in the Valendar Wilds. His mother was Debbilinea and his father was Flockinero.
- **5119 5A:** Jebedioh began studying his family's alchemical arts at a very young age. While he wasn't the most exceptional student, Jebedioh pushed himself the hardest, and was even more willing to push the boundaries of experimentation, even by gnomish standards.
- **5124 5A:** Jebedioh was encouraged to travel to the royal capital of Whismerlund (Whistarkastell) to study alchemy there (and to stop the random explosions in the village).
- **5132 5A:** Jebedioh's village became afflicted by a mysterious deadly disease. It was resistant to both mundane and magical healing, and was very contagious. Gleamstadle quarantined itself, and the Nacklenums alerted Jebedioh via magical communication. Jebedioh begged the Whismer King for aid, and he agreed to send an expedition of healers and sages for their fellow gnomes. None could solve the disease. Jebedioh was actually the closest to treating it with an experimental concoction, but even that only slowed down its spread, and gave some comfort to the afflicted. Sadly, Jebedioh's mother was among those who succumbed to the disease before he could arrive.
	- The disease was an experiment by the Coil Society, in cooperation with the Church of Thraxis. It was a new disease to eliminate other races, with this strand focusing on gnomes. Other experiments were being conducted on other races around the world. Like with Gleamstadle, these experiments targeted small villages inhabited by one specific race. Not all of them were successful (not deadly enough, not contagious enough, easily cured, etc.), and the project was still a work in progress. The Coil also had to be careful to not conduct too many of these experiments to avoid gaining attention (and limits to their resources, which have to be shared with other Coil initiatives, added to further restrictions).
- Jebedioh became obsessed with curing the disease, and crafted his experimental tonic as much as possible for his family and village. However, the ingredients were expensive and hard to get. The Whismerlunding expedition and the Whistarkastell lodge of the Argent Rose Society tried to help, but there were limits to how much Whismerlund was willing to give to a village outside of their borders. Frustrated, Jebedioh set out himself to gather those ingredients, and to learn anything that might lead to a cure.
- Jebedioh travelled across the eastern seaboard of Asteropa, specifically stopping by centres of alchemical studies. He learned much, however, he could not stay long. With very limited funds, Jebedioh ended up stealing much of the exotic ingredients he needed for his tonic. Oftentimes, he would also break into places to gain access to restricted knowledge. The Argent Rose was willing to share what they know, but did not appreciate the thefts. Other independent guilds and academies were even less cooperative, and bounties were placed on his head. In spite of the danger, Jebedioh was able to send either the ingredients or crafted tonics back to Gleamstadle to continue their survival.
- **5134 5A:** Jebedioh arrived in Solaris, and initially gained access to the Royal Alchemist Guild. After learning all that he could, Jebedioh tried stealing the ingredients he needed. However, he was caught this time, and placed in jail.
	- Little did he know, the Coil had set up a trap for him. Jebedioh came to their attention when they learned of his tonic interfering with their experiment. However, instead of killing him, the Coil agent in Solaris, Court Count Danath of House Kendleleth (Steward to King Abrihm and also secret Zhaokhanite), sought to use his alchemical expertise to break through the defences surrounding the Crown of Tyranny. While formidable, the ancient defences did not take into account advancements in the field of alchemy.
- Jebedioh's experimental concoctions were able to slowly break through the ancient defences. Jebedioh went along with Danath's scheme in order to survive. Danath even offered to keep sending the ingredients to Gleamstadle, mostly to make sure no one suspected anything happened to the gnome. However, Jebedioh knew he was dead as soon as he completed his task. He bluffed and delayed as much as possible, but he could not halt his progress. He used the time he bought himself to come up with an escape plan.
- The key part of his plan arrived when [[Kain Gallant|Kain]] and [[Kyle Windswood|Kyle]] were captured by Danath, framed for killing Marshal of Hynaeria. Danath planned on using his three prisoners as the necessary sacrifice to break the last trapped defense around the crown.
