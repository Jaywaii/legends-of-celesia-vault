**Description:**

**Origin:**
- **5051 5A:** Sinnara was born as the youngest daughter to Matriarch Ilvryssea of House Lysenthiir in the Deep Hollows dark elf city of Belzorathyr (under Valendar).
- Sinnara became closest to her brother [[Feydarrin Lysenthiir|Feydarrin]], for both his sense of humor and his dedication to mastering his fighting skills. Feydarrin treated Sinnara as his favorite sibling in return.
- While Sinnara had no qualms at killing innocent lives with her own hands, she found no enjoyment in inflicting pain just for the sake of it, unlike the rest of her family who practiced torture as a pasttime. Sinnara's lack of sadistic tendencies made her the disappointment of the family, especially to her mother and older sisters. They viewed her as weak-willed. The only exception was Feydarrin. Feydarrin could be just as sadistic as the rest of the family (he particularly enjoyed toying with his opponents by making them die from a thousand cuts instead of a clean strike), he at least respected Sinnara's preferences, and encouraged her to live as she felt she should.
	- Has no qualms with ending lives, even if they are innocent and undeserving, so long as their deaths advance her goals.
	- Has participated in torture on many victims, with the goal of mastering the techniques and finding out what is effective in gaining information from the victim, but never found any pleasure in inflicting the pain, only satisfaction in gaining what she sought. She would stop any torture immediately as soon as she judges it to no longer be effective or necessary.
	- Cool, calm, and calculating to a terrifying degree.
	- Lacks most self-defeating emotions. She is proud and confident, but not to the point of being arrogant or blind to her faults. She does not deny her failures, but instead learns from them. She never forgets past transgressions against her or those she cares about, or past grudges, but rarely obsesses over them, and is willing to forgive or at least come to an understanding.
- Despite her differing attitude, Sinnara remained valued by the family for her mastery at intrigue. This also earned her the favor of Noué, which also raised her value. And at the end of the day, the Lysenthiir still adhered to family ties; Sinnara may be a disappointing dark elf, but she was their disappointment. They may not respect her much, but they still loved her as one of their own.
- What Sinnara really loved (aside from her brother) was uncovering secrets and manipulating others. She was particularly smart, had good judgment, and captivatingly alluring. She soon took over the Lysenthiir's spy network, and was responsible for their intelligence gathering, often going on missions herself. Feydarrin would often accompany her as her bodyguard and chief assassin.
- **5101 5A:** Sinnara completed her rites to become a full-fledged priestess of Noué under her mother's tutelage. As part of the ceremony, Sinnara summoned a demon in service to Noué, and sacrificed her virginity to it. Seven nights later, she gave birth to a cambion daughter who the demon took, granting Sinnara Noué's blessing in return. Normally, the priestess abandons all ties to their offspring of their ritual coupling, but Sinnara would secretly long for her daughter. She named her "Zelliiara", but spoke the name only once before she was taken. Zelliara would be called "Felzarra", raised in one of the Hells to be a ruthless killer, but unbeknownst to all, a dormant emotional connection between the young mother and wayward daughter had formed, waiting for something to awaken it. Sinnara would never know of her, and tried to never think about her, but could never forget that special little life she held in her arms for that brief moment, and never forgave the demon, her mother, or even Noué for taking her daughter from her.

#### Miracle of Resurrection

In order to fully divest herself of the Nightsinger's influence, Sinnara is forced to undergo a lengthy spiritual trial.

- she must face the cruelties she inflicted on the innocent, and bear the judgment from the Mavericks as they are all revealed to them
- at first, she is defiant, rationalizes her actions
- the confrontations to meant to break her spirit, to show her unworthiness of redemption
- the Nightsinger separates the Mavericks from Sinnara, trapping them in mortal danger
- Nightsinger has her brother's soul (sacrificed by their matron mother in her madness and obsession to end the line of Ari'luth)
- forced to choose between giving her soul to the Nightsinger in eternal service to save her brother from eternal torment, alternatively sacrifice the Mavericks to Nightsinger
- finally dispensing with the cool, snarky attitude, Sinnara lets the guilt she buried deep within her come to the surface
- she is torn between the love for her brother, and the possibilities walking in the moonlight alongside [[Cyrussilar Ari'luth|Cyrus]] and the Mavericks
- Cyrus makes the decision for her, willingly putting his life and soul on the line, telling her to save her brother, that he and the Mavericks will fight their way out of the Nightsinger's grasp
- Cyrus is then killed by the Nightsinger
- Sinnara finally chooses selflessness over greed and ambition, willing to sacrifice her soul to save Cyrus and the others, finally acknowledging her desire to have them as friends and family
- Sinnara admits that she is not worthy of the Moonseer's aid, and asks only that the moon god saves Cyrus
- The Moonseer shines the path of enlightenment for Sinnara, allowing her to have enough faith to cast the resurrection art, reviving Cyrus
