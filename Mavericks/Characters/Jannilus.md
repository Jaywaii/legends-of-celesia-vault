**Alias:** "Saturus"

**Physical description:** Five and a half feet tall. Skinny, bony build. Ghostly white skin. Gaunt face. Long, pointy nose. Sharp angular eyebrows arching upwards to the sides. Slim, upturned eyes with dark red irises. Long, purple hair swept back, exposing a long widow's peak in the front. Elf features (long pointed ears, angular eyes, slightly shorter than humans).

**Flaw:** ambitious, prideful, snarky.

**Arc:** maybe letting someone get close to you can be a good thing, but you may need to let that bitterness go.

**Origin:**
- **5051 5A:** Jannilus was born Kaïshan 2nd, 5051 5A in Suldeinor.
- He was born into a family of simple farmers (Ephalia and Celerus of Lerucian village) and was an only child.
- Jannilus had poor health since his birth, but he demonstrated incredible intelligence and talent for magic at a very early age. He began learning magic under the tutelage of the village's local hedge wizard (Alfanna). Although he learned quickly, Jannilus was too eager to learn, always trying to cast magic beyond his capabilities, causing a lot of magical accidents.
- **5071 5A:** For years growing up, Jannilus was mocked and bullied by the other children for his weakness that prevented him from being a productive member of the village, and for his magical mishaps. One day, an athletic red-haired girl appeared out of nowhere and defended him from the abuse. She was so impressive that it left an unforgettable impression on Jannilus. Her smile and kindness to him only made him more smitten than ever. However, before he could get her name, the girl was dragged away by her guardian; both were wandering vagrants, and the guardian admonished the girl for making a ruckus and getting attention on them.
- **5072 5A:** A year later, the village was attacked by wandering monsters. Jannilus' parents were killed in the attack, as was Alfanna and many others in the village. Jannilus tried to save them with his meagre spells, but he was not powerful enough. All his magic could do was keep him alive until a party of elven adventurers (The New Dawn Knights) arrived in time to slay the monsters.
- The wizard Elshallia of the NDK witnessed Jannilus's casting and saw potential in him, and offered to become his new teacher. With nowhere else to go, Jannilus accepts.
- In reality, the NDK caused the attack. The NDK were agents of the Twilight Houses. They were experimenting with a new weapon to create and use monsters as living weapons, and Lerucian village was their test. They also wanted to eliminate Alfanna who had gained clues of their activities. Alfanna was a retired Argent Rose agent, and some info was passed along to her for consultation.
- Beyond his significant intelligence and magical talent, Elshallia saw the pride and ambition in Jannilus. She wanted to groom him into a powerful agent of the TH.
- Elshallia both taught and tried to convert Jannilus to the TH cause. She did not tell him their true allegiance, and started his training in a caring manner. However, her lessons gradually gained a more and more brutal and crueler aspect to them. She tried to instil a sense of elven superiority and destiny into Jannilus.
- While Jannilus was influenced by his 2nd teacher, in his heart, Jannilus was only ever concerned with his own power to never let the world hurt him again. While he went along with the NDK's goals, even after pretence was all but dropped, Jannilus cared nothing for ideals. They were just a means to an end for his own goals. The only thing he and Elshallia shared was a genuine love of magic and learning.
- **5115 5A:** the NDK bring Jannilus on a mission to hunt down a pair of fugitives, two elves, one older man, the other a girl, upon the orders of the TH. The girl, [[Loreilana Amerlyth]], was the last scion of the Amerlyth martial clan, and she had her family's secret arts locked in her mind. The TH that eliminated her clan (the Kirthendil clan) wanted her so they could marry her to their own heir, thus gaining a legitimate claim in taking over their rival's legacy as well as getting their secrets. The NDK found the fugitives, and Jannilus recognized the girl as the same one that defended him so many years ago. Jannilus tried to help the girl, but the NDK came upon them after killing her guardian, and took her prisoner.
- While Loreilana was in the NDK's custody, Jannilus volunteered to watch over her as an excuse to try and get to know Loreilana better. However, she was not in the mood for it, for she was grieving the death of her guardian, and all she knew of Jannilus was that he worked with his killers, even after he tried to tell her of that one time she helped him.
- Jannilus learned of the NDK's plans for Loreilana. The Kirthendil clan ordered the NDK to keep Loreilana unharmed and unspoiled, but also use magic to warp her mind into being pliant to the Kirthendils' plans. Jannilus would not allow this, and began planning to escape the NDK with her.
- Although reluctant to trust Jannilus, Loreilana went along with him. After they escaped, she tried to flee from him, but the NDK found her. Jannilus intervened in time, and was gravely wounded by Elshallia when he declared his defiance and put himself in-between them and Loreilana. The act gave Loreilana a chance to escape, but she chose instead to grab the dying Jannilus and try to save him. However, the NDK easily surrounded them.
- As the NDK were about to kill Jannilus and take Loreilana, the Greenleaf Company made a surprise attack. The GC had become entangled in one of NDK's many plots, and followed the clues behind their monster weapon experiments all the way to their lair. From there, they followed their trail to the wilderness where they came upon them with Jannilus and Loreilana. The GC defeated the NDK, and rescued Jan and Lore.
- The GC healed Jannilus, and recovered Loreilana's guardian and laid him to rest. They invited Jan and Lore to come with them, and find refuge in Feywood. The two young elves accepted.
- Jannilus accepted to continue his tutelage under the GC's wizard, the gnome Belladella Bopplecrum. Although their attitudes were completely different and Jannilus would often express annoyance and frustration from the gnome's merry nature, he recognized and respected her power, and accepted learning from her.
- In Feywood, Jannilus reluctantly became involved in [[Kain Gallant|Kain]]'s circle of friends only thanks to Loreilana. He found Kain, and later [[Kyle Windswood|Kyle]], to be extremely annoying. Thankfully, [[Cyrussilar Ari'luth|Cyrus]] was almost always quiet, and he and Loreilana were more than willing to try to rein in the boisterous boys. The only company he truly enjoyed was Loreilana's. The girl had warmed up to him, and saw that beneath his snarky and pompous attitude was a brave soul willing to put his life at risk for what he believed in.
- Jannilus' dislike of Kain and Kyle stemmed from the fact that they reminded him of those bullies in his younger years. However, unlike those children, Kain and Kyle respected his intellect and magical skills. The boys never made fun of him for his freakish appearance (only for his pompous and snarky attitude towards them), and they always tried to get him involved in their fun. That act of inclusion, and their obviously growing martial skills, woke a very small amount of tolerance, and maybe respect, from Jannilus towards them. That was why, despite all his protesting, he would eventually tag along with their adventures, although much of that was due to Loreilana's involvement.
- Under Belladella's tutelage, Jannilus formed a familiar pact with a female cat with dark purple fur and red eyes. He bestowed the name Amaranthine upon her. Amaranthine was just as intelligent, snooty, and prideful as Jannilus, rarely obeying his commands, but was surprisingly helpful in his magical studies by bringing his attention to clues that would aid him. For that, Jannilus tolerated her attitude, and was even a bit amused by it. The magic of the familiar pact probably also played a factor.

**Skills:**
- Expert knowledge of arcane magic and planar physics.
- Skilled in spellcrafting.
- Trained in writing spell scrolls.
- Uses a staff as his spell focus.
- Intelligent cat familiar named Amaranthine.
- As an elf, Jannilus has keen senses, especially his eyesight and hearing.
- *Analyse Magic:* For a few moments, caster can detect the presence of magic within a certain range, and can take time to determine its nature and effects, including understanding any magical script.
	- The more powerful the caster, the longer the spell persists, and the further its range reaches.
- *Mage's Hand:* The caster conjures an invisible hand of magical force that allows them to carry or manipulate objects at a distance for a moment.
	- The more powerful the caster, the longer the spell lasts, the more weight the hand can carry, and the further it can reach.
- *Arcane Bolt:* A small, hand-size, arrow-like missile of pure magical force that explodes on impact. It never misses as long as the caster has line of sight with the target, and has a long range. The bolt glows bright white, tinted with a colour of the caster's preference (purple in Jannilus' case).
	- The more powerful the caster, the more damage the bolt inflicts. Alternatively, the caster can shape the extra energy into different offensive effects.
	- Energising the spell at a higher valence increases the number of bolts that can be fired at once. The caster can split the number of bolts at their choice among different targets within sight. Each bolt can be shaped to have different effects.
- *Mage's Aegis:* An invisible, weightless layer of magical force surrounds the caster's body, protecting them from physical impacts that would cause harm. The aegis briefly flashes white tinted with the caster's preferred colour upon receiving impacts. Alternatively, the caster can shape the aegis into a weightless disc and wield it like a shield. As a disc not layered on the caster's body, the aegis better deflects oncoming physical attacks instead of absorbing the damage.
	- The aegis' protection increases the more powerful the caster becomes.
	- Energising the spell at a higher valence allows the caster to shape the extra energy into different defensive effects.
