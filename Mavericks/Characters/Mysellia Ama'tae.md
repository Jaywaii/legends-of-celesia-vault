**Description:** Slender, nearly gaunt woman standing five and three-quarters tall with pale skin. Long, straight golden hair tied back into a long braid that reaches down past the end of her back. Slender eyes with grey irises. Long, sharp, and angular eyebrows.

**Origin:**
- **4815 5A:** Mysellia was born to the 2nd eldest child of the Ama'tae matriarch.
- **4890 5A:** Mysellia finished her training to become a cleric of Kaï-den. She chose to become an adventurer, much to the disapproval of her family.
- **4900 5A:** Mysellia joined Kaïdenite paladin [[Thanatos|Eadred]] of Caerwedd on his quest to bring peace and justice to his realm.
- **4922 5A:** Mysellia and Eadred fought side-by-side against the forces of Somerstark. At this point, she fully embraced the faith of the Tyrant, especially as it was his teachings that allowed her and her lover to destroy their enemies.

Fatally wounded, Mysellia uses her Final Sacrifice to allow her weaker Ama'tae blood to be usable for the ritual to resurrect Zhao-Khan.
