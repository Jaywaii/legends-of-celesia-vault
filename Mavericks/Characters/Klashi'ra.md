**Description:** Six and three quarters feet tall. Powerful, muscular, and voluptuous build with light green skin. Wolf-like eyes with yellow irises. Long, dark green hair tied in braids, shaved on each side of her head. Pair of small, pointy underbite tusks.

**Origin:**
- **5118 5A:** Klashi'ra was born to an male orc blacksmith and a female human mercenary warrior in a village in the Valendar Wilds.
- **5121 5A:** The Shinkiba's Raiders pillaged Klashi'ra's home, killing her parents. Shinkiba enslaved the surviving children. Impressed by her strength and ferocity at such a young age, the Raiders raised the child as a gladiator-slave, renaming her as Klashi'ra. The training was brutal, but Klashi'ra endured all of it. Much of her education was martial in nature; the SR taught her only the bare minimum of language just so that she could understand their orders, and she could not read.
- **5130 5A:** Klashi'ra killed her first living creature, an attack dog, as part of her training.
- **5131 5A:** Klashi'ra killed her first intelligent primanoid, another gladiator-slave trainee (a human boy) as part of her training. While the act did not guilt her (they had no choice, either the boy killed her or the SR killed them both), Klashi'ra immediately understood how pointless and cruel the act was. Nonetheless, she knew that he was only the first, and that she needed to do whatever she could to survive.
- **5133 5A:** Klashi'ra officially joined the gladiator-slave ring. Throughout the year, she would win fight after fight, earning her owners a good amount of profit.
- **5134 5A:** Klashi'ra sparred with a newly captured dwarf slave named [[Baroth Strikinghammer|Baroth]]. Baroth was the first to fight her to a standstill, and the first to show her respect afterwards even though they just beat the crap out of each other.
- A halfling named [[Jacqueline deLouvin|Jacqueline]] was Baroth's companion, and was secretly tailing the SR in hopes of finding a way to free her friend. At Baroth's request, Jacqueline shared some of the food and healing she stole to the battered Klashi'ra. This was the first act of kindness Klashi'ra ever experienced.
- Over the next months, Klashi'ra found herself befriending Baroth and Jacqueline.
- Baroth and Klashi'ra had a very deadly gladiatorial match that nearly killed them, surviving only by some very desperate actions both of them did to save each other. That night, hidden away from the guards (who were celebrating their new gains), and their wounds tended to by Jacqueline, Baroth, Klashi'ra, and Jacqueline were overwhelmed with adrenaline and the fear of their lives. The three of them together engaged in a night of intimacy and comfort, cementing their bond together.
- Klashi'ra and Baroth were forced to beat two newly captured slaves, an aelfkyn named [[Kain Gallant|Kain]] and a human named [[Kyle Windswood|Kyle]]. The four beat each other up, but like Baroth before, Kyle and Kain showed respect to Klashi'ra. Kain understood immediately that they were slaves with no choice in the matter and that they bore no cruel intentions. Kyle was just excited to meet such strong fighters.
