# Lineage Traits

- **Type:** Sylphs are primanoids with the air subtype.
- **Size:** Sylphs  can be either Medium creatures and gain no bonuses or penalties due to their size, or Small creatures and gain -1 to Strength and Constitution, +1 to Dexterity, have a reach of 0 ft., and 7 additional LP to spend on traits from any Small lineage.
- **Speed:** Medium sylphs have a land speed of 30 ft., and small sylphs have a land speed of 20 ft.

## Standard

- **Elemental Sight (darkvision):** Thanks to their elemental heritage, sylphs have 60-ft. darkvision.

## Expert

- **Air Heritage (magic affinity ([[Aeromancy|Air]])):** The elemental heritage from the plane of Air grants sylphs a bonus +1 refinement capacity with mystic arts of the Air school.
- **Like the Wind (swiftness [fly]) (Mg):** Sylphs with this trait can manifest a little bit of wind to improve their fly speed by 5 ft. as a lineage bonus.
- **Whispering Wind (skill advantage [Stealth]) (Mg):** Manifesting winds to distort sound and sight around them, sylphs with this trait gain +1 advantage on Stealth checks.
- **Wind at my Back (swiftness [land]) (Mg):** Sylphs with this trait can manifest a little bit of wind to push themselve forward, improving their land speed by 5 ft. as a lineage bonus.

## Master

- **Breeze-Kissed (natural mystic art ([[Tempest Invocation]])):** Sylphs with this trait have a strong connection to their elemental heritage of Air, and can use this mystic art using their level as their tier and their Charisma as their cultivation modifier.

## Legendary

- **Dancing with the Winds (attribute boost [Dexterity]):** The elemental heritage of the air grants these sylphs with swiftness and flow of the winds.
