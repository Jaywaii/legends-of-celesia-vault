# Orcs

# Characteristics

Orcs stand between five to seven feet, but lean towards the taller end on average. They have a bulky build, and usually have thick muscles.

# History

It is currently believed that orcs developed naturally alongside humans and lucklyns on the ancient island continent of Xian around the start of the 2nd Age. They were the more aggressive of the three peoples competing for resources, yet human ingenuity and lucklyn sneakiness prevented them from dominating and wiping the other peoples out. The orcs also began migrating to other parts of the world when the competition grew fierce. However, they, along with the uumans and lucklyns, fell prey to the more advanced and expansionistic civilizations of the sserassins and the neraxins.
