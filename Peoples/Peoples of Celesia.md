# Peoples of Celesia

# Playable Lineages

**[[Aelfkyns]]**—Also known as half-elves, aelfkyn are a people descended from the union of [[Elves]] and [[Humans]].

**[[Anthousais]]**—Fairy spirits born of the Eternity Tree that have chosen to bloom on Middle Realm as a mortals.

**[[Caelephilims]]**—Mortals of any lineage whose ancestry is tied to outsider celestials.

**[[Dabyuns]]**—A bestial lineage with hyena-like features.

**[[Dhampirs]]**—Mortals of any lineage whose bloodline was affected by vampirism.

**[[Dwarves]]**—A stout, stocky people with ties to the [[Terramancy|Earth]].

**[[Duerghendars]]**—A offshoot of the dwarven people that have developed natural psionic abilities.

**[[Elves]]**—Descendents of the first fairies to have transitioned to Middle Realm, imbuing both nature and [[Magic]].

**[[Gnomes]]**—A short people descending from [[Terramancy|Earth]] fairies that transitioned to Middle Realm.

**[[Goblins]]**—A short, ravenous people descending from fairies that transitioned to the Middle Realm.

**[[Goliaths]]**—A tall, muscular people descended from giants.

**[[Hobgoblins]]**—A people descended from fiery fairies that transitioned to the Middle Realm.

**[[Humans]]**—An adaptable people with no distinct characteristics except for their great potential.

**[[Ifrits]]**—A race of mortals whose ancestry is tied to the elemental plane of [[Pyromancy|Fire]].

**[[Jiaorens]]**—A race of aquatic people with the upper torso of a human, and the lower body of a fish tail.

**[[Jirens]]**—A sapient people of constructs created in a similar fashion as [[Humans]].

**[[Kitais]]**—A bestial people with feline features.

**[[Kitsunes]]**—A shapeshifting people with vulpine features.

**[[Kobolds]]**—A short people with a mix of dog and dragon-like features that are descended from devious earth fairies.

**[[Legarens]]**—A bestial people with lupine features.

**[[Liantis]]**—An aquatic people descending from [[Humans]].

**[[Lucklyns]]**—A short, supernaturally lucky people.

**[[Malephilims]]**—Mortals of any lineage whose ancestry is tied to outsider fiends.

**[[Nui'anthyri]]**—An offshoot of the [[Elves]] that have adapted to the lightless underground.

**[[Orcs]]**—A brawny people of incredible endurance with predatory bestial features.

**[[Oreads]]**—Mortals of any lineage whose ancestry is tied to the elemental plane of [[Terramancy|Earth]].

**[[Sakkadis]]**—A bestial people with lizard-like features.

**[[Sei'anthyri]]**—An aquatic offshoot of the [[Elves]] that have adapted to live underwater.

**[[Skrivens]]**—A close communal people with rat-like features.

**[[Sylphs]]**—Mortals of any lineage whose ancestry is tied to the elemental plane of [[Aeromancy|Air]].

**[[Tengus]]**—A tricky and superstitious people with crow-like features.

**[[Undines]]**—Mortals of any lineage whose ancestry is tied to the elemental plane of [[Aquamancy|Water]].

**[[Uroknars]]**—Also known as half-orcs, [[Uroknars]] are a people descended from the union of [[Orcs]] and [[Humans]].

**[[Vanaras]]**—A bestial people with monkey-like features.

**[[Vishkanyas]]**—A graceful human-like people with serpent-like features.

**[[Wayangs]]**—A short people descending from shadow fairies that transitioned to Middle Realm.

# Non-Playable Lineages

**[[Centaurs]]**—A people with a human-like upper torse attached to a horse's body.

**[[Cyclopses]]**—A giant people with one eye, descended from a fabled Titan Cyclopes who sacrificed one eye to get prescient sight.

**[[Harpies]]**—A people with winged arms and bird-like features with magical voices.

**[[Minotaurs]]**—A giant people with bull-like features, descended from the cursed Titan Minos.

**[[Neraxins]]**—A people that resemble giant spiders.

**[[Ogres]]**—A giant people descended from cursed Titans known for their over-proportioned maws and ravenous hunger.

**[[Sserassins]]**—A people that resemble giant snakes with arms and terrible intelligence.

**Surtruvars**—Giants with fiery characteristics and powers, descended from the cursed Titan Surtr.

**[[Thul-Koons]]**—Giant alien worms that serve the Cosmic Horrors.
**[[Trolls]]**—A giant people descended from cursed Titans known for their regenerative bodies.

**Ymirnars**—Giants with icy characteristics and powers, descended from the cursed Titan Ymir.
