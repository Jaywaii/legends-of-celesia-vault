# Humans

Humans are perhaps the most prolific peoples of Celesia, having spread themselves across the world thanks to their ingenuity and drive.

# Characteristics

Humans are perhaps best known for their distinct lack of unique physical characteristics or abilities. They possess only the physiology common to all primanoid peoples. However, it is perhaps that simplicity itself that has allowed them to thrive.

It is said that a blank page holds infinite stories, and humans perhaps exemplify that. Because they have no particularities, humans are not innately swayed towards one direction of development over another. While they are no more intelligent than any other people, that flexibility of having many paths to choose from enables them to be highly adaptable to many different circumstances, be it environmental, magical, or threatening in nature.

Humans' physical characteristics are fairly average compared to other peoples. Their average height lies between dwarves and orcs, though they be as short as the former and as tall as the latter. They can be as slender as elves or be as close to the bulkiness of orcs. Their skin tone ranges from dark brown to light pale, depending on the intensity of their regions sunlight. However, skin coloration remains constant throughout their lives, only changing after multiple generations living in the same area.

Hair color varies between black, blonde, red, and brown. Eye color varies between shades of black, blue, green, and brown. However, humans whose lineages have intertwined with magical beings can often display much more fantastical hair and eye colors.

# History

The current theory is that humans came into being on the island continent of ancient Xian, developing alongside the lucklyn and orc peoples during the 2nd Age. Competition for resources eventually drove all three peoples to migrate in all directions. However, they then fell prey to the more expansionistic and predatory civilizations of the sserassins and the neraxins.

# Lineage Traits

- **Type:** Humans are primanoid creatures with the human subtype.
- **Size:** Humans are Medium creatures, and gain no bonuses or penalties due to their size.
- **Speed:** Humans have a land speed of 30 ft.

## Standard

- **Curious Minds (adaptable):** Humans are very mentally flexible, quick to divest themselves of useless knowledge, and equally quick to pick up new skills.
- **Driven (bonus skill [any]):** Humans select one skill to start with Trained proficiency.
- **Ambitious (bonus standard talent [any]):** Humans select one standard talent as a bonus talent. They must still meets the talent's prerequisites in order to use it.
- **Knack (skill use advantage [any]):** Humans gain +1 advantage on checks made to one selected use of a skill.

## Expert

- **Eager (bonus expert talent [any]):** Humans select one expert talent as a bonus talent. They must still meets the talent's prerequisites in order to use it.
- **Specialization (skill advantage [any]):** Humans cgain +1 advantage with all checks made with one selected skill.

## Master

- **Hunger (bonus master talent [any]):** Humans select one master talent as a bonus talent. They must still meets the talent's prerequisites in order to use it.
- **Adeptness (casting affinity [any]):** Humans can select one casting tradition to have affinity with.

## Legendary

- **Potential (attribute boost [any]):** Humans can select one attribute to boost.
- **Zeal (bonus legendary talent [any]):** Humans select one legendary talent as a bonus talent. They must still meets the talent's prerequisites in order to use it.
