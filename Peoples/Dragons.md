# Dragons

True dragons, as defined by the great beasts themselves, are any creature with six limbs, the ability to fly, skin that grows harder with age without losing its flexbility, the innate ability to manifest mystical arts, an innate connection to a form of energy and the ability to morph that connection, the ability to exhale their chosen energy through their mouth, and the ability to live eternally.
