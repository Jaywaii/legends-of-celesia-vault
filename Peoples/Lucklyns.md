# Lucklyns

# Characteristics

The first thing lucklyns are known for is their small stature, standing between 3 and 5 feet tall.

Aside from their stature, there is little else that is physically remarkable about lucklyns. Their skin tone ranges from dark brown to pale light depending on the intensity of the sun i  the region they developed in. Their skin tone does not change after their birth, but could adapt to their regions climate after many generations.

Hair color can be shades of black, brown, blonde, or red. Eye color can be shades of black, brown, blue, or green. However, magical influence could allow some generations to develop more fantastical hair and eye colors. Lucklyn ears ears tend to be roundish.

Lucklyn feet tend to be slightly proportionally larger than the rest of their body. The underneath of their feet tend to have thicker and rougher skin, and thick hair generally grows on top.

One less concrete fact about lucklyns is that possibly have some sort of supernatural luck. It has been extremely hard to verify such an ability, but historical facts do showcase that the lucklyn people often encounter very fortunate happenstances that have allowed them to survive and thrive within a world of larger, more dangerous beings.

# History

It is currently believed that lucklyns developed naturally on the island continent of Xian during the 2nd Age, alongside the humans and the orcs. Despite their small stature, or because of it, the lucklyns became a very nomadic people, avoiding the predation and expansion of their larger neighbors. It is believed that the lucklyns were the first to start migrating out to the other continents. The other two peoples soon followed, but all three fell prey to the more developed and aggressive civilizations of the sserassins and the neraxins.

# Culture

Lucklyns have many diverse cultures, influenced by the regions they developed their societies in, and the attitudes of the neighboring peoples. One trait that is fairly common among all lucklyn cultures is that they can be quite gregarious. It is thought to be an attitude they developed in response to dealing with many larger folk. With such a significant physical disadvantage, lucklyns have survived more often than not with clever words and amiable attitudes.
