# Goblins

# Characteristics

Goblins are short-statured, between 3 and 5 feet tall. Their skin tone depends on the environment they were born in: shades of green in plains, forests, and jungles, shades of brown in deserts, and shades of grey in mountains. Their bodies and limbs appear scrawny, but they are made of tough, stringy muscle.

Goblin facial features tend to have bigger proportions compared to the rest of their bodies. Their big eyes can be black, yellow, or red. Their big ears are pointy. Their noses can vary in shape, but are quite prominent in any case. Their mouths are wide and filled with jagged teeth.

Despite being small, goblins need at least twice as much nutrition for their size to feel sated, and barely at that; goblins tend to eat so fast that they overeat before their bodies register that they are full.

# History

It is believed that somewhere around the end of the 1st Age and the beginning of the 2nd, a breed of diminutive, mischevious fairies transmigrated from Faerie to the Middle Realm, lured by its more concrete substance to play around with. These fairies were reborn into several different peoples, with the more mischevious and ravenous ones becoming goblins.
