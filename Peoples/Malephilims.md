# Lineage Traits

- **Type:** Malephilims are primanoids with the outsider subtype.
- **Size:** Malephilims can be either Medium creatures and gain no bonuses or penalties due to their size, or Small creatures and gain -1 to Strength and Constitution, +1 to Dexterity, have a reach of 0 ft., and 7 additional LP to spend on traits from any Small lineage.
- **Speed:** Medium malephilims have a land speed of 30 ft., and small malephilims have a land speed of 20 ft.
