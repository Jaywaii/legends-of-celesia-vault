The Valdemar Vagabonds are a nomadic mercenary company with a strict, religious-like code of conduct. They trace their origins to the Valdemar Royal Guard. After their liege was betrayed and his line extinguished, the Royal Guard went on a quest of vengeance against the perpetrators. After succeeding, they became without purpose. However, with their martial skills and experience, they were able to transition as a highly competent and dependable mercenary company. The challenges they faced forced them to transform their previous oaths of loyalty into a highly specialized code of conduct that all members must follow religiously.
