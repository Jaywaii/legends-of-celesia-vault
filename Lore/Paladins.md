# Paladins

Holy warrios. Knights of justice. Slayers of evil.

These are just some of the words that come to mind when paladins come to mind. However, the truth of these specialized martials is so much deeper and more subtle.

# Misconceptions

Let us begin with what paladin are not.

Paladins are not divine soldiers. While many paladins can be, and are, devout champions to higher powers, this is only if said powers' values are compatible with those of the paladin's, and not the inverse.

Paladins are not punishers of evil. While their call to duty often involves them to battle dark forces, paladins seek to end conflict peacefully, and to rehabilitate those who transgress the health of others.

# Virtues

The truth about paladins is that they are champions of the Virtues. They are individuals who have sworn their lives to live up to the ideals of Compassion, Diligence, Generosity, Integrity, Humility, Patience, and Temperence.

Through deep belief, reflection, and adherence to the Virtues, the paladin awakens special powers within themselves. These powers reflect the nature of the Virtues. # Origins

The paladin tradition originates from a hero of the Second Age by the name of Palla-ah'din. She was a woman who somehow believed in seven ideals, and strove to live up to them despite all the horror that surrounded her. By the time she and her companions freed Celesia from the Cosmic Horrors, she had gathered a significant following among the survivors. She passed on her teachings to her followers, and those that succeeded in living up to those ideals became the first paladins, named in honor of their inspiration.

# Fallen Paladins

When paladins fail to uphold the Virtues, they do not necessarily fall from their calling. However, repeated transgressions and failing to reflect on their mistakes reveal the falling paladin's true inclinations. While all beings are born with the potential of ing good and evil, a paladin who lets vice sway their actions find themsleves less and less able to harmonize with the nature of the virtues.
