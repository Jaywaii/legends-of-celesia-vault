# The Haojing

The haojing is a term used for the part of society of individuals who are willing to ignore societal norms and face dangers in the pursuit of personal goals.

"Haojing" is an old Xianese term that roughly means "unrestrained realm". It originates from a poem by Xianese poet  who became enamoured by the rising umber of heroes and villains that lived life outside of normal society during the early 3rd Age.
