# The Veddhic Reformation

# Background

It all started when the Lightbringer slew the Exalted Tyrant to bring an end to the Fiend Soul War.

The Lightbringer had little choice to do otherwise. The Tyrant was the one who initiated the assault, and was on the cusp to slaying the Lightbringer themself. Doing so would allow the Tyrant to claim the Lightbringer's power for themself, and rule both the Heavens and the Middle Realm in a cruel tyranny.

But the Lightbringer loved the Exalted Tyrant more than anyone in the multiverse save the Sage Supreme. To end the existence of one whom they shared with more lifetimes of camaraderie and rivalry beyond the that of reality itself brought no sense of triumph or even relief to the victor. The Lightbringer fell into deep mourning what was loss, and their part in it. This would have repercussions on his followers in the Middle Realm.

The Lightbringer's mortal followers of the Middle Realm were completely ignorant of their god's malaise. In fact, the Lightbringer's faith saw a surge of growth from their victory over the dark forces of the Tyrant. Much of mortal kind looked to the Lightbringer's champions and priests for leadership, especially in the turbulent time of the Age of Strife.

However, the Lightbringer, then withdrawn in their mourning, no longer illuminated as brightly as before. While the faithful continued to be blessed with their grace as before, the insights into the Lightbringer's ideals became more opaque. The faithful then had to rely more and more on their own instincts and interpretations, and the circumstances developing around them, to live as their believed their god wished them to. It was so gradual and incremental that none notice this slight shift in their connection with the solar deity. In fact, in any other scenario, the Lightbringer would have been pleased for their children to be more reliant on their own selves. But then was not the right time for this, for there was still much the children of the Middle Realm had yet to learn.

Moreover, other powers were aware of the new vulnerability in the forces of courage and justice. Exploiting this, the plots of dark gods and powerful demons surged. Ironically, the strife from these threats solidified the resolve of the Lightbringer's faithful to grow stronger. They would lead the charge in holding the darkness back, and would protect world. Alas, only those with near godlike foresight could only hope to see how the steps they took would lead them astray.

Multiple crises would crystallize the straying path the Lightbringer's faithful to the breaking point. With every successful crusade, liberation, and felling of dark lords, the leaders of the faithful became more and more convinced of their rightousness, and the necessity of the Lightbringer faith to take the lead.

Despite each major region of Celesia having their own variation of the Lightbringer's faith, with different customs and hierarchies, each branch seemed to be following the same path.
