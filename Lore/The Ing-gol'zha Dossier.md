# The Ing-gol'zha Dossier

**Compiled by Joliette Beauchamp, chapter-sage of Rochefort Lodge. Updated as of Minyan 3rd, 113 6A.**

# Overview

The following is a compilation of all documents and reports in Argent Rose possession that are relevant to the Necropolis of Ing-gol'zha.

Ing-gol'zha is a city created by the thul-koon believed to have built in the Second Age, towards the end of the occupation of the Cosmic Horrors on our dimension. However, its existence had never been confirmed until now. Up to the present day, we knew only rumours that such a city existed.

Its name had appeared only a few times in mythos texts. Occasionally, a void singer would dream themselves in a twisted, alien city dedicated to the Old Ones. Despite vast times and distances separating these dreamers, all heard the same name whispered to them: "Ing-gol'zha". It is from their accounts that we are aware of the thulkish necropolis at all, but the maddened and raving nature of their writing and state of mind obscured any clue to its location or purpose.
