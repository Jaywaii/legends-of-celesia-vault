During the 3rd Age, the [[The Exalted Tyrant|Exalted Tyrant]] developed a way to distill demons into an essence they could infuse into mortals. A single demon's essence could give birth to a hundred cambions, and the Tyrant captured thousands.

The Tyrant instructed his priests to fashion special wells from which they could draw the fiend essence from the Tyrant's hell. The essence was infused into children and pregnant mothers. The majority died horrifically. However, those that survived were terribly powerful, and the dead were fed to them to help them grow stronger.

The Tyrant secretly grew an army of millions of cambions on Celesia while they also built up their forces in the Seven Hells.
