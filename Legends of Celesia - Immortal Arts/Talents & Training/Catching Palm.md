# Catching Palm

*You are able catch an incoming missile and throw it back.*

### Prerequisites

[[Deflecting Palm]].

### Benefit

When you use the Deflecting Palm technique, you can catch the projectile. When you do so, you can spend a reaction or 1 stamina to throw the weapon at its origin or at another target as a thrown weapon with a range of 5 ft. + 5 ft. times your Strength modifier (minimum 5 ft.). You use your attack modifier plus the attack and critical modifiers of the weapon that fired the projectile (if any). Apply any modifiers or effects for thrown weapons that you may possess, and the traits of the projectile or the weapon that fired it (if any). You cannot use the talents against spells. You cannot use the talent against firearm or siege engine projectiles.
