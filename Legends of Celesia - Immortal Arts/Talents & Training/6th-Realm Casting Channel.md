# 6th-Realm Casting Channel

### Prerequisites

11th rank in the selected tradition, [[5th-Realm Casting Channel]] with the selected tradition, CM +6.

### Benefits

Select a mystic tradition. You gain one 6th-realm casting channel. At 13th rank of your mystic tradition, and every 3 ranks after, you gain one additional 6th-realm casting channel.

### Special

You can take this talent multiple times. Each time you take it, select a different tradition.
