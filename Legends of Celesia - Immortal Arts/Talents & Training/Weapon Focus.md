# Weapon Focus

### Prerequisites

Martial bonus +1.

### Benefits

Select a weapon group. You gain a +1 bonus on attack rolls with weapons of that group.

### Special

This talent can be taken multiple times. Its effects can be applied to a previously selected weapon group, and its effects stack. Alternatively, you can select a new weapon group. The maximum bonus a weapon group can benefit from this talent is equal to your level.
