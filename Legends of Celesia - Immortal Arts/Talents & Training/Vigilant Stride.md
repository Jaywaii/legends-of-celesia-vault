# Vigilant Stride

### Prerequisites

Attack bonus +1.

### Benefits

As a greater action, you can expand your threatened area by 5 ft. + 5 ft. for every +4 attack bonus. Until the start of your next turn, any opponent that provokes in your expanded reach also provokes against you. As part of your reaction in response to the opponent provocation, you may move up to your land speed to bring the target within your reach. This movement provokes as normal.
