# Increased Longevity

### Prerequisites

Longevity lineage trait.

### Benefits

Increase your age category limits by an additional 100 years. If these new limits result in your current age being in a younger age category, your age penalties are reverted to your new age category.

### Special

You can take this talent multiple times. Its effects stack.
