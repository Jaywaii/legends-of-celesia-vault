# Insightful Defense

### Prerequisites

Wisdom +1.

### Benefits

You may use your Wisdom modifier in place of your Dexterty as your defense modifier. If so, your defence modifier is still affected by anything that would limit or remove your Dexterity modifier to your DV, such as wearing armor or being off-guard. You may disable or enable Insightful Defense as a free action at the start of your turn, once per turn.
