# Mystic Tradition Training

### Benefits

Select a mystic tradition you do not possess. You become a 1st-rank mystic artist in that tradition. Select three casting styles belonging to that tradition. You have opened your root casting channels and can now cast root-realm mystic arts.

### Special

You can take this talent multiple times. Each time you take it, select a different tradition.
