# Enlightened Elusiveness

### Prerequisites

[[Qi Pool]].

### Benefits

As long as you have at least 1 point of qi, a Wisdom bonus of +1 or higher, and are not wearing armor, you can add your Wisdom bonus to your DV in addition to your defense attribute, and add your Wisdom save bonus to Dexterity saves.
