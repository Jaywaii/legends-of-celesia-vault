# Expert Proficiency

### Prerequisites

Standard proficiency with the selected armor, shield, skill, tool, or weapon.

### Benefits

Select an armor, shield, skill, tool, or weapon with which you have standard proficiency. You gain expert proficiency with it.

### Special

You can take this talent multiple times. Each time you take it, select a different armor, shield, skill, tool, or weapon with which you have standard proficiency.
