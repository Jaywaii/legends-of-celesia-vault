# Specialized Grip

### Prerequisites

Attack bonus +1, [[Expert Proficiency]] with the selected weapon.

### Benefits

Select a two-handed weapon with which you have expert proficiency with. You can wield that weapon with one hand with a -1 penalty to your attack rolls while retaining its circumstance bonus, and you no longer incur the +1 disadvantage to attack rolls for doing so. The weapon is no longer considered to be an improvised weapon when wielded with one hand.

### Normal

Wielding a two-handed weapon with one hand is treated as wielding an improvised weapon. It loses its circumstance bonus to its attack modifier, and you gain +1 disadvantage on your attack rolls when attacking in this way.

### Special

You can take this talent multiple times. Each time your take it, select a different two-handed weapon with which you have expert proficiency.
