# Stance Fusion

### Benefits

You can maintain one additional stance at the same time. If the additional stance is from a different school, you do not end your current stance. You are still limited to entering one stance per simple action.

### Normal

You can maintain only one stance at a time. Entering a stance of a different school ends your current stance.

### Special

You can take this talent multiple times. Its effects stack.
