# Defying Stillness

### Prerequisites

[[Acrobatics]] (master).

### Benefits

You may attempt an Acrobatics check as a reaction to suppress the effects of the slow condition by one step for every degree of success for 1 round at a -10 penalty. You may attempt to suppress the effects of the paralyzed condition for 1 round at a -20 penalty. The check DC is the same as the effect's save DC. This action counts are purely mental.
