# Standard Proficiency

### Prerequisites

Untrained proficiency with the selected armor, shield, skill, tool, or weapon.

### Benefits

Select an armor, shield, skill, tool, or weapon. You gain standard proficiency with it.

### Special

You can take this talent multiple times. Each time you take it, select a new armor, shield, skill, tool, or weapon with which you are untrained.
