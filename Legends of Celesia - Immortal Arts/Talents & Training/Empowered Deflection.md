# Empowered Deflection

*By hardening your body and enhancing your reflexes with qi, you are able deflecting supersonic or enormous projectiles.*

### Prerequisites

[[Deflecting Palm]], [[Legendary Proficiency]] (unarmed strike), [[Qi Pool]].

### Benefit

You can expend qi to use the Deflecting Palm talent against firearm and siege engine projectiles. See Table: Deflecting Palm Qi Cost to determine how much qi to use.

#### Table: Deflecting Palm Qi Cost

| Projectile | Qi Cost |
|---|---|
| Small Arm | 1 |
| Rifle/Slug | 2 |
| Pellets | 3 |
| Siege Bold | 5 |
| Siege Stone | 8 |
| Cannonball | 15 |

### Normal

You cannot use Deflecting Palm with firearm and siege engine projectiles.
