# Base Talents

# Standard

[[Attribute Save Increase]]: Increase an attribute save bonus by 1.

[[Dodge]]: Gain a +1 dodge bonus to your DV.

[[Fleet]]: Increase one of your speeds by 5 feet.

[[Martial Bonus Increase]]: Increase your martial bonus by 1.

[[Stamina Increase]]: Increase your stamina by 5 points.

[[Standard Proficiency]]: Gain standard proficiency with an armor, shield, skill, tool, or weapon.

# Expert

[[Art Specialization]]: Increase an art's capacity for refinements by 1.

[[Expert Proficiency]]: Gain expert proficiency with an armor, shield, skill, tool, or weapon.

[[Force Deflection]]: Use your Strength modifier as your defense modifier.

[[Insightful Defense]]: Use your Wisdom modifier as your defense modifier.

[[Stance Fusion]]: Become able to assume more than one stance at the same time.

# Master

[[Master Proficiency]]: Gain master proficiency in an armor, shield, skill, tool, or weapon.

# Legendary
