# Force Deflection

### Prerequisites

Strength +1.

### Benefits

You may use your Strength modifier in place of your Dexterity as your defense modifier. If so, your defence modifier is still affected by anything that would limit or remove your Dexterity modifier to your DV, such as wearing armor or being off-guard. You may disable or enable Force Deflection as a free action at the start of your turn, once per turn.
