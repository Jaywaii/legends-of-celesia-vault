# The Cultivator

The talents of the Way of the Internal Essence revolve around the usage of qi.

# Standard

[[Adamantine Strikes]]: Your unarmed strikes overcome adamantine damage reduction.

[[Cold Iron Strikes]]: Your unarmed strikes overcome cold iron damage reduction.

[[Qi Pool]]: Gain a qi pool.

[[Qi Strikes]]: Your unarmed strikes overcome magic damage reduction.

[[Silver Strikes]]: Your unarmed strikes overcome silver damage reduction.

[[Unlock Chakra]]: Unlock a chakra.

# Expert

[[Enlightened Elusiveness]]: Add your Wisdom bonus to your DV as long as you have qi.
