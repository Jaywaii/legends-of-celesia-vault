# Beast Handling

### Benefits

You are able to use the @[Diplomacy](article:763fd413-dcc1-40c5-8491-1bf2e69e534fy skill on creatures with an Intelligence modifier of -4 or lower.

When a creature if that low Intelligence has a minimum relationship of Friend, attitude of Friendly, and a trust of Confident, then the subject can be adopted as a pet.

### Normal

You can only use the Diplomacy skill on creatures with an Intelligence modifier of -3 or higher.
