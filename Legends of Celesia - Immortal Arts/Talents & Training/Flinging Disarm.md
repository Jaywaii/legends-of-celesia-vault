# Flinging Disarm

# Prerequisites

Acrobatics (expert).

# Benefits

On a successful disarm, you fling the opponent's weapon 5 feet away in a direction of your choice. You can have it flung an additional 5 feet in the same direction for every degree of success. If you have a free hand and you fling the weapon towards you, you may catch it.

At master proficiency in Acrobatics, the distance increases to 10 feet on the initial fling, and 10 feet per successful degree.
