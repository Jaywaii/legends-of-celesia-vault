# Engine Master

### Prerequisites

[[Craft]] (expert)

### Benefits

The secrets to crafting the giant, mechanical devices known as engines come naturally to you, as well as how to pilot them. Multiply the result of your Craft checks by 10 to determine your progress when crafting engines.
