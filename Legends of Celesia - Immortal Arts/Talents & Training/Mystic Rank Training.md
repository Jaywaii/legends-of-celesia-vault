# Mystic Rank Training

### Prerequisites

[[Mystic Tradition Training|Mystic Tradition]] for the selected tradition.

### Benefits

Select a mystic tradition that you are trained in. You increase your rank in that tradition by 1, up to your level.

### Special

You can take this talent multiple times. Its effects stack.
