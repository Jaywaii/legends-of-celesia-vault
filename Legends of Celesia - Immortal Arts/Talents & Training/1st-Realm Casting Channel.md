# 1st-Realm Casting Channel

### Prerequisites

[[Mystic Tradition Training|Mystic Tradition]] with the selected tradition, CM +1.

### Benefits

Select a mystic tradition. You gain one 1st-realm casting channel. At 3rd rank of your mystic tradition, and every 3 ranks after, you gain one additional 1st-realm casting channel.

### Special

You can take this talent multiple times. Each time you take it, select a different tradition.
