# 8th-Realm Casting Channel

### Prerequisites

15th rank in the selected tradition, [[7th-Realm Casting Channel]] with the selected tradition, CM +8.

### Benefits

Select a mystic tradition. You gain one 8th-realm casting channel. At 17th rank of your mystic tradition, and every 3 rank after, you gain one additional 8th-realm casting channel.

### Special

You can take this talent multiple times. Each time you take it, select a different tradition.
