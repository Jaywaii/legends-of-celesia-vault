# Overcome Immunity

### Prerequisites

None.

### Benefits

Select one effect. When your attack, ability, or art inflicts that effect against a target that is immune to it, the target can now be affected by it. Any save the target needs to make against the effect gains a bonus equal to 10 + 1 per level it possesses above yours.
