# Legendary Proficiency

### Prerequisites

15th level; master proficiency with the selected armor, shield, skill, tool, or weapon.

### Benefits

Select an armor, shield, skill, tool, or weapon with which you have master proficiency. You gain legendary proficiency with it.

### Special

You can take this talent multiple times. Each time you take it, select a different armor, shield, skill, tool, or weapon with which you have master proficiency.
