# Improved Magic Affinity

### Prerequisites

Magic affinity lineage trait.

### Benefits

Select a magic affinity that you possess. Increase its refinement capacity bonus by 1.

### Special

You can take this talent multiple times. Each time you take it, you can either select a magic affinity already affected by this talent and stack their effects, or select a new magic affinity that you possess.
