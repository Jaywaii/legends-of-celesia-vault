# Deadly Fling

# Prerequisites

Acrobatics (master), [[Flinging Disarm]].

# Benefits

On a successful disarm, you may spend a reaction to make a ranged attack with the flung weapon against a target within range of your fling (including the weapon's original wielder). Use your disarm result as your attack roll. The weapon has a range increment of 10 feet.
