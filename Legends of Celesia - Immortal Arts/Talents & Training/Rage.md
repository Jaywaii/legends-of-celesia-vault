# Rage

### Prerequisites

Constitution +1.

### Benefits

You enter into a state of rage for a number of rounds equal to your level + your Constitution modifier. While in this state, you gain a +1 advantage bonus to attack rolls, Strength- and Constitution-based checks, saves, and skills, and saves against mind-affecting effects. You also regain a number of stamina points equal to your level at the start of your turn. However, you are unable to use Intelligence- and Wisdom- based skills, and attacks against you gain +1 advantage.

At the end of your rage, your stamina is reduced to 0.

For every 5 levels you possess, you gain a +1 circumstance bonus to attack rolls, Strength- and Constitution-based checks, saves, and skills, and saves against mind-affecting effects. You also regain an additional 5 stamina points at the start of your turn.
