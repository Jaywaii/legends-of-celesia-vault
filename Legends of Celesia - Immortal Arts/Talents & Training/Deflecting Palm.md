# Deflecting Palm

*With rapid reflexes, you push away projectile coming towards you.*

### Prerequisites

Dexterity +1, Wisdom +1, [[Expert Proficiency]] (unarmed strike).

### Benefit

You can spend a reaction to deflect a ranged attack that would hit you. You can perform this talent any number of times as long as you have reactions available, but each time you do so beyond the first costs 1 stamina. You must have at least one free appendage that can hold an item to use this talent. You cannot use this talent when you are off-guarded. You cannot deflect a ranged attack from a spell or fired from a firearm or a siege engine.
