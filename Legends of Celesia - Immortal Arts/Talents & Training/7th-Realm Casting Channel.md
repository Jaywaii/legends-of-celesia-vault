# 7th-Realm Casting Channel

### Prerequisites

13th rank in the selected tradition, [[6th-Realm Casting Channel]] with the selected tradition, CM +7.

### Benefits

Select a mystic tradition. You gain one 7th-realm casting channel. At 15th rank of your mystic tradition, and every 3 ranks after, you gain one additional 7th-realm casting channel.

### Special

You can take this talent multiple times. Each time you take it, select a different tradition.
