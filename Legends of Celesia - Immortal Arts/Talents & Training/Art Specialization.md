# Art Specialization

### Prerequisites[/h]

At least one art known.

### Benefits

Select an art that you know. Increase that art's capacity for refinements by 1 without having to increase its minimum form/realm.

### Special

You can take this talent multiple times. Each time you take it, you can select a new art or an art already selected by this talent. If you pick the same art, the effects stack.
