# Shareable Pills

### Prerequisites

[[Mystic Tradition Training]] (alchemy).

### Benefits

You are able to give one alchemical pill you have prepared to another creature who can then use it one themselves or against valid targets. You are still considered to be its caster to calculate its effects.

Once your alchemical pill leaves your possession, it remains active for a number of rounds equal to your cultivation modifier. If the pill returns to your possession before it expires, its active duration resets if you pass it on again.

Passing an alchemical pill to another creature is a simple provoke action.
