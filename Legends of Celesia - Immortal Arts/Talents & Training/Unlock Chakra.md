# Unlock Chakra

### Prerequisites

[[Qi Pool]].

### Benefits

Select a chakra slot that is locked for you: Brow, Feet, Groin, Hands, Heart, Lungs, Stomach, Throat, or Wrists. You have unlocked that chakra.

### Special

You can take this talent multiple times. Each time you take it, select a locked chakra.
