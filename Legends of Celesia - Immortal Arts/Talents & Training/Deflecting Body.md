# Deflecting Body

*Your martial training allows you to deflect oncoming projectiled with kicks, elbows, and even your head.*

### Prerequisites

[[Deflecting Palm]].

### Benefit

You do not need to have any free appendages to use the Deflecting Palm talent.

### Normal

You need to have at least one free appendage to use the Deflecting Palm talent.
