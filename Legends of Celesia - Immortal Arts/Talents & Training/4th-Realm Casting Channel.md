# 4th-Realm Casting Channel

### Prerequisites

7th rank in the selected tradition, [[3rd-Realm Casting Channel]] with the selected tradition, CM +4.

### Benefits

Select a mystic tradition. You gain one 4th-realm casting channel. At 9th rank of your mystic tradition, and every 3 ranks after, you gain one additional 4th-realm casting channel.

### Special

You can take this talent multiple times. Each time you take it, select a different tradition.
