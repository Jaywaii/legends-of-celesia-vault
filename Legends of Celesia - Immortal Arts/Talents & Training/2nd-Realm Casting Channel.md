# 2nd-Realm Casting Channel

### Prerequisites

3rd rank in the selected tradition, [[1st-Realm Casting Channel]] with the selected tradition, CM +2.

### Benefits

Select a mystic tradition. You gain one 2nd-realm casting channel. At 5th rank of your mystic tradition, and every 3 ranks after, you gain one additional 2nd-realm casting channel.

### Special

You can take this talent multiple times. Each time you take it, select a different tradition.
