# 5th-Realm Casting Channel

### Prerequisites

9th rank in the selected tradition, [[4th-Realm Casting Channel]] with the selected tradition, CM +5.

### Benefits

Select a mystic tradition. You gain one 5th-realm casting channel. At 11th rank of your mystic tradition, and every 3 ranks after, you gain one additional 5th-realm casting channel.

### Special

You can take this talent multiple times. Each time you take it, select a different tradition.
