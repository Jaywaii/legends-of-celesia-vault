# Improved Energy Resistance

### Prerequisites

lineage trait.

### Benefits

Select one energy resistance trait you possess. You ignore one additional injury of that energy type.

### Special

You can take this talent multiple times. Each time you take it, you can improve a different energy resistance or a previously selected one. Its effects stack.
