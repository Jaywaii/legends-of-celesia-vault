# Imperious Challenge

### Prerequisites

Attack bonus +1.

### Benefits

As a simple action, you can issue an imperious challenge against one opponent within line of sight. The target gains +1 advantage on all attack rolls against you, but incurs +1 disadvantage on all attacks aginst other targets. The number of disadvantages against other targets increase by 1 for every +4 attack bonus that you possess.  The effects of the imperious challenge persists until either you or the target is rendered unconscious or killed, you challenge a different target, or the combat encounter ends.

Multiple challenges against the same target do not stack. Only the challenge from the source with the highest attack bonus applies. If two or more sources have the same attack bonus, only the first challenge issued applies.

Mindless creatures cannot be targeted by this talent.
