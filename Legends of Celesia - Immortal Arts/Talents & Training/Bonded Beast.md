# Bonded Beast

### Prerequisites

[[Beast Handling]], one pet.

### Benefits

Upon selecting this talent, select one of your pets that has a level of no more than 3/4 of your current level. A special spiritual bond forms between the two of you. As it becomes your bonded beast. As you grow in power, so to does your bonded beast. For every 100 XP that you earn, your bonded beast gains 75 XP to spend on creature traits.

A pet remains ceases to be your bonded beast if you willingly release it from your service. It may also seek to sever the bond if it suffers extensive and severe mistreatment at your hands. If your bonded beast dies, your bond can remain if you bring it back to life as long as you do not release it beforehand.

### Special

You can take this talent multiple times. Each time you take it, select a different pet to become a bonded beast. The amount of XP gained by each of your bonded beasts is divided by the total number of bonded beasts that you possess (minimum 1 XP).
