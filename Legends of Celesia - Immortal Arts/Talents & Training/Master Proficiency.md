# Master Proficiency

### Prerequisites

8th level; expert proficiency with the selected armor, shield, skill, tool, or weapon.

### Benefits

Select an armor, shield, skill, tool, or weapon with which you have expert proficiency. You gain master proficiency with it.

### Special

You can take this talent multiple times. Each time you take it, select a different armor, shield, skill, tool, or weapon with which you have expert proficiency.
