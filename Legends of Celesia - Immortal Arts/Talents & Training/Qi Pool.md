# Qi Pool

### Prerequisites

Wisdom +1.

### Benefit

You gain a pool of qi points equal to your level + your Wisdom bonus. You can recover 1 qi as a standard action by spending 2 stamina. You recover all of your qi after a full rest.

### Special

You can take this talent multiple times. Each time you take it, increase the number of qi points you gain per level by 1.
