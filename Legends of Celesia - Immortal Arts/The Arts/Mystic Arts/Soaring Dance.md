# Soaring Dance

**School** force (stance)

**Action** 1 simple

You gain a fly speed of 5 ft./tier with perfect maneuverability.

# Refinements

## Quick Steps (+1)

You can assume this stance as a reaction, but only when falling.

## Zephyr's Aid (+1)

Increase your fly speed by 5 ft./tier.
