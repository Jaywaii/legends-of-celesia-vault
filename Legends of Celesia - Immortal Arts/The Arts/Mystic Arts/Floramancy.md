# Floramancy

# Counters

: Prevent yourself from being shoved or falling by rooting yourself into the ground like a tree.

# Sparks

: Gain plant immunities for 1 round.

# Spells

: Cause plantlife to grow and entangle targets in an area.

# Stances

: Adopt natural characteristics of plant creatures.

# Strikes

: Entangle a target with vines conjured from the impact from your attack.
