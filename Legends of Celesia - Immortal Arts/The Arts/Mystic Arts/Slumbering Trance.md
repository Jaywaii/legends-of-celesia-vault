# Slumbering Trance

**School** mentalism (spell) [mind-affecting]

**Action** 1 standard

**Range** medium (100 ft. + 10 ft./tier)

**Target** 1 creature

**Duration** rounds equal to your cultivation modifier + 1 round/tier

**Attribute Save**  Wisdom (partial), **Magic Resistance** yes

You force your target to fall into a mgaical sleep. Only direct physical contact as a standard provoke action can awaken a target asleep by this art. On a successful save, the target does not fall asleep and instead becomes dazed for 1 round.

The target's bodily needs and activities continue to function as nirmal while they are asleep from this art.

# Refinements

## Hibernation (+3)

Your art's duration changes to a number of days equal to your cultivation modifier+ 1 day per tier.

## Long Sleep (+1)

Your art's duration changes to a number of hours equal to your cultivation modifier + 1 hour per tier.

## Slumbering Chorus(+2)

Your art's target changes to 1 creature per tier in a burst area with a radius of 5 ft. + 5 ft. per 4 tiers.
