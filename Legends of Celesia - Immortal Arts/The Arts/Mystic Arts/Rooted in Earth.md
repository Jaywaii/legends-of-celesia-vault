# Rooted in Earth

**School** terramancy (boost) [earth]

**Action** 1 simple

**Range** personal

**Duration** 1 round

You strengthen your footing against the ground. Until the start of your next turn, opponents incur +1 disadvantage on shove and trip attempts against you, and you gain +1 advantage on Acrobatics checks and saves to avoid falling prone as long as you are standing on dirt or stone.

# Refinements

## Empowered (+1)

Increase the number of disadvantages your opponents incur and the number of advantages you gain each by 1.
