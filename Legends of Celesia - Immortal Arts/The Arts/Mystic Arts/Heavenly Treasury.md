# Heavenly Treasury

**School** warp (spell)

**Action** 1 greater

**Range** close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 tiers)

 **Target**  1 container that fits in a 5-ft. square.

**Duration** hours equal to your culitvation modifier + 1 hour/tier

You conjure a container that appears however you wish as long as it fits in a 5-ft. square. This container contains an extra dimensional space with a capacity of 100 lbs. times your cultivation modifier. You can store any item that can fit in the container's space. Retrieving an item from the *heavenly treasury* is a simple nonprovoke action as the desired item is always immediately the first item available. Any creature can store or retrieve items from your treasury.

Creatures can enter the treasury as long as they fit in its space and their total weight does not exceed its capacity. However, when the treasury is dismissed, there is only 10 minutes worth of air per 100 lbs. of capacity.

If you are killed, all items that were stored in your treasury are discharged at the site of your death.

# Refinements

## Expanding (+1)

Increase the treasury's capacity by 100 lbs.

## Selective (+1)

Instead of allowing your treasury to be accessed to all creatures, it is now inaccessible to all except for a number of creatures you designate up to your cultivatioj modifier.

## Widened (+1)

Increase the container's space by 1 size larger. You may choose to keep its height unchanged.
