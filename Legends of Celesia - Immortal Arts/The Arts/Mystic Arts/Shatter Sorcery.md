# Shatter Sorcery

**School** thaumaturgy (spell)

**Action** 1 standard

**Range** medium (100 ft. + 10 ft./tier)

**Target** 1 magical effect

**Duration** instant

This art shatters the magical energies that sustain ongoing magical effects affecting a creature or an object, or an ongoing magical area of effect. Only non-curse magical effects with a duration longer than instant can be shattered. Magical effects that originate from mythic arts and items cannot be shattered.

To shatter a magical effect, you need to make a dispel check (1d20 + your casting tier + your cultivation modifier). The DC is equal to 20 + the target magical effect's casting tier. If you succeed, the effect of your art depends on nature of the magical effect.

If the magical effect has a duration longer than instant and is not permanent, it ends immediately.

If the magical effect has an ongoing duration due to an item, or has a permanent duration, the effect is suppressed for a number of rounds equal to your cultivation modifier + your tier.

# Refinements

## Disjunction (+3)

You can target a magical item. When you succeed on your dispel check, you permanently disenchant a magical item into a mundane object, unless it is mythic.

## Empower (+1)

Gain a +2 bonus to your dispel check.

## Unravel Curse (+2)

You can use this art to dispel the targeted curse effect. Your dispel check is made against the curse's DC.
