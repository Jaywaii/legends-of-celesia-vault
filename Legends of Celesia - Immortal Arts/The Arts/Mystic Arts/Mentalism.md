# Mentalism

# Boosts

: Increase the DC of your next mentalism art.

: Gain an enhancement bonus to one mental attribute.

# Counters

: Make a tier check to make the opponent think they successfully hit, but in fact, did not.

: Gain a bonus on your save against a mind-affecting effect, critically saving on a regular save, and have a chance to reflect against its origin.

# Spells

[[Converse with the Unknown]]: Language becomes no barrier for you.

[[Collective of Minds]]: Create a mental link of two or more minds to allow telepathic communication between them.

[[Slumbering Trance]]: Make your targets fall asleep.

# Stances

: Gain telepathy.

# Strikes

: Inflict confusion on a successful strike.
