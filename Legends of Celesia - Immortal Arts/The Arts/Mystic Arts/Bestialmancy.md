# Bestialmancy

# Boosts

: Gain a boost of beastly strength, dexterity, or constitution.

: Gain animal senses (scent, keen senses, nightvision).

: Grow animal hide for natural armor.

# Counters

: Evade an attack with bestial instincts.

# Spells

[[Beast Tongue]]: Communicate with beasts.

: Transform targets into the form of a beast.

: Empower a beast to be more powerful.

# Stances

: Gain a spiritual connection with beasts.

: Grow beastly natural weapons.

# Strikes

: Deliver poison or disease in your attacks.

: Grow fangs, claws, or horns to make natural attacks when unarmed.
