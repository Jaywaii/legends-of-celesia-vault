# Fungalmancy

# Counters

: Instantly grow a giant mushroom to take an oncoming attack.

: Assume fungus immunities for one oncoming effect.

# Sparks

: Gain fungus immunities for 1 round.

# Spells

: Cause fungus to grow and expel hazardous spores in an area.

# Stances

: Adopt natural characteristics of fungal creatures.

# Strikes

: Rot flesh with attacks.
