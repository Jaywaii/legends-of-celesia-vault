# Fireball

**School** pyromancy (strike) [fire]

**Action** 1 standard

**Range** close (25 ft. + 2 ft./rank)

You shoot a tiny projectile of magical fire that flies under your control to a target within line of sight and within range. Make a ranged attack roll. Your strike inflicts fire injuries.

# Refinements

## Burst (+1)

Upon impact, your strike explodes in a burst with a radius of 5 ft. + 5 ft. per 5 ranks. Targets caught in the burst receive the same number of fire injuries as the original target, but may make a Dexterity save to halve the amount.

## Empowered (+1)

Increase the number of fire injuries received by all targets by 1.

## Long Range (+2)

Increase the art's range to long (400 ft. + 40 ft./rank).

## Medium Range (+1)

Increase the art's range to medium (100 ft. + 10 ft./rank).

## Selective Burst (+2)

As the *burst* refinement, but you may choose omit a number of targets equal to your cultivation modifier in your strike's area of effect from receiving fire injuries.

## Shockwave (+1)

All targets who receive fire injuries from your strike are knocked prone unless they make a Dexterity save.
