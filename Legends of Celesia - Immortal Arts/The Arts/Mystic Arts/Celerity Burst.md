# Celerity Burst

**School** time (spark)

You gain a +5 ft. bonus to all forms of movement. Every realm attained by this art increases this bonus by 5 additional feet.

# Refinements

## Surge (+2)

Gain an additional beat this turn.

## Swift Nimbleness (+2)

You gain a +1 bonus to your Dexterity modifier.
