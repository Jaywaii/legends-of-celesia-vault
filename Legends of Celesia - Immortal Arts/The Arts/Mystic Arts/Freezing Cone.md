# Freezing Cone

**School** aquamancy (spell) [cold, water]

**Action** 1 standard

**Area** 10-ft. cone

**Duration** instant

**Save** Dexterity

You shoot a 10-ft. cone of freezing temperatures, inflicting a number of cold injuries equal to your cultivation modifier on all creatures in the area. A successful save halves the number of injuries.

# Refinements

## Empowered (+1)

Increase the number of cold injuries inflicted by 1.

## Selective (+2)

You may choose omit a number of targets equal to your cultivation modifier in your art's area of effect from receiving cold injuries.

## Widened (+1)

Increase your art's area by 10 ft.
