# Broker Beyond the Stars

**School** conjuration (spell)

**Action** 1 greater action

**Range** close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 tiers)

**Target** 1 specific outsider

**Duration** 1 hour

You summon an outsider that is perfectly tailored to your personality and needs whose sole purpose is to acquire and sell you items. This otherworldly broker can sell you any non-magical adventuring equipment (weapons, armor/shield, consumables) whose value does not exceed 10 gp per tier, any magical consumable (elixirs, scrolls, tokens) whose value does not exceed 5 gp per tier, and has a percentage chance equal to your tier to sell any high-value item whose value does not exceed 1,000 gp per tier.

If the otherworldy broker does not have the desired item in stock, you can make a request for it. The broker will take 1d6 days per 1,000 gp of the item's value to find it. You will receive a mental notification when they have added the item to their stock. Only one request can be made per summon.

The broker will not appear in dangerous situations, and will vanish immediately if attacked.

# Refinements

## Blessed Bazaar (+1)

Increase the value limit of the broker's non-magical adventuring equipment by 10 gp per tier, magical consumables by 5 gp per tier, and high-value items by 1,000 gp per tier.

## Merchant's Insight (+1)

Increase the chance for the broker to have a high-value item by 1% per tier (to a maximum of 95%).

## Swift Trader (+1)

Reduce the amount of time needed for the broker to find a specific item by 1d6 days per tier (to a minimum of 1 day).
