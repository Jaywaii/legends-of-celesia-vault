# Stolen Moment Rebuke

**School** time (counter)

**Target** self

**Duration** instant

When you are the target of an attack or must make a save against an effect, you hasten your personal flow of time to immediately perform 1 simple action. You have one less beat on your next turn.

# Refinements

## Expanded Burst (+2)

Your immediate action expands to instead be a standard action.

## Greater Expanded Burst (+4)

Your immediate action expands to instead be a greater action.

## Greater Twin Expanded Burst (+8)

You can perform a standard action in addition to your immediate action. This action cannot be combined with your initial immediate action.

## Superior Twin Expanded Burst (+10)

You can perform a greater action in addition to your immediate action. This action cannot be combined with your initial immediate action.

## Twin Expanded Burst (+6)

You can perform a simple action in addition to your immediate action. This action cannot be combined with your initial immediate action.
