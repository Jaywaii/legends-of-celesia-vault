# Lethargic Strike

**School** necromancy (strike)

**Action** 1 standard

**Range** melee

**Target** 1 creature

**Duration** instant

**Attribute Save** Constitution (partial), **Magic Resistance** yes

On a successfull attack, reduce the target's stamina by 1d6 + your cultivation modifier + your tier. The target takes half of the stamina loss on a successful save.

# Refinements

## Heavy Blow (+1)

Increase the stamina loss by 1d6.

## Vampiric (+2)

You absorb half of the stamina loss by your target, up to your maximum stamina.
