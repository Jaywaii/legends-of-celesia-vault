# Transplanar Summoning

**School** conjuration (spell)

**Action** 1 greater

**Range** close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 rank)

**Target** one creature with the outsider type (without the native subtype) or with the extraplanar subtype

**Duration** rounds equal to your cultivation modifier + 1 round/rank

This spell summons an temporarily real echo of the target creature that you know. The target's (TODO: "total build points") cannot exceed 1/2 of your rank x 10. It appears where you designate and may act immediately. Your summon only acts when you use the Command Companion action. Your summon may still use reactions outside of your turn. You choose which kind of creature to summon, and you can choose a different one each time you cast the spell.

As an echo, summoned creatures are identical to the target creature, with the same personality, memories, and abilities with some exceptions. The echo cannot summon or otherwise conjure another creature, nor can it use any teleportation or planar travel abilities. Creatures cannot be summoned into an environment that cannot support them. Creatures summoned using this spell cannot use arts that duplicate arts with expensive material components.

# Refinements

## Ascension (+1)

Increase the maximum (TODO: "total build points") of your summon by 5. The total cannot exceed your rank x 10.

## Mirroring (+2)

You can summon an additional echo.
