# Embrace Greatness

**School** inspiration (spell)

**Action** 1 standard

**Range** personal

**Area** close burst (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 tiers)

**Target** number of allies equal to your cultivation modifier + 1 ally/tier with line of sight of you

**Duration** number of rounds equal to your cultivation modifer + 1 round/tier

**Save** Charisma (harmless)

Upon casting this art, choose one of the following effects to grant to your allies:

- +1 morale bonus to attack rolls,
- +1 morale bonus to all skill checks,
- +1 morale bonus to DV.

Allies affected by this art retain its effects for its duration even after they lose line of sight of you after you performed it.

# Refinements

## Empowered (+1)

Increase your art's morale bonus by 1.

## Far-Reaching (+2)

Your art's area changes to a long burst (400 ft. + 40 ft./tier).

## Persistent (+2)

Your art's duration changes to a nimber of hours equal to your cultivation modifier + 1 hour/tier. You no longer need to concentrate on this art after a number of rounds equal to the art's realm.

## Salvation (+1)

You can choose to grant a +1 morale bonus to all attribute saves as one of the choices of your art.

## Versatile (+1)

You can choose 1 additional effect to affect your allies.

## Widened (+1)

Your art's area changes to a medium burst (100 ft. + 10 ft./tier).
