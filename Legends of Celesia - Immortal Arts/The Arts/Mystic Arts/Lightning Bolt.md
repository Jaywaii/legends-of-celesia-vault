# Lightning Bolt

**School** pyromancy (spell) [electrical]

**Action** 1 standard

**Area** 30-ft. line

**Duration** instant

**Save** Dexterity

You shoot a bolt of lightning in a straight line, inflicting a number of electric injuries equal to your cultivation modifier on all creatures in its path. A successful save halves the number of injuries received.

# Refinements

## Chained (+1)

Instead of a straight line, your *lightning bolt* flies from creature to creature. The bolt cannot travel a number feet beyond the length of its original area. You choose the next creature for the bolt to target after striking the current one.

## Empowered (+1)

Increase the number of injuries inflicted by 1.

## Lengthen (+1)

Increase the length of your art's line by 30 ft.
