# Beast Tongue

**School** beast (spell)

**Action** 1 standard

**Range** touch

**Target** 1 beast with an Intelligence modifier of -3 or lower

**Duration** hours equal to your cultivation modifier + 1 hour/tier

**Save** Charisma

You are able to clearly communicate and understand of the touched beast as if it was as intelligent as you. However, the creature is no more friendlier towards you as before the spell came into effect.

# Refinements

## Affable (+1)

You gain +1 advantage on Diplomacy checks against the target.

## Deceptive (+1)

You gain +1 advantage on Bluff checks against the target.

## Flocked (+2)

Your art's range changes to close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 tiers) and its target changes to 1 beast with an Intelligence modifier of -3 or lower per tier.

## Insightful (+1)

You gain +1 advantage on Insight checks against the target.

## Intimidating (+1)

You gain +1 advantage on Intimidation against with the target.
