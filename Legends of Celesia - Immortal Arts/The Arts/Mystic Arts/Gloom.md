# Gloom

**School** illusion (spell) [, shadow]

**Action** 1 standard

**Range** touch

**Target** object touched no larger than a 5 ft.-cube

**Duration** hours equal to your cultivation modifier + 1 hour/tier

The object you touch decreases the light level in a 10 ft.-radius by one step, down to darkness (bright light to normal light, normal light to dim light, dim light to darkness). Creatures with darkvision can see in the art's area of dim light or darkness without penalty.

*Gloom* can be countered by *[[Radiance]]*

# Refinements

## Expanded (+1)

Increase *gloom*'s darkness radius by 10 ft.

## Obscurity (+2)

Dim light and darkness caused by your art affects darkvision as if it was normal sight.
