# Divining Eye

**School** divination (spell) [scrying]

**Action** 1 hour

**Range** long (400 ft. + 40 ft./tier)

**Effect** magical sensor

**Duration** 1 round/tier

**Save** see text

This art allows you to create an invisible sensor within range that allows you to see through. You may choose to target a creature or a location as the art's subject. If you choose a location to scry, your sensor does not move, but you can see everything in a 30 ft. radius as if you were there yourself.

If the subject is a creature, they can make a Wisdom save. If they succeed, the art fails and the target knows they were the subject of someone's scrying, but not their identity. The difficulty of the save depends on how much you know about your target, and what sort of relationship you have with them.
