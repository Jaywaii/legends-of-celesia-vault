# Divination

# Boosts

: A sudden burst of precognition grants you +1 advantage on your next attack.

# Counters

: Predict an incoming attack, gaining +1 advantage on saves or inflict +1 disadvantage on an incoming attack.

# Spells

: See and hear on a creature across far distances.

# Stances

: With heightened precognition, you can spend a reaction to force a +1 disadvantage on an attack against you.

# Strikes

: On a successful attack, the target becomes afflicted with false divination, inflicting +1 disadvantage on all attacks.
