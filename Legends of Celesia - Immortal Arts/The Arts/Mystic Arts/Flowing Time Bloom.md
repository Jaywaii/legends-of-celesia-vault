# Flowing Time Bloom

**School** time (stance)

While in this stance, you gain a +5 ft. bonus to all forms of movement that you possess. Every realm attained by this art increases this bonus by 5 additional feet.

# Refinements

## Surge (+2)

You gain 1 additional beat during your turn.

## Swift Nimbleness (+2)

You gain a +1 bonus to your Dexterity modifier while in this stance.
