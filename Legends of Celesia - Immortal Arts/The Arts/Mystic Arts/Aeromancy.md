# Aeromancy

# Sparks

: Burst of movement aided by air.

: Increase the range of your ranged attacks with air.

: Temporarily transform into a gaseous form.

# Counters

: Conjure a wall of wind to deflect projectiles.

: Emit a blast of air to shove yourself out of the way of an attack.

# Spells

: Bestow upon targets the ability to generate and breathe their own air.

[[Tempest Invocation]]: Conjure air and manipulate it.

# Stances

: Wrap your projectiles with wind under your control.

: Manipulate the air around you to fly.

# Strikes

: Deliver sonic damage with attacks.

: Send your foes twirling by wrapping them in a whirlwinf on a successful hit.

: Slice foes outside your melee reach with arcs of concentrated thin air.

: Send foes tumbling by delivering hurricane winds with your swings.

: Use wind to make a incredibly fast and distant powerful ranged attack.
