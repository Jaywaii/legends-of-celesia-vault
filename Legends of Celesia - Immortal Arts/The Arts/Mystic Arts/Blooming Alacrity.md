# Blooming Alacrity

**School** time (spell)

**Action** 1 standard

**Range** touch

**Target** 1 creature/rank

**Duration** rounds equal to your cultivation modifier + 1 round/rank

**Save** Charisma (harmless)

For the target, time slows down, while for everyone observing him, the target appears to be moving and reacting with accelerated speed. The target gains a +5 ft. bonus to all forms of movement. Every realm attained by this art increases this bonus increases by 5 additional feet.

# Refinements

## Short Burst (+1)

Your art's range changes to personal, and its area changes to a short burst (25 ft. + 5 ft./rank).

## Surge (+2)

The target gains 1 additional beat during their turn.

## Swift Nimbleness (+1)

Your art grants a +1 bonus to Dexterity.
