# Collective of Minds

**School** mentalism (spell) [mind-affecting]

**Target** number of creatures equal to your cultivation modifier + one creature per 2 tiers

**Range** medium (100 ft. + 10 ft./tier)

**Duration**  hours equal to your cultivation modifier + 1 hour/tier (D)

**Attribute Save** Charisma (harmless), **Magic Resistance** yes

You form a mental link between yourself and your targets within range and line of sight, forming a collective.  All members of the collective may communicate telepathically with each other as long as they are in range of you; they still need to speak or understand the spoken language.

A member leaves your collective when they become out of your range, when they are killed, or when they enter an antimagic area. As long as you maintain your collective, you can add new members to it up to your limit of targets as a standard action. The target must be within your line of sight in order to add them to your collective.

# Refinements

## Beyond Infinity (+5)

The range of your collective is unlimited across all planes. You must still have line of sight of a target in order to add her to your collective.

## Collective Awareness (+1)

You are aware of the direction and distance of any member of the collective in relation to you without needing line of sight. You are also aware of of their current stamina, and any injuries and conditions they currently have.

## Conduit of Power (+1)

As a provoking simple action, you can manifest a phantom echo of yourself upon a member of your collective, allowing you to perform actions from her location.

## Extension of Self (+1)

You can cast any art with a range of personal on one willing member of your collective.

## Infinity (+3)

The range of your collective is unlimited on your current plane. You must still have line of sight of a target in order to add her to your collective.

## Reach Through Minds (+1)

You can cast arts that target willing creatures with a range greater than personal on willing members in your collective, regardless of the art's original range.
