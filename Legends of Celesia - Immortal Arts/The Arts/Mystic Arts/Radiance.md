# Radiance

**School** illusion (spell) [light, shadow]

**Action** 1 standard

**Range** touch

**Target** object touched no larger than a 5 ft.-cube

**Duration** hours equal to your cultivation modifier + 1 hour/tier

The object you touch increases the light level in a 10 ft.-radius by one step, up to normal light (darkness to dim light, dim light to normal light).

*Radiance* can be countered by *[[Gloom]]*

# Refinements

## Brightness (+1)

*Radiance*'s maximum light level is increased to bright light. Creatures that take penalties from bright light are affected by the bright light from *radiance*.

## Expanded (+1)

Increase *radiance*'s light radius by 10 ft.

## Sun-Kissed (+2)

*Radiance*'s maximum light level is increased to bright light. Creatures that take penalties from bright light, or are damaged or destroyed by sunlight, are affected by the bright light from *radiance*.
