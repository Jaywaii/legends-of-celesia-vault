# Acid Spray

**School** terramancy (spell) [acid]

**Action** 1 standard

**Area** 10-ft. cone

**Duration** instant

**Save** Dexterity

You spray a 10-ft. cone of acid from you, inflicting a number of acid injuries equal to your cultivation modifier on all creatures and objects in the area. A successful save halves the number of injuries.

# Refinements

## Empowered (+1)

Increase the number of acid injuries by 1.

## Selective (+2)

You may choose omit a number of targets equal to your cultivation modifier in your art's area of effect from receiving acid injuries.

## Widened (+1)

Increase the cone's area by 10 ft.
