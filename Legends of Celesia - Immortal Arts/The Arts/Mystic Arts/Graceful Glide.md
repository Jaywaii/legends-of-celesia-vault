# Graceful Glide

**School** force (spell)

**Action** 1 standard

**Range** touch

**Target** 1 creature

**Duration** rounds equal to your cultivation modifier + 1 round/tier

**Attribute Save** Wisdom (harmless), **Magic Resistance** yes

Your target now falls at a slow enough pace to land safely.

# Refinements

## Ascending Step (+1)

Your target can now float up or down with a fly speed of 5 ft./tier.

## Far-Flung (+1)

Increase the range of your art to close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 tiers).

## Flying Formation (+1)

Your art can now target  up to 1 creature/tier.

## Soaring Flight (+2)

Your target can with perfect maneuverability with a fly speed equal to 5 ft./tier.

## Reactive Flight (+1)

You can cast this art as a reaction, but only on a target that is falling.

## Zephyr's Aid (+1)

Increase the fly speed granted by this art by 5 ft./tier.
