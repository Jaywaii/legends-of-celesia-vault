# Life Manipulation

**School** necromancy (spell)

**Action** 1 standard

**Range** touch

**Target** one living creature

**Duration** instant

**Attribute Save** Constitution (harmless), **Magic Resistance** yes

Make a DC 10 Heal check, and add your rank as an enhancement bonus to the result. On success, you heal one injury degree of a non-destroyed body part. For every 10 that you best the DC, you heal one additional injury degree.

# Refinements

## Healer's Reach (+2)

Your art's range changes from touch to close.

## Group Healing (+5)

Your art's range changes from touch to a close-range burst centered on you. Within that range, you can target a number of creatures up to your cultivation modifier + 1 creature/rank to heal. You choose how to spread your art's injury degrees among your targets.

## Persistent (+2)

The healing magic of your art persists in your targets. Your art's duration changes to a number of rounds equal to your cultivation modifier + 1 round/rank. Starting on the next round after you healed your target, your target chooses at the start of their turn a body part to heal to the next lesser injury level for the duration of the art.

## Regenerate (+3)

Your art can now heal a destroyed body part to crippled injured status by spending an injury degree. The target must still be alive to benefit from this art.

## Resurrection (+4)

Your art can target a dead creature and regenerate the minimum number of body parts to keep the target alive. The target cannot be dead longer than a number of rounds equal to your rank + your casting modifier.

In exchange, you and the target are each subject to a geas by the powers that oversee the transition between life and death or interceded on your behalf. The geas prevents you from training 100 XP per level of the target resurrected. The geas also acts as an aspect that the GM imposes on you. When you abide by the geas, you do not initially gain karma points as you would with normal aspects. Instead, each point you would have gained allows you to train 100 XP until you reach the XP amount blocked by your geas. After you reach that amount, you gain karma points as normal for abiding the geas. You can ignore the geas' compulsion as normal, but each time you do, choose  either to increase the amount of XP blocked from training by 400 or take a -1 penalty to your current highest attribute modifier. Each time you would gain a karma point from abiding the geas' compulsion, you can choose to remove a -1 penalty from this geas instead of training 100 XP.

The GM chooses the details of the geas, and it should be tied to the nature and goals of the powers involved in the resurrection.
