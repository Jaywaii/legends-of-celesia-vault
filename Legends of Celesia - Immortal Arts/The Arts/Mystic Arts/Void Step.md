# Void Step

**School** conjuration (spell) [teleport]

**Action** 1 standard

**Range** personal

**Duration** instant

**Save** Charisma

Teleport yourself to a location within close range (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 ranks). You can carry up to your heavy load.

# Refinements

## 1st Expansion (+1)

You can now *void step* to a location within medium range (100 ft. + 10 ft./rank).

## 2nd Expansion (+2)

You can now *void step* to a location within long range (400 ft. + 40 ft./rank).

## 3rd Expansion (+3)

You can now *void step* to a location within 1 mile/rank.

## 4th Expansion (+4)

You can now *void step* to a location within 10 miles/rank.

## 5th Expansion (+5)

You can now *void step* to a location within 100 miles/rank.

## 6th Expansion (+6)

You can now *void step* to a location within 1,000 miles/rank.

## 7th Expansion (+7)

You can now *void step* to a location within 1 mile/rank.

## 8th Expansion (+8)

You can now *void step* to a location on another planet seen in the sky.

## Collective (+1)

Bring one other willing creature per 2 ranks  with you as long as they are either touching you or part of a group of creatures connect by touch that includes you. Creatures larger than you take up 1 additional creature space in the collective for each size larger, while smaller creatures free up 1 space for each size smaller. Each additional creature can carry up to their heavy load.

## Forced (+1)

You can force an unwilling creature to teleport with you. You need to be in physical contact with them. On a successful save, the creature remains in their original lovation, but is stunned for 1 round.

## Heavier Loads (+1)

You can *void step* along with objects beyond weighing beyond your heavy load, up to 10 lbs./rank. You do not need to be carrying the items, but you need to be in physical contact with them. Objects in a container can be teleported with you as long as you touch the container.
