# Reflexive Barrier

**School** force (counter)

**Action** 1 reaction

**Target** personal

**Duration** instant

When an attack target's you, you reflexively conjure a shield of force to intercept it. Make a Craft check, using the attack's roll as the DC. On success, your barrier is strong enough to block the attack, negating it.

# Refinements

## Bulwark (+2)

Your *reflexive barrier* can now be performed in response to an effect that requires you to make a save. Make your Craft check against the effect's DC. On success, the effect is negated.

## Expanding (+1)

When you perform this art, it also protects additional creatures equal to your cultivation modifier who are adjacent to you or adjacent to another creature also protected by this art. Creatures larger than you take up one additional creature slot for each size larger, while smaller creatures free up one addition slot for each size smaller.

## Reinforced (+1)

Your Craft check made as part of this art gains a +2 bonus.

## Supporting (+1)

When an attack targets an ally within close range (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 tiers), you can now perform this art on that ally to benefit them.
