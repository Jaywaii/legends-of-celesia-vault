# The Arts

- [[Martial Arts]]
- [[Mystic Arts]]

# Art Types

### Counters

Counters are performed as reactions.

### Sparks

Sparks are performed as simple actions, and only apply affects to yourself and their effects generally last until the start of your next turn.

### Strikes

Strikes require an attack roll in order for the art to affect the target.

### Stances

The effects of a stance persists as long as you are in it. Assuming and changing your stance is a simple action. You remain in a stance until you choose to change it or you are taken out of it and all its effects immediately end when:

- you enter into a stance of a different school
- you are killed
- you have at least frightened 3
- you are prone
- you are resting
- you are running
- you are unconscious
- you are helpless

# Using Arts

To use an art of a particular school, you must be in a stance belonging to that school.

## Saves

If an art or its refinements require an attribute save from the target, the target only needs to make on save per attribute.

## Refinements

Unless otherwise stated, you can apply a refinement multiple times and their effects stack if applicable.
