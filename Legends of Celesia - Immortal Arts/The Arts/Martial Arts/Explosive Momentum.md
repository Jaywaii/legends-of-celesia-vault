# Explosive Momentum

**School** thundering bull (boost)

**Action** 1 simple

**Target** personal

**Duration** instant

After performing this boost, until the end of your turn, your next charge inflicts 1 additional injury.

# Refinements

## Empowered (+1)

Increase the number of additinal injuries by 1.
