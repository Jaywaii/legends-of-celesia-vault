# Flurry of Death

**School** empty hand (strike)

**Action** 1 standard

**Target** 1 creature

**Range** melee

**Save** Dexterity (half)

You deliver a rapid series of unarmed strikes against a foe. You inflict a number of injuries equal to 1 + 1/2 of your martial bonus against your target. The target makes a Dexterity save to halve the number of injuries.

# Refinements

## Extra Strike (+1)

Increase the number of injuries of your flurry by 1.

## Spreading the Pain (+1)

Select an additional target within range. Divide the number of injuries among your targets as you wish.
