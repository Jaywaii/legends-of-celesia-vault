# Iron Skin Stance

This stance allows you add both your Strength and your Dexterity modifiers together as your defence modifier to DV.

**Iron Shrug:** As a reaction, you can reduce the number of incoming injuries from one attack or effect by 1. You are immediately taken out of your stance after using this reaction.

# Refinements

## Hardening (+1)

The number of injuries reduced by your iron shrug increases by 1.

## Perseverance (+1)

The number of times you can use your iron shrug before leaving out of your stance increases by 1.
