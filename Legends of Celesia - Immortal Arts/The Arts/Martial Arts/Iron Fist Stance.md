# Iron Fist Stance

Upon entering this stance, and at the start of your turn while in this stance, you may change the type of damage of your unarmed strikes to either bludgeoning, piercing, or slashing as a free action.

**Iron Strike:** While in this stance, you may increase the number of injuries inflicted by one unarmed strike by 1. Doing so immediately takes you out of your stance.

# Refinements

## Deadly Fist (+1)

Increase the number of injuries inflicted by your iron strike by an additional 1.

## Iron Flurry (+1)

Increase the number of times you can execute your iron strike before leaving your stance by 1.
