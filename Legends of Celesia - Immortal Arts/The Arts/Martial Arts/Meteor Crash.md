# Meteor Crash

**School** thundering bull (strike)

**Action** 1 greater

**Range** up to twice your speed

**Target** 1 creature

**Save** Strength

You must charge at your target to perform this strike. You gain a bonus on your attack roll equal to your cultivation modifier. On a successful attack, the target must make a Strength save or be knocked prone. On a successful save, the target is flat-footed until the start of your next turn.

# Refinements

## Empowered (+1)

Increase the number of injuries inflicted by your charge attack by 1.
