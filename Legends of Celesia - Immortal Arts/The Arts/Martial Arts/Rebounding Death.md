# Rebounding Death

**School** hurling star (strike)

**Action** 1 standard

**Target** 1 creature (see text)

**Range** within 1 range increment of the thrown weapon

Make a thrown ranged attack with a bludgeoning weapon. On a successful hit, your weapon bounces off your target towards another target within 1 range increment. You can make another thrown ranged attack with +1 hindrance against this new target.

# Refinements

## Extra Ricochet (+1)

Your thrown weapon bounces one additional time to another target within 1 range increment of the last target hit. You may make one additional thrown ranged attack against the new target with a +1 hindrance. The extra bounce can be directed to a target that was previously attacked with this thrown weapon.

## Practised Bounce (+1)

Remove the hindrance of one thrown attack roll made after the initial target of your choice.

## Returning Catch (+1)

You are able to catch your thrown weapon as it rebounds off its last target as long as you are within 1 range increment of it.
