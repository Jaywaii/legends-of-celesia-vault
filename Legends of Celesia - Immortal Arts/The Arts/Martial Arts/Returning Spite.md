# Returning Spite

**School** hurling star (counter)

**Target** 1 creature

**Range** thrown weapon

As long as you have a hand free, you can make a thrown attack roll against a thrown attack targeting you. Use the thrown weapon's attributes in your attack roll. If your result is equal or higher than the opposing result, you catch the weapon and immediately throw it back towards any target of your choice using your result.

# Refinements

## Blended Strike (+1 (variable))

When you throw the caught weapon back, you are able to do so with a hurling star strike that you know. The refinement cost equals 1 + the form rank of the selected strike.
