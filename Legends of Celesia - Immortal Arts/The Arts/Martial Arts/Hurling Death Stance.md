# Hurling Death Stance

While in this stance, you are able to throw one extra thrown weapon as part of a thrown ranged attack action as long as you have one available on you. You roll a separate attack roll for this extra thrown ranged attack with the same extra modifiers as the original. The extra thrown ranged attack does not need to be at the same target as the original attack.

# Refinements

## Extra Throw (+1)

The number of extra thrown ranged attacks per attack action increases by 1 as long as you have enough thrown weapons on your person.

## Far-Flung (+1)

Increase the range of thrown ranged weapons by 10 ft.
