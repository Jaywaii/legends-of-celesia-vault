# Frenzied Flurry

**School** thrashing fury (strike)

**Action** 1 greater

**Range** melee

**Target** 1 creature

You deliver one attack with each weapon that you currently wield against two targets within that weapon's reach. You gain a circumstance bonus equal to the art's form to all attack rolls made as part of this art.

After performing your strike, you become [[Off-Guarded|off-guarded]] until the start of your next turn.

# Refinements

## Overflowing (+1)

Increase this art's number of targets by 1.
