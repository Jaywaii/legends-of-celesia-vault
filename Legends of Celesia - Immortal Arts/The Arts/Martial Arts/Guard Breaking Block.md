# Guard Breaking Block

**School** iron shell (counter)

When a melee attack roll is made against you, perform a Block reaction. If your block succeeds, instead of preventing one injury, you can cause your attacker to become [[Off-Guarded|off-guarded]] until the start of their next turn.

# Refinements

## Disarming (+1)

On a successful block, you may attempt a free disarm attack against your attacker, targeting the weapon used to attack you.

## Knockback (+1)

On a successful block, you may attempt a free shove attack against your attacker.

## Open Opportunity (+1)

An opponent made off-guarded by this counter provokes against you.

## Powerful

Your attacker is instead off-guarded until the end of your next turn.

## Weapon Breaker (+1)

On a successful block, you may attempt a free sunder attack against the weapon used to attack you.

## Unbalance (+1)

On a successful block, you may attempt a free trip attack against your attacker.
