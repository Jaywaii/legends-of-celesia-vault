# Sentry's Watch

**School** eternal sentinel (boost)

**Action** 1 greater

After executing this boost, you increase your threatened area by 5 ft. Until the start of your next turn, you may perform reactive attacks to any creature that provokes against you in your expanded threatened area. You must move to the creature for it to be within your melee reach, and you can do so as part of your reactive attack. The total number of feet moved as part of your reactive attacks until the start of your next turn cannot exceed your base speed. Any movement you make provokes as normal.

# Refinements

## Assist (+1)

When an ally within your threatened area, not including yourself, makes an attack, is targeted by an attack, or must make a save, you can spend a reaction to move adjacent to that ally and perform the aid another action to improve their attack roll, their DV, or their save. This movement counts towards your limit of feet that you can move as part of this boost.

## Covering Path (+1)

Allies moving within your threatened area, not including yourself, do not provoke from movement.

## Expansion (+1)

Increase your threatened area by 5 ft.

## Punishing Rebuke (+1)

When a creature targets an ally within your threatened area, not including yourself, with an attack or an ability, they provoke against you.

## Quickened Pace (+1)

Increase the maximum number of feet you can move as part of reactive attacks by 5 ft.

## Roving Flanker (+1)

Your allies in your threatened area, not including yourself, may consider all squares threatened by you to be occupied by an ally for the purpose of determining flanking bonuses.
