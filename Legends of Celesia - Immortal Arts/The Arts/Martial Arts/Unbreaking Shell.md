# Unbreaking Shell

**School** iron shell (stance)

You are able to perform the block reaction while off-guarded.

You gain a bonus equal to 1 + this stance's form to attack rolls for block reactions and to your DV when taking the ready shield action.

# Refinements

## Bulwark (+1)

Increase the number of injuries reduced by a successful block by 1.

## Skillful Defence (+1)

Reduce the number of hindrances taken from using the block reaction by 1.

## Untouchable (+1)

You are able to make block reactions against critical hits and touch attacks.

## Wave Breaker (+2)

You are able to make block reactions against cone and line effects. Treat the effect's DC as the result of the opposed attack roll.
