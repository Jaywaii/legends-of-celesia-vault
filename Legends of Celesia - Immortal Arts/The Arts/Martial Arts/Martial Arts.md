# Martial Arts

# Martial Arts Basics

Martial arts are all extraordinary abilities, with its most powerful forms almost, but not quite, breaking into the realm of the supernatural.

## Forms

Martials arts are ranked in "forms". The higher the form, the more complex, yet more efficient, and ultimately more powerful a martial art is. The 9th-form of a martial art is generally the upper limit of what mortals are capable of. The 0th-form of a martial art is also called its "root".

The highest form of martial art you can perform is equal to 1 + 1/2 your martial bonus.

## Refinements

Refinements alter or improve your martial arts. Adding a refinement increases the art's minimum form by the refinement's value.

## Attribute Save DCs

The DCs of your martial arts are equal to 10 + your art's form + 1/2 of your martial bonus.

# Performing Martial Arts

You must know a martial art to perform it.

After performing a martial art, you cannot perform arts of that form for a number of rounds equal to that art's form. For example, if you perform a 5th-form martial arts, you cannot perform any 5th-form martial art until 5 rounds later.
