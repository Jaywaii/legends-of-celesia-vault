# Acid Injury

When a creature receives acid injuries, they must make a Dexterity save against a DC equal to 10 + 5 per acid injury received. On failure, the acid continues to dissolve at the creature's body, and they take one additional injury at the start of their turn for a  number of round equal to the number of acid injuries received. The creature can choose which body part receives these additional injuries.
