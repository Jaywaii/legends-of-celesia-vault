# Creating Your Character

# 1) Attributes

Generate your [[Attributes]] using either their point-buy or roll method.

# 2) Lineage

Choose your [[Lineages|Lineage]] and its traits. You have a base pool of 10 LP.

# 3) Talents

Choose your starting [[Talents & Training|Talents]]. You have a starting pool of 1,000 XP.

# 4) Skills

Update the proficiencies of your [[Skills]] based on your lineage traits and selected talents.

# 5) Equipment

You begin with 100 gp to spend on equipment, gear, and art manuals.

- Design your [[Weapons]].
- Design your [[Armours|Armours & Shields]].
- Select other items and tools.
- Buy your [[The Arts|Art]] manuals.
