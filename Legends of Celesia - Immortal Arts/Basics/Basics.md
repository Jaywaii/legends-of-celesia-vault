# Basics

# Edges and Hindrances

- Edges and hindrances are applied to rolls and checks using a d20.
- An edge lets you roll another d20 to your roll or check, and you take the highest result.
- A hindrance forces you to roll another d20, and you must take the lowest result.
- Different circumstances can let you gain multiple instances of edges, or force you to incur multiple instances of hindrances. Each adds one more d20 to roll for each instance.
- One instance of edge and one instance of hindrance applying to the same roll cancel each other out.

# Experience Points

- Experience points (XP) represents what you have learned from life and how it has affected you.
- You earn XP from overcoming challenges, defeating enemies, and completing quests.
- You spend XP to learn new abilities or improve your attributes.
- Creatures are also built with XP worth of abilities.
- XP is generally earned as a party, regardless of how much each individual contributed as long as they are present within the encounter.
- GMs: add up all XP value of PCs during the encounter. This is the party experience (PXP). Add up all XP value of opponents and challenges of the encounter. This is the encounter level (EXP).
- Divide the EXP by the PXP, and multiply the result by 100. The result is the amount of XP each PC gains.

> **XP per PC** = (EXP / PXP) x 100

# Level

Level is a measure of your character's overall prowess. You start at 1st level. Every 1,000 XP you possess increases your level by 1. At 1st level, you have 10 LP worth of lineage traits (see Lineage), and 1,000 XP worth of cultivation options (see Growth and Training).

You do not need to cultivate your experience into talents for your level to increase.

Creatures weaker 1st level PCs have levels denoted as fractions. One level below 1st is 1/2, the next lowest is 1/3, and so on (1/[n+1] where n equals the number of levels below 1st). Creatures have 10 LP worth of NPC-only lineage traits, and 1,000 XP times level worth of abilities. So a 1/3 level creature has 10 LP worth of NPC lineage traits, and 333 XP worth of abilities.
