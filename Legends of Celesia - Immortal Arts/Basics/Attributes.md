# Attributes

Each creature possesses six attribute modifiers, and almost all abilities revolve around them. The attributes are divided in two types: physical and mental.

When a mechanic mentions an ability bonus, only apply the attribute's modifier if it is positive. When a mechanic mentions an attribute penalty, only apply the attribute's modifier if it is negative.

### Strength (Str; physical)

Your ability to exert physical force.

- You may choose your Strength modifier as your melee attack modifier.
- reduces armor check penalty
- carrying capacity

### Dexterity (Dex; physical)

Your ability to coordinate your body movement and reaction time.

- defense value
- You may choose your Dexterity modifier as your melee attack modifier.
- You may choose your Dexterity modifier as your ranged attack modifier.

### Constitution (Con; physical)

Your body's innate health, endurance, and resistance to harmful effects.

- Multiply your Constitution modifier with your level, and add the result to your Stamina pool (to a minimum of 0).

### Intelligence (Int; mental)

Your ability to reason and retain knowledge.

- You are able to learn a number of languages equal to twice your Intelligence bonus. At Intelligence +0 and -1, you can speak and read only one language. At Intelligence -2, you can only speak, but not read, one language. At Intelligence -3 and lower, you are unable to speak or read any language.

### Wisdom (Wis; mental)

Your ability to judge and evaluate.

- You may choose your Wisdom modifier as your ranged attack modifier.

### Charisma (Cha; mental)

Your ability to exude self-worth, confidence, and appeal, and to interact with others.

# Attribute Generation

## Point-Buy

All attribute modifiers start at +0.  You may decrease an attribute by -1 to gain a +1 to a different attribute. You cannot lower an attribute lower than -2 in this way, nor increase an attribute greater than +3.

## Rolling

Roll 1d6-3 six times, and assign each result to an attribute as desired.

# Attribute Damage

Attribute damage temporarily reduces an attribute's modifier. Attribute damage is gradually removed by resting, or can be wholly removed by magical healing. Attribute damage stacks with attribute drain and penalties.

# Attribute Drain

Attribute drain is similar as attribute damage, except that it cannot be healed by resting. Specific magical healing is needed to remove attribute drain. Attribute damage stacks with attribute damage and penalties.

# Attribute Penalty

Attribute penalty temporarily reduces an attribute's modifier. Penalties are inflicted by an ongoing effect. This penalty cannot be healed, but once the effect causing is removed, so is the penalty.
