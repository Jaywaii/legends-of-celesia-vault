# Legends of Celesia: Immortal Arts

Legends of Celesia: Immortal Ascension (LoC:IA) is a classless turn-based tactical combat system derived from Paizo's Pathfinder 1e and its expansions, along with 3rd-party ideas from Dreamscarred Press and Drop Dead Studios.
