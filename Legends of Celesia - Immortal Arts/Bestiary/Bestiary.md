# Bestiary

# Creature Types

### Aberration

Aberrations are creatures that feature unique and alien anatomy and mystical abilities.

### Animal

Animals are creatures that evolved naturally in nature without interference from outside influence. They do not possess any mystical abilities or qualities. However, their physical traits are generally incredible, enough to survive extinction in a world with mystic beasts and alien aberrations. Their intelligence is limited, relying solely on instinct, though animals on the higher end of intelligence are capable of having emotions and memories. When animals become capable of full self-awareness, reasoning and logic, and understanding complex ideas, they cease to be animals and enter the realm of being mystic beasts, and become capable of cultivation.

### Construct

Constructs are creatures that are created by another intelligent creature using crafted materials, whether through mundane or magical means. They are closer to being objects than living creatures. However, the more closely constructs are built to mimic natural life, the more vulnerable they become to effects felt by that natural life. Constructs are generally mindless, built to follow specific instructions or follow commands, but more advanced ones can reach full intelligence as any primanoid. Constructs can be completely non-magical and incapable of mystical feats, while others, especially those crafted though magical means or built with magical gear, can naturally use mystical abilities. Finally, full intelligent constructs may be able to cultivate as any natural primanoid.

### Fey

Feys, also called fairies, are creatures native to the realm of Faerie. What sets them apart from outsiders is that they can freely traverse between Faerie and the Middle Realm thanks to the inherent connection between the two planes. As such, fey are considered to be native to both realms. Fey are immortal so long as they remain curious and driven, dying only when they achieve absolutely boredom. Fey that are forcefully slain are reborn in Faerie.

### Mycophytes

Mycophytes encompass both fungal- and plant-based creatures. While fungi and flora are two distinct organisms, the creatures that have developed from those separate origins possess many of the same qualities. As such, mystic techniques developed specifically for one tend to also affect the other just as well, leading scholars to categorize both types of creatures together.

### Mystic Beast

Mystic beasts generally possess animal features, but possess full intelligence and natural mystic arts. Most mystic beasts evolved from mundane animals who gain cultivation from nesting in or near a mystical source, or consuming food and creatures who themselves possessed high amounts of cultivation. However, some mystic beasts are the result of magical manipulation, like from a deity or a powerful mystic.

### Primanoid

Primanoids are the most common intelligent creatures native to the Middle Realm. They are notable in that they naturally form complex social structures, yet are capable of retaining their individuality. They are creatures with physical features and intelligence that allow them to create and use complex tools to craft even more complex artifical creations. All primanoids are capable of cultivation.

### Outsider

Outsiders are creatures native to the outer planes.

### Undead

Undead are any creatures that were once alive with a soul, then killed. It then becomes an undead creature when cursed energy is used to enslave its soul to the Middle Realm, preventing it from passing through the cycle of reincarnation. Undead can be raised as mindless minions under the control of their creator, or retain their full intelligence. However, while intelligent undead may start with their original personality, the effect of curse unnaturally chaining their soul from continuing the spiritual cycle twists their thoughts towards resentment, bitterness, anger, and hjealousy of those who still live around them.
