# Creating New Creatures

Creatures are created using creature lineage traits (or simply "creature traits") which cost creature points (CP). Like [[Lineages|lineage traits]] for PCs, creature traits are ranked as standard (1 CP), expert (2 CP), master (3 CP), and legendary (5 CP). Unlike PCs, however, creatures can have traits totaling more than 10 points. A creature with 10 or less CP worth of traits is at level 0. At 11 CP, and every 10 CP after, the creature is one level higher. Creatures can be built with lineage traits available to PCs in addition to the creature traits, however creature traits are not available to PCs.

# Body Parts

All creatures begin with the exact same body parts as a PC primanoid. Additional body parts can be added by purchasing traits with CP. Body parts can also be removed by taking drawbacks which grant CP.

- 1 head
- 1 nose
- 2 eyes
- 1 mouth
- 2 ears
- 1 neck
- 2 arms
- 2 lungs
- 1 heart
- 1 stomach
- 2 legs

# Creature Size

A creature's starting size determines how many creature points it starts with and basic statistics. The effects and CP costs of multiple size increases or reductions stack with each other.

### (-1 size smaller) (-4 CP)

- -1 Strength, +1 Dexterity, all other attribute modifiers are +0.
- -1 size penalty to Constitution saves.
- Base speed is reduced by 10 ft.
- The number of creatures of the same size that can fit in the same square increases by 1.
- Reach is reduced to 0 ft. The creature must be in the target's space to make a melee attack.

### Medium (0 CP)

- All attribute modifiers are +0.
- Base speed is 30 ft.
- The creature's space is 5 by 5 ft.
- Reach is 5 ft.

### (+1 size larger) (+4 CP)

- +1 Strength, -1 Dexterity, all other attribute modifiers are +0.
- +1 size bonus to Constitution saves.
- Base speed increases by 10 ft.
- The creature's space increases by 5 ft. on each axis.
- Reach is increased by 5 ft.
