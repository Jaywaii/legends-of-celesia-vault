# Instantaneous Initiative

### Benefits

Add your mythic rank as a bonus to your initiative. When you roll for initiative, you may spend one spark of ascendence to add +20 to your result. Additionally, on your turn, you may spend one spark of ascendence as a free action to grant yourself an additional 2 AP for this turn, limited to once per round.
