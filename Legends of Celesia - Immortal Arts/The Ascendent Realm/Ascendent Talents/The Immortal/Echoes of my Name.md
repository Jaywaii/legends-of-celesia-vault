# Echoes of my Name

### Benefits

Like those transcendental beings of power, you know when others dare to speak your name out loud. You can activate this ability, and for a period time, you can hear your name, as well as a number of titles adopted by you equal to your rank, whenever it is spoken, regardless of distance. You can use this ability a number of times per day equal to your mythic rank for free, and you can spend a spark of  ascendence to gain another use beyond this limit. The duration of this ability depends on how narrow your target audience is. The ability lasts a minute per rank if there is no limit, 10 minutes per rank if limited to a specific lineage, an hour per rank if limited to a specific organization (such as a religion, clan, family, guild, or team), and a day per rank if limited to a specific individual. While this ability is active, you can expend one spark of ascendence to use *[[Divining Eye|divining eye]]* as a natural mystic art to scry on the creature speaking your name. If your scrying is successful, you can expend an additional spark of ascendence to deliver a message to the target as *sending*. You can dismiss this ability as a free action at any time.

If you have at least 3 mythic ranks, this ability functions across planar boundaries. If you succeed in scrying on the target, you can spend one additional spark of ascendence to use *project image* as a natural mystic art. The image appears at the location of the scrying sensor, which is considered the point of origin of the spell. If you scrying sensor is dispelled, so is the projected image.

If you have at least 6 mythic ranks, if you successfully scry on a creature, you can also read its mind as *detect thoughts*, and you can spend one additional spark of ascendence to *discern location* on the speaker. If a creature says your name three times in a single breath, you learn that creature’s true name and can expend two sparks of ascendence to possess the target if it fails a Charisma save (DC 10 + half your level + your mythic rank + your Charisma modifier). If the creature successfully saves, it is immune to possession attempts made by you for a year and a day. A failed save establishes a mental connection between you and the target, but you may also read the target’s thoughts or use its senses as share senses at any time simply by concentrating. If the target is within a non-mythic effect that blocks compulsions or possession, you can bypass that effect for 1 hour by expending an additional spark of ascendence.

As a simple action, you shift your consciousness into or out of the target. Your possession lasts for 24 hours, though you may expend one spark of ascendence each day to renew it. You can possess only one creature at a time. If a creature is able to dispel or block your possession, the caster must succeed on a caster level check (DC 10 plus your level plus your rank); if this check fails, you can expend one spark of ascendence as a free action to attempt to possess the caster as if she had spoken your name three times. Casting an area spell that blocks possession does not carry this risk unless the caster touches the possessed creature.
