# Reaping the Chaff

### Benefits

Carve through the ranks of inferior foes with lethal speed and precision. As a full action, you can move up to twice your speed, moving through the space of any creature smaller than you or whose level is less than your own. Make one melee attack roll at your highest bonus and add all permanent bonuses. Apply your attack to all such creatures in the path of your movement. You may spend a spark of ascendence to increase your injuries by 1 degree of success for each rank you have over 3rd. You may apply the effects of talents that can be applied to any type of melee attack, but not those that do not apply to multiple attacks or opponents per round. Injuries that apply against certain creature types apply when you use this ability to attack creatures of that type even if you also attack other kinds of creatures with this ability. A critical hit applies to all targets. Creatures in your path can make a reactive attack against you with +1 hindrance, or they can forgo this reactive attack and attempt a Dexterity save to reduce the injuries by 1 (DC 10 + your martial bonus + your mythic rank), and reduced by another 1 for every degree of success. You cannot strike a creature more than once per round with this ability, even if you move through its space more than once.
