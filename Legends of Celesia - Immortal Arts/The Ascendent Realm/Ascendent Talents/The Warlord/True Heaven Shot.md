# True Heaven Shot

### Benefits

As a standard action, you can expend one spark of ascendence to make a ranged attack at your highest attack bonus. You may instead expend two sparks of ascendence to activate this strike as a simple action. This is in addition to any other attacks you make this round. When making this attack, ignore the target’s cover and concealment other than total cover, and add your rank to the attack roll. Increase your range increment by your base range increment for each of your rank. Damage from this attack bypasses all damage reduction.
