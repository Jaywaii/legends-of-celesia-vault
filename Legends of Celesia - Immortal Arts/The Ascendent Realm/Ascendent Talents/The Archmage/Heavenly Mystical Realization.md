# Heavenly Mystical Realization

### Benefits

You can expend one spark of ascendence to cast any one 1st-realm mystic art without knowing it and ignoring your current dispersion. For every additional spark of ascendence expended, the realm level of the art you can cast increases by two. Your casting tier is considered 2 tiers higher for this cast, but the art's action increases by 1 AP.
