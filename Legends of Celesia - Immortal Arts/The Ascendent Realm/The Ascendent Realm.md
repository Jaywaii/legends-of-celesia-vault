# The Ascendent Realm

An optional separate progression track to attain power and abilities that are as close to god-like as mortally possible. Reserved for those who overcome truly mythic encounters.

# Playing Mythic Adventures

Mythic characters will gain abilities that will drastically unbalance your game. This optional subsystem is meant to allow players to perform deeds and experience adventures that are larger than life, worldchanging, and rival the epic feats of legends and myths throughout history.
