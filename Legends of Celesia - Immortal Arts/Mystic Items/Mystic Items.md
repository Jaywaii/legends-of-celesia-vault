# Mystic Items

# Resonance

Using mystic items requires your soul to interacts with their magical nature, a phenomenon known as "resonance". There is a limit to how much magical energy you can resonate with, but it is a limit can be increased as you grow more powerful.

Mystic items with ongoing effects require their wearers to invest their resonance in them.
