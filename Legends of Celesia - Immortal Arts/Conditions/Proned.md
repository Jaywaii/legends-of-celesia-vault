# Proned

You are lying on the ground. Non-prone opponents gain a +1 edge on melee attacks against you, while you take a +1 hindrance on melee attacks against them. Ranged attacks against you take a +1 hindrance. You cannot use a bow when proned, and you take a +1 hindrance on ranged attacks with a thrown weapon. You gain a +1 edge on ranged attacks made with a crossbow or a firearm.
