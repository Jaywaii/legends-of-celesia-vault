# Confused

You are unable think clearly and act wildly. You become [[Off-Guarded|off-guarded]], unable to use arts, skills, or special abilities. During your turn, you use all of your actions to attack a target within reach that the GM chooses, typically at random. If there are no targets within reach, you are forced to move towards a random target as long as you are able to deliver one attack at them. If no targets are within range, you attack yourself instead. If it is impossible for you to make an attack, you babble incoherently and lose your turn.
