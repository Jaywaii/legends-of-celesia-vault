# Stupefied

Your thoughts and senses become cloudy. You incur +1 hindrance on mental attribute-based rolls. Targets gain +1 edge on saves against your DCs that are based on your mental attributes. Any time you perform an art, you must make a concentration check against a DC equal to 10 + twice the art's realm. On failure, the art fails to finish and the action is spent. If your concentration check is based on a mental attribute, it is also affected by the hindrance previously mentioned.
