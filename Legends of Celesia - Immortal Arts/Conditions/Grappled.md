# Grappled

You are grappled when your body is restrained by another creature, a trap, or an effect. You cannot move, and cannot use wings to fly if you possess them; if you are in the air, you immediately fall unless you are held by another creature or other effect. You take a +1 hindrance on attack rolls, cannot cast spells with a somatic component, and you also have the [[Off-Guarded|off-guarded]] condition.
