# Paralyzed

While paralyzed, you are considered to be helpless. You cannot move or perform any physical action, but as long as you are awake, you remain aware of your surroundings and can perform purely mental actions. You cannot communicate through physical means, such as talking, but telepathic communication is still possible. You take a +1 hindrance on Perception checks as you cannot move to orient your senses. You also become [[Off-Guarded|off-guarded]].
