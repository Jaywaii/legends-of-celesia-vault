# Staggered

You are unable to act at full capacity. Each degree of staggered reduces the number of acts you can perform during your turn by 1. You can regain an act by paying stamina equal to the number of degrees; this is in addition to any other stamina costs. You can continue to perform reactions, but each reaction costs one extra stamina point. In addtion, you cannot run or charge, and all of your movements speeds are halved.
