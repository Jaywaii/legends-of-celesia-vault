# Exhausted

While exhausted, you cannot run or charge, all of your movement speeds are halved, you take a +1 hindrance on attack rolls, and your carrying capacity it halved. You also take a +1 hindrance on all Strength- and Dexterity-based attribute checks, saves, and skill checks. Exhausted is removed when you have at least 1 point of Stamina. You also become [[Off-Guarded|off-guarded]].
