# Blinded

You cannot see using the visual medium. All checks and activities that require sight, such as reading or visual-based Perception checks, automatically fail. Attacks and arts that require line of sight automatically miss unless you know the target's position. You remember the position of all targets within your line of sight when you become blinded, but not of any that changes afterwards. You can spend a simple action to make a Perception check know the new positions of any targets within what would have been your normal line of sight. Even then, all targets are considered to have total concealment against you (50% miss chance).

Strength- and Dexterity-based skill checks and opposed Perception checks take a +1 hindrance. Attacks against you gain a +1 edge. You can move safely by spending twice as much movement per square than normal. To move at full speed, make an Acrobatics check every square that you travel; on failure, you fall prone. The DC starts at 10, and increases by 5 every square you travel. The DC resets at the start of your next turn. Increase the DC by 10 if the square is difficult terrain.
