# Sickened

You feel violently ill. Each degree of sickened inflicts a -1 penalty on all d20 rolls. You are unwilling to ingest any anything, including elixirs and potions unless you make a Charisma save against DC 10 + 10 for each grade of sickened.

You may spend a standard action retching to recover. Make a Constitution save against DC 10 + 10 for each grade of sickened. On success, reduce your sickened grade by 1.
