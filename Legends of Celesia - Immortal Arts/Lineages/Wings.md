# Wings

### Benefits

You have 2 wing body parts that grant you a fly speed 30 ft. if your are medium-size or 20 ft. if you are small-size.

### Special

You can take this trait multiple times. Each time you take it, you gain 2 additional wing body parts and your fly speed increases by 5 ft.
