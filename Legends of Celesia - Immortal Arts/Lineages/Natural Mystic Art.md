# Natural Mystic Art

### Benefits

You can perform the mystic art listed with this trait once per day using your level as your tier with a maximum realm of 0th. Your cultivation modifier is the highest modifier among your mental attributes.

At 4th level, and every 3 levels after, you can perform this art an additional time per day, and your natural art's maximum realm increases by 1.
