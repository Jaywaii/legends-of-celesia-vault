# Human

# Standard

**Human Flexbility:** [[Adaptable]].

**Human Talent (Standard):** Select 1 standard talent as a [[Bonus Standard Talent]]. You must still meet its prerequisites to gain its benefits.

**Human Training (Standard):** Select 1 skill as a [[Bonus Trained Skill]].

# Expert

**Human Talent (Expert):** Select 1 expert talent as a [[Bonus Expert Talent]]. You must still meet its prerequisites to gain its benefits.

# Master

**Human Talent (Master):** Select 1 master talent as a [[Bonus Master Talent]]. You must still meet its prerequisites to gain its benefits.

# Legendary

**Human Boost:** Select 1 [[Attribute Boost]].

**Human Talent (Legendary):** Select 1 legendary talent as a [[Bonus Legendary Talent]]. You must still meet its prerequisites to gain its benefits.
