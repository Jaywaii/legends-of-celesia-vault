# Lineages

Lineage is what type of creature you are. Each lineage is defined by a unique set of physical traits along with a general common physical appearance. A creature does not possess all of their lineage's traits, only a combination of a certain number of them, but anyone else would still easily recognize them as belonging to that lineage.

You gain starting pool 10 Lineage Points (LP) to buy your lineage traits. Traits are tiered into standard, expert, master, and legendary traits.

- Standard traits cost 1 LP and generally grant a single and specific one-time benefit.
- Expert traits cost 2 LP and generally grant a single benefit that is more versatile.
- Master traits cost 3 LP and generally grant a versatile benefit that scales with your level.
- Legendary traits cost 4 LP and generally grant a benefit or ability that significantly affects your entire character throughout your career.

> DESIGN NOTES: Each lineage will need a mix of traits that total at least 10 LP.

Spending 6 or more LP of your starting LP pool on a single lineage grants you a +1 full lineage bonus LP to spend on that lineage.

Some lineages have branch lineages to denote distinct peoples that have emerged over time or due to certain circumstances. Some branch lineage traits have requirements that prevent you from picking one or more traits from the parent lineage. You can take traits from different branch lineages.

Unless otherwise stated, you can take a lineage trait only once. However, lineage modifiers from different traits stack with each other.

In addition to lineages and their branches, there is a universal lineage package with its own set of branches. These universal lineage branches exist because of interbreeding from magical NPC lineages like celestials and infernals. LP spent on universal traits do not count towards eligibility for the full lineage bonus.

### Full Lineage Bonus

It is assumed by default lineages are capable of interbreeding with each other, allowing for traits from two or more lineages to appear in offspring. Sometimes, some traits skip a generation only to reappear in descendents further down the family line. If you choose traits from more than one lineage, you are free to describe your physical appearance (appear fully as one of your lineages, mostly with one or few features of your other lineages, or a more even mix of between all of your lineages), however some lineage traits will specify distinct physical appearances associated with them. For any effect that affects a creature of a specific lineage, you are affected as such if you have at least 1 trait belonging to that lineage. Your GM may allow or restrict any trait purchases across different lineages.

You are free to identify yourself as any lineage, a mixture of your various ancestries, or as a unique being. Whether or not you are accepted as such by others of those lineages is up to them and your interactions with them.

### Drawbacks

For each trait taken in a lineage, you can take one of that lineage's drawback traits of equal tier. Drawback traits apply some sort of difficulty to you. When you pick a drawback, you gain a bonus LP equal to its tier. This bonus LP can only be spent on traits belonging to that drawback's lineage.

### Lineage Body Parts

All PC lineages are assumed to be primanoids by default. All primanoids have the following body parts:

- 2 arms
- 2 ears
- 2 eyes
- 1 head
- 1 heart
- 2 legs
- 2 lungs
- 1 mouth
- 1 neck
- 1 nose
- 1 stomach

Some lineages allow you to gain more body parts by purchasing them with LP, or remove body parts by taking drawbacks. Your GM may modify the default set of body parts for some or all lineages with additional costs, but bear in mind this may affect game balance.

**Size:** Medium is considered to be the default size with a base speed of 30 ft., and sizes smaller and larger than medium change certain attributes. Some lineages are Small size. From your starting LP pool, if you spend 8 or more LP on traits from Small lineages, your default size is Small. If you spend 3 to 7 LP on traits from Small lineages, you may choose your size to be Small or Medium (this choice cannot be changed once made). When you are Small size (either by default or by choice), you gain the following effects:

- Small size effects: -1 to Str, +1 to Dex, -1 to Con, base speed 20 ft.
- Small PCs get 5 bonus LP to spend on traits from any Small lineage.

# Types of Traits

Lineages are composed of traits from the following types. Some trait types also have a subtype.

Unless otherwise specified, if two or more lineages each have a trait that are the same type, you can only pick one of them. The only exception is if the type of trait has a subtype, such as specifying an ability attribute or a talent, and those subtypes are different between each lineage.
