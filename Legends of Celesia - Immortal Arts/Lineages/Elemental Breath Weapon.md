# Elemental Breath Weapon

### Benefits

As a standard action, you are able to spew out elemental energy with your mouth. The type of energy is specified with this trait, and the injuries it inflicts will be of that type. When you use this ability, you can choose to use it as an area (30 ft. cone or 60 ft. line), inflicting a number of injuries equal to your Constitution modifier, and affected targets can make a Dexterity save to halve the injuries. Your breath weapon's DC is 10 + 1/2 your level + your Constitution modifier. At 6th level, and every 5 levels after, your area inflicts one additional injury.

Alternatively, you can use it as a 60 ft. ranged touch attack against a single target. Its attack modifier starts at +0, and increases by 1 at 6th level and every 5 levels after.

You are able to use your  breath weapon a number of times per day equal to your Constitution modifier + your level.
