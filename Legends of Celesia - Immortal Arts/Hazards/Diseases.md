# Diseases

# Disease Characteristics

Diseases should have long-term effects on the scale of days or longer. They do not have immediate effects, and only reveal themselves after an onset of time longer than what combats would last. However, they are very difficult to cure, and require multiple saves as part of the recovery process. Diseases often have multiple stages that progressively inflict worse effects as time goes on and are not cured.
