# Armours

# Armour Basics

**Armour Bonus:** Armours have an armour bonus and is added to your DV when worn. You can only wear one set of armour. Your armour's bonus begins at +0.

**Armour Check Penalty (ACP):** Wearing an armour inflicts a penalty known as an Armour Check Penalty to Strength- and Dexterity-based attribute checks, saving throws, and skill checks. ACP also reduces your defence modifier if it is a positive bonus, but cannot reduce it to below +0. If your defence modifier is negative, ACP has no affect on it. Your Strength bonus (if any) can reduce the ACP of the armour you are wearing, to a minimum of 0. A Strength penalty has no affect on ACP. Wearing armour with the **broken** condition applies its ACP to your attack rolls, concentration checks, and initiative check. Your armour's starting ACP is equal to its armour bonus.

**Defence Value (DV):** An armour's DV is used when it is targeted by the sunder maneuver. Your armour's starting DV is equal to its armour bonus.

# Customizing Your Armour

You can design your armour by spending build points (BP) on it. There is no limit to how many BP you can spend, but the more you spend, the more expensive and heavier the armour will be.

**Base cost** of the armour is equal to 20 gp times the squared amount of BP spent on it.

**Donning an armour** takes 1 round per BP spent. This action provokes.

**Removing an armour** takes half the time to don it. This action provokes.

## 2) Customizations

Unless otherwise stated, a customization can be taken multiple times and its effects stack. The BP cost of each customization depends on its tier.

### Standard Customizations (1 BP)

#### Easy-Fit

The armour is considered to be 1 BP less when calculating the time needed to don it.

#### Flexible

Reduce the armour's ACP by 1.

#### Lightweight

Reduce the armour's weight by 1 lb.

### Expert Customizations (2 BP)

#### Sturdy

Increase the armour's DV by 1.

### Master  Customizations (3 BP)

#### Reinforced

Increase the armour's bonus by 1.

### Spikes

(WIP) These sharp spikes embedded in your armour point outward to deal piercing damage.

## 3) Drawbacks

You can choose to add drawbacks to your armour to lower the cost. Each drawback reduces the armour's BP cost by 1 (to a minimum of 1). Unless otherwise stated, a drawback can be applied to an armour only once.

### Heavy

Increase the armour's weight by 1 lb.

**Special** This drawback can be taken multiple times. Its effects stack.

### Restrictive

Increase the armour's ACP by 1.

**Special** You can take apply drawback multiple times. Its effects stack.

## 4) Armour Size

Determine the creature size your armour is designed for. Halve the cost and weight for each size smaller than medium, and double the cost and weight for each size larger. Wearing armour not of your size grants no armour bonus to DV, but you are still affected by its ACP.

# Armour Proficiency

Armour proficiency represents how accustom you are to wearing any armour and performing physically taxing activities. You begin with untrained proficiency in wearing armour, and you take a +1 hindrance on all checks affects by armour check penalty, even if your armour's ACP is at 0.

At standard proficiency, you lose the hindrance from being untrained. At expert, your ACP is reduced by 1, by 2 at master, and by 3 at legendary, to a maximum of 0.
