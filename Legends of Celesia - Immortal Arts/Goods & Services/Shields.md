# Shields

# Shield Types

**Light:** Shields that are designed to be strapped on your forearm while leaving your hand free, allowing you to use that arm to hold an item and use it for other actions, albeit with a +1 hindrance on any checks or rolls. Because it requires to be strapped, a light shield requires a standard provoke action to be wielded, and a simple provoke action to remove it.

**Standard:** Shields that must be wielded with one hand. The default type of shield. It takes a simple provoke action to wield a standard shield, and a free action to drop it.

**Tower:** Larger shields that must be wielded with one hand, but can be planted on the ground to provide cover for one creature. It takes a standard provoke action to wield a tower shield, and a free action to drop it.

# Shield Basics

**Shield Bonus:** Add this bonus to your block roll when using the Block reaction with this shield or to your DV when using the Ready Defence simple action. Your shield's bonus begins at +0.

**DV:** The defence value of your shield. Used when an opponent attempts to sunder your shield. Your shield's starting DV is equal to its shield bonus.

# Customizing Your Shield

You can design your shield by spending build points (BP) on it. There is no limit to how many BP you can spend, but the more you spend, the more expensive and heavier the shield will be.

## 1) Customizations

The BP cost of a customization depends on its tier. Unless otherwise noted, you can take a customization multiple times and their effects stack.

### Standard Customizations (1 BP)

#### Resistant

The shield is particularly resistant against one type of damage: bludgeoning, piercing, slashing. Increase the shield's bonus and DV against that type of attack by 1.

#### Efficient

The shield is more efficiently built, providing greater defence with less material. Reduce your shield's weight by 1 lb. You cannot apply this customization if it would reduce your shield's weight below 1 lb.

### Expert Customizations (2 BP)

#### Sturdy

The shield's structure is better built. Increase your shield's DV by 1.

### Master Customizations (3 BP)

#### Reinforced

Additional material makes the shield provide better defence. Increase your shield's bonus by 1. Adjust your shield's DV and weight based on its new shield bonus.

## 2) Drawbacks

You may add drawbacks to your shield. Each drawback reduces the shield's gp cost depending on its tier. Unless otherwise noted, you can take a drawback multiple times and their effects stack. You cannot apply any further drawbacks if it would reduce the shield's gp cost to less than 1 gp.

### Standard Drawbacks (-1 gp)

#### Heavy

Increase the shield's weight by 1 lb.

#### Weakness

The shield is particularly weak against one type of attack: bludgeoning, piercing, slashing. Reduce the shield's bonus and DV against that type of attack by 1.

### Expert Drawbacks (-2 gp)

#### Fragile

Reduce the shield's DV by 1.

## 3) Determine Cost

This shield's **base cost** is equal to 1 gp times the squared amount of BP spent on it. Halve the gp cost and weight for a light shield, and double them for a tower shield.

# Shield Profiency

You begin with untrained proficiency with each type of shield. While wielding a type of shield you are untrained with, you take a +1 hindrance on Block reactions with it, and are unable to use the Ready Shield action.
