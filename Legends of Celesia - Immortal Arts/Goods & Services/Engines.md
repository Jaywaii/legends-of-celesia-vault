# Engines

Engines are complex items that are controlled by a user from a cockpit.

### Base Statistics

- **Size:** medium.
- **Strength:** +4.
-  **Dexterity:** +2.
- **DV:** 12. DV is modified by the type of material used to build the engine.

The base DC to craft an engine is 20. Each CP built into the engine increases the DC by 1.

# Customizations

- **Advanced Actuators:** The engine gains a dodge bonus to DV. (1 CP per +1 bonus)
- **Armor Reinforcement:** Increase the engine’s hardness. (1 CP per +1 to hardness)
- **Arm:** The engine gains a fully integrated mechanical arm which allows it to wield an appropriately sized weapon without slotting it first. An engine may make as many attacks with such a weapon as their pilot’s base attack bonus would normally allow. While wielding a weapon in such a manner, the engine is treated as having two fewer weapon slots (minimum 0); any weapons currently occupying slots that would be made unavailable cannot be used while the weapon is in hand. (2 CP per arm)
- **Carriage:** The engine can carry 2 passengers, each with a size two steps smaller than the engine. Twice the number of passengers can be seated in the carriage for each passenger size smaller. This customization can be taken multiple times. (1 CP)
- **Cerebral Reinforcement:** The engine grants immunity to all mind-affecting effects (charms, compulsions, morale effects, patterns and phantasms) to its pilot while they operate it. (5 CP)
- **Engine Weapon Slot:** The engine gains a slot to install an engine weapon. (1 CP per slot)
- **Expanded Cockpit:** The engine's cockpit can carry additional pilots. There is enough room for one pilot of a size two steps smaller than the engine's size; the room for pilots is doubled for each pilot size smaller. These additional pilots can control the engine, but only one creature can act as the pilot per round. (1 CP)
- **Hauler:** The engine treats its carrying capacity as if its Strength was 8 points higher. (1 CP)
- **Hover Stabilizers:** Engines can use the slow fall monk ability, except they do not need an adjacent surface to activate it. Treat your ranks in Knowledge (engineering) as the engine’s monk level to determine the total distance negated by this ability. Each use of this ability uses 1 charge for a medium engine; multiply the number of charges by 2 for each size larger. (1 CP)
- **Legs:** The engine gains a pair of powered mechanical legs, giving it a base speed for its size and allowing it to be tripped. Each pair of legs beyond the first increases the engine’s base speed by one size category larger and grants +4 bonus to CMD against trip. The engine consumes 1 charge for each pair of legs for 1 hour of movement; increase the charge by 1 per pair for each size larger. This customization cannot be taken with Treads or Wheels customizations. (3 CP per pair of legs)
- **Modified Aerodynamics:** Engines can make a DC 15 Fly check to fall safely from any height without taking falling damage, as if using //feather fall//. If the engine is piloted, the pilot can make the Fly check instead. When falling safely, it or the pilot may make an additional DC 15 Fly check to glide, moving 5 feet laterally for every 10 feet it falls. If the engine has the Hover Stabilizers customization, the lateral movement increases to 10 feet. (1 CP)
- **Nautical Superiority:** The engine gains a swim speed equal to the base land speed for its size. The engine consumes 1 charge for 6 hours of movement. The engine can remain submerged for 1 hour per rank of Knowledge (engineering) before its air filters start to fail and the pilot has to make Constitution checks to hold their breath or begin suffocating. This requires the Sea-Worthy customization. (2 CP)
- **Powered Servomotors:** The engine is capable of fantastic feats of agility, and gains a circumstance bonus to Acrobatics or driver Ride checks made to perform a long jump or high jump. In addition, it is always considered to have a running start when jumping. (1 CP per +2 bonus)
- **Powered Treads:** The engine's treads now provide the engine with a base speed for an engine one size larger. The engine consumes 1 charge for 6 hours of movement; increase the number of charges by 1 for each size larger. This customizations requires the Treads customization. (1 CP)
- **Powered Wheels:** The engine's wheels now provide the engine with a base speed for an engine one size larger. The engine consumes 1 charge for 6 hours of movement; increase the number of charges by 1 for each size larger. This customizations requires the Wheels customization. (1 CP)
- **Sea-Worthy:** The engine is buoyant on water and can use water current as propulsion. (1 CP)
- **Shielded Cockpit:** The engine grants immunity to bleed, disease, death effects, necromancy effects, paralysis, poison, sleep effects, and stunning to its pilot while he operates it. (6 CP)
- **Siege Weapon Slot:** The engine gains a slot to install a siege weapon. (2 CP per slot)
- **Sticky Treads:** The engine gains a climb speed equal to half its base speed. This customization can only be taken by engines with the Treads customization. (1 CP)
- **Targeting Upgrade:** The engine is built with devices to aid its pilot’s aim, granting a circumstance bonus to the pilot’s attack rolls. (1 CP per +1 bonus)
- **Thrusters:** The engine gains a fly speed equal to the base land speed for its size with a maneuverability of clumsy. If the engine has the Modifier Aerodynamics customization, the engine's maneuverability increases to poor. This customization can be taken multiple times; each time it is taken, improve its fly speed by either an additional 10 feet or its maneuverability by one step (up to perfect). The engine consumes 1 alchemical charge for 1 minute of movement; increase the number of charges by 1 for each size larger. (3 CP)
- **Treads:** Attached to the bottom of the engine, treads allow the engine to ignore difficult terrain, and treat Desert, Hills, and Tundra hexes as Plains in regards to travel time. The engine also gains a +4 bonus to CMD to resist bull rush and reposition attempts. This customization cannot be taken with Legs or Wheels customizations. (1 CP)
- **Wheels:** Attached to the bottom of the engine, wheels allow the engine to weigh half of its actual weight when being pulled or pushed. This customization cannot be taken with Legs or Treads customizations. (1 CP)
