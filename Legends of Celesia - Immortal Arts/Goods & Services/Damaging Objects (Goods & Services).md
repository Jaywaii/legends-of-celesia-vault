# Damaging Objects

An unattended object has a base DV of 5.

The object's DV is modified by what material it is primarily made of, and its thickness.

### Table: Material DC Modifiers

| Material | DV Modifier |
|---|---|
| Glass, Ice | +1/inch |
| Leather | +3/inch |
| Wood | +5/inch |
| Cold Iron, Iron, Ironwood | +10/inch |
| Mythrym, Steel | +15/inch |
| Adamantine | +30/inch |

Attacks that beat the object's DV increases the object's damage condition by one step per degree of success.

First step of damage makes the object **dinted**.

Second step of damage makes the object **battered**.

Third step of damage makes the object **broken**.

Fourth step of damage makes the object **destroyed**.
