# Weapons

# Weapon Basics

**Attack Modifier:** This represents how deadly your weapon is. Add this modifier to your attack roll against your target's DV. The starting bonus depends on the weapon's handling.

**Capacity:** This the amount of ammunition the weapon can hold before needing to be reloaded. This is only needed for weapons that require ammunition.

**Critical Range:** Your weapon inflicts its critical effect when your attack roll lands naturally on a number in this range. The starting range is 20. While a natural 20 automatically lands an injury, any other number in the critical range still requires the roll to result in a successful attack in order to deliver the critical effect.

**DV:** Your weapon's DV is used when attacks target it with the sunder maneuver. Your weapon's starting DV depends on its handling.

# Customizing Your Weapon

Weapons vary wildly across the world and throughout history. Their designs are heavily dependent on the resources available, the type of foes they are used against, and the level of knowledge.

When you first create your character, you are permitted to design your own weapon instead of selecting one of the premade weapons listed here. Later on, you need to use the [[Craft]] skill to alter your existing weapon or craft a new one with different statistics.

## 1) Weapon Type

Choose the weapon's type: melee, ranged, thrown.

**Melee:** You can only attack targets with this weapon when they are within your reach. Melee weapons use Strength or Dexterity as their attack modifier. Melee weapons can be thrown with a 10 ft. range increment at a +1 hindrance to attack rolls.

**Ranged:** Ranged weapons fire a projectile and require ammunition. Ranged weapons use Dexterity or Wisdom as their attack modifier. Their starting range increment is 60 ft. Ranged weapons can be used as an improvised melee bludgeoning weapon at a +1 hindrance to attack rolls. Each piece of ammunition costs 1/10 of the weapon's base cost and weighs 1/10 of the weapon's base weight, and is consumed as part of the attack. Ammunition can only be shared between weapons that have the same damage type, handling, size, and weapon group.

**Thrown:** Thrown weapons can be used as a melee weapon, but are also designed to be thrown as a ranged weapon. Thrown weapons use Strength, Dexterity, or Wisdom at their attack modifier. Their starting range increment is 10 ft.

## 2) Handling

Weapons are divided into 3 types of handling: light, one-handed, and two-handed. Each type of handling provides your weapon's base statistics.

**Light Weapons** can be wielded with only one hand, and are designed to be light and nimble, and have a base weight of 1 lb. Wielding a light weapon with two hands provides no additional benefit. Light weapons do not have any circumstance bonus to their attack modifier, but also reduce the number of hindrances from iterative attacks by 1. Light weapons have a starting attack modifier of +0 and a DV of 15.

**One-Handed Weapons** have a starting attack modifier of +1 and a DV of 10, and have a base weight of 4 lbs. They are meant to be wielded with only one hand, but can also be wielded with two hands to increase its circumstance bonus to its attack bonus to +2.

**Two-Handed Weapons** require you to wielded them with two hands, and have a base weight of 10 lbs. Two-handed weapons have starting attack modifier of +3 and a DV of 5. You can still try to attack with a two-handed weapon while wielding it with one hand, but it is treated as an improvised weapon, and it loses its circumstance bonus to its attack modifier and you incur +1 hindrance on your attack roll.

## 3) Choose Your Weapon's Damage Type

Your weapon's damage is one of the following types: bludgeoning, piercing, or slashing. Certain creatures may have defenses or vulnerabilities against specific damage types. Ranged weapons do not have a damage type (aside from its improvised use); instead, you determine its projectile's damage type. Ranged weapon can fire projectiles with different types as long as the projectile is designed for it.

## 4) Customize Your Weapon

You can now modify your weapon's properties with build points (BP). The base cost of the weapon in gp is equal to the squared number of BP spent (a weapon of 0 BP costs 1 sp). The amount of BP you can spend on creating your own weapon is limited to how much gp you are willing to spend or how long you are willing to wait for it to be crafted.

**Attack Modifier:** You can increase your weapon's attack modifier by 1 for every 2 BP spent on this customization. Alternatively, you can decrease it by 1 to gain an additional BP to spend.

**Critical Range:** You can increase your weapon's critical range by 1 for every 2 BP spent on this customization.

**Traits:** You can add traits to your weapon for 2 BP each. Unless otherwise stated, you can only buy a trait once. Some traits may have prerequisites to meet to buy them. You only gain the benefits of a weapon's traits when you wield it.

**DV:** You can increase the weapon's DV by 1 for every 1 BP spent on this customization.

**Weight:** You can reduce the weight of your weapon by 1 lb. for every 1 BP spent on this customization. Reducing a weapon that already weighs 1 lb. reduces it to 1/10 of a pound, and cannot be reduced any further.

#### Attached

A weapon with this trait cannot be disarmed.

#### Brace

**Prerequisites** melee.

A weapon with this trait can be used to brace against a charge as a reaction. The wielder can make a free attack with her weapon against the first creature that charges at her once they become within her reach.

#### Capacity

**Prerequisites** ranged.

**Special** You can take this trait multiple times. Its effects stack.

#### Cleave

**Prerequisies** melee.

Cleave attacks made with a weapon with this trait gain +1 edge.

#### Close

A weapon with this trait can be used while grappled.

#### Concealable

You gain a +1 edge to Sleight of Hand checks to conceal this weapon on your body.

#### Disarm

Disarm attacks made with a weapon with this trait gains +1 edge.

#### Defensive

A weapon with this trait reduces the severity of injuries received by 1 step when wielded while fighting defensively or using the total defense action. Minor injuries are negated.

#### Grapple

Grapple attacks made with a weapon with this trait gains +1 edge.

#### Reach

**Prerequisies** melee.

Your reach for attacks made with a weapon with this trait increases by 1 size step.

#### Thrown

**Prerequisies** melee.

A melee weapon with this trait can be thrown as a ranged attack. Its range increment is equal to 10 feet plus 10 feet per Strength bonus (for a minimum of 10 feet).

#### Trip

Trip attacks made with a weapon with this trait gains +1 edge.

#### Two-Handed

A weapon with this trait is designed to be wielded with either one or two hands. When wielded with two hands, the weapon's attack bonus is increased by +1.

#### Versatile

This trait comes with a damage type. A weapon with this trait can be used to inflict that damage type. You choose which damage type to apply each time you attack.

### Drawbacks

**Drawbacks:** Drawbacks are detrimental customizations. Each drawback reduces your weapon's BP by 1 (to a minimum of 0 BP). Unless otherwise stated, you cannot add a drawback more than once.

#### Attack Penalty

Reduce the weapon's attack modifier by 1.

**Special**This drawback can be taken multiple times. Its effects stack. The weapon's attack modifier can go below +0.

#### Fragile

Decrease the weapon's DV by 1.

**Special** This drawback can be taken multiple times. Its effects stack. The weapon's DV can go below 0.

#### Heavy

Increase the weapon's weight by 1 lb.

**Special** This drawback can be taken multiple times. Its effects stack.

#### Misfire

**Prerequisites** Firearms group.

The weapon misfires on a natural 1 of your attack roll. When this happens, your shot misses, and your weapon gains the broken condition. While broken, another misfire with the weapon causes it to explode and it is destroyed. When it explodes, make a Dexterity save against a DC of 10 + the weapon's total attack bonus. On failure, you receive one injury to one body part that was wielding the weapon.

**Special** This drawback can be taken multiple times. Each time increases the misfire range by 1.

#### Reload Time

**Prerequisites** This weapon consumes ammunition.

The time to reload this weapon is increased by 1 beat.

**Special** This drawback can be taken multiple times. Its effects stack.

#### Two-Handed

This weapon requires two hands to be used, even if it would be considered to be a one-handed or light weapon.

**Amphibious:** This weapon can be wielded without the penalties for fighting in aquatic environments.

**Blunt:** This weapon is only suitable for bludgeoning attacks. Attempting to have this weapon deal piercing or slashing attacks reduces its number of injuries by its rank (down to no injury even on a successful attack).

## 7) Size

Determine what creature size your weapon is appropriate for. Cost and weight assumes the weapon is meant for medium-size creatures by default. Halve the cost and weight for each size smaller, and double the cost and weight for each size larger.

# Weapon Proficiency

You begin with Untrained proficiency in all weapons. When you wield a weapon that you are untrained with, you make attack rolls with +1 hindrance. You can increase your proficiency with a weapon by cultivating the appropriate talent.

At standard proficiency, you no longer have the hindrance from being untrained. At expert, you gain a +1 bonus to attack rolls. This bonus increases by 1 at master, and another 1 at legendary.

To improve your profiency with a weapon, you must cultivate the appropriate talent and select that weapon's group. For example, when you cultivate the [[Standard Proficiency]] talent, you select the sword weapon group. You then gain standard proficiency with all swords.
