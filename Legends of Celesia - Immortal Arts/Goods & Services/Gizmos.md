# Gizmos

Gizmos are special wearable equipment that can be customized to provide different benefits.

Because of the complexity of gizmos, their users have a harder time using them if they are not the ones who crafted them. Each time they activate the gizmo, they must make an Engineering check against a DC of 10 + 1 per CP used in the gizmo. Gizmo users can avoid this check if their Engineering skill proficiency is higher than the gizmo's quality. A special talent is needed to avoid Engineering checks for legendary gizmos.

A gizmo's quality is based on how many CP is used to build it. Standard gizmos have up to 5 CP, expert gizmos have up to 10 CP, master gizmos have up to 20 CP, and legendary gizmos have over 20 CP.
