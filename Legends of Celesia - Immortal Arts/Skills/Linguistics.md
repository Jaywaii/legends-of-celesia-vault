# Linguistics

Linguistics is your ability to learn and recognize languages, to write and decipher script, and to create and detect written forgeries.

# Untrained Uses

## Recognize Language (Untrained)

After spending a minute listening or reading a language you do not know, you can attempt a check to recognize it. You do not necessarily know the name of this language, but you are now able to recognize it should you encounter it again at a later time. Mortal languages have a DC of 25, while outsider languages have a DC of 35.

**Action:** 1 minute.

**Retry?** Yes, after spending another minute listening or reading the language.

# Trained Uses

## Learn Language (Trained)

To learn a new language, you need a language resource and spend time learning as a downtime activity. At the end of the day, make your check. On success, you learn 1% of the language. Every 10 that you best the DC gains you an additional 1%. Repeat until you reach 100%, at which point you learn to speak and write the language. The base DC to learn a new language is 35, and is modified by the type of language resource you are using during the action.

You must have at least Intelligence +1 to perform this action. You can learn a number of languages up to twice your Intelligence bonus.

### Table: Learning Language DC

| Language Resource | DC Modifier |
|---|---|
| Texts written in the language | +0 |
| Passively listening to the language | -10 |
| Being taught by someone who knows the language | -20 |

**Action:** 1 downtime activity.

**Retry?** Yes; each retry requires 1 downtime activity.
