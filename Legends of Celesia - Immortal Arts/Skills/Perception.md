# Perception

**Attribute:** Wisdom.

# Untrained Actions

## Notice

### Table: Perception DC Modifiers

| Conditions | DC Modifiers |
|---|---|
| Distance from the stimulus | +1/10 feet. |

**Action:** 1 standard.

**Retry?** No.

**Proficiency:**

- *Trained:* The DC modifier from the distance from the stimulus condition is now +1/20 feet.
- *Expert:* The DC modifier from the distance from the stimulus condition is now +1/30 feet.
- *Master:* The DC modifier from the distance from the stimulus condition is now +1/60 feet.
- *Legendary:* The DC modifier from the distance from the stimulus condition is now +1/90 feet.

## Insight

This is your ability to intuit what another creature is thinking or if they are trying to deceive you by observing their behaviour or making sense of what they are communicating to you.

You must consciously choose to make this check. Other creatures make checks against your passive Perception to deceive you.

**Action:** It takes one minute of focus and attention that provokes to gain insight into the subject. You can avoid provoking by increasing the DC by 10. You can reduce the time to focus to one full action by increasing the DC by 10, to one standard action by increasing the DC by 20, or to one simple action by increasing the DC by 30. The increases to DC from avoiding provoking and reducing the action time stack with each other and must be chosen before making the check.

**Retry?** No.

**Proficiency:**

- *Trained:* At the start of any social interaction, you may make a Perception check as a free action. Any participant in the encounter attempting to use Deception against you uses your result as their DC intead of your passive Perception.
- *Expert:* You can attempt to evaluate the prowess of another creature in relation to you. The DC is equal to 10 + the creature's Deception modifier. On success, you learn how many levels higher or lower the target is compared to you. On failure, the number of levels you learn are inversed.
- *Master:* You can attempt to determine if a creature's behaviour is magically influenced or controlled using only your intuition alone. The DC is equal to 10 + the magic's realm + the magic's caster tier. If there are multiple sources of magical influence at the same time on the same target, use the magic with the highest combined value of its realm and its caster tier. On success, you learn how many magical influences are affecting the target, each of their realms, and each of their caster tier.
- *Legendary:* You are able to read the surface thoughts of another creature. The DC is equal to 20 + the target's Deception modifier. You're ability to read the target's surface thoughts lasts for one minute, plus an additional minute for each successful degree.
