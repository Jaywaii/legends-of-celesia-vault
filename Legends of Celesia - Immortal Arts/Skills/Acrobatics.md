# Acrobatics

**Attribute:** Dexterity.

### Acrobatics Terrain Conditions

The following terrain conditions incurs +1 hindrance on all Acrobatics checks. Overlapping conditions stack hindrances.

- Difficult
- Slippery
- Sloped
- Unsteady

# Untrained Actions

## Balance

Acrobatics can be used to traverse narrow or uneven surfaces without falling, or remaining upright on unstable grounds. When you perform the move action, you can choose to make an Acrobatics check to balance throughout all movement in this action. While you balance, you move at half speed, and become off-guarded. You can move at full speed by choosing to increase the DC by 10, and you can avoid becoming off-guarded by also increasing the DC by 10. These increases stack with each other. You must choose either or both increases before you move and make your check.

If you fail your check, you do not make any progress. If you fail by 1 degree or more, you fall prone and off of the narrow surface. If the surface you are moving across becomes narrower enough to increase the DC and your previous check was not high enough to succeed against it, you must make a new check against the new DC.

### Table: Narrow Surface DC

| Surface Width | Base Acrobatics DC |
|---|---|
| 1 to 3 feet (1/2 sq.) | 15 |
| 6 inches to 1 foot (1/4 sq.) | 25 |
| 1 to 5 inches (1/8 sq.) | 35 |
| Less than 1 inch (1/16 sq.) | 45 |

**Action:** Made as part of a Move action.

**Retry?** Yes, but each retry costs 5 feet of the current Move action. When there is no more feet left in the current Move action, a new Move action must taken.

**Proficiency:**

- *Trained:* Reduce the DC increase for moving at full speed or removing the off-guard condition by 5.
- *Expert:* Reduce the DC increase for moving at full speed or removing the off-guard condition by 10.
- *Master:* You may make an Acrobatics check at a -10 penalty in place of a save against falling.
- *Legendary:* You are able to balance on any solid surface even if it would not normally support your weight. However, the Acrobatics DC is increased by 10.

## Disarm

You make a special melee attack that does not deal damage, but does target a weapon wielded by an opponent. A disarm attack requires you to make an Acrobatics check against your opponents DV in place of your melee attack roll. On success, your opponent drops the weapon at their feet. This special attack provokes against the opponent you are trying to disarm.

**Action:** One melee attack.

**Retry?** No.

**Proficiency:**

- *Trained:* You do not provoke when making a disarm attack.
- *Expert:* Your DV against disarm attacks increases to +1.
- *Master:* Your DV against disarm attacks increases to +2.
- *Legendary:*On a successful disarm, the opponent's defence is broken open and they provoke against you. This occurs after they have lost their weapon.

## Escape

Your nimbleness helps you escape bindings, grapples, and confined spaces.

**Action:** 1 standard.

**Retry?** Varies.

### Table: Escape Actions and DCs

| Action | DC | Retry? |  |
|---|---|---|---|
| Escape rope/bindings | Binder's Dexterity check | No, unless there is a change in the state of the binding. |  |
| Escape grapple | Grappler's grapple check | You make only one escape attempt against each grapple check made against you. |  |
| Escape manacles | Based on manacle's quality | No, unless there is a change in the state of the binding. |  |
|  |  |  |  |

**Proficiency:**

- *Trained:* You may take a -10 penalty to reduce the action's time to a simple action. You may choose to gain a +5 bonus on your check for each additional simple action spent on this action. You must choose either of these options before starting your action.
- *Expert:* The penalty to reduce the action's time is reduced to -5. Your bonus for additional simple actions is increased to +10.
- *Master:* The time to perform this action is reduced to a simple action at no penalty to your check.
- *Legendary:* You may take a -10 penalty to perform this action as a free action or as a reaction.

## Fly

If you possess the ability to fly, either with wings or other appendages, or through magic, this skill helps you perform complex manoeuvres while airborne.

### Injured While Flying

When you fly using wings and you receive one or more injuries, either from an attack or colliding with an obstacle of your size or larger, you immediately make a check to avoid plummeting down and take falling damage as long as your wings are not crippled or worse. The DC is equal to 10 + 10 per injury received.

### Flying Up and Down

Flying upwards costs you 10 ft. of movement per 5 ft. of elevation, while flying downwards costs you 2.5 feet of movement per 5 ft. of descent (assuming normal gravity and still wind conditions).

### Wind Effects on Flying

**Checked Size:** Flying creatures of this size or smaller must make a check when taking the Move action. Failing the check prevents them from moving and uses up the Move action.

**Blown Away Size:** Flying creatures of this size or smaller must make a check at the start of their turn. Failure results in the creature being blown 2d6 x 10 feet in the direction of the wind, and take stamina damage equal to the listed dice. If the creature crashes into an obstacle, treat it as if the creature fell that amount of distance between the creature's starting position and the obstacle to determine if they suffer any injuries injuries.

The DC for either being checked or blown away is listed in the last column of the table below.

| Wind Strength | Checked Size | Blown Away Size | DC |
|---|---|---|---|
| Strong | Tiny | — | 20 |
| Severe | Small | Tiny (1d6) | 30 |
| Windstorm | Medium | Small (2d6) | 40 |
| Hurricane | Large | Medium (3d6) | 50 |
| Tornado | Huge | Large (4d6) | 60 |

**Action:** Made as part of a Move action or in response to effect while flying.

**Retry?** No (see proficiency).

**Proficiency:**

- *Trained:* The cost to fly upwards costs 5 feet less (to a minimum of 5 feet).
- *Expert:*If you fail a check to keep flying after being injured, you can make another check as a reaction to stop your fall after falling 2d6 x 10 feet.
- *Master:* You can make checks after falling 1d6 x 10 feet from being injured , and can repeatedly try again after falling an additional 1d6 x 10 feet with each failure. Each check spends a reaction.
- *Legendary:* You count as three sizes larger when determining wind effects on flying.

## Squeeze Through

You use your nimbleness to squeeze through tight spaces. A tight space is any space smaller than your space. Moving through such a space halves your movement speed and makes you off-guarded. Starting from DC 0, each step the tight space is smaller than your space adds 10 to the DC. A space's step is measured in 5 feet (1 square) for creatures with a space of 10 feet and larger. Creature spaces with a space of 5 feet or smaller measure smaller steps in fractions of a square, with each step being half the size of the next higher step (so 5 feet > 1/2 square > 1/4 square > 1/8 square, and so on).

You can move at full speed by increasing the DC by 10, and you can avoid becoming off-guarded by also increasing the DC by 10. You must choose either or both of these increases before you move and make your check. These increases stack with each other.

If you fail your check, you do not make any progress. If the space you are moving through becomes smaller to increase the DC and your previous check was not high enough to succeed against it, you must make a new check against the new DC.

**Action:** Made as part of a Move action.

**Retry?** Yes, but each retry costs 5 feet of the current Move action. When there is no more feet left in the current Move action, a new Move action must taken.

**Proficiency:**

- *Trained:* Reduce the DC increase for moving at full speed or removing the off-guard condition by 5.
- *Expert:* You may choose to gain a +5 bonus on your check for each 10 ft. reduction of your current Move action. You must choose this before making your check.
- *Master:* Your Move action reduction is reduced to 5 ft. per +5 bonus.
- *Legendary:* Your bonus for reducing your current Move action is increased to +10 per reduction.

## Trip

You make a special melee attack that does not deal damage, but does trip your opponent opponent. A trip attack requires you to make an Acrobatics check against your opponents DV in place of your melee attack roll. On success, your opponent falls prone. This special attack provokes against the opponent you are trying to trip.

**Action:** One melee attack.

**Retry?** No.

**Proficiency:**

- *Trained:* You do not provoke when making a trip attack.
- *Expert:* You can attempt a check against trip attacks against you as a reaction, using your result in place of your DV.
- *Master:* When you successfully trip an opponent, they provoke against you before they fall prone.
- *Legendary:* When you successfully trip an opponent, you can have them fall prone 5 feet away from their original location in a direction of your choice. You can have them fall an additional 5 feet in the same direction for every degree of success.

# Trained Actions

## Tumble

When you perform the move action, you can choose to tumble in order to move through threatened squares without provoking from enemies, and even move through squares occupied by an opponent. The DC to move threatened spaces without provoking is equal to 10 + the opponent's attack bonus, while moving through their space is 20 + the opponent's attack bonus. You make one Acrobatics check at the start of your move action, and it is checked against each opponent that would be provoked individually. On failure, you provoke against that opponent as usual for moving within their threatened area. If you fail when trying to move through an opponent's space, your move action ends, and you provoke against that opponent as normal as well. You move at half speed when you tumble, but you can choose to move at full speed by increasing the DC by 10; you must choose to do this before tumbling and making your check.

**Proficiency:**

- *Expert:*
	- The DC to move through threatened spaces is reduced by 10.
	- The DC to move through an opponent's space is reduced by 5.
	- The DC increase to tumble at full speed is reduced by 5.
- *Master:*
	- The DC to move through threatened spaces is reduced by 20.
	- The DC to move through an opponent's space is reduced by 10.
	- The DC does not increase when you tumble at your full speed.
- *Legendary:*
	- The DC to move through threatened spaces is reduced by 30.
	- The DC to move through an opponent's space is reduced by 15.
