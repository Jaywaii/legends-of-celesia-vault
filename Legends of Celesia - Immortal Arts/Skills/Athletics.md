# Athletics

**Attribute:** Strength.

The Athletics skill represents your physical prowess.

# Untrained Actions

## Climb

This is your ability to move across sloped and vertical surfaces. This includes going up, down, and across. This skill is also used to traverse across a ceiling provided there are means to hold onto it. You must have two hands free and arms that are not crippled or destroyed in order to climb, but you can use one hand while clinging and not moving.

While climbing, you move at half-speed and are off-guarded. You can choose to move at full speed by increasing the DC by 10, and avoid being off-guarded by increasing the DC by 10 as well. You must choose to do either or both before your make your check. These increases stack with each other.

Failing your check prevents you from moving and wastes your Move action. Failing by one degree or more results in you falling.

### Table: Climb DC Examples

| DC | Example Surface or Activity |
|---|---|
| 5 | A knotted rope with a wall to brace against, a ladder. |
| 15 | An unknotted rope with a wall to brace against, a knotted rope on its own. |
| 25 | A vertical surface with usable handholds (natural or artificial) such as a natural rock surface or a tree, an unknotted rope on its own, pulling yourself up from a dangling position. |
| 35 | An uneven surface with narrow handholds, such as the wall inside of a dungeon or of a building. |
| 45 | An overhang or ceiling with handholds. |

### Injuries While Climbing

When you receive an injury, make a Climb check against the surface's DC. Increase the DC by 10 for each additional injury beyond the first received from the same event. Failure results in you losing your grip and falling.

### Wind Effects on Climbing

**Checked Size:** Climbing creatures of this size or smaller must make a check when taking the Move action. Failing the check prevents them from moving and uses up the Move action.

**Blown Away Size:** Climbing creatures of this size or smaller must make a check at the start of their turn. Failure results in the creature being blown off the climbing surface or tool, and begins falling.

Add the modifier listed in the table below to the climbing surface or tool's DC as the DC to roll against being checked or blown away.

| Wind Strength | Checked Size | Blown Away Size | DC Modifier |
|---|---|---|---|
| Strong | Tiny | — | +10 |
| Severe | Small | Tiny | +20 |
| Windstorm | Medium | Small | +30 |
| Hurricane | Large | Medium | +40 |
| Tornado | Huge | Large | +50 |

**Action:** Made as part of a Move action or in response to an effect.

**Retry?** No.

**Proficiency:**

- *Trained:* The DC increase to move at full speed or avoid being off-guarded is reduced by 5.
- *Expert:* When falling, you are able to catch yourself as long as the climbing surface or tool is within reach. After falling 2d6 x 10 feet, you are able to make another Climb check. On success, you stop falling, but you must make a Constitution save against the surface's DC or one of your arms of your choice receives an injury. You can keep retrying to catch yourself after falling 2d6 x feet after each failure.
- *Master:* You are able to make a Climb check to catch yourself after only falling 5 feet.
- *Legendary:* You are able to climb with only one free hand.

## Grapple

You make an Athletics check in place of an attack roll to make a grapple attack that grabs hold of your opponent. This special attack deals no damage, but imposes the [[Grappled|grappled]] condition on both you and your opponent. When you succeed on your grapple attack, you are the grapple's initiator and have control of the grapple.

When you have control of the grapple, you must make an additional grapple attack at the start of your turn against your opponent to maintain it. When you succeed at the grapple attack to maintain it, you continue to maintain control of it over your opponent, and at the same time perform one of the following actions as a free action.

**Drag:** You can drag your opponent to another square adjacent to you. You may move at half-speed and drag your grappled opponent with you and have him end in a square adjacent to you of your choice. Your opponent does not provoke when being dragged, but you provoke as normal if you choose to move.

**Inflict Injury:** Inflict 1 injury on your opponent.

**Pin:** You

**Action:** 1 standard.

**Retry?** No.

**Proficiency:**

- *Trained:* You do not provoke when making a grapple attack.
- *Expert:* Initiating and maintaining a grapple attack now takes 1 simple action.
- *Master:* When someone initiates a grapple against you, you can contest it with your own grapple attack against them as a reaction. If your attack roll beats theirs, you can choose to avoid the grapple, or take control of it.
- *Legendary:* You do not gain the grappled condition while grappling an opponent as long as you control the grapple.

## Jump

This is your ability to leap into the air and land back safely You jump as part of a Move action, however your action ends when you land even if you have feet of movement remaining. Movement from your jump provokes.

The amount of distance you jump depends on the result of your check. You need to move at least 10 feet in the same direction of your jump, or else your check's result is halved.

### Long jump

You jump ahead  a number of feet equal to your check's result.

### High jump

You jump up a number of feet equal to your check's result divided by 5.

**Reach Up and Grab:** The maximum height of a ledge or handhold you can reach above you is based on your melee reach + the number of feet you jumped up.

### Safe Fall:

When falling, you can make a DC 20 Athletics check to treat the fall as if it were 10 feet shorter. For every successful degree, treat the fall an additional 10 feet shorter.

**Action:** Made as part of a Move action.

**Retry?** No.

**Proficiency:**

- *Trained:* You only need to move 5 feet to avoid having your check's result being halved.
- *Expert:* You can continue your Move action after you jump if you have remaining movement. You cannot jump again during the remaining movement.
- *Master:* You do not need to move before a jump to avoid your check's results being halved.
- *Legendary:* When you make a successful check to fall safely, treat the fall as 30 ft. shorter instead of 10 ft., and an additional 30 ft. for every successful degree.

## Shove

You make an Athletics check in place of an attack roll to make a shove attack that moves an opponent 5 feet away from you. This special attack deals no damage. The opponent's movement from this special attack does not provoke. Every successful degree increases the distance the opponent is shoved by an additional 5 feet.

**Action:** 1 standard.

## Swim

This is your ability to traverse through a liquid medium. You must have two legs and two arms that are not crippled or destroyed to swim.You may hold items in your hands while swimming.

While swimming, you move at half-speed and are off-guarded. You can choose to move at full speed by increasing the DC by 10, and avoid being off-guarded by increasing the DC by 10 as well. You must choose to do either or both before your make your check. These increases stack with each other.

Failing your check prevents you from moving and wastes your Move action. Failing by one degree or more results in you falling 10 feet deeper in the liquid.

### Water Force on Swimming

**Checked Size:** Creatures of this size or smaller must make a check when taking the Move action. Failing the check prevents them from moving and uses up the Move action.

**Washed Away Size:** Creatures of this size or smaller must make a check at the start of their turn. Failure results in the creature being washed along the liquid's current by 2d6 x 10 feet and take the listed stamina damage.

The DC against being checked or washed away is listed in the last column in the table below.

| Water Force | Checked Size | Blown Away Size | DC Modifier |
|---|---|---|---|
| Calm | Tiny | —) | 10 |
| Rough | Small | Tiny (1d6) | 20 |
| Stormy | Medium | Small (2d6) | 30 |
| Whirlpool | Large | Medium (3d6) | 40 |

**Action:** Made as part of a Move action or in response to an effect.

**Retry?** No.

**Proficiency:**

- *Trained:* The DC increase to move at full speed or avoid being off-guarded is reduced by 5.
- *Expert:* You are able to make melee attacks while in or under water as if you were on land.
- *Master:* You are able to swim with only two legs that are not crippled or destroyed. Swimming with two arms grants you a +5 bonus to your check.
- *Legendary:* You do not need legs or arms to swim. Swimming with two arms grans you a +5 bonus to your check, and swimming with two legs also grants you a +5 bonus. These bonuses stack with each other.
