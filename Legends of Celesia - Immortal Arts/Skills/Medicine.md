# Medicine

**Attribute:** Intelligence or Wisdom.

Medicine is your knowledge of anatomy, diseases, healing techniques, medicinal remedies, and your ability to them all together properly to help a subject heal and recover from ailments.

Certain actions can only be performed when you have a free hand and a medicine kit within reach.

# Untrained Actions

## Treat Bleeding

Reduce your target's bleeding by 1 degree. When the bleeding reaches 0 degrees, the condition is removed. The DC is 15, each degree of bleeding beyond the first adds 5 to the DC. This action requires a medicine kit.

**Action:** 1 standard provoke action.

**Retry?** Yes.

Proficiency:

- *Trained:* You no longer provoke when performing this action.
- *Expert:* Reduce the bleeding by 2 degrees with one success.
- *Master:* You can treat bleeding as a simple action.
- *Legendary:* Reduce the bleeding by 3 degrees with one success.

## Treat Disease

You can help a subject recover from a disease by treating them before they take a full rest. After a full rest, the subject makes a save against the disease with a +1 circumstance bonus if you succeed your check, and the bonus increases by 1 for each successful degree. The DC is the disease's DC.

**Action:** 10 minutes; once per full rest per subject.

**Retry? No.**
