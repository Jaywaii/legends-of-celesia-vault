# Intimidation

**Attribute:** Strength or Charisma.

### Target DC

The DC Intimidation checks are made against is equal to 10 + the target's level + the target's Wisdom modifier.

# Untrained Actions

## Coerce

You are able to force a person to be compliant to your demands. Upon success, the target acts as though they have a Friendly attitude towards you for 1d6 hours + 1 hour per successful degree. However, their real attitude towards you drops by one step.

**Action:** 1 minute of conversation with the target.

**Retry?** Yes, but each failure increases the DC by 10.

**Proficiency:**

- *Trained:* The duration of your coercion is doubled.
- *Expert:* The duration of your coercion changes to 1d4 days + 1 day per successful degree.
- *Master:* The duration of your coercion changes to 1d4 weeks + 1 week per successful degree.
- *Legendary:* The duration of your coercion changes to 1d4 months + 1 month per successful degree.

## Demoralize

You intimidate your opponent into being [[Frightened|frightened]]. Each successful degree of your skill check increases the frightened condition's degree by 1.

If you cannot communicate with the target, the DC increases by 10. Mindless creatures are immune to this skill's action.

**Action:** 1 standard action.

**Retry?** Yes, but each failed attempt increases the DC by 10.

**Proficiency:**

- *Trained:* The action to demoralize is reduced to a simple action.
- *Expert:* Upon success, the target suffers Frightened 2.
- *Master:* The DC no longer increases when you are not able to communicate with the target.
- *Legendary:* Upon success, the target suffers Frightened 4.
