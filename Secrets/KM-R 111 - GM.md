## Assassination!

Montari Entreprises have allied with the Coil to gain dominance over Lurindor. Enzio Montari himself is a recent, fast rising member of the secret society. While Enzio becomes the ruler of Lurindor, the Coil gains a wealthy nation to fund their operations. In order to do so, one of the goals they need to accomplish is to topple the Sovrani Syndicate. They plan on using [[Elvia Sovrani]]'s presence in Prateria to simultaneously eliminate her and set the Syndicate against the Teredoro and Prateria. As Rochefort is already an unofficial enemy of the Syndicate, adding the new colony of Prateria and the powerful Teredoro Consortium as foes will weakend their position further.

The DC to detect the assassins' actions is the nation's Control DC+30. Prateria's security forces can succeed in interrupting if the check reaches Stability+40, but the assassins get away. The assassins' get caught by security if the check reaches Stability+50, in which they commit suicide, leaving no one the PCs can interrogate, but remains of physical evidence of the plot. If the check reaches Stability+60, then they succeed in capturing 1d4 Coil agents alive. However, all of the Coil agents modified their memory to believe they are working with Teredoro Consortium against Sovrani Syndicate.

A written set of orders from "-M" can be found on the assassins if they are caught. They are somwhat vauge, only stating that the goal is to assassinate Elvia Sovrani on behalf of their "friend" and to implicate Prateria and their friend's rival as the backers behind the plot. Again, they end with "I.C.E.".

## Tournament Events

During the Viridian Games, the Evergreen Company has the chance to increase their renown. For every 10 points of Renown, Prateria gains +1 Fame with all factions in Lurindor. However, their Fame is reduced by 1 if their Renown is 0 or less, and reduced by another 1 for every 10 below 0, as the nation becomes ridiculed for how they mismanaged their games.
- One way to gain renown is to sway each of the dignitaries. Minor sway = 1 Renown, Moderate sway = 2, and Major sway = 3.

## Tournament

- The Viridian Games represents using the Festival Edict 8 times. It costs Prateria 8d6 BP to prepare Promethea to host the Games and the invited guests, and the prizes of the Games. At the last day of the Games, Prateria's Ruler rolls for the Games' success. Unlike a normal festival edicts, the effects of the civic festival last for an entire year instead of 1 month. If the Games are a resounding success, Promethea gains either the benefits of an Outstanding Success (01-50), Visiting Celebrity (51-95), or both (96-00) nation events for each of the next 12 months. If the festival is a semi-failure, the 50% chance that disgruntled citizens issue a Building Demand occurs for each of the next 6 months. If the festival is a complete failure, Prateria loses -5 Fame instead of -1, gains 1d4 Unrest and Squatters (01-50), Vandals (51-95), or both (96-00) nation events for each of the next 6 months. For every 10 points of their Renown score at the end of the Games, the Ruler gets a +1 bonus to each edict roll to determine success.If their Renown score is below 0, they instead get a -1 penalty, increasing by 1 for every 10 points below 0.
- There is an influx of visitors to Promethea in the week coming up to the Viridian Games. The Emerald Hotel and Promethea's remaining inns are filled to capacity with high-ranking members of Lurindor's Trade houses, Baron Theodore Duloup of Grondeloup, Baron Rafael Drelevo of Drelevona, and their each personal retinue of aids, servants, guards, and champions. However, some of Lurindor's envoys, more precisely those of the Sovrani Syndicate, Sotillini Cartel, and the church of Kimylia make their stay at Lurindor's embassy. The representatives of the church of Kaï-den make their stay at the Cathedral, while the remaining church representatives make camp around their respective shrines.
- Envoys were sent south to Carpathos, and one was sent west to Lorthens. However, no response was returned. More ominously, the envoys have not yet returned themselves.
- It is a chilly, overcast day when the opening ceremony for the Viridian Games begins. Despite the cold weather, the streets and seats are packed with people in warm, winter clothing. Promethea's merchants are out in force to show off and sell their wares. Street stalls and their hawkers selling warm food and drinks are partiularly popular. Much of the public walk the streets with cups of hot teas and soups, while those with extra coin opt for more exotic coffees and cocoa. Steaming warm pies and stick meats are enjoyed by all. The brothel and dance halls enjoy a significant amount of activity.
- The Cypress Memorial Grove is especially a wonderful sight. Sister Félicité, working with the dryads, have transformed the grove into an amazing sight of blooming spring, a resplendence of bright colors and exotic smells. Sweet treats evoking the tastes of the Otherworld are available to all. Dryads and fairies continuously play enchanting music, welcoming all to dance at their hearts' content.

## Prateria Events

### Tierlan 111

*The immigration crisis continued to worsen. Despite new homes being built, more followers kept arriving to take over the land squatting of the previous, now housed, group of followers. Worse, the squallid conditions of the tent city were excellent breeding grounds for a plague to break out! Typhoid fever breaks out in the camps, and spreads quickly. Rishoi's hospital and the Madonna's Cathedral are quickly rushed by the infected, and the disease spreads throughout Promethea.*

Plague (Hex or Settlement, Continuous): A deadly sickness strikes the target hex or settlement. You cannot construct terrain improvements or buildings there while plague persists. Attempt two Stability checks, each with a penalty equal to the number of Brothels, Foreign Quarters, Highways, Inns, Piers, Roads, Stables, Stockyards, Tenements, and Waterfronts in the hex, and a bonus equal to the number of ss, Cathedrals, Herbalists, Hospitals, and Temples in the hex. If you succeed at both checks, the event ends, but Stability decreases by 2 for 1 year and Treasury by 1d3 BP. If you fail at one check, Stability decreases by 4 for 1 year, Treasury decreases by 1d6 BP, and Unrest increases by 1d3. If you fail at both, Stability decreases by 4 for 1 year, Treasury decreases by 1d6 BP, Unrest increases by 1d6, and in the next Event Phase the plague spreads to an adjacent hex.

### Asmolan 111

*The sight of pitched tents and wagons made into makeshift shelters were now becoming a common sight in Promethea. It seemed that the fame of the Evergreen Company was coming back to haunt them. As new settlers entered the new homes built for them, another group of immigrants were quick to take their makeshift shelters in the squatted lands. However, it was not all bad news. Davona's police force had managed to prevent an attack on the dryads of Memorial Park. The Radimite zealots were back at it again, preying upon the malcontent new arrivals in the tent city to convert them to their point of view. However, loyal followers among the camps alerted the Evergreen Company of the plot, and Davona's guard arrested several influential people, crippling the organization of the zealots for some time to come.*

Unable to build new buildings in Promethea; Fame and Stability decrease by 1 for 1 year, and Unrest increases by 2. You may try to disperse the squatters with a Stability check. Success means the squatters are dispersed, the penalties are removed, and the event is not continuous. However, if a House or Tenement is not built by the next Event Phase, Infamy increases by 1 for 1 year and Unrest by 2. Failing the Stability check means the event is continuous, and you may not construct new buildings in that settlement until the event is resolved.
