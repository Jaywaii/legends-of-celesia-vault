# Notes

- Legio II, led by Davona, arrives at the Greenbelt River. They discover at their desired location of crossing that there is already a bridge there, a double-leaf bascule bridge. A worn and faded sign stands at the entrance; "Nettle's Crossing—5 silver—ring bell for service" can still be read on it. Sky recalls hearing about a Rochefort engineer by the name of Davik Nettles. Years ago, in anticipation of the future settling of the Evergreen Plains, Nettles built this bridge. Something of a miser, the engineer did not care who used his bridge as long as they paid. However, the local hunters have not heard from Nettle's after banditry became rampant in the region. It is now obvious that the bridge has not been maintained for some time. It is stuck in its open position, and the gears and pulleys controlling it have broken down. Jupus, however, determines that there are enough spare parts around to fix it, given a day. Davona inquires about having the bridge only controlled from their side of the river, to prevent Klorgfang from using it on their end. Jupus says it is possible, but will require more resources to modifiy the bridge. Investigating the control room, Jupus is assaulted by the stench of rot and decay. Within the room lies a rotten, but intact, corpse of a man. When Jupus enters the room, the corpse wakens, and shambles up to stand, clutching a mean-looking ranseur. In a moaning and haunting voice, the corpse speaks and says *"You are not my tormentors. Throw the Stag Lord’s body into the river that I may look upon his death, or join me instead."*. Jupus, and then Davona, tries to explain to him that the Stag Lord is dead, but Nettles' corpse merely repeats his warning.
- After consulting with Arina via the Consul's mythic senses and scrying, Legio II will resolve the Nettles situation later. Instead, Legio II will change course and occupy the fort east of them. Cortez's intelligence gathering indicated that the fort across the river is unoccupied. It was likely the fort that the hill giant Arzhulig sortied with his army from to assault Silvermine. Legio III, led by Arina, will continue southeast along the Greenbelt. Cortez had learned that a Klorgfang army now occupies the remains of the Stag Lord's fort. The Evergreen Company decides to eliminate this threat, and look for the Stag Lord's corpse to resolve Nettles' torment.
- Legio II occupies Arzhulig's fort without issue. Along the way, they come across evidence of Klorgfang's cruelty. Although their occupants have now fled in anticipation of the army's passing, Legio II discovers manacles, cages, and tools of torture used by Klorgfang to run their slave farms. They even discover bodies of the old and weak along the roads; human, halfling, and elf slaves who their overseers decided were better off dead than slowing their flight. Davona silently seethes at the discover of all of this. Once Legio II occupies the fort, Davona orders her forces to occupy the lands directly to the east and west fo the fort, to destroy the slave farms, and liberate the slaves.
- Two days later, Legio II's scouts soon discover that an army from the west is now approaching them. Led by a minotaur, it is likely the army that was Legio II's previous target. However, their numbers seems to have doubled since Cortez's last report.
- Meanwhile, Legio III finally arrives at the Stag Lord's fort. Grimly, it seems that Klorgfang had spent resources rebuilding it. Once a ruined stronghold, a fully reinforced and occupied fortress now stands before the Praterian army.

- Menakedes' Horde is currently stationed at Fort Ironhorn.
