# Notes

### Lonán and Dragormir

Things they know about the dungeon:

- There are multiple rooms, but they are not directly connected. Instead, each room has 2 portals that are each one-way: an entrance and an exit.
- Travelers can travel through the exit portals by unlocking it by an action that Vorakios and his lieutenants know and are capable of. For anyone else, the act is extremely vile that only the most depraved persons would be willing to do it.
- However, the magic can be manipulated with great enough skill. Lonán and her companions were able to work together to accomplish this, but Vorakios has since modified the portals that past manipulations no longer work, and Lonán's companions are now dead.
- Their current location is the Fairy Garden, which is sort of the connecting "hallway" between each main room of the dungeon. Failure to pass through to a desired room through the portal results in the traveler ending up in the Garden.
- Vorakios resides in the heart of the dungeon, which is even more locked away from the rest of the dungeon. However, as Vorakios has integrated himself as the Fairy Grave's dungeon master, it could be possible to manipulate the exit portal's magic to open up to the dungeon. Lonán theorizes that to do so, they must find and discern aspects of Vorakios. Such aspects have integrated into each room of the Dungeon. Lonán and Dragormir have not yet uncovered even a single one aspect as each room is either defended by Vorakios' servants or have hazards that the pair are currently unable to overcome.
- As long as Vorakios remains at the Heart of the Fairy Grave, he can expand the planar dungeon's impact on the material plane. With the Thunderhoof wards now broken, and Lonán's own warding also broken, Vorakios can expand the Fairy Grave's physical presence more and more in the region, turning the land into a growing area of blighted Faerie capable of birthing swarms of vile fey and undead.
