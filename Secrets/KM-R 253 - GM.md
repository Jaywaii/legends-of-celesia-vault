# Notes

## City of Cenatoth

- You find yourselves within a dark and damp twisted complex. All around you is a network of dark organic tunnels, not just ahead, behind, left or right, but also heading above and below. All of the walls and ground and ceiling appear very organic, like a mess of tendrils, webbing, covered in a layer of fluids, and pulsing every now and then.
- A Somnus calls out to the PCs. They are embedded in a column, wrapped in tendrils, partially merged with them. Their silver raven-like mask, like that of a plague doctor, remains mostly visible, only partially fusing with the environment around the edges. They speak with an eerily metallic voice.
	- *"Beware, dreamwalkers, for you enter a realm of nightmares beyond your comprehension. Leave now, lest your minds break and become one with Cenatoth."*
	- *"The foul minds of the thul-koon have twisted this place into a reflection of the madness within them."*
	- *"You are not prepared for the horrors in this accursed place."*
	- *"Beware the mindlurker. They were once phobetors of the Dreamlands, now corrupted. The madness melted their forms, and they coalesced into horrific amorphous masses. The mindlurker is the greatest among them."*
- PCs need to navigate a labyrinth of horrors. The thul-koon thoughts manifest as a haunt. Defeating the haunt allows it to be captured by the dreamcatcher.
- Navigating requires 3 successful challenges in a row (DC 30)
	1. Obstacles like gaps, spikes, shifting structures: Acrobatics
	1. Search by senses: Perception
	1. Avoid the Mindlurker: Stealth (fight against gibbering abomination, DC 23 for 6 San dmg)
	1. Navigating the maze: Survival
- Failure resets the count and inflicts 2d4 Sanity dmg (DC 25 Will for half). When finally successful, still take 2d4 San dmg (DC 25 will for 1/2).
- When the PCs find the haunt and it awakens and begins attacking the PCs, this alerts the aberrant phobetors in the area (gibbering mouthers; DC 15 for 2 San dmg).
	- tendrils wrap 9 semi-translucent figures currently being experimented on, having their chest and heads carved open and tools probing and picking at their insides. the probing tendrils come from a central column
	- sucks party into tentacle area via telekinesis (DC 30 Will save) and prone. save still enters the area, but just on the edge of tentacles.
	- end the haunt by freeing the captives (100 hp) and destroying the center (300 hp)
	- every 1d4 rounds, a telekinetic wave pulls and pushes the PCs, trying to knock them down.
	- tentacles have CMB +25, 3d4+15 dmg
	- center column of brains and tentacles: SR 20, DR 30/armor.

## Sea of Mulemria

- Barloth-keem: a port city on the Sarmassus Shore. It is a vast city replete with tall, sandy towers connected with numerous bridges and platforms, covered by hanging gardens. A cornucopia of rich, spicy smells reaches your nose. The loud hubbub of a vibrant and active populace fills the air, accented by music echoing in the background.
- Players need to find transport to take them to the Sea of Mulemria. Xura Nythagoi can help.
- Nythagoi's ship, the *Ruby Siren*, is captained by Gaxxio Lethghul. The ship gets attacked by the Zorathigon, a giant tentacle sea monster. The tentacles have bulges on them that occasionally slide open to reveal slimy eyeballs.
- Some tentacles wrap around the ship. Others slam down onto the ship to hit crew, then try to slam and grab nearest crew. Killing over half of the tentacles releases the ship.
