# Notes

## Viriquia

At midnight, Sky receives a sending from Iolanthe: "Scouts report new fog tendril moving fast south towards Radimus."

The deathmist tendril reached Viriquia at 10 pm, catching its defenders unaware, consuming hundreds of people and unleashing thousands of zombies. However, the Radimite clergy are able to dispel the fog in 30 ft. radius with their *channel energy* ability, which also prevents the fog from permeating into that area for 30 mins. This special effect of the channel energy is thanks to the holy symbols that have been blessed with the power of the Cord fragment locked away in the Radiant Mercy's reliquary.

Marquis Isakios Xemonos was with Bishop Basilmon when the fog attacked, discussing late in the night about campaigning into the Thalamech Uplands. Xemonos is wroth at the situation. His wife and son were fortunate to be within the sphere of protection, but they cannot leave without battling thousands of zombies.

The Cord powers the Radimites' holy symbols by being within a divine diagram that continuously draws power from the relic and blesses many of the Radimites' holy implements. Taking the Cord fragment from the reliquary will also remove the the Radimites' ability to dispel the fog.

The Radimites will only allow the Cord to leave if another form of protection is put in place (i.e. another Pall of Shining Life).

Evacuation by Doors is a possible avenue (normal teleportation is blocked by the presence of the mist even in the cleared areas), but Xemonos will strongly resent Prateria, Thunderhoof centaurs, Grondeloup for unleashing this threat and causing the death of his seat of power and responsibility.
