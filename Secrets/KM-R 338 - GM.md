# Notes

## Fairy Garden Exit

- The exit is guarded by 9 banelights
- Getting into a fight with the banelights may draw Vorakios' attention
- Taking out all the banelights within the 1st round prevents them from alerting Vorakios (they alert Vorakios on the turn in round 2)
- They can be tricked into being lured away, 4 at a time (each degree of success lures 4 more away)
- When all 9 banelights are no longer around the portal, they are gone long enought for one skill check to analyze the portal
