Each city-state has at 50,000 cyclops (25,000 in the city itself, and the rest spread across villages). Additionally, there are 50,000 to 500,000 slaves per city-state.

Each city-state has a Security level at least 100.

The capital Kentrokona has a Security level of at least 150.

Each city-state maintains one Legion of 10k cyclops troops, plus a Corps of 25k fodder slave troops. Carpathos thus has a total force of 90k cyclops and 225k slave troops (315k troops in total)
