# Notes

- Defeating 16 green hussars: 2,400 XP (all 8)

## Test of the Old Arborfane

The Old Beldame warns the Evergreen Company that they will be confronted by many who would take the Heart away from them. Even if they are native inhabitants of the forest, the lure of the power of the Heart is too tempting for them. However, they are bound by ancient laws that prevent them from simply taking the heart by force. They must test the carriers of the Heart. The more tests that the Company succeed at, the more they prove themselves to be worthy of the Arborfane's power. If they wish to secure their claim, the PCs must force their opponents to test thme as much as possible.

The forest leads them ever deeper; paths shift, compasses veer, noises lure them on and dangerous creatures block the way home. They’re drawn inexorably into the deep woods.

Soon, trees tower overhead like living buttresses in a titanic hall, half formed ﬂoral faces hover at the edge of vision, and the laughter of unseen fey mixes with the almost-words of whispering leaves. Fireﬂies brighten in the gloom, for only occasional shafts of light break through the canopy, and large seeds drift inexplicably in the breezeless air. The atmosphere feels heavy with an unnerving sense of anticipation, and PCs should have no doubt that they’re walking in an ancient and powerful place:

*Before you is a living arch formed from a pair of intertwining trees. Flanking it are gnarled wooden statues of horsemen, almost swallowed by weeds. Vines curl around their wooden lances, and tny ﬂowers poke through their beards of moss. Beyond the arch, thick briars and brambles ﬂank a narrow path.*

**Features:** The statues are green hussars who planted themselves here long ago and have never yet had cause to move. Only if attacked or if the PCs try to burn the bramble maze do they animate. Anyone examining them closely notices tiny ribbons, twisted leaves, and other offerings in their wings. A Knowledge [nature] DC 25 reveals that adding a token marks a PC as respectful and faithful to the forest’s power, which is noted by the fey that wait further in. If they do so, a few minutes onto the path, they notice a glint of sunlight reflect on something on a high branch before its source falls onto the ground into a soft pile of leaves. PCs who beat a Perception DC 25 check notice a little winged figure disappearing into the leaves of the trees overheard. The object is a finely crafted hand mirror framed in ornate gold.

### Tokens of the Arborfane

- Gift for the bagienniks: a hand mirror framed in ornate gold.
- Special arrow to gain advantage on archery attack rolls to win the contest with the vila.
- Special flower to keep the swarm of wasps away while playing the balalaika.
- Special mushroom that when eaten reveals the borovoi's illusory size change and gain advantage on the grapple checks.
- Special backgammon piece for the bul gara game to cancel out the enchanted board's advantage for Stuhac, and instead give advantage to the PCs.

### Failing the Tests: Mora (CR 5)

Some say the [[mora]] is a swarm awoken by the Arborfane, others that its moths are possessed by the spirits of witches and shadow fey, who gather to drink the dreams and souls of travelers. If PCs fail all the tests, refuse to try them, force their way out of the maze or otherwise anger the forest, it sends the mora to punish them.

### The Test of Knowledge

The PCs’ knowledge and respect of the old ways is tested as their path crosses a river:

*There’s a break in the canopy as you find a river running across your path. Dark, muddy water swirls and bubbles around a ford of mossy stepping stones.*

The party may well be suspicious of such a tranquil scene but no immediate danger presents itself. If anyone examines the stones, there is something not quite right about some of them (Perception DC 20).

**Creatures:** Only some of the stepping stones are real—three are really the moss and mud covered heads of [[bagienniks]] lurking just below the surface. A fourth creature circles in the muddy water in case anyone tries swimming:

*Monstrous humanoids burst from the water, their long-limbs caked with mud and fish bones. “Defilers!” one shouts. “How dare you tread in her sacred place? We demand tribute for this aﬀront!”*
*“Tribute!” its companions echo in ever-louder shouts, “TRIBUTE!”*
*"You carry a source of green power. Give it now!"*

**Terrain:** The river is 25 ft. wide and looks no challenge to a decent swimmer. Hopping across using the stones is also possible (Acrobatics DC 15, but see below).

**Development:** Bagienniks look like lean, web-toed ogres with wrinkled skin and a bulbous snout located between their beady eyes. They are brutish, degenerate creatures related to banniks (bathhouse spirits). Unlike their more civilized cousins, bagienniks live like brigands in the wild, robbing and sometimes devouring travelers. Their anger is like an avalanche of falling stones: it starts small but rapidly grows in size.

Bagienniks serve an obscure but proud divinity known as Nimué, the Fairest of Lakes and Streams, a powerful fairy queen (Knowledge [nature or religion] DC 22).

A Knowledge [nature] DC 17 informs that demands they be gone or questions like “whose sacred place is this?” only insult and enrage the bagienniks. A polite and respectful response, apologizing for trespassing and requesting passage appeases them, as can a prayer in Lada’s honor (Diplomacy or Knowledge [religion] DC 25, increase the DC if the bagienniks are especially annoyed). If the Knowledge [nature] check beats DC 22, then the PCs know they can also make an offering: an animal sacrifice, a gift fit for a noble lady, or meat and beer for the bagienniks, to improve their attitude.

Killing the bagienniks fails this test. If the PCs negotiate their way past, award them half the XP value of the encounter, but it counts toward completing the mythic trial. If the PCs give the bagienniks an offering, the bagienniks give them the mistletoe arrow.

### The Test of Resolve

Shortly after their ordeal at the river (or whenever the party is feeling tired and looking for a place to rest), their resolve is tested by a pair of distracting [[vila]]:

*A break in the bramble wall reveals a clearing where two shapely feminine forms await. They have skin like polished mahogany but hair and eyes as green as emeralds. They nod as you approach—charming smiles blunted by something haughty in their storm cloud eyes.*

**Creatures:** These two are Iwona (meaning “yew tree”) and Jagoda (“berry”). Iwona is the eldest, calmer and shrewder. Jagoda is more vivacious but quicker to wrath. They are friendly, if somewhat aloof, but show a little more respect to druids and rangers. They invite the party to rest a moment, becoming angry if they try to depart in disrespectful haste. They *fascinate* (DC 17) reluctant PCs.

**Terrain:** The glade behind them looks like the perfect campsite although PCs who examine the ground can find countless wolf tracks. It contains a green pavilion tent, beside which is an archery butt and a stone altar overﬂowing with cakes, ribbons, fresh fruits, and ﬂowers. These are traditional offerings to the vila (Knowledge [local or religion] DC 15).

**Development:** The vila suggest a meal, then a friendly archery competition, and various other diversions (including hints of a possible romantic liaison) to deflect the PCs from their aim.

*"You carry a great and terrible burden," Iwona says. "Such power was not meant for mere mortal hands."*
*"Why not leave it with us?" Jagoda suggests. "Who better to care for the Heart of the Forest than her own daughters?"*
*"Fools," Jagoda hisses when the PCs refuse. "You know not who you cross!"*
*However, Iwona holds her hand out before her sister. "No need for violence, Jagoda. At least, not just yet. By ancient laws of the forest, will let a contest of skill and resolve?"*

The archery contest is won by whoever reaches 100 points first. The center is worth 50 points (AC 30), the inner ring is worth 30 points (AC 25), and the outer ring is worth 10 points (AC 20). If the PCs have the mistletoe arrow and use it, it grants +20 to the attack roll. If it is used first, the mistletoe arrow attracts any arrow with a twig of it attached (Knowledge [nature] DC 25).
