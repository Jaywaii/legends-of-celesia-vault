# Notes

## Argent Rose

- Last session: 189
- Vandarkaï 1st, 110 6A
- Starting at level 9

*Previously, Argent Rose Occult Investigation Team 13 opened the passage through the 7th focal tower of Ing-gol'zha, the Gate of Shorok'logoa, granting you access to the dreadful unknown core of the necropolis. However, after harrowing battle with its guardian, an outer spawn, you opted to return back to the surface, resealing the towers behind you. The forbidden lore you have acquired will allow you to figure out how to truly subdue the necropolis.*

*You updated the Rose Scholar Thonra 'Mindshield' of the forewoman Hushglow Expedition of what new dangers you uncovered, allowing her to adjust both the inner and outer defences around the ruined tower accordingly. You returned to Lodge Rochefort, and updated Chapter-Sage Joliette Beauchamp, and proceeded to do some training. A month later, Team 13 is reconvened at Sage Beauchamp's office. However, Team 13 has seen seen another round of personnel changes. The ordeal has taken a toll on the kobold sage Raggi, severing his connection to his zeitgeist that maintained his immortal, so he has chosen to retire from field duties to live out his remaining years in peace. Due to his experience and centuries of great service to the Society, he was offered the seat of Chapter-Sage at Lodge Mirkoveski. "Either way, the faculty of his university will no doubt be relieved."*

*"I have been staying up to date on your progress," she tells you. "The Society's crafters are still hard at work sculpting those dreadful figurines you requested. As you can imagine, acquiring the needed adamantine is no easy task, nor is the sculpting. The healers have their hands full with treating the mental breaks the sculptors are experiencing, and we have few replacements available willing to dare this dangerous feat. As to the rest of the items you require for the rituals, I'm afraid you'll have to tackle those tasks yourselves. Our other OI teams are either occupied with their own investigations or in recovery. However, any other this department or the Society as a whole is ready to support you in any way we can. In fact, we have a number of volunteers to join your team on this expedition."*

*The libraries of all of the Argent Rose Society are available, accessible via the network of teleport circles connecting from lodge of lodge. The Grand Lodge on Zhoulong is connected to each Continental Lodge, and each Continental Lodge is connected to five regional lodges (other lodges will require other means of travel). With access to the vast repositories of lore and expert scholars, you are able to uncover how you can retrieve the needed ritual components to seal the focal towers.*
