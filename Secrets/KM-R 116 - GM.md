# Notes

- The Evergreen Company continues to plan for the upcoming conflict with Klorgfang. Funds are paid out as Argus sends out order for his followers to retrain as high lords and warpriests. Two and a half thousand of Argus' followers are then organized into three armies, to be led by Argus, Davona, and Arina. Unfortunately, word arrives from Grondeloup informing them that they are under renewed assault from the native Thunderhoof centaur tribes, and are thus unable to send any aid as earlier promised. On the other hand, Prateria receives confirmation that Montari Entreprises are sending a contingent of their security force to Arbor's Rest. Rochefort confirms the departure of two thousand of their musketeers to Prateria, and that they are also sending forces to Grondeloup. Prateria also receives loans from the Sovrani Syndicate, Varano Trade Company, and the Harkûr Trade Guild.
