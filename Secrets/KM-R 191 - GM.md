# Notes

- Desert Break team uses *doors* to enter Jafarket invisibly, ending up in an alley at midnight. Maalek has a childhood friend in this city, Shemir Saqat, who works as a waitress at a high-class inn called the Sapphire Djinn. Asako, Maalek, and Domenica shed their invisibility to pretend to be a noble merchant from Xifangsaifu and her two guards. They book three nights at the inn, but hit a snag when the innkeeper asks for their travelers' papers. They claim that they lost their papers when one of their satchels went missing on their way here. The innkeeper Hakim believes them, and suggests that he is friends with a captain of the Jafarket guard who is currently having drinks in the commons with some of his officers. The captain can help them quickly get replacement papers. Seeking to get official papers legally, the team agrees, and manage to make Captain Kasmut believe them of their sincerity. Kasmut happily accepts doing a favour for his friend Hakim. He and another officer, Lt. Taia, accompany Asako, Domenica and Maalek to the nearest gate. Sky, still invisible, follows them from above in the sky.
- Maalek makes a show of trying to retrace their steps and looking for the missing satchel. Meanwhile, Domenica makes small talk with Kasmut and Taia. She learns a bit about the rivalry between the city guard and the Fists of Amonkhaten. The former is trying to keep the city stable and economically prosperous while the latter seeks to enforce the God-Pharoah's will and dominance. Domenica asks about what threats the city guard faces, and they speak and boast of some of the monsters and planar dungeons they have overcome. They also mention that they focus a lot on eliminating terrorist groups. Domenica inquires about what groups are enemies of the state, and the officers mention Kaïdenite worshipers being clandestinely supported by the Kahadun Caliphate. They advise Domenica to avoid displaying any Kaïdenite symbology or speaking of its scriptures publicly in Jafarket.
- While Domenica is gaining this information, she, Maalek, and Sky are coordinating telepathically on what to do. The plan is to have Sky *dominate* the captain. The captain would distract the gate guards for them to forge their names into the gate's documents and get the repalcement travelers' papers. However, the captain has a sturdy mind and resists the *dominate person*. He quickly grabs his weapon, and signals his lieutenant to do the same.

- Cortez arranges a discreet route from Seormaw through Carpathos, Minossus, and Qar-Kadaj.
- At Qar-Kadaj, Cortez's itineary leads them to the mountainous settlement of Tarmuk, the team is met with a golden-haired caelephilim woman accompanied by a floating pig covered in tattoos of stars, and a small pale-green waifish anthousai woman with lilac petals for hair who is also accompanied with a large bumblebee. Osla and Lumina warmly welcome their old friends with open smiles. Osla recounts that she and Lumina have led her Bodai acolytes on a pilgrimage of enlightenment through the lands of Valendar before Zazen's stars guided them back to Qar-Kadaj.  They had only just arrived and finished settling in Tarmuk when Cortez found them on his way to Ashak to recover the magic licenses. Cortez explained their situation, so Osla got to work seeiking out potential allies in the Ashaki Empire that might help them. Osla got into contact with a small resistance group, but who has been inflicting a lot of significant trouble to the Pharoah's rule (aka Orok). Osla informs them that one of their leaders is hiding out in Jafarket, waiting for their arrival. Osla and Lumina wishes they could go with them to help, but they must remain here and help organize more resistance among the various in-fighting tribes of Qar-Kadaj against Ashaki and Radimite expansion into the region. However, she promises them that Tarmuk can serve as a safe haven for the team if they need it.
- *Ashaki magic licenses* come in the form of a metal wristband. Its owner must wear it (taking up a wrist slot) and attune 1 resonance with it. Inscribed upon it is the identity and address of its owner, the date, location, and official who issued the license, and any additional restrictions that comes with the license (such as maximum caster level casting, total magic item resonance, locations where valid, etc.). The license will glow brightly and sound an alarm if the wearer casts magic or uses resonance outside of the set restrictions, and can only be turned off by an Ashaki official using another magic item known as a *license authenticator*. The *authenticator* is a small rod, and the official needs to tap a license with it to verify the license and turn off its alarm. This can be done up to 3/day, requiring 1 resonance afterwards. When taps, an illusory headshot of the assigned owner of the license appears from the license. The *authenticator* also glows green if the license was legitimately created. The license must be invested with resonance for this reaction to occur. License holders that have not invested resonance at time of checking or have exceeded their license's restrictions are considered to be the same as criminals who cast or wield magic items without a license. Magic licenses are created with Craft Wondrous Item, *alarm*, *arcane mark*, *detect magic*, *scrivener's chant*, *peacebond*, *silent image*. The authenticator is created with Craft Wondrous Item, *identify*, *legend lore*.
- A fake license will need *claim identity* (8,000 gp), *greater magic aura* (4,000 gp), *legend lore* (12,000 gp), *silent image* (2,000 gp). Total is 26,000 gp x desired caster level. When confronting an *authenticator*, the fake license wearer can make either a concentration check or a Bluff check, adding the fake license's CL as a bonus, against the *authenticator*'s wielder's Sense Motive check, who adds the //authenticator's CL as a bonus (usually +15).

## Jafarket

*Jafarket stands as an oasis amidst the surrounding arid and sandy region of the area. It is a large city composed of many multistoried beige sandstone buildings, topped with a kaleidoscope of colorful rooftops, with streets lined with tropical bushes and palm trees. It is a bustling city, with long lines of caravans and travelers moving in and out the settlements many wallgates. The streets are crowded, with the shouts of merchants hawking their wares. Overhead, the skies are lined with flying ships and patroled by flying griffons and sphinxes., while the streets are patroled by squads of Jafarket's guards and the occasional team of Fists of Amonkhaten.*
- **Entry:** Entry into Jafarket is regulated, requiring travelers without proof of residence to sign in with their name, purpose of visit, how long they will stay. Scribes will record their appearance. Travelers are issued entry notes which states this information and date of entry, and guards inform the travelers that they must present these papers upon demand by authorities. Bluff, Disguise vs. Perception, Sense Motive.
- **Finding Resistance:** Gather information without arousing suspicion to find the local resistance member (Orok). Orok has learned that Aedre is being held at the hidden prison of Sandrock, a high-security prison meant for powerful or politically important individuals. Adriel would fit this profile. He also knows that Jafarket does see the occasional flying prison barge make a stop here.
