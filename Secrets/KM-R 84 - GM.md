# Notes

## Asmolan 110

*Prateria experienced an unexpected windfall in supplies during the spring of 110. Farmers' crops were showing signs to be a incredibly bountiful harvest later this year. Attacks and disease on livestock have sharply decreased, allowing much more to be sold off. After the sharp and deadly winter Praterian just recently experienced, many took it as a sign that the powers that be were happy with the Evergreen Company's victory over the Dancing Lady. Indeed, many among the commoners gossip of waking up in morning to see their crops and livestock already tended to, only to barely catch a glimpse of a tiny figure darting around the corner.*

## Vandarkaï 110

*It seemed that Prateria's good fortunes would not only continue into the next month, but crescendo to wondeful new height. Throughout the entire month, the weather was perfect to raise spirits. Bright and sunny during the day, rain when needed, comfortable temperatures day and night. All of it combined to help grow the expected bounty that many saw blossom in the previous month.*

*And what a spring blossoming it was. All across the nation, rare and beautiful wildflowers of fantastic colours and exotic smells flourished. The sight and smell of it was euphoric, like something out of a dream. For common folk who dealt with the munddane day-to-day chores, Elyssria's Blessing as it became to be known was a wonderful an uplifting sight.*

## Zhaoren 110

*The troops stationedat the Silent Bastion uncovered a surprising discovery while inspecting and repairing the fort. While dismantling a damaged wall in the cellar of the fort, the soldiers stumbled upon a secret alcove. Within were several well-preserved books of elven make.  They were the personal journals of Princess Valendris, last heir to the elven kingdom of Selimbral. Her diaries contain information on the last days of the elven nation, and gave an inside look at the war between the elves and the green dragons, and the life of the royal family. Scholars, expecially elven ones, flock to Prateria once again to study these journals.*

## Karthoran 110

*Prateria's period of peace was not without trouble. After the Moondancer War, the little nation's notoriety shot up. Many wished to join this life on the frontier and adventure. On top of that, the writings of the Madonna of Crimson Eyes spread like wildfire, thanks in no small part by the church of Kaï-den, and mobs of pilgrims from far and wide flocked to Promethea to witness her. Unfortunately, the nation was ready for such a sudden increase in immigrants, and had no available housing for them. Undeterred, the new arrivals set up tents, wagons, and shanties around the city. Although now sheltered, their presence was upon the very construction sites for new buildings.*

- Roll Loyalty checks for Role Abdication for both Ayaki, Cethin, and Arina.

## Arbor's Rest

- Domenico Donofrio of the Steel Peacock Company has been assigned as Governor-General of the settlement by the Montari Entreprises.
- His request to the Evergreen Company is that they regularly invest in Arbor's Rest, commisioning a House every month, and focusing on commisioning trade buildings.
- Donofrio is unhappy that the Old Arborfane requires regular tribute, which comes in the form +1 BP to building costs and additional Consumption equal to one-half of the settlement's number of districts (minimum 1).
