# Notes

- 6 pm: Legio II's forces deploy themselves along the south-east corner of the battlefield. They adopt an aggressive posture, while Norsgurd's forces switch to the defensive, falling back into the fort. The fort itself comes under fire from Legio III's heavy cannon. As Davona's units advance, they rain projectiles upon the Klorgfang units. Meanwhile, the lone hobgoblin heavy cavalry, surrounded by Legio III's units, fights to the last. With every fall of its soldiers, the remaining soldiers of the unit fights ever more fiercely, taking as many of Legio III's troops as possible. Arina's unit of wizards also manage to defeat Norsgurd's kobolds with a magical barrage.
- 7 pm: Norsgurd's unit continue to fall back into the fort and take up position within its fortifications. Meanwhile, Legio II unites with Legio III and begin providing cover fire for their retreat from the battlefield. Units within range of their enemy exchange long range fire, but both sides are content to increase the distance between each other.
- 8 pm: Legio II and Legio III continue to retreat from the battlefield, while Norsgurd's forces bunker down in the fort. Davona and Cortez's units remain the last units on the field in order to allow the rest retreat safely away before Hrondgren's Horde reaches them. Arina and Sky detach from their units to provide additional support.
- 9 pm: Hrondgren's Horde arrives from the south of Fort Menakedes, with drums pounding and horns blaring. The king of Klorgfang bellows out a deafening roar, one that is responded by another roar from Norsgurd from the fort. The trolls converge on the remaining Praterian units on the field, and attempt to savage them as much as possible. Although they manage to inflict casualties, the Praterian units manage to disengage and retreat in order from the field.
- Hrondgren chooses not to follow the Praterians, as his forces had to push hard to reach the fort in time, and Norsgurd's forces are in no condition to provide any assistance. Legio II and III retreat swiftly, but orderly, to the north. They will cross the Greenbelt, using Legio I's improvised bridge, and manage to recuperate under Legio I's protection.
- The Evegreen Company discuss their options. Their initial plan was good, and would have worked perfectly as they had Norsgurd's army on the ropes, if only that third army had not appeared. They wonder if they could use the corpse of Silverich's daughter, the slain Deilenwyn, to taunt him and lure him out for a straight fight or an ambush. Arina decides to finally apply her *unspeakable name* ability on Hrondgren, Norsgurd, and Silverich. Perhaps now that the enemy's leadership is directly involved against them, they might say her name or title.
- Vandarkaï 30th, 8 am: The Praterian forces are now fully rested. No movement from the enemy has been detected; both Klorgfang armies remain camped at Fort Menakedes. The Evergreen Company decides to try to lure Silverich out of his army. They receive word that Silverich's forces have been sighted southwest of Castrum Aurum, in the process of building a supply line from the south towards the undefended fort. The Company, aside from Davona who will remain to command their forces, will *teleport* to Aurum. Davona loans Jupus her boots and cloak so that he may move faster and be able to fly. Before doing so, Arina speeds her way to the former site of the Hazel Court, and retrieves Deilenwyn's corpse, lifting it up on shields of golden light. The Consul is too fast and nimble to be intercepted by the local Klorgfang sentries. Once she returns, Sky *shrinks* the corpse, then *teleports* the group to Castrum Aurum. Upon arriving, Sky renders them *invisible* and grants them magical flight, with the exception of Argus, who will act as the sole target, and carrying the dead forest drake.
- On their way to the Silverich camp, Arina suddenly becomes aware of someone saying her name. Immediately, she knows that it is Norsgurd, and she spends her mythic power to scry on him. She sees that Norsgurd is inside a fortified structure, and the two-headed troll is knocked to the floor, one of his heads bleeding from his mouth. Out of her sight, she hears Hrdongren roar in Gronbrosk, ***"Fool! You were warned not to speak her name! Have you not failed me enough this day?!"*** In a more whimpering tone, Norsgurd replied, *"Forgive my misstep, your highness! But this is an opportunity! The accursed witch's minion's information has always been correct. Their entire leadership is away, we must strike now!"* ***"Had you not had your army savaged, we would have done so hours ago!"*** Hrondgren growls. ***"Still, you are correct. We are now rested, and Silverich continues northward. I shall reconvene our council. But not you! Who knows if she looks upon you now. Seclude yourself, now!"*** Norsgurd scuttles away to a chamber. He roars and rages within it, letting out his frustration.
- Arina decides to try to provoke him by projecting her image to him. Sky worriedly disagrees, but Arina does so anyway. Arina appears in Norsgurd's chamber, puckishly sitting on the edge of a table. "That was a pretty pathetic display," Arina taunts him. "This is supposed to be Klorgfang's mighty general? Castigated like a mere child? We had you on the ropes, an army three times larger than ours, walled up in a fort like a chickenshit. You loss thousands of troops to mere humans and halflings, and had to be saved by Hrondgren!" At Argus's suggestion, Arina then tells him that all their prisoners are now begging to join Prateria since Norsgurd was such a loser, a loser with two heads and no brains. The mutant troll roars in rage, and slams his mighty fist through Arina's image, and shatters the table. Arina's image merely gestures as if to dust off her shoulder.
- Norsgurd roars and rages at Arina's face, spewing spittle through her image. After a minute of this, Norsgurd grows silent, then both heads slowly grin. *"Accursed little bitch, spew all the lies you wish. You think you have the upper hand? You ahve no idea, you didn't even realize the very snake in your own court. How do you think we knew all of your moves, all of your powers? You are bare to us!"*
- "Well, you must be dumber than I thought for having to reveal that to us," Arina retorted.
- *"Hurl your insults all you want, the dagger above your heart inches ever so closer..."*
