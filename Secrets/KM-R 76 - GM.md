# Notes

## Louis' Coma

**Complexity** 20
**Research** Knowledge (arcana), Knowledge (history), Lore (dreams)
**kp 5** Puppets are common imagery used to represent someone manipulating others. Puppets are rarely crafted to be the exact image of a person, and rely mostly on mimicking a few notable features of the person they represent.
**kp 15** Golden threads are commonly used to represent the threads of fate.
**kp 30** 'The Gentleman in Black' refers to an enigmatic figure that many whisper exists only in the corner of your eye, never seen when attempting to look directly at him. He is commonly described as a tall, gaunt, pale figure dressed in decrepit black nobleman clothing. He is often seen in areas with a strong fairy presence.
**kp 60** There have been several references to 'The Gentleman in Black' found in ancient poems about fairies. The mentions refer to a Fairest exiled from Oberon's Court after his insidious intrigues went too far and insulted the fairy. Since then, this Eldest has appear in ancient tales as a schemer in the shadows, tricking others into involving themselves into his enigmatic plots.
**kp 120** The 'Gentleman in Black' was one of the many monikers used by the Fairest Calefaigdh, exiled Count of Oberon's Court. He is known for two things: seducing young, fairy and mortal maidens, and manipulate them into his enigmatic plots, discarding them as soon as he loses interest into them before moving onto the next lass destined for great things, and experimenting with fate, destiny, and the potential of others.

## Victory Celebration

- **Victory Celebration:** (uses Festival Edict)
	- Unveiling of Promethea's Town Hall and Bob's Bar.
	- Sootscale kobolds accept the invitation to Promethea for the victory celebration. King Sootscale, General Mikmek, and High Shaman Samska, along with a retinue to 20 kobold honor guards make it to the city.
	- Sootscale has ambitions of power and expansion. He has never seen a kobold tribe become so powerful as his own, and he wants more. However, he is aware of the Evergren Company's strength, and does not want to incur their wrath. Instead, he seeks to ingratiate himself, and possibly manipulate them to his own benefit.
	- Sootscale speaks with Prateria's leaders, trying to suss out their plans for expansions, and tries to subtly infer his own ambitions for growth. He hopes to see how the Evergreen Company will react so he can plan accordingly.
	- *A whirlwind of activity descends upon Promethea. Optimism and relief fills the air, finally replacing the worry and fear that permeated for the past several months. Your citizens smile and laughed with sincerity as they hustle and bustle about, preparing the town for the grand festivities. The celebration is being centered around the Great Cypress tree. Sister Félicité works with the ten young dryads to decorate Carnival Memorial Park. Trees bloom and the ground is covered with fantastical fairy flora. With they aid of other fey, the dryads concoct delightful and exotic sweets and drinks, while Sister Félicité oversees and makes sure that they aren't too magical. Stands are set up for musicians to play, playsets are built for children to play, and numerous tables and seats are laid out. A plentiful feast is prepared for all to eat and drink to their hearts content.*
	- *There is a commotion among the folks on the edge of town. "Kobolds!" Shouts a young boy running into town. "Kobolds are coming!" There is indeed a train of two dozen kobolds on giant weasels arriving in Promethea, bearing a black banner with silver dragon emblem. At the head of the group was a tall kobold of dusky, dark grey scale wearing a silver crown. It is King Sootscale, and you recognize that he is accompanied by Mikmek and Samska.*
	- *Although your people are well aware that the Sootscale kobolds are nominally at peace with your nation and that they were invited, their presence nonetheless puts your people on edge. Davona's guard quickly provides an escort for the Sootscale, partly due to honor them, as well as to make sure no ill encounters occur.*
	- *Ayaki welcomes Sootscale and his retinue to Promethea, and invites them to join in on the festivities. The kobold king offers his thanks on behalf of his people for the invitation and for defeating the Dancing Lady. He offers a gift of half a dozen slain boars to help with the feast.*
	- *The celebration is marked by several events. Ayaki provides an encouraging speech to the people to get the festivities started. The Ironwatch forces are rewarded for their victory at the Dancing Lady's keep. The new town hall and taverm are unveiled. During the grand feast, King Sootscale speaks with you all about the future of the Evergreen Plains. "You are now the most powerful force in the region. As fellow monarchs, I must ask what do you intend to do next?"*
	- *"We kobolds have never before attained such growth. Thanks to the friendship between our two people, hundreds of kobolds now flock to Sootscale as a beacon of kobold civilization. All thanks to the security you have provided in the region."*
	- *"For now, we have focused on expanding our village. However, the time will soon come where we will need to claim additional land. I hope that each of our ambitions do not come into conflict."*
	- *"Under my kingship, I intend for the Sootscale kobolds to remain as allies with Prateria."*
