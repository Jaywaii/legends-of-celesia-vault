# Notes

- Sky,  Tabrir, and Osla return to Promethea. Sky begins researching at the Argent Rose Promethea Lodge to find out if anyone had ever warded the Hearts of the Forest of the Old Arborfane, and to examine Elyssria's ritual spell. He could not find any instances in thousands of years of recarded history of the Old Arborfane of anyone attempting to cast susch magic on the Hearts, although such recorded history was sparse to begin with. Regarding the ritual spell, he determined that it was indeed a form of warding spell, and that it drew its power from Faerie. Its design was unique, definitely a custom spell with notable telltale signs of fairy magic.
- Tabrir and Osla send out missives to the invitees to the Viridian Games. Tabrir also requests if there are any special accomodations they require, and Osla requests of the religious representatives if they would participate in a grand blessing during the opening of the games.
- Arina flash-steps into the sky above Lake Iridescence to scout its southern shore. She find a stream flowing from the forest on the southern shore. She returns to the Stag Lord's Fort, and she and the rest of the Company determine that they need a boat. They return to Promethea by foot. Upon being informed of the issue, Sky, with Asako's and Louis' help, crafts an *unfolding boat*. Since Attila funded its construction, the *boat* is technically his property, but he willingly loans it out to the Company for this endeavour.
- The Company *teleports* back to the Stag Lord's Fort. They unfold the *boat* into a large ship, and embark. A flying Argus then hauls the ship with rope towards the southern stream. As they approach the bank, they see the forest's trees begin grown and swaying towards them.
- As soon as the Company sets foot on shore, the tree ripple, and a dozen of 8 feet long lances of gnarled branches spit out from the trees, slamming into each of them, except for Sky thanks to Davona's timely warning. The wooden lances begin blooming, and roots begin growing into the flesh of their victims. Roots and vines begin to slither out towards the Company.
- Sky erected a *wall of force* between them and the forest. Jupus attempted to appease the Old Arborfane with *feed the forest*, but discovers that the forest continues draining life and magic from him before he could forcefully rip his metaphysical essence away. The same happens to Arina when she morphes into a pudle of blood and the soil starts absorbing her life essence. Rishoi and Tabrir combine forces to keep everyone alive.
- The forest ripples again, and another dozen lances of gnarled wopd slams into the *wall of force*, embedding into it. They begin blooming , and roots start growing into the *wall*. The vines and roots begin creeping up along the *wall*'s side and also embed themselves in it. Ske can sense the magic in the *wall* begin to sap away.
- The Company decides to leave. Sky casts *haste* on the party, but even then, they could feel their newly enhanced speed begin to sap away by the ground. They quickly get back on the *boat*, amd Argus pulls the ship away. Attila manifests an ectoplasmic wolf to distract the vines as the *wall of force* collapses. The wolf is enveloped by the vines and roots, letting out an echoey yelp before being consumed by the forest. As the Company gets further away from the shore, the lances embedded within them wither and die. From the forest itself, they hear an unearthly, haunting screech.

- The flight over the south Arborfane is perilious. The trees sway and grown beneath fliers, atracted by their presence, as if trying to reach up to them. Hefty spears of twisted branches spit upward from the tree like bolts (attack bonus +30, 3d6 P). Vines shoot up to grab any low flyer to pull them into the tree where they would be torn apart.

## Prateria Events

### Firhen 111

*The flow of new settlers would not be stemmed this month. More followers of the Evergreen Company came to join their leaders in Promethea. However, there was still a lack of housing, forcing many to seek shelter by squatting the land around the settlement, interfering with additional construction.*

Unable to build new buildings in Promethea; Fame and Stability decrease by 1 for 1 year, and Unrest increases by 2. You may try to disperse the squatters with a Stability check. Success means the squatters are dispersed, the penalties are removed, and the event is not continuous. However, if a House or Tenement is not built by the next Event Phase, Infamy increases by 1 for 1 year and Unrest by 2. Failing the Stability check means the event is continuous, and you may not construct new buildings in that settlement until the event is resolved.

### Tierlan 111

*The immigration crisis continued to worsen. Despite new homes being built, more followers kept arriving to take over the land squatting of the previous, now housed, group of followers. Worse, the squallid conditions of the tent city were excellent breeding grounds for a plague to break out! Typhoid fever breaks out in the camps, and spreads quickly. Rishoi's hospital and the Madonna's Cathedral are quickly rushed by the infected, and the disease spreads throughout Promethea.*

Plague (Hex or Settlement, Continuous): A deadly sickness strikes the target hex or settlement. You cannot construct terrain improvements or buildings there while plague persists. Attempt two Stability checks, each with a penalty equal to the number of Brothels, Foreign Quarters, Highways, Inns, Piers, Roads, Stables, Stockyards, Tenements, and Waterfronts in the hex, and a bonus equal to the number of ss, Cathedrals, Herbalists, Hospitals, and Temples in the hex. If you succeed at both checks, the event ends, but Stability decreases by 2 for 1 year and Treasury by 1d3 BP. If you fail at one check, Stability decreases by 4 for 1 year, Treasury decreases by 1d6 BP, and Unrest increases by 1d3. If you fail at both, Stability decreases by 4 for 1 year, Treasury decreases by 1d6 BP, Unrest increases by 1d6, and in the next Event Phase the plague spreads to an adjacent hex.
