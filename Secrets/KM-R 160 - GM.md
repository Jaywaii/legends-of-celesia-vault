# Notes

## Trials of the Old Arborfane

### Laellien's Lament

#### The Guards' Redoubt

- Following the trail and the song require a DC 40 Perception or Survival check. If no one succeeds, 1 day passes, otherwise 1 hour passes. Upon success: *Following the path, you notice the ground on both sides becoming much more wet. Two earthen banks, thickly spread with trees, rise up from either side, forming a narrow valley. Water from the stream has soaked through the ground, making a muddy bog. The remains of a wooden path built into the slope provide an obvious path upwards. Further ahead, the stream seems to have disappeared into heavily boggy ground under the path. Past that, continuing upward, you see a narrow waterfall pouring into a muddy pool. Standing at points around the bog are six finely-armored figures, as still and silent as statues. Their armor is rusted and decayed in places, their faces covered by closed helms. The wooden path forms a balcony over the side of the bog.*
	- The muddy path is treacherous. Each time a PC tries to move, there is a 50% chance that they become stuck in the mud, requiring a DC 20 Strength check to pull themselves out as a standard action. If someone gets stuck, they notice  When the PCs pass by the still armored figures, there is a screech, and six banshees emerge up and out of the suits of armor. Each round that the PCs do not reach the platform is 1 hour that passes. As each round passes, PCs get clues that time is shifting faster. After the 1st round, a woman can be heard singing Laellien's Lament, up to the 4th verse from the upward direction, towards the balcony.
	- Upon reaching the balcony: *Hand and foot, you pull yourself up the slope. The banshees fall back once you step onto the balcony. In the distance beyond the balcony, you hear a woman's voice. It seems to be shouting the name “Laellien”. The call is echoed by sharp barks. As you climb, the barking seems to get louder. Suddenly, in front of you growls a heavily built hound, materialized from mist. Another growl is heard, and another hound appears beside the first. Barks give warning, and more and more large hounds appear, forming a wall of growls and barks, barring your way forward. Suprisingly, the singing of Laellien's Lament pierces through the growls and barks, with a third, younger voice joining in, singing the 5th verse.*
	- The hounds emanate an aura of fear (DC 23 Will). First fail results in shaken, the second results in being frightened. Frightened characters flee back down the muddy slope where the banshees remain and are attacked by them. The hounds can leap high into the air if PCs try to fly over them, grab them, and pull them back down. Like the banshees, if killed, a hound reforms at the start of the next round. Each round spent cost 1 hour of real time.

#### *Laellien's Lament*

##### 1st Verse

*Oh, let me free to come to you / Come to you, my son / Like a dream I see you / Our lives entwined in death*
*Why couldn’t I see your heart was full / Why didn’t I see your longing / I was blinded by my fear / I was guided by my anger*
- Response: *My mother, years have passed now / And my blood washed through the stream*

##### 2nd Verse

*Like a curse I remember that boy / White hair appeared through the trees / Where our caravan camped / Not worthy of the house of Lallwyn*
*I recall the rage at my throat / As I tore at the walls of your heart / Your bed empty, your window open / In my rage, I was ready to kill*
- Response: *My mother, years have passed now / And I gave my heart to my Nemeth*

##### 3rd Verse

*He hid among the trees that night / And helped you find the shadows / Rage burst through my heart / I screamed at your gentle face*
*A mask to hide your defiance / To defend what I thought was mine / Stay a step before my guards / Disappear between the branches*
- Response: *My mother, years have passed now / And my love still blooms from beyond*

##### 4th Verse

*I remember the sight of your hair / As I unleashed my guards in the glade / Fear rooted them to the ground / White robe blowing as you ran*
*Fleeing my rage for your love / Your precious ankle slipped away / Oh, if I could just take it back / I, your mother, twisted by my hate*
- Response: *My mother, years have passed now / And I fall forever in your dream*

##### 5th Verse

*Hounds teeth caught your flesh / You stumbled on the wet rock / Froth flashes white when water falls / Over stone I saw my son slip*
*I rushed to the edge of the ridge / Watched the water tumble over / Your skull broken bleeding / Ribbons of your blooming blood*
- Response: *My mother, years have passed now / And I am lost for ever to my only love*
