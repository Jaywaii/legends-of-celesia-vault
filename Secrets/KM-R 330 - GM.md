# Notes

## Pyrouranos's Purpose

PCs will have to fight through the horde to help the centaur champion reach the edge of Vorakios' barrier to this seat of power. In there way are a series of challenges.

### Challenge 1: Help allies.

**Focus on protecting champion:** The enemies attacking the champion directly are scaled to be even with the champion + PCs, so if the PCs split up to help the allies, defending the champion will be harder. The champion pleads with the PCs to help her friends.

**Save elf mages:** Should the elf mages be saved, the PCs gain a buff during the next challenge and (+5 to attack) the finale.

**Save dwarf warriors:** Should the dwarf warriors be saved, this unit serves as a tank during the next and final encounter.

### Challenge 2: Despair

Dark fey prey conjure up a powerful wave of magical despair that is both necrotic and enchanting. Allies fall into despair, especially the champion's lover. In his despaired state, he is vulnerable to being overwhelmed by the horde. With the threat of her lover and the despair of the enchantment, the champion's progress falters.

**Save the lover:** Lover is a human ranger. Failure to save him results in the champion gaining disadvantage on all d20s for the rest of the encounter and in the final push.

**Defeat the fairy curse:** The curse makes everyone on the map shaken and enemies gain +1 advantage on attacks (Will DC 35). Those who save at the start of their turn can act normally.

**Rally the champion's and allies' morale:** The allies are already under the effect of the fairy curse of despair. PCs can inspire allies to overcome the curse for a round by spending a standard action to make a DC 30 Oratory check. Each 10 over the DC increases the resistance by 1 round.

### Challenge 3: Final Push

*You have broken through the main line of enemy forces. Although still surrounded by the undead and dark fey, their lines and formations are in disarray, and are fully engaged with the forces of the alliance. Ahead of you is another line of undead, but behind them, floating in the air with dark energy surrounding is the giant form of Vorakios. "I have a clear shot!" shouts the champion. "Shield me from harm while I gather the power!" The champion draws Pyrouranos and closes her eyes. She mutters a mantra over and over as motes of light appear and coalesce around her, but the gathering of power attracts the lich's attention. Yet, Vorakios could not directly attack her as a great silver dragon battles him head on, backed by the magic master mages in the backline. Much to his rage, Vorakios has no choice but to rely on his minions to end the champion, roaring in his deathly voice: "Destroy her!".*

The champion breaks through the enemy lines to gain a clear shot at Vorakios. The champion needs 3 rounds to concentrate her power (+3 rounds if lover died). Each time she receives damage, the count reduces by one (once per round) (*"The Champion cries out in pain as she is harmed, and you see her stance falter. Some of the energy dissipates as her concentration is disrupted before she regains her focus."*).

**Round 1:** 12 Zombie cyclops with 2 zombie hordes each.

**Round 2:** 6 Cold Riders with 2 zombie hordes each.

**Round 3:** 6 Ankous with 2 zombie hordes each.

### Vorakios is Defeated

*In an explosion of power, the arrow, glowing brightly with energy, is loosed by the champion. Like a bolt of lightning, it streaks over the mass of grotesque undead. Vorakios merely raises one hand to reinforce his mystical defenses. But even from this distance, you perceive his gaunt, skeletal one glowing red eyed visage twist in in fearful surprise as the arrow pierces through his shields and wards in a series of explosions. The arrow slams into his glowing red eye. The lich unleashes a bone-chilling scream of agony as he is engulfed in an explosion of bright, life-giving light. As the light fades, ashes float where the lich once stood as a dark shadow shoots away towards the mountains in the distance.*

## Vanishing of Pyrouranos

*The horrific battlefield fades into mist. When haze parts, you find yourselves once more amidst battle in a different grassy plain. However, unlike the noble alliance of different peoples warring against a common, terrible foe, the scenes of battle around you paint a tapestry of abhorrent misery. Gone was the hope between disparate companions, replaced with bitterness and hate as centaur killed centaur with unbridled rage.*

*"Pyrouranos is mine!" cries one centaur warrior before he is skewered by a spear.*

*"No! The Bow of Chiefs belongs to the Bloodtails!" shouted another before an arrow pierces her head through her eye*.

*Similar scenes repeat themselves all around you, a tragic inter-tribal massacre. At its center, not too far from you, stands the source of the conflict. Atop a pile of centaur corpses, held in the death grip of a slain centaur is Pyrouranos. It calls to you, whether it was the bow itself, or the nature of the dream compels you to do so is unknown. You are not the only one lured to it. Centaurs frothing with rage are climbing over each other's corpses to reach it. Then, everyone freezes in place, fear and presence imposing themselves on all's sense of will, including yours.*

*A deafening roar screeches from above, and everyone's sight is blinded for a moment as the sun is reflected of brilliant silver. Clouds of dust are whipped up, and a tremendous impact shakes the ground as a dragon dozens of feet long lands over the legendary bow.*

### 1st Test

*"Enough! For too long I have watched and hoped that reason would prevail, but you all continue to trample kin under your hooves, clawing at glory into the grave you will all rest together. Children of Thalamech, you covet Pyrouranos as a crown, but it was never meant to be one. It was meant to guide and unite! So hear me now, I will not allow this great relic fall into the hands of the unworthy. No more warfare! Let only those who seek to wield Pyrouranos alone step forward, and prove themselves as the truly worthy one amongst you!"*

*A dozen centaur leaders, the only ones with strong enough will to tolerate the dragon's overwhelming presence, step forward, each claiming that they are the worthy one, and soon enough, their declarations are followed by blood as they begin dueling each other to the death. The dragon looms over Pyrouranos, observing the conflict around him, until his stare lands upon you all.*

*"And you, too, seek Pyrouranos? How will you prove yourselves worthy?"*

- Sterlingastrax hopes that the PCs will intervene somehow and stop the centaurs from killing each other. If they kill the centaurs, fight Sterlingastrax, or otherwise try to steal Pyrouranos, that marks one failure.
- Diplomacy attempts take one whole round, and made at DC 60. PCs can aid on the check. Success convinces one centaur (1d12) to cease fighting. Each degree convinces one additional centaur to stop fighting.
- Alternatively, using non-lethal combat means to stop the centaurs from fighting and healing them meets Sterlingastrax's approval.

- once satisfied, the bow magically appears in the dragons hands and he warns the tribes of their foolishness
- dragon transforms into a younger, female silver dragon who speaks to the players directly
- silver dragon shoots across the sky to the southern mountains, leaving a trail for the PCs to follow
