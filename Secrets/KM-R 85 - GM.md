# Notes

- Attila Rakirsson leads over a hundred Rughir orc refugees into Prateria, causing a mild panic that the Evergreen Company calmed down. Attila had heard from his friend Tabrir of this new nation being built, and he had deemed it a suitable place for his rescues tribesfolk to resettle. Attila, despite his fearsome presence, was actually quite cordial and amenable in his request. With Tabrir vouching for his character, the Evergreen Company allowed the Rughirs to settle in their lands, albeit becoming citizens of Prateria in the process. In exchange, Attila pledged his service to the Company should they ever need it, and donated over twenty thousand gold to help fund the sorely needed infrastructure of the nation.
- Speaking of which, due to the mass immigration of thousands of people seeking to follow the Evergreen Company, Prateria suffered food shortages and lack of housing. Thanks to the excellent weather and careful resource management, the food shortage was not significant. However, the housing crisis required more effort. Davona had to regularly disperse squatters to allow room for more construction. Much resources were dedicated to building houses and claiming new lands to settle. Much debate was had on what needed to be built, and jails were a hot topic, splitting the vote down the middle. Arina, as Consul, cast the tie breaker in favor of building more parks, but Davona planned on building the jails with her own personal resources anyways.
- Having found a higher calling in guiding both people and and fey into living in harmony with one another, Sister Félicité offered her position as Prateria's Pontifex to her friend Osla. For the past six months, the two had struck up a strong bond of friendship, and Félicité was deeply impressed by Osla's insight and teachings. The halfling priestess of Deianeira felt confidant that the caelephilim shaman would serve the office of Pontifex admirably. Touched by the offer, Osla would eventually accept after giving it some thought. Félicité announced to the rest of the council of her intention to step down, and nominated Osla to succeed her. The rest of the Praterian High Council agreed to the choice, and Osla now counted among them, though Félicité would still remain in Promethea, tending to the newly constructed shrine of Deianeira.
- Sky sent an intellectually diplomatic letter to Erikos Pentras, Magister of Grondeloup. Sky wished to speak with him and apologize for his previous act that harmed him. Erikos accepted, and the two met in Wolfshold. Sky sincerely apologized, and offered to shared his knowledge and discoveries with Erikos. The Argent Rose scholar, very impressed by Sky's apology, readily accepted it, and the two struck up a sudden friendship of an intellectual nature found only between learned men.

## Overpopulation

Due to the fame of the Evergreen Company, and their massive recruitment drive, Prateria is being overrun by new immigrants. Without housing, the new arrivals are force to squat on any free land available, hampering construction efforts. In addition, so many new mouths to feed are overburdening the nation's food supply.

While the party's number of followers are significantly over Prateria's population capacity, Promethea and the nation suffer the following events every month until the population capacity is sufficient:

**Food Shortage:** Spoilage, treachery, or bad luck has caused a food shortage this turn. The Ruler must attempt a Stability check. If you succeed, Consumption in the next Upkeep Phase increases by 50%. If you fail, Consumption in the next Upkeep Phase increases by 100%.

**Squatters (Settlement, Continuous):** All of the free area in the settlement is taken over by beggars, troublemakers, and people unable to find adequate work or housing; they camp there with tents, wagons, and shanties. The cost to construct buildings in the settlement increase by 50% of the base price. Fame and Stability decrease by 1, and Unrest increases by 2. The Warden may try to disperse the squatters with a Stability check. Success means the squatters are dispersed and the event is not continuous, but if a House or Tenement is not built in the settlement on the next turn, Infamy increases by 1 and Unrest by 2. Failing the Stability check means the event is continuous.

## The Rughir Tribe

A group of over a hundred orcs, many of whom are women, elderly, and children, enter Praterian territory. They are led by a half-orc, Attila Rakirsson, and they seek refuge in Prateria. They are the survivors of the destroyed Rughir tribe, originating from Norgrimm. Most of these survivors were sold into slavery after their defeat, and many of them found themselves into Lurindori slavery black market.

Their arrival causes panic and commotion. The Ruler, Councilor, or Warden must make a Stability check. If successful, Unrest increases by 1. If failure, then Unrest increases by 1d4+1 as panic and violence erupt.

Prateria can accept or reject these orcs. Accepting these orcs require the ruler or Councilor to make a Loyalty check to reassure the people that this is a safe act and convince them that this is the right thing to do. Failure results in Unrest increases by 1d4. The orcs will join the squatters. Prateria can create a new settlement for the orcs, or the orcs can reside in Promethea. They can reside in houses as normal, though their cultural identity will likely be lost. Alternatively, a Foreign Quarter can be built, allowing their cultural identity to remain much stronger, but may continue to support the divide between the Rughirs and the rest of the Praterian population.

The Rughirs would prefer to have their own settlement to continue their traditions of following the Ulfeddin, although a vocal minority among the youth seek to abandon they ways that failed them. They resent the pity and charity given to them, and are angrily driven to forge their own future and power.
