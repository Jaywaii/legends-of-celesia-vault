# Notes

- Vandarkaï 31st, 1 pm: Second Battle of Orn Sini begins. Having seen that the Praterian forces have mustered for a sortie, Klorgfang's forces encircled Castrum Argentum, and bring forth their four heavy catapults from the southeast. The fort itself is built atop the entry point to the underground village of Orn Sini, thus Klorgfang will first need to take the fort before invading the settlement. The opening shot of the battle is from Prateria's heavy cannon into Klorgfang's orc cavalry.
- During the first hour of the battle, half of Prateria's forces pour out of the north gate, with Argus and Davona at its head, and charge upwards onto the hill to strike at Klorgfang's norther forces, composed mainly of the trolls' trihorn cavalry. The rest of the forces guard the eastern walls of the fort, with their bombard able to shell the heavy catapults to the southeast while positioned in the northeast corner of the fort. However, Hrondgren's and Norsgurd's personal units shield much of the attack on the catapults, while their witches focus on healing and repairing. Argentum's south gate was protected by the obstacles created by Jupus, but the west gate is left completely undefended. However, Klorgfang is unable to take advantage of that side as the mass sortie has forced all of their cavalry on the west and north to converted onto the Praterian attackers, while the southern and eastern forces rally to the southeast to provide support to their leaders.
- In the next hour, two units of troll cavalry charge from around a hill, and slam into the exposed west flank of the Rochefort Fusiliers that followed Argus and Davona out of the fort. Up higher on the hill, the fighting it fierce, with Argus' unit receiving significant casualties, followed by Davona's unit, though the latter had more numbers to absorb the damage. With all five units of the troll cavalry engaged with the sorties forces, the battle along the slope of the northern hill sees numerous casualties on both sides. Both sides are roughly equal in number, two and a half thousand on each side. The trolls inflict numerous casualties, but the PRaterian forces focus their attacks on each enemy unit at a time, with warpriests enchanting their warhammers with *sun metal* and their wizards launching *fireballs* to stop the trolls from regenerating. In the south, Hrondgren and his units continue protecting and repairing the heavy catapults from the bombard's artillery, while their crews work hard to set them up.
- In the third hour, Hrondgren's catapults finish setting up. They begin hammering at the fort's southeast corner, and eventually make a breach in the walls. Hrondgren leads all of his forces into the breach. In the north, the fight between the sorties forces and the troll cavalry continues to be fierce. A number of Fusiliers and Praterian units, including Argus' personal guard and Attila's orcs, are decimated, and are forced to flee back into the fort, but the damage inflicted on the trolls are also starting to take its toll.
