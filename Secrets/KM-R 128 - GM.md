# Notes

- A lull of inactivity descends upon the War for the Evergreen as neither side makes any significant moves. Instead, both Prateria and Klorgfang focus on building up their forces while their borders have now reached each other. Jupus, with the aid of his Steamflogger Industries, begins the high priority task of constructing the large number of siege engines their armies will need.
- Asako returns to Rochefort to help improve her standing with the Argent Rose Society. She singlehandledly crafts an exquisite table carved with significant moments from the Argent Rose's history, and donates it to the Society. Rochefort's chapter-sage Joliette Beauchamp is greatly impressed, and has the table prominently displayed in the main hall of the lodge. Asako then spends the next couple of days performing in the public notable acts to promote the Society, and convincing people to visit the lodge and join the Society. Over the next few weeks, the Rochefort lodge would see a significant increase in visitors and interest in joining the Argent Rose, all the while admiring the fantastically crafter table on display.
- During the war's calm, Davona goes to Nettle's Crossing with the Stag Lord's wooden husk. Under the unblinking gaze of the shampbling corpse, Davona tosses the husk into the Greenbelt River. However, no peace comes to the corpse. After calling Davona's act a mockery, it once more threatens her to toss in the Stag Lord's corpse, or join him in agony. Davona tries to convince the corpse that she had done so, but it remains unconvinced. The corpse takes a step forward, raises its ranseur menacingly at Davona, and narrows its malevolent gaze on her. The Praetor of Prateria feels a chill run down her spine, but she shakes it off. With regret, she draws her sword, and sorrowfully declares that she will grant the man peace, one way or another. The corpse charges at Davona. She steps to the side just as it reaches her, and slices its torso in twain with one downward stroke. In an instant, Davona spins around with another swing to cut across the torso again. As the pieces of body hit the ground, they melt into water, even the ranseur, and the puddles of water trickle down into the river. Mournfully, Davona gathers several stones to raise a small cairn for Nettles, and inscribed a small memorial to the man.
- Upon returning to Legio II, Davona recruits a team to go an build a proper burial mound and statue for Nettles at his crossing, as well as a new sign for the bridge with a dedication to the man and ensuring the bridge's official name to be Nettles' Crossing. After the Praetor finishes a short text for the memorial's plaque, in which she recounts Nettle's tragic story, Davona tries to sleep for the short few hours that she only needs thanks to *Elyssria's Memento*. However, no rest comes to her. For those couple of hours, she tosses and turns, her mind plagued with painful memories of her fall from grace, memories she thought she had laid to rest after her atonement. Amidst of all of the disturbing imagery, one remains constant. The wet, decomposed face of Davik Nettles is at the centre of the nightmares, repeatedly moaning its threats to Davona to drag her to the deadly depths. Davona would later wake up, drenched in sweat, still tired and receving no rest. While her teams are able to alleviate her fatigue and heal her sickness from a lack of sleep, the nightmares remain constant. A day later, the crew that Davona ordered to construct Nettles' memorial came running back to the fort. In a panic, they explain they were confronted by a ranseur-wielding, animated decomposed corpse calling for the Stag Lord's body to be thrown into the river. It seems that the undead was not as destroyed as Davona originally thought.
- Davona consults with the rest of the Company. Sky and Argus volunteer to *teleport* to the Stag Fort, hoping to find any of the Stag Lord's remains. They appear in the middle of the fort, surrounded by a meagre skeleton crew of Klorgfang soldiers. Argus immediately low-key threatens them in a nonchalant tone to keep their distance, and to not disturb them. The guards call out the alarm, but fall back and remain behind cover, content to only watch the intruders. Meanwhile, Sky casts *locate object*, focusing on the Stag Lord's body. The pair tour around the fort, searching for anything they could use, and the Klorgfang guards stay out of their path, hanging back a good distance away. Finally, Sky concludes there are no remains of the Stag Lord at the fort. He sits down to think about the situation, while Argus stands beside him, nonchalantly conversing with the cowering guards. Sky concludes that Nettles' is now a special haunt, and cannot be destroyed through mundane means. Additionally, the mythic magic of the Heart of the Forest had mutated the Stag Lord's corpse when it left him, transmuting it into something else. With no remains existing, it will be impossible to fulfill Nettles' request in order to help him move on from the mortal plane. With no access to more powerful magicks, they will need to exorcise him through a ritual.
- Argus and Sky return to Promethea, and explain the situation to the rest of the Evergreen Company. Sky then begins making preparations to perform the exorcism, and confront Nettles' in a psychic duel to help him move on to the afterlife.
