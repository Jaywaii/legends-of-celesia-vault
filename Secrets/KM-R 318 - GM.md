# Notes

## Viriquia

### 5 AM to 6 AM

**Crisis #1:** A food depot has beed covered by the fog. The survivors need those supplies. Escort a team of Radimite priests to the deport so they can clear the fog. Protect them while they clear the fog and the team to get the supplies out from zombies and fairies. Every supply pack recovered counts as 1 favor towards the Bishop. The death of each priest reduces the Bishop's trust, and failure to recover any supplies worsens his attitude.

**Crisis #2:** Nearby, a waystation set up for the wounded is assaulted by zombies. The healers need help evacuating the wounded.

**Solving Both:** However, the route gets cut off from the route used to retrieve the supplies from the depot. Traversing both routes is possible by going through the fog, but those who do automatically take zombie damage as they are swarmed by unseen near-infinite numbers of zombies until they reenter a cleared zone. Once they exit the fog, one zombie horde token appears after them.

#### Step 1: Fight to Depot

PCs must fight a horde of zombies clustered around the depot while protecting laborers and priests. Some guard units are there for backup, but Marquis requests PCs to do the majority of the fighting.

#### Step 2: Help Aid Station

Once PCs clear a way to the depot, guards from nearby Aid Station run towards them for help. The Aid Station is at risk of falling to the horde, and they need help evacuating the wounded. Once some PCs cross over to the Aid Station, special mist wraiths emerge from the fog and explode, filling corridors with fog.

#### Step 3: Evac

PCs now have to escort supplies-carrying laborers and wounded patients and staff down south off the map. They need to reach off the map, at which point, new reinforcements reach them to fully cover the escape.

### 6am to 7 am

**Crisis #1:** a huge amalgamation of zombie corpses in cyclops shape (the Amalgamated Abomination) with a small horde attacks the cathedral

**Crisis #2:** a huge whirlwind of wraiths (the Wraithly Whirlwind) that casts spells and ejects wraiths with a small horde attacks the castle gates

### The Zombie Horde

- each token has 100 hp, DV 20, no DR.
- does automatic damage (8d12 per adjacent zombie horde token)
- when damaged, attempt grapple +30 (+10 per adjacent zombie horde token)
- when grappled, +40 to maintain grapple (+15 per adjacent zombie horde token) to inflict 2d12 x zombie dmg + move 1 square towards the mist
- if damage passes DR, inflict 2d4 Str or Dex dmg per adjacent zombie horde token (Fort save vs DC 30 for half (+5 per adjacent horde token)
- zombie horde begins climbing up over each other for form rising wave of zombies against flying PCs
