# Notes

## Argent Rose

### Six Months Later

- We last saw Occult Investigation Team 13 in Jeshakaï 109. They had descended into the necropolis of Ing-gol'zha all the way to the 4th focal tower, the Gate of the Mother. There they had fought the apparition of Agatha Sorliu, a member of the failed Firesong Expedition two hundred years ago. The team returned to the surface with Agatha's remains in order to recover, both physically and mentally.
- The team returns to the Rochefort Lodge, returning Agatha's remains and reporting to Chapter-Sage Joliette Beauchamp. Agna decides to take her leave to recover the amount of mental trauma she experienced in such a short time.
- With the growing amount of danger being reported from the necropolis by the team, the Argent Rose decide to take a more cautious approach. The Society gives Team 13 a break to recover, train, and do more research. Meanwhile, Beauchamp organizes an expedition to secure Hushglow Tower from any outside interference. The Hushglow Expedition is composed of around a hundred people, a mix of scholars and security teams. It is being led by Rose Scholar Thonra 'Mindshield' (female half-orc abjurer 7). Thonra is only responsible with administrating the expedition's personnel and site security. Team 13 does not need to answer to her (and neither she to them), but she will assist them in any capacity as long as they do not interfere with her primary task. The expedition personnel are copper experts or lower, so they will not be able to perform any delving mission into the necropolis, but are capable enough to perform mass cooperative casting to provide a minimal level of magical containment measures. The Hushglow Expedition departed at the end of Jeshakaï, and sets up at the tower at the end of Orcsaï 109.
- During the off time, Rex is requested for another mission for his expertise and background, and needs to leave the team. Two new members are assigned to Team 13, and time is take to bring them up to speed and for the team's new roster to gel together.
- Team 13 researches lot of needed information. They piece together the rituals to seal the three towers they passed through so far. They also discover that they will need to travel to the Dimension of Dreams, otherwise known as the Dreamlands, to collect the components for those rituals. They will also need a legendary-crafted dreamcatcher to bring with them. They do learn the ritual to travel to the Dreamlands, the Breach of the Veil of Dreams. Team 13 also spends a lot of their time gathering blocks of adamantine to be crafted into the idols. They also work to garner the influence and favour of the Argent Rose to get some of their legendary sculptors to craft the idols. The cost is hefty, as soon after their work is completed, those sculptors are committed to Argent Rose sanatoriums to attend to their new madnesses. Suffice to say, there are not many in the Society who are happy with Team 13 crippling some of the world's greatest artists, regardless fo the need.
- The next step is to have those idols blessed by their respective Elder Horror. Again after much research, Team 13 is able to piece together the rituals, each unique to each of the seven Elder Horrors, that will bless the idols. There is a lot of risk, however, so Team 13 also spends a lot of time training and improving their skills. Once they feel confident, they will be able to get the idols blessed. Their training will also help them when they decide to enter the Dreamlands to retrieve the material components for the sealing rituals.
- Unfortunately, Team 13 will need to return and continute exploring the necropolis of Ing-gol'zha in order to unlock the remaining focal towers first before they can shut down and seal the whole necropolis. Fortunately, Team 13 has been assigned to the Prateria lodge upon its completion for the duration that Ing-gol'zha remains an active threat. Not only can they use the Promethea lodge as a closer base to launch their expeditions into the necropolis, they can keep a closer eye on it and Hushglow Tower. In fact, Team 13 had to eliminate several groups of cultists and monsters that became attracted to Hushglow Tower, with each encounter contributing to their training.
- Six months later (Tierlan 110), Team 13 is ready to go: has rituals for Par'manorok, Askulioth, Xin'carlath, Breach the Veil of Dreams, idols for the 3 horrors, knows which items to get for the 3 horrors.
- *By the time you return to Hushglow Tower, the Hushglow expedition has fully established their base upon the tower's hill. The tower, whose remains were just a circle of crumbling stone walls, is now covered by a web of scaffolding, covered with thick cloth. Tents, makeshift watch towers, and wagons converted into living spaces encircle the now cleared out area around the tower. Argent Rose scouts are the first to meet with you when the base comes into sight. They obviously saw you coming and were expecting you. After a quick verification of your identities and Argent Rose allegiance, they escort you to the base and to Thonra.*
	- *"Welcome back, Team 13," the urokhan woman greets you. "as you can see, we've secured the site. We've also been doing archeological digging in the surrounding area. Apparently there used to be a small settlement that was centered around the tower, so we have that to occupy ourselves while maintaining the perimeter." Thonra shows you around where you can get supplies, where you can rest, and the mobile infirmary. There are actually a number of Argent Rose personnel here. They don't seem to have any visible wounds. "It's mostly headaches and bouts of mild confusion. Troublesome, but not completely unexpected for this type of situation. We typically have these issues with our people working in the tunnel below. We have people on short rotation in those areas."*
	- *Thonra leads you into the tower ruins. There are several scholars studying the remaining walls, excavating the top layer of the ground, and several armed personnel around the stairwell at the center of the tower ground, the one that winds down into the underound. They seem to be both guarding and studying the round stone slab that once covered it. The slab itself is now wrapped in chains and hooked up to a series of pulleys that can be operated to move it on or off the stairwell. You descend the stone stairs, which have now been reinforced and now has safety handrails installed by the construction crew. At the bottom of the stairwell, a long corridor stretches out. The corridor's walls are engraved withcarvings depicting various different peoples performing vile ritualistic acts to appease representations of the Cosmic Horrors. Thonra explains "We've been able to confirm your findings that these carvings were engraved over many different periods of time, from the more recent towards the stairwell, to the older ones further in. We've identified no less than 7 distinct cultures have been present at this site over many millenia." Different from the last time you were here are the groups of scholars studying the carvings, and the guards stnading by newly built reinforced gates installed at regular intervals. "Not impregnable, but hopefully just enough to slow down anything that tries to come out."*
	- *You arrive at the final security gate, which opens up to a balcony overseeing a vast cavern in which the necropolis of Ing-gol'zha. The cavern is lit up by seven columns of sickenly yellowish light, which is carried along a thin mist that permeates throughout the city. Each column of light is centered upon a twisted tower. The city itself is a small sea of twisted, alien architecture, with no straight lines or roads, and buildings twisting into each other. From the balcony you stand on, there is a staircase built into the side of the cavern that descends down towards the city. "This is as far as I go. None of the expedition personnel have gone down, but we have been getting reports of howls echoing from the cavern, sometimes whispers as well, though none can recall what exactly was said. Best of luck to you all."*
