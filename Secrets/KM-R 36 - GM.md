# Notes

- The Grand Colonial Ball continued. Arina approached Captain Gilda Farini who represented the Sotillini Cartel. After some pleasantries, Farini offered a deal for resources in exchange for the Mariporta Lumber Consortium owning a sawmill in the Company’s nation. Arina renegotiated for a larger initial investment in exchange for guaranteeing the sawmill’s completion in a much shorter time frame of 6 months. Farini accepted but stated that she was only authorized to increase the investment by double.
- Remembering that their father Darawesh worked with the dwarves of Harkûr as a master carpenter, Sky and Davona sought out Soberk Harkûr. The young dwarf was pleased to meet with the children of Darawesh, and was saddened to hear of his passing. He wished to offer an investment in Prateria in exchange for the construction of a foundry in Prateria to be run by the Harkûr Trade Guild. Sky and Davona renegotiated instead of a foundry, they would build a guildhall for them to run. They haggled on the costs, and came to an agreement.
- Ayaki was approached by Joliette Beauchamp, Chapter-Sage of Lurindor’s branch of the Argent Rose. She stated that she wished to expand the Society’s presence in their nation by investing the Society’s resources. It would involve building a bardic college in their capital, to be run by the Argent Rose.
- Celia Sovrani met with the Company to offer her cousin Niccolo’s congratulations on their success, and to establish a trade route between Promethea and Coracentia.
- Arina came back to Varano with an alternate offer. For his investment, they would build him a noble villa, give him an honorary title, and pay for his house’s servants and cost of living. Aldo found this new proposal acceptable.

Cethin had been stealthily spying on Drelevo. Although the other colony leader hadn’t committed to any deals yet, Drelevo seemed eager to get his hands on as much investment as possible.
- After the Grand Colonial Ball, the Evergreen Company discuss several investment offers that were brought up. Accepted deals were:
	- Mariporta Lumber Consortium sawmill
	- Aldo Varano’s noble villa
	- Harkûr Trade Guild guildhall
	- Argent Rose Society bardic college
	- Sovrani Syndicate trade route
- A couple of days later, the Company finalized their deals, and begin making their way back to Promethea. At Onestrio’s, they retrieved Darawesh’s body.
- Upon arriving in Promethea, they retrieved the Heart. Félicité and Bekka kept an eye on the vineyard where they buried the Heart of the Forest. They had to shoo away some inquisitive fey, but the most notable effect was that the vines and grapes now grew large with a slight pink glow. The wine produced now carried an exotic, otherworldly taste. The farmers had dubbed the win ‘Promethean Pink’. The land had also been transformed to be more bountiful than before. Sky and Jupus worked together to splice the vines to grow these marvelous grapes elsewhere, but it seems theybwill only grow in this particular vineyard. Fortunately, this small patch of land seemed to be permanently transformed by the Heart of the Forest as far as they can tell.
- As the Company made preparations to return the Heart of the Forest to the Old Arborfane, word reached them that many travelers and hunters were being waylaid by magical beasts such as large wolves, plant monsters, and hostily fairies, all along the areas bordering the Old Arborfane. Sky suggested they visit the Old Beldame before entering the forest to see if she has any insight into the forest.
- The Company departed from Promethea, riding upon the vardo they had taken from the Havenford family. However, along there way, they saw in the distance a dust up heading their way. When they got closer, they saw what was approaching them. It was a troupe of a dozen green hussars, beings made of vines and branches, shaped like winged human riders on horses. They were no doubt attracted to the heart they were carrying. Although the hussars may have been acting on the wil of the Old Arborfane, their charge with lances pointed at the Company forced them the defend themselves, and Jupus fired the opening shot. The foremost hussar took the shot straight in the chest, wood splinters expliding out of its back.

## Session 36.5

Blasted by Sky's *fireball*, the troupe of green hussars were easy pickings for the combined might of the whole Evergreen Company. Already burnt and smoldering, the remaining wooden agents of the Old Arborfane were smashed and cut down, but not before inflicting some solid blows, as Ayaki could attest. The hussars spoke no words nor voiced any pain as they were chopped down, except for the creaking of wood. Once truly the dead, the Company could see that man and horse were actually one whole plant creature made of wood and leaves. Even their weapons, shields, and armor were a part of them.

The Evergreen Company continued their traveling to the hut of the Old Witch of the Evergreen. Along their way, packs of dire wolves, inquisitive fairies, and strange plant creatures appeared. Some of the more hostile creatures attempted to approach, lured by the power of the Heart, while others hanged warily back to observe the party. The Evergreen Company easily drove off the aggressive ones with a fantastic show of force. Aside from the assault from the green hussars, the Company faced no other opposition on their trek.

On the evening of the third day of traveling, the Company arrived at the witch's hut. They noticed many charms made of sticks, stones, or bones adorning the hut or arrayed around the hut. A plume of smoke escapes from the chimney, and a flickering light illuminates the small window. Before you can ring the bell, the door creaks open, and the silhouette of a crooked hand beckons you in.

Upon entering the small hut, you see the old pale-green crone sitting in a rocking chair near the firepit. "So, have you finally made your preparations to return the Heart?" the Old Beldame asked while peering intensely at you all.
