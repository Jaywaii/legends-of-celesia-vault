# Notes

**<u>Session 130 - April 11, 2020</u>**
- After recovering from his psychic duel with Davik Nettles, Sky and Asako researched the affliction he and Davona are suffering from. He learns that he and his sister are under the effect of a *nightmare* enchantment. Asako is able to relieve them with a *dispel evil* spell by drawing upon her mythic connection to Sky. While Asako goes out to acquire a *scroll of dispel evil* for more permanent access to the spell, the Evergreen Company decide to put off dealing with Nettles' to a later day.
- The Evergreen Company decide to focus on expanding Prateria's borders. They issue orders to claim the land along Klorgfang's northern border to halt their encroachment. They also have a fort, Castrum Ferro, built in their southwest corner of their territory, and reassigned Legio I to garrison it. The army makes good time to their new base, thanks to the massive armament program pushed by the Evergreen Company the previous month.
- The Evergreen Company also decide during this time to formally invest the position of Proconsul to Legatus Rialla, should anything disastrous happen to Arina, especially in this time of war. It is decided that the former pleasure girl of the Carnival of Tears has grown into the right kind of person Arina and the rest of the Company feel comfortable to lead Prateria. Rialla humbly accepts the responsibility, hoping that she will never have to exert its authority.
- A surprise from Rochefort arrives in Promethea. Prateria's neighbour to the north decided to demonstrably show their vested interest in them by establishing two major trade routes from Rochefort to Promethea. What is initially thought to be another army by the Prometheans turns out to be an enormous train of merchants and wagons filled with goods and luxuries.
- Klorgfang is apparently not being idle during this time. Additional attempts at sabotage on Praterian farms, smithy, and stables occur over the course of the week. Fortunately, Bekka and Davona's leadership manage to ensure that the fires are put out in timely fashion, livestock is recuperated, and pre-planned surplus quickly replace the damaged tools and equipment. Interestingly enough is that all staff were not harmed in the sabtoage. Instead, they found themselves waking outside of their buildings, with no memory of how they got there. The Evergreen Company find this mercy odd for their enemies. Upon closer inspection of the sabotage sites, Bekka and Davona discover traces of fairy dust left at the sites.

- Second fairy sabotage: Davona is informed that the Smithy in Promethea is sabotaged. Tools are stolen, equipment is broken. The staff suddenly find themselves waking up outside of the building, or finding themselves there with no memory of leaving, while the building set aflame. If Davona succeeds at her leadership check, the fire is quickly put out, and the equipment and tools are quickly replaced by surplus that was stored away.
