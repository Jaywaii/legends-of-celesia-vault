	- Seer of Souls: *"Upon achieving his apotheosis to godhood, the Deathless Shroud's apprentices each became his first apostles. In recognition of their dovtion and mastery of magicks of life and death, he bestowed upon each of them a title: the Wraith of Doom, the Specter of Ruin, the Oblivion of Light, the Ghast of Loss, the Seer of Souls. Each claimed part of the world to reshape in their master's image. Their plans never came to pass, for the nightmare they would inflict upon the world was usurped by Horrors from Beyond the Veil. What became of the Apostles of the Shroud has been lost to the secret darkness, for the great heroes of the Second Age now had the power to stand against. With beings with enough power to stand against them, the Apostles, if any survived the Horrors, must have receded into the shadows to act in secret for their master."*
	- The True Path: *"The True Path beckons us to relinquish the shackles of this fake reality and embrace the gospel that pulses in the void beyond the veil. The Old Ones gifted us this truth upon our feeble consciousnesses before the false ones interfered and hid it from us. But we still hear their song. Let us chant their hallowed words, and tear down the lies the false ones! Spread the hymn to all minds and reshape our world in the image of the true masters!"*
	- The Whyngard Asylum: *"Nestled on the secluded island of Whisperlight, the imposing Whyngard Manor stood as a beacon of both opulence and obscurity. Originally the ancestral home of the enigmatic Whyngard family, the manor took on a new identity under the stewardship of Dr. Claudius Whyngard, a figure as brilliant as he was eccentric. With his vast wealth and unorthodox methods, Dr. Whyngard transformed the estate into the renowned Whyngard Asylum, a sanctuary for the troubled souls of society. Behind its grand façade, the asylum housed dozens of disturbed minds, offering them respite from the torment of their afflictions. Yet, whispers of strange occurrences echoed within its walls, tales of patients emerging from its confines changed, their once fractured minds now imbued with an eerie clarity and purpose. As quickly as it had appeared, the Whyngard Asylum vanished from Whisperlight, leaving behind a legacy shrouded in mystery and speculation, its true purpose and the fate of its patients forever entwined with the island's enigmatic past."*
	- Vorakios: *"The Doom That Came to Veridia ushered the end of Selimbral and Barom-gar. Its gloom shrouded the verdant land, breathing death to elf, dwarf, human, and centaur alike, and grew from its hideous work. In its wake came twisted horrors turning loved ones against each other. A desperate shot in the dark by forces united in life pierced the wave of death from which shone a glimmer of hope, and a moment to bulwark against it. But the toll had already burdened, and the gloom gave way to emerald fangs and wings who found easy prey."*
- Sky's research:
	- Sealing Ritual on Vorakios: *The ritual to seal Vorakios and his power was a desperate last-ditch effort cobbled together between the archmages of Selimbral, the high priests of Barom-gar, and the Thunderhoof shamans. With Vorakios on the cusp of ascending godhood, his destruction was well beyond the means of ven these powerful masters. However, containing him was possible. The bow Pyrouranos was originally crafted by the Thunderhoof centaurs to destroy Vorakios. Once it was realized that this was insufficient, the elves of Selimbral and the dwarves of Barom-gar instead used its unique magical nature to use as a focus for a ritual that would be known as the Sealing of the Undying Eye. Special totems were prepared by the Thunderhooves that would be integral in sealing Vorakios' power. Some were able to be planted in advance free of danger, but most would have to be magically transported to their locations as part of the ritual. When the time came, all three peoples united all of their forces together for one last battle. Luring Vorakios to their planned last stand, the allied forces died in tremendous numbers against the lich's horde while the casters enacted the ritual. Just as their last line of defense started to crumbled against the unending wave of undead, the casters completed the ritual. The totems teleported to specific points around the Grey Fang Peaks, and Pyrouranos was empowered. One centaur champion seized the bow, and fired an impossible shot from it towards Vorakios. The arrow, enchanted with the rituals magic, pierced all of Vorakios' magical defenses and pierced him in the eye, triggering the sealing. The lich was shunted back into his lair, and his Gloom retreated with him. All of the undead collpased into withered corpses, but so too did all of the ritual casters. Four high priests of Barom-gar, five archmages of Selimbral, and seven great shamans of the Thunderhooves each gave their Final Sacrifice to complete the ritual, and seal Vorakios and his power away.*
	- Argent Rose records on Vorakios:
		- *Vorakios is a cyclops lich who was active during the years of 12,564 to 12,575 of the Third Age. Known aliases are the Undying Eye, the Seer of Souls. His origins, both as a living being and when he transformed into a lich, are unknown.*
		- *Based on eyewitness accounts of his appearance and visible paraphernalia, Vorakios appears to be a worshiper of the Deathless Shroud, aka Xarios.*
		- *Vorakios was able to command vast armies of undead, numbering in the hundreds of thousands.*
		- *Vorakios' forces were composed primarily of mindless zombies and skeletons. In addition, he had several junior cyclops liches as lieutenants, and a cadre of living cyclops acolytes called the Cult of the Undying Eye. However, most peculiar was a number of units composed of Unseelie fey at his command. According to first-hand accounts, these fairies' sylvan nature appeared to be twisted with necromantic energy.*
		- *It is unknown how or where Vorakios is drawing his tremendous power.*
		- *On a peculiar note, prehistoric archeological findings found in Carpathos makes mention of another Vorakios from the pre-Horror cyclopean civilization of Halakon. Halakonese hieroglyphs indicate that this Vorakios was also a powerful spellcaster serving as a vizier to the Halakon emperor.*
