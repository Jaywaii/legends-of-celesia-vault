# Notes

## Charcalthiak

- Hoard: 145 Goods, 110 Magic, 255 Wealth.
- A female black dragon seemingly in the process of transforming into a green one. Most scales were black, but about a fifth of them were in the process of turning into shades of green.
- A ring of grey hair found around one of her claws.

## Going After Vorakios

The beam of light from Pyrouranos' arrow is quickly fading. As the PCs decide on what to do, Agus receives a mental alert that his Protean Guard just activated. Rishoi receives a mental alerted from her followers that dark clouds suddenly formed above Promethea. Waves of blighted fey and undead began surging forther from them. Arina tells the others to go to Pyrouranos' light before it fades away. Arina and Rishoi will go back to Promethea and lead the defence against the attacks. A re-awakened Iolanthe appears and states that she will lead Thunderhoof volunteers to aid in Promethea's defence, much to her mother's concern.
