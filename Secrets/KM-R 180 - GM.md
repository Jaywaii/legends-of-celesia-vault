# Notes

- Defeated Marguerite La Sagesse (CR 14): 11,250 XP (2,812 XP each)
- Total: 10,912 XP each

## Library of Golzha'thorak

- Enter a large room with shelves reaching from floor to ceiling. On the shelves are stone-like spheres about two feet in diameter. Each spheres surface is covered with tiles with thulkesh characters. The tiles can slide, and each movement seemingly composes a new line. The amount of character tiles possibly reveal a near infinite script, though the order to slide the tiles is dizzying for mere mortal minds.
- Upon entering, a moaning howl echoes, and suddenly the library begins to rumble. The characters on the spheres begin shifting and locking into a new combination. Shelves begin to shift, from side to side, shifting into and out of the ceiling and floor, forming a maze, splitting your party apart into different areas.
- Each separate group must make an DC 20 Intelligence or Wisdom check to figure out the thulkesh record sphere (needs to read Thulkesh). Otherwise need DC 25 Survival check. Each time 2 groups succeed, they figure out how to reach each other. Success 1 sanity, fail 2d4 sanity.
