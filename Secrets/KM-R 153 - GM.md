## Arina's Death

*The blinding pain overwhelms you as Norsgurd rips open your chest with his bare, clawed hands. All you see is red, and then... You are surrounded in darkness, yet you remain visible to yourself. The pain is gone. You look down, and instead of your exposed, torn organs, you are whole, completely untouched. You see no ground beneath your feet, yet you know you are merely standing, not floating. A gentleman's humming from behind you casues you to whip around. Before you is a simple, yet elegant round table. Three sets of children-like decorative tea cups, a tea pot, and a plate of sweets sit atop it. There are three seats; two are empty, and the last is occupied by a tall, pale thin man in a black suit, his eyes obscured by the pulled down brim of his tall bowler hat.*

***"My little sweetling, please join us,"** the thin man invites you, with a grin far too wide for his head.*

*Before you can respond, you find yourself already sitting at the table, the man already pouring fresh, steaming tea into your cup. **"Please, have a bite to eat."** The thin man places a small plate with one of the sweets on it. An angel cake. "It was your favourite, don't you remember?"*

*The thin man leans back into his chair. You still can't see his eyes. **"Well, my dear Arina, you seem to have bitten off more than you can chew. Not that I'm disappointed or anything, but I had hoped for you to grow even more. Still, perhaps this would help you reconsider some decisions you've made? I've noticed that you have not quite let go with your past. I only seek to protect you, to aid you in your growth. And I can offer you so much more..."***

*The thin man gestures, and your eyes follow to the third seat. A small, feminine figure in a dark cloak now sits there, unmoving, silent as death. The thin man continues. **"I admit, you never cease to surprise me with the choices you make. Always unexpected... but that is the spice to living, wouldn't you say? Ah, but how rude of me. 'Living' is the issue here, isn't it? Perhaps you would allow me to help you with that..."***

*A bright from above light suddenly begins illuminating the darkness. You notice the thin man recoil slightly before regaining his composure. **"Tsk. The old man seeks to interfere, it seems. Perhaps I should have continued to paid more attention to her before she found him. Ah, but then, how could I divert any attention from you, my dear. Our little tea time appears to be at an end. But please, allow me to show you my sincerity in the challenges to come."***

*As the warm, bright light engulfs you, the thin man tips the brim of his hat up, and gives you a wink with his right eye. One you recognize as the same as a dear friend. And then, all is bright, and warm, and safe, and no hint of darkness exists.*

*You gasp for breath, and you find yourself among your companions, returned to below the troll keep, with the sounds of battle around you. As you return to battle, a man's faint humming accompanies you.*

## Davona's Death

*It is cold and dark. The last you remember was Hrondgren's fearsome claw breaking through Arina's sunlight shield, ripping into your gut, then ripping it all out of you. And then, there is warmth and comfort. Opening your eyes, you see that you are home again. Not in Promethea, but in Ashaki, before the troubles reached your childhood home. Outside, the sun shines warmly, and the familiar sandy wind wafts through the window.*

***"You know, you've given myself and a few others quite a few worries in the past several years."***

*You whip your head around to the source of the man's voice. There is a man now standing in the middle of the room, where there was none before. He is tall, with a trim beard and mustaches, and shoulder-length hair slicked back. You are unsure of the man's ethnicity, but there is a hint of the Eastern people within his features. He is dressed in a simple, black suit, and seems out of place of Ashaki environment, yet completely at ease.*

***"That being said,"** the man continues, **"the path you've followed since your first failing has impressed quite a few of the higher-ups, myself included. It wasn't the path we hoped for, but one that we are happy to see you travel, little sister. Remember that, and continue growing. There are many paths to Justice, and even with vengeance in your heart, though they have many ledges from which you can still fall. You know that all too well, sadly. But look at where you are now, and how the lives you've touched have grown, and you will know your answer."***

*The sun outside begins growing brightly more and more. **"Ah, Pops is here. I'm sorry that you have to go through so much pain, but I'm glad it gave us this opportunity. Before you go, remember that we have another little sister still in pain. She is strong, though, and we are supporting her as much as we could, but the little godling is just as strong... and just as cruel. I wish I could go down there myself, sword ablazed like in the old days, but this is your task to finish."***

*As you are engulfed in the warm light, the man's voice reaches you as a whisper. **"Stay strong, and know that He, and the rest of us, love you, and support you. Show'em who's boss, little sis."***

*You gasp for breath, and you find yourself among your companions, returned to below the troll keep, with the sounds of battle around you. As you return to battle, a familiar warmth, long thought lost, flutters in your heart.*

## Jupus & Gnawfang's Deaths

*At first, there was pain, incredible pain as the draconic drake tore you open. You thought it was a cliché, but your last memories are those of your life. In particular, those of your family. Happy dinners with your parents and your brothers and sisters. The day your father came home signing the deal of a lifetime, one that would set up your family for life. The day the Regulators arrested him for mass murder. When your family were thrown out of your childhood home. The disdain and insults and abuse hurled against you. Running away from your family in shame, finding refuge in the filth of the sewers. Swearing vengeance against the ones that tore down your family.*

*You wonder what happened to that anger. The choice to join the Evergreen Company was made out of desperation, yet so much has happened. Bequeathed with mythical power. Becoming leader of a new nation. Operating your own business. At what point did your family, and your vengeance, fall to the wayside? And, what now? Now that you are dead, will they even know? Will they even care after you abandoned them?*

*Warmth suddenly fills the coldness. Darkness is broken by bright light. You gasp for breath, and you find yourself among your companions. In a way, your new family. Beside you, you hear a familiar gasp. Gnawfang, covered in her own blood, clothing torn, but now fully whole and bathed in warm light, just like you, though now in dire rat form. It is a shocking moment when you realize that she who started out a just a rat companion, and was now a faithful friend, had also perished. That you could have lost her, like you lost your father, your family.*

*But there is no time to dwell on such thoughts. Your companions are still fighting. And now that you are breathing, so can you.*

---

*You are resting in Legio I's camp, hours after the final confrontation with Hrondgren, who is now dust in the wind. You are enjoying some well-deserved rest inside a private tent, all the meetings and post-battle discussions done for the day. Your relaxation is interrupted by the tent's flap. It is Gnawfang, having resumed her gnome girl appearance thanks to her magical hat.*

*"Master, a moment?" she asks. She looks troubled, and you invite her in. She takes a seat across of you.*

*"Master... I-I'm scared..." she whispers, hugging herself tightly. "Never felt pain like that. Never... died before..."*

*Before you can reply or comfort her, Gnawfang suddenly looks up to you. While she is still hugging herself, her eyes are steel. "Never want to feel this again. Before being able to think like you, I follow instinct. Fight, or run. But now I know."*

*"Stronger", she growls out. "I want to get stronger. Like you. To survive. And to kill enemies. Whatever it takes."*

*Her fierceness is evident. You lean back in your seat. You were already making plans. Gnawfang had been with you since the beginning, even before you met the Company. Your bond is deeper than any friendship. Your strength is her strength, and hers is yours. You nod to her. Whatever it takes.*

## Rishoi's Death

*Pain overwhelms you as your body is torn apart by Norsgurd. It does not last long, replaced by sudden warmth and light. You find yourself amidst a green field on a bright, sunny day. There is a light wind, carrying the faint scent of sweet flowers. You realize in this moment, this is what peace is.*

***"I wish your mother could feel it as well,"** came a man's voice. You whip your head around to the source, and there is now a man in a red suit standing beside you, gazing out towards the field as you were doing so. The man appears to be one of the Eastern people, not quite as tall as you, but obviously well-fit. You know that this small frame holds in tremendous power.*

*He turns, and looks you up and down. **"She would have been proud of you, I think, had she remained on her path. We were saddened when you took the path you did. But now, look at you. All on your own, you've taken up where she left off, and now transcended beyond her. And it was not us who set you on this path, you sought our Father's light all on your own. Through your own growth, your own will. If you've ever felt that you did not deserve the gifts bestowed upon you, don't be. You've earned this."***

***"I am sorry I could not do more for your mother. She had been growing so well, and happiness was within her grasp. If it wasn't for that little upstart..."** The man's eyes narrow, and he reaches up to your shoulder. You see him wiping away a wisp of shadow clinging onto you. Most of it is blown away, but your shoulder remains marked with a hint of darkness. **"I'm afraid that will be up to you. But you are not alone. You have the support, from both the temporal and the supernal."***

*The man gives you a small smile, but it warms you and comforts you. **"I'm afraid we have to end this little chat here. You still have much work to do. I would help you along your way, but I think we both know you already know how."** And in a moment of insight, one based off the accumulation of all that you have learned, you realize he is right. Upon this realization, you find yourself being pulled to a colder, darker place, but one where you know you have to be. As you depart, the man's voice whispers to you. **"Remember that we, and especially He, loves you, little sister. And we are proud of you."***

*Light overwhelms your senses, and when it fades, you find yourself among your companions. Back in the cold underground beneath the trolls' keep. The clash of battle surrounds you, and though there is no time for contemplation, you rejoin the fight with light burning strong in your heart.*

## Asako's Death

*It is hours after the fight with Hrondgren, and Sky allows himself float in the cool, breezy night sky above Legio I's camp. All post-battle discussions have been done, and all loot identified. For Sky, it was all done almost automatically. He had other, more important thoughts on his mind.*

*His sisters had died. Yes, they now lived (he'll have to think about showing some form of gratitude to Rishoi for that), but that did not ignore the fact that he almost lost them for good. Or that they could have perished altogether. They may not be of the same blood, but they were his only family left nonetheless. And for all his power now at his fingertips, he almost lost them.*

*Glancing down, Sky notices Asako sitting in the camp, alone beside a camp fire. She seemed unnerved. He decides to float down, and join her. Davona may have died as well, but she came back with renewed vigor. No doubt that fiery god of hers played a hand in that. But Asako was not Davona. She barely stepped out of the library, and probably would still be there, if it was not for Sky bringing her out of that life and into his. Where she died. The realization of this gave Sky a rare moment of guilt.*

*The sylph joined his sister, who welcomed him quietly. They spent a few moments in quiet, not quite sure what to say. Only the crackling of the fire could be heard. It was Asako who finally broke the silence.*

*"I never thought I would experience that," she whispered. "I don't blame you, by the way. Yes, you know I can read you like my books. I knew what I was signing up for. I've read enough Argent Rose reports to know what to expect. Still... it's one thing to read about it, and then to live it."*

*Asako turns to her brother. "I feel we've drifted apart. Not just you and I, but Davona as well. It's understandable, we're adults now, and we all have our own goals. But now I realize, death is closer to us than most, and we might not be so fortunate to have Rishoi there the next time..."*

*Asako trails off, then shakes her head. "What I mean to say, we don't have all the time in the world. I have goals I want to accomplish, and I want my family close." She looks at Sky straight in the eye. "I know what you've been working on, and I want to help. And I want you and Davona to help me. Just like when we were kids, the three of us can accomplish anything. Just promise me, promise we'll always be there for each other, all right?"*

*Sky's answer is obvious. And now that this war is over, he can finally devote his attention to what is important to him. To him, and his sisters.*
