# Notes

## Trials of the Old Arborfane

### Laellien's Lament

#### Laellien's Ridge

- *Moving forward, you arrive at the edge of an outcropping, a ridge overlooking the creek below. Before you stands a tall woman with long black hair. Ahead of her is a slender young man, bearing a strong resemblance to the woman. He stands close to the edge. Both glow ethereally, but appear to be solid. The woman calls out "Laellien!" with her arm stretched out to the boy, while the boy appears frightful, taking trembling steps back. From all around you, a chorus of voices sing the entirety of Laellien's Lament.*
	- The goal is to prevent Laellien from falling. Every round, Lallwyn will try to move one square towards Laellien. If she does so, Laellien moves one square towards the edge. Both Lallwyn and Laellien can be targeted and interacted with. However, banshees fly up from below the outcropping and the hounds arrive from behind to interfere with the PCs. The PCs need to stop Lallwyn's advance, convince her to forgive and accept her son's love (Diplomacy 40). They must also convince Laellien that he has no need to fear anymore (Diplomacy 40). If a PC advances beyond Lallwyn towards Laellien, he takes a step back.

#### *Laellien's Lament*

##### 1st Verse

*Oh, let me free to come to you / Come to you, my son / Like a dream I see you / Our lives entwined in death*
*Why couldn’t I see your heart was full / Why didn’t I see your longing / I was blinded by my fear / I was guided by my anger*
- Response: *My mother, years have passed now / And my blood washed through the stream*

##### 2nd Verse

*Like a curse I remember that boy / White hair appeared through the trees / Where our caravan camped / Not worthy of the house of Lallwyn*
*I recall the rage at my throat / As I tore at the walls of your heart / Your bed empty, your window open / In my rage, I was ready to kill*
- Response: *My mother, years have passed now / And I gave my heart to my Nemeth*

##### 3rd Verse

*He hid among the trees that night / And helped you find the shadows / Rage burst through my heart / I screamed at your gentle face*
*A mask to hide your defiance / To defend what I thought was mine / Stay a step before my guards / Disappear between the branches*
- Response: *My mother, years have passed now / And my love still blooms from beyond*

##### 4th Verse

*I remember the sight of your hair / As I unleashed my guards in the glade / Fear rooted them to the ground / White robe blowing as you ran*
*Fleeing my rage for your love / Your precious ankle slipped away / Oh, if I could just take it back / I, your mother, twisted by my hate*
- Response: *My mother, years have passed now / And I fall forever in your dream*

##### 5th Verse

*Hounds teeth caught your flesh / You stumbled on the wet rock / Froth flashes white when water falls / Over stone I saw my son slip*
*I rushed to the edge of the ridge / Watched the water tumble over / Your skull broken bleeding / Ribbons of your blooming blood*
- Response: *My mother, years have passed now / And I am lost for ever to my only love*

## Mister Angelcakes

The 6 Evergreen Company members must be in a circle. They are drained 1 Mythic Power (gain 1 negative level if they have 0 power).
A circle of green light beams up from the ground underneath your feet. Glowing sparkles begin floating up. A ring of giant multicolored mushrooms grow up from the ground. The light brightens so strong enough to blind you. By the time you are able to see, you find yourself in a homely manor, yet everything seems distorted. Walls, furnitures, decorations appear stretched or warp.
