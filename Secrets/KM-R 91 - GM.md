# Notes

- The Evergreen Company returns to Promethea, figuring that more research is needed to figure out how to best enter the portion of the Old Arborfane south of Lake Iridescence.
- Tabrir goes to the Promethea Lodge of the Argent Rose Society to research about devils Bael or Furfur.
- Tobias takes some time off from adventuring to focus on building up Prateria's spy network. He will give Arina a budget request at the next council after he scouts out possible agents.
- Jupus, worried about his family back in Rochefort, requests some of his Steamflogger employees to look in on their last known address the next time the Steamfloggers need to go to Rochefort. He asks them to be discreet as he believes garnering more attention to the Gardefort family would be detrimental to everyone's health.
- After his employees had left, Jupus' most senior employee, a halfling named Sebastien Durant, approached him. Durant proposes that Jupus speak with the rest of Prateria's council to form treaties specifically with Rochefort. Durant has family and contacts back in Rochefort who have expressed wanting deeper ties with Prateria, and that both sides would benefit greatly. Jupus agrees to broach the proposal with the rest of the Evergreen Company.
- Attila is approached by a Rughir shaman named Murag Old-Bones. Murag confides to Attila that their tribe, while grateful for Prateria to have allowed them to seek refuge in their new nation, fears that their culture and traditions will not survive this generation unless they have a settlement of their own. The old orc urges Attila to speak with the Evergreen Company to aid in founding a new Rughir home where they can practice their ways and pass on their traditions to the next generation. In exchange, the tribe will aid Prateria. However, Murag senses that their people's patience is not for long, and that they younger folk are more willing to do whatever it takes to build a new home. Attila agrees, and speaks with Arina about the issue, and Prateria's Consul is open to the idea.
- Osla is prodded by Zazen, and she discovers that a new constellation of stars has appeared on the pig's back. Meditating on the stars' insight, Osla determines that she is urgently needed elsewhere. The trip should be short and not dangerous, but she must leave immediately. She does so, letting the others know that she should be back for next month's council.
- The Evergreen Company, with no progress being made in exploring the southern Old Arborfane, makes plans to distract themselves before the next council and the upcoming Viridian Games. They make preparations to explore the plains west of the Cypress Hills of the Evergreen Plains. Wondering about their nation's scouting ability, Davona seeks out Bekka, their Explorator. She finds the half-orc having drinks with Acteon, Dante, and Toro at the Old Leaf. Sharing drinks with them, Davona tries to fnd out how Bekka is doing. She thinks the half-orc is doing fine until Acteon speaks with her privately and confides that Belka has lost confidence in herself since all of the former Stag Lord bandits serving as her scouts were lost in the field. Davona thinks it would be good for Bekka to get out and convinces her to join the Evergreen Company on their next outing. Bekka agrees, and Davona rushes to tell the others. Thankfully, no one objects.

- Upon returning to Promethea, Osla's companion Zazen gets her attention. A new constellation of stars appeared on her back. After examining the stars and meditating on their insight, Osla informs the Company that she needs to depart for a short while. The stars are telling her that she is urgently needed elsewhere, though for what she is not exactly certain. However, she does not believe that the trip will be dangerous for her, though it is urgent. She promises to return as soon as she can.
