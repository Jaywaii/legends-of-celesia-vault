# Notes

- Preparation for the Viridian Games are underway, and invitations are sent out.
- Meanwhile, Arina assembles the Company to discuss her issue with her mysterious companion, Mister Angelcakes. The orc leader Attila was also invited by Tabrir, in hopes of proving his value to Prateria and its leaders. Arina had sought out the aid of Elyssria to find a way separate herself from Angelcakes. She received a vision of of an elder tree in a clearing of a thick forest. She was given the knowledge of a ritual to bind a Heart of the Forest and use its power for her to escape Angelcake's influence.
- The Company traveled to Arbor's Rest first, but realized that Arina's vision showed herself following a lake and a river from it heading into the Old Arborfane. While they spent the night there at the inn Arbor's Home, Arbor's Rest's governor, Domenico D'onofrio of the Steel Peacock Company, met with them to discuss the development of the settlement, and express concerns about the drag caused by the power of the Old Arborfane. Arborestians need to spend time, resources, and blood in performing the Old Rites, small superstitious rituals meant to appease the Old Arborfane and allow them to build and go about their daily lives. The Company informed him that the Old Arborfane is extremely old and powerful, moreso than them, and that it would be wise not to disturb it, as the Stag Lord had done.
- The next day, the Company traveled to the old Stag Lord's fort, then headed east, following the northern shore of Lake Iridescence, and then down the Thistledown Creek. Performing the *Oldwood Rite* daily, and constantly casting magic, the Company was relatively unmolested as they traveled through the Old Arborfane. After 3 days, they arrived at the Central Heart of the Forest, where they had replanted the Heart after it had been stolen by the Stag Lord. Within the cracked open trunk of the old elder tree, a new Heart sapling had grown forth. A ring of multicolored mushrooms enclosed around the crater the tree resided. The Company performs the ritual Arina had learned, but they are surprised when the ritual fails, its magic bouncing off. Sky realized that this ritual establishes a ward, and the Central Heart was already warded by the ritual they had performed to replant the Heart. The new ritual was also similar to the one they perfomed on the Northern Heart as part of Davona's task for Elyssria.
- The Company attempts to depart the forest by flight, but upon reaching the overhead canopy of leaves and branches, thorny vine shoot out to grab them. Osla and Rishoi are caught, but Sky *greased* Rishoi, allowing her to escape. Osla *jaunted* herself free, and the Company escaped. They returned to the Stag Lord's Fort to rethink their plans. They realized that they needed to cast this ritual on an unwarded Heart ofthe Forest. Also, there was more to Leak Iridescence to the south that they had not explored. Perhaps they were meant to follow a different river. They plan to scout the area south, but Sky advises that spending anymore time will likely disrupt their current plans, including the Viridian Games which was coming up soon.

## Prateria Events

### Firhen 111

*The flow of new settlers would not be stemmed this month. More followers of the Evergreen Company came to join their leaders in Promethea. However, there was still a lack of housing, forcing many to seek shelter by squatting the land around the settlement, interfering with additional construction.*

Unable to build new buildings in Promethea; Fame and Stability decrease by 1 for 1 year, and Unrest increases by 2. You may try to disperse the squatters with a Stability check. Success means the squatters are dispersed, the penalties are removed, and the event is not continuous. However, if a House or Tenement is not built by the next Event Phase, Infamy increases by 1 for 1 year and Unrest by 2. Failing the Stability check means the event is continuous, and you may not construct new buildings in that settlement until the event is resolved.

### Tierlan 111

*The immigration crisis continued to worsen. Despite new homes being built, more followers kept arriving to take over the land squatting of the previous, now housed, group of followers. Worse, the squallid conditions of the tent city were excellent breeding grounds for a plague to break out! Typhoid fever breaks out in the camps, and spreads quickly. Rishoi's hospital and the Madonna's Cathedral are quickly rushed by the infected, and the disease spreads throughout Promethea.*

Plague (Hex or Settlement, Continuous): A deadly sickness strikes the target hex or settlement. You cannot construct terrain improvements or buildings there while plague persists. Attempt two Stability checks, each with a penalty equal to the number of Brothels, Foreign Quarters, Highways, Inns, Piers, Roads, Stables, Stockyards, Tenements, and Waterfronts in the hex, and a bonus equal to the number of ss, Cathedrals, Herbalists, Hospitals, and Temples in the hex. If you succeed at both checks, the event ends, but Stability decreases by 2 for 1 year and Treasury by 1d3 BP. If you fail at one check, Stability decreases by 4 for 1 year, Treasury decreases by 1d6 BP, and Unrest increases by 1d3. If you fail at both, Stability decreases by 4 for 1 year, Treasury decreases by 1d6 BP, Unrest increases by 1d6, and in the next Event Phase the plague spreads to an adjacent hex.
