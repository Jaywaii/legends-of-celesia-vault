# Notes

- PCs  travel to Argentia's hidden lair. Series of skill challenges to determine how prepared Charcalthiak is for their arrival.
- Charcalthiak has corrupted Argentia's magical wards and defenses of her lair.

## Traveling to the Lair

- Base DCs are set to 70.
- Each challenge can be avoided, but takes longer and increases number of checks to 3 at -10 DC. Increase travel by 1 hour.
- Checks are made as group, using lowest score and other results as aids.

### First Challenge

- **Raven Watch (air):** Charcalthiak has ensorcelled flocks of ravens that patrol the skies above the mountains. Bypass with Stealth or illusionary deception. Killing the ravens still alert Charcalthiak by their disappearance, but she has no further knowledge. If fail, ravens begin flying off in the direction of the lair. PCs get one chance to stop them.
- **Rock Avalanche (ground):** The mountains around Argentia's lair have been booby trapped to cause landslides on intruders. Perception to detect the trapped landslides, Disable Device to get through. Reflex DC 40 for half damage (20d6), DR applies.

### Second Challenge

- **Acidic Gas (air):** Plumes of poisonous gas fill the skies from pools of toxic acid created by Charcalthiak's corruption of Argentia's wards. Perception to detect the near scentless gas, Fortitude to avoid taking 3d4 Constitution  dmg each round. Each failed Perception check keeps the PCs inside the gas. After succeeding, the poison remains for 6 rounds, and the PCs need to make 2 consecutive Fort saves to remove the poison. Regardless, entering the poisonous gas disrupts it enough to alert Charcalthiak.
- **Toxic Pools (ground):** PCs must carefully navigate across a field of pools of toxic acid with unstable ground. Acrobatics to safely navigate, Reflex for half acid damage (20d6). Falling or near falling into the pools causes them to geyser upwards like a warning flare.

### Third Challenge

- **Battering Winds (air):** Enchanted gales of winds ensnare and batter flying intruders.  Fly check to traverse smoothly through the gales. If fail, the winds batter the PCs, wind slashes the PCs or slams against them, or slams each PC against each other. Reflex save for half damage (30d6), DR applies. The ruckus alerts Charcalthiak.
- **Illusory Sentries (ground):**  An illusory maze blankets the mountain paths around the lair.  Survival check to successfully pass through. DC 40 Will save to see through the illusions to gain +1 edge on Survival check. Failure results in  PCs being lost, losing time, and being magically drained, taking 30d6 dmg (Fort for half) and Charcalthiak is alerted.

## Charcalthiak's Levels of Alertness

### 0 Fails

Charcalthiak is eating and resting in front of Argentia's lair, recovering from another round of dissoluting Pyrouranos. PCs have the chance to sneak up on her against her passive Perception.

### 1 Fail

Charcalthiak is aware that something or someone passed through the lair's defences, but it was possible it was a false alarm caused by a large creature. Charcalthiak gets to use active Perception to sense any intruders, and readies buffs at the appearance of any intruders.

### 2 Fails

Charcalthiak is alerted, can use active Perception, and readies buffs at the appearance of any intruders. She currently flies above, searching for intruders.

### 3 Fails

Charcalthiak can use active Perception, readies buffs, and actively hides at mountain peaks, and has acid rock elementals summoned (hidden as normal rocks).

# Previously

The Evergreen Company try to decide on what to do next. They must go to Argentia's lair and recover the bow Pyrouranos, but they worry that doing so will bring Vorakios' wrath down upon them while they are vulnerable. They could quickly find refuge under the shielded areas, and thanks to Prateria's aid, Andrastreia believes the Thunderhoof centaurs can defend the refuge against Vorakios' undead and blighted fey for about a week. The Company goes to Arbor's Rest, and test to see if they can move the Pall of Shining Life by moving its anchor ritualist, in this case Adriel. While they find out they can move the anchor caster and maintain the Pall (as long as the ritualist can maintain their concentration, the Pall does not move with her. And when they move Adriel out of the Pall, the shield ceases to exist. The Company also consider requesting the Holy Radimus Empire to volunteer their most powerful casters to fend off Vorakios, but Arina, who has been the anchor for the Pall protecting Viriquia, advises that she has observed Radimite leadership, and they have grand ambitions on the Thalamech Uplands, so requesting their aid may bring more problems in the future. Ultimately, any further actions depended on what Orok would discover.

Orok flew above the Jadehorn Peaks towards Argentia's lair, starting at 6 pm. As he neared its location, he discovered flocks of ravens routinely patrolling. He spent an extra hour to sneak past them. Next, he encountered an area where the air was filled with toxic gasses. Holding his breath, he sped through the area without succuming to its poison. Finally nearing the lair, Orok encountered an area where the air and wind slashed wildly anything in the air. He descended to the ground, whereupon he found himself in a maze to confusing passages and crevasses amidst the mountains. He finally contacted the rest of the Company over their shared mental collective to decide how to proceed further.
