# Notes

## Pyrouranos's Purpose

The PCs will have to fight through the horde to help the centaur champion reach the edge of Vorakios' barrier to this seat of power. In there way are a series of challenges.

### Challenge 1: Help allies.

**Focus on protecting champion:** The enemies attacking the champion directly are scaled to be even with the champion + PCs, so if the PCs split up to help the allies, defenindg the champion will be harder. The champion pleads with the PCs to help her friends.

**Save elf mages:** Should the elf mages be saved, the PCs gain a buff during the next challenge and (+5 to attack) the finale.

**Save dwarf warriors:** Should the dwarf warriors be saved, this unit serves as a tank during the next and final encounter.

### Challenge 2: Despair

Dark fey prey conjure up a powerful wave of magical despair that is both necrotic and enchanting. Allies fall into despair, especially the champion's lover. In his despaired state, he is vulnerable to being overwhelmed by the horde. With the threat of her lover and the despair of the enchantment, the champion's progress falters.

**Save the lover:** Lover is a human ranger. Failure to save him results in the champion gaining disadvantage on all d20s for the rest of the encounter and in the final push.

**Defeat the fairy curse:** The curse makes everyone on the map shaken and enemies gain +1 advantage on attacks (Will DC 35). Those who save at the start of their turn can act normally.

**Rally the champion's and allies' morale:** The allies are already under the effect of the fairy curse of despair. PCs can inspire allies to overcome the curse for a round by spending a standard action to make a DC 30 Oratory check. Each 10 over the DC increases the resistance by 1 round.

### Challenge 3: Final Push

*You have broken through the main line of enemy forces. Although still surriunded by the undead and dark fey, their linea and formations are in disarray, and are fully engaged with the forces of the alliance. Ahead of you is a second of or undead, but behind them, floating in the air with dark energy surrounding is the giant form of Vorakios. "I have a clear shot!" shouts the champion. "Shield me from harm while I gather the power!" The champion draws Pyrouranos and closes her eyes. She mutters a mantra over and over as motes of light appear and coalesce around her. "Destroy her!" roars the deathly voice of the cyclops lich.*

The champion breaks through the enemy lines to gain a clear chost at Vorakios. The champion needs 3 rounds to concentrate her power (+3 rounds if lover died). Each time she receives damage, the count reduces by one (once per round).

### Vorakios is Defeated

*In an explosion of power, the arrow, glowing brightly with energy, is loosed by the champion. Like a bolt of lightning, it streaks over the mass of grotesque undead. Vorakios merely raises one hand to reinforce his mystical defenses. But even from this distance, you perceive his gaunt, skeletal one glowing red eyed visage twist in in fearful surprise as the arrow pierces through his shields and wards in a series of explosions. The lich twists his body to escape, the only thing that prevented the magical bolt from piercing his glowing red eye. Instead, the arrow slams into his chest, where his heart would have been. The lich unleashes a bone-chilling scream of agony as he is engulfed in an explosion of bright, life-giving light. As the light fades, ashes float where the lich once stood as a dark shadow shoots away towards the mountains in the distance.*

### Elite Dwarves

- 1000 hp, DV 40, DR 40.

### The Zombie Horde

- each token has 200 hp, DV 20, no DR.
- does automatic damage (16d12 per adjacent zombie horde token)
- when damaged, attempt grapple +40 (+10 per adjacent zombie horde token)
- when grappled, +50 to maintain grapple (+15 per adjacent zombie horde token) to inflict 16d12 x zombie dmg + move 1 square towards the mist
- if damage passes DR, inflict 4d4 Str or Dex dmg per adjacent zombie horde token (Fort save vs DC 40 for half (+5 per adjacent horde token)
- zombie horde begins climbing up over each other for form rising wave of zombies against flying PCs
