# Notes

- Althea, young acolyte of Firhanna and devotee to Calameia, was instructed by her teacher, the Greenmother Phaenemene, to stay in their tent while she went to join the tribal council. However, Althea's curiosity about the new prisoners was too much, and she left the tent to go see them.
- Althea convinced the warriors guarding the Scarred Wolves to let her heal their wounds. She got to know their story and why they were here.
- Althea then went to the main pavilion to plead their case, but was not allowed in. A guard passed along her request, and came back with a message from her teacher. The council had already learned from the Scarred Wolves belongings what their purpose here was, but they remain skeptical on their veracity.
- Althea went back to the prisoners, and tried to gauge their truthfulness. After hearing their stories again, she became convinced that they were sincere.
- Althea returned to her tent, where her teacher admonished her for talking with the prisoners instead of studying. Althea countered by answering all of Phaenemene's test questions. The Greenmother told her that the council has decided to put the prisoners through the Trial of Iron and the Trial of Heart, and that Althea should get some rest as there will be much to do in the morning. Both went to rest, but Althea snuck out to go warn the prisoners.
- Althea snuck throughout the camp, but the guards still saw her. They denied her this time to talk to the prisoners, so Althea spoke in a loud voice that it would be a shame if she could not help prepare the prisoners for the skill-needed Trial of Iron and the spiritual Trial of Heart, and that they need a good long rest. The guards were well-aware of what Althea was doing, but there was no helping it now. They shooed her back, and Althea got an earful from her teacher who was waiting for her to return.
- In the morning, Andrastreia declared to the Scarred Wolves that their sincerity will be tested. The Trial of Iron will require them to each retrieve an armful of the Waking Dreambloom plant in Kankreta's hunting grounds. Then, should they survive, the herbs will be used in the Trial of Heart, where they will be sent into a dreamscape where their true nature will be revealed.
- Phaenemene told Althea to behave at the camp while she joins the other seniors on the expedition. However, Althea argued that their faith and traditions passed down from Calameia required her join and help, and Phaenemene gave in.
- The expedition traveled by foot for several hours. Andrastreia stopped the party when they arrived at the hunting grounds. The shamans and witches formed a perimeter of totems and special powder. The Scarred Wolves were given their weapons, told to go and grab the herbs that were seven hundred feet away. When the party had traveled over a hundred feet, the centaurs began rythmically stomping their hooves and hammering the totems in the ground.
- Althea, overwhelmed by what Calameia would do, ran after the party to help them. Phaenemene called after her and tried to grab her, but Andrastreia stopped her and let the young centaur go.
- About halfway to their goal, the Scarred Wolves and Althea are ambushed by Kankreta, an ancient landshark. Kankreta erupted from below the earth, devouring Usagi in one fell swoop. Circe was next, and she managed to avoid the same fate, but not without breaking several fingers. However, she quickly surmised that Kankreta could be distracted from its current prey if anyone else pounded into the ground like the centaurs did before. She quickly screamed out that bit of knowledge, and Oskar and Bonzar both began stomping the ground to draw Kankreta to them. Meanwhile, Althea carried Thassi to the Waking Dreambloom flowers.

- Althea is a young acolyte of the Firhanna, Goddess of Nature, aspirant of Calameia. She has never ventured away from her tribe, but is extremely curious about life outside and other places. Her parents and teachers disapprove of her curiosity, and urge her to focus on the tribe's rituals and religious responsibilities. Her teacher, Greenmother Phaenemene is teaching her some lessons when the prisoners are brought into the camp. The prisoners fascinate her, but Phaenemene orders her to stay away from them. She needs to fool the guards and sneak to the prisoners to talk with them. Eventually she will be caught by her teacher and sent away.
- In the morning, the tribe's council decide to test the sincerity of the Scarred Wolves. They will be put through a dream test. But first, they must acquire the sacred herbs ("the Waking Dreambloom") needed for the ritual. The herbs are located in the hunting grounds of Kankreta, an ancient bulette.
- Along the way, Althea is punished for her transgression from the night before by being tasked to take responsibility of the prisoners. If they escape or harm the tribe, she will be at fault and receive some form of punishment. At Kankreta's hunting grounds, the SW are ordered to go and retrieve the herbs. Phaenemene orders Althea to stay with the tribe.
- The PCs can gain more info on how best to survive and win the challenge by succeeding Diplomacy checks against elders and warriors.
	- Iolanthe (female ranger): Info on how to track Kankreta, and how to hide their scent/track from it (special bait to attract, special soap to hide scent, special shoes to soften or augment tremors).
	- Xenastos (male warrior): Information  on Kankreta's abilities.
	- Cilleira (female elder): To drive away the bulette, you can throw a bag of special herbs (ranged touch attack). These bags of herbs can be acquired from the elder.

## The Test of Iron

- Travel through Kankreta's territory.
	- Aggro: Individual PCs can draw aggro from Kankreta. Kankreta goes after whoever has highest aggro score. If scores are equal, goes after nearest target.
		- A hit that deals damage generates 2 aggro
		- A hit that deals no damage generates 1 aggro
		- Repellant pouch (range touch atk) reduces 1 aggro
- Gather herbs: any person who enters Kankreta's territory must bring back a handful of herbs (1 round). If they don't, they are to be executed. One person can get herbs for other people (1 round each time).
- Return back through Kankreta's territory.
