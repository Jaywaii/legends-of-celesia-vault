# Notes

The Jafarkhet team continued their information gathering over the next several nights.

They were able to learn that two prisoners were currently being held in the cells of the Jafarkhet Ministry of Amonkhaten. The first was an Ashaki nobleman by the name of Musim Kabarka, the Flightful Voice. He was the eldest son of the very wealthy and influential noble Ashaki family of Kabarka. A cowardly cynic, Musim was known to indulge in grand parties across the empire, but also to be a fantastic orator and storyteller. He was currently under arrest for having publicly recounted stories banned by the Church of Amonkhaten for containing unapproved themes and ideas.

The other prisoner was a foreign aelfkyn woman by the name of Alys deGrey. deGrey was a mercenary with a reputation who has operated in the region for several years. She was captured after being involved with an attack on a border patrol force. An upper-tier iron expert, she is known for being extremely focused and a perfectionist.

Either of those two were potential candidates to be sent to the Sand Rock. From overhearing off-duty guards and inquisitors, and making subtle inquiries, the team learned that Musim made for a valuable political hostage. With him in custody, the Church could extract more concessions from the Kabarka family, threatening them to send their son to Sand Rock. It all depends on how the negotiations will pan out between the Church and the Kabarkas.

Meanwhile, Alys was currently being interrogated on who she worked for, how else was working with, and what plans was she involved in. The guards were impressed by how she has been resisting the questioning so far. If she kept this up, they would have to send her to Sand Rock for more intensive interrogation. However, who knows, the Fists here may break her yet, and avoid the need to bother with the transfer to the secret prison.

Arina also identified who among the anointed of Amonkhaten here in Jafarkhet would be assigned to join the crew of the secret barge to Sand rock. Three captains: Kambatet, Raziya, Shakansu. Arina spent a day shadowing Raziya, and Argus shadowed Kambatet, both to learn their patterns and habits. Raziya, despite her rank, was a sloven woman with a bad attitude. Kambatet, on the other hand, was fairly affable, except that it seemed only for the purpose to further his own ambitions.

In addition to this intel, the team also found out that while circumstances may alter the when and how, the Ministry prefers to perform its prisoner transport from the holding cells to the ship during the day under the guise of a cargo transport backed with decoy transports.

The next step for the team is to figure out if they can replace either Kambatet or Raziya, or both, with Argus and Arina respectively. Doing so would allow them to infiltrate the coumpound, and set things in motion for the Jafarkhet Ministry to call in the prison barge to transport the prisoners to Sand Rock.
