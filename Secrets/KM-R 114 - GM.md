She promises favorable loans, and is able to commit at least a regiment of Châteaufoy musketeers to help Prateria. Osla expresses her nation's thanks for the generous offer. She then goes to see the Drelevos, who are quite happily consuming the food and drinks of the banquet. Rafaelo Drelevo is especially jovial, telling Osla that he had his doubts about these Games, but that the events of the day have grossly amused him to no end. Osla tries to convince him that they share a common enemy, the trolls, and that their two colonies should work together. Rafaelo retorts that it will be a long time before the trolls become his concern, and by then, he will be powerful enough to take them on. Nonetheless, if Prateria is willing to pay the right price, he would be amenable to some sort of deal that would benefit the Company's nation.
- Arina and Argus approach Cyrano Varano, and try appealing to his sense of obligation to get help from him. However, they do so in such a roundabout manner that wears the Trade Lord's patience thin. All that they come away with from the conversation is that Varano is open to any request for aid, Prateria merely needs to ask specifically.
- That night, after returning to her home, Osla is surprised by a glowing set of constellations on Zazen's back. After she deciphered it, she learns that they represent a journey for the new followers of Bodaï. It is a journey to teach and test them of Bodaï's ways, where the destination remains a mystery. It is also likely that that the higher powers of Bodaï seek to move their fledgling practitioners out of harm's way. Osla must bear a new task, one that will take her away from her new home, and Lumina agrees to join her friend.
- The following day, the foreign dignitaries depart for their homes. Osla informs the Company of her need to depart as well. They are forlorn, but each wish each other well; safe travels for Osla and Lumina, and fortune for Prateria in the upcoming conflict. Osla and Lumina are assured that there will always be a place for them here in Prateria.

However, Attila believes in loyalty and repaying the kindness Prateria provided by sheltering his people. Arina asks what can be done, and Attila admits that many of his people still desire their own settlement. If Prateria promises to found a settlement for the Rughirs, he is confident that they will remain loyal to Prateria, and provide a company of orc warriors. The Company agrees to the proposal, and begin planning where to establish this new settlement.
