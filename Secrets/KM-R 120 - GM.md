# Notes

- On Tierlan 20th, the Sootscale kobold settlement of Silvermine becomes under attack by a Klorgfang army, led by the hill giant Warlord Arzhulig. Fortunately for the tiny village, the Evergreen Company, sans Arina, are present in the settlement. They had been overseeing the transition of power by the Sootscales' joining of Prateria, and overseeing the completion of the Sootscale's first army, the Consul Draconis composed of fifty ramskull-riding hunters. At 10 in the morning, the tattered black flags of Klorgfang are seen approaching. Quickly, Argus takes charge of the defences. King Sootscale takes his people into the mines, except for the newly formed Consul Draconis, who are eager to test their skills. Sky is given charge of the unit, while the rest of the Company spread out.
- Arzhulig has his units encircle the village, with his centre facing Silvermine's south gate. The hill giant holds his forces back, ordering his range units to defeat the defenders of Silvermine's walls. Once a section of the wall is undefended, his melee forces will assault the wall and breach it. However, thanks to his superior training, Argus is able to anticipate his opposing number's strategy. Consul Draconis, with additional range support from Jupus, focused their attack against Arzhulig's harpies and kobold sorcerers. However, Consul Draconis is badly hit by return fire from the harpies and additional attack from gnoll skirmishers. They suffer further casualties when Arzhulig's own unit advances and throws large rocks into them.
- Davona, who was overseeing the defence of the northeastern flank, decides that her abilities are better suited for offence. She flies up and assaults the flight of harpies, inflicting even more casualties. However, seeing the now-defenceless northeastern wall, the right-flank hobgoblin cavalry charge forward to assault the wall, causing a breach. But before they can pour in, Argus rushes over to that side, and holds the hobgoblins at the gap. Meanwhile, the northwestern wall remains protected by Rishoi and Rialla, but the southern gate comes under direct attack a unit of raging orcs.
- After the first hour of battle, Silvermine and its defenders remain standing, but the defences are being broken through.
