# Notes

## Viriquia

Arina orders Sky to assemble a team to help with Viriquia's defenses until the Pall team is free to join them.

### 4 AM to 5 AM

The western marketplace is at risk of being overrun.

**Crisis #1:** Dozens of citizens are fleeing down the street cleared by the priests. The PCs need to protect them from zombie hordes that emerge behind them and from nearby alleyways. The citizens are only safe once they get behind the erected barricade manned by the city guard. Each survivor group that makes it to safety is +1 favor with the Bishop, but if all perish, the Bishop's trust worsens.

**Crisis #2:** On the other side of the marketplace, an advanced outpost there is being overwhelmed by zombies led by undead cyclops martials. The PCs need to both defeat the cyclops martials and hold the line for a period of time until reinforcements arrive, increasing the Marquis' trust. The loss of the outpost won't collapse the marketplace's defenses, but it will disprove Prateria's ability in the Marquis' eyes, and even worse if the troops there (who include several scions of the city's nobility) are not helped to at least fall back safely to the barricade. Reinforcenments arrive on the map at round 4.

### 5 AM to 6 AM

**Crisis #1:** A food depot has beed covered by the fog. The survivors need those supplies. Escort a team of Radimite priests to the deport so they can clear the fog. Protect them while they clear the fog and the team to get the supplies out from zombies and fairies. Every supply pack recovered counts as 1 favor towards the Bishop. The death of each priest reduces the Bishop's trust, and failure to recover any supplies worsens his attitude.
