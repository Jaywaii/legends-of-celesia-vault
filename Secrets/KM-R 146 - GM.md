# Notes

- 5th Rochefort Fusilier Regiment, led by Colonel Angéline d'Amboise, arrives in Promethea in the first week of Karthoran. Along with the remnants of the 2nd Fusiliers, the Rochefort mercenaries fortify Prateria's borders.
- The Evergreen Company lead Legio I Prateria into Klorgfang. They join Rishoi's Immortals, and meet up with the first of their mercenary forces, the 1st Auxiliaries at Grunrahmen. They begin occupation of Klorgfang lands between the kobold village, and Greentusk Keep to the southwest.
