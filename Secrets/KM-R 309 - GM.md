# Notes

## Erixarchos Lyceum

The Cult uses wards that mentally push away people and illusions to hide the secret path to their lair in the undercroft.

### Infiltrating the Lyceum

**Stealth:** Even with mythic invisibility, the PCs must carefully navigate through the lyceum. They are many perceptive people, and the school keeps many creatures with strong scents or listening abilities.

**Failure:** The PCs are noticed by a pair of worgs being part of a magical experimented. They begin sniffing and growling as the PCs pass by, putting their tenders, a group of students, on edge.

### Finding the Trail

**Sense Motive:** By observing the Lyceum's staff and students, you notice several of them acting suspiciously.
