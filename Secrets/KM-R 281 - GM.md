*The decrepit, unseen voice of Talien Firesong echoes throughout the arena, as if coming from every direction at once.*
**Talien:** *"There is the true threat. Come on, Argent Rose, do your duty!"*

## Phase 2: Talien Firesong

**Talien:** *"Merisiel. You were always the kindness to me growing up, in a family that hated simply because our mother cared for me as equally as you and your trueborn siblings. Me, the son of a human concubine!"*

**Talien:** *"Merisiel. There is power here, and I am willing to share it with you. Abandon the ARgent Rose. They see, but are blind to the truth! They know, but are ignorant of what reality truly is!"*

Killing blow:
**Talien:** *"Puahaha! You think this is the end of me? I am a lich! I will always come back as long as my vessel remains. And once more, I have defeated you, Teshaad! Thank you for leaving me a tome filled with such dark energy. It is at home somewhere in this labyrinthian city that only I have mapped out over all these years. And you will. NEVER. Find it!"*

**Teshaad:** LAUGHS *"You were always shortsighted, Firesong. It is you have I have to thank! When I sensed you earlier, it was only due to my tome in your possession. And when I no longer sensed it, I knew you hid it. But I no longer need it. Thank you, Firesong, for foolishly binding your soul to my tome. Now, all I need to complete myself is YOU!"*

**Talien:** *"What... no, NOOO!!"* *Talien screams as the last of his corpse crumbles, with a gust of glowing mist flowing out of his mouth and eyes.*

*The mist whirls and shoots towards Igvald's left hand, and it is absorbed within it. Igvald's left hand begins to pulse with power and pain. Teshaad's voice roars in your mind. "YES! YES! I AM COMPLETE ONCE MORE!"*

## Phase 3: Te'Shaad

Igvald's hand begins turning black. Bones cracks, and flesh tears as it becomes more scaly, distended, with claws growing out of the fingertips.

**Teshaad:** *"Thank you, Igvald. We made a good team. As thanks, I'll take good care of your body. But first, it's been soooo long since I tore an Argent Rose apart!"* *Black veins begin crawling up Igvald's forearm.*

Igvald must make a Will save each round, or attack the closest teammate (gaining 1 Fate point each fail). The starting DC is 40. After 3 fails, Teshaad takes over Igvald's body. Beating the DC by 10 or nat 20 removes 1 fail.
Each time Igvald saves, compel him to cut off his left hand. DV 45, hp 40 hp, Igvald takes half damage received (DC 35 Fort for half of that). Igvald's bleed damage is doubled (or gains 10 if at 0).

After Teshaad gets cut off, the dismembered hand burns, and the smoke forms into the shape of a man at first. He is familiar to you. You recognize him as Temerin, the sage you first encountered in the library dreamscape, then the dreamscape filled with ruined temples. The shape dissipates, and reforms into a giant demon made of dark smoke. "NOOO! My form may not yet be complete, but I have enough power still to destroy you!"

**Teshaad (incorporeal):** hp 500, DV 45, DR 20/good, atk +45 (touch), dmg 4d4+20 negative, Fort +29, Ref +17, Will +25, SR 20; Large creature

Death: *"Nooooo! Not again! Noué! Please! One more chance! One more chaaaaanceee....."*
