# Notes

Cortez reports back to Arina that Maalek's childhood friend and contact Shamira continues to work at the Sapphire Djinn, but appears to be under scrutiny. The guard and inquisition are spending more time more frequently the inn at all hours.

He also reports that there is a Captain Husani (male human fighter 7) who Cortez had actually been grooming as a possible contact under one of his aliases. Crotez will need additional funds. Arina arranges delivery through Promethea's *doors* for him to receive 35 thousand gold pieces worth of gifts and goods to start bribing the Jafarkhet captain.

The Evergreen Company evaluate other options. Now that the Ashaki Empire is being engaged by clandestined resistance supported by the Arsan Empire and the Kahadun Caliphate, the Praterian rulers decide on working with one of those powers. Because of the significant presence of the Kaïdenite church in Prateria, the Company requests the diplomatic help of Lurindor to arrange a meeting between the Praterian leaders and Kahadun officials.

Days later, the Praterian leaders are invited to the capital of Lurindor, Coracentia, where the Kahadun embassy is located. Arina, Sky, Argus, and Orok use the mythic *doors* of Promethea to instantly travel to their own embassy. They are met with Lurindor's grand diplomat, Elvia Sovrani, who welcomes them warmly, especially Arina. She has arranged transport to the Kahadun embassy. Along the way, Elvia expresses interest in improving Lurindor's relationship with Prateria, which she feels has been neglected by the merchant nation. She proposes that Lurindor seeks to establsih more trade routes with Praterian settlements, which the Evergreen Company are open to.

Upon arriving at the Kahadun embassy, Elvia leaves the Praterians' company. The Praterians meet with Ambassador Nadiyaa inside one of the embassy''s conference rooms. Arina explains their desire to work with the Kahadunans against Ashak. They speak specifically about the hidden prison of Sand Rock. Nadiyaa explains that her government has tried to locate the prison before, but without success. The divine power of the Ashaki's ruler blocks their divinations. As such, the Caliphate has focused the efforts and resources on other aspects of resistance. However, if Prateria is able to find Sand Rock and break out its prisoners, Nadiyaa is able to promise at least some form of aid.

Arina explains they need Ashaki currency for their operations inside the empire, and is willing to pay for it. Nadiyaa tells her that this is possible, and since they will be allies in this, the Caliphate will accept a simple one-to-one conversion of Prateria's coin with Kahadun's reserve of Ashaki coin. Arina requests two hundred thousand coin, and Nadiyaa says this is possible, and asks that they come back in two days to make the exchange.
