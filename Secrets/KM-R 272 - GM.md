## Groves of Nobu-Shugath

The dark young of Xin'carlath are a unique intelligent spawn of the Mother of All Horrors. However, autopsies of dark young samples have not revealed any organ within their alien structure that could be construed as a heart. Only in the dreaded forests of the Breeding Groves of Nobu-Shugath can you find a dark young with a heart that can be claimed.
- research in the AR archives refer to a book called *The Pakulkan Astralogues*,  the collected works of the ancient seer Pakulkan from Anzica about his exploration beyond this world through psychic projection. this book is thought to provide a much more detailed description of the Groves of Nobu-Shugath.
- expedition to an isolated forest taken over by Void Singers: the Skethelwold, a small forest nestled in the western mountains of Pendrik, accessible via a trail linked to Skethelburgh. The old blood of Skethelburgh (the Bentwicks (security under Captain [[Torrence Bentwick]]), the Cricksturts (merchant's guild under [[Bryant Cricksturts]], the Deplagets (town's rule Lady [[Esmerth Deplaget]]), the Stackmoores (labor's guild under [[Marek Stackmoore]]), and the Wesslurks (royal foresters under High Ranger [[Satrine Wesslurk]]) carefully guard the sacred site in the forest.
	- The total number of cultists in Skethelburgh number over a hundred (out of a population of 3,000), composed by the members of the 5 old blood families and many others in the university and town leadership inducted into the cult. In addition, the resources of the 5 families and other wealthy cultists allow them to recruit a mercenary force numbering over 300. The security forces, except for its top leaders close to the families, are unaware of the cult's activities and do not have access to the cult's tunnels.
- Skethelburgh inscribes a symbol of Xin-car'lath as clues to cult locations: resembles a headless obese pregnant figure with a fanged maw where the neck is supposed to be, with 7 tendrils rippling out from its bottom.
- has an alien 2nd age summoning platform that summons dark young, activated by primanoid sacrifices
- platform resembles the base of an enormous tree with numerous thick roots twisting into the ground, about 20 ft. in diameter, seemingly sawed off at 5 ft. above the ground
	- its side exterior is that of black, gnarly bark, while the exposed interior atop the platform appears like grey stone with numerous eldritch patterns and symbols grooved into it with traces of dried blood within those grooves

## Roth-Teghan

The gnoph-keh were an experimental race created by the thul-koon during the 2nd age, and became near extinct going into the 3rd age. However, they are extremely long-lived and can easily impregnate each other. Individuals still persist in the cold polar regions of Inaktivut and Tunakuraq, though they are few and far between. The gnoph-keh primarily worship Yin-Zaggoth.
- An ancient ruined city of gnoph-keh in Tunakuraq
- ARS doesn't have a huge presence on Tunakuraq, just a small lodge for research (Chapter-Sage Amaruqiel Wintersong (female elf); intellectual, secure) who also maintain relations with local Tunakuraqi nomads (Nanukhir elves, descendants of Nanukhil [female snow elf] hero of the 2nd Age).
- The seasonal snow storms make tracking and mapping extremely difficult, even with the help of experienced local trackers
- inspired by 'At the Mountains of Madness'
- research in the AR archives refer to a collection of books called *The Onidawik Journals*, research journals of archeologist Onidawik from Anowakowa. the AR research informs that Onidawik was the foremost expert on Roth-Teghan in the past 4000 years.

## University of Skethelburgh

The University of Skethelburgh is a many centuries old intitute of higher learning. Located in the isolated town of Skethelburgh in the west mountains of the Duchy of Pendrik. Almost all of Pendrik's elite, and many from the rest of Aerdwish Empire, have spent years of study in its hallowed halls. The town's original people, the Skethel clan, were secretely void cultists. While the majority of modern day population of Skethelburgh are normal people, its old blood families still carry on their practices in secret, and the whole town's atmosphere is steeped with mystery and superstitions. The University itself has been infiltrated by agents of the Coil, specifically its Occult Research and Development Initiative (ORDIn). They have established themselves over several centuries to become the administrators of the Obscure Collections Wing which contains a haphazard number special tomes and manuscripts that have beeen collected (i.e. looted) from across the world and have yet to be fully analyzed and organized. The *The Pakulkan Astralogues* and *The Onidawik Journals* are located within this wing.

The Cult of the Great Mother and the Coil know of each other. The Cult tolerates the Coil as the Coil has offered a large amount of lore and a steady supply of sacrifices and they do not interfere with their activities. The Coil was initially interested in the vast amount of lore the university possesses, but are now more interested in recruiting the young noble scions of the Aerdwish Empire to their cause, and are willing to work with the Cult as long as they allow their influence. However, the Coil view the Cosmic Horrors as a threat to their eventual dominion of Celesia, so the Coil monitors the Cult closely to make sure their activities do not doom the very world they seek to conquer.

The Argent Rose has a delicate relationship with the Aerdwish Empire. The Society's presence is strictly monitored and conditional. The Society presence is only tolerated within the Empire by being forced to integrate within Imperial approved institutions. ARS has a chapter in the Empire (headed by Chapter-Sage Talbert Winster) but it does not have its own lodge. Instead, it is integrated as a branch of the Royal Grand Library of Caerlinstrel (in the imperial capital on the island), and oversees ARS operations throughout Aerdwyn, Pendrik, Erland, Tambria, Senwarth, and Darsa. Talbert is deliberately stretched thin and overworked by Imperial decree to make sure the ARS doesn't have time or resources to mess around in the Empire. As such, Talbert is aware of Skethelburgh, but has no assets there or even in the nearby region. He does have maps and info on its senior administrators.
