# Notes

## Viriquia

### 6am to 7 am

**Crisis #1:** a huge amalgamation of zombie corpses in cyclops shape (the Amalgamated Abomination) with a small horde attacks the castle

*A giant cyclops made of rotting flesh stops forth from the fog. Behind it lurches a horde of zombies. The cyclops halts, raises its arms, and lets out a horrific, unearthly roar. The zombies aroud it begin going manic, and madly climbs all over the cyclops. As they pile upon each other, the grotesque sounds of bones cracking and sloshing of flesh echo in the eerie night as everyone watches with horrific disgust as the zombies grossly fuse together. The amalgamated abomination then stretches up, revealing itself to be a huge giant of fused zombies in the shape of a cyclops. Its enormous maw bellows a roar in unison with the many mouths pocketing the rest of its body.*

**Main Tactic:** Throws 2 zombie hordes onto castle wall (1d3 cooldown), attack gate, slam/grapple.

**Off Tactic:** Bite surrounding foes, spit bile (1d3 cooldown).

**Ballista:** On Guards turn, if they are not engaged with zombies, they can fire a Ballista at the abomination, knocking it back 10 ft. and dealling 10 dmg.

**Gate:** Gate can take 3 hits from the Abomination.

**Crisis #2:** a huge whirlwind of wraiths (the Wraithly Whirlwind) that casts spells and ejects wraiths with a small horde attacks the cathedral

*From the fog, it starts as a whisper, but quickly grows to a cacophony of wails. A multitude of wraiths fly out from the fog, However, they do not fly directly at the defenders. Instead, the swirl around each other, more and more, their incorporealy forms meshing together. Then it comes into shape, a whirlwind of dozens of wraiths, acting as one.*

**Main Tactic:** [banshee blast](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/magic/all-spells/b/banshee-blast/) (every 6 rds), [eyebite](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/magic/all-spells/e/eyebite/) (every 6 rounds), [waves of exhaustion](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/magic/all-spells/w/waves-of-exhaustion/) (every 7 rounds), [umbral strike](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/magic/all-spells/u/umbral-strike/)(every 7 rounds)

**Off Tactic:** Release a wraith, wind buffet around self (1d3 cooldown), [ghoul touch](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/magic/all-spells/g/ghoul-touch/) (every 2 rounds), [scare](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/magic/all-spells/s/scare/) (every 2 rounds)

### The Zombie Horde

- each token has 100 hp, DV 20, no DR.
- does automatic damage (8d12 per adjacent zombie horde token)
- when damaged, attempt grapple +30 (+10 per adjacent zombie horde token)
- when grappled, +40 to maintain grapple (+15 per adjacent zombie horde token) to inflict 2d12 x zombie dmg + move 1 square towards the mist
- if damage passes DR, inflict 2d4 Str or Dex dmg per adjacent zombie horde token (Fort save vs DC 30 for half (+5 per adjacent horde token)
- zombie horde begins climbing up over each other for form rising wave of zombies against flying PCs
