Success reveals this is a [cosmic horror summoning ritual](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/alternative-rule-systems/occult-adventures/occult-rules/occult-rituals/#Fiendish_Conjuration) (DC 45). Upon summoning the [[outer spawn]], the outer spawn seeks to merge with a sacrifice, a true soul. If refused, it will attack to force a merger. Once defeated, the PCs can consume its flesh to see into the ether and unlock the door (DC 30).
