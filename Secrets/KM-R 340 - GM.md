# Notes

## Enemy Strats

### The Cryptkeeper

- summons gravedirt elementals (1 standard)
- summons hordes from ground or sky (1 simple)
- summons spectres from graves or sky (1 simple)
- necromancy spells
- conjures spectral hand for chill touch and vampiric touch
- has 2 turns per round

### Gravedirt Elementals

- (melee) slams and grabs, buries into the soil for negative damage and suffocation
- (range) spews cursed gravedirt in cone or line, impact can grab and pulls to ground. Even if target has freedom of movement or grab fails, they are pulled to the ground unless they make Ref save.

### zombie hordes

- falls from clouds onto flying targets, deals damage, and attempts to grab and pull to ground. Even if target has freedom of movement or grab fails, they are pushed to the ground unless they make Ref save.
- emerge from ground underneath targets and swarms to deal damage and attempt to grab.
- each token has 200 hp, DV 20, no DR.
- does automatic damage (8d12 per adjacent zombie horde token)
- when damaged, attempt grapple +40 (+10 per adjacent zombie horde token)
- when grappled, +50 to maintain grapple (+15 per adjacent zombie horde token) to inflict 8d12
- if damage passes DR, inflict 3d4 Str or Dex dmg per adjacent zombie horde token (Fort save vs DC 40 for half (+5 per adjacent horde token)
 - zombie horde begins climbing up over each other for form rising wave of zombies against flying PCs
