# Notes

- After determining nothing more can be gotten out of Norsgurd, Arina ceased her scrying. The Company, high up in the air, then discuss about the possible traitor in their midst. It certainly explained how Klorgfang knew to send another army to relieve Norsgurd at the last battle. And how they knew that they are now separated from their army. To their horror, the Company realize that Klorgfang knows about their plan to bait Silverich. Sky starts to give himself a headache trying to go back and forth about who knows what each other side knows. They list through possible candidate who could be the leak: Bekka, Sister Félicité, Louis, Rialla, maybe even the departed Osla. Maybe even Arina herself due to Mister Angelcakes! But only a few of those people were privy to their recent plans. Eventually, the Company decide to go through with their current plan.
- The team flies onward to Silverich's camp. They evade the harpy scouts, and arrive to the temporary camp. Most of the units are out establishing the necessary infrastructure to ferry supplies from their nation to the front line. However, there is a large flight of forest drakes over the centre of the camp, and the team deduce that this must be Silverich's command area. They charge in as fast as they can, and before any of the Klorgfang troops can react, Argus tosses the piece of cloth that Sky then commands to return to its original form. The rotted corpse of Deilenwyn hits the ground with a wet thud, as Argus roars out for Silverich to show himself. The troops in the camp finally react to his presence. Harpies and drakes fly up and encircle Argus, while troops on the ground raise up their range weapons and aim at him. There is a pause, and finally a forest drake comes out of the command tent, roaring orders to everyone. An old human man, somewhat well-dressed, but with an iron collar around his neck, reluctantly steps out of the tent. He begins interpreting the dialog between the forest drake commander and Argus.
- "Silverich, come out and the wise decision to surrender!" Argus commands. The old man yells back at Argus after the drake commander roars. *"You are foolish to have come alone! The only one to surrender will be you!"*
- "Your daughter has already surrendered! With her life!" Argus yells back, pointing at the corpse. Oddly enough, the forest drake commander barely spares it a glance. *"Your threats are meaningless! We have you surrounded! Surrender now, or face the wrath of a whole army!"*
- Argus scoffs. "Every time our forces have faced in battle, Prateria has always proved mightier! You larval-eating miscreants dare invade our lands like a plague? Like a plague, we will burn you out! Leave, leave now, or be buried where you stand!" The interpreter quails beneath Argus' dominant presence. The commander's bluster remains defiant, but a bit more subdued. *"If that is all the words you have to say, then no more words are needed. All forces! Attack!"*
- Upon hearing the order, and before the interpeter can translate and before the arrows and javelins could reach them, Sky grabs his companions, and teleported back to his magic shop in Promethea. With access to his silver mirror, Sky attempts to *scry* on Klorgfang's treasurer, the green hag Strega Lagia. Meanwhile, the rest of the team discreetly supply themselves without anyone knowing of their presence. Unfortunately, Sky's spell is rebuffed by its target, and because of the amount of time needed, the Praterian Haruspex opts to no longer attempt any scrying. Instead, the Company discuss their options and plans, all of which are complicated by the presence of the spy among their command. Argus notes that they have a pretty solid list of candidates, though Sky comments that it is a pretty sad affair that they were able to come up with that list so fast, or the fact that one exists at all.
- Frustrated with no good ideas coming up, Argus finds a quiet corner of Sky's workshop, sits down and closes his eyes, and focuses on the green-haired rind on his finger. *My lady Elyssria, we are at a loss as to what to do next, and we ask for your guidance.* A silent moment passes, and Argus suddenly feels a light breeze brush pass him, with the scent of crip autumn. When he opens his eyes, and without anyone else noticing right away, the leanansidhe Nyphadora appears among them in a twirl of multicolored leaves. With a gentle smile on her ethereal elf-like face, Elyssria's emissary greets the Company. *"Champions of Elyssria, our lady has heard your plea, and I come bringing knowledge that may aid you. Be aware, though, this aid is worth only the favour you are accustom to owing. Any greater aid, we fear, may push you to take more dire measures"* Argus nods. "Depending on how things go, we may indeed take up those dire measures." *"We shall see,"* Nyphadora comments. She approaches the table in the centre of the room, and from within her cloak of leaves, she pulls out and unfurls a map upon the table. *"This map represents the entire knigdom of Klorgfang, up to the current date. This kingdom has been growing for over ten years now, and have built a number of settlements. It is only now that their reach has met the Greenbelt, and so to their desire for the lands north of it. To their south are the lands of the cyclops, Carpathos. Although the trolls and cyclops are rivals, for now they are allies in trade. The majority of Klorgfang's inhabitants migrated from those lands, as are most of their slaves. One thing of note that might interest you is that you may have noticed that there are no sakkadi among their army ranks. The sakkadi of the Bluecrest tribe are not fond of their overlords, acquiescing to their rule only out of fear for their survival."*
- "Can we scry on someone there?" Nyphadora nods. *"The name of the Bluecrest chieftain is 'Voshk'."* The Company discuss ideas about gaining the sakkadi as allies, and how they could help them fight for their freedom behind they common enemy lines. *"You should also know that the three armies that stand against you are the entirety of their active forces. They have drained and consolidated all of their forces to press against you."*
- With that information in hand, the Company engage in another flurry of planning and discussion. But again, the issue with the spy complicates things. Arina turns to Nyphadora. "My lady, as you are here, you wouldn't happen to have any insight on this spy, would you?" Nyphadora slowly shakes her head. *"Alas, we could not divine their identity. This spy and their actions are occluded from both our mundane and magical means of observation. One thing of note that may be of note to you. Our lady is most powerful. To be able to hide from her divinations indicates that they are intimately familiar with the ways of the fey."*
- "Could it be Louis? Fuck, it could be Louis," Arina mutters. To the others' questioning gaze, Arina reminds them that Louis was whisked away from Carpathos to her side as an emissary of Elyssria. Nyphadora give Arina a quizzical look. *"Our lady had sent you no emissary other than myself."*
	- "GODS DAMN IT!"
	- "What?!"
	- "He was involved with all of our magical crafting!"
	- "That explains the weird marionette prophecy from that time he was in a coma!"
	- "What is Louis' specialty skills?" "Divination. He literally has future vision." "FUCK! Ah! But I'm magically warded to detect scrying!" "Which he knows!" "FUCK!"
- Now confidant on who their spy is, the Company is able to form a more solid plan. They now plan to pull their forces back north, and inform all commanders not currently present, that they plan on falling back to Promethea. However, midway there, they will issue new orders for their army to swing west, and confront Silverich's army. They will keep close watch on Louis to determine how and when he passes on this information to Hrondgren. With that plan set, they turn to thank Nyphadora, but she is no longer there, with only a few autumn leaves drifting across the floor. They instead give their thanks to her and Elyssria into the air above them.
- As Nyphadora vanishes, Jupus hears a whisper in his ears from her: *"I sense your deepening connection to Faerie, and it is potent. Should you require aid in regards to your family, you need but ask our Lady."*
- Using their *far crystal*, the team informs Davona, with Cortez and Louis present, of the plan to withdraw to Promethea, and that they will meet them at Orn Sini. At 6 pm, All three legions, along with the Rochefort Fusiliers, head north to Orn Sini. Meanwhile, word reaches them that Sivlerich's horder has begun moving closer towards Castrum Aurum.
