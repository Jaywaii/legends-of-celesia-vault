- The banquet now starts to wind down, heading very late into the night. Jupus and Argus are called away by their aides to attend to some last minute business. The rest of the Evergreen Company used their mental link to aid Arina in influencing Alcina Sotillini and Elvia Sovrani. Among the voices in her head, an old, familiar one pushes her to form a deeper relationship with the Sovrani diplomat, one that the licentious woman eagerly indulges in. When the banquet concludes, Elvia invites Arina to accompany her back to the embassy for a more... private discussion. With the same prompting in her mind, Arina accepts, while the rest of the Company suddenly find other things to be occupied with.
- Returning to her garrison, Davona is provided with a report of the day's security performance. Aside from an odd incident with a disappearing/reappearing cart, Prateria's security have nothing of significance to report.
- At the Lurindor embassy, Elvia escorts Arina into a private room. Servants provide late night refreshments, and quickly leave the two in private. Elvia engages in playful banter with her guest, and soon invites Arina to engage in a more... intimate form of diplomacy. With that voice still whispering to her at the back of her mind, Arina accepts once again, allowing herself to be lead by the older woman. After a very physical and pleasureable night, Arina wakes up in Elvia's bed. The diplomat is already up, and compliments the Praterian Consul for a delightful and very satisfying night. She invites Arina to share breakfast with her, and the two chat idly. While Elvia is quite chipper, Arina is more subdued. The staff of the embassy give her curious looks that is quickly replaced with understanding when they see her in Elvia's company. She swears she could hear faint laughter at the back of her mind. When breakfast ends, Elvia thanks Arina for her company, but she must sadly part ways here as she has much to do on this first day of games. However, she is looking quite forward to their appointment that evening. Without waiting long for a response, Elvia leaves, and the embassy staff escorts Arina back out.
- In the morning, Osla meets with Sunmother Isabelle Beauciel, the Kaïdenite matriarch of Lurindor. After performing the morning prayer at the main arena to bless today's games on behalf of Kaï-den, the two hold a discussion within Kaï-den's cathedral. Osla grants the Sunmother an open invitation to the church of Kaï-den to establish as many shrines and temples in Promethea and Arbor's Rest, and eventually any other settlement in Prateria, though it will be up to the church to fully fund such buildings. Isabelle agrees, on the condition that Prateria holds at least one yearly religious festival in the name of Kaï-den on one of his holy days.
- After freshing up, Arina joins Sky, and the two go the King's Castle hotel to meet with the Drelevo's. The hotel staff have prepared a hall for them, with refreshments being served. The annoying yapping of a dog soon alert them to the Drelevo's arrival. After the arrival of Rafael and Pavetta are announced and seated, Arina reuses the deferential tone she used the night before. She wishes to discuss forming a closer partnership with Drelevona, perhaps a joint settlement agreement as they already have with Grondeloup and Montari Entreprises. She suggests having the Sovrani Syndicate involved in a similar capacity, to which Drelevo slyly remarks that Arina sure has been getting... intimately cozy with Sovrani of late. Drelevo continues to exhibit a cocksure and dismissive demeanour, though his tone is softened by maintaing diplomatic etiquette. His wife is far less restrained in her disparaging remarks, and Drelevo only makes a token effort to excuse her rudeness. Before they enter any agreement, Drelevo is reluctant to involve any involvement in their affairs from any faction in Lurindor. Sky also asks if he could speak with Pavetta's brother, Drelevona's magister Ilario Strazza. Sky has a proposal for him, and Drelevo says that he will mention it to him, though his magister is quite busy, and that Sky will need to meet his price and provide a significant incentive to get his attention. At the end of the meeting, no agreement is finalized, though Drelevo will keep the proposal in mind while he observes how Prateria benefits with their current deal with Montari and Grondeloup.

- *The Opening Day banquet is being held at Prateria's town hall. The government building has been redecorated into a high class banquet hall, featuring fancy wall draperies, rows of tables covered in food and drinks lining the walls, and many round tables and chairs. A band of musicians play soft ball music. There is enough seats and room for each group of delegates, their athletes, and Prateria's leaders. Arina's staff takes charge of catering to everyone's needs, with Rebald Bitterswood announcing everyone's entrance, and Bradon Tourmaline overseeing the wait staff.* The banquet is around 4 hours long, broken up into 16 social rounds, each lasting about 15 minutes of interaction. During each social round, a PC can take one major action which can be either 1) attempt to influence an NPC, or 2) engage in a group discussion. Option #1 is a direct attempt to increase your sway with one NPC. Option #2 allows you to learn information, either about the topic of discussion, or about the discussion's participants. During one social round, you make a skill check relevant to the topic of discussion, and if you succeed, you choose either to gain a chance to make a discovery check on one NPC part of the discussion or gain an advantage to influence checks to one NPC. For every 10 above the DC, you can select another participant to choose what bonus to get, or select the 2nd bonus on the same NPC. If you fail, you embarass yourself, and take a disadvantage when attempting to influence any of the participants until you succeed at an influence check with that particular NPC. The DC is 35.
