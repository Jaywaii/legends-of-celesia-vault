# Notes

## The Wolfshold Withering

Emergency message from Châteaufoy requesting aid for Valenlet.

Elyssria sends a dream to Rishoi about a ritual to shield a city against the Gloom of Vorakios: *As you close your eyes, an ethereal breeze carries your thoughts along soft currents. When you open your eyes, you find yourself in a glade bathed in gentle moonlight. Blooming with beauty, and veiled with twinkling starlight, the Queen of Whisperveil awaits you at the glade's center, with an enigmatic smile gracing her lips. You already know what is expected of you, and you step forward and offer of you what is due to her. Her smile never leaves her lips, but where before there was expectation, there is now appreciation. She leans forward, and soft words flow from her lips to your ears. Knowledge, deep and arcane, swell your mind. A vision appears before your of a great spring of sunlight showering above a city to bath it with a curtain of golden power, holding at bay a deathly gloom. At the light's source stands a lone figure, gifted with power by those who love her, battling with mind and soul for however long it takes to keep the darkness at bay.*

Elyssria's ritual, the Pall of Shining Life, is fairly simple. It requires 25 mythic power to be reserved for as long as the Pall is maintained, and needs Rishoi to remain in a meditative state at its center, where she will be in a mental battle against the source of the Gloom.

Thousands of zombies are seen approaching Arbor's Rest: *Scouts returned. Report mist held at forest border. Unending zombies march towards Arbor's Rest. Estimate arrival in one day.*
