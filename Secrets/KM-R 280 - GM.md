# Notes

- Morrigan, Igvald, Yujimbo, Tao
- Outer Spawn (CR 17): 16,200 XP
- Total: 16,200 XP / 4 = 4,050 XP each.
- To level 10: 135,454 / 385,000 XP.

- Gate of Shorok'logoa: The round stone slab is completely covered in engraved symbols and patterns, all twisted together. It is dizzying just staring at it. Understanding it requires a DC 45 Knowledge check to figure out it is a summoning circle (deals 4 sanity damage on success, 8 sanity damage on fail). Success reveals this is a [cosmic horror summoning ritual](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/alternative-rule-systems/occult-adventures/occult-rules/occult-rituals/#Fiendish_Conjuration) (DC 45). Upon summoning the [[outer spawn]] ([d20pfsrd](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/bestiary/monster-listings/aberrations/outer-spawn/outer-spawn-the-third-stage/)), the outer spawn seeks to merge with a sacrifice, a true soul. If refused, it will attack to force a merger. Once defeated, the PCs can consume its flesh to see into the ether and unlock the door (DC 30).
- Luring Team 13 to the center of the Ing-gol'zha:
	- Talien wants to lure Team 13 to the center so that they can destroy the thul-koon overmind.
	- Te'Shaad absolutely tries to compel Igvald into going in.
	- The Overmind uses its connection to the city to hamper the PCs efforts to seal the tower.

- Navigation (7 gates):
	- Sanity damage: 8
	- DC 60 vs Will
	- Sleep DC 45 for half
