# Notes

- **The Chamber of Worship:** *The chamber is vastly large, curving upward into a dome. Its walls are completely engraved with bas-relief depicting alien imagery and symbols. Seven great statues have been erected around the chamber, each one depicting the alien forms of each of the Seven Cosmic Horrors entombed within Celesia. A large, dark pit features in the middle of the chamber. The floor of the chamber rises up around the pit to a slightly more elevated landing, an altar space of some kind. At the centre of this landing sits a long, emaciated, dessicated humanoid figure, sitting in a lotus position.*
