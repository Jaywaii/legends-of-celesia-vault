- The Evergreen Company surrounds their invisible quarry, outlined in sparkling glitter and frozen in place. Once Davona and Argus recover from their blindness caused by Sky's *glitterdust*, Prateria's Praetor goes to ground and grapples the paralyzed spy while the Tribune pulls out some rope and binds him. Argus also threatens the invisible man with a terrible fate if he does not cooperate, while Rishoi feels around his body, amd removes the spy's sword and bow. Osla dismisses the *hold person*, and Davona can feel the man start stuggling in her arms. Just as the Company starts questioning him, an arrow lands in the man's throat.
- Lumina immediately takes to the air, and Arina flashes to the top of a rooftop. Yet despite their keen sight, they are unable to see anyone who might have made such a shot. Splashed with blood, Davona feels the life leave the man in her arms, and his invisibility ceases. Davona and Argus fly up as well. Rishoi acts quickly; drawing upon the deep serenity within her, she lays her hands on the deceased man, and power flows from within out into the body. The man suddenly gasps for breath, coughing up blood, but evidently alive, his wide open eyes staring in shock up at Rishoi. For a moment, he thinks he sees this red-skin malephilim surrounded by a heavenly halo. Osla, in a gentle voice, once again asks the man his name. Perhaps befuddled by his sudden resurrection, he replies with "Puccino". When pressed for further information, he says, "I-I need time... to understand... but all I can admit, is that you don't know the forces you are facing."
- Worried about another attack, Sky grabs the man, Puccino, and tells the others that he is going to take him to a safe place, and if anyone wants to come, to grab hold of him or their captive. Osla opts to go with them, while the rest will focus on finding the sniper. Sky *teleports* the three of them to the backroom of his magic shop, the Sky's Wondrous Emporium. Puccino then starts coughing and hacking up blood, and Sky, examining the black veins, pale skin, and other symptoms, surmises that he was poisoned with deathblade from the arrow that struck him. Thanksfully, Osla is able to heal the damage to their captive's body long enough for the poison to past. Puccino thanks her, and then drops the rope that was binding him. He makes a dash to the door, but Sky quickly *dominates* him, and has him return to his seat.
- The rest of the Company soon gave up on trying to find their mysterious shooter. They spent the past fifteen minutes flying in the sky or hopping from rooftop to rooftop. The only results were onlookers in the street gawking at them in awe. They head to the Emporium, where Sky explains the current status of their prisoner. They begin questioning him, although Sky cautions that it they try to ask Puccino to answer questions that he is naturally opposed to answering, he may have a chance to break the domination.
- Using *share memory*, Sky succeeds in viewing two of Puccino's memories. He sees Puccino with seven other companions, all disguised as various civilians, disembarking from a ship and infiltrating Promethea at night. The second memory was also at night. Puccino was on a rooftop adjacent to the hotel where the Teredoro Consortium were lodging. He was with another Coil agent, a woman. Puccino kept watch outside while the woman leapt onto the outer wall of the hotel, and easily climbed up and into the room through a window. However, when she came out a minute later, she was seen by one of Argus' guards from the ground. She leapt back to the adjacent rooftop, and both she and Puccino vanished from sight.
- With a few questions, the Evergreen Company learns that Puccino is an agent of the Coil, an organization claiming to be the true inheritors of ruling this world. Sky remembers a few mentions of a group identified as the Coil scattered within a variety of history books. This group is almost always mentioned as a footnote, influencing the direction of nations from the background. Sky also remembers Asako mentioning that her Argent Rose teachers warned her against looking into the Coil, saying that she is not ready to get into that subject yet. Asking more about the Coil, they Company learns that the Coil believes they earned their claim by taking it from the serpentfolk. Sky informs the rest of the team that the serpentfolk most likly mean the Sserassin, a reptilian race that ruled the surface of Celesia and enslaved all the other races over five hundred thousand years ago, in the Second Age. This prompts Sky to begin erecting a *mage's private sanctum* in his shop.
- While Sky does his casting, Arina activates her *unspeakable name*. About five minutes later, she suddenly becomes aware of someone speaking her title of 'Consul'. She concentrates and *scries* the utterer. She gains a vision of a woman, dressed in the same gear at Puccino. She is speaking with another woman and two men dressed the same in what appears to be a secluded backalley. "...including the Consul. are with him in the shop. We'll need to wait for them to leave before we can make an attempt to silence Puccino." The supposedly Coil agents then discuss of what to do regarding the assassination tonight, and agree to observe and wait for an opportunity to silence their companion until 2 pm, before reconvening at the hideout with the others. The Coil split up, and Arina's sensor follows her target to the entrance of another alley a block away from the Emporium.
- Arina toys with the female Coil agent by manifesting a *project image* of herself before her. Argus charges out of the Emporium, with Osla trailing behind him. They both see the Coil agent at the entrance of the alley, attacking and missing Arina's image. Osla's *hold person* fails to take effect, but Argus swings his earth breaker down at the agent. For a brief moment, a second, duplicate Coil agent appears beside the first, but then immediately disappears as Argus' earth breaker smashes down into her head, crushing into the middle of her torso. Blood and bone explodes onto the Argus, the wall, and into the street. Onlookers gasp and scream at the sight.

- The Company catches up to the prone observer. However, the *held* observer activates the *freedom of movement* of his *unfettered shirt*, breaking free of the *hold person* spell.
