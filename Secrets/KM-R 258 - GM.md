## Sea of Mulemria

- Barloth-keem: a port city on the Sarmassus Shore. It is a vast city replete with tall, sandy towers connected with numerous bridges and platforms, covered by hanging gardens. A cornucopia of rich, spicy smells reaches your nose. The loud hubbub of a vibrant and active populace fills the air, accented by music echoing in the background.
- After the team gives the signet ring to the Ruby Mistress, she rewards them with a milky white conch shell that flickers rainbow colours when shone with light. She tells them to blow the conch at their target destination, and it will summon what they desire.
- Players need to find transport to take them to the Sea of Mulemria. Xura Nythagoi can help.
- Nythagoi's ship, the *Moon Siren*, is captained by Gaxxio Lethghul. Upon entering the Sea of Mulemria, blowing the conch summons a sea storm. The ship begins rocking from the waves and being hit with sheets of rain and strong wind.
	- Ship is considered difficult terrain.
	- On round 1, and every 1d3 rounds later, a wave washes over the ship (bull rush +30). DC 35 Acrobatics to stay standing.
- * Upon blowing the conch, a colossal primordial ancestor of the incutili swims up below the ship, called the 'Othoculoid'. 8 tentacles (DV 45, DR 20/armor, reach 100 ft., BAB +30, dmg 4d4+20, grab +40, fast swallow, swallow whole 2d4 acid, move 60 ft.) emerge to grab and swallow victims. The Othoculoid' main body is housed in an enormous nautiloid shell covered in thousands of pearls. Each pearl is a thought of an incutilis lord.
- 4 [deep one elders](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/bestiary/monster-listings/monstrous-humanoids/deep-one-elder/) called Dragalurgs emerge to assist their master.
