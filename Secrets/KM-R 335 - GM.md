# Notes

## Dealing with the Deathmist

- After the PCs enter, Pyrouranos' arrow winks out  However, Vorakios closes the portal to trap the PCs in. The fog is at full strength in here, but thankfully not leaking out, but deadly to PCs. Elyssria's ring flares to life. PCs can offer favor to protect against the fog.
- **Vorakios Deathmist:** DC 55 Will save against being mind-controlled. CMB +55 as undead arms grab and claw through the fog (20d6 piercing/slashing). Blocked vision at 5 ft.
- **NOTE:** Myles PC needs backstory as to how he survives the fog. Possible final blessing from Champion and allies to allow them to infiltrate Vorakios' lair.

## Introduction to the Fairy Grave

- **As the portal's light fades from your eyes, you are faced with an unbelievable sight. Gone are walls of grey stone and the darkness of caverns. You find yourselves in a field of purple grass, pink-leafed trees, and a pastel orange sky above your heads. A sweet scent reaches your nose, but is quickly spoiled by an accompanying scent of grave rot.**
- **The fog at your feet begins rising, here at its source, it is at its full potency. As the fog overhwelms you, and your sight is blocked out, you feel the gnarled, undead head reaching out to grab and tear i to you. But the ring of green hair flares to life, and you feel the familiar warmth of Elyssria, beckoning your will to meet with her power.**
- **Vorakios Speech:** **A powerful, raspy voice booms throughout the sky above you, seemingly from nowhere.*,  "You believe that by entering here, you think you can defeat me? Fools, you have only consigned yourselves to a fate worst than death. Bemoan your terrible fate, for your little acts of defiance against the inevitable have given you false hope. For I am the Apostle of the Deathless Shroud, the Seer of Souls, and this place will be your Fairy Grave!"*
- **As soon as the voice ends, the trees bulge and the ground shifts. Out emerges amidst green smoke lithe primanoids with emerald eyes and horns and antlers grown of out of their heads. Accompanying them are large hounds with similar emerald eyes and horns.**

## Myles' PCs

- Myles' PCs were once members of the Thunder Companions, heroes united against Vorakios's threat, led by the Thunderhoof centaur champion Althea (female centaur). Other members include:

- The High Beacon of Barom-gar (female dwarf): sacrificed her life (along with many other Lightbringer high priests) to clear the battlefield of Vorakios' deathmist for the final battle.
- The Stellar Sage of Selimbral (male elf): sacrificed his life (along with many other high casters) in creating Pyrouranos.
- Cassivan the Bright Mender (male human): student of the High Beacon and her successor, lover of Althea.

- Before the final battle with Vorakios, Myles' martial and caster, along with other companions (TBD by Myles), volunteered to sneak into Vorakios' lair to find and destroy his soul vessel should Pyrouranos not be the final end of him.
- Myles' companions all died in the Fairy Grave. In order to have their souls be claimed by Vorakios, they and the caster agreed to have the caster host their souls until they can get out. These souls kept the caster alive (the martial already had immortality).
- To survive trapped in Fairy Grave, Myles' PCs had the power (*Slip Into Oblivion*) to make Vorakios forget them for a while, but this cost them their own memories as well.
- ***Slip Into Oblivion*:** This fairy magic was gifted to one of Myles' PCs as a reward for an important service. However, the characters can no longer remember what that service was, nor the fairy who gifted it to them. (3 acts to cast, costs 1 negative mythic or 2 negative lvls, another willing creature can take the negative, max targets equal to caster level + ).
- To survive Vorakios' *Deathmist*, Myles' PCs and their companions were blessed with a portion of the *Pall of Shining Life* by the High Beacon (before her sacrifice) and Cassivan. This aura prevented the necromantic fog from controlling their minds and shielded them from being grabbed and torn by the undead limbs that emerged from the mist. However, in order to maintain this protective aura, Myles' PCs had to regularly sacrifice their power (i.e. levels) to fuel it over these many millenia.
