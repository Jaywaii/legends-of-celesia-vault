- The Evergreen Company continues to host their high-ranking guests and their athletes at the evening banquet to celebrate the opening of the Viridian Games.
- Sky joins a conversation between the Duloup and Châteaufoy magisters, Alcina Sotillini, and Joliette Beauchamp of the Argent Rose who are discussing the application of magic in warfare and mercantile pursuits. Joining her brother, Davona speaks with Beauchamp, offering her perspective on magical warfare as a soldier. The Argent Rose chapter-sage finds her perspective fascinating, and the two engage in a productive conversation.
- Arina and Rishoi joins the group of Rafael Drelevo, Elvia Sovrani, Sarpino Montari, and Cyrano Varano. They are discussing the current politics of Lurindor, and how they compare to those of other nations. By being overly respectful and deferential towards him in their tone, Arina and Rishoi succeed in opening up a dialog to the normally dismissive Drelevo, and the three of them actually engage in a productive dialog. They discover that behind all of the bravado and sense of self-importance, Rafael Drelevo has a keen mind, and it comes to no real surprise that he has managed to run his colony so well so far.
- Osla approaches the group of Adeline Châteaufoy, Giovanni Biancardi, and Fidenzio Teredoro. They are going back and forth about the origins of certain Lurindor traditions, whether the Valderêve halflings or the Ispirani humans originated them. Osla tries to engage in the conversation, but she gets many of her facts wrong, leaving her to awkwardly just stand there. She does have a bit more success with talking to Châteaufoy directly. Osla, being new to the region, inquires on halfling culture and traditions, and Adeline is more than happy to talk to her about it.
- Argus and Jupus arrive at the banquet after having finished their duties. Argus approaches Sotillini to discuss the issues with the Ironwatch and the mercenary drain, and trying to impress her with their fight with the Dancing Lady. However, he fails to make much of an impression with her as Alcina gives him only curt responses before returning to her conversation with the others.
- Jupus joins Osla, with the intent on observing the Châteaufoy matriarch. However, the gnome failed to remember his history lessons from his youth, and was unfamiliar with the events brought up during the discussion. When Osla begins speaking with Châteaufoy directly, Jupus joins in. However, he still is not able to make much of an impression. Finally, he pulls out his trump card by bringing up that his knowledge of Rochefort by his interactions with the Montplaisir family. At that family's mention, Jupus could only determine that Châteaufoy puts up the typical politician façade of non-committal response. The two tip-toe around the subject for a bit before Châteaufoy redirects the conversation back to the original topic at hand, and Jupus discreetly slips out of the group.
- Needing to better coordinate their networking efforts, Argus sent a runner to bring his cohort Maalek. After the second-in-command of the Iron Watch arrives, the aelfkyn tactician mentally adds the Company to his collective, allowing them to communicate telepathically. Using this mental link the party helps Arina approach Elvia Sovrani and succeed in engaging in a productive discussion with her while impressing the diplomat in how she subtlely navigates around Sovrani's blatant innuendo, teasing, and crude remarks.
- A fight nearly breaks out between the Sootscale athletes and a group of Lurindori athletes. The humans are insulting and mocking the kobolds, while the kobolds are threatening to eat the humans alive. King Sootscale and his personal retinue are trying to placate the athletes, but seem more afraid of their own athletes than the taller humans. The delegates' attention is drawn to the conflict, and many express their disapproval at the scene and disappointment at the disruption of the festivities.
- Rialla steps in between the two groups. Immediately, her presence prevents tempers from flaring even more, though just barely. Argus comes up to stand behind imposingly, arms crossed across his broad chest. Davona signals to the guards to stand by, but be ready to jump in. Rialla addresses both parties, appealing to them to take out their aggression in the games starting tomorrow. With a special power behind her voice, Rialla is able to diffuse the situation. The Lurindor athletes turn away, making comments that the kobolds are not worth it, and that Rialla is pretty cute. The kobolds turn away, eager to finish their meal. To the dignified onlookers, Arina and Rishoi are able to spin the situation to them as a demonstration of the conflict resolution skills of Prateria's leaders, an example of what their nation can provide in the world of international politics. While the dignitaries are quite familiar with such hyperbole and spin, they are nonetheless impress by Prateria's leaders skill in capitalizing on the opportunity from a near-disaster.
- With the situation completely diffused, the banquet resumes, and everyone splits off into new groups. The Evergreen Company switched tactics, and used their telepathic link to help each other to focus on influencing particular targets.
