# Notes

- Defeated squad of forest drakes (UCR 5): Cohor Consul Magica +1,600 XP
- Defeated company of harpy rangers (UCR 11): Cohor Consul Magica +12,800 XP

- Cohors Consul Magica continue scouting the southern coast of the Greenbelt. On their second day, the immediately discover another Klorgfang squad of forest drakes. The unit ambushes them as they did before to the last one, and as the last one, the unit eliminated the drakes before they could retaliate. Continuing east and south, and discovering farms worked by chained humans and halflings under trollish and orcish yoke, the unit is finally detected by the enemy. As they approached another Klorgfang fort, a company of harpies flies towards them. The harpies are unable to find the squad of invisible wizards, but are so on guard that the Consul Magica are unable to leave or set up another ambush, only focus on evading the harpies. After several hours of this game of cat-and-mouse, Geraldo, the squad's leader, calls out for Arina. The Consul scries them, and they report their situation to her. Arina rapidly flashes to them, and is able to join them amidst the harpy patrols. Under Arina's command, Consul Magica manages to ambush the harpies, blasting them from the sky with a magical barrage. In the end, they capture twenty harpy survivors. However, a second company of harpies had observed the fight and reported the outcome to their commander. Consul Magica detects that the second harpy company had left the fort, joined by two companies of goblin worg-riders on the ground. Arina leads her wizards and the prisoners north, evading the harpy scouts and outpacing the riders. Cohors Consul Magica manages to touchdown in Promethea, exhausted, but welcomed with elated cheers from the people as news of their string of victories spread.
- In light of their initial success, Prateria commissions magical gear to equip several of their units, many *rings of sustenance* in particular. Meanwhile Legio I and II continue to be organized, as are the remaining units for Legio III who will join Cohors Consul Magica. Attila Rakirsson himself will lead his Rughir warriors as part of Legio III. Arina arranges for his orcs to have direwolf mounts and the training needed to ride them.
