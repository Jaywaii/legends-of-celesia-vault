# Notes

- Tabrir researches the devils Furfu and Baal at the Argent Rose's Lodge in Promethea. He spends ten days among the tomes, and uncovers the following information, exhausting the college's collection of infernal lore:
	- Furfur (30 kp):
		- Furfur is an Earl of Hell, patron of storms and love. He is depicted as a winged man-hart hybrid, and is a known patron of perytons. His cult actively form alliances with these beasts. Manipulating relationships to gain control over people is one of their primary means of furthering Furfur's influence.
		- Furfur rules his realm of the Tempest Spire, a towering spike of screaming flesh, protruding from the Gilded Depths. The Spire is perpetually shrouded in clouds flashing with lightning and thunder.
		- Furfur belongs to the court of Belial, Infernal Prince of Lust, and is reputedly one of his most loyal retainers. Furfur served Belial since their time in the Celestial Host. Many believe that Furfur is simply using his relationship with Belial to further his own ends, or to manipulate the Prince into doing his bidding. Others also suggest that Belial is fully aware of this, allows it to occur, confident that he is the one manipulating Furfur.
	- Baal (30 kp):
		- Baal is a Duke of Hell, and a master of illusion and deception. Baal manifests himself through various forms, but usually picks being a man, toad, bull, or any combination thereof. His cult excels at masking their true intentions, and worming their way into the confidence of others without revealing their true selves.
		- Baal rules the Shrouded Fathoms of the Gilded Depths. A perpetual mist permeates this realm, trapping souls within, doomed to wander for eternity, forever haunted by painful memories.
		- Baal serves Beelzebub, Infernal Prince of Gluttony. Baal once held his liege's position, a powerful Infernal Prince in his own right, but the Lord of the Flies outmaneuvered, and tricked Baal into losing his seat. By the terms of the contract that caused his downfall, Baal is locked into faithfully serving Beelzebub, but that does not prevent him from inciting intrigues to bring his lord's own fall from grace.
	- Elyssria: Tabrir has one more day reserved at the Lodge, so he spends it looking into Elyssria, but is unable to learn anything of substance in just the one day spent there.
- Tabrir refocuses his attention into building dossiers on the incoming dignitaries for the Viridian Games. Along with Arina, the two collect every scrap of rumour and public report on several of them, hoping that this knowledge will help them in future discussions.
- Bekka wishes to start rebuilding Prateria's scout forces, but needs the buildings required for recruitment. Unfortunately, Prateria's finances had to be audited, and it turns out that the nation had less resources than they fought, as their calculations failed to take into account the tribute they had been sending to Lurindor. Arina promised that Watchtowers would be built, but they would have to wait. On the plus side, The followers of Argus, Arina, and Davona had earned them hundreds of gold pieces over the past year.
- Plans are being made for the 9 days of the Viridian Games. The first day would be the Opening Ceremony, and the last day will be the Closing Ceremony. The Evergreen Company need to make plans for the morning, afternoon, and evening, and perhaps the night for more clandestine activities, of each day. Many games are already being scheduled, but the Company need to plan for banquests for their guests, and possible meeting appointments to discuss business and issues.
- On Moonday 14th, the Groundeloup delegation arrives by carriage and horseback. On the 15th, the Rochefort delegation is planned to arrive by skyship, and the the rest of the Lurindor delegations the day after via similar means of transportation. The delegations from Drelevona and Sootscale will arrive on the 17th, the day before the start of the Viridian Games.

- Ensure payment to Lurindor for the month of Firhen.
- Bekka wishes to rebuild her scouts. She needs a military building to start recruiting, but all the current garrisons are occupied with Argus' or Davona's people. A Watchtower would be a good start, and Promethea can benefit from 9 of them.
- *The flow of new settlers would not be stemmed this month. More followers of the Evergreen Company came to join their leaders in Promethea. However, there was still a lack of housing, forcing many to seek shelter by squatting the land around the settlement, interfering with additional construction.*
	- Unable to build new buildings in Promethea; Fame and Stability decrease by 1, and Unrest increases by 2. You may try to disperse the squatters with a Stability check. Success means the squatters are dispersed, the penalties are removed, and the event is not continuous. However, if a House or Tenement is not built by the next Event Phase, Infamy increases by 1 for 1 year and Unrest by 2. Failing the Stability check means the event is continuous, and you may not construct new buildings in that settlement until the event is resolved.
- Justice Prevails (settlement): Authorities shut down a major criminal operation or thwart a plot against the settlement. Law and Loyalty increase by 1 and Crime and Unrest decrease by 1.
- Tabrir unveils some results from his research into devils.
- Dignitaries begin arriving in Promethea, starting with Theodore Duloup.
