# Notes

- Morrigan, Igvald, Yujimbo, Tao
- (CR ):  XP
- Total:  XP / 4 =  XP each.
- To level 10: 113,380 / 385,000 XP.

- Amaruqiel Wintersong thanks Team 13 for saving the remaining Whitewind elves and defeating the reawakened gnoph-keh. With the discovery of Roth-Teghan's location, Wintersong has requested reinforcements from the Argent Rose to form an expedition to investigate Roth-Teghan for archeological and threat assessment purposes.
- Team 13 can instantly return to Mysthaven via Kuvyiak's teleport circle. Morrigan will have to spend 2 weeks on fast airship to reac the same location. Chapter-Sage Sumiko welcomes the team back.
- Team 13 will then have to travel back to Hushglow Tower which by now a small settlement has been developed. Rose Scholar Thonra 'Mindshield' remains in command of the expedition. The Hushglow expedition have been keeping watch around the tower, doing cursory archeological surveys, but maintain strict policy of not letting anyone enter the tower. However, that hasn't stopped a foolhardy soul or a reckless plunderer risk themselves from slipping past the Argent Rose guard and explore what's now called the outer districts of Ing-gol'zha. Needless to say, they were never seen or heard from again, at least not as they originally were. Their screams sometime echo up to the underground hallway that led to the main stairs to the city. On occasion, the Argent Rose sentinels had to hold down a hapless explorer driven mad, or their undead form. Over the past 3 years, Thonra also had to deal with the machinations of cultists trying to insinuate themselves into the settlement with the cooperation of Prateria's security forces. While their plots were foiled, Thonra is sure there are still cultist hiding among the new citizens of Hushglow.
- One thing of note is that the sentinels report hearing faint music and singing echoing through the city that managed to reach upward. It is alien and haunting, but still feels nostalgic in some way. It started shortly after Team 13 unlocked the 7th and final gate 3 years ago. Thonra suspects that the singing is like a siren's call, and what caused the majority of hapless Argent Rose guards to disobey orders and venture into Ing-gol'zha. Precautions have since been used and Argent Rose losses have significantly dropped, but other people not so protected are still finding their way into that damn city.
