# Notes

## Davik Nettles

- When Arina enters the psychic duel, she finds herself standing beside a tall, thin man in a black, genetlman's suit, wearing tall top hat, and wielding a black cane. He wears a wide-grinning theatre clown mask with blue and red makeup, with a single black tear. The tall man dances around Arina, cackling. *"Oh my dear, how you get yourself in the most entertaining situations!"* he taunts. If Arina accurately guesses that this is Mister Angelcakes, he merely gives her a deep coutrly bow before continuing to dance and taunt her.
	- With a wave of a hand in front of his face, Angelcakes' mask changes to a sad one. *"How could you seek to be rid of me, my dear? We can still have so much fun together!"* With another wave of the hand, the mask changes back to a grinning one.
	- *"I have I loved you, since the day you gave me my name. And have I not guided you to the one that you love?"* Angelcakes spins his cane, and then points to an empty area.
- Shadows coalesce into a lithe, petite form, cloaked in tattered torn black clothing. She is visibly battered, one side of her body and head crushed inward. Arina recognizes when she last saw this ghostly form. It is the cloaked woman who interferred in the battle of the Dancing Lady, crushed by Argus' enlarged blow. She carries an expression of woe. *"Why, my love? Why did you kill me?"* (Marcelette now participates in the psychic duel against Arina as a thought-form maifestation under Angelcakes' control against Arina.)

> **AC** 13; **hp** 141; **Attack Bonus** +23 x 3; **Damage** 1d10 + 6; **Saves** +19 (Ref +21); **Psychic Backlash** 1d10+25; evasion, Nettles immune to attacks to AC.

- If Arina is on the verge of defeat, Angelcakes offers his help. Each time she refuses, she gains 1 FATE point.
	- Angelcakes can redirect a crippling injury from Arina to Louis ("a hand"), Marcelette ("a heart"), or himself ("your skin"). Angelcakes counts off each time the injury is redirect to him.
	- Angelcakes can heal Arina by transferring hp from one source to another, phrasing it the same way as a crippling injury (8d8+8). Angelcakes counts off each time hp is drawn from him.
- At the end of the fight, Angelcakes claps his hands together.
	- **If Arina wins:** Angelcakes congratulates her. *"Well done! I've alway known you would grow so strong, and with more potential left unchecked."*
	- **If Arina loses:** Angelcakes admonishes her. *"Are you so sure now that you should be rid of me?"*
