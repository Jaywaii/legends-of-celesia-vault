# Notes

- Sky got Erikos, Evard, and Illario to agree to fund their own wings of the magic academy, and to spend at least 1 day a week attending to academy affairs.

## Nouyan 113

- It's technically winter, but we won't apply winter effects to the realm for now. In the future, winter effects reduces Food levels by half.
- With winter in full force, the conflict in the Thalamech Uplands has died down to a stalemate with ongoing small skirmishes. All 3 factions are focusing on building up supplies.
- During this downtime, Iolanthe summons the Book of Recall. In it, she write that she is taking up Sky on his offer about sharing information about artifacts. In it, she requests to know what you know about a legendary bow called Pyrouranos, a relic important to her people, but has been lost for many generations.

## The Coil

- The Coil continue to spread rumours that discredit the Argent Rose. The Society's close association with the Evergreen Company lowers Support.
- During Nouyan, there is an incident in Hushglow. The emblem of the Argent Rose on the wall of the Bardic College was splashed with red paint during the night, with the word 'murderers' hastily and sloppily painted next to it.
- Investigation will reveal that the act of vandalism was committed by a pair of teenagers whose parents were killed in the Hushglow madness. They were encourage to do what they did after overhearing some merchants talking about how the Argent Rose seemed to have gotten off scot-free, and that someone should remind the public of their crimes.
- The Coil will start targeting Steamflogger Industries with sabotage by 3rd-parties in order to make them seek out Gaspario Vespari for help.

## Fang & Claw Society

- F&C work to raise Morale and lower Prateria support in Greentusk.
- Orok's efforts in Morgarag to raise support for the Evergreen Company was successful, but there appears to be some backsliding as dissenting talk reaches his ears. The dissent came in the form of older residents talking about how under Klorgfang rule, their people could unleash their primal urges so freely.
- Investigation will reveal that a lot of the conversations originate from elderly residents that were known to be devout worshipers of Sevrash or Tiamat. Furthermore, the dissenting talk is not limited to Morgarag, but similar conversations are taking place throughout the Greentusk province.

## Rishoi

- Rishoi entered into seclusive meditation, seeking wisdom about her mother's fate. Make a Wisdom check.
	- less than DC 30: Rishoi's meditation is peaceful, but does not gain any insight this month. However, it is good training on inner reflection and she gains a +1 bonus on next month's seclusion.
	- DC 25: Rishoi experiences feelings of love, followed by betrayal, then deadly pain, followed by regret accompanied with a twisted amusement.
	- DC 30: Focusing on these feelings, Rishoi comes face to face with a vision of her mother Prithvi. She is twisted and sinister, playing with a pair of dolls, at first making them perform acts of romance before having them kill each other. She projects a long shadow behind her, far greater in size than her.
	- DC 35: The shadow seems to cling to Prithvi. Focusing on the shadow, it takes the form of a long, lanky figure with an oversized top hat. You suddenly start feeling a pressure surrounding you, almost suffocating, and you realize that you have gained the attention of a powerful entity. Prithvi remains oblivious, continuing to play with her dolls, but the shadow expands, and sweeps around her and both vanish into darkness.
