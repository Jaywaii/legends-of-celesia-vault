- In the morning of the 4th day of games, Osla meets with Battle-Brother Roghar Steelfist of Karthamor. They speak of future competitions, and Roghar offers that the church of Karthamor would be more than happy to help organize such tests of strength. Such aid would be much easier once they establish temples within Prateria.
- Shortly after, Arina, Argus, Jupus, and Osla go to meet Beltrude Harkûr at her hotel. Jupus proposes to the stoic dwarf diplomat that they modifiy their current business deal to have the Harkûr Trade Guild partially fund a trade route from Promethea to one of their settlements in exchange for Prateria to complete the upgrade of Harkûr's trade shop in Promethea to a guildhall significantly before the due date. Beltrude proposes that if Prateria completes the guildhall upgrade within six months, the Trade Guild will fund half of the proposed trade route. The two parties come to an agreement, and Jupus further delights the diplomat with talk of engineering crafting that he has come to embrace. The dwarf and gnome engage in a technical, but very animated, discussion, much that goes over the heads of the rest of the audience. At the end of the meeting, Beltrude mentions that she will speak with her clan's engineers to see about establishing a venue of communication with Jupus, and that he is invited to one day visit Dol Harkûr to witness their own feats of engineering.
- In the afternoon, Arina, Argus, and Osla went to the *Nomad* to have a late lunch with the Sootscale delegation. Osla invited her friend Lumina to come along. They are seated at two sets of tables, one for the Evergreen Company and King Sootscale and his personal retinue, and the other for the Sootscale competitors. The competitors are loud and boistrous, gorging themselves on roasted meat and ale. King Sootscale thanks the Evergreen Company for their hospitality, and inquires on their expansion plans. Arina is quick to reassure him that Prateria intends to respect the Sootscales and give them space to grow. Sootscale turns to Argus and inquires about his military forces. Argus confirms that they are many and well-trained. Sootscale is glad, and says that he merely wishes to confirm the strength of his neighbours. Argus reassures him that Prateria will forever remain a good friend to the Sootscales, to which the kobold king is thankful. Arina and Osla both notice how nervous the king is throughout this whole discussion, and he keeps glancing to his competitors. His retinue, Mikmek, Carsca, and Samska, appear equally nervous. When asked about this, Sootscale reassures them that there is nothing to be worried about, it is just some slight intimidation at the growth of Promethea. Before everyone leaves at the end of the meeting, Osla approaches Sootscale again, one-on-one, to let him know that if there is anything bothering him, he could tell her, but Sootscale once again makes assurances that all is fine. As the kobold contingent departs, the lead champion of hte Sootscale competitors throws an arm around his king's shoulders, openly joking and laughing, with Sootscale joining in, albeit less enthusiastically. Argus thinks it is a grand thing that a king would be so friendly with his subjects, but Arina and Osla notice how stiff Sootscale's tail is throughout the ordeal. Lumina volunteers to stealthily observe the Sootscales to make sure everything is all right with them. The Company agrees and suggests that Cortez accompanies her. Lumina seeks out Cortez, who surprises her from behind. Cortez thinks the task will be a good exercise for him, and joins the anthoulai in her surveillance.
- That night, Argus and Davona receive an urgent report from their security forces. Their people discovered cloaked individuals sneaking into the quarters of the Teredoro delegation at their hotel. Arriving on site, the two leaders are informed that the culprits managed to escape capture, demonstrating possible master-level skills or magic in evading the guard. However, the guard is able to confirm that the target room was the one assigned to the Teredoro melee champion Rocco Leonti. Rocco was not present, and is currently out enjoying the city's offerings of entertainment. Argus orders his people to search the room, and to find Rocco and watch him.
- They return to the town hall and inquire with the Games' organizers who Rocco is facing tomorrow. Rocco's next challenger is the melee champion from Mariporta, who Argus also orders to place a watch on. Maalek joins him at the Town Hall some ten minutes later, having received the findings from Rocco's rooms. The guard found no dangers in the room, but did find two notes that were out-of-place. The first was a flowery letter with a scent of sweet perfum, and was an invitation from Elvia Sovrani to Rocco to come visit her at her quarters tomorrow night so that she may have a more hands-on appreciation of his prowess. The second note is a trade contract regarding pigs and sheep. Suspicious of it, Argus has Jupus meet them at the town hall, and when the gnome examines it, he agrees that this deal makes no sense. As written, there is no profit to be made, and the humbers do not add up. Calling in the rest of the Evergreen Company, each take their turn examining the trade contract, and are sure that something is hidden in there, but they are unable to break the code. Finally, a tired Tabrir, after another day of attending to diplmatic business with each of the delegation, stumbles into the town hall, wondering what the commotion is about. After a brief explanation, he examines the contract, and with growing trepidation, reveals that if he deciphered this note correctly, it says here that Prateria has entered into an agreement with the Teredoro Consortium to allow them to assassinate the Sovrani diplomat!
