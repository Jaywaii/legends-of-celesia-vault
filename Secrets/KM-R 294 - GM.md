# Notes

## Gutted Boar

The Gutted Boar is an inn/tavern built out of Onestrio's old homestead when the trade post was just one building with a few shacks. The trade post is now a fort with tall, thick stone walls that surrounds multiple buildings that house troops, their mounts, and enough accomodations for the numerous traders and their caravans that pass through between Rochefort and Prateria.
