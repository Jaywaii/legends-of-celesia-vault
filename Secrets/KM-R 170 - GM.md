# Notes

- Save innkeeper and maid: 250 XP
- Defeat 4 CR 1 bandits: 1,000 XP
- Total XP to date: 7,750 XP (1,937 XP per PC)

- The Toursoline town guard has a mole, officer Gautier Bonnot (male halfling). He's under the pay of the Lecastor Exports to provide information. It is through him that Sibilla Lecastor knows of the PCs plan. She passes the information along to the bandit groups and to Lupo. Lupo decides to have the bandit groups (White Spiders, Bloodsong Cartel, Westwood Riders, Redhill Crew) take care of these interlopers. She wants them captured as she wants to know what their involvement is with Bronzebuckle. The bandits want payback, so as payment, they get to raid the town. During the raid, they are to capture at least one of the PCs. If not possible, then kill them. They should also kill Bronzebuckle, as this would be a great opportunity to eliminate a loose end without Lupo's direct involvement.
- Gautier will allow the bandit groups into town during the night. They will set fires as distraction, and then begin ambushing the town guard, then raid the town's stores and rich homes. Meanwhile, the elite members of the bandits go directly to Bronzebuckle to kill her.
- At some point in battle, Captain Elysant Verany gets killed in front of the PCs by the bandits. When the PCs encounter Bronzebuckle (female dwarf fighter 4), she will be in the middle of battle with the bandit, down to half hp.
