# Notes

- Officers Shuvi and Lawtomaton find themselves at the end of their shift at the Dryad Harp Inn, having drinks, though Lawtomaton is mainly there for moral support. Shuvi bemoans how their career has stalled, and in fact suffered, due to the Kundal case two months ago. Shuvi has been relegated to simple foot patrol among the Rughir orcs, while Lawtomaton is nothing more than a glorified paper pusher. They heard that Larry has been shunted to animal control, Accursia and Geoffrey were also reassigned to foot patrol, and Sophia resigned her commission. With the Viridian Games coming soon, the entirety of Prateria's police forces are extremely occupied with setting up security, yet the former Vigiles have been assigned with nothing but menial tasks.
- The pair leave the inn to retrace Shuvi's beat route in hopes of coming across something interesting. They find it when they hear a girlish scream cut short down an alley. They find three men at the end of the alley, who somehow manage to convince the two officers that the scream was from one of them due to his high-pitch voice due to stubbing his toe. As the two officers began to step away, they hear a trash bin get knocked over from around the bend behind the three men. Peeking around the corner, Lawtomaton saw that there is a fourth man, and he was grabbing a young woman, hand over her mouth. The lead man tries to explain that this was a working girl they had hired, and that this was all part of the roleplay game they hired her for, but the officers are skeptical. Shuvi and Lawtomaton insist on speaking with the girl, and when the lead thug attempts to offer the officers a little gold to part ways, Lawtomaton swings his flail up into his chin, knocking him out.
- The thugs draw their swords and try to attack. Lawtomaton instantly kills one of them in one swing of the flail against his temple. He then trips the other one and grapples him, locking his manacles on him. Shuvi rushed to intercept the last man, and threatens him into surrendering. He does not, but he becomes visibly afraid of his half-orc baring her fangs at him and seeing his companions go down so easily. His grip on the girl loosens, and Shuvi pulls her away. The thug tries to run past Shuvi, but the half-orc tackles him and succeeds at placing her manacles on him.  The officers recite their Bekka Rights. They try to stabilise the knocked out thug who is bleeding out, but their first aid skills cause more harm than good, and the thug breathes his last.
- Shuvi and Lawtomaton escort the girl, their two prisoners, and carrying the bodies of the slain thugs back to the guard station. The prisoners are escorted to the cells, the bodies to the morgue, and the girl is seated in the bullpen. She explains her name is Bianca, a teenaged girl working as a courtesan at the Jade Lounge, Promethea's brothel. Her friend and co-worker Eliza went missing three days ago, and Biance began asking around town about her whereabouts. She learned that the men that attacked her were seen in Eliza's company before she disappeared. But when she went to go find them, the men instead ambushed her in that alley. However, from what she heard them say, their intent was not sexually assault, but instead stating that they've caught another fish, and to take her to their boss.
- Sergeant Naldo, Shuvi and Lawtomaton's immediate superior comes stomping in to find out what the commotion is all about. After being briefed by the two officers, Naldo complains about how he will need to bring this up to the higher ups, like Captain Cortez. Meanwhile, he orders Shuvi and Lawtomaton to take down Bianca's statement, then escort her back home, then he returns to his office, complaining about all the new paperwork he will have to deal with. Bianca tells the two officers that she has no family, and neither did Eliza. Both of them made their home at the Jade Lounge. Shuvi and Lawtomaton escorted Bianca back to the Jade Lounge. They met the bouncer Vico, and the owner of the Lounge, a man named Mino. Mino is somewhat reluctant to involve the authorities, but Eliza is not the first to have departed the Jade Lounge. Two others in the past two weeks also left the Lounge: a young man named Daniello, and a woman named Stella. Mino initially thought its was just the usual departures from the tough life of a courtesan, but three in two weeks is starting to be suspicious. Bianca mentions that this all started since that Mister Fontana visited the Lounge.
- Mino reluctantly explains that Fontana was an older gentleman of wealth who insisted on buying the Jade Lounge, though Mino refused. Fontana was accompanied by a tall, tough-looking man who was claimed to be Fontana's assistant. Vico mentions that there were a few new people occasionally hanging around the Jade Lounge's neighbourhood. Mino also allowed the two officers to examine Daniello's and Stella's belonging. The only thing of note that they found was a half-written letter from Daniello to his younger brother staying at Promethea's orphanage. The two guards then returned to the station.
- Upon their return, they go down into the morgue to examine the bodies. However, they learn from the mortician that the two dog corpses they brought it had been disposed of. Very confused, Shuvi tries to make the mortician understand that they brought human bodies in by bringing her tea. Lawtomaton went to the cells, and discovers that the two thugs they brought in are no longer there. He goes to see Sgt. Naldo, who congratulates him and Shuvi for keeping the streets clean of drunkards. Lawtomaton tries to explain that they brought in kidnappers involved in a potential person-trafficking ring, but Naldo dismisses the claim as Lawtomaton and Shuvi's attempt to revive their dying careers. He advises Lawtomaton that the best way to get back on track is to grind away at their duties, and orders him back to dealing with paperwork. Lawtomaton and Shuvi quietly confer that something really weird is going on. While Lawtomaton makes a show of resuming paperwork duties, Shuvi heads back to the Jade Lounge to check in with them.
- Dealing with some of the recent paperwork, Lawtomaton discovers that the two prisoners they brought in were visited shortly thereafter by their legal counsel, a Miss Andrea Benelli. They were release after a fine was paid by a man named Barbo. Writing these names down on a scrap of paper and tucking it away onto himself, Lawtomaton departs the station to go join Shuvi. Meanwhile, Shuvi already arrived at the Jade Lounge, and thankfully confirmed that Mino's memories lined up with her own. Shuvi leaves the Jade Lounge, and meets up with Lawtomaton. The two decide to stake out the Jade Lounge in case Bianca's attackers reappear. They set themselves up in an alley near the Lounge.
- Unfortunately for Shuvi and Lawtomaton, they are neither discreet nor attentive in their stake out. In the dead of night, they are ambushed from behind by three attackers. One of them is one of the two thugs they arrested. The other was a woman armed the same as the first thug, and the last matches the description of Fontana's assistant. Shuvi and Lawtomaton get badly hurt, but manage to return several deadly blows. The fight is drawn out, as both sides bleed each other. In the end, Fontana's assistant and the woman are killed by Lawtomaton, while the first thug is knocked out by Shuvi. However, Lawtomaton pushed himself far beyond what his systems could handle, and one by one, his systems begin shutting down. Shuvi, barely able to keep her eyes open, attempts to stabilise her companion, but cannot make heads or tails of his sophisticated technology. The effort of failing causes her to pass out. Lawtomaton begins shutting down, and darkness consumed them both.
- Surprisingly, both Shuvi and Lawtomaton manage to reopen their eyes. They find themselves in beds, in what appears to be in a room of the Jade Lounge. Bianca is there. She explains that the people of the Jade Lounge heard the fight, and they arrived to find the two officers dying. They used their limited healing resources to prevent the two officers from dying, and brought them inside to recuperate. They also brought in the bodies and the lone survivor of their attackers, locking them in the basement. Bianca asked if they should alert the guard, but Lawtomaton suspects that the Sergeant and others in the Promethean Guard are compromised. Shuvi says there are some they can be sure to be untouched by whatever is compromising the guard...

## Vigiles

- **Lawtomaton:** Re-assigned doing paperwork thanks to being a machine.
- **Larry:** Re-assigned to animal control.
- **Accursia**, **Geoffrey:** Re-assigned to guard duty/foot patrol.
- **Shuvi:** Re-assigned to foot patrol among the Rughir orcs.
- **Sophia:** Resigned her commission.

- Several of the former Vigiles meet up after their shift at the Dryad Harp Inn for drinks. It has been almost 2 months since the Kundal case that cost them their prestigious positions. They have the chance to commiserate about their careers. They've been re-assigned to some very menial tasks (see above). With the Viridian Games coming soon, the entirety of Prateria's police forces are extremely occupied with setting up security, yet the former Vigiles weren't given any duties of value.

- The Coil is seeking to establish themselves in Prateria. This means setting up a base, a means of income, and begin destabilizing the current law and order. Using Montari connections, the Coil plans on becoming the primary drug dealer and brothel runner of the new nation. They plan on discretely selling drugs that are both highly addictive and damaging, as well as taking over or eliminating Promethea's only brothel, the Jade Lounge.
- The PCs hear a cry for help in an alley. They see a young woman being grabbed by a group of 5 men. The men claim that it's all just a misunderstanding, that the girl is a working girl they've paid for, and that this is all just roleplay as part of the game. One of the men has grabbed the girl from behind, and has his hand clamped over her mouth. If the PCs insist, the lead rogue tells the PCs that they're sorry for the disturbance, and would be happy to compensate for wasting the PCs' time, as helowers his hand down to a pouch hanging from his belt. He gives it a little tap, and you can hear the jingling of coins. If the PCs refuse, the thugs get frustrated and attack the PCs.
- The girl's name is Bianca, and she actually is a working girl for the Jade Lounge, but she was not hired by these men. She was on her off time, and that she was looking into the disappearance of a friend of hers by the name of Eliza who also worked at the Jade Lounge. Eliza left the lounge on an errand three days ago, and hasn't come back. Both Bianca and Eliza have no family, and live at the Lounge.
- The Lounge is run by an effette man by the name of Mino, a slim man wearing tight clothing, decorated in elegant makeup. He cares for his girls and boys, but it isn't uncommon for them to abruptly leave; the life of a prostitute is not an easy one. However, Bianca insists that Eliza wasn't like that, and Mino admits even he thought Eliza was satisfied working here. In fact, she was the third to have left his employ in the past two weeks. He didn't report anything to the authorities because he thought that it was nothing, but also that it would be best if the Jade Lounge remained beneath the notice of said authorities. Bianca says out loud that this all started with that Fontana business, but Mino tries to shush her about talking about their business.
- Mino explains that he was approached by a party wanting to buy the Jade Lounge, a **Mister Fontana (NE male [[Middle Age Human Grammaton 4|middle-age human grammaton 4]])**. He was an older man, with grey muttonchops wearing a black bob hat and a gold tip cane. Fontana said he was a merchant specializing in the entertainment industry. He was quite insistent on acquiring the Jade Lounge, but Mino had no interest, and had to be quite firm in his refusal. While Fontana remained civil and polite, Mino remembers being quite intimidated by his assistant, an imposing, bald human man with with a red goatee.
- The surviving thugs in custody don't provide any information, because as soon as they were brought in, their legal counsel shortly arrived before any interrogation begins, a miss **Andrea Benelli (LE female [[Human Psion Mindbender 2|human psion (mindbender) 2]])**. The thugs left the guards' custody after a hefty payment was made for their release, by a man listed as **Barbo (NE male [[Human Stalker 2|human stalker 2]])** in the records. If asked why thugs who attacked Prateria's police were let go, the clerks and officers respond by saying that no one was taken into custody for such a thing. These men were simpyl brought in for public drunkenness. In actuality, Benelli and her team hypnotized the clerks and officers into thinking that.
- Sgt. Naldo tells the PCs on good work on bringing in the drunks, keeping public order and all that. But he dismisses any notion about kidnapping, saying that he knows that the PCs want to redeem their reputations, but that they should keep their heads down and just keep working their assigned tasks.
- If the PCs continue to investigate, they can track down the thugs with a DC 15 Gather Information check. Getting a list of locations that the thugs were seen at by bystanders, the PCs can narrow the thugs' base to one of the recently built neighbourhoods. Fontana and his recruited thugs moved in here after disposing its original occupants before they were able to move in. Fontana, Barbo, and Benelli are the only real Coil agents; the thugs are recruits with no ties to either the Coil or Montari except for the fact that they all hail from various towns of villages of the Valdargent region. There are 6 thugs(NE [[Human Rogue Dual 1|human rogue 1]]), 3 men and 3 women,  in total working for Fontana.
