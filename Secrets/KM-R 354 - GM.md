# Notes

## The Shattered Observatory

- The Voidgazer uses Veiled Moon maneuvers to teleport self and enemies.
- Every round, a 10-ft. radius blackhole appears in a random location. Creatures that fail the save get sucked in and appear in a different location (1d8 for direction, 1d6 for elevation, 4d4 x 5 ft. for distance). Also take 10d12 dmg as gravity crushes creatures. Reflex DC for half damage and avoid being sucked in.
- There is only air and atmosphere within 10 ft. of each platform. Outside of that range exposes creatures to vacuum of space. They begin suffocating and boiling of moisture inside eyes, mouth, and lungs.
