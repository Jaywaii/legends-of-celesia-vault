# Notes

- During the occupation of Greentusk, Cortez gathers as much information as possible. Although it seems what few written documents the trolls maintained have been burned to ashes, he is able to gather some bits of information.
	- From scraps of maps with markings, it seems Klorgfang was planning a thrust into the eastern side of Prateria, cutting off Promethea's eastern roads. Obviously, other events pushed that idea to the wayside.
	- Some remaining scraps of accounting tracking informs Jupus that Hrondgren was heavily funded far beyond Klorgfang's means, with a massive sum of starting capital at the founding of his kingdom ten years ago, and more recently at the start of the war. The money trail seems to end here, though. The accountants do discover that the hoard collected from Greentusk has a significant mix of centuries-old and modern lurins, the gold currency of Lurindor.
- In more mundane news, the coming weeks and months will see an increase in marriages, especially among the Ironwatch, as many of its troops formed attachments to the former Klorgfang slaves, who flocked to them in the days after Prateria's victory.
- Plague in Orn Sini due to thousands of bodies. Rishoi and Sky takes care of it, clearing out the bodies, and healing the sick.
- Prateria exchanges land with Bluecrest sakkadi, who go on to create the nation of Bluefens. Prateria imposes a condition that they do not practice slavery, which the sakkadi agree to.
- Prateria grants autonomy to Hazel Court in north Draketooth Forest. Prateria claims formal ownership of the land, but form an agreement with the Hazel Court regarding their autonomy. Prateria also agrees to remove all structures in the area previously constructed by Klorgfang. The agreement forms a deep bond between Prateria and the Hazel Court.
- Evergreen Company spends time to refine their skills. During this time, the Heart of the Forest in their possession warps woodwork around them, causing them to grow branches and roots and blossom flowers. Promethea's local population of fey are drawn to the Heart, spending time close to the Company. They also become full of energy, and gradually start acting more and more feral. Many citizens send complaints to the Company about dryad roughhousing locals, playing magical pranks, and causing local wildlife to run wild.
