# Notes

- Madeline Dambrose:
	- Investigation of Dambrose's office reveals furniture having been recently moved (dust patterns), and documents not sorted correctly and in a haphazard manner. The documents regarding the Gardefort case are missing.
	- Dambrose has actually gone into hiding. In the previous week, she felt that she was being followed and watched. After Berendikoff's "suicide", Gunhild's reassignment, and Pewdersnit's death, Dambrose became paranoid and made preparations to hide.
	- The Gold Scale Syndicate, an underground crime syndicate formed by the Coil to further disrupt Rochefort stability in cooperation with Lurindor, is assisting Lupo, and ransacked Dambrose's office after she disappeared from their snooping. This crew is led by a Gold Scale lieutenant, Vic Garmel (NE male human psionic 4), who is an agent of the Coil (codename "Snaptrap"). The rest of the crew are unaware of their ties to the Coil.
	- Gold Scales had a sentry hiding keeping watch on Dambrose's office. Ulgan notices someone looking at them through a window from the 2nd floor of the house from across the street. The top floor is a rented out living space; bottom floor is a rented out storage space, owned by a landlord (Marge Feron) who is just a regular civilian.
