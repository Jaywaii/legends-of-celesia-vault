- Sky leaves the group for a while. He speaks with Évard Châteaufoy and Erikos Pentras and explains what had happened to his Emporium. He asks for their aid to take on the Coil agents, stating that they will likely be a threat to them all. The two other wizards agree to help. They turn themselves *invisible* and make themselves *fly*. They arrive at the Lurindor embassy, hovering in the air, waiting for the Coil to attack.
- The rest of the Company leaves the burnt out Emporium and heads to the Embassy. Some of the stealthier members of the group hang back a block away from the others while those ones make a noticeable entrance into the embassy. They wanted to see if they could find anyone observing them, but if there were, the observers were well hidden.
- As Elvia Sovrani had already informed the embassy's staff of the Company's arrival and purpose, the Praterian leaders are escorted to the room right next door to Elvia's bedchamber. Lumina and Argus opt to remain outside the embassy. Argus would patrol the embassy grounds, while Lumina flies up and lands on a nearby rooftop in line of sight of Elvia's room. She has to go buy a cold-weather outfit to hand the cold weather.
- Many hours pass by as the team spends the whole afternoon cooped up in the room. They talk, play games, but otherwise patiently wait. Arina, thanks to her ability to see through invisibility, notices Sky's trio of mages flying outside. The sun sets, and night descends. The outside team members are able to see Rocco Leonti walk up to the embassy and enter it. Sky and his fellow wizards immediately begin enchanting themselves with enhancing and protective spells. While doing so, Sky notices a group of shadowy figures quickly and quietly climb over the embassy's walls and slip into the shadows of the yard.
- Minutes later, a servant quietly informs the inside team that Rocco has entered Elvia's bedchambers. Sky receives the mental alarm that he had previously set up, indicating that deathblade poison has entered the room, but the wizard ignores it, waiting for the opportunity for all of the Coil agents to appear. Shortly before Rocco's arrival, Osla has been continuously been casting *clairaudience/clairvoyance* on Elvia in her room. She witnesses Rocco's arrival, the aggressive flirting between the two, and things start heating up fast. However, just as the diplomat and the athlete are beginning to become intimate, a pair of swords appear from shadows in Rocco's hands, and the slashes both at Elvia, one across her throat and the other across her belly. Osla shouts to the others that Elvia has been attacked, and the team bursts into action.
- Arina is first out of the door in a burst of speed. She crashes the door into Elvia's bedchamber. She sees Elvia clutching her throat and stomach, reeling back from "Rocco", blood running down her nightgown and splattering on the carpeted floor. She also sees another three Coil agents had burst into the room through the balcony door. "Rocco"'s true appearance is revealed as his disguise dissipates into wisps of shadows. "Just as expected!" yells one of the bow-wielding assassins upon seeing Arina. "Forget the plan, just kill them all!" He punctuated his orders by firing an arrow into Elvia's back, and the diplomat falls to the floor, blood splattering everywhere. The two other archers fire at Arina. It appears that Arina dodged both arrows, but in reality, that Arina was just an afterimage left behind as the Consul had just flash-stepped into the room in the open closet.
- Elvia is on the brink of death, but Rishoi was right behind Arina. The malephelim rushed to Elvia's downed form, and immediately infused her with healing power, saving her life. Jupus makes it to the door, and fires arrows at the archers, providing cover for Rishoi. Davona charges in after, and swings her sword at the fake Rocco. However, the Coil agent slips into the Astral Plane for just a moment, and Davona's sword passes through his form harmlessly. Osla sends her pig Zazen rushing into the room. The porcine familiar bumps her snout to the healing Elvia, and through her special connection, Osla is able to translocate the diplomat to the doorway.
- On the outside, Argus had only looked away for a second, so he is surprised to see shadowy figures already on the balcony outside of Elvia's bedchambers. He hears shouting and the sounds of combat, and he immediately charges. He swings his enormous earth breaker upwards, and smashes the balcony from underneath. The structure shudders, cracks, then crashes apart, bringing down two of the assassins. Due to their skills, the two manage to tumble and roll away from the debris safely. Meanwhile, broken stone collapses on top of Argus. As the dust settles, he stands unharmed by the debris, protected by his psionic armor. The pair of assassins draw their swords, and dive in to flank the Praterian Tribune.
- Osla leaves the other room, and both she and Rishoi pick up Elvia to take her to safety. Inside the bedchamber, the archers attempted to cloak the room in magical darkness, though Arina succeeds in countering one of them. From the shadows, the archers are able to inflict a few deadly shots at the Evergreen Company, though most missed. Lumina lives up to her name, as she enchants her clothing with light, and flies in, breaking the darkness.
- Sky descends from above, and hovers over the debris of the fallen balcony, at the level of the bedchamber. Willingly breaking his *invisibility*, he inflicts overclocked arcane potency'd *phantasmal killers* on a pair of Coil archers. One manages to resist, but the other dies screaming in horror. With inhuman speed, Sky follows up on the survivor by evoking an overclocked empowered *bone shatter*. The archer's screams are overshadowed by the snapping and popping of his bones, which then explode and rip out his flesh. He collapses in a dead heap of shards of bone and torn flesh. Above, Évard Châteaufoy looks on with amusement. He gestures at Erikos to stay back. "This will be over quickly. Besides, he looks like he needs to gethis out of him."
- Davona bissects the assassin that attacked Elvia across the waist. She then charges at the last archer, also cleaving her in two in one stroke, from right hip up through left shoulder.
- With the way clear, Arina drops down from the balcony entrance, and lands behind one of the assassins flanking Argus, just in time to see the Tribune cave in one of his attackers' chest in. The man is bleeding from deep cuts, but remains steadfast. Arina stabs the remaining assassin the back, and Argus finishes her off by smashing her skull in.
- It is then the Lurindor guards finally arrive on scene, though their assistance is not needed. In less than twenty seconds, the entire Coil assassin team is slain by the power and skill of the Evergreen Company, and the Sovrabi grand diplomat fully healed and safe..
