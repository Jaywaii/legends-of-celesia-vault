- After relieving Old Gnash of her curse and being congrtulated by the Northern Erlking, the Company rested. The next day, after performing the Ritual of the Oldwood, they continued their journey to the Heart of the Forest. However, Ayaki suddenly noticed that Tobias was no longer with them. Asako, using a spell to gain lore about the Arborfane, explained that this Heart and the area around it is closely tied to the Plane of Shadows, and mortals often fall or into it. Unable to come up with a solution to retrieve their companion and having spent so much time already, the Company reluctantly pressed on, determined to seek out Tobias when they are done.
- The Company arrived at the glade at thw center of the Heart, where a great tree stood. Within its branches, Sky made out the outline of the great zmey Drazekmir resting. However, the Company's approach was noisy, and the zmey immediately woke and leapt to the air, roaring at the intruders.
- Drazekmir enguled half the party with flames. Ayaki badly loss the use of an arm. Sky and Asako casted spells to buff the party, but Drazekmir swooped down twice to bite a chunk out of Argus and Davona.
- Meanwhile, the shaman caelephilim Osla and her pig companion Zazen, were lost in the Old Arborfane. While she meditated on this problem, she heard the zmey's roars and the sounds of battle. She ran towards it, and came upon the Evergreen Company battling Drazekmir.
- Davona failed to grapple the dragon in the air, and Arina failed to hit it when the teleported up to it. Argus, still in giant form thanks to Rialla, rushed at the zmey, and it seemed that he would have hit it. However, all he struck was its afterimage, as the zmey reappeared further away behind him. But Argus was supernaturally sped up by Sky's magic. The giant spun around on the spot, and charged at Drazekmir, bashing his mauls into him multiple times.
- Drazekmir retaliated. He bit several chunks of flesh off of Argus, but his fourth bite swallowed nothing but psionic ectoplasm from his armor. It sickened the beast, who regurgitated on Argus.
- Osla rushed to the Company's side, and began to look after the injured Ayaki. However, the battle ended as quickly as it began. Argus slammed his earthbreakers into the nauseated zemy. The first blow knocked him down out of the air. Argus then juggled the huge beast with every subsequent blow, knocking the dragon back up and side to side, before delivering one final blow downward. The dragon smashed into the earth, exploding into a mass of guts and scales.
- Arina welcomed Osla to the group as she and Tabrir proceeded to heal everyone's wounds. Sky directed Asako in collecting bones and blood from the slain zmey. Sky offered Tabrir a chance to do so as well, but Tabrir was not skilled enough to collect the beast's hide. The company proceeded to plant Elyssria's seeds around the Heart of the Forest tree. Once planted, the seeds sprouted roots and vines to form a ring around the tree. Exotic flowers blossom, and in a flash of light, the power of both the ring and the Heart synchronized as one.
- The Company, along with Osla were able to leave the Old Arborfane unmolested. After three days of travel, they emerged from the forest. Waiting for them at the edge was a dazed Tobias, who only came out of his stupor when the Company addressed him. Together, they all returned to Promethea.

- *In order to fulfill Davona's favor to Elyssria, members of the Evergreen Company ventured once more into the magical forest of Old Arborfane. Within, they were confronted  by the Northern Erlking, who bestowed upon them a task before they could continue on to the Northern Heart of the Forest. The Evergreen Company fulfilled this task by calming down the enraged primordial razor boar known as Old Gnash.
