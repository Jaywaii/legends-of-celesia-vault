# Notes

## Creation of Pyrouranos

PCs protect a fortified site where the creation ritual takes place from different types of undead trying to stop it.

If a caster is killed, a PC can take their place and spend their turn to make a DC 50 Arcane or Religion check to help the ritual. A PC can cast for each additional slain caster, but the DC increases by 50 for each one.

After the ritual is complete, the magic washes over the PCs to transport them to the next dreamscape.

**Round 1:** Zombies.

**Round 2:** Cyclops with 2 zombies.

**Round 3:** Banshees with another wave of cyclops and zombies. Banshees attack the casters directly.

## Pyrouranos's Purpose

The PCs will have to fight through the horde to help the centaur champion reach the edge of Vorakios' barrier to this seat of power. In there way are a series of challenges.

**Challenge 1:** Help allies.

Focus on protecting champion: Even challenge encounter, elf and dwarf unit lost, +2 difficulty in final encounter.

Save elf mages: harder time to defend champion, gain buff in final encounter.

Save dwarf warriors: harder time to defend champion, gain heavy defensive units to protect during final encounter.

**Challenge 2:**

### The Zombie Horde

- each token has 200 hp, DV 20, no DR.
- does automatic damage (16d12 per adjacent zombie horde token)
- when damaged, attempt grapple +40 (+10 per adjacent zombie horde token)
- when grappled, +50 to maintain grapple (+15 per adjacent zombie horde token) to inflict 16d12 x zombie dmg + move 1 square towards the mist
- if damage passes DR, inflict 4d4 Str or Dex dmg per adjacent zombie horde token (Fort save vs DC 40 for half (+5 per adjacent horde token)
- zombie horde begins climbing up over each other for form rising wave of zombies against flying PCs
