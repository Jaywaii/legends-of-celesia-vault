# Notes

## Vorakios

Vorakios is now wroth. The failure of his cultists in Anatolkerat has infuriated him, and he decides that they are better off under his control. He directs his fog to consume Anatolkerat as well, and also to seep into Prateria through its south. Having divined the location of the last fragment, he also directs a tendril towards Viriqia.

The deathmist travels at 60 ft. in the air, taking 0.5 hours per hex. Starting from Dancingwind, it travels 25 hexes to claim Ankerata. Ankerata will be taken at 6am of Moon day, Asmolan 24th. Vorakios then gains thousands of cyclopean zombies, along with tens of thousands of slaves. Thessamene is punished by becoming an intelligent zombie mage, but one without the freedom to ignore her master's commands.

The Viriqia tendril travels at 60 ft. in the air, taking 0.5 hours per hex. It takes 8 hexes to reach Viriqia city, claiming the city at 10 pm. However, the Cord fragment, combined with St. Radimus' power, prevents the fog's mind control. However, the inhabitants have to fight against the horde of zombies that roam the streets. The priests are able to blast sections of the fog with channel energy in 30 ft. radius burst, clearing the fog for an hour.

Vorakios also unleashes more banshees against Arbor's Rest (2 companies). Traveling at 120 ft. in the air at 0.25 hours per hex, they arrive at Arbor's Rest at 8 pm.

A 4th wave of zombies emerges in the Gap at 6 pm. They will reach Arbor's Rest on Waterday 25th at 6 pm.
