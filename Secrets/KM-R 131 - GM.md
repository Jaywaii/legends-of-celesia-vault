# Notes

- During Vandarkaï, Prateria is forced to deal with several disasters.
	- In Orn Sini, there are reports of growing dissent among the Sootscale kobolds. Agitators are promoting the idea that the humans and halflings of Prateria merely seek to exploit the kobolds, and that the kobold people will prosper as part of Klorgfang. Arina and King Sootscale quickly organize a series of public meetings with the kobold people to reassure them that as they are now part of Prateria, they will benefit and share of the prosperity and security their nation provides, and they will have a far stronger voice in the nation than they would in the troll-dominated Klorgfang. These meetings go a long away of countering the dissenting mood, but there remains a lot more work to completely bring the kobolds back on Prateria's side.
	- There is an attempt by what appears to be an organized surged of bandits aiming to prey on the newly acquired southern lands of Prateria. However, they achieve no success as Prateria's Explorator, Bekka, was expertly prepared for such a scenario. Thanks to her experience as a bandit herself, Bekka had given out instructions to Praterian's people on what to do when bandits appear. At the first sign of banditry, the people quickly regroup with their valuables to safe havens, and then posses of volunteers are formed to chase off the bandits.
	- More farms on the western border of Prateria are again attacked. Much like previous attacks in the area, no lives were lost, though build were set aflame, livestock set loose, and equipment destroyed. Thankfully, Bekka continues to be on top of things. She quickly organizes volunteers to put out the fires and rebuild damaged infrastructure, and stored surplus quickly made its way into the people who needs it. Upon close inspection, Bekka discovers more traces of fairy dust at the scenes.
	- Unfortunately, it was only a matter of time before the toll of these sneak attacks became too much. It is soon discovered that a concerted attack on Prateria's food supplies has been executed. Food stores have been spoiled or poisoned. Wagons and carts making up the nation's supply lines have been waylaid and carted off to locations unknown. Surprisingly, no live were lost; guards and drivers found themselves waking up away from their posts or wagons with no memory of how they got there. Fortunately, Prateria had enough surplus and backup supply lines to mitigate the damage. Nonetheless, the nation will need to import a significant amount of supplies from their neighbours to ensure that its people remain well fed.
	- Rumours spread among the populace of Promethea that the fey were behind so many of the attacks. This idea spread like wildfire among the Prometheans, and anger among them was stoked to take action. The dryads of Promethea's Memorial Park start being harrassed, and their trees vandalized. The group known as 'Friends of the Fey', led by Sister Félicité also start being harassed as well, with some encounters nearly coming to blows. Unfortunately, the Evergreen Company cannot get a handle on the discontent. Their attempts to calm their followers are surprisingly ignored as anger continues to boil over. The troubles in Promethea will not be resolved any time soon.
- Despite the covert attacks at home, or perhaps due to them, the Evergreen Company plan to make some offensive actions. Argus plans to retake the Silent Bastion on his own, while Rishoi, Sky, and Davona plan on exploring and occupying the Klorgfang adjacent to their western border. Jupus remains in Promethea, working with his Steamflogger Industries to build bombards and cannons for the Ironwatch. Meanwhile, Arina travels to the Temple of the Elk to speak with Father Joram regarding the fey. Joram informs Arina that in fact they had not interacted with the fairies of the Gnarlwood for over a month now. He suggests that Arina seek out Tyg-Twitter-Twig and Percilash at their nest. Arina thanks him, and also inquires if the war has reached them yet. Thankfully, Joram confirms that there has been no sign of trolls or goblins in the Gnarlwood. Yet.
- Arina quickly travels to the Fairy Nest. The area is oddly silent except for some faint weeping from above. Arina believes it to be Percilash, and calls out to the faerie dragon. Percilash is reticent, and Arina needs to gently coax him out of his nest. The faerie dragon timidly drifted down to the forest floor before Arina, and tearfully recounts the current situation.
	- *"We were visiting out friends in the Hazel Court. We regularly have tea parties with Lady Hazalea at her home in Draketooth Forest. Everything was fun, until the shadows overhead. Green drakes, dozens of them, led by one who was dragon-born." Percilash whimpers at the memory. "They burned our friends with acid. I can still hear the screams..." The faerie dragon shivers and curls up tight in a ball. "Their leader, known as Silverich, King of the Forest, declared all of Draketooth his now. Hazalea and her court will continue to live, as long as they serve him. Tyggy got caught too. He left his daughter, one called Deilenwyn, to guard them both, forcing the lady's consort, the satyr Falchos, to lead raids against you. Deilenwyn... she... she pulled off one of Tyg's legs!" Percilash starts bawling. "She promised to pull off another one each time we try to defy or fail her. Falchos has been trying not to hurt your people, but we can't win that way! We've failed four times now, and Tyg... Tyg..." Percilash can no longer continue as he sobs heavily.*
- Arina gently scoops up the little dragon, and embraces him in a tender hug. The Consul promises Percilash that they will rescue Tyg and free Hazalea from the forest drakes. With Percilash securely snugged in Arina's bag, Arina dashes at high speeds towards Castrum Aeris, where Sky teleported with Davona and Rishoi. Once there, she explains the situation to the rest of the Company. Sky teleports to Castrum Ferro to retrieve Argus, and once they return, the whole group sets of west, into the Draketooth, with Percilash leading them the way to the Hazel Court.

- Klorgfang enacts several espionage edicts against Prateria with a +30 to DC. All are great successes.
	- Bandit Activity (Continuous; +25 DC): Bandits are preying upon those who travel through your nation. The Marshal must attempt a Stability check. If you succeed, your nation's defenses stop the bandits before they cause any harm. If you fail, the bandits reduce your nation's Treasury by 1d6 BP (each time you roll a 6, add the result to the total and roll again), and the event continues in the next Event Phase.
	- Inquisition (settlement, continuous; +25 DC, vs Haag Rughir and Orn Sini): Zealots mobilize public opinion against a particular race, religion, nation, behavior, or nation leader. The Ruler or High Priest must attempt a Loyalty check. If you fail, the zealots run rampant; Infamy and Law increase by 1 and Lore, Loyalty, Productivity, and Stability decrease by 2. If you succeed, the zealots are somewhat suppressed; Lore, Loyalty, Productivity, and Stability decrease by 1. Two successful checks in a row end the event (if a check ends the event, no penalties from it occur that turn).
	- Slavers (Settlement, Continuous; +20 DC, vs Promethea): Criminals begin kidnapping citizens and selling them into slavery. The Spymaster must attempt a Loyalty check, and the Warden must attempt a Stability check, each with a penalty equal to the number of Brothels, Tenements, Waterfronts, and lots with squatters in the settlement. If both checks succeeds, the slavers are caught before your nation takes any penalties from the event. If you fail at one of the checks, Loyalty, Stability, and Unrest decrease by 1, but the event is not continuous. If you fail at both checks, Loyalty, Stability, and Unrest decrease by 2, and the event is continuous.
	- Smugglers (Continuous; +25 DC, vs Promethea): Unscrupulous merchants are subverting legitimate businesses. The Treasurer must attempt a Loyalty check, and the Warden must attempt a Stability check, each with a penalty equal to the number of Piers, Waterfronts, and trade routes in the nation. If both checks succeed, the smugglers are stopped before your nation takes any penalties from the event. If you fail at one of the checks, Corruption increases by 1d2 in each settlement, Crime increases by 1 for the nation, Productivity for the nation decreases by 1d3, Treasury decreases by 1d3 BP, and the event is not continuous. If you fail at both of the checks, Corruption increases by 1d4, Crime for the nation increases by 1, Productivity for the nation decreases by 1d6, Treasury decreases by 1d6 BP, and the event is continuous.

### Fairy Hostage

The dryad Hazalea is held hostage by Hrondgren's trolls. A beloved figure in the fey world, her captivity forces the fairies of the region to serve Hrondgren. Hazalea is the young queen of the Hazel Court, a very small and peaceful court of fairies located in the Draketooth Forest. Although she claims only the area of the forest as her sphere of influence, she has good relations with other benign fey in the Evergreen Plains. Silverich has effectively toppled her as the ruling power of the forest, and his daughter Deilenwyn ([[forest drake stalker 7]]) holds Hazalea and her tree as hostage with a retinue of 3 forest drakes ([[forest drake stalker 5]]), 4 trolls ([[troll primal disciple 4]]), and 2 hags ([[green hag witch 4]]).
- Along with Hazalea, the grig Tyg-Twitter-Twig is also being held hostage. Tyg is a close friend of Hazalea and was captured at the same time when Deilenwyn arrived.
- Hazalea's consort, the satyr Falchos, has been forced to lead a band of Hazel Court fairies into acts of sabotage against Prateria. However, he tries to avoid killing anyone, using nonlethal attacks, and focusing only on destroying property. Percilash is forced to aid Falchos with their fairy friends from the Gnarlwood to prevent any harm to Tyg.
- When not on raids, Percilash can be found at his and Tyg's nest in the Gnarlwood. He can be heard audibly weeping before even being seen. However, he refuses to meet with anyone, partly out of shame, partly out of fear if Deilenwyn discovers he met with the party. He requires a Diplomacy DC 20 check to be lured out. Through tears and sniffles, he recounts how the fairies ended up helping the trolls.
