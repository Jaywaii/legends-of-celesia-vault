- The Evergreen Company arranges for Bekka to go on a training trip to improve her skills, funded by the nation's coffers. Cortez embarks on another mission to gain the current locations of all of Klorgfang's armies.
- Rialla approaches Arina. She asks if the Consul had heard back from Tabrir recently. Ever since he had gone on his goodwill tour of Lurindor to gather more support for Prateria, he had always managed to send an update on his progress every 3 or 4 days. However, it has now been over a week since his last message. His last update mentions that he made some small progress in Ferrocitta, and that he would start traveling eastward, visiting towns along the way, and either visiting Valdargent or Mariporta next.
- Prateria directs its three new legions to occupy their new forts. Legio I is deployed to Castrum Aurum west of Silvermine. Legio II is deployed to Castrum Argentum in Silvermine itself. Legio III is deployed to Castrum Aes to the southeast.
- In brighter news, Prateria's farmers experiences an unexpected bountiful windfall this month, providing a surplus of goods for the nation.
- Speaking of troops, the reinforcements from Rochefort arrive in Promethea. The 2nd Rochefort Fusilier Regiment, under the command of Colonel Arnoullet Delisle. He relays Adéline Châteaufoy's regret that she could not send more, but many of Rochefort's units are otherwise occupied with prior duties and contracts. Additionally, the 2nd Fusiliers orders are to defend Prateria territory only; they are not sanctioned to launch offensive operations. However, they are 2,000 soldiers strong, and most have already undertaken at least one campaign. They can split their forces, either into 2 reinforced battalions, one in the east and the other in the west, or four normal battalions.
- Riding on a golden shield of light, Arina leads her Cohors Consul Magica, along with Rishoi as support, on another raid against Klorgfang forest drake scout teams. She succeeds in ambushing two of them and taking four prisoners and their supplies. It is a harrowing trek from the first to the second forest drake team as Consul Magica flew into a thunderstorm along the way. Arina and one of the Praterian wizards are struck by lightning, but are immediately recovered thanks to Rishoi's intervention.
- While Arina is making her raids, Davona makes a sortie with all of Legio II, striking out from Silvermine. She leads her army south, planning to attack one of Klorgfang's army stationed at a fort south of the Greenbelt River. Jupus is with her so that they may build a bridge to cross the river south.
