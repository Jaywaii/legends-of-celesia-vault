# Notes

- Menakades' Horde (64,000 XP): 6,400 XP each unit (Legio II).

- With Legio II occupying Arzhulig's fort on Klorgfang territory, Klorgfang had merged two armies together under the command of the minotaur warlord Menakedes to retake it. Knowing this, Sky *teleports* to Argus, who is with Legio I stationed at Castrum Aurum. He explains the situation to the Praterian Tribune, and Sky *teleports* them both back to Legio II. Once back to the fort, Sky proceeds to cast *guards and wards* along the walls of the fort, enveloping them with magical fog and webs, an aura of confusion, and toxiv vapours.
- The Klorgfang army finally arrives at 10 am the next morning, during heavy rain. The minotaur commander has his army surround the fort. Not eager to grind his troops against Legio II's fortified position, Menakedes attempts to whittle down the defenders on the fort's ramparts with ranged attacks. Legio II respond in in kind. As the exchange of fire intensifies, the minotaur commander grows concerned as his units are faring worse, while Legio II's units defend themselves well from their attacks, and are actually recovering from any casualties being inflicted. The situation grows more dire for the Klorgfang forces as the enlarged Argus flies out to decimate one of the harpy units hovering above the battlefield. Menakedes make the grim decision to retreat from the battle, and orders his troops to begin withdrawing.
- Not content to let such a large enemy force get away, Arina, Argus, and Sky chase after them; Arina flying on her sunlight shield, Argus empowering his astral juggernaut with flight, and Sky with magical flight. They repeatedly swoop down on the fleeing foes, cutting down large number of enemy soldiers each time. The three Praterian leaders hold the enemy units in place long enough for Davona and Legio II to catch up. Together, they succeed in defeating the fleeing Klorgfang army, taking hundreds of prisoners, and killing hundreds more.
