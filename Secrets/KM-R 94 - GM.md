# Notes

- After writing up her altered report, Accurisa is asked by Sgt. Naldo where Lawtomaton and Kosven are, they didn't show up for roll call. Naldo tells Cinqemani that if she sees them, send them to his office immediately. He has some questions for them regarding last night's arrests and several irregulaties that came up, a lot of officers, including himself have conflicting memories of what happened. He then orders Cinqemani to go down to the orphanage and follow up on a disturbance there. He leaves, rubbing his head, muttering about what was in his coffee last night.
- At the orphanage, the matron is speaking with a pair of guards. One of them approaches Accursia and tells her the a pair of suspicious individuals were attempting to speak to one of the orphans before the matron scared them off. The matron tells Accursia of the couple: a stiff man and burly woman wanted to speak to an orphan named Antonio, claiming to be friends of his brother Daniello, and then claimed to want to adopt him. They tried to sneak their way in, but fled the orphanage when the matron called for help. Accursia manages to convince the matron to allow her to speak with Antonio. The officer asks the boy when did he last hear from his brother. Antonio tells her that he received a letter from him a little over a week ago which he shows her. Daniello says in his letter that he will be away for a few weeks on a big job that should be able to provide for the both of them for a long while, and for Antonio to not worry. Curious, Accurisa asks if Antonio has any other letters from his brother, which he does. Accurisa examines the handwriting between the letters, and the last one, while she is certain that it matches Daniello's handwriting from his older letters, has some odd strokes to it. Antonio also tells her that he last saw his brother in person over two weeks ago. Accursia thanks the boy and assures the matron that neither of them needs to worry about that suspicious couple, and the officer departs the orphanage for the Jade Lounge. While waiting for Shuvi and Lawtomaton to return, she notices some pamphlets regarding the teachings of the Crimson Madonna.
- Lawtomaton and Shuvi return to the Jade Lounge and reunite with Accursia. They explain to her about someone spying on them and chasing after them to one of the new housing blocks of the city. Accursia tells them that Naldo wanted to see them, and seemed confused about the event of last night. Now not sure is Naldo was part of a conspiracy or was drugged, the officers returned to the station. Upon entering the station, their former companion Larry arrives, looking quite haggard, covered in feathers and dirt. He has just come back from recapturing Mrs. Matelizi's cat after a long and labourous chase. In a more private corner of the building, they explain to the gnome about the case they are working on, and Larry happily agrees to help.
- Shuvi and Lawtomaton go to see Sgt. Naldo. They explain their absence by telling him the truth, that they were attacked, and explained all the events leading up to their uncovering of a potential person trafficking ring in Promethea. Naldo is astounded that their city guard has so blatantly come under attack, and that it explains much of what happened the precious night. He congratulates the two on surviving and discovering this plot, and that he will report to his superiors and get some serious firepower to deal with this. He tells the two to make a report and bring it back to him when done. Shuvi and Lawtomaton went back to the bullpen to start writing the report. Still not fully trusting their sergeant, they ask Larry to send his eagle Demetrius to follow Naldo. Larry does so, adding an instruction for Demetrius to drop a pebble on Naldo. Meanwhile, Accursia and Shuvi look through police records for any mention of Andrea Benelli or Fontana, but find none except for last night's signature.
- A short while later, Lawtomaton returns to Naldo's office with his report. Naldo is there, looking irritated and a small bruise on his forehead, but he is not alone. With him are three other men: Acteon, Dante, and Toro, top lieutenants of Davona's Guard. Captain Cortez is taking the case extremely seriously, and has tapped his top people to take care of it. Lawtomaton hands in his report, along with the emerald lens, and he once again explains things to the lieutenants. Acteon thanks Lawtomaton, and decides to inspect the bodies and the prisoner at the Jade Lounge. Dante, as a regular, easily leads them through the back-alleys to the brothel. They collect the bodies and the prisoner, using their past criminal experiences to disguise them in public, and return to the station, where more higher-ups have arrived, such as the head of the morgue, Bjorn, and the twin mages Samara and Lisa. They take custody of the prisoner and bodies, thank Lawtomaton for his and Shuvi's efforts, and take over the case.
- Several days later, Shuvi and Lawtomaton would learn that the guard, under Acteon's command, raided a house, and successfully recovered the three courtesans of the Jade Lounge. However, all other details became classified. Regardless, Bianca met with Shuvi and Lawtomaton again, thanking them for saving her friends. The two officers would also each receive a commendation, marking the first step to repairing their reputations.
- After the raid, Davona receives a report of the incident from Cortez and Acteon:
	- A summary of the events involving Shuvi and Lawtomaton, from when they rescued Bianca to all the way to when they submitted their report to Naldo and Acteon.
	- Following an investigation on the neighbourhood where the suspects might be hiding out, the Guard discovered that one home was originally destined to a couple, Roberto and Isabella Alberti. However, there was an apparent last minute transaction, transferring the house's ownership to another couple, Salvadore and Agnola de Fiedricis. Closer investigation revealed that Agnola's signature shares the same details as Andrea Benelli's signature.
	- After confirming the suspects were indeed based out of that specific address, Acteon led the Guard in a raid. There were a total of six suspects in the house, and all six were killed during the raid. Several guards were injured, including Acteon, but no deaths occurred. Among the dead was an older man who matched the description of Mister Fontana, and a woman matching the description of Andrea Benelli. The three kidnapped courtesans were found safe and sound, albeit drugged up and mentally addled. Apparently the drugs used are used in combination with psionic powers to brainwash people.
	- An investigation of the aftermath revealed that both 'Fontana' and 'Benelli' drank *elixirs of amnesia* before Fontana shot Benelli in the back of the head before turning his pistol on himself.
	- Bjorn reports that the other four suspects all had different street gang tattoos:
		- A rose with the words "13th St.".
		- A skull with crown on top with the words "King's Row".
		- A red bat.
		- A cyan mining pick.
	- The lone prisoner was interrogated and admitted to being a former member of the King's Row Skulls street gang of Valdargent. He was hired by Fontana, along with other bangers from other street gangs. They were just hired muscle, and Fontana, Benelli, and Barbo never confided their plans with them, only promising them a place in a new criminal organization that would take place here in Promethea.
	- Bjorn reports the only people without tattoos was the bald man (suspect 'Barbo'), 'Fontana', and 'Benelli'. However, he did discover suspicious patches of skin on their bodies that suggested that a tattoo was made there. Fontana had his on the inside of his left wrist, Benelli had hers on the back of her neck, and Barbo had his on his right shoulder.
	- Fontana and Benelli also had similar emerald lens hidden in their possessions. In a moment of inspiration, Davona takes one of them and looks through it over the suspicious patch of skin. The emerald lens revealed a green tattoo of a snake coiled around the world, devouring its own tail. All three of the main suspects had the same tattoo. Later on, Sky would determine that the ink of the tattoo and the emerald lens were treated with a unique alchemical mixture that would make the tattoo invisible to sight, but visible through the lens. Unfortunately, Sky sacrificed one patch of skin trying to determine the alchemical makeup of the tattoo ink, but with no success. Replicating these special emerald lens remains impossible for the time being. Davona orders that Cortez and Acteon each take possession of the remaining emeralds.
	- Several items of note were found in the suspects' house. They had collected several uniforms that match those that belong to maids and servants of Promethea's hotels. There are also a list of names, some are of Ispirani origin, others are Valderêvan. Those that are Valderêvan have notes by their names indicating keys to specific facilities. The Ispirani names have notes regarding shift times and locations. There is also one letter and one unfinished report found:
		- Letter: *Deadshot, it is imperative you arrange openings to place our people and acquire the access they require. We do not know where exactly the target will stay, so cast a wide net. We will need to move fast to complete Operation Naga, so make sure to ready the evidence to point at the the target dupes. Additional resources will be sent upon confirmation of your initial goals. It is vital we succeed; our partner is anxious to expand into this new nation, as are we. I.C.E. –M*
		- Unfinished report: *M, we have started taking action to take over the local brothel to use as our front. We have also gathered the necessary intel for Operation Naga. However, some of the henchmen we hired ran afoul of some of the local constabulary. Mindbender had to pose as their cousel, and successfully hypnotized the authorities into covering up the incident. Rest assured that the interlopers will be dealt with, but the secondary plan should be ready just in case...*
- Meanwhile, Arina, Argus, Attila, Bekka, and Sky spend eight days exploring the plains to the west. Bekka's mood noticeably improves, and Attila speaks with Arina about the future of his people. Bekka tells Arina that she'd be happy to join them on future explorations, and hopefully help deal with more exciting encounters.
- Osla does not need to travel far from Promethea. The stars lead her south and east. She soon arrives at a small wooden hut bordering the edge of the Old Arborfane. A pumpkin head scarecrow stands planted in the front yard that is bordered by a wooden fence. A bell hangs at the gate. There is smoke rising from the hut's chimney, and light shines within the windows. The Old Beldame (Elga Verniex) angrily calls out to Osla. Once Osla identifies herself, a light, feminine voice is heard from within the hut. "Osla? Osla, is that truly you?" she calls out. Osla recognizes the voice belonging to her old friend Lumina. "It's all right, mistress Elga, Osla is a dear friend. May she come in, please?" The Old Beldame relents, and tells Osla she may come in. Once entered, Osla see her friend and her bee companion. Both Zazen and the bee also greet each other. Lumina looks health, but haggard. She explains that she was drawn to the Evergreen Plains by nightmares of a wailing forest. She approached the southern Old Arborfane, where the forest tried to attack her. After communing with the land and speaking with the forest inhabitants willing and able to speak to her, Lumina learned that the forest was wounded years ago. Normally that would explain the forest's rage, but until some months ago, the wounded forest remained dormant, as if in some kind of thrall. However, a recent influx of power into the Old Arborfane had reawakened this part of the forest, breaking free of whatever was suppressing its rage, and now it lashes out in pain. Lumina came north as it became too dangerous for her to remain. She had heard of the Green Witch who knew much about the Old Arborfane, and came to consult with her. In fact, the Old Beldame had just recounted to her about some foolhardy adventurers trespassing into the forest, meddling with powers beyond their comprehension. Osla invites Lumina to join her and return to Promethea so that they can investigate this problem with the Evergreen Company.
- After a few days, Jupus' workers return from Rochefort, bringing back supplies needed for their business. While there, they looked in on the last known address of the Gardefort family. They discovered that the ramshackle house at that address had been abandoned and foreclose. No one had lived there for the past year, and a few discreet questions to the local neighbours only reveal that the destitute gnomish family that lived there left no forwarding address. Jupus thanks his employees and ponders his next move.
- When the Praterian Council reconvenes in Firhen, they discover one of their members missing. Prateria's Frumentarius is not present. A note is left on the table. It is a letter from Tobias. It reads: *"Evergreen Company, I am honored that you have counted myself among your company, and for offering me a role in leading your young nation. Sadly, my path must lead away from your own, and so I must part ways here. I apologize for the short notice, but it cannot be helped. I bid you good fortune in you and Prateria's future. Signed, Tobias Ignatious Malakay."*
- Suspicious of the timing, Arina and Sky go over the letter in detail. The letter was indeed written by Tobias, but under mental compulsion. A DC 25 Linguistic's check reveals that this was not written naturally. The slight deviations in strokes indicate that he was trying to resist writing. While they deal with this new problem, Arina assigns Louis to hold the position of interim spymaster of the nation.
- Amidst these problems, the council decides for Prateria to expand its territory to the Mud Bowl and construct an aqueduct from it. With the tedium now dealt with, the Evergreen Company now focus on the problems at hand, including the imminent Viridian Games.

The true owners (Roberto and Isabella Alberti) never made it from Rochefort to their new home; Fontana and his crew waylaid them on their way and killed them, and forged the ownership documents. Eliza, Daniello, and Stella are here, sedated from a steady dosing of drugs while [[Benelli]] works on brainwashing them into servants of the Coil.

Barbo has his tattoo on the back of his right shoulder. Benelli has her tattoo on the back of her neck.
