# Notes

- 6 butcher ants (CR 1): 200 XP each
- Total XP: 560 XP each (Thassi, Bonzar, Oskar, Circe, Zaccaria)

## Coteverte

- 12 giant butcher ant workers erupt from the ground around the sheep. Coteverte guards will fight half of them in 3 groups of 4 guards vs 2 ants. This leaves 6 ants against the PCs.
- PCs can follow the tunnels back to the colony's location. Tunnels are only 5 ft. wide, room enough for workers and soldiers to go back and forth side-by-side. Whole sheep can be carried, but cattle get chopped up to fit through.
- PCs need to sneak around and pick their fights carefully.

## Argent Rose

- Asmolan 110 6A
- Starting at level 9

*Previously, Argent Rose Occult Investigation Team 13 opened the passage through the 7th focal tower of Ing-gol'zha, the Gate of Shorok'logoa. However, after harrowing battle with its guardian, an outer spawn, and the dreadful unknown centre of the necropolis, you resealed the passage, and made your way back to the surface with the lore you have gained.*

*You updated the Rose Scholar Thonra 'Mindshield' of Hushglow Expedition of what new dangers you uncovered, allowing her to adjust both the inner and outer defences around the ruined tower accordingly. You returned to the Rochefort lodge, and updated Chapter-Sage Joliette Beauchamp, and proceeded to do some training. Weeks later, Sage Beauchamp informed you that your team has been requested back to the Grand Lodge in Tzongshulin (in the Dalong Empire on the continent of Zhoulong). You are to meet with your direct superior, the director of the Occult Investigation Department.*

*From Rochefort, you take the teleport circle to the continental lodge of Asteropa in the city of Mysthaven. From there, you hop through another teleport circle to Tzongshulin. You meet with your director, Sage Xiaodwa, in her office. She is a middle aged female skriven who stands about four and a half feet tall, covered in a smooth layer of soft light grey fur. She wears the proper academic blue-and-silver robe of the Argent Rose, and a pair of masterfully crafted spectacles upon her nose, but you can see underneath more comfortable explorer clothing, with belts and bandoliers brimming with pouches and vials. The tip of the handle of a holstered revolver peaks out from under the clothing.*

*"I have been staying up to date on your progress," she tells you. "The Society's crafters are still hard at work sculpting those dreadful figurines you requested. As you can imagine, acquiring the needed adamantine is no easy task, nor is the sculpting. The healers have their hands full with treating the mental breaks the sculptors are experiencing, and we have few replacements available willing to dare this dangerous feat. As to the rest of the items you require for the rituals, I'm afraid you'll have to tackle those tasks yourselves. Our other 12 OI teams are either occupied with their own investigations or recovering. However, any other this department or the Society as a whole is ready to support you in any way we can."*

*The libraries of all of the Argent Rose Society are available, accessible via teleport circles from the Grand Lodge. The Grand Lodge is connected to each Continental Lodge, and each Continental Lodge is connected to five regional lodges (other lodges will require other means of travel). With access to the vast repositories of lore and expert scholars, you are able to uncover how you can retrieve the needed ritual components to seal the focal towers.*
