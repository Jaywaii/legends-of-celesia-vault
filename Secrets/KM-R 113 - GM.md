## Assassination!

Montari Entreprises have allied with the Coil to gain dominance over Lurindor. Enzio Montari himself is a recent, fast rising member of the secret society. While Enzio becomes the ruler of Lurindor, the Coil gains a wealthy nation to fund their operations. In order to do so, one of the goals they need to accomplish is to topple the Sovrani Syndicate. They plan on using [[Elvia Sovrani]]'s presence in Prateria to simultaneously eliminate her and set the Syndicate against the Teredoro and Prateria. As Rochefort is already an unofficial enemy of the Syndicate, adding the new colony of Prateria and the powerful Teredoro Consortium as foes will weakend their position further.

The DC to detect the assassins' actions is the nation's Control DC+30. Prateria's security forces can succeed in interrupting if the check reaches Stability+40, but the assassins get away. The assassins' get caught by security if the check reaches Stability+50, in which they commit suicide, leaving no one the PCs can interrogate, but remains of physical evidence of the plot. If the check reaches Stability+60, then they succeed in capturing 1d4 Coil agents alive. However, all of the Coil agents modified their memory to believe they are working with Teredoro Consortium against Sovrani Syndicate.

A written set of orders from "-M" can be found on the assassins if they are caught. They are somwhat vauge, only stating that the goal is to assassinate Elvia Sovrani on behalf of their "friend" and to implicate Prateria and their friend's rival as the backers behind the plot. Again, they end with "I.C.E.".

## Tournament Events

During the Viridian Games, the Evergreen Company has the chance to increase their renown. For every 10 points of Renown, Prateria gains +1 Fame with all factions in Lurindor. However, their Fame is reduced by 1 if their Renown is 0 or less, and reduced by another 1 for every 10 below 0, as the nation becomes ridiculed for how they mismanaged their games.
- One way to gain renown is to sway each of the dignitaries. Minor sway = 1 Renown, Moderate sway = 2, and Major sway = 3.

## Tournament

- The Viridian Games represents using the Festival Edict 8 times. It costs Prateria 8d6 BP to prepare Promethea to host the Games and the invited guests, and the prizes of the Games. At the last day of the Games, Prateria's Ruler rolls for the Games' success. Unlike a normal festival edicts, the effects of the civic festival last for an entire year instead of 1 month. If the Games are a resounding success, Promethea gains either the benefits of an Outstanding Success (01-50), Visiting Celebrity (51-95), or both (96-00) nation events for each of the next 12 months. If the festival is a semi-failure, the 50% chance that disgruntled citizens issue a Building Demand occurs for each of the next 6 months. If the festival is a complete failure, Prateria loses -5 Fame instead of -1, gains 1d4 Unrest and Squatters (01-50), Vandals (51-95), or both (96-00) nation events for each of the next 6 months. For every 10 points of their Renown score at the end of the Games, the Ruler gets a +1 bonus to each edict roll to determine success.If their Renown score is below 0, they instead get a -1 penalty, increasing by 1 for every 10 points below 0.
