# Notes

## Trials of the Old Arborfane

### Shadow Fey Tea Party

The party finds themselves in a glade surrounded by tangible shadowy smoke. At its center is a grand dining table with a number of peculiar and unique individuals seated at it. Upon the table are sets of finely crafted tableware, made of porcelain and gilded in gold. The smell of colourful and fanciful cakes and exotic tea reach your noses. The sounds of gentle, crystal twinkling fill the air. Although the partiers exclaim surprise at the PCs appearance, Marquis Duskleaf invites them to partake, and there are the exact number of open seats and set dishes equal to the number of PCs.
- The PCs need to score enough impression points in order to be able to leave the tea party. The longer they stay, the more time passes in the real world.
- **Marquis Duskleaf**—A shadow fey noble of the Court of Shadows. The host of the tea party. Tall, slender, long ears, long black hair slicked back, pearl white skin. Dressed in a dark suit that twinkled with minute stars, and wears a monocle. Creative, Psychopathic.
- **Dame Moongallow**—An alseid knight of the Court of Shadows. Elegant, Ignorant.
- **Kynthalig Lickshadow**—A goblin hunter, Master of the Hunt of the Court of Shadows. Understanding, Undisciplined.
- **Lady Sorreminx of the Iridescent Court**—A haughty sei'anthyr elf sorceress who looks down on those with poor manners. Incorruptible, Insensitive.
- **Gwathion Everglade**—A shadow fey courtier with a temper. Caring, Rebellious.
- **Dame Yngress the Red of the Iridescent Court**—A young sei'anthyr elf knight eager to make a name for herself with her sword skills. Reliable, Blunt.
- **Vale**—A shadow fey dancer. He has a streak of white in his dark, long hair. Focused, Overemotional.
- **Ladra**—A shadow fey illusionist, Royal Gloomier of the Court of Shadows. Charitable, Cruel.
- **The tea**—The tea is sweet, accompanied with a euphoric effect. Those that fail their save are overwhelmed with a sense of overconfidence, noticeable by their cheeks gainining blueish tint (*save* Fort DC 20, *addiction* minor, +2 on all checks using Charisma, -2 on all checks using Wisdom). Those who fail are met with smirks from the guests, and may be target of mocking remarks. However, those that take sips and resist the effects impress the party-goers.
- **The cakes**—The cakes are sweet and fruity, and impart a rush of energy. Those that fail their save become overactive and hyper, with their eyes gaining bright specks upon them (*save* Fort DC 20, *addiction* minor, +2 on all checks using Dexterity, -2 on all checks using Wisdom). Same reaction from partygoers if the PC fails or succeeds in resisting the effects.
- Duskleaf stands up, his tea cup in hand. *"Now that all those meant to be here have arrived, I thank you all for attending this little party. I do apoligize for the short notice, the whim suddenly struck me to revel with friends, both old and new. As such, I am even more appreciative of your presence."* At the end, he raises his cup. As he does so, all of the guests also raise their cups. *"To an enjoyable party!"* The gathering toast with their tea cups, and take a gulp of their tea. Each PC who takes a gulp must make a DC 20 Fort save.
- **Proper Introductions**—Diplomacy, Knowledge (local; Old Arborfane), Lore (Shadow Fey). After remarking on the PCs appearance, he looks at the expectantly.
- **Dialogues**
	- *Once Duskleaf retakes his seat, Dame Moongallow leans forward. "Marquis, it is always a pleasure to take part of your parties." The goblin beside her pipes up. "Indeed! I am further impressed by your preparedness." "I concur with the Huntmaster, Marquis," Lady Sorreminx adds. "How did you know that we would have such... unconventional company?" she says this glancing at you with a raised eyebrow, taking a sip of her tea. Meanwhile, Dame Yngress appears to be looking intensely at each of you, up and down and examining your demeanour and equipment, while taking a bite of cake. Vale, on the other hand, is in the midst of whispering in Ladra's ear, to which the Gloomier responds with a subdued smile.*
- **Diffusing Argument**—Diplomacy, Knowledge (local; Viridian Marches). An argument breaks out between Gwathion and Lady Sorreminx about proper etiquette among fairy courts. Gwathion is particularly abrasively dismissive over a bunch of customs, while Sorreminx is becoming more and more denigrating in Gwation's opinion and his character. Their argument is getting heated and threatens to disrupt the party.
- **Stirring Performance**—Perform. The mood of the party starts slowing down, becoming more dull. Sense Motive to notice Duskleaf's growing displeasure at the way the party is going. If none of the PCs take action, he comments *"Hmm, ennui is a terrible gift to offer a party."* and gives a look at the PCs. Many of the guests start turning their gazes to them as well.
	- *The shadow fey Vale lights up in delight to your performance. He steps out from the crowd, and begins an enrapturing dance in perfect sync with your own performance. His movements are so delicate and sophisticated that this is an art from beyond the world, and there is no doubt why this fey is a courtier at a court. Vale then draws in Gwathion Everglade into his dance, and the onlookers are mesmerized by the dance couple's sensual movements. After several minutes of breathtaking dancing, Vale is not done, deftly switching from Gwathion to Ladra, with a gentle applause from the crowd. The two shadow fey embark on another round of heart-stirring movements of passion. The performance is mesmerizing, and all too soon, Vale disentangles from Ladra, to then turn towards you.* The PC must succeed at either an Acrobatics or Perform (dance) check to not embarass themselves. The PC making the Performance can aid the dancer by changing the performance to match their pace, granting them an aid bonus. Success = esteem, fail = mockery.
	- *The partygoers cheerful applause Vale, who makes a deep sweeping bow, not even sweating or out of breath despite his passionate performance.*
	- If Performance success: *The applause is also directed towards you. "A wonderful gift you ahve provide us, my dear," the Marquis exclaims. "Rarely have I heard such exotic art! A welcome performance," Dame Moongallow adds. "Aye, it has been so long since I was last moved by such wonder," Vale adds, bowing towards you, taking your land to land a kiss upon your fingertips, then giving you a wink while straightening up. The other partygoers raise their cups to salute you as well.*
- **Deflecting Jealousy**—Diplomacy, Perform (act). After the start of the party, the PCs notice that Vale acting quite flirtatiously with Ladra Moongallow, and Kynthalig. However, as the party goes on, Vale begins to interact more and more with one of the PCs (1d6 random). While it was fine that he was flirting with fairy couriters, this attraction to a single outsider irritates the party goers (Sense Motive). Rebuffing Vale without letting him down gently also causes him to disrupt the party, able to deftly lay the blame on the PC.

- **Win Duel**—Dame Yngress approaches Argus, stating *"Tribune DeMonteblanco, I have heard of your skill at arms, and I wish to match them against my own. I formally challenge you to a courtly duel!"*
	- Have to deal 3 hits without killing or incapacitating the other duelist. Ending a duel in one blow is considered to be bad form.
