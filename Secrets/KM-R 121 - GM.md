# Notes

- Battle of Silvermine (Azrhulig's Horde ACR 74):
	- Consul Dracons: +3,842 XP
	- Rialla: +3,143 XP
- Argus gains 1 FATE point for not re-rolling for a new boon due to his aspect.
- Prateria +2 Fame (Battle of Silvermine)

- It is at the beginning of the second hour of the Battle of Silvermine that the tide turned in favour of Prateria. Rishoi rushes over to Consul Draconis. With astounding speed, she is able to stabilize and heal the wounded and near dead kobolds. Just in time to defend against the orcs assaulting the south gate. Consul Draconis are able to hold the orcs back without sustaining anymore casualties. in fact, under Sky's somewhat lackadaisical leadership, the kobolds' return of fire proves to be devastating for the orcs, many of who are cut down.
- South of the village, Davona flashes through the air like a bolt of silvery light amongst the harpies, untouched by any of their arrows and those from the gnoll skirmishers from the ground. Flashing from harpy to harpy, winged bodies rain from the above in explosions of blood and feathers.
- On the northwestern side, the wolf-riding goblins attempt to fire a volley of arrows at Rialla, but a misfire spooks one of their wolves, which then sends the pack into a frenzy, requiring the goblins to spend much time calming them down and reorganizing their lines.
- On the northeastern side, Argus holds the breach. Feet firmly planted in the ground, his massive psionic armor making him large enough to fill the breach. He smashes his two earthbreakers into any mounted hobgoblin trying to get pass him. Most of the hobgoblins never make it through the breach, and the few survivors are repelled.
- Atop of the southern rampart, Louis aims his pistol at the hill giants. Using his cyclopean insight, the halfling's shots hit true. Jupus adds his own firepower against the giants. An enraged Arzhulig orders his giants to return fire, and Louis is bashed around as large stone smash his position. However, the exchange of fire ends in the favour of the gnome and halfling. Most of the hill giants are cut down by the combined firepower of Louis and Jupus.
- Seeing his units being decimated by so few opponents, Arzhulig calls for a retreat. However, Prateria takes advantage of the retreat to inflict as many casualties as possible. Due to their vulnerable numbers on a strategic level, they have to take this chance to inflict as many casualties on Klorgfang as possible. The units engaged in melee with the Praterian side are completely destroyed in their attempt to flee, while others are cut down by ranged fire. Only one unit of goblin wolf-riders, hobgoblin calvary, and the gnoll skirmishers manage to flee the battlefield. Yet, this does not save them. Argus and Davona are able to move far faster than their mounts. Davona repeatedly sweep down on the fleeing soldiers, cutting down two or three at a time. Argus shifts his armor's power so that he can burrow into the ground, then suddenly burst out of the earth beneath them, smashing their numbers to pieces. After two hours of chase, the remaining Klorgfang units are eliminated, and Argus and Davona return victorious with prisoners in tow. In total, the Praterian forces take around 180 prisoners, and manage to loot much needed supplies from the enemy.
- Among the enemy leaders, the following were captured:
	- Arzhulig the hill giant
	- Narog the orc barbarian
	- Scakne the kobold sorcerer
	- Draazix the hobgoblin cavalier
	- Frelki the goblin hunter
	- Glork the goblin hunter
- These leaders escaped:
	- Ghurkiz the gnoll hunter
	- Grilka the hobgoblin cavalier
- The only leader to escape the battle is Nephyth the harpy bard.
- Consul Draconis, Mikmek in particular, are given a raucous cheer from the kobolds after they emerge from their shelter. They and King Sootscale are extremely grateful for the Evergreen Company's protection, vindicating their decision to join Prateria. After a night of celebration, the Company leave Consul Draconis in Silvermine with Mikmek in charge while they return to Promethea with the prisoners. There, they are joined by an exhausted Consul Magica led by Arina with their harpy and forest drake prisoners. The people of Promethea are jubilant at the string of victories by their leaders, and Arina gives them a grand speech that invigorates their morale beyond expectations.
- Going into the month of Asmolan, there are no further attacks from Klorgfang, and neither do they Evergreen Company make any further raids against the enemy. Worries are lessened as all three Praterian legions become active and forts are constructed. Additional lands are claimed, and additional forts are commissioned.
- Argus fears for the safety of the Old Beldame, whose home is near the Klorgfang path of expansion. He goes to her hut, evading forest drake patrols. After the usually cranky greeting, Argus tries to convince the old witch to leave, but she states that she is too old move and reluctant to leave her closeness to the Old Arborfane. The Old Beldame tell Argus that the best way to protect her is to ensure that the trolls do not make this far north. She also states that her old bones are feeling more and more pain, and warns Argus that the Old Arborfane is in pain, a far more older pain that was made clear after the Evergreen Company healed and protected the Northern and Central Hearts of the Forest, and that something is wrong with the Southern Heart. Argus recounts their dangerous attempt to land on the southern shore of Lake Iridescence bordering the sourthern part of the Old Arborfane. This confirms the Old Beldame's suspicions, and urges Argus that his people must push south, and find a way to heal the Southern Heart. Argus shares what he learned from the Old Beldame upon his return to Promethea.
