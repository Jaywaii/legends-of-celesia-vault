- The Toursoline town guard greets the party at the entrance. They'll take the prisoners and bodies into custody, and ask the PCs to come iwth them to their guard station to take down their identities and statement. There, Captain Elysant Verany (female halfling) speaks with them. At the same time, they'll record their reason for visiting. They'll waive the entrance fee. They ask the PCs to stay in town for at least one night as they question the prisoners. Tomorrow morning, the PCs can return to the station, and if everything lines up with their story, they will receive 10 gp bounty per bandit, and claim tothe bandit's loot and gear. The first night at the local inn and meal will be paid for by the guard.
- In Toursoline, Gunhild has been assigned menial tasks involved paperwork regarding what little TRA involvement in the town. She is under constant observation by a local caravan company, Lecastor Exports, that makes its base in Toursoline. They've worked with Carmella Lupo, providing inside information of Rochefort to the TRA, and they're being bribed with favorable TRA treatment (fees and tariffs discounts) to report on all of Gunhild's actions.
- Gunhild has been preoccupied with the increased bandit activity in the area. It has been something to distract her from the humdrum of outskirt life and from the frustration of not working on the Gardefort case. However, the bandit activity falls under the local Toursoline town guard, and she has been ordered not to get involved in local affairs. However, her sense of justice drives her to want to do something about it.
- The increased bandit activity is a ploy by the TRA, with the backing of the anti-Rochefort Trade Houses. Gangs from neighbouring regions are being persuaded to move their criminal activities in Rochefort, or target Rochefort own businesses in other regions. The TRA would crack down on gang activitiy in other regions except Rochefort, and also spread rumours in the criminal underground that Rochefort has more lax security. The TRA would turn a blind eye towards gang movements towards Rochefort, while at the same time cracking down on any attempt to move to other regions. The main gangs in the area around Toursoline are: White Spiders, Bloodsong Cartel, Westwood Riders, Redhill Crew.
