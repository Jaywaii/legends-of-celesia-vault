# Notes

- Successfully scout the TRA branch office: 250 XP (62.5 XP each)

- In Rochefort Fireday Jeshakaï 1st, 111 6A, new freelancers Kraz, Ulgan, Vasiir, and Shortstuff form a party to take up a mysterious job request. It was one ignored by the more experienced higher ranking freelancers of the guild, both for its mysterious nature and low reward, and was the only left for them to take. The request only wanted them to meet at a specific house where they will receive further instructions. Arriving at a rented out house at 652 Rue De La Cerise where they are met by an older halfling man by the name of Monsieur Vincoq. Vincoq explains the job to the freelancers.
- Vincoq explains that he is not the originator of the job. He too was recruited by someone who wanted to remain anonymous. He is being paid for this, and was given additional funds to make the request with the Freelancer Guild (paying extra for having the request listed as anonymous as well), and to give the freelancers who did sign up 20% of the reward (50 gp of a total 250 gp) up front upon showing up. The house has been rented out for the long term, so it can be used as a base of operations, and Vincoq will be residing here. When the freelancers have completed the mission, he has the means to contact their patron, and retrieve the rest of the reward in exchange for the results.
- The job itself is threefold:
	- First, the requestor wishes to retrieve information from one TRA agent named 'Gunhild Ronsbuckle' regarding the investigation into the Montplaisir Massacre and the prosecution of the Gardefort family business 'Gardefort Silverworks and Fine Gems'. The agent is not to be killed, but can be detained or coerced as needed to retrieve the information.
	- Second, the requestor believes that the patriarch of the Gardefort family, Berendikoff Gardefort, is being unjustly imprisonded, and desires his extraction.
	- Thirdly, the requestor desires that the rest of the Gardefort family is extracted from Rochefort as well.
- The freelancers plan to scout out the TRA branch office. Shortstuff goes out and buys a child's noble outfit and riding dog. The team will assume the persona of a child of a wealth family being escorted by his freelancer bodyguards. They had to spend a few hours getting the TRA office's address and directions, but finally manage to make there way there. They have to enter the high-class wealthy district of the city, and their unique appearance do draw a lot of looks, but as many wealth merchants also hire out-of-place mercenaries, the team are not fishes completely out of water in this place. They find the TRA branch office, it is a multistoried stone building surrounded by a stone and iron fence. During the day, the main gate is opened, watched over by a pair of guards, and the main doors to the lobby are open as well. Officers and citizens come and go without issue, though the guards keep a close eye on certain people. The team gets looks from them as well, but as they do not approach the entrance, they are dismissed just as quickly.
- Shortstuff pretends to be the child of a wealth family with Ulgan as his bodyguard. They spend some time at a tavern called The Golden Maple Pub, with Ulgan able to use his freelancer license to gain entry. Shortstuff bribes the headwaiter for information. The headwaiter does not know about any man with the TRA by the name of Gunhild Ronsbuckle, but does inform them about Gunhild Bronzebuckle, a dwarf woman with red hair. She was an officer with the TRA, but has not been seen for over a year now. Shortstuff also manages to sneak around to the tables where other TRA officers are having drinks or eating meals. He overhears conversations about how pathetic the Rochefort Guard is handling the increased gang activity, and that the TRA would be more than happy to extend their reach into the local politics.
- Kraz and Vasiir sneak around the office building. Vasiir is scene immediately by the regular patrols in the alley behind the building. The skriven decides to play up his race's reputation, claiming to be digging in the trash to retrieve some lost documents. He is mocked by the TRA guards, and they do stick around to keep an eye on him, forcing Vasiir to pretend he found what he was looking for, and then leave.
- Kraz is more successful in scouting the area unseen. He is able to gather details about the building's layout, patrol timing, and gets a good look at the courtyard in the back of the office building.
- During the evening, Kraz heads out and started looking around for dwarven women who match Gunhild Bronzebuckle's description. After a few hours, he learns of a dwarf woman with bright orange hair who works for the Rochefort Guard. As she gets off her shift, Kraz approaches her on the street. He addresses her, but fails to appear sincere and makes her wary. Kraz tries asking if she knows the name Gunhild Bronzebuckle, but he merely ends up making her more defensive. The woman actually grabs the hilt of her mace, asking why Kraz is asking her about this. Kraz, sensing that he isn't making a good impression, backs off and leaves.
- The next day, Kraz informs the rest of the team about the dwarf woman (after getting up early to eat as many eggs before anyone else, prepared by Vincoq). The freelancers decide to track down this dwarf woman.

## Gardefort Investigation

### Intro

*Rochefort, a metropolis of over 30 thousand inhabitants. The majority of the people are halflings, and the city's architecture reflects this, with buildings, streets, and other structures being designed more to the comfort of a people of a smaller stature, and with a more homely and rustic aesthetic. This is also reflected in the prominent Cathedral of Tierra, the dominant faith of the area, supported by temples of Kaï-den and Deianeira. However, a stark reminder of its overlordship from its taller neighbours is present in the sizable minority of humans living in Rochefort. Many descend from the land's former overseers, but most are now immigrants, or their descendents, seeking opportunity now that Rochefort and its people and lands are connected to the wider web of trade of the region, service by the new temples of Kimylia and Belthazar. Also living in the city are notable minorities of gnomes, dwarves, and elves, as well as the odd individual much like yourselves.*

*It is people like yourselves, peculiar and unique, that typically sign up with the local Freelancer Guild. A place and organization where, for a regular fee (to be paid on the 1st of every month, starting a 50 gp at leaf ranking, and double each rank higher), its members are granted a license to take on odd, and usually dangerous, jobs that official forces just don't have the time or resources to deal with immediately. As long as you don't bring harm upon the citizens of Rochefort nor egregiously violate its laws, these licensed freelancers are allowed a little leeway and liberty in how they get their jobs done in exchange for a worthy reward, something that most civilians would be heavily fined or even arrested for doing. The world just spews out too many monsters and threatening supernatural phenomena at a such chaotic rate for government funded organizations to handle all at once.*

*You yourselves are freshly inducted volunteers to the guild, with a leaf license. The four of you unfortunately missed out on all the more juicy job postings, snatched up by the more experienced and influential members of the guild, leaving one mysterious and cryptic request left that everyone else felt was not worth their time. Still, a job is a job with payment, so you four decided to form a party and take it up. The job had you meet up with an older halfling man with a bald head and a brushy grey mustache by the name of 'Monsieur Vincoq'. The location is a rented out home, 652 Rue De La Cerise. There, he explains the request in more detail, which he himself admits he was just paid to set up the job request with the guild, and give you the details as well as the advance (50 gp), all anonymously. The house can be used as a base, and he'll be waiting here for their completion, whereupon he'll contact the person who hired him for the remaining reward (200 gp).*

## Argent Rose

### The Society Gets Involved

The Argent Rose Society finally has resources to mobilize. They cannot let Hushglow Tower and the Necropolis of Ing'gol-zha remain vulnerable to dangerous parties that would unleash its horrors into the world. Asako and Team 13 are called to the Rochefort Lodge to meet with Chapter-Sage Joliette Beauchamp. In her office is an elf. Before anyone speaks, the elf places down a stone on the desk, speaks a word of power, and the stone glows. The room is envoloped in a bubble of faint blue light. "We can speak more freely without any magical observation now." Rose Knight-Captain Aleric Sunleaf, a male elf with notably long, red hair with a white streak running through it. He has a deep and jagged scar running down from his forehead, down through his right eye to near the edge of his jaw. Where his right eye would have been is now covered with a blue eyepatch with arcane symbols imprinted on it. He is dressed in a fully equipped wizard's adventuring gear, with numerous pouches, weapons, and arcane jewelry.

The captain congratulates Team 13 on their expedition so far. He explains that they discoveries are dangerous, and need securing from hostile parties like the Old Cults. However, much of their more powerful resources are currently occupied dealing with other threats or recovering from recent operations (Aleric flexes his left land, and you see how freshly scarred and torn it is.). Team 13 has been doing well so far despite some casualties, so the Society is fine with them taking the lead on this. However, they do intent to provide more support.

That's where Asako comes in. The Society wants to establish a more permanent base and fortification around Hushglow Tower, and they want to make sure Prateria will allow them to do so without any interference. This will also be an excellent opportunity for Asako to demonstrate leadership potential by how much work she decides to take on in establishin this base (funding, organizing, etc.). Joliette tells Asako the arrangement from before still persists: While Argent Rose activity is technically under the Rochefort Lodge's Jurisdiction, Joliette will let Asako oversee day-to-day operations herself. Team 13 will report to Asako, and in turn, Asako will support them. The more Asako takes on by herself, the more likely she is to advance within the Society.

"Well, I must be off. I need to chase down an old artifact in Pravica. Word has it a vampiric demon cult is hot on its trail as well. Keep up the good work, both you Team 13 and you Asako." Taking his stone, Aleric teleports away.
