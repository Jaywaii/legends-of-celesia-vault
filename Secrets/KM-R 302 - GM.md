# Notes

## Arbor's Rest Aftermath

Zombie Gredda Stonehelm was discovered in the piles of corpses still active, albeit with half of her body torn apart.

During the night, a hundred banshees and a hundred grave sprites attack the Praterian air forces if they are not inside the Pall.
