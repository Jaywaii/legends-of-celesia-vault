# Notes

## Vanishing of Pyrouranos

### 2nd Test

Sterlingastrax has 5 acts per turn.

- **Round 1:** Sterlingastrax flies up and breathes silvery gas all around on the centaur leaders. The leaders cough and gag, but when they recover, their eyes glaze over, and begin frothing at the mouth. They then turn towards the PCs, raising their weapons. Then Sterlingastrax roars, and the ground in a half-circle around Pyrouranos rips open. Any PC who enters a square adjacent or over the crevasse finds themself magically sucked down even if they are flying (only nat 20 Reflex save avoids being sucked).
- **Round 2:** Sterlingastrax looms over Pyrouranos on the ground. He targets one PC repeatedly. He wants to see how much the PCs will sacrifice to save their comrade.

- once satisfied, the bow magically appears in the dragons hands and he warns the tribes of their foolishness
- dragon transforms into a younger, female silver dragon who speaks to the players directly
- silver dragon shoots across the sky to the southern mountains, leaving a trail for the PCs to follow amidst a distinct pattern of mountain peaks

### After the Dream

- The PCs reawaken at the Thunderhoof refuge. Sounds of battle can be heard. Thunderhoof warcries and chanting of shamanistic magic fill the air. Iolanthe's mother Andrastreia greets them, looking grim. Iolanthe is nearby, in meditation as she upholds the Protection of the Ancients.
- Andrastreia tells the PCs that only a few hours have passed in their dream journey. In the meantime, hordes of undead and blighted fey  have poured out of the fog, and have been assaulting the refuge. The centaurs are able to hold them back thanks to the power of the Ancients.
- Upon hearing the description of the mountains, Andrastreia believes the PCs are describing the mountains claimed by the silver dragon Argentia, a great protector of the region, but one they had not seen in more than a decade, but this was not out of the ordinary for the elusive dragon. However, considering the danger of Vorakios at the moment, her absence is very odd. Argentia had always favored the Silvermane tribe as well, with legend stating that her ancestor once mated with centaurs to give birth to Andrastreia's tribe.
