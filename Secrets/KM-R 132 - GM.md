# Notes

- The Evergreen Company, led by Percilash, makes their approach to the seat of the Hazel Court. They fly upon shields of golden light manifested by Arina and Rishoi. In this way, they are able to perceive a number of forest drakes circling above their destination. At the same time, they make no use of stealth in their approach, as evidence of the forest drakes descending down into the trees.
- Both sides quickly make preparations for the battle. When they make sight of each other, it is at the glade at the heart of the Hazel court, with an enormous tree at its centre. Pressed against it is a beautiful, elf-like lady with bright, orange leaf-like hair, though worry and fear mar her delicate face. Towering over her is a powerful forest drake, one who has a wounded and whimpering grig clutched in one claw. They recognize the poor captive; it was Tyg-Twitter-Twig. Three hags of green skin surround her. Five other forest drakes prowl amongst the branches up in the trees around the glade. A row of six trolls form a line between their mistress and the approaching Evergreen Company. And one faun stands among them with a worried look in his face.
- Percilash identifies the forest drake gripping Tyg as Deilenwyn. Upon making eye contact, the daughter of Silverich roars out, *"Fools! Come forth and surrender, or watch this pathetic fairy die before you!"* But the Evergreen goes into action before any of the Klorgfang soldiers can react.
- Arina manifests golden shields around them and Percilash. Sky then *dimension doors* them, except for Percilash, right nest to Deilenwyn. Arina quickly orders Argus to strike, and strike he does. He smashes his enhanced earth break right through Deilenwyn's skull, obliterating it into pieces of smashed flesh and bones. All of the Klorgfang soldiers freeze as they cannot believe at the instant loss of their leader. The dead forest drake collapses to the forest's floor, and Tyg is released from her grasp. Hazalea lunges forward to catches the grig, and begins bestowing healing magic upon her.
- The hags attempt to hex Argus and Davona, but Argus still manages to smash one of them into the ground into a bloody pulp of green flesh before the other two fall back out of range. A forest drake flies up from the trees, rears its head, and spits out a ball of acid at the Company. However, the golden shields of light blocked the acid. In response, the other forest drakes fly up into the air, and throw javelins at the Company, breaking the shields on impact. With a single word, Arina transitions Davona in front of her to block an incoming javelin. The weapon bounces harmlessly off of Davona's fearsome armor. In response, Davona swings upward at the assailing drake, and with her enhanced reach, slices open its entire underbelly. However, with some of the shields broken, one forest drake spits a glob of acid at the Company. Arina reacts with a hastily manifested shield of light. The shield protects the party, but Arina's skin sizzles as she absorbs all the damage. Rishoi expands her divine power over the area to *consecrate* the area around them.
- Meanwhile on the ground, the trolls close in on the Company. The light shields break easily, but absorb a fair amount of damage from the trolls' attacks. Argus takes one out in a single blow. He follows up by breaking through their ranks to crush another of the green hags, breaking the witch's hex on him. He turns to Hazalea, and shouts at her to run. *"I cannot! I am tied to this tree!"* she shouts back in despair. *"What?! I see no ropes on you!"* Argus yells back. *"She means spiritually. She's a dryad."* Sky remarks, and Argus sighs sheepishly. Falchos takes advantage of the break in the line of trolls to manifest his *fairy veil*, and sneak through invisibly to his queen's side.
- Argus continues to smash his earth breakers at the oncoming trolls. The Company's teamwork renders them nearly invincible, as Davona helps Argus avoid a blow with a quick shout of warning. But even then, the trolls are not toothless. Literally, as one troll manages to bypass Argus' defences to badly chomp down onto his shoulder. The troll rips away a large chunk of flesh, and for the first time in a long time, Argus' unshakable will is broken as the troll snarls and openly chews his flesh before him. However, with a shout of encouragement, Arina is able to bring back his confidence, and in response, Argus slams his earth breaker across that troll's jaw.
- Davona charges up into the air, and in one swing, utterly guts the nearest drake. Before that one falls to the ground, the caelephilim quickly banks around to another drake. In a flash, Davona slices its head off. On the ground, Sky, shouts out, *"Everyone fine with a* fireball*?"* Arina and Argus quickly reply to him with *"Do it!"* while Rishoi readies to take care of any burns. Sky fires his *empowered fireball* above the battlefield, and the ensuing explosion of fire catches all of the trolls, along with Arina and Argus. Unlike the barely singed Arina and Argus, all of the trolls fall to the ground, smoldering. Sky follows up with a *bone shatter* upon the two remaining forest drakes, exploding them into a rain of torn flesh and broken bones.
- Argus kills another of the hags, and the remaining one becomes invisible. The Company hears an unseen sing-song chanting from a male voice, and then there is an explosion of *glitterdust* where the hag was. The glitter clings to the hag's form, forming a visible outline. Sky is not sure of the motive for such an attack, but he is glad to have fairies on their side once again. Davona takes advantage, and kills the hag in one blow.
- With all the threats neutralized, the fairies reveal themselves. Rishoi approaches the feebled Tyg, cradled in Hazalea's arms. The tiefling bestows healing energies upon the grig, and slowly, her missing legs begin regrowing. Meanwhile, the rest of the Company notices that the trolls are still regenerating, despite being burned, something that should not be happening. Arina suggests to use acid instead, and Sky conjures an *acid fog* upon the bodies. Oddly enough, the bodies continue to regenerate, albeit at a slower pace, and Sky can see that the acid is doing less damage than expected, as did the fire from before. After almost two minutes have passed, a moment of nervousness passes as Sky sees that the troll bodies cease regenerating, and finally die from the acid, seconds before the spell ends. Sky explains what happened with the trolls to Arina. The description reminds Arina of stories of barbarians able to resist acid and fire. Also, as part of researching their enemies, Arina knows that trolls with the correct training are able to remain enraged while unconscious, and that they can also train to keep their regeneration ability keep going even when afflicted with acid or fire, albeit at a slower rate. Arina explains to the others that these trolls can get so angry enough to power through their weaknesses, and even being unconscious! If all of Klorgfang's trolls are trained in such a manner, Arina surmises that they will need to find means to counter this training.
- Tyg-Twitter-Twig comes to after all of her wounds and legs have been fully healed. In a flash of movement, Percilash zips into the area, and crashes into his friend with a tight hug and tears. Hazalea addresses the Evergreen Company with sincere thanks. *"Dear Tyg-Twitter-Twig reassured us that you would come to our rescue. She never lost faith in you. On behalf of myself and all of the fey of the Hazel Court, I thank you all for freeing us. You have saved the Hazel Court this day, and proven that you are true friends of the fey. I promise you that this friendship between us will last beyond these trying times, and if you need our aid, you need but ask."*
- Arina thanks Hazalea for the offer of friendship on behalf of Prateria, and inquires about Silverich. Hazalea is able to inform them that Silverich used to claim the portion of the Draketooth Forest south of the Greenbelt River. However, the self-styled King of the Forest now claims the northern portion of the forest after the trolls grew to power. As to his current whereabouts, Hazalea overheard a messenger to Deilenwyn stating that her father now leads a significant portion of Klorgfang's troops. Arina asks if the Hazel Court can find out more about the whereabouts of their mutual enemies, though not to put themselves at dangerous risk. Hazalea is willing, but informs them that the current situation is not ideal. The Court is weakened, and they are now surrounded by Klorgfang's minions. Their fairies cannot move too freely, and a significant portion of them need to remain to defend the Court and their Queen. The Evergreen Company agrees that they will return to their original intent then, which is to explore and occupy enemy territory in the Draketooth Forest, and hopefully relieve the Hazel Court of the threat of the Klorgfang.

- Klorgfang enacts several espionage edicts against Prateria with a +30 to DC. All are great successes.
	- Bandit Activity (Continuous; +25 DC): Bandits are preying upon those who travel through your nation. The Marshal must attempt a Stability check. If you succeed, your nation's defenses stop the bandits before they cause any harm. If you fail, the bandits reduce your nation's Treasury by 1d6 BP (each time you roll a 6, add the result to the total and roll again), and the event continues in the next Event Phase.
	- Inquisition (settlement, continuous; +25 DC, vs Haag Rughir and Orn Sini): Zealots mobilize public opinion against a particular race, religion, nation, behavior, or nation leader. The Ruler or High Priest must attempt a Loyalty check. If you fail, the zealots run rampant; Infamy and Law increase by 1 and Lore, Loyalty, Productivity, and Stability decrease by 2. If you succeed, the zealots are somewhat suppressed; Lore, Loyalty, Productivity, and Stability decrease by 1. Two successful checks in a row end the event (if a check ends the event, no penalties from it occur that turn).
	- Slavers (Settlement, Continuous; +20 DC, vs Promethea): Criminals begin kidnapping citizens and selling them into slavery. The Spymaster must attempt a Loyalty check, and the Warden must attempt a Stability check, each with a penalty equal to the number of Brothels, Tenements, Waterfronts, and lots with squatters in the settlement. If both checks succeeds, the slavers are caught before your nation takes any penalties from the event. If you fail at one of the checks, Loyalty, Stability, and Unrest decrease by 1, but the event is not continuous. If you fail at both checks, Loyalty, Stability, and Unrest decrease by 2, and the event is continuous.
	- Smugglers (Continuous; +25 DC, vs Promethea): Unscrupulous merchants are subverting legitimate businesses. The Treasurer must attempt a Loyalty check, and the Warden must attempt a Stability check, each with a penalty equal to the number of Piers, Waterfronts, and trade routes in the nation. If both checks succeed, the smugglers are stopped before your nation takes any penalties from the event. If you fail at one of the checks, Corruption increases by 1d2 in each settlement, Crime increases by 1 for the nation, Productivity for the nation decreases by 1d3, Treasury decreases by 1d3 BP, and the event is not continuous. If you fail at both of the checks, Corruption increases by 1d4, Crime for the nation increases by 1, Productivity for the nation decreases by 1d6, Treasury decreases by 1d6 BP, and the event is continuous.

### Fairy Hostage

The dryad [[Hazalea]] is held hostage by Hrondgren's trolls. A beloved figure in the fey world, her captivity forces the fairies of the region to serve Hrondgren. Hazalea is the young queen of the Hazel Court, a very small and peaceful court of fairies located in the Draketooth Forest. Although she claims only the area of the forest as her sphere of influence, she has good relations with other benign fey in the Evergreen Plains. Silverich has effectively toppled her as the ruling power of the forest, and his daughter Deilenwyn ([[forest drake stalker 7]]) holds Hazalea and her tree as hostage with a retinue of 4 forest drakes ([[forest drake stalker 5]]), 6 trolls ([[troll primal disciple 4]]), and 3 hags ([[green hag witch 4]]).
- Along with Hazalea, the grig [[Tyg-Twitter-Twig]] is also being held hostage. Tyg is a close friend of Hazalea and was captured at the same time when Deilenwyn arrived.
- Hazalea's consort, the satyr [[Falchos]], has been forced to lead a band of Hazel Court fairies into acts of sabotage against Prateria. However, he tries to avoid killing anyone, using nonlethal attacks, and focusing only on destroying property. [[Percilash]] is forced to aid Falchos with their fairy friends from the Gnarlwood to prevent any harm to Tyg.
- Deilenwyn holds Hazalea and Tyg-Twitter-Twig hostage. At the start of the encounter, Deilenwyn has Tyg in a grapple, and on her turn, readies an action to kill Tyg when one of the PCs makes an overt attack. After Tyg is dead, she eats the body, and threatens to harm Hazalea's tree if the PCs continue to attack.
- Falchos is forced to attack the PCs to protect his love, and Hazalea is unable to do anything as long as the enemy is within striking distance of her tree.
