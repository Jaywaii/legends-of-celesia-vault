# Notes

## Session 69.5

**[Davona]**
*During the night, Davona falls asleep, suddenly feeling far more tired than normal. You dream, and sense a female presence beside you, always just out of the corner of your eye. A strand of long, green hair flutters into your sight every now and then. A hand emanating so much power places itself on your shoulder.*

*You make your request. Wind blows leaves before you, obscuring your vision. When it clears, a bastard sword made of cold iron stands before you, blade planted straight into the ground. The text inscribed along its blade is foreign to you, yet you are able to understand it: "Faerus Sicariah" - Fairy Slayer. You reach out, and place your hand upon its hilt. Before you can pull it out, the hand of power rest upon yours.*

*"A powerful blade, dangerous to my kind. It is yours, my champion, should you pledge to never raise it against I and my own."*

*Upon giving your word, she removes her hand. You pull the sword out of the ground easily, and marvel as sunlight reflects upon its clean blade. The light begins to engulf you. Before you are completely blinded, an image burns into your mind. It is that of another bastard sword, with end of the hilt and the crossguard shaped as roses, and the quillions shapes as entwining briar stalks. Similar imagery roses and briar are inscribed along the blade.*

*"A sword for a sword. Bring Briar to me, my champion, and your reward will be beyond imagination."*

*You wake, and the sun is just breaking up from the horizon. It is hard to clearly remember your dream, but you feel the heavy weight of steel in your hand. You find yourself gripping the hilt of a bastard sword. Faerus Sicariah rests in your hand.*

*+2 fey bane cold iron bastard sword "Faerus Sicariah"* (20,370 gp)
