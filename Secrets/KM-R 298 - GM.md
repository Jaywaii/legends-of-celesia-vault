## Elyssria's Offer to Aedre and Orok

*As you close your eyes, an ethereal breeze carries your thoughts along soft currents. When you open your eyes, you find yourself in a glade bathed in gentle moonlight. Blooming with beauty, and veiled with twinkling starlight, the Queen of Whisperveil awaits you at the glade's center, with an enigmatic smile gracing her lips. You already know what is expected of you, and you step forward and offer of you what is due to her. Her smile never leaves her lips, but where before there was expectation, there is now appreciation. She leans forward, and soft words flow from her lips to your ears. Knowledge, deep and arcane, swell your mind. Your contemplation of the beauty of the arcane formula is ended with the nymph queen's next words. " I have seen you, child of the burning sands. You have aided my champions, and they have aided you. You possess tremendous power and ambitious dream, but face a foe greater than you in both. You have seen the success they have achieved, and I would offer you the same. Become my champion, and you may call upon my help to achieve your dreams." She leans back to stand regally, looking at you with expectation.*

## The Dead At Valenlet

*The Pall held, and the Gloom could not, or more worriedly chose not to move past Valenlet. Instead, it surrounded the settlement, only held at bay by the curtain of golden light springing from Rishoi. But as fearsome as the Gloom was, there was relief and confidence by the few people left in Valenlet, a skeleton crew of town guards and the members Prateria's expedition. The ritual was a success, the lands of Rochefort were spared from the terror of the Gloom, and hope for overcoming this darkness grew. It was when the sun set after the first day of raising the pall that that the terror renewed.*

*With Valenlet's inhabitants evacuated, the only persistent sound was the soft shimmer from the Pall. But as soon as the sun set, other sounds took over. The shimmering hum transformed into bursts of sparks. The guards went to go look, and quickly, shouts of panicked alarm filled the air. "Zombies!" From within the shadows, decrepit limbs emerge to slam against the pall. Upon contacting, they burst into golden flame. There is a sigh of relief among the guard, but the limbs keep coming. Over and over again, the dead crush and immolate themselves as they pound against the pall, until pieces of burning flesh begin falling through. It takes only a minute bigger and bigger pieces of burning zombie flesh to breach throught the pall, until what everyone's worse fear happens:  whole corpses are pushed, burnt and singed, but still moving, still lurching for living flesh.*

*The town guards cry out in alarm, but they are cut short. Luckly throats are slashed open, their blood spraying, seemingly without cause.*
