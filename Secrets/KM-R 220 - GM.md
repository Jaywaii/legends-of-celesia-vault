# Notes

- 8 butcher ants (CR 1): 200 XP each
- Total XP: 693 XP each (Thassi, Bonzar, Oskar, Circe, Zaccaria, Fenrir)

## Coteverte

- 12 giant butcher ant workers erupt from the ground around the sheep. Coteverte guards will fight half of them in 3 groups of 4 guards vs 2 ants. This leaves 6 ants against the PCs.
- PCs can follow the tunnels back to the colony's location. Tunnels are only 5 ft. wide, room enough for workers and soldiers to go back and forth side-by-side. Whole sheep can be carried, but cattle get chopped up to fit through.
- PCs need to sneak around and pick their fights carefully.
