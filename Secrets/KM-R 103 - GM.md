- On the morning of game day 2, Jupus meets with Adéline Châteaufoy at a private room in their rented lodging. The two dance around the exact nature of their discussion, but it soon becomes clear that Adéline wants to find out why Jupus is so interested in the Montplaisir-Gardefort incident. Jupus eventually reveals himself to be Janus Gardefort, confirming Adéline's suspicions. Due to many details of the case being sealed by legal rulings, Adéline is only able to provide the name of the TRA investigator who was initially in charge of the case, a dwarf woman named Gunhilde Bronzebuckle. She is also able to inform Jupus that the Gardefort lawyer, Madeline Dambrose, was trying to fight back against those rulings. When Jupus asks who took control of the Montplaisir and Gardefort assets, Adéline is only able to note that the significant size of either the Montplaisir family assets and the Gardefort family assets alone would require numrous parties involved, but unfortunately the exact identities of those parties are also sealed. At the end of the meeting Adéline wishes good fortune to Jupus on any action he may take; the loss of the Montplaisir family and the downfall of the Gardefort family eliminated two powerful pillars of Rochefort's economy.
- At the end of the day, Argus and Davona receive a report from their security forces. It seems that several game rigging attempts were discovered, and that several groups of spectators were paid to cheer on particular athletes. The athletes who benefited from the game rigging and paid cheering are melee fighter Rocco Leonti of Ferrocitta, foot racer Sandra Calza of Drelevona, mixed teams combatant Leonardo 'the Crusher' Calandrini of Coracentia, mixed teams combatant Nezetta 'the Netherwitch' Priuli of Blébonté, and mixed teams combatant Cicilia 'Firebrand' Firavanti of Santo Venito.
