# Notes

## Firhen 114

- Davona will ask her follower Ingvild the troll cleric (former troll unit leader and current leader of Seormaw) about any information regarding who funded the nation of Greentusk. If she doesn't know, Davona will ask her if she knows anyone that might. Any persons of interest will be called to a meeting at a future date.
	- Ingvild admits that she does not possess much knowledge of how Hrondgren gained his wealth. She does know that the faithful of Tiamat was already backing him, so she suspects that the church of Tiamat was either the primary backer, or at least a major contributor to Hrondgren's mission.
	- Davona goes around speaking to notable elders about Hrondgren. The elders are not friendly to Davona, and are reluctant to even speak with her (HD and Sense Motive +20, Hostile +15, Enemy +15, Wary +15, Moderate Risk +15).
	- DC 35 Sense Motive: The elders initially say a myriad of tales of how Hrondgren gained his wealth: that he killed a human ruler in Radimus, that he was blessed by the gods, that he uncovered the lost treasure of a Carpathos city tyrant. Davona is able to suss out that these are tall tales and rumours, and drives her questions to get what the elders really know about Hrondgren.
	- DC 90: Davona barely gets any tangible information out of the elders. They speak with reverence of how Hrondgren marked with greatness the day the Greentusk tribe found him as a baby with his dragon blood already apparent, how he grew powerful and charismatic already in his childhood, and how he left the tribe upon reaching puberty before returning years later with an entourage of powerful followers and wealth beyond all imagination.
	- DC 100: The elders reluctantly give a bit more information in order to cease Davona's insistence. Hrondgren already had great ambitions when he left, but upon his return, he spoke that in his travels, his destiny had found him, and blessed him with the power to achieve his true purpose. He did not elaborate further, but the power and wealth he possessed was enough to prove the truth of his words, and all submitted to him.
	- DC 110: A few of the less hostile elders speak in hushed tones that Hrondgren would sometimes seclude himself in his room alone or travel alone outside of the keep. When he would return, he would have new plans or bring new resources.
	- DC 120: Grudgingly, the less hostile elders reluctantly admit that as Klorgfang's armies were losing, Hrondgren would enter seclusion more often. Despite the thick doors and walls, sometimes he can be heard raging, screaming at someone before the room would tremble, and Hrondgren calmed down.
- Davona will also ask around/investigate in the Greentusk province for any citizens present at/around the founding of the Klorfang Nation. Once Davona spends the time figuring out all the logistics of getting them together in a room, she will set the meeting and invite Asako/Sky to attend.
	- On the day of the meeting, Davona will ask them questions regarding memories of anything related to who funded the founding of the nation.
	- Asako will give each of them divine insight (+15 to check) to help them remember. Sky will then view their memories.
	- Davona will pay each of them 10 gold for showing up, and 100 gold on top of that if they have a useful memory.
- Jupus wants Eumenethos to investigate Demodokos Trading Company. He aims to set up a network that reaches into Carpathos so he can discreetly provide funds and resources to the real Eumenethos.
	- DC 30 Diplomacy to gather information on money transfers work, gaining a +1 bonus to Merchant check. Each activity day spent grants an additional +1.
	- DC 30 Merchant check to get 10% of his cash to Eumenethos (the rest went to bribing officials to hide its tracks). Every degree increases the amount in Eumenethos' hands by 10%. Each activity day spent on it gains a +1 on check.
		- result: 70. Eumenethos gains 12,500 gp out of 25,000.

## Viridian Games

- Promethea once again hosts a week long festival of contests. Lurindor, Grondeloup, Drelevona all send representatives.
- Benefits:
	- Make a DC 91 Politician check. Spend Wealth and Influence to get +1 bonus per point spent. 7 times, one for each day.
	- Each success garners Influence equal to the population level. Every 10 above the DC garners another gain of Influence equal to Population level. Also increase Wealth development by the same amount.

## The Coil

- Continues to reduce public Support for the government.
- The Coil gets its gangs to attempt to disrupt the Viridian Games in order to embarass the government. To detect, make a DC 30 Security check (1d20 + an amount of Security levels from province + whatever Wealth/Influence from Treasury).
	- A load of ammunition for ranged weapons contest were switched out with defective versions.
	- Feed for mounts were switched out for spoiled feed.
	- Bed bugs are found in the sleeping quarters of the athletes.

## Fang and Claw Society

- Continues to reduce public Support for the government and increase Morale of the working-class.
- FCS try to sabotage the other teams in order for troll/giants/orcs/goblin athletes to win. To detect, make a DC 25 Security check (1d20 + an amount of Security levels from province + whatever Wealth/Influence from Treasury).
	- Meals for athletes from Promethea and Rochefort were switched out for spoiled food.
	- Equipment for Promethean athletes and Rochefort go missing or are broken.

## Steamflogger Industries

- Steamflogger supplies are again disrupted this month. Make a DC 40 Merchant check to scrounge supplies or lose 10% of income.
- On success, Jupus manages to gain new supplies. Several suppliers he had to do business with last time are happy to aid him again, and hope to further business relationships with Steamflogger: Artellio Imports, Carvina Workshop, Servilla Transports (all 3 are part of the Vespari Conglomerate).
