## Nythagoi Merchant Caravan

Xura Nythagoi is an oneiros of indeterminate gender and merchant master, a human-like person with light golden skin, a green left eye and a purple right eye.
- Caravan composition: 1 carriage (Xura, clerk, driver, 2 guards), 1 wagon (driver, guard, 2 laborers), 4 guards on horseback.
- *Shouts and sounds of weapons interrupt the surroundings' tranquility.*
- The caravan is under attack by a dozen [[Nightgaunt Stalker 5]] seeking to capture them. (hp 200, DV 28, DR 15/armor, claw +21 (2d4+18 [34/26] plus grab, CMB +25 (+29 grapple), CMD 32)
- Saving the caravan leads to Xura feeling grateful towards the PCs. Xura can explain the Blooming Fields of Mykariota.
- Failing to save the caravan, the PCs can uncover clues in Xura's journal and files to lead them to the Blooming Fields of Mykariota.
