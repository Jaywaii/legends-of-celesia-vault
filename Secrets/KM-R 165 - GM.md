# Notes

- Successfully scout the TRA courthouse archive: 250 XP (62.5 XP each)
- Successfully gain info on Gunhild from Thangreka and gain her as an ally: 250 XP (62.5 XP each)

- The next day, the freelancer team review their options. They decide to investigate the TRA courthouse and see if they can find any documents related to the Montplaisir-Gardefort case. They opt to not use any disguise, appearing as themselves as it is not uncommon for freelancers making appearances at the courthouse (usually as the defendents). However, they choose to proceed carefully; this is only a reconnaissance, and they have little skill in deception. They choose to use the excuse of Shortstuff looking to see if there are any records of his family at the courthouse in order to find the records room and scope out how it works.
- They arrive at the courthouse, and scout out the building. It is strongly built and well-guarded. They are allowed entry by the guards after displaying their freelander license, and sign in at the lobby. Although the freelancer license allows them to use aliases, their freelancer ID is recorded along with their appearances, and they must note down their reason for entry.
- The team make their way to the records room. The room is separated by a secured counter, forming a waiting area at the entrance, and the rows of shelves and cabinets behind. Clerks stand ready at the counter and others in the back handling miscellaneous paperwork. Shortstuff asks about records regarding his family (revealed to have the family name of 'Cortix'). While Shortstuff learns about how retrieving copies of court records work, the rest of the team try to scout out the room. They are not able to see much, only that there do not seem to be any windows into this room, but the shelves and cabinets obscure enough of the room that they are not able to get a proper idea of the layout.
- Ulgan leaves the room and tries to find where the guards' room is. He encounters a pair of them leaving their security room, and he tries to befriend them, offering some drink. However, the appearance of an orc freelancer with booze in the courthouse has the guards suspicious, and warn him off.
- The team eventually leaves the courthouse, having to sign out as well. They return to the Cherry House to plan their next actions. Breaking into the courthouse's records room seems out of their skills. They focus on the dwarf woman of the Rochefort Guard that Kraz encountered. They locate her and stealthily track her on her patrol route with her halfling guard partner. After her shift, they follow her to her home, where they see her with her dwarf husband and daughter.
- Returning to the Cherry House, the team discuss what to do about the dwarf. There is talk of kidnapping her, kidnapping her family to use as blackmail, blackmailing her with the knowledge of the records of her "legal indiscretions", or bribing her with the idea of the team disposing of those records. Ultimately, the team realizes the high risks involved in these ideas, and that they are not even sure they have the right dwarf. They do have plenty of time available to them, however, as the job established no timeline on completion. The team decide to take a few days  of gathering information on the dwarf woman through indirect means. Shortstuff opts to stakeout the woman's home for a day.
- While the rest of the team gathers resources, makes contacts, and quietly collects information about the dwarf, Shortstuff observes that the daughter plays with other children in the neighbourhood while her father tends of the home. Later, Vasiir suggest Shortstuff befriend the daughter, as the gnome, being a child himself, can spend some time with the other children, and try to get information that way. He purchases some colourful glass marbles, and the next day, appears on the street in front of the dwarf's house while her daught is playing with three other halfling children. He introduces himself as "Micky", and manages to get invited to join in, using the tragedy of not being able to speak to his adventuring parents as a way in. Over the course of their play time, Shortstuff learns that the dwarf's daughter is named Jowlra, and that her mother is the best guard in the city for many years. This family has lived in Rochefort for as long as she remembers. Eventually, play time is over as Jowlra's father calls her in for lunch, and Shortstuff takes his leave.
- Another day passes, and the team sifts through everything they've learned about the dwarf guard. The information they received lines up with what Shortstuff uncovered the day before. The dwarf woman is Thangreka Fireshield, a sergeant of the Rochefort Guard for many years. She is known for being a fierce fighter, down-to-earth and direct, but prefers friendly relations with the locals in her district. The team decides to take a diplomatic approach first, with Kraz making contact.
- The next day, the teams heads towards Thangreka's home during the morning, and stealthily follows her to her station. While the rest of the team hides nearby, Kraz waves down Thangreka before she reaches the station. While Thangreka is on guard, Kraz apologizes for his previous encounter, and is more honest and acts less suspiciously this time. He inquires about Gunhild Bronzebuckle again, this time honestly explaining the job he and his team are working, looking into the suspicious investigation of the Gardefort family and the Montplaisir Massacre. Willing to discuss this, Thangreka leads Kraz into an alley, out of sight from the public. After getting Kraz to spill everything he knows so far about the case, Thangreka explains that Gunhild is from a cousin clan of her own, and that they've been each other's contact in their respective organization. They would share information to help each other's investigations. Thangreka knew about Gunhild's investigation of the Gardefort, and that her cousin told her she uncovered some suspicious facts, but before she could investigate further, she was accused of corruption and underwent an internal investigation by the TRA. She was pulled off the case. Gunhild was eventually cleared, but by the time that happened, the Gardefort case was resolved. For the next several years, Gunhild looked into the case on her own time, but last year, she was transferred out of Rochefort to another small town, Toursoline. Before she left, Gunhild warned Thangreka to forget everything about this case and to stay away from her; Gunhild suspected she was being watched. That's all Thangreka knows, and she heeded her cousin's warning.
- Thangreka tells Kraz that she's willing to help the freelancers if it means helping her cousin. Kraz agrees, promising to provide her information on what they find, using the Freelancer Guild as their intermediary. Before Kraz leaves, Thangreka tells him that she knows that her cousin was working with a lawyer who was involved in the case, one Madeline Dambrose, a halfling woman.

- The dwarf woman is Thangreka Fireshield, an officer with the Rochefort Guard. Gunhild Bronzebuckle is a distant relation to her clan, but they were each other's contact within their respective organizations.
