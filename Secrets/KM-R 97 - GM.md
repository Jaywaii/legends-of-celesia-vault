# Notes

- Major Sway: Adêline Châteaufoy, Theodore Duloup
- Penalties: -2 from the halfling population for not inviting Tierra

- After dinner with the Grondeloup delegation, Sky speaks privately with Erikos Pentras. He gifts Pentras the use of the back room of his magic shop as a teleport destination for his use. The sylph magister also explains his plan of creating a magical academy open to all spellcasters in the Viridian Marches, and that he would like Erikos as one of its founding masters. Each founder would be able to customize parts of the academy and personally train students that fit their own philosophies. Erikos is intrigued at being able to leave such a legacy, and agrees to participate. Sky outlines his plan on inviting Rochefort's magister as a member, and Pentras agrees to help convince him to join in as well.
- The next day, Rochefort delegation arrives on Waterday 15th by way of skyship. The flying sailing ship lands on the outskirts of Promethea, anchoring down on a nearby hill. At the head of the delegation is Lord-Executor of Rochefort herself, Adéline Châteaufoy. She greets the leaders of Prateria warmly, thanking them for their invitation, and that she is looking forward to her first visit to their realm. Accompanying her is her Grand Diplomat Johan Lapierre and her Magister Évard Châteaufoy.
- That evening, the Praterian Council and the Rochefort delegation have dinner at the chic new restaurant called *Pinnacle*, a venue that specializes in fusion cuisine of various cultures. The Lord-Executor compliments on the place, and Arina explains that the *Pinnacle* is like Prateria, a place where old ideas come together to form new ones. Adéline is eager to discuss establishing stronger relations with Prateria, and finds Arina very receptive to the idea. Adeline inquiries what kind of nation and government does Prateria aim for, and Arina explains for her plan to move Prateria towards a more populist outset, reducing the autocratic nature of its consul. This impresses Adéline, and the two find more common ground on their dislike of Lurindor's current oligarchic regime, though the Lord-Executor herself does not openly admit such treasonous ideas.
- After the dinner, Sky walks with Évard and they meet up with Erikos at the Grondeloup lodging. They head over to Sky magic shop and go into its backroom. There, Sky outlines his plan for the magical academy. Both he and Erikos succeed in convincing Évard to join in. Sky then explains who else he intends to ask: Carsca of the Sootscale tribe, and Illario Strazza of Drelevona. The other two magisters are surprised by the choices. They do not recognize the name Carsca, but the fact that she is a kobold is certainly not expected. The two magisters are both pragmatic, however, and Évard's only concern is if Carsca is skilled enough to earn the position. As for Illario, both Évard and Erikos know of the man's reputation as a skilled and meticulous wizard, but also that he is a cold mercenary at heart. They warn Sky to be wary with him, and know that Illario's ultimately loyalty is to his own wealth. Sky also gives Évard permission to use his shop's backroom as a private teleport location.
- The next day, the rest of the Lurindor and religious dignitaries arrive by their respective skyships on Windsday 16th, likewise landing on the outskirts of Promethea. The Coracentia envoy, Elvia Sovrani, leads the contingent. She greets Arina quite huskily, eyeing her up and down. She tells the Praterian Consul that she is looking quite forward to speaking with her in more private surroundings, to which Arina states that she hopes so as well in two days. Elvia smiles and says it's a date. The Montari delegation arrives on the same day by skyship as well, but separately as they stopped by Arbor's Rest first, and brought its governor Domenico D'onofrio.

	- Cyrano Varano and his son Aldo Varano will be residing at their villa. They have invited the Biancardis, Lord-Executor Giovanni, his consort Milena, and their son Manfredo, to lodge with them.
		- The Dinovio, Harkûr, Montari, and Teredoro delegations will be lodging in the penthouse suites of Promethea's hotels.
