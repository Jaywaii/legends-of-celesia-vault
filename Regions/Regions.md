# Regions

The world of Celesia was created to be the crowning jewel of the Creator Gods when they fashioned the universe. It is a fairly large world, with a circumference of over fifty thousand miles. Water covers a little over half the planet. There are nine continents, and five oceans. There are also many groupings of islands, some large enough to hold powerful nations.

### Religion, Population, Languages

Each region lists the common, uncommon, and rare religions, racial ethnicities, and spoken languages present. Common religions and ethnicities have a 90% chance of being encountered while traveling through the region; 50% chance for uncommon, and 10% chance for rare. The rarity chance for languages is the chance of a person of the same lineage of as that language speaking it. The language rarity drops by one step for people not of the same lineage as the language.

# Oceans

The world of Celesia is split into nine continents and five oceans.

Much of how the world is currently recognized as is due to the mapping efforts of the Argent Rose Society during the height of the Empire of Xian in the early half of the Third Age.

The names of each continent and ocean used here are those used by the Argent Rose Society. Once a continent's or ocean's size had been mapped, and at least half of its states or ethnic groups had an Argent Rose lodge established, a Grand Lodge was founded. Amidst the Grand Lodge's duties, each regional lodge's members, made up of local and indigenous scholars and sages of each region who elected to join the Society, were invited to convene a council to name the continent or ocean which will be used to reference the greater region in Argent Rose writings.

In addition to naming the greater region, the Grand Lodge also created a continental- or oceanic-wide common language in order to ease the passing of knowledge between different regions and people. However, its adoption was met with limited success even into modern time. Individual regions still prefer use their local or ethnic languages to this day. These greater regional tongues created by the Society are mostly used just by its members and intellectuals who travel wide and far, although many interregional treaties and contracts are written in these tongues as well.

The Argent Rose Society used the location of its first Grand Lodge on the Isle of Xian as world's prime meridian. Most maps still use this to determine the western and eastern hemispheres of the world.

# Continents

## Anoakowa

Westernmost continent of the northern hemisphere. Its southern edge is connected to Anzica. It is bordered by the Zucatec ocean on its eastern coast, and the Shenlei ocean on its western coast.

## Anzica

Westernmost continent of the southern hemisphere. Its northern edge is connected to Anoakowa. It is bordered by the Zucatec ocean on its eastern coast, and the Dhanastri ocean on its western coast.

## Asteropa

Located in the northern part of the western hemisphere, nearly adjacent to the prime meridian. Its southern edge is connected to Khaliram. It is bordered by the Xian-Luan ocean on its eastern coast, and the Zucatec ocean on the western coast.
