# The Deathless Shroud

*Master of Undeath. The Grave Maven. The Chainer of Souls.*

The Deathless Shroud, commonly named as Xarios, is the god of undeath. They are master of all things that defy the natural order of life and death, and patron to all those who seek to chain the cycle to their own will.

The Deathless Shroud's totems are the beetle, the blowfly, and the sea worm.

# Relations with Other Faiths

The Deathless Shroud is reviled by the followers of the Pale Judge as undeath violates the natural cycle. In return, the Deathless Shroud's followers have nothing but disdain for the Pale Judge and their worshipers, viewing them as sheep slaved to an archaic concept.

The Deathless Shroud's nature is disgusted by many. The only deity willing to work with them is the Plaguebringer. The latter creates the corpses that the former claims as their own.
