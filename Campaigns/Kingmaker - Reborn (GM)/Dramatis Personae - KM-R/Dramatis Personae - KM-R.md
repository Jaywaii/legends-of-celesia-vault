## Harkûr Trade Guild

Dol Harkûr descends from the Ohramdûr nation. It's ruling clan, Harkûr, united all of the trade guilds under its leadership to counter the growing power fo the Ispirani conglomerates. The Harkûr Trade Guild is nominally the parent company of all trade operations, but has direct control on security, mining, and smithing operations. Harkûr adopts a fiercely protectionist policy towards trade, causing friction between them and the other Trade Houses. However, Harkûr compensates for its un-competitive attitude with contributing generously to the Emerald Throne.

**Personality:** Cautious and isolated. The Harkûr have a strong sense of law and order, and strongly supports the regulations that prevent the trade houses from engaging in violent trade wars. Thus, the Harkûr are one of the strongest supporters of the Trade Regulation Authority.

**Goals:** Continued prosperity of Dol Harkûr above all else.

## Sotillini Cartel

The Sotillini Cartel controls the territory of Mariporta. Their primary business ventures are all maritime related. They own many fishing companies and the export companies that distribute their produce to the rest of the country. They exclusively own all trade with the water elf city of Selfuril.

The Sotillini maintain a powerful fleet of ships, and are charged with keeping order on Lake Lurin.

**Personality:**

**Goals:** The Sotillini Cartel controls much of the mercenary operations of Lurindor. Although many independent companies operate in the nation, they must all be registered and pay dues to mercenary guilds, which are either controlled by the Cartel, or that give the Cartel a significant cut. Each trade territory have one mercenary guild nominally controlled by their respective Trade House, but operate only with a contract with the Cartel, in exchance for other concessions. Aside from the mercenary business, the Cartel controls much of the Lurin maritime trade. The Sotillini's do not hold as much control on that trade as they do with the mercenary business, but they do hold exclusive trade rights with the water eld city of Selfuril.

## Sovrani Syndicate

The Sovrani Syndicate was once second only to the Amiatti Conglomerate. With the vanishing of the Amiatti, Sovrani is now the largest and richest Trade House of Lurindor. While working under his father, Niccolo Sovrani successfully brokered many agreements between Sovrani and Amiatti. Niccolo eventually inherited control of the Syndicate, and when all of the Amiatti's disappeared, he used the connections he had forged between the two companies to gain control of all of Amiatti's assets. The Sovrani Syndicate now controls the territories of Coracentia and Nuevo Venicar. With the assets of both territories, the Sovrani also boasts the largest military power in the nation.

**Personality:** Ambitious and ruthless. The Sovranis enjoy the power and influence their wealth brings them, and are eager to take on any challenge. They are supremely confident in their abilities. They have no qualms in completely eradicating those that get in their way. However, they fully understand that endeavour requires some compromise and give-and-take. They will reward their allies and servants that succeed, and will keep their word in agreements. They have no need for treachery because they are confident in being able to arrange things so that benefits are always in their favour before they commit to anything. They are willing to take risks, and willing to invest in talented people to benefit from their skills and win their loyalty.

**Goals:** The Sovranis will not rest until their Syndicate is the sole pillar of Lurindor, and that all of its wealth flows into them. The Syndicate gains its wealth primarily through land ownership. They also control much of the housing industry: houses, inns, taverns. Through their acquisition of Amiatti assets, they also now control much of the financial institutions of Lurindor.
