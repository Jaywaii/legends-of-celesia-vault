# Elyssria

# Queen of the Whisperveil Court

Long ago, the nymph Elyssria was chosen by the Fairest Count Calefaigdh to be his consort. Elyssria had garnered his attention for her exceptional beauty, artistic talent, and her charisma. Elyssria was well beloved by her followers, and loved them in return. Elyssria was enamoured by Calefaigdh through his courting, and their affair was a torrent of passion. During that time, Calefaigdh introduced Elyssria a taste of Fairest power which she quickly became addicted to.

However, Calefaigdh was selfish and fickle. Although Elyssria remained enamoured with him, Calefaidh had long grown disinterested in their liaison. However, what did interest the fey count was Elyssria's potential. Calefaigdh wanted to experiment and see how far he could push this once simple fey through the overly complex machinations of fey courts.

Through subtle manipulations, Calefaigdh pushed Elyssria to pursue more and more loftier goals. Elyssria rose through the ranks of the fey, and grew more and more prideful. She eventual caught the interest of Oberon. However, Elyssria became too deluded with her fame and power, and made the deadly mistake of openly courting the Fairest King in the presence of the other Queens of the fey.

Backed by Titania and Mab, the Morrigan cursed Elyssria, and took from the nymph her most potent ability: her capacity to love. The three queens then took that power and refashioned it in the sword Briar, and flung it into the Prime, where the sword's nature would obscure it from Elyssria's sight. Elyssria herself was exiled to her realm, the Whispering Vale, that she had crafted at the peak of her power. There, Elyssria changed.

Without her love, she became cold and unfeeling, and the void left in her ached unendingly. Elyssria could still charm and inspire others into following her, but she now felt nothing for them. She did not care for their welfare as she once did, and would now inflict fear and pain upon them at her whim without hesitation or regret.

Elyssria grew obsessed with restoring her status and ending the emptiness within her. She came up with a plan to merge her realm of the Whispering Vale with the Viridian Marches, a piece of land on the Material Plane rich with not only the magic of Faerie, but also unlimited magical potential. The land was considered a jewel by the fey sullied by the hands of mortals. By merging the land with her own piece of Faerie, Elyssria's power would be magnified exponentially. She would truly become on the same level as the eldest of Fairest, perhaps achieving power beyond even Oberon himself.

In order to do so, Elyssria would need to create special seeds that would need to be planted at specific ley lines throughout the Viridian Marches. In addition, these ley lines needed to be purified and prepared by elaborate fairy rituals and architecture. It would take Elyssria centuries to do so, but now that her love was gone from her, the nymph queen had no problems being patient and focused enough to accomplish her goals.

To allow her agents prepare the Marches for her plan, Elyssria would manipulate the mortals from the shadows to prevent them from bringing the land under a unified rule. Over the centuries, Elyssria's manipulations have forced mortal nations to abandon the Viridian Marches because of disasters or war. No one nation was able to control the realm for beyond a short period of time.

During all of this, Elyssria was obsessed with another matter: the sword Briar. When they ripped love out of Elyssria, the Fairy Queens had prophesied that when she finally reunites with the sword, it would be wielded in the hands of a mortal, and it would be the death of her. Elyssria became obsessed with locating Briar, not only because of the threat to her that it represented, but also because she was subconsciously driven to reunite with the part of her that was stolen. However, the agents of the Fairest did their best to keep it hidden from her thus far. Little did anyone know, Calefaigdh, in his experiments of controlling fate itself, had caused Briar to always find its way to the Viridian Marches throughout the millenia. Although he was done with Elyssria, observing what would happen amused him as a side project, and perhaps he can profit from these events somehow.

Elyssria has now reached the point where her seeds are ready to be planted in the Viridian Marches. Once the sites have been prepared and the seeds planted, they will bloom with the power of Faerie, effectively bridging and merging the two realms together. Faerie dust will spread from the blooms, spreading throughout the Viridian Marches to allow Elyssria to pull the land into Faerie and merge it with the Whispering Vale.

However, Elyssria is not unopposed. Due to events in Lurindor, that nation will begin another attempt in securing the Viridian Marches under their rule. The Viridescian dragons also seek to reclaim their stolen land. Other fairy rulers have their own plans, either to oppose Elyssria, or claim the Viridian Marches for themselves.

### Goals

Elyssria seeks to translate the entire Viridian Marches to Faerie as a gift to Oberon and Titania as the land is considered a shining jewel of melding of Faerie and Prime energies. However, the translation would leave a scarred wasteland upon Celesia, a an untamed rift which would allow entities from other planes to pour in unchecked.

In order to accomplish her goal, Elyssria must bind specific fonts of power located throughout the Veridian Marches with a powerful spell of her own devise:

- (ACHIEVED) The 3 Hearts of the Forest within the Old Arborfane.
- The font of power within Vorakios' lair in the Thalamech Uplands.
- (ACHIEVED) The Great Cypress tree in North Evergreen (which was sealed long ago by the elves; the Great Cypress was planted there). Elyssria has devised a modified binding spell to reopen the font and instead use the Great Cypress as a conduit, though it requires a dryad to bind with the tree.
- The font of power within Armag's Tomb in the Drakethorn Glades.
- The font of power within the Castle of Knives located in the forest of a Thousand Whispers in the Ursan Highlands.
- The font of power that became the faengard, the locked gate between the Prime Material and the Fellnight Realm.

As part of the ritual to cast the spell, Elyssria had to collect several talismanic components harvested from powerful beings with strong ties to the Viridian Marches:

- (ACHIEVED) The horn of the elder unicorn Mistwind, who rode throughout the Evegreen Plains. Elyssria claimed this one a year before the Evergreen Company arrived in the Marches.
- (ACHIEVED) The heart of the silver dragon Argentia, who protected the eastern lands of the Marches. This was claimed by the black dragon Charcalthiak and given to Elyssria in exchange for empowering her enough to kill her hated rival and letting her take Argentia's hoard.
- The heart of Hungerdark, aka Minognos-Ushad, the legendary wyvern of the Thousand Whispers in the Ursan Highlands.
- The heart of Euphrosyne, legendary hydra of the Drakethorn Glades.

As a bonus, to make her spell even more powerful, Elyssria seeks out the following bonus components:

- (ACHIEVED) The heart of the Dancing Lady.
- The heart of Rhoswen, the Fellnight Queen.
- The heart of Hrondgren.
- Vorakios' phylactery (which she will claim when the Company destroys Vorakios).
- Armag's sword, Orvinbaane.

Lastly, Elyssria seeks out one last item: the sword Briar. It was forge from the essence of Elyssria's capacity to love that was stripped from her by the Three Queens (Titania, Mab, Morrigan). It was then cast down upon the Prime Material. Over many centuries, it made its way to the Viridan Marches, a coincidence, or perhaps providence. When it was forged, the Morrigan prophesied that this sword would be the one to end Elyssria's life, and because it was created from her love, it could be, and was so, cursed so that Elyssria will never be able to trace it through any means, even through epic magic, and this was on top of more usual magical obscurement enchantments. Elyssria tasked Damiano Scarzini as her latest agent to track down and locate the sword, having a flash of insight into the man's fate when she met him when he was still a boy.

### Lore about Elyssria

**Complexity 30**

**Research Check** Lore (fey), Knowledge (history), Knowledge (nature)

**kp 10** Elyssria is a nymph and Queen of the Whisperveil Court. She is said to have potent magical power.

**kp 20** She and her Court reside in the realm of the Whispering Vale on the Plane of Faerie, a realm of great natural and tranquil beauty. Numerous fairies make their home there, to relax and frolick.

**kp 40** Elyssria is known for the great love that she has for her subjects. Her compassion and mercy are legendary, and she accumulated numerous followers and suitors over the millenia.

**kp 80** Among her suitors was the Fairest Count Calefaigdh, and their passionate dalliance made for exciting and scandalous tales. She even caugh the interest of Oberon, King of all Fairies, and that tale of lofty romance influenced generations of romantic authors.

**kp 160** Ancient fairy legends speak of the tragic tale of Elyssria and Briar. The Fairest known as the Morrigan and aided by Queens Titania and Mab, stripped Elyssria of her ability to love, and crafted it into the sword Briar, and flung it into the Prime Material. Separated from it, Elyssria has become a cold, amoral creature with only two obsessions: attaining power fill the void within her, and retrieving the sword. She can achieve the first by merging the fey-touched land of the Viridian Marches with her fairy domain of Whispering Vale. Doing so would multiply her power beyond imagination, possibly allowing her to attain godhood, and protect herself from the prophecy.

## Before Kingmaker

- In the Evergreen Reach, Elyssria guides the future Stag Lord to beconing a powerful bandit capable of uniting the bandits of the region.
- In the Thalamech Uplands, Elyssria tricks the centaur tribe into attacking Lurindor trade caravans.
- In the Draketooth Glades, Elyssria manipulates the bogwoads into attacking any caravans that pass through the region.

## The Viridian Marches

- Elyssria came to the Stag Lord in a dream when he was a boy, seeing his potential in keeping the Marches free from human activity. She seduced him and gave him a ring made of her hair.
- After the PCs defeat the Stag Lord, Elyssria takes notice of them. She enters the dreams of the ruler. If the Ruler is attracted to females, she will attempt to seduce them. If not, she gives them dreams of power, wealth, or whatever their heart desires.

### Rivers Run Red

- Elyssria quietly lends her support to the PCs in their battle against Hrondgeren's monster nation. Aid comes in the form of local feys aiding the PCs and their forces, with compliments of the Whispering Queen.

### Wolfshold Withering

- Seeking to stem the PCs control of the region, Elyssria enters the dream of the archeologist, giving him hints that will lead him to trespass into Vorakios' tomb, awakening the lich. Meanwhile, Elyssria again lends her support to the PCs, although not enough to completely defeat the lich.
- While the lich is occupied by the PCs, Elyssria's agents steal his phylactery. After the lich was destroyed, Elyssria now controls him by keeping his phylactery under her control.

### War of the Viridian Kings

- Years ago, when Scarzini embarked on his mission of revenge, he stumbled into Faerie, specifically the Whisperingvale. He managed to impress Elyssria with his artistic talent. However, Elyssria saw his potential to rule and to find the sword Briar. She became his muse and gave him a ring made of her hair. With Elyssria's backing, Scarzini gained mythic power in the Wilds of Faerie. Upon returning to the Middle Realm, he achieved his vengeance against the four ruling crime families of Lorthens, and crowned himself as the city's king. In exchange, Elyssria tasked Scarzini to find Briar. Scarzini had since used his temporal quest for power as his cover for his matron's quest. Scarzini still intended to fulfill the mission, enough so as to not break his oath to Elyssria, but had twisted his reasons; he now sought enough power to stand on Elyssria's level so he could use Briar to claim her as his consort. He dreamed that the two of them together could rule the merged Whispervale-Viridia as equals.

### Holy War

- With the goal of keeping the PCs occupied with other affairs so that they don't focus on hers, Elyssria has her fey antagonize the people of the Holy Radimus Empire. She starts this when the PCs found their nation. Her goal is to push the Radimites into declaring a holy crusade against the fey in the Viridian Marches. In other words, starting a war between the PCs' nation and the Holy Radimus Empire. Elyssria plans to play up the presence of fey in the PCs' nation, and will attempt to forge deep bonds between the PCs and the fey so that the Radimites consider the PCs and their nation as fairy heretics.
