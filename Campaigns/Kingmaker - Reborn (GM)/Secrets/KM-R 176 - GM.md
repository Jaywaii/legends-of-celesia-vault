# Notes

- Team has captured a Golden Scale lieutenant (Beneta Zorzi). She is not a Coil operative, but she was one of the first recruits when the Coil formed the gang. Her recruiter and now captain is a man named Signore Serpe. He is an actual Coil agent. Beneta knows that he works with 2 other captains, another man (Signore Krait) and a woman (Signora Vipera).
- Beneta's crew was supposed to follow the PCs to find Dambrose. The Golden Scales don't have Dambrose or any of the Gardefort files; she disappeared before word was given down to them to take her.
- In the sewers, there are some recent marks scratched into the brick wall, about every 30 feet. They are hidden, and require a Survival to find them and decipher which direction they are pointing to; 5 successful checks are needed. Every fail reduces number of successes by 1, and causes an encounter (dire rats, giant cockroaches, giant amoebas). Each Survival check triggers a Stealth check from the thugs vs. pasive Perception.
- Dambrose has established refuge in a disused mantenance room that was previously used for smuggling. Dambrose fires a crossbow bolt, but misses, hitting the wall next to the PCs. "T-take one more step, n-next one's in your eye!"
