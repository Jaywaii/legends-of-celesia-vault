## Trials of the Old Arborfane

Upon first entering the Old Arborfane with the Southern Heart of the Forest, the forest does not attack them, but branches and vines sway and curl around them, as if wary of them. The PCs are allowed to perform the Rite of the Oldwood, and upon completion, the forest seems to calm around them, although continue to display movement and awareness of their presence. However, they are unable to penetrate deeper into the forest, as they always seem to end up where they began.
- Each time the PCs loop back, they lose a day. Each time this happens, they lose 1 mythic power as the forest begins leeching their energy from them. Once they run out of mythic power, each day of being trapped incurs a negative level.  Once they incur negative levels equal to their character level, they lose 1 Con each day until they die at 0 Con. Abilities and spells like *teleportation* can help the PCs escape, but the Old Arborfane automatically causes the spell go Off-Target.
- Survival checks do not help them forward, merely inform the PCs that the forest is actively working to contain them. The PCs are also unable to leave the forest once they enter, as the same warping of the forest prevents them the same as they try to move forward.
- Lore (Old Arborfane), or Knowledge (nature) at disadvantage, informs the PCs that the Old Arborfane has gone feral, seeking to devouring any source of magic as a result of losing its Heart. It is so far gone that it even tries to devour its own heart, incapable of recognizing it, only knowing that it is a source of great power, of which the PCs also count towards.
- Lore (Old Arborfane), or Knowledge (nature) at disadvantage, reminds the PCs of old tales of a ritual learned from the hidden witches of the Old Arborfane Coven. Although such knowledge is usually kept secret by the coven, sometimes they are granted to worthy strangers, and the ritual did manage to slip out into the hands of non-Coven members. The ritual is called the Besseching the Old Wood's Guidance, and it involves requesting the Old Arborfane providing guidance to one's destination. The guidance comes in many forms, and may not be obvious, and may not be without additional cost. The Old Beldame knows the ritual and is willing to teach it to the PCs (6 days, DC 16 Intelligence).
- Every day the PCs are within their nation with the Heart of the Forest, their nation gains 1 Unrest higher than the previous day, starting from the day they returned to Promethea. They can reduce the Unrest gained that day to 1 Unrest by making a Stability check from the Ruler, Councilor, or Warden (add the Unrest to the DC).
