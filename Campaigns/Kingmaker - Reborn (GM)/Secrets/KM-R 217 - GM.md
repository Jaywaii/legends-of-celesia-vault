# Notes

- Duloup's recruits: defetated 8 wolves (CR 1, 200 XP each)
- Total XP: 320 XP each (Thassi, Bonzar, Oskar, Circe, Zaccaria)

## Duloup Recruits

*A call for action was sent out, and you all answered it. Theodore Duloup, a Rochefort adventure with a fair bit of fame, was seeking fresh new recruits to help his newfound nation of Grondeloup. With few laws set in stone, in a land of equally great dangers and opportunities, it was the perfect place for ambitious young adventure to seek excitement, treasures, and fame.*

*In answering the call, you find yourselves in Duloup's castle, the Wolf's Keep, located in the city of Wolfshold. You are presently in a waiting lounge just outside Duloup's office. The colonial lord was up to his brow in the duties of running a small nation under siege, so hence the waiting. However, this does present you the opportunity to get to know your new colleagues in this new venture.*

After introductions.

*The door to Duloup's office opens, and his aide, a young female halfling, appears. She tells you that Lord Duloup is ready to meet with you now, and welcomes you into the office. It's a fairly big office, with room for two couches around a coffee table on your left side of the room, and a larger table on other right side. You can see a large map of the region spread out atop of this large table, with many miniatures and tiny flags placed strategically upon it. At the far end of the room is Duloup, sitting bhind his thick desk, with a large floor-to-ceiling behind him letting light in. Duloup's desk is littered with stacks of parchment, and the halfling man himself looking quite haggard, with dark bags under his eyes. Nonetheless, he still appears sharp and energetic. He waves at you to come take a seat in front of this desk, his assistant having brought extra chairs for you.*

*"Thank you all for coming," he says. "I'll be brief. I have a lot of problems to deal with, and not enough people to resolve them. Worse, I now find myself with one person less. My marshal, Giovanni Vinozzi, has gone missing. My old companion is usually out in the field most of the time, but he keeps in regular contact with me. However, I have not had word from him in over 2 weeks. Just as important was his mission; Gio was tasked with finding a way to bring the Thunderhoof centaurs to the negotiation table for a peaceful coexistence with us and them."*

*"With everyone else I can count on with their hands full with the other problems we're dealing with. That's why you're here. I need someone to find my marshal. However, before I task you with this, I need to see that you have the skills to solve this problem."*

- Duloup wants PCs to handle a bug infestation in the nearby village of Coteverte.
- First, escort the coteverte villager, a young halfling woman named Adelette, who came to Wolfshold requesting help in dealing with the ants.
- Duloup informs the PCs that aggressive beasts have been prowling the region, likely artificially encouraged by the centaurs.
- Coteverte is suffering from loss of livestock and destruction of property from an infestation of giant ants.
- PCs need defend the village from giant ant raids, track their nest, and eliminate it.
- PCs will get ambushed by rabid wolves on the way to Coteverte.
- Soon after arriving at Coteverte, a swarm of giant ant workers raid the village to steal livestock and rip materials from buildings.
