# Notes

- Minor Sway: Alcina Sotillini, Sarpino Montari
- Major Sway: Adêline Châteaufoy, Theodore Duloup
- Penalties: -2 from the halfling population for not inviting Tierra

- Arina meets the Mariporta diplomat Alcina Sotillini at the *Intermission*, a restaurant that specializes in personalized minplays while patrons dine. Having a drink at the bar, the two speak about trade relations between Mariporta and Prateria. Alcina expresses Mariporta's concerns regarding the mass influx of current and former soldiers and recruits into Prateria. Although Prateria does not hold significant interest to the majority of Lurindor's mercantile elite, many mercenary captains have expressed their concerns on the drain of personnel, and that Prateria is positioning itself to compete with them and the Sotillini Cartel's dominance over the mercenary trade. Arina assures her that the mass migration of soldiers was unintentional, just people attracted to the legend that the Evergreen Company is building and of the potential future of Prateria, and that the Iron Watch is a temporary measure to defend the colony. Arina proposes that Prateria and Mariporta form a partnership to encourage soldiers to transfer freely without hassle between the two nations. Alcina is intrigued, and says that she will look into such an arrangement. In the meantime, the Mariporta diplomat is willing to promise that the Cartel will use its influence to assuage the offended companies and dissipate any ire towards Prateria. In exchange, the Cartel would like to acquire resources in Prateria, much like how they did with the sawmill some time ago, albeit at a much cheaper price. Arina renegotiates a slightly higher cost, and Alcina agrees easily, likely due being quite amused by the acts being played in the *Intermission*.
- That evening, the Praterian Council has dinner with the Montari delegation at the *Nomad*, a traveling, outdoor restaurant that changes its cuisine based on their current locale and run by devotees of Gunther, God of Travelers. Theodore Duloup and Domenico D'onofrio also attends the dinner. Sky was not invited, by shows up anyways. Sarpino Montari finds the venue quite quaint. Discussion focuses on Arbor's Rest, the jointly invested settlement nestled in Arborfane's Pass. Sarpino inquires on the lack of development of the settlement. Arina explains that they do not see any immediate need to focus on the settlement's future at the moment, subtly conveying to Duloup that perhaps it is time to see their share of Arbor's Rest's returns increase beyond what was initially agreed upon. Theodore gets the message, but it seems Sarpino as well. The diplomat from Valdargent turns the conversation on its head by proposing that Montari Entreprises is willing to fund the entirety of future development while still agreeing to the current split of return. The only thing that they ask is that they are given full permission to build as they see fit. Jupus questions at such a generous deal, to which Sarpina replies that Montari Entreprises sees deep potential in the settlement, one that will certainly make all three parties involved quite prosperous. Jupus suddenly reverses his concerns and jumps on board with the idea when Sarpino explains their plans to make Arbor's Rest a trade hub, and that means constructing ports for both air and water travel. After some additional discussion, and some reluctance on Duloup's part, the Arbor's Rest Committee comes to an agreement to allow Montari Entreprises free reign to develop the settlement with their own funds while all three parties continue to benefit from the settlement's profits. The agreement can be reviewed in 5 years time. As a gesture of generosity, Arina also shared the details regarding their deal with the Sotillini Cartel, inferring that Mariporta is hurting for resources and personnel, weaknesses that Montari Entreprises may benefit from.
- The next day, the Sootscale delegation arrives. With Samska's arrival, Osla convenes a meeting together with the religious delegates to discuss the blessing for the opening ceremony, and the specialized daily blessing. Before they had arrived, Sky gave Arina a report on his investigation on the Sootscale magister Carsca. Sky does not think that she has much potential, but might be trainable, and might be a good diversity pick. Arina, face in palms, is quick to acknowledge the report and end this meeting as soon as possible; as ever, Sky appreciates how Arina is so considerate of his time and the efficiency of her meetings with him. Soon after, the last of the delegations, Drelevo's, arrives in Promethea.

- **Alcina Sotillini:** Wants to discuss Prateria's military situation as Sotillini has noticed the mass influx of former soldiers flocking to Argus' and Davona's banners. Sotillini controls the bulk of the mercenary trade from Lurindor, and wants to gauge how Prateria will impact on that industry. However, Mariporta is always in need of material, raw or otherwise produced, and if Prateria is able to provide that need, then Mariporta is willing to tolerate any competition to its primary industry.
- **Montari & D'onofrio:** Montari is interested in increasing Montari presence in Prateria. He offers to fund additional buildings in Arbor's Rest in exchange for receving Loyalty bonuses.
