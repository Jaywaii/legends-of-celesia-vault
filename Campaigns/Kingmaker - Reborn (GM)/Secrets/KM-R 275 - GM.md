## Roth-Teghan

The gnoph-keh were an experimental race created by the thul-koon during the 2nd age, and became near extinct going into the 3rd age. However, they are extremely long-lived and can easily impregnate each other. Individuals still persist in the cold polar regions of Inaktivut and Tunakuraq, though they are few and far between. The gnoph-keh primarily worship Yin-Zaggoth.
- An ancient ruined city of gnoph-keh in Tunakuraq
- ARS doesn't have a huge presence on Tunakuraq, just a small lodge for research (Chapter-Sage Amaruqiel Wintersong (female elf); intellectual, secure) who also maintain relations with local Tunakuraqi nomads (Nanukhir elves, descendants of Nanukhil [female snow elf] hero of the 2nd Age). The lodge is located in the port town of Kuvyiak.
- The seasonal snow storms make tracking and mapping extremely difficult, even with the help of experienced local trackers
- inspired by 'At the Mountains of Madness'
- research in the AR archives refer to a collection of books called *The Onidawik Journals*, research journals of archeologist Onidawik from Anowakowa. the AR research informs that Onidawik was the foremost expert on Roth-Teghan in the past 4000 years.
- Within Roth-Teghan, a single gnoph-keh survives. A priest of Yin-zaggoth, this gnoph-keh magically hibernated for millenia while his people died out, and was only awoken when the Evergreen Company activated Ing-gol'zha.
- Within Roth-Teghan, the walls are covered with carved hieroglyphics that, once deciphered, recount:
	- the creation of the gnoph-keh by giant alien worms
	- the worship of Yin-Zaggoth
- In order to gain the gnoph-keh's ecstasy, the PCs must capture the emotion from the ancient creature.
	- trick gnoph-keh priest into a state of stupor by manipulating the architecture of Roth-Teghan
	- engage in psychic duel with ghoph-keh priest
	- reawaken past memory within the gnoph-keh priest of their greatest connection to Yin-Zaggoth
	- survive the horrors of such a close connection to the cosmic horror
	- capture the priest's ecstasy within an appropriate vessel
