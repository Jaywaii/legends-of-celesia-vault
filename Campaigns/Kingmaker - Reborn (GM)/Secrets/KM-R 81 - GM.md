# Notes

- In Ferrocitta, Osla and Tabrir met with the Teredoro Consortium's Magister, Alessandra. Tabrir managed to smooth talk her, and she offered the *scroll of limited wish* for a sum of 10,000 gp. Neither Osla nor Tabrir knew the true value of the scroll or of money in general, so they agreed to the price. Acquiring horses, they made the trip back to Promethea.
- Arina toured Lurindor, looking for investments to fund a tournament between the 3 colonies of Prateria, Regno Drelevo, and Groundeloup. She garnered interest from the Churches of Karthamor, Kimylia, Phaenya, Lucial, and Kaï-den. While at the church of Kaï-den, Arina learned that Sunmother Joliette Beaucial and Sir Thoros Brighthammer had already departed to visit Prateria.
- Jupus, having received no reply to his letters to his family in Rochefort, consulted with Cethin and the bedridden Tim, and asked them to send a team of spies into Rochefort to look into his family's whereabouts.
- Upon Osla and Tabrir's return, Jupus was flabbergasted with how they agreed to the scroll's price. After getting over that, the group collected some of the magical gear they've accumulated and planned on liquidating for gold anyways. Tabrir, Osla, and Jupus would head back to Ferrocitta to purchase the scroll. Sky returned to town just in time to join the group. He *teleported* them to Rochefort, where they acquired horses to ride towards Ferrocitta.
- The transaction for the scroll proceeded smoothly, with Jupus sweetening the deal with talk about sharing some Promethean Pink with the ever drinking Alessandra. The group later spent the day familiarizing themselves with Ferrocitta before teleporting back to Promethea.
- Sky successfully cast the *limited wish* and removed the *geas* from Tim. When Tim returned home, he was met with a shadow fey noble, who warned him that he had offended the Fellnight Queen, and she does not forgive or forget. The fairy then disappeared into the shadows.

## Ferrocitta

- Tabrir and Osla meet with Alessandra Teredoro (NE female human wizard 13) in her caster's tower. Alessandra was unwilling to meet with the PCs, but she owed Carlo a favor and hated to be in his debt. She is brusque and rude, and immediately demands 10,000 gp in exchange for the scroll. She can be negotiated lower down, but she will require a favor: retrieval of a work of art. Alessandra's hated rival Dario Dinovio of the Dinovio Trade Group of Santo Venito had it commissioned and liked to show it off. Alessandra doesn't care about the work of art, but only wishes to see her rival deprived of it. She also doesn't how the PCs retrieve the item or what it costs them, or even if they simply destroy it. Dario is unwilling to part with the item. It is a diamond statuette of a young woman he had loved in his youth who had long ago disappeared. Dario is selfish and cunning.

## The Gardeforts

- Jupus' missives to his family were unanswered for some time. This is because his remainigfamily were forced to move from location to location as they were vagrants. Even if they did reach them, the Gardeforts are very bitter towards Johan for abandoning them.
- The Gardeforts will become even more pissed when they learn that Johan is Jupus. Not only had he founded his own nation and wealth for over a year, he even came back to Rochefort and never bothered to seek them out.
- The Gardeforts last known residence was a rental house in the Slums of Rochefort. They have since been evicted and moved from homeless shelter to homeless shelter. They now reside in a ramshackle on the outskirts of Rochefort, alongside the other destitutes of the city.
- News from Lurindor doesn't arrive regularly. Rather, it is  through word of mouth as travelers and merchant move back and forth between the two nations. Normally, the news is trivial; changes in prices, celebrity gossip. One recent piece of news is getting particular attention. Word has it that a disgraced merchant was found dead in his prison cell, by apparent suicide. That bit of news was popular among the lower class who love to see arrogant merchants get their comeuppance. Sadly, the merchant in question was Berendikoff Gardefort.
- The investigation into Berendikoff's death was headed up by Rochefort City Guard investigator Sabine Duroche (female halfling investigator 4). However, Sabine was paid off anonymously to speed up the investigation, and steer its conclusions towards Berendikoff having committed suicide. Sabine's family were failed merchants, and heavily in debt. She was paid half of the debt amount up front by a human woman with red hair and a mole on her cheek (a disguised Carmella Lupo).
- Berendikoff was actually killed by fellow prisoners with a few guards paid by Lupo to turn a blind eye towards the act.
- Months prior, Altziferro Prison records indicate that Berendikoff had been meeting frequently with his lawyer Madeline Dambrose and a male gnome identified as Vrickgarp Goldfarm. They would meet at least once a week, sometimes as frequently as four times as week.
