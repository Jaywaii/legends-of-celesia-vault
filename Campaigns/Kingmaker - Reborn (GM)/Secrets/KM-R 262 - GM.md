## Dagorrad Marshes

The whispers from will-o-wisps are collected in a massive effigy in a ruins deep in the Dagorrad Marshes. In this ruined temple, at its center is a raised dais wrapped in massive tentacles that reach up 60 feet in the air. These tentacles seem to originate from within a moat filled with black tar that encircles the dais.  Up north is another dais surrounded by green mist rising from a carpet of bones.
survive skill challenge to capture whisper
1. Every round, PCs in the temple area are assaulted by Babbling Whispers. These whispers cannot be taken by the dreamcatcher as they are too scrambled together to extract. The babbling drives people insane. The longer a person is in the area, the more harder it is to resist the insanity.
	1. Babbling Whispers: Will DC 30 vs. 1d8 Sanity dmg + confusion for 1 round; success negates the confusion and halves the san dmg. Increase DC by 2 every consecutive round spent in the fog.
1. outer temple surrounded by a ring fog
	1. Fog of Whispers: Survival DC 30 or Perception DC 40.
1. Chanting: PCs must chant in sync with the whispers. Each round the chant is successful gives a +2 bonus to another PC's skill check on another part of the ritual or resist the whispers.
	1. Understanding: Linguistic DC 30 to understand the whispers is needed first before chanting. Increase DC by 2 after each Linguisitic or Perform failure
	1. Chanting: Perform (sing) DC 30. Each round increases the DC by 2. Each round the chanting is successful, the tentacles unwrap to reveal the colossal effigy.
1. Writhing tentacles surround the effigy that smack down any who attempt to approach the central effigy. The tentacles can be taken down, but a new one shows up the next round.
	1. Tentacle: 100 hp, DV 45, DR 20/armor, atk +40, 6d4+40
1. Once unwrapped, a PC needs to stand before the effigy and convince it to release a whisper.
	1. Diplomacy DC 45: on success, the effigy breathes a wisp of glowing smoke, this is the whisper. On the next round, anyone can try to catch it, make atk w/ bab + Dex vs. DV 40 to catch. The whisper last for 1d4 rds, and DV goes up by 2 each round.

## Awakening

Upon awakening, the party is met with surprise by the staff nursing them. First was Yujimbo who awoke first before the others. They then receive the sendings from the rest of the team. As they reply, the rest of the party starts waking up. The party finds themselves in an emaciated state from not being physically active. If checked, all of the components they acquired now appear in the physical waking world in their dreamcatchers. After getting freshened up, the party meets with Chapter-Sage Joliette Beauchamp. She informs them that 3 years have passed since they first entered the Dreamlands (Team 13 entered the Dreamlands on Vandarkaï 1st 110, and the day they exit is Zhaoren 27th 113).

Joliette informs Team 13 that Hushglow narrowly avoided being embroiled in a war between Prateria and the troll kingdom Klorgfang. The site remains secured by Rose Scholar Thonra 'Mindshield', however, jurisdiction has been transferred to the newly established Promethea Chapter under the newly-elected Chapter-Sage Asako Hino. Throughout the 3 years, there had been very little activity coming forth from below Hushglow, but neither did Thonra allow anyone to enter below. The Hushglow expedition was mostly occupied in turning away any curious people, marking several of them as potential cultists. Sometimes, the expedition would have to fight off monsters roving too close to the site. The frequency of travelers and monsters have been steadily increasing over these 3 years. Prateria expanded to claim the territory around Hushglow, and in Asmolan 112, the nation started building a new settlement on the site, incorporating the expedition's base into it.

## Groves of Nobu-Shugath

The dark young of Xin'carlath are a unique intelligent spawn of the Mother of All Horrors. However, autopsies of dark young samples have not revealed any organ within their alien structure that could be construed as a heart. Only in the dreaded forests of the Breeding Groves of Nobu-Shugath can you find a dark young with a heart that can be claimed.

## Roth-Teghan

The gnoph-keh were an experimental race created by the thul-koon during the 2nd age, and became near extinct going into the 3rd age. However, they are extremely long-lived and can easily impregnate each other. Individuals still persist in the cold polar regions of Inaktivut and Tunakuraq, though they are few and far between. The gnoph-keh primarily worship Yin-Zaggoth.
