# Notes

- Morrigan, Igvald, Yujimbo, Tao
- Gnoph-keh priest psychic duel (CR 19): 20,000 XP
- Retrieved gnoph-keh ecstasy (CR 9) x 4: 20,000 XP
- Total: 40,000 XP / 4 = 10,000 XP each.
- To level 10: 113,380 / 385,000 XP.

- PCs will need to enter into a [psychic duel](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/alternative-rule-systems/occult-adventures/occult-rules/psychic-duels/) with the gnoph-keh.
	- PCs need to have the gnoph-keh experience ecstasy by making it believe that it had recreated Yin-zaggoth's miracle of creating a new gnoph-keh.
		- Gnoph-keh conviction: 556 hp; DV 25; +45
			1. Doubt (direct attack): in the form of failed abominations.
			1. Expose Flaws (debuff attack): exposing the flaws in the PCs' attacks; +1 disadvantage on enemies.
			1. Sow Doubt (debuff defense): overwhelm PCs mental with madness; decrease PC DV by 1d4 per 10.
			1. Self-doubt (conviction heal): gnoph-keh's past selves humbling it; heal conviction by 1d4 per 10.
			1. Self-reflection (defense buff): gnoph-keh goes into a monologue about its failings and lessons learned; increase DV by 1d4 per 10.
			1. Enraged (attack buff): gnoph-keh's past frustration sends it into a rage; +1 advantage on all checks
	- PCs will need to battle the gnoph-keh's doubts and mental safeguards while also creating a dream scenario for the ecstasy.
		- Bluff
		- Oratory
		- Illusion
		- Enchantment
