# Notes

- Evergreen Company, now joined by Orok, begin planning their next move. They interrogate Capt. Kasmut and Lt. Taia to learn what they know about Magistrate Khemrif, General Tabira, Prime Eye Nubiyet, and Prime Fist Harsendes. They learn about where they reside, their daily habits, their armaments. They also learn the names of most of the empire's leadership.
- Cortez contacts Arina to find out what happened to the team. After debriefing each other, Cortez informs Promethea that  as soon as Orok's resistance cell evacuated from Ashak, Amonkhaten launched a new offensive against the Free Ashaki League, now that he doesn't have to devote effort and personnel towards a major resistance force that is no longer active. A day after, the Caliphate of Kahadun and the Empire of Arsa denounce the empire's actions and promise support to the League. Cortez is tasked with getting back into Jafarkhet and to find out if Maalek's friend Shamira is safe. Cortez reassures them that he will be good on his own as much of the Ashaki's authorities are now focused on the new conflicts.
- Orok is given funds to by Sky and the Company to the tune of two hundred thousand gold to acquire new gear as recompense for getting his resistance group into so much trouble.
- Possible next steps involve kidnapping Nubiyet, digging underneath the city, infiltrating the Amonkhatenite Fortress compound, pretending to be military forces transferring to Jafarkhet, allying with Arsa or Kahadun, even pretending to offer Sky and Davona as prisoners so they could trace them to Sand Rock.
- The Company develops a new plan to have Shamira purchase a building to run as an inn. They plan on bribing an unscrupulous guard captain to avoid suspicion how a simple waitress manages to purchase land. They get the name of such a captain from Kasmut. Once they get the building, they will use it as justification for appearing as travelers in the city while they scout for this invisible prison transport to Sand Rock.
