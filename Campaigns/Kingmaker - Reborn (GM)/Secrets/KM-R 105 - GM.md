# Notes

- Dalaster of Westgate (CR 9): Rex, Igvald, Agna (2,133 XP)

- After destroying the undead ghouls of former Argent Rose agents "Diamond-Eye" Connornack and Garska Drumsong, Team 13 decides to split the group for a short while. Raggi and Jericho will remain at the Gate of Radiance to prepare the remains of the deceased agents for transportation, while Agna, Igvald, and Rex will scout further into the necropolis. After following a few short, twisting paths, the scout team discovers a chamber with a central, alien-looking spire, and a clump of dessicated bones with several still-intact items strewn around it.
- When the agents take a closer look at the remains, slabs of calcified stone slammed down in each of the chamber's entryways, sealing them in. A haunting, howling screech rips through the air, and a whirlwind of dust and debris whips up from above the skeletal remains. Chunks of stone are ripped from the central spire by an unseen force, and slam into Rex and Igvald. There is another horrific scream, and in a flash of eerie light, a floating, ghostly apparition of a skeleton dressed in a torn robe suddenly appears above the skeletal remains. *"Connornaaaack... Garskaaaa"* it groans before shrieking once more and vanishing from sight. Agna succumbsits unnatural fright, and it cracks her mind. She comes to the sudden realization that the entire undeground city is alive, and out to devour them.
- Rex recognizes the apparition. It is a poltergeist, an incorporeal and mostly invisible undead. Fortunately, Team 13 are skilled enough to pick up on the slight displacement of dust and air to have an idea of where it is, and their weapons are are magically enhanced. Though they miss just as often, the three Argent Rose agents manage to land blows on the poltergeist. Agna is able work around her fear of the undead to fire a blind electric blast that suceeds in destroying the undead. However, Rex warns that a poltergeist will return in several days unless put at peace.
- The team collects the posessions around the skeletal remains. Thanks to a wand and spellbook being labeled, they discover that these are the belongings of Dalaster of Westgate, an Argent Rose Knight that was part of the Firesong Expedition. The three rejoins Raggi and Jericho at the Gate of Radiance, and they collect the remains of Connornack and Garska. While Agna is still deluded that Ing-gol'zha seeks to devour them, Team 13 succeeds at getting back to the surface, and return to Promethea to put the remains of the dead Argent Rose agents to rest and to heal their maladies.

- Dalaster of Westgate was killed and devoured by Connornack and Drumsong. When the PCs enter the room, they find a dessicated set of bones in worn rags, with several objects littered around it. When the PCs approach, the doors slam shut, and Dalaster's poltergeist attacks.
