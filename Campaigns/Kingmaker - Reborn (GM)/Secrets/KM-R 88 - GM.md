## Prateria Events

### Nouyan 110

Sensational Crime (Settlement, Continuous): A serial killer, arsonist, or daring bandit plagues your nation. The Marshal and the Warden must each attempt a Stability check, adding the settlement's Law and subtracting its Crime. If you succeed at both checks, the criminal is caught before your nation takes any penalties from the event. If you fail at one, the criminal escapes, Unrest increases by 1, and the event is continuous. If you fail at both, the criminal makes a fool of the authorities; Law and Loyalty decrease by 1, Treasury decreases by 1d4 BP, Unrest increases by 2, and the event is continuous.

*Murder in Promethea! Among the squatting camp folk, a young human serving girl by the name of Saki is found dead, brutally mutilated. Her body was found in the morning, and she was last seen the evening before heading out on the outskirts of the settlement's border to collect berries. While her death is investigated, the next morning, another body is found. A young halfling shepard boy by the name of Beven is found to be killed in a similar manner as Saki. Beven was last seen the day before taking his flock out, and was supposed to return at night. Indeed, his body was found near the outskirts of town, along with a half dozen multilate sheep. All of the bodies appear to be partially eaten. The townsfolk begin to start hurling accusations against the orcs.*

If the Warden's Stability check is successful, she manages to break up any fights between the folks and the orcs before things get serious. If the Marshal and Warden checks succeed, an orc by the name of Kundal is arrested. After some hasty investigations, Davona's guards discovered that Kundal had been acting strangely in the past several days, and disappeared from everyone's view during the past two nights. His tent was searched, and personal effects belonging to Saki was found (an earring splashed with blood).

If the PCs investigate further, it is revealed that Kundal had contracted lycanthropy, some time after the Rughir tribe was destroyed. Apparently Kundal had betrayed the Ulfeddin's teachings through cowardice and betrayal, and kept this complete secret. While on their exodus, it was easy for Kundal to find excuses to be away from the rest of his tribe during the full moon. Only now that he is in close proximity of so many people that he ended up killing someone other than mere animals.

PCs can imprison, execute, cure, exile, or a combination of any of the above. Depending on their judgement, they will need to make a Loyalty check (success results in 1 Unrest, failure in 1d4+1), and risks offending either their people or the Rughirs.

### Kaïshan 111

Improvement Demand (hex): This event is identical to the building demand event, but the citizens want the construction or destruction of a terrain improvement in the hex. If the demand is not met by the next Event Phase, Unrest increases by 1. Alternatively, you can suppress the citizens’ demands and negate the event by succeeding at a Loyalty check, but this reduces Loyalty by 2 for 1 year and increases Unrest by 1.

### Firhen 111

*The flow of new settlers would not be stemmed this month. More followers of the Evergreen Company came to join their leaders in Promethea. However, there was still a lack of housing, forcing many to seek shelter by squatting the land around the settlement, interfering with additional construction.*

Unable to build new buildings in Promethea; Fame and Stability decrease by 1 for 1 year, and Unrest increases by 2. You may try to disperse the squatters with a Stability check. Success means the squatters are dispersed, the penalties are removed, and the event is not continuous. However, if a House or Tenement is not built by the next Event Phase, Infamy increases by 1 for 1 year and Unrest by 2. Failing the Stability check means the event is continuous, and you may not construct new buildings in that settlement until the event is resolved.

Plague (Hex or Settlement, Continuous): A deadly sickness strikes the target hex or settlement. You cannot construct terrain improvements or buildings there while plague persists. Attempt two Stability checks, each with a penalty equal to the number of Brothels, Foreign Quarters, Highways, Inns, Piers, Roads, Stables, Stockyards, Tenements, and Waterfronts in the hex, and a bonus equal to the number of ss, Cathedrals, Herbalists, Hospitals, and Temples in the hex. If you succeed at both checks, the event ends, but Stability decreases by 2 for 1 year and Treasury by 1d3 BP. If you fail at one check, Stability decreases by 4 for 1 year, Treasury decreases by 1d6 BP, and Unrest increases by 1d3. If you fail at both, Stability decreases by 4 for 1 year, Treasury decreases by 1d6 BP, Unrest increases by 1d6, and in the next Event Phase the plague spreads to an adjacent hex.
