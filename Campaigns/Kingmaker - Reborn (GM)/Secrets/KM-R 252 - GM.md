## Blooming Fields of Mykariota

The Blooming Fields of Mykariota has the appearance of a grand garden court. Trimmed bushes form walls. Carefully grown tree trunks and roots form tables and chairs. Slender trees curve and wind their branches together to form gazebos. Over a hundred figures can be readily seen milling about the fields, engaging in discussions, dances, drinking and dining. All of them appear tall and lanky, dressed in fine clothing, and most are wearing multicolored caps on their heads. Their gender is not easily discernible.
- These individuals are the remnant persona of people consumed by a fungal bloom on Midgard. This town was infected  by an unnaturally aggressive fungal colony that infected them with its spores at night.
