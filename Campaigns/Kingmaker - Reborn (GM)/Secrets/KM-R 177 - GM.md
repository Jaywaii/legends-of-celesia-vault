## The Gardefort Case

### TRA Branch Office

*Rochefort technically has its own police force, the Rochefort Guard, which is nomincally a private security force employed by House Châteaufoy. However, since Rochefort is part of the Lurindor Combine, its territory also falss under the jurisdiction of the Lurindor Trade Regulation Authority. As such, the TRA, or 'Regulators', maintain an office branch in Rochefort. As the city is also a capital of a trade territory and a bustling trade hub in  its own right, the Rochefort Branch Office has been significantly built up, with facilities and staff numbers second only to the head office in Coracentia. It is a multistoried building. Its main entrance is a large, professionally decorated lobby with a large front desk with an officer behind it, and there are several other officers standing by doors or dealing with paperwork.*

### Rochefort Guard

*The Rochefort Guard has the responsibility to protect the safety of the Rochefort citizenry, be the first responders to any dangers, investigate breaches of Rochefort laws.  It's no secret that they chafe against the greater jurisdiction of the TRA. While the TRA is primarily responsible for investigating breaches to trade contracts, corporate espionage or sabotage, and ensuring that Lurindor policies are upheld. However, the wording of their mandate, backed by the TRA courts and the wealth and influence of the top Trade Houses, allow the TRA to pretty much declare almost any case within their jurisdiction. Thus, the Guard has made it a habit to try to keep events as low-key as possible so as to be as beneath the TRA's notice as much as possible.*
