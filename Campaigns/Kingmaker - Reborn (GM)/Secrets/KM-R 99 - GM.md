# Notes

- Minor Sway: Alcina Sotillini, Sarpino Montari
- Major Sway: Adêline Châteaufoy, Theodore Duloup, Sootscale
- Penalties: -2 from the halfling population for not inviting Tierra

- Arina meets with King Sootscale in private to discuss the kobold's expansion plans. They come into an agreement that Prateria will leave room for the kobolds to expand, though that does not seem likely any time soon. They do reaffirm continuing to build good relationships between the Sootscale tribe and the people of Prateria. The council and the Sootscale delegation later have dinner together that evening at the *Nomad*. Dinner discussion focused mainly on updating each other of the developments in their respective regions. Of particular note, and forewarned by Sootscale, the kobold champions devour nearly the entire stock of the mobile restaurant, packing in far more than their small frames would imply.
- The Drelevona delegation arrives that afternoon. Self-styled Baron Rafaelo Drelevo greets the Praterian Council with the required diplomacy, though his wife is far more vocal of her opinions. Drelevo makes a show of smoothing things over, but the greeting is thankfully short. Arina sets up a meeting with Drelevo at a later date. Drelevo's appearance, while still primp and proper, does seem to bear the telltale signs of being affected by life on the wild frontier, more tanned and rugged.
- Osla oversees a many hours-long meeting between her and all the representatives of the different invited faiths. The discussion is about how to form an appropriate opening ceremonial blessing that includes all their differences. It gets heated at times, but Osla successfully mediates the arguments and keeps things calm and civil. They succeed in creating their ceremonial blessing, and Osla concludes the meeting with arranging the schedule for the daily prayers throughout the week of games.
- In the evening, Argus and Davona get a report from their people. The only thing to note is that their is a heavy amount of activity in town, and a lot of people moving around. It is more that they expected, and it is straining their current security organization, but no trouble has been reported so far.
- The next day is the opening ceremony of the Viridian Games. A long parade to show off each delegation's leaders and athletes, and Prateria's guards and soldiers, wind through the streets of Promethea. They finally arrive at the centre of town, at the Great Cypress in Memorial Park. They dyrads dance and play music at their arrival. The area is crowded with athletes, dignitaries, security personnel, and those fortunate Promethean lucky enough to find space. Arina gives a grand speech to open the games to everyone's acclaim. Osla leads the priests in the ceremonial blessing, encircling the Great Cypress, with each priest reciting part of the blessing in turn. The crowd is moved by the holy words, and cheer as the Games are offcially started. Vendors and carnival games are open throughout the streets. Music fills the air, along with the smell of delicious, warm food drinks. While the athletes participate in the festivities and perform demonstrations of their prowess, the dignitaries split off into smaller groups to conduct meetings and business on their own.
