# Notes

- Defeated Old Gnash: 4,800 XP (Arina, Argus, Davona, Ayaki, Sky, Tabrir, Tobias, Jupus)
- Cohorts: 3,840 XP (Rialla, Asako)
- 1 FATE point

- The Evergreen Company found Old Gnash and defeated her. Arina put *Warlock's Stride* and Sky formed an *wall of ice* in her way. Argus was enlarged, and after dealing a powerful blow to her, he wrestled her. Davona leapt at Old Gnash from a distance, and slammed the blunt side of her greatsword onto the razor boar's already bruised head, and knocked her out.
- Sky determined that a permanent *rage* spell was enchanted on her. Tabrir casted *calm emotions* on her, and his spiritual might broke through the enchantment, curing the beast of her madness. Tabrir  healed the beast, and when she woke, Jupus leapt forward. With a few friendly words and animal sounds, the gnome managed to assuage the razor boar and convey the group's friendliness. Old Gnash understood, and lumbered off into the woods.
- After she departed, The Northern Erlking and his retinue galloped towards the Evergreen Company. He congratulated them on their vicotry, and thanked them for sparing her life. He informed them that the theft of the Central Heart of the Forest woke Old Gnash from her hibernation, and the Old Arborfane channeled her anger through her. The Erlking told the Evergreen Company that their actions have proven their worth, and that he prays that the rest of their nation follow their example. As thanks, he promised them that their path to the Northern Heart of the Forest will be direct and free from any threats. He and his retinue all bowed to them, then departed, leaving the Company to rest.

## Expedition to the Northern Heart

### Seeking a Guide

The Erlking of North Arborfane, along with a hunting party of alseid rangers, confronts the PCs. The PCs must bargain with him to gain passage, and can also gain a guide to the Heart of the Forest.
- *There is a commotion throughout the forest. A hunting horn blows in the distance. The sounds of hooves slamming into the ground approach your position. The approaching figures first appear as deers, but as they come into view, they reveal themselves to not be deers, but actually tauric creatures with a lower body of that of a deer, and an upper torso of a wood elf with antlers on their brow. However, the largest figure leading the herd was quite different. A regal elf lord, cloaked in wings of autumn leaves, rides upon a great deer. He, too, bears a pair of large antlers upon his brow. The elf-deers surround you, drawing their bows upon you. The antlered elf lord speaks in an echoey voice: "I, the Erlking of North Arborfane, demand to know where you mortals intend to go. The Old Growth may have allowed to pass her borders, but I guard the path you find yourselves upon. To cross me is to cross the Old Arborfane."*
- The PCs will need to satisfy the Erlking's question, by Diplomacy or Bluffing. Failure results in the Erlking demanding that they leave the Old Arborfane, escorted by the alseid. If the PCs resist, they will be attacked, by both the Erlking's herd and the Old Arborfane. If the PCs answer the Erlking satisfactorily, he nonetheless requires them to complete a task. *"Your answer is satisfactory, but I still cannot let you pass freely. A toll must be paid, one in the form of a task. Old Gnash has recently awoken, and in a terrible mood. In her rage, she is causing unnecessary destruction. Find her and end her swath of destruction, and we shall see if you are worthy of moving further within the Old Arborfane."*
- [[Old Gnash]] is an advanced razor boar, centuries old. She periodically hibernates for years before waking in a terrible mood. However, this time, her mood is further worsened by outside means, caused by the Stag Lord's theft of the central Heart of the Forest recently.
	- If the PCs kill Old Gnash, the Erlking allows them to pass, but is disappointed. He will forewarn the zmey of the PC's approach, and ensures that the PCs path is fraught with danger, increasing encounters and the Suvival DC to navigate the forest. He tells the PCs that the Path of the Tree-Folk will lead the to the heart, which is true. However, the Old Arborfane's anger has mutated the treants into [[gnarlwoods]]. Six of them will ambush the PCs.
	- If the PCs succeed in subduing Old Gnash and calming her, the Erlking is impressed. He warns the PCs of the zmey [[Drazekmir]] guarding the Heart, and pacifies the path to it, allowing the party to travel at normal forest speed. Old Gnash is affected by a permanent *rage* spell. It can be dispelled as normal with *dispel magic* or countered with *calm emotions* (requires a successful caster level check, otherwise the rage comes back after the spell expires). It can also be removed by performing a ritual, but it will require Old Gnash to remain still for the duration of the casting. The *rage* spell has a 20th-level caster level, and the caster level check DC is 31.

### A Lull in the Wild

**School** enchantment (compulsion) [emotion, mind-affecting]; **Level**
**Casting Time**
**Components** V, S, M (poppy flowers), **SC** (up to 4)
**Skill Checks** Heal, Knowledge (nature)
**Range** touch
**Target** one creature
**Duration** instantaneous; see text
**Saving Throw** ; **Spell Resistance**
**Backlash** The primary caster is exhausted.
**Failure** All casters are rendered unconscious for 1d4 days.
**Effect** All emotion-based effects are removed from the target. The target's attitude to the casters and their allies becomes indifferent for 24 hours.
