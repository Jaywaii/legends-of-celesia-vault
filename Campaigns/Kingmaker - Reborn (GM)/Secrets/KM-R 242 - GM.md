# Notes

- SW begin fighting Silvermane centaurs at gathering site. Getting badly hurt, they surrender. The centaurs would have killed them then and there, but another group of Silvermane centaurs interrupted the warriors. A young woman leading this second group ordered them to stop. They tied the SW's hands together, and the young woman gave them one chance to speak in their defence before they are executed.
- SW tried to explain that they only just arrived, that these deaths were not done by them, but the leader did not believe them. Even Zacc telling her that Thassi was Duloup's niece, but that made things worse. The young elader pointed as one of the slain centaur women, stating that she was her aunt, so it would be poetic justice to return Thassi's head to her uncle. They dumped the bodies of the dead halfling soldiers in front of them.
- That was when the SW noticed something strange about the bodies. Thassi saw that their uniforms were worn and badly made. Even the Grondeloup emblem was made with mistakes. Zacc noticed that the halflings were emaciated, and covered in old wounds that have healed over many years. Some of them even look like scars from whipping. These halflings were not fit to be soldiers, might even be slaves. The centaur leader rebutted that it merely proved that the two-leggers are forced to use leftover equipment and have no more good soldiers. However, they got the leader's attention when they bring up the fact that their wounds are too deep and large to have been done by elderly women and children.
- The centaur leader examined the halfling soldiers. Then she went to check her slain kin. Afterwards, she ordered the SW to be tied together, to gather their kin, and to go back to their tribe for judgment by the Chief. After several hours of harsh trekking as many of the SW were still badly injured, they are brought into the camp. After the young leader went into the main pavilion, she came out with an older Silvermane woman. She introduced herself as Andrastreia, Chief of the Silvermane. She would order the SW execution, but her daughter Iolanthe told her that there are suspicious circumstances. Therefore, the SW will be kept prisoner while the tribe's council convenes, and deliver their decision in the morning.

- One female centaur elder is barely conscious. If healed, after the battle, she confirms that Giovanni spoke to them asking them about the totems. They told him that was a sacred and dangerous path, but Giovanni insisted. He seemed almost obsessed about it, and let slip that he dreamed that the discovery of the bow required him to follow a path of totems. The elder said that he could gain safe passage to the sacred path if he succeeds at a Trial of Worthiness. If the PCs wish to follow the same path, they must speak with their Chieftess.
