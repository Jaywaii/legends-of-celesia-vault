# Notes

- After Sky gathers the components needed to exorcize Davik Nettles' haunting, and some extra for backup, he, Argus, Davona, and Asoka travel to Nettles' Crossing. They confront Nettles' undead corpse. Argus literally disarms Nettles, and pins him to the ground. As Argus holds him in place, Sky places the candles around Nettles' head, ignoring his gnashing and threats. Sky lights the candles, recites the exorcism chant from the lexicon for forty minutes. At the end of the litany, he pours a glass of wine into Davik's mouth, then himself. Sitting cross-legged next to Davik's head, the sylph haruspex touches Davik's forehead, and insteantly the two become still as their consciounesses are flung into their own separate domain.
- Sky sees that the mindscape resembles their original location in the physical world, but far more violent and morbid. The waters of the Greenbelt churn violently, the grass is somber, and the sky is obscures by a thick blanked of dark clouds with lightning and thunder crashing overhead. Before him stands the undead form of Davik. However, instead of the decrepit, malformed shape that he was in the physical world, here in the mindscape, Davika stands tall and thick, emanating a palpable menacing aura.
- Sky immediately manifests mental defences, and fires a mental projection of a *disintegration* spell at Davik. The green beam hits Davik in his chest, and there is a flash of green light. However, once the light fades, Nettles' remains standing firm, untouched by the spell. In response, Nettles' raises his hands, and behind him, an enormous wave of water rises up from the Greenbelt, and smashes down onto Sky. In the physical world, Sky's still, sitting form suddently coughs up blood. Asako immediately recites a healing spell to offset the physical harm that the psychic duel is inflicting on her friend.
- Sky retaliates by spending his *guards and wards* spell. Magical glyphs appear and surround Davik, binding him in place, then launching him upwards. Etheral armored guards appear in the air, continously slashing at Davik, and then smash him into the ground. Davik stands, pieces of his body burning up into wisps of blue psychic flames. He snarls, and stretches his arm and grip towards the air above Sky. A shadow suddenly casts over the wizard. Sky looks up, and an enormous bascule bridge has appeared directly above. With a roar, Davik clenches his fist, and the bridge slams down onto Sky, breaking his bones in the physical world.
- Sky then manifests a mental projection of an *empowered piercing fireball* at Davik. The undead haunt is blown away by the explosion, and lands in a splash in the river, disappearing under its churning surface. An instant later, Nettle's is launched into the air by a jet of water. He raises his ranseur towards teh thundering sky, and a bolt of lightning strikes it. Nettles' then launches it at Sky at the speed of light. Sky is only able to raise his arm to intercept the projectile before it tears right through him. In the physical world, the bones in his forearm and his sternum explode. Asako is shocked and splattered by Sky's blood. Quickly, she recites another healing spell on him as he collapses backwards, practically dead. However, thanks to Asako's quick action keeps him clinging to life, ever so barely. Davik begins laughing, cursing Sky before dissolving into water that trickles back down into the Greenbelt.
- Sky is able to recover thanks to Asako's power, but he is shaken by his near death. The group return to Promethea to inform the rest of the Company of what happened. Much to Sky's horror, he discovers that he now has the same *nightmare* affliction that Davona suffers, and is now weakened even further from being unable to properly rest. The Company focuses on their next attempt to exorcize Davik Nettles, for the safety of their realm, and of their companions. They determine that Arina may be their best chance of succeeding.
