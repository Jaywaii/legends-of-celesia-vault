# Notes

- 2 Fate points for Davona and Sky each for Josh writing the letter and Darawesh's death.

## Boons

As long as the PC wears Elyssria's ring, they can call upon a boon once per day. However, each time they call on a boon, they incur a favour from Elyssria. Failure fulfill the favour will curse the PC with disadvantage on a number of d20 rolls equal to 3 + 3 per previous favour unfulfilled.

**Arina**
Somehow help Arina to find Marcelette. Maybe let her know how to recover Louie's memory?

**Argus**
Have the fey help Argus' company by helping him recruit fairy soldiers.

**Ayaki**
Help Ayaki rule her nation (advantage on nation checks).

**Cethin**
Have her fey help Cethin in his spycraft (advantage on Spymaster checks).

**Davona**
Have her fey help Davona on Warden checks.

**Jupus**
Help Jupus uncover who was behind the fall of the Gardefort family.

**Rishoi**
Help Rishoi uncover what fey corrupted her mother.

**Sky**
Help Sky in creating magical works of art, or protect him from the Ashaki Empire.

## Old Beldame

When the PCs revisit Old Beldame with the Heart, she teaches them the ritual to befriend the forest. However, she tells them, that even with the ritual, the native creatures have gotten even more aggressive, and they can expect many attacks. What they can hope for is that the forest herself will not render them eternally loss. The ritual will require all 8 of them to shed blood within the forest.

If the Witch is told of Elyssria and the rituals, she is surprised and impressed. She tells the party that from what she understands of the ritual as they explain them to her, they should do what Elyssria told them it would do.

---

The Ritual of the Heart's Repose does what was promise, but also binds the heart to Elyssria.
