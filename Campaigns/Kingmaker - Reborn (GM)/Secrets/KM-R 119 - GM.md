# Notes

- Defeated squad of forest drakes (UCR 5): Cohors Consul Magica (1,600 XP)
- Defeated company of harpies (UCR 11): Cohors Consul Magica (12,800 XP)

- King Sootscale speaks with the Evergreen Company, expressing his concern of his tiny kingdom being so isolated and on the forefront of the conflict with the trolls. He asks for greater protection from Prateria, something more tangible. With some surprisingly great reluctance on the Company's part, the rulers of Prateria proposes that the Sootscale kingdom become part of Prateria, an offer that King Sootscale accepts, albeit with sadness. The kobold king bitterly knows from his interactions with the Evergreen Company and the people of Prateria that they would do a far better job than he can at growing his people, and that they would keep their word in protecting them. The only consolation is that Sootscale will remain the leader of the Silvermine settlement, and the Evergreen Company will interfere very little in his administration. On Tierlan 9th, 111, Prateria expands its southern border, and Silvermine becomes under the rule of Prateria, with the Sootscale people becoming full-fledged citizens of the nation.
- Steps are taken to equip Cohors Consul Magica with *rings of sustenance* to enable them to recover quickly. However, it will take a week for their rings to become active. In the meantime, Arina leads them on another scouting mission, this time towards the Silent Bastion, southwest of Prateria. The unit is able to scout the Silent Bastion, now garrisoned by a Klorgfang army led by a unit of trolls. Consul Magica then travels east and encounters a squad of forest drakes scouting the area. Consul Magica ambushes and defeats the squad, taking loot and two forest drakes prisoner. However, this battle takes place within the patrol area of Torgard's Horde's harpy scouts. The harpies rally back at the Silent Bastion, and fly off in pursuit. Consul Magica focuses on stealth to evade the harpies, but they have now been active for over twenty hours, with only Arina's encouragement keeping them going. However, finally fatigue overcomes them, and while the second harpy company has yet to find them, their pursuers remain a threat.
- While Arina leads Cohors Consul Magica's scout mission, the rest of the Evergreen Company remains in Silvermine since the ceremony of its joining Prateria. They are overseeing the transition of authority as well as the newly trained reinforced platoon of ramskull-riding kobold hunters. It is at this opportune time that Arzhulig's Horde suddenly arrives marching towards the kobold settlement. Numbering a little over nine hundred troops, the Klorgfang soldiers are armed for battle, ready to pay Prateria back for their loss of harpies.
