# Notes

- Legio III: 67,200/8 = 8,400 XP each unit.

- Legio III, under the command of Consul Arina, takes a novel approach at attacking the Stag Lord's fort, currently occupied by one of Klorgfang's armies. Arina, along with Louis, Rishoi and Rialla, detach from their units, and approach one section of the fort's wall, accompanied by Cohors Consul Magica. Arina orders the rest of Legio III to fall back. Arina's party then begin hammering the fort's wall. Louis fires his pistol with pinpoint accuracy; the defenders on the walls laugh at this supposedly ineffectual attack, not realizing that Louis is hitting the same point over and over. Rishoi blasts the wall with holy light. Rialla telekinetically launches boulders of stone and earth. Arina flashes to and back from the wall, slamming her sword into weakened cracks.
- The Klorgfang forces quickly rally all of its range units to the section of the fort under attack. They rain arrows upon Arina and her party. The hail of projectiles never hits their mark. Arina and Rishoi manifest golden shields of light to intercept the arrows, while Rialla used her telekinesis to simply deflect them. However, concentrated fire on Consul Magica does inflict significant casualties on the unit, even with Rishoi's intense care. Meanwhile, the Klorgfang's melee units sally forth form the fort.
- The concentrated fire from Legio III's command party eventually breaches the fort's wall. Arina then orders Louis to retreat from the field, his role complete. Just in time as the heavy cavalry come swooping down onto their position. The hobgoblin heavy cavalry units make sweeping attacks against Rishoi, but the Crimson Madonna manifests her light shields around her. Shielding her from their attacks, Rishoi is in fact able to use the light shields to herd the horse-riders towards directions of her choosing.
- Rungar leads his trolls and orcs to surround the Praterians. However, their superior numbers are countered by the EVergreen Company's mythical powers and experience. Fighting back-to-back, Rishoi and Rialla take down every troll that comes at them with divine and psionic energies. Cohors Consul Magica ensures they stay down with well-placed *fireballs*, but receive significant casualties when the Klorgfang harpies focus fire on them. Rishoi is able swoop in and heal their injuries, but not soon enough for two of the ten wizards who perish from their wounds. Nonetheless, the exchange is severe enough to cause Rungar and his remaining trolls to flee the battlefield.
- With the flight of their commander, the rest of Rungar's Horde begin to flee. Only the two hobgoblin heavy cavalry units remain defiantly on the field. Though brave, they are swiftly defeated by Arina's party who can now focus solely on them. Rungar's forces flee northwest, but since Arina had Legio II leave the battlefield and fall back, they are in perfect position to intercept them. Arina and company rejoins Legio III, and together, they swiftly defeat the remains of Rungar's army. After a full day of battle, Legio III stands victorious with only two dead, and around two hundred prisoners.
- After the battle, Arina hears Davona call her name. Scrying her and projecting her image at Davona's location, Arina is informed of the reinforced Klorgfang imminently arriving at Legio II's location. Davona also requests that she retrieves the Stag Lord's body from his fort for the issue with Davik Nettles. After directing Legio III to continue escorting the prisoners northward, Arina flies to the fort. She discovers that the Stag Lord's body had remained exactly how they left it after they defeated him years ago, still kneeling with fist pounding the ground. However, the body had now been covered with a layer of rugged bark, enveloped with vines, moss, and blooming flowers. Upon closer inspection, Arina discovers that no bones or flesh remains. What stands before her is a hollow husk in the Stag Lord's form, with a hole in his back where his heart should be. Gathering what remains of the Stag Lord that she can, Arina flies back to Legio III, then gathers Rishoi and Rialla, and all three of them fly on ahead of the army to join Legio II.
