# Notes

- At 1 pm, on Vandarkaï 29th, the battle of Fort Menakedes begins. Arina has the heavy cannon and bombard immediately deployed after dispelling the *shrink item* enchantment upon them. Upon doing so, the entirety of Norsgurd's army makes a sortie out of the fort and immediately make ranged attacks at the siege engines.
- Norsgurd makes a steady advance towards Arina's position, hitting her units with ranged attacks as they approach. Legio III responds in kind, with the siege engines devastating one of Norsgurd's harpy units with deadly accuracy. Damage starts accumulating on both sides, but Norsgurd's units have the numbers to take them, whereas Arina's forces do not, being outnumbered five-to-one.
- Arina has the heavy cannon *shrunked*, but lacks enough power to do the same to the bombard. She begins ordering all of her units to circle around the fort to the north, while the bombard crew is ordered to remain firing at the enemy for as long as possible.
- At 3 pm, Legio II is only three hours of joining Legio III at the fort. However, its scouts notice another large army along the road to the south, heading north. This one is composed of one thousand harpies, four thousand trolls, and four heavy catapults, all mounted on trihorn beasts. At the rate of movement, they will arrive at the fort at 9 pm. Davona uses her *far crystal* to inform this news to Argus, and calls Arina's name to do the same via *clairaudience*. Argus, with Legio I, is held up on the north shore of the Greenbelt. Jupus and his team will not have the bridge completed until after midnight. Only then can Legio I cross the river, and even then, it will take them another 8 hours to reach the fort.
- It is decided that when Legio II arrives, both Praterian legions will head north to join Legio I. Legio I will set up for an ambush in case the enemy decides to follow them north. Meanwhile, Argus flies to Orn Sini to order the 2nd Fusiliers' 1st and 2nd battalions in Castrum Argentum to move south to join Legio I, with an ETA of 9 pm.
- Arina continues to have Legio III circle around the fort to stay out of melee with Norsgurd's forces. However, the range attacks take their toll on both sides. Arina loses her unit of bards, and switches her banner to the heavy cannon crew. Meanwhile, the bombard crew is forced to abandon their siege engine and retreat by an attack of hobgoblin heavy cavalry. However, Norsgurd has lost one unit of harpies and his gnoll skirmishers, one thousand troops in total. His hobgoblin heavy cavalry attacking the bombard also becomes surrounded and savaged by Arina's units who made a whole circle around the fort to end up back on the south flank.
- At 6 pm, Davona, Sky, and Legio II arrive on the battlefield.
