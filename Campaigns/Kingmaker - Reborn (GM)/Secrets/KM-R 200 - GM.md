# Notes

The following day, the team continued their portrayal as foreign alchemist vendors. Before their do so, they bring in Maalek from Promethea to Jafarkhet through Promethea's mythic *doors of many paths*. They needed his ability to form a telepathic collective for their plan to fool Inspector Bakat. Of course, they first needed to disguise him, as he was still a wanted fugitive from his last visit to the city. Once they altered his looks to perfectly blend in with the crowd, Davona provided him with intel on the city's guard patrols around the area. She had observed their patrol routes and timing while standing guard for the past two days.

At the open foreign market, the team hawked their wares as usual. Inspector Bakat appeared as promised, a little before noon. She began her inspection, but the team was prepared. The night before, when he went out to pay the inspector's fine, Sky had purchased a book containing all of Jafarkhet's laws and business codes. Arina then put herself into a meditative state, and speedread all of it, and committed its entirety to memory.

Bakat began to inspect their stall, books, and accounts, but everything to the tiniest detail was in order and in compliance with even the most obscure and minute regulation, all thanks to what they had learned in the business book. Slightly annoyed at the lack of any infractions to extract more "fines" from, Bakat then began questioning Sky some more about Jafarkhet business laws, hoping to find gaps in his knowledge that she could penalize him on. However, Arina, who was sitting nearby, fed Sky all the correct answers over Maalek's collective connection. The aelfkyn tactician himself was calmly walking around in the area, accurately avoiding any guard patrols, and blending in perfectly.

Sky was able to make Bakat believe that he knew the answer to every question she posed to him. "Well, it seems everything is now in order," the trade inspector reluctantly admitted. "We shall see if it remains so in the follow-up inspections."

Sky cleared his throat. "I'm sure that such follow-ups will be an unnecessary expenditure of your time and efforts, especially considering how profitable our business is for you and your office." While saying this, Sky picked out a small chest full of Ashaki gold coins out of his haversack, and slid it across the counter towards Bakat.

The inspector arched her eyebrows, and rifled her free hand in the pile of coins. She lifted up a handful of random coins, and gave them a quick inspection. Years of experience informed her that the chest contained five thousand real gold coins. She poured the coins back in the chest, closed the lid, and gestured to one of her guards. The burly bodyguard took the chest, and Bakat made a note in her notebook.

"The Office of Trade would find that further inspections to be unnecessary for such an honest and profitable business that is fully in compliance," she stated. She gave Sky a sly smile. "On behalf of the city of Jafarkhet, we wish you good business and fortune in our fine city."

With their business cover now secured, the Evergreen Company could continue pretending to be vendors during the day, and gather information during the evening as they did the two nights before. Arina, Sky, and Davona were the ones to go out and ply information from loose lips, while Argus and Orok focused on preparing their skills and tools for whatever they plan on doing.

The three undercover investigators used guile and subterfuge in their discreet inquiries to learn vital information from off-duty Fists of Amonkhaten. Though the inquisitors did not provide any overt answers, the team was able to get them talking and bragging, and were able read between the lines.

They learned that areas of importance within the Ministry's compound were secured with additional *glyphs of warding*, with added trapped locks. The locks could only be opened by those with the correct key. The traps were a mix of both magical and mundane dangers. These security measures are generally set up at the doors of the offices and quarters of the officers and leading clergy, the cells, the treasury, and important meeting areas. There may be additional glyphs, locks, and traps inside protecting containers, some of which may also be hidden.

Another inquiry the team made was how the Amonkatenites secured prisoners in the compound. The cells were underground. Spellcasting prisoners would be chained, gagged, and bound. A Fist of Amonkhaten would remain present within sight, using *detect magic*. Sometimes, the Prime Eye would maintain an *antimagic field* in the cell if necessary; only for the more riskier of prisoners as the Prime Eye's time and attention were often needed elsewhere. Worse comes to worse, however, the Fists would simply amputate the prisoner's limbs, pierce their ears and eyes, and shatter their jaw, then later *regenerate* them at a later time.

They also inquired as to the crew of the prisoner barge. They learned that the ship generally has a static crew of inquisitors and clerics of at least iron expert up to steel master ranking. They would only have new crew members on board as replacements. Such replacements may happen en route, such in the case of a crew member being killed by an unruly prisoner. Sometimes, though, a prisoner is dangerous enough to warrant additionaly security personnel coming on board at the time of the transfer from the Ministry's facility to the ship. In either case, each Ministry would have a shortlist of trusted iron experts or higher available to transfer on board the ship.

Sky postulated a possible plan of using him as a prisoner to be delivered to Sand Rock. Arina added that it might be possible to take the place of one of those trusted possible replacement crew here in Jafarkhet. She planned on seeking out who those likely candidates might be, though they still need to find out what could they do to provoke Sky's transfer to Sand Rock here in Jafarkhet without getting him killed, as well as making sure that their undercover person gets selected to join the prison barge's crew.
