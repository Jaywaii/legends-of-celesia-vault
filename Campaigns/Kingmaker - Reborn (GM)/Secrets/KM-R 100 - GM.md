# Notes

- Minor Sway: Alcina Sotillini, Sarpino Montari
- Major Sway: Adêline Châteaufoy, Theodore Duloup, Sootscale
- Penalties: -2 from the halfling population for not inviting Tierra

- Sister Félicité introduces Father Joram to Osla at the Great Cypress Memorial Park. They find a nice, quiet spot amidst the festivities to talk. Joram thanks her for her invitation, it has been a while since he last visited civilization. Tending to the Temple of the Elk in the Gnarlwoods is hard, but never a dull affair due to the number of fairy pranksters. He has his hands full trying to keep the peace between pilgrims and the tricky fey, but he's become good friends with Tyg-twitter-twig the Grig and Percilash the Fairy Dragon. His main concern is with the Sotillini Sawmill. The priest finds them quite aggressive in their productivity. He has tried to speak with the foreman Corax, but he has been rebuffed, and fairly rudely. He suggests to Osla for her or other leaders of Prateria to come visit the Gnarlwood as soon as the Games are over. Perhaps speak with the Sotillini representative while she is in town as well. Osla agrees, and promises that they will come visit the Gnarlwood as soon as they can.
- In the evening, the Opening Day banquet is being held at Prateria's town hall. The government building has been redecorated into a high class banquet hall, featuring fancy wall draperies, rows of tables covered in food and drinks lining the walls, and many round tables and chairs. A band of musicians play soft ball music. There is enough seats and room for each group of delegates, their athletes, and Prateria's leaders. Arina's staff takes charge of catering to everyone's needs, with Rebald Bitterswood announcing everyone's entrance, and Bradon Tourmaline overseeing the wait staff. Introduced by Tabrir who is serving as the banquet's host, Arina opens the banquet with a beautiful violin performance that starts out as a slow, dirgeful rendition of Prateria's anthem, but then kicks into celebratory and kicking melody, backed by the band, much to everyone's cheer and excitement. Food and drinks start being served out, and the attendees split up into group to discuss various topics of interest.
- Arina tries to join a discussion with Selene Dinovio, Fidenzio Teredoro, and Cyrano Varano. They are discussing recent development in trade pratices and emerging business opportunities in western Asteropa. Arina is unable to keep up witht he conversation, making several mistakes each time she tries to join in on the conversation, sadly exposing her lack of mercantile knowledge to the others. Rishoi tries to contribute, relying on what she had learned during her mercenary and traveling days, but the other in the group point out that her knowledge is woefully out of date. After taking a break from this group, Arina recomposes herself and returns to the conversation with these merchant-diplomats. This time, things go more her way as she is able to accurately discuss several negotiating tactics and trade movements in the local region, impressing them.
- Sky and Rishoi join a conversation between Theodore Duloup, Rafael and Pavetta Drelevo, Elvia Sovrani, and Sarpino Montari. They are discussing their impressions of Prateria thus far. They are able to steer the conversation towards more positive direction. In doing so, Sky is able to notice that Rafael Drelevo enjoys being addressed in a more superior manner, as if he was a true high lord. Rishoi, paying closer attention to Elvi Sovrani, is a bit discomforted by how openly brazen she is in regards to her innuendos. Davona later joins, and encourages Duloup to think about increasing his involvement in Arbor's Rest. Osla later joins in, catching their attention with her own positive impressions as a newcomer. Elvia Sovrani reacts quite positively, though Osla is unsure of her intentions as the diplomat eyes her up and down at length, speaking of her exoticness.
- Adeline Châteaufoy, Alcina Sotillini, Giovanni Biancardi, and Joliette Beauchamp are discussing acting troupes and plays are are in currently trending in Lurindor. Osla, wanting to get close to Sotillini to discuss the lumber issue, tries to join in, but gets the names of actors and what plays they star in mixed up. Finally, she redeems herself by latching onto the name of an acting troupe that she chanced upon in her travels. She is able to give the rest of the group a fairly detailed review of their performance, catching Alcina's attention. Arina, having slipped away from her previous group, tries to ingratiate herself with this one, but embarassingly enough ends up making the same mistakes as Osla did just before.
- Sky walks over to the Sootscale contigent, who are seated somewhat apart from the others. He immediately ingratiates himself with them by speaking their native tongue of Rustim. Their athletes are once again devouring quite an amount of food that belies their small sizes. Sky chats with King Sootscale, Mikmek, Samska, and Carsca. He entertains them with the story of how he won the magical gul bara set from the crone ala Stuhac. Despite Sky's more technically precise wording, he recounts the story so well that the kobolds are enraptured by it. They are particularly amused as the gul bara's ability to hurt its own players. Sky invites them to come watch the contest with it in the coming days, and Carsca is particularly interested. Sky later focuses his discussion with Carsca, talking to her about magical theory and how to improve her bloodline magic. The young kobold witch-doctor is fascinated, and is eager to learn more from him, just as Sky hoped she would be.
- About an hour into the banquet, disaster strikes in the kitchen. There is a noticeable stop in the flow of refreshments, and the attendees are starting to get antsy. The Company go into the kitchen and see the staff running around, some arguing. Bradon informs them that a cart carrying a load of ingredients broke down in the streets on its way here, and they do not have enough to build the rest of the menu. Arina goes out to explain the situation to the crowd, gaining some patience from them, while Sky, Bradon, and Davona fly out to where the cart is. Davona latches herself to the broken cart, and Sky *teleports* the three of them plus the cart, but the teleport goes awry. They suddenly appear in the middle of the street some distance north of the town hall. Drawing upon his mythical reserves, Sky *teleports* them again, this time successfully. There is a delay, but food once again resumes flowing out to the delight of the partygoers.
