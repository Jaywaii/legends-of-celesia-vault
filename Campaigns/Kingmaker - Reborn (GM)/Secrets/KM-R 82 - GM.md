And then they began hearing of rumours of a demonlike lady who, despite her appearance and origin, had been freely tending to the ill and injured. ## The Madonna of Crimson Eyes

- Rishoi needs to make a Knowledge (religion) check to determine the quality of her writings.
- Sunmother Isabelle Beauciel and Sir Thoros Brighthammer arrive in Promethea. They had noticed the influx of Kaïdenite pilgrims passing through. Word had reached them of a hand built cathedral in Promethea. And now there are rumours of a demonlike lady who, despite her appearance and origin, has been freely tending to the ill and injured. Although magical healing is quite common, witnesses speak of feeling a divine touch when she healed them, or witnessing an aura of higher power manifesting around her.
- Isabelle and Thoros at first enter the cathedral and silently observe Rishoi tend to the pilgrims and faithful. At the end of the day, when there is a lull in visitors, they approach her. They greet her, and sincerely tell her how good it is to see her again. "We did our best to guide you along the path of Kaï-den from your violent past. What you've done, what you are becoming, is amazing beyond anything we could have imagined. However, that is where we are starting to become concerned."
- Regarding Rishoi's recent writings. "Your words echo the goals of the new church. How much of the church's history do you know? Just before the Tyrant's World War, the Church of Kaï-den endured a terrible conflict known as the Great Schism. At that time, the Asteropan Orthodox Church of Kaï-den had grown powerful and influential enough that it could be considered its own empire. Although the church as a whole continued to follow Kaï-den's teachings, its own interpretations emphasized some aspects over others due to actions we had taken in the past to defeat great evils in the past. Although the church was not yet straying from the true path, the acts it was doing... were concerning. It all kicked off by a lone maverick priest and his paladin cousin got themselves involved in an Inquisition's investigation, which led to Kaï-den's Silence, the Kaï-denite Interregnum, the Crusade against Moloch, culminating into the Middle-Path Reformation under the teachings of the Daoshi Buddha, God's Mind on earth."
- Thoros: "I was but a young initiate back then. And though much of the conflict occurrect in the east of Asteropa, we keenly felt it here in the west and other places. Around the world, conflicts erupted, brother against sister, Kaï-denites against demons."
- Isabelle: "The old Orthodoxy had gained too much temporal power. It became more concerned with politics and land, all in the cause of defending life and justice against evil, of course. It's teaching became more conservative, more dogmatic, following the letter of the law and losing its spirit. After the Reformation, the new path focuses on the individual, teaches them to look inwards, and to strip away everything until only the truth remains. To take in all the teachings of the faith as a whole, and resolves its paradoxes through achieving *Chieshi*."
- If Isabelle is impressed by Rishoi's writings, she asks her to continue writing, but also asks if she would donate the cathedral to the church and allow it to be staffed by the clergy. She would like to continue building thebdiaog between Rishoi and the church.
- As Grand Diplomat, Tabrir reached out to the Sootscale kobolds and discussed establishing an Embassy treaty between the two nations. Construction of a mansion to serve as Prateria's embassy in Silvermine is planned.
