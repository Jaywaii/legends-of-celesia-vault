# Notes

- Bandit activty succeeds.

- Despite this, the village of Orn Sini is in a very celebratory mood. When the Praterian forces return, they are welcomed as heroes, especially Mikmek who is propulsed to the head of the column with his Cohors Draconis unit. Sootscale heaps much praise upon the General, but a Sense Motive check indicates that the kobold king does so reluctantly. Despite the uplifted morale, death hangs in the air around Orn Sini. Thousands were slain upon their hills. The kobolds have collected the bodies from the field, with the Praterian slain carefully placed with care and honour, while the Klorgfang corpses heaped in piles and set aflame in giant pyres.
- Carsca reports that Louis continues to whisper what he knows to unseen observers. Carsca and her assigned guards occasionally do detect the presence of fey, but are unable to stop them. However, the nature of Louis' information is very repetitive and of little value as the halfling remains imprisoned. It has led to a growing paranoid amidst the Sootscales that fairies are always around  the corner, spying on them.
- Colonel Arnoulet speaks to the Company, the halfling commander looks grim and haggard: "We have accomplished great victories so far, but at great cost. The effectiveness of the 2nd Fusiliers has significantly diminshed to serve as reinforcements and defenders to your nation. However, worry not, we will fulfill Rochefort's commitment to seeing your nation safe. I am willing to send a request for additional reinforcements along with my report for our actions this week to House Châteaufoy, should you so desire them."
- With his armies destroyed, Hrondgren falls on the defensive while he rebuilds his forces. He hires mercenary companies of mounted cyclops mercenaries(fighters and oracles) in units of 500 troops (recruit 36 BP, Consumption 26 BP, fee 1,200 gol/week, equipment fee 11,500 gol/month).
- Klorgfang will exploit all occupied hexes by Zhaoren 8th.
