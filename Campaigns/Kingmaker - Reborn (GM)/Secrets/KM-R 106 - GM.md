# Notes

**Jeshakaï 4th, 109**
- Within the cover of the spellbook they had recovered were some hastily written words: *"We have failed. Connornack and Garska have fallen to the madness of this place. The now chase me, seeking to consume my flesh in honour of the Devourer From Within. There are others too, and I was not their only target. I pray to El-Sathys that Darmok manages to escape. If any read these words, flee from this pl—"*
- Team 13 has to spend the next two weeks in Promethea. They ask Prateria's High Priestess, Sister Félicité, to quickly put the remains of Dalaster, Connornack, and Garska to rest before Dalaster's poltergeist returns. The slain Argent Rose agents are given a short, but sincere, funeral rite, and laid to rest in Promethea.
- Agna spends the first week to recover from her delusion with the aid of Sister Félicité. The rest of the team needs to require from their mental trauma as well, but not as long. During that time, the team returns to the Argent Rose lodge in Rochefort. They report to Chapter-Sage Joliette Beauchamp about what they have discovered so far, and to return their silver rose badges. Joliette grants the team the right to keep and use the gear they have recovered, but asks that they do not sell them to a third party. Instead, they may hand them in for a monetary reward, or in exchange for other items. Team 13 does so, and must wait another week to receive their requested item. During that time, Jericho needs to leave the team as his skills are needed elsewhere and he is given a new assignment.
- Once they receive their new items, and Agna is fully recovered, Team 13 returns to Ing-gol'zha. Retracing their steps, they succeed in making it to the third focal tower. The chamber is of similar dimensions as the previous two, but its floor and lower walls are covered by a semi-solid layer of pulsing, black bile with hundreds of marble-size yellowish orbs. They are unable to see within the orbs due to the fellowish fluid, but they manage to catch a tiny black thing dart within one of them. The walls clear of bile host engraved murals depicting horrific imagery and alien symbols. A blocked entryway stands across the room from them, and an altar stands in the middle of the chamber, with bones and clothing lying upon it. Upon closer inspection, the remains appears that of a small humanoid.
- The moment the team approached the altar, there is a loud howling from above them. The entry they came through is suddenly shut as a slab of stone drops down. From above, a glowing ghostly skull descends down towards them.
