## The Steamflogger Case

- [[Steamflogger Case]]
- The PCs are assigned a new case by Captain Acteon. Steamflogger Industries have been suffering supply problems for the past several months, and it's gotten to the point that there's suspicion of a coordinated attempt at ruining the business. As this is the business personally owned by one of our rulers, we can't discount the possibility this is an attack of some kind.
