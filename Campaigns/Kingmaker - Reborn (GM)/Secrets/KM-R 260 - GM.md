# Notes

- Party surrenders to swampfolk and are taken prisoner. They convince the village chieftain that they mean no harm, and tell him their quest. The tribe decides to test them by making them face the legendary beast Zagugrak as is their tradition.

## Dagorrad Marshes

arrive in Dagorrad Marshes
- murky, stagnant water reaches up to your thighs and chest
- surrounded by thick, gnarly trees covered in vines and moss, with massive exposed roots that bridge from island to island of small mounds of dry land
- the sky above is hidden by a thick canopy of leaves and branches
- glowing mushrooms and large fireflies provide some illumination around you
- air is thick and humid, with a pungent smell in the air
- air echoes with warped croaking and chirping, withthe occasional gurbling from muddy bubbles of gas popping up through the surface
- the sounds of eerie music composed of drums and horns can be heard in the distance

fight swamp monster to save swamp people / get info from swamp people
- ahead, multiple light sources break through the web of trees
- you can see a collection of wooden structures built amidst the tree, surrounded by a thick wall of logs and mud
- the eerie music originates from within this swamp village
- PERCEPTION: a number of figures move stealthily to surround you
- STEALTH: approach the village without notice to see it filled with people (the Hagleth-Kla) performing day-to-day activities, and worshipping a statue of a grotesquely obese frog-like broodmother
- they appear primanoid, but with sickly pale green skeen, bulging wide eyes, and flabby lips
- the Dagorrad marshfolk Hagleth-Kla speak in an alien dreamtongue
- the marshfolk want to take the PCs as prisoners, they can fight or peacefully negotiate with the initially HOSTILE villagers
- the village elder (Hlanith) forces the team to face a mega-worm ("Zagugrak") as a challenge to prove their worth, or unleash the beast on them if they are hostile
- [Zagugrak](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/bestiary/monster-listings/dragons/linnorm/linnorm-tarn) will flee after taking 450+ dmg: bab +30, bite 6d4+36, tail 5d4+36, DV 40, DR 20/armor, 20/coldiron
- defeating the worm earns the villagers' respect, who give them information to Sarinavere who will give them the knowledge they need to collect the whisper

convince great swamp hag Sarinavere to give secret to getting whisper / fulfill her request

survive trek through Dagorrad to ruined temple

survive skill challenge to capture whisper

The whispers from will-o-wisps are collected in a massive statue in a ruins deep in the Dagorrad Marshes.
