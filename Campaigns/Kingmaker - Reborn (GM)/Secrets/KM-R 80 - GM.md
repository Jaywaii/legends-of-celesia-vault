# Notes

- Tobias was abducted by the Court of Shadows. He was then tested by Queen Sinmaven through her majordomo. He failed to please the queen and prove his worthiness by failing the first four tests. The queen placed a *geas* upon him. *"Bring to me an enchanted locket of your queen Elyssria's hair."* he was tasked. He was returned to the outside border of the Old Arborfane, where he was then found by the rest of the Evergreen Company.
- After reaching Promethea, Osla sought to meditate under the Great Cypress. There she met with Lavendris, the dryad inhabiting the Great Cypress, and her fellow dryad sisters. She explained to Osla how the Evergreen Company revived the Great Cypress which allowed her birth, and the sad origins of her sister dryads who now reside in the park with her.
- Because Tim was unfamiliar with the spell, he had no idea he was cursed, and took no action to fulfill his task. The next day, he found himself weaker. The day after, he was even worse, and fell out of bed, unable to stand up. The rest of the company, noticing his absence from their regular morning meetings, went to look for him. Tim called upon Elyssria about what Sinmaven could do with a locket of hair. He received visions of Sinmaven using lockets of hair from different people to cast all sorts of spells upon them. He also asked if Elyssria could break the geas, but she replied that the means of doing so were within his own reach.
- Upon finding him crippled, the Company returned Tim to bed, and analyzed what was wrong with him. It was surmised that Tim may be under the effects of a *geas*. Tim brokenly recounted his encounter with Queen Sinmaven of the Court of Shadows, and that he was tasked with giving her a locket of Elyssria's hair. The party debated on how to proceed, as they were not powerful enough to remove the curse. One idea involved getting a horse and naming it Elyssria so they could give the horse's hair instead to fulfill the task. Another suggestion was to wait it out. The geas would not kill Tim, only leave him bedridden, and would likely expire after 2 months. They could keep him under watch in Rishoi's hospital. It was also discussed to seek out a *scroll of limited wish* in the mean time.
- Louis *communed* with the patron source of his power to inquire if handing over Elyssria's hair over to Sinmaven would be a hostile act, and if a *scroll of limited wish* was within their grasp. However, the cryptic answers put forth from the entity cast doubt as to its supposed origin. All this time, Louis and the Evergreen Company believed that Elyssria was Louis' mysterious patron. *Communing* once again, Louis discovered that neither Elyssria nor Sinmaven was the source of his power. Instead, it was a mysterious entity with ties to the two queens. However, they did learn that a *scroll of limited wish* was within reasonable reach.
- With no other recourse, the Company transported Tim to the hospital, and Arina, Tabrir and Osla departed for Lurindor. While Arina went around the nation to garner support and resources for Prateria, Tabrir and Osla searched for where a *scroll of limited wish* might be acquired. They had no luck in Rochefort, but learned in Ferrocitta that the Teredoro Consortium's magister, Alessandra, could possibly have one. After meeting with Carlo Teredoro, the Consortium's treasurer, they managed to secure a meeting with Alessandra the next day.

## Tobias and the Court of Shadows

- During the trek to the Northern Heart, Tobias was absconded by the shadow fey. Queen Sinmaven of the Court of Shadows wanted to know what were the intentions of the Evergreen Company.
- *You find yourself standing in a large chamber. It is dark all around you.* (Perception check to notice that there are people standing around him in the shadows.) *Suddenly, little motes of moonlight appear before you, revealing two tall figures, one seated in a throne of black branches and vines, and another standing before her. Though both apear elf-like with moon white skin and long, black ahir, the seated one is she who catches your attention. She is of an otherwordly beauty, fragile yet implacable. Atop her head rests an aetherium crown jeweled with starstones. She rests lazily in her throne, amd gazes at you with half-closed eyes. She makes a slight gesture with the tip of her finger, amd the man standing before her speaks.*
- *"Genuflect, mortal, for you bask in the umbral presence of her Royal Majesty Sinmaven, Queen of the Court of Shadows."*
- Tim will need to pass 6 tests put to him by the majordomo. He needs to past at least 4 tests to succeed, and passing all 6 is considered a great success. If he only passes 1 test or none of them at all, it is considered a great failure. Regardless of the outcome, he will be returned to the mortal plane just outside of the Old Arborfane. The initial Diplomacy and Intimidate DCs are 37, and the Queen's Sense Motive modifier is +33. Every success lowers the DC by 2 for the next test, while every failure increases it by 2.
	- *Great Success:* Tim is returned without harm and given a gift: a *cloak of flash and shadow*, marking him as having earned the favour of the queen.
	- *Success:* Tim is returned unharmed.
	- *Failure:* Tim is returned with a geas placed upon him. The geas is to give Sinmaven an enchanted locket of Elyssria's hair.
	- *Great Failure:* Tim is returned with the geas above, and a curse placed upon him. The Curse of Ages will age him 1 year for every day at dusk that passes.
	- Tests:
		1. <u>Greeting the Queen:</u> *"Mortal, you may now greet her majesty."*
		1. <u>Introduce Yourself:</u> *"And to whom does her Royal Majesty graciously grant audience to?"*
		1. <u>Explain Your Presence:</u> *"Now, mortal, explain your presence in this illustrious court?"*
		1. <u>State Your Intentions:</u> *"And now, what are your intentions regarding this quest of yours?"*
		1. <u>Display Knowledge of the Shadow Fey:</u> *"Tell us, what know you of this grand court?"* Knowledge klocal), Knowledge (nature), Knowledge (nobility), Knowledge (planes), Lore (Old Arborfane)
		1. <u>Compliment the Queen:</u> *"And what words have you to gift your magnificient host?"*
	- The Queen only responds with a slight upward tug from the corner of her dark lips if pleased, or downward if the opposite, before restting her features back to neutral.
