# Notes

- Defeated Connornack and Garska: 2,240 XP (Agna, Raagi, Igvald, Rex, Jericho)
- Unlock Gate of Radiance: 1 FATE points

- Occult Investigation Team 13 returned to Rochefort where Zen could be taken in and treated for his psychosis. Agents Agna Boulder and Jericho Crowley joined the team. After resupplying and debriefing the new team members, Team 13 returned to Hushglow Tower. Descending into Ing-gol'zha, the team made it all the way to the 3rd focal tower, the Gate of Radiance. Jericho found Talien Firesong's note about the tower in nearby rubble, but it was very distubring. The team worked together to decipher the mural to figure out how to unlock the gate. Eventually, Jericho determined that the needed to look into the void within the obsidian skull. Fortunately for Jericho, he managed to hold onto his mind to the unearthly revelations from beyond. He also managed to avoid having his eyes burned out by the truth. The gate then opened up.
- Before the team moved on, they needed to rest. While Rex and Agna stood watch, the ghastly forms of former Argent Rose agents "Diamond-eye" Connornack and Garska Drumsong. Rex was lured into a trap, and was badly shot by Connornack. However, the team soon rallied. Garska was blown away by Agna, and Rex, though badly damaged, managed to stay up against Connornack, and sliced him to pieces.
