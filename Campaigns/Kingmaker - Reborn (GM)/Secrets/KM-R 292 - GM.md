# Notes

- The PCs teleport to Wolfshold, find it empty and a mist quickly moving past them towards the west. They rush into the town to go to Wolfshold Castle. At an open market, they become surrounded by zombies. They also notice a pusling golden light in the distance.
- Aedre and Orok reach the golden light, and discover that it is holding back the mist and zombies. They learn that centaurs are inside who don't want ot let them in. However, the light drops, and a woman's voice orders them to let Orok and Aedre in. However, this allows the zombies to begin encroaching on the house they barred themselves in.
