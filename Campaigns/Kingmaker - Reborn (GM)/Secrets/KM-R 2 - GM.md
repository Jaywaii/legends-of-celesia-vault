# Notes

- Arina, Cethin, Davona, Jupus, Minchdoya

Davona needs up to 8 days to recover her ability damage (minimum 4 if successful Heal check each day).

Vico (LE male human ranger 1)—Vico is a crude and foul-mouthed man who turned to banditry after he was caught running a protection racket in Rochefort while also, in theory, serving as a soldier and city guard. He fled the city when he learned that the law was coming for him, abandoning a wife and two children to suffer the shame of his crimes. He’s found that banditry agrees with him—he quite enjoys the outdoors, and the occasional tumble with Ravenna is nice too. He suspects that she’s working for an even more important bandit, but for now is content being the second-biggest fish in the bandit camp at Thistledown River camp (area K). As an officer in the Stag Lord’s army, Vico wears a small silver amulet shaped in the likeness of a stag’s skull—even though Vico has yet to actually meet the Stag Lord in person.
Mick—grizzled bandit with brown beard. A bit more experienced than the rest of the group, he keeps his calm and actually uses his brain. The others look to him for guidance. He originally came from the Tassimar Reach, living a rough life of petty thievery.
Guido—skinny bandit with braided dark hair. He lost his livelihood in a failed investment and fled south into the bandit's life to escape his creditors.
Tienzo—slim well-built man from the eastern lands, bald except for a braid of black hair. He killed a guard in a drunken state in a bar fight while traveling Lurindor, and escaped to the Viridian Marches.
Fernand—heavyset man goatee. He worked as an enforcer for a local crime boss in Lurindor, and fled south when the law finally took his boss down.
Marcius—a short man with curly blond hair. He is a devout follower of Sindall who fled the theocratic Holy Radimus Empire for heresy.

Vico is the hardest of the bunch to turn, but he can be convinced for a short while if given money. The others respect strength and wealth, and only need some serious convincing to give up their bandit ways for more honest living (for now). They will require wages paid out by the PCs.

Onestrio is furious at having to house the very same bandits that threatened him and his wife. Sabina is equally nervous of having them around, and both won't feel safe leaving if the PCs leave the bandits alone with them.

On the 3rd day after the battle, Lorenzo Dinovio arrives with 3 other guards. These are the guards that Onestrio requested.
Lorenzo Dinovio (LN male human fighter 3)—Lorenzo is a member of a merchant family part of the Sotillini Cartel in name only. A DC 20 Knowledge (nobility) check is enough to recall the scandal when an affair between Lorenzo and a woman named Tania, the wife of a business party of Lorenzo's father, became public knowledge. His father Enzo Dinovio, after his business partner broken off their partnership and ruined their business, disowned Lorenzo, who fled to Rochefort to serve as a mercenary. He volunteered to lead the small group of soldiers to help guard Onestrio’s Trading Post after his own depression and shame grew too much to handle. Lorenzo hopes to either make a name for himself as the defender of a remote fort, or to find an honorable death among the bandits and other dangers in the region.

In addition to bringing with him an opportunity for another quest, Lorenzo could well grow into an important NPC as the PCs continue to settle the Viridian Marches and grow their nation. For now, Lorenzo need only be a somewhat moody and morose guard. If the PCs get into more trouble than they can handle, you can have Lorenzo and his men come to their rescue, but otherwise, he should remain at the trading post, if only to give the post protection and relieve the PCs of any need to do that job themselves.

Lorenzo and his men set their small tents up just south of the stable, in the shadow of the palisade.
Vecchio (LN male middle-aged human warrior 3)—Vecchio is a grizzled old vet has seen some combat before. He uses his experience to keep calm in dangerous situations.
Roark (LN male dwarf warrior 1)—Roark is young and eager for excitement and adventure. His posting to Onestrio's Trade Post is his first time out of his home.
Marcel (LN male halfling warrior 2)—Marcel is disciplined; he acts as a professional soldier.

Accompanying them is **Gaspasi Savino**, a 3-star member of Monster Hunting Association. He had heard about the expedition, and had heard of some interesting monsters with some reputation in the Evergreen Plains. He came down in hopes to enticing the party to become members. If they succeed in hunting down these monsters, it will help increase both their fame and the association's. If Gaspasi succeeds in getting the party to become monster hunters, then the association will have an easier time of expanding in the future colony the party will establish.

On the 4th day, another traveler comes to the area. This is a priest of Deianeira named Joram Kaerny (NG male human cleric 4), a traveling cleric and hunter who claims to have heard of the exploration charter and came to Onestrio’s to offer his help. In truth, Joram has been all but excommunicated from the priesthood after he helped form a lynch mob against a traveler his home village thought was a werewolf responsible for several recent killings. Only a few hours after they lynched the man, the true killer was caught by a hunter and revealed to be nothing more sinister than a particularly wily worg. An investigation by the church of Deianeira followed, and they found Joram’s involvement in the village’s overly aggressive pursuit of the traveler’s lynching to be actionable. Only the fact that the traveler happened to be a bandit spy sent to town to look the place over (a fact that Joram discovered only after the man was executed and his belongings were searched) kept the church from fully excommunicating the priest—and even then, only if he accepted exile. And so Joram traveled from his home in Valendar. From there, his wanderings eventually brought him Lurindor, where he had a particularly vivid dream about an overgrown temple of Deianeira guarded by a huge, angry bear. Upon waking from the dream, Jhod felt an irresistible tug to the southwest, and realized that Deianeira had given him a chance to redeem himself. The tugging led Joram to Onestrio’s Trading Post, but there it stopped—and soon after he learned about the PCs and their charter from Sabina. Jhod has also heard rumors of “lost temples” to Deianeira in the Stolen Lands, and the first time the PCs return to the trading post after his arrival, he tracks them down and asks them to keep an eye out for anything of that nature—particularly an old temple guarded by an angry bear.
