# Notes

## Trial of Heart

- PCs breath in the smoke from the burning of the Waking Dreambloom.
- The PCs appear at the herb gathering site. Over a dozen centaurs are here, about half are elderly matrons, and the other half are children.
- 6 Cyclops and a dozen halfling "soldiers" arrive. The cyclops drove the halflings into towards the centaurs, then proceed to slaughter both the halflings and the centaurs.
- The PCs need to be willing to give up their lives to protect the centaurs. They must allow the halflings to die in order to save the centaurs. Each time a PC dies, they can choose to wake up from the dream, or respawn some distance away, but in the waking world, they get a random crippling injury.
- If they instead save the halflings or wake up before all the centaurs or cyclops die, they fail the test and will be executed.
