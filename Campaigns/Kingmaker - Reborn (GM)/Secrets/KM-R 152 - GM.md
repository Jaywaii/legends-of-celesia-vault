# Notes

- Evergreen Company defeats Hrondgren and his retinue.
- They discover that Tartuk had disappeared days before, absconding with Hrondgren and Hildr's son, the now Exiled-Prince Skora.
- The Cyclopean mercenaries are given guaranteed safe passage back to Carpathos as long as they do not take action against Praterian forces. They loot and pillage the now leaderless Klorgfang lands along the Praterian-controlled roads back into Carpathos, much to the Company's dismay and frustration.
- Prateria declares victory in the Evegreen War, and begins plans to annex Klorgfang.
