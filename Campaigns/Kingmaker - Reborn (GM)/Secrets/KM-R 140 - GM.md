# Notes

- The fourth hour of the Second Battle of Orn Sini sees a turn in the melee to the north of Castrum Argentum. While Praterian and Rochefort units continue to be defeated and fall back to the fort, the remaining units still fighting are slowly, but surely destroying the troll cavalry, unit by unit. Meanwhile, to the south, Another of Hrondgren's unit of harpies arrive on the battlefied, having been recalled from their scouting duties to join the battle. Along with its sister unit, it flies above the eastern outer wall of the fort to the unmanned and undefended eastern middle wall. Its sister unit lands and occupies the wall and its gate, while the first harpy unit flies overhead, providing cover. However, this proves to be a poor position as the Praterians swivel the direction of their heavy cannon, and fires it into the mass of flying harpies. Blood and feathers rain from the sky in equal measure. Worse still, the other hunit of harpies discover that they cannot open the gate and the area is covered in fog and other magical obstacles. Sky had enchanted this part of the fort with *guards and wards*, and while this prevents even the PRaterian forces from operating the gate, it proves to be a boon as the troll forces now pouring in through the breached outer wall are unable to move further in without having to take a longer route either south or north.
