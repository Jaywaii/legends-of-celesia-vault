# Notes

## Trials of the Old Arborfane

### Laellien's Lament

#### Pantillion

- As the PCs travel forward, the arrive at a stream, and easily notice a red streak flowing down in it. As they follow it, they begin hearing singing among the tree tops. It seems to be the same two verses. The singer, a male voice it seems, sings the two verses, then pauses. After a few seconds, the same two verses begins being sung again. DC 40 Lore (fey), DC +10 Knowledge (nature) to recognize the song as *Laellien's Lament*, a mournful song among fairies based on a true story, and realize that the pause is for a response from another singer or chorus. DC 40 Survival to follow it; if everyone fails, 1 day passes, otherwise 1 hour.
- The PCs arrive at a sharp, rocky outcropping, from which the source of the singing is at the top. The PCs require 3 climb checks to make it up to the top. Each time the group fails to make it to the next one, an hour passes.
- Sitting at the top is a satyr, Pantillion, the singer of the song. If the PCs make the proper response to the song, Pantillion greets them, calling them them from afar and beckoning them to follow his voice if they make the response from the stream, and beckons them to meet him atop the rock. Otherwise he ignores them, and flees if attacked. After he greets the PCs, Pantillion produces a leather wineskin, takes a deep breath and braces himself, then greedily swallows a large gulp. After gulp, Pantillion shudders, and slobberly wipes his mouth with a swipe of his forearm. He then asks if they had come across a stream with a red streak in it. When the PCs confirm, the satyr regretfully informs them that they are now on Laellien's trail, entrapped within an old fey haunting. He tells them that if they follow the stream, they will enter Laellien's land, warning that the memory of his death haunts the surroundings. Once again, he takes a deep breath, then a greedy gulp of his wineskin, followed by another shudder. If asked about it, he just tells the PCs that it's just a little something to get him through the day. A DC 40 Sense Motive reveals signs that he is reluctant, but compelled to drink it. The scent of the drink indicates that it is very strong. He wishes the PCs luck, telling them they must now be off, so does he, but he might be around to check in on your progress. With another shuddering gulp of his wineskin, the satyr hops down the outcropping, and disappears among the trees.

#### Golden Palisade

- *Ahead of you, the stream becomes wider and shallower The trees hang over steep mud banks that rise from the water on each side. Wading to your ankles, you follow the stream around a bend. By the water’s edge, the trees part to form a clearing and within it are the remains of a large campsite. As you approach, you see the wooden ribs of tents exposed under decayed canvas, in places heavy with mold. Rotten lengths of string that might once have held bunting hang between the remains of large tents.*
	- This clearing is what remains of Lallwyn's camp, the Golden Palisade. Upon closer inspection, the PCs can find several skeletal remains. A Heal check reveals that the skeletons have not decayed at the same time, some are older than the others. All appear to have their deaths caused by violence. *Searching through the rotten litter of the campsite, you hear a whistle outside. Peering through the sunken canvas wall, you see the thick trees of the forest behind. Just when you decide to turn away, the breeze seems to whisper the name, “Laeillon”.* If the PCs make a DC 40 Perception check, they catch a glimpse of white hair behind a tree. If no one succeeds, an hour passes. They hear Pantillion sing the 2nd verse of the song, and they once again get to make the Perception check. Each failure cost an hour, and Pantillion repeats the verse.
	- If the PCs offer the proper response, or if they say at any point the name 'Nemeth': *Upon speaking the name 'Nemeth' aloud, a gust of wind blows into the clearing. It grows storng enough to start picking up pieces of debris and bones, throwing them around in the air around with the clearing. There is a sudden womanly screech that rips through your minds.* PCs must make a DC 20 Will save. Those who fail take 1d4+1 Sanity damage and become enraged as the barbarian rage ability, and targets one random companion, crying out "Die, Nemeth!". The DC increases by 1 each following round. Those who succeed take half Sanity damage. Casting *calm emotions*, *hymn of peace*, *aura of peace*, *mantle of calm*, or performing a DC 40 Diplomacy check as a full provoke action to appeal for peace and calm can dispel the rage and end the haunt.
	- Upon ending the haunt: *The breeze increases and a gust of wind circles through the deserted camp. The leaves, bones, and debris from the floor start to swirl as the gust seems to circle back on itself. The force of the wind increases, and the old tent walls start to flutter freely, the poles strengthening as they rise and straighten. As they stiffen, each canvas becomes a golden square, the dirt of ages falling as they form their old shapes. Triangular flags unfurl in an array of bright colors as the bunting stretches taut between the tents. Bones rearrange to form full skeletons, laying in the ground in a restive pose. Before long, the entire site is restored to its old glory as Lallwyn’s Golden Palisade. All of the finest crockery, exquisite satin sheets and bedclothes, and beautiful chests and traveling cases are now intact as if brand-new, though completely empty. You hear in the distance a male voice, not Pantillion's this time, singing the 3rd verse of Laellien's Lament, carried from deep within the woods carried on a gentle breeze. You catch the sight of a slender person with long white hair disappear deeper into the woods, in the direction of the singing.*

#### *Laellien's Lament*

##### 1st Verse

*Oh, let me free to come to you / Come to you, my son / Like a dream I see you / Our lives entwined in death*
*Why couldn’t I see your heart was full / Why didn’t I see your longing / I was blinded by my fear / I was guided by my anger*
- Response: *My mother, years have passed now / And my blood washed through the stream*

##### 2nd Verse

*Like a curse I remember that boy / White hair appeared through the trees / Where our caravan camped / Not worthy of the house of Lallwyn*
*I recall the rage at my throat / As I tore at the walls of your heart / Your bed empty, your window open / In my rage, I was ready to kill*
- Response: *My mother, years have passed now / And I gave my heart to my Nemeth*

##### 3rd Verse

*He hid among the trees that night / And helped you find the shadows / Rage burst through my heart / I screamed at your gentle face*
*A mask to hide your defiance / To defend what I thought was mine / Stay a step before my guards / Disappear between the branches*
- Response: *My mother, years have passed now / And my love still blooms from beyond*

##### 4th Verse

*I remember the sight of your hair / As I unleashed my guards in the glade / Fear rooted them to the ground / White robe blowing as you ran*
*Fleeing my rage for your love / Your precious ankle slipped away / Oh, if I could just take it back / I, your mother, twisted by my hate*
- Response: *My mother, years have passed now / And I fall forever in your dream*

##### 5th Verse

*Hounds teeth caught your flesh / You stumbled on the wet rock / Froth flashes white when water falls / Over stone I saw my son slip*
*I rushed to the edge of the ridge / Watched the water tumble over / Your skull broken bleeding / Ribbons of your blooming blood*
- Response: *My mother, years have passed now / And I am lost for ever to my only love*
