# Notes

- Ignis Volantis defeats Forest Drake scout squad (ACR 5): 1,600 XP

- Continuing their governing work during the month of Tierlan 111, the Evergreen Company divests many of the magical items they have accumulated to gain funds for training their followers and of the 50 Sootscale kobolds, and to fund construction of necessary buildings. They also secure another loan from the Teredoro Consortium, but at a 20% annual interest.
- Arina deploys her squad of 10 expert wizards to scout the nearest Klorgfang army that Cortez had learned about. Flying and invisible, the squad encounters another squad of forest drakes belonging to Klorgfang. The drakes were exploring the lands southwest of the Sootscale capital of Silvermine. The Klorgfang scouts did not discover the Praterian wizards, allowing them to call Arina's name. With her mythic ability to know who calls her name, Arina scrieds her team, and manifests an illusion of herself before them. Her team informs her of their discovery, and thanks to her flash-stepping technique, Arina is able to link up with her team before the drakes leave the area.
- When the drakes finally move, Arina's team ambushes them in the air. *Fireballs* explode wthin the flight of drakes, with a second volley completely finishing them off. Enflamed drakes fall to the ground leaving a trail of black smoke. Prateria claims first blood in the conflict with Klorgfang. The team collects any valuable and harvest valuable body parts from the fallen drakes. The team them arrives at their intended destination undetected, and are able to learn the composition of the Klorgfang army garrisoned at a fort.
