# Notes

- Jafarkhet goes into lockdown. It is about 2 am local time. Inquisitor forces take command of the city guard. All gates are sealed. Suspicious people under surveillance are arrested. Travelers are interrogated. Suspected resistance safe houses are raided. The only travelers allowed to leave or enter are the Fists and Eyes of Amonkhaten. There is currently one ship waiting to depart to the Sandrock, carrying a high-security prisoner, a cyclops general.
- Jafarkhet is swarming with patrols of city guards and Amonkhatenites. City guard patrols are composed of six lvl 5 fighters led by a lvl 7 lieutenant. Amonkhatenite patrols are composed of five lvl 7 inquisitors led by a lvl 10 inquisitor lieutenant assisted with lvl 7 cleric. Every hour, one patrol has a chance of detecting the party. 80% will be a city guard patrol and 20% will be in Amonkhaten patrol. City guards use Perception +32 without any additional help. Amonkhaten patrols use Perception +48 combined with *see invisibility* and *detect magic*.
- Orok suspects that any information about Sand Rock can be found in the Prime Eye of Amonkhaten's office in the Templar Fortress.
