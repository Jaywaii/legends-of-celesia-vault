# Notes

- At 2 am, Vandarkaï 29th, Norsgurd's Horde arrives as Castrum Lapis. By this time, Legio III and 2nd Fusiliers 3rd Battalion were reinforced by 2nd Fusilier 4th Battalion, arriving from Castrum Aes, and by Legio II from Orn Sini. Importantly, Sky arrives with them with Jupus' new bombard and heavy cannon in pocket after using *shrink item* on them. He returned them to their normal size on the fort's rampart, alongside the Praterian and Rochfort defenders. As soon as Norsgurd begins setting up his catapults to assault the fort, Rishoi climbed up to the highest peak of the fort, and drew upon the power of light to light up the night sky to be as bright as day for several miles. Immediately, the cannon and bombard open fire on one of the in-progress catapults, completely destroying it in their opening volley. Seeing as his other catapult will require another hour to set up, and the numerous defenders on the walls, Norsgurd decided to immediately call for a retreat.
- Norsgurd's horde retreats to the west. The troll commander pushes his army hard to reach the nearby fort at the center of the Klorgfang-Prateria border. Sky follows them discreetly with magical flight and invisibility. Hot on Norsgurd's heels is Legio III, just an hour behind him. Norsgurd manages to arrive at the fort at noon, with Legio III arriving an hour later. Meawhile, Legio II is resting at Castrum Lapis after their hard march. Legio I, which previously regrouped at Orn Sini after leaving Castrum Ferro, heads south to the Greenbelt after Argus is updated on the situation by Arina via their new *far crystals*. At the river, Jupus leads a team to build a temporary bridge to cross the river while the rest of Legio I rests.
- News reaches the Evergreen Company that Silverich's Horde in the west crossed the border. While Norsgurd faced Prateria's legions in the east, Silverich had units of harpies cut off access to many miles of Praterian land surrounding Castrum Ferro. Prateria loses all intelligence from the territory cut off by Klorgfang.

- Louis, under Calefaidgh's influence, informs Klorgfang of Prateria's deployments. While Louis sleeps, the Shade Man has him write down what he knows, and passes them to a Shadow Fey loyal to Calefaidgh, who then passes the information to Klorgfang's agents.
- At 6 am, both of Silverich's harpy units split off from main horde. One heads west to reoccupy a single hex 5 hexes away, and the other heads east to occupy Prateria hex 5 hexes away. Silverich marches his horde into one hex at 2 pm and begins occupying it (next start time is 6 am).  If successful, Prateria loses 11 hexes to occupation and cut-off. Harpy units finish their occupation at 3 am and return to Silverich at 7 am (if at Castrum Ferro) or 8 am (if at Silent Bastion).
- At 6 am, Norsgurd's horde arrives at 2 pm one hex away from Castrum Lapis. They make camp, and will rest until 10 pm. They move at Castrum Lapis and arrive at 2 am, and begin a night attack.
