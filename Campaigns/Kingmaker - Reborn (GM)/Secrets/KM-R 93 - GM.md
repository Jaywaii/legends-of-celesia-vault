# Notes

- Officer Accursia Cinqemani is assigned to look into a noise disturbance. Arriving at the neighbourhood of the reported incident. She spends several hours talking to the locals and looking around without making any progress. Just as she was about to leave, a slim boy waves her from a nearby alley. When she goes to see him, he asks her if she is Officer Accursia, and that her fellow officers need her help. The boy Tito shows Accursia the badges of Shuvi and Lawtomaton. Tito leads Accursia through some back alleys to the rear entrance of the Jade Lounge, and takes her in to see Shuvi and Lawtomaton resting on beds, recovering from grave injuries.
- Shuvi and Lawtomaton explain the situation to Accursia, about the recent kidnappings of the Jade Lounge workers, the disappearance of the bodies and suspects from the guard station and the potential corruption within the police force, and of their ambush in the streets last night. Accursia agrees to help the two out, and begins by inspecting the bodies of their attackers. The basement rooms of the Jade Lounge, expressly designed to tie people down in soundproof rooms, are where the Jade Lounge people stored the bodies and the lone survivor of last night's foes. Accursia is unable to find anything of note on her initial inspection, nor is she able to interrogate the other thug as he was only semi-conscious. However, Shuvi and Lawtomaton manage to descend into the rooms to join her with Bianca's and Tito's help. Together, they spent the better part of an hour going over the bodies and items.
- They discover an emerald in a hidden pocket in the jacket belonging to the well-dressed, bald thug who matched the description of Mr. Fontana's assistant. The emerald is unique as it was chiseled to be a disk the size of a large coin, slightly curved, and quite transparent. They can look through it to have their vision slightly distorted with a green tinge. However, it reveals nothing else, and the light passing through it reveals no other clue. Knowing a skilled craftsman friendly to them among Steamflogger Industries, the officers plan on showing the emerald to her to get her opinion.
- While the bald man had a unique look, the two other thugs are fairly average and non-descript, and carried no identification. However, they do discover tattoos on them. The dead woman had a tattoo of a rose with the words "13th Street" on her right shoulder, and the unconscious man had a tattoo of a skull with a crown with the words "Kings' Row" on the back of his neck. Accursia is certain that these were gang tattoos from Lurindor, but is unable to determine anything else.
- By now, Accursia needs to head back to the station and file her report. She says that she will write up that the disturbance was nothing, maybe an animal. She will meet up with the other two later. She recommends that Shuvi and Lawtomaton seek out a healer and get back into full form. As she leaves, Bianca suggests that she knows someone who can heal them without being tied to any known organization in the town. She later comes back with a young woman dressed in simple, plain clothing. She introduces herself as Nerea, and she has helped the workers at the Jade Lounge before, as well as providing healing to the less fortunate in town. She invoked the blessing of the Crimson Madonna, and heals Shuvi and Lawtomaton. Before leaving, Nerea leaves behind some pamphlets about the teachings of the Crimson Madonna, and asks that the officera consider the Disciples of the Crimson Madonna as friends of justice.
- Now back to full strength, Shuvi and Lawtomaton disguise themselves with Bianca's and Mino's help. They leave to go find Bernadette Granet, a skilled halfling craftswoman with the Steamflogger Industries. By this time, Bernadette is usually taking lunch at the Steaming Mug Inn. The two undercover officers head over there. Unfortunately, they stand out like sore thumbs, amd their attempts to discreetly catch Bernadette's attention only serve to freak her out. Lawtomaton is forced to vreak his cover to convince her to speak with them.
- Outside around the corner of the inn, the officers show Bernadette the emerald. She is quite amazed by its craftsmanship, stating that a gem cut this masterfully would be well worth over a thousand gold pieces. She says that it is cut like a lens, like one would make for glasses, but aside from that and its exceptional clarity, there is nothing else of note about it. She knows that no jewelry in Prateria is skilled enough to craft such a gem. Lawtomaton and Shuvi thank Bernadette with a promise to share a drink and recount the story of this case before they part ways.
- Trying to follow up on another lead, the pair of officers head towards Promethea's orphanage to speak with Daniello's brother, Antonio. They speak with the Matron of the orphanage, but their attempts to see Antonio only serve to make the matron more distrustful of them. They even try to claim that they are a couple seeking to adopt Antonio. When that fails, Shuvi attempts to get further into the orphanage, claiming to require the washroom. It is at that point that the matron began calling for help and for the guards. Shuvi and Lawtomaton hastily left the orphanage.
- At a lost of leads, the pair of officers returned to the Jade Lounge. When they get close, they notice someone well-armed observing them from an alley. They give chase, but lose sight of the person. However, the suspect left tracks in the snow-covered streets. The pair discreetly followed the tracks to one of the recently built housing blocks of the Promethea. Suspecting that if the perpetrators are using one of these houses as a base and would therefore have lookouts, the officers opted to return to the Jade Lounge and wait for Accursia. They hope that she could be able to access housing records at the town hall, and find out which address possibly houses Fontana or Benelli.
