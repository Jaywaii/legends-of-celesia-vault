# Notes

With the additional funds, Cortez succeeded in wining and dining Captain Husani well enough for the unscrupulous Jafarkheti captain to fast track the purchase of a building under Shumira's name and overlook any odd details in the paperwork. A week later, the 'Whimsy Camel' inn was open for business. Cortez recruited two local laborers that he made sure can be trusted to stay quiet in exchange for above average pay. In his local persona, Cortez became the manager of the inn, and made sure that it looked run down enough to avoid customers.

Back in Promethea, Arina made a series of superhuman one-mile leaps, crossing over a thousand miles of Carpathosi and Qar-Kadaji lands to arrive within sight of Jafarkhet under three hours. She then found an isolated location, out of sight from the city and travelers. At the arranged time, Sky called out her name, allowing the Praterian consul to *scry* on him. Through that connection, Sky prepared to *teleport* himself, Davona, Argus, and Orok, along with a pair of draft horses to Arina's location.

However, after he cast the spell, a rare mishap occurred. The dimensional warping of time and space went wild, and the teleporters felt their insides turn inside and out. An instant later, they found themselves in a green plain, coughing up blood or bleeding from their noses and eyes. Several cyclops traveling on a nearby road stared at them in shock. Quickly, Sky cast a benign *treacherous teleport* spell as the energies of the previous *teleport* lingered on him. This time, the party appeared before Arina.

First aid was quickly delivered to the horses before they bled to death, thanks to Arina's new alchemy training she prepared for their disguises. Speaking of which, after the team were healed up, they got to work on their alternate personas. They would portray themselves as a caravan business that sells alchemical items. Sky *fabricated* a wagon for the thousands of gold pieces worth of alchemical tools and reagents. Then, everyone helped each other to make sure their disguises were top notch.

Once all that was done, they traveled to the nearby road, and began lining up for entry into Jafarkhet. The guards examined the party for inconsistencies in their stories, but found none. They also detected for magical items and abilities, but thanks to Sky's casting of *greater magic aura*, the guards were left to assume that the party was non-magical except for Sky's *all tools vest* which was part of his persona and low-level enough to not warrant any special attention. After signing in, getting their portraits recorded, and getting their travel papers, the party entered the city.

It did not take them long before they were flagged down by a local offering them to guide them around the city and to a perfect place to rest. Through subtle gestures and key words, this local Jafarkheti secretly signalled to the party that this was Cortez in disguise. He led them to the Whimsy Camel, and told the two labourers to take care of the wagon and luggage. Meanwhile, he invited the party into the back office, and dropped his Jafarkheti persona.

Sky memorized the back office for future teleportations while they discussed their plans. Cortez will be working on lowering the suspicion of Jafarkhet's guard and Amonkhaten's Eyes on Shumira. He has a long con in play to eventually get Shumira working at the Whimsy Camel without arousing any suspicion. The rest of the party begin discussing options on how to find Sand Rock now that they have a base of operations in Jafarkhet.
