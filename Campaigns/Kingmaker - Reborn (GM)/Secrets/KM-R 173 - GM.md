# Notes

- The freelancers left Toursoline in the middle of the night after checking to see that the fighting had died down and that the town guard wre getting things under control. They picked up Gunhild Bronzebuckle outside the west gate. On the way back to Rochefort, Gunhild shared her suspicions that the bandits were not operating on their own, that they were being coordinated from behind the scenes.
- On their trip back, Gunhild teaches a few skills to the freelancers. She also informs them about what happened during the investigation. How she was close to removing the Gardeforts from the suspect list, how she got accused of corruption and a well-known investigate by the name of Carmella Lupo took her place. Her follow up investigation uncovered some names involved in the auctioning of the Gardefort assets.
- The investigation had wrapped up quickly by Lupo, and TRA judge Athos Cerulli oversaw the Gardefort trial. One Dominique Drelevo was assigned as ombudsman to oversee the auctioning of Gardefort assets, and Gunhild had uncovered some companies that acquired them: Cristallo Resorts, Nuveni Food Group, Savonio Trade Company. However, before she learned that Drelevo dies in an accident, and that his son Rafaelo liquidated their company's assets to move to a new nation. That was when she was reassigned to Toursoline.
- Gunhild also informs them that she learned of Berendikoff's apparent suicide in prison last year from an article in the Rochefort Gazette. She also tells the Freelancers that she worked with Berendikoff's lawyer Madeline Dambrose in the investigation. Gunhild heard from Madeline that they uncovered some new info, but they were separated before she could learn anything new. Dambrose was visiting Berendikoff regularly at his prison.
- The party returns to Rochefort. Gunhild opts to camp outside the city in a remote location known to her and her cousing Thangreka. She passes a messages with her seal to the freelancers for them to pass on to her cousin. Kraz succeeds in passing the message along and updating the guard sergeant on what happened. Back at the Cherry House, Vincoq prepares dinner for the party, and Ulgan takes an extra part and other supplies to Gunhild outside and keeps her company that night.
- The next day, the freelancers went to the Rochefort Gazette to retrieve a copy of the newsletter with the article about Berendikoff's death. After, they went to go see Madeline Dambrose's office. However, the office is closed, with a note in the window stating that Dambrose will be away for some time. Asking questions to the neighbouring baker, they learn that Dambrose has not been present for the past 3 days, something the baker noticed since she regularly buys her lunch here.

- Gunhild Bronzebuckle makes an attempt to fake her death. With her office burnt down, all her records that she kept about the Gardefort case are destroyed, only kept in her mind. The deception will no doubt be uncovered, but it will take some time. She grabs some quick supplies and tells the PCs that she'll wait for them outside the west gate, but will leave on her own by sunrise.
- Gunhild will need to sneak into Rochefort, where she will attempt to hide in her cousin's house. She will need the PCs assistance in getting in, contacting Thangreka, and getting to their house.
