# Notes

- While in Rochefort, the Evergreen Company attended the Grand Colonial Ball, a feast hosted by Lady Adeline Châteaufoy to celebrate the founding of colonies of Prateria, Regno Drelevo, and Grondeloup.
- While the banquet was being prepared, Sky and Davona went to go see Darawesh's house in town, and retrieve Davona's inheritance. They were accompanied by Arina, Argus, and unbeknownst to the rest of the team, Cethin followed invisibly. Darawesh's house was in the middle class residential area of the city, and also served as his workshop.
- At the front door, the party felt watched. Sky noticed fluttering drapes of the front window of the house on the other side of the street. The mage casted *message* on the party, and quietly informed the rest of the party. Davona noticed that the door was already unlocked. Arina leapt up, and caught the sill of the second floor window, but fell onto her backside into the front bush when she tried to climb into it. Meanwhile, the rest of the party carefully crept inside house. Argus remained outside, standing guard at the doorway.
- The inside of the house was dark. Everything seemed as how Sky and Davona remembered it to be for the brief years they lived here with their father. However, Sky and Arina noticed little things. Things seemed to be placed too normally, lines of dust not lining up with objects, scuff marks on the floor. Sky deduced that someone must have been in here looking for something, and then placed everything back as they were.
- The group descended to the basement, where Darawesh's workshop was. Sky remembered late nights of spying on his father at work. He remembered seeing Darawesh finish pushing his workbench back in place. Upon closer inspection, he found hidden extra latches attached to the workbench.
- Meanwhile outside, an elderly human couple walking their dog came down the street. They pause upon seeing Argus, and asked him what he was doing at Mister Darawesh's house. Argus refused to answer, which increased the elderly man's suspicions as to his armored and well-armed presence in this residential area. Argus glowered and imposed his presence upon the couple. The couple are taken aback. "This neighborhood is certainly going to the dogs," gasped the lady. The man warned that he is going to notify the guard. Bringing his weapon to bear, Argus warned them to leave and that if the guard come, he will know it was them. The couple scurried off with their dog.
- In the basement, Sky figured out his father's hiding system, and unlocked the workbench and move it, to reveal a hidden safe. Cethin revealed himself behind the party, surprising everyone. He then proceeded to attempt to unlock the safe. He found and disabled a trap, shocking Sky and Davona. He then unlocked the safe. Within it was a suit of heavenly full plate armor and Darawesh's special tool vest. Unlike the rest of the house, the safe and its content did not appear to be disturbed. Davona took her mother's armor, and put it on. Sky went up to inform Argus, who acquiesced vaguely.
- With both of Darawesh's inheritence secured, and Davona visibly upset, the group were ready to leave. Cethin once again slipped into the shadows, now unseen. However, upon exiting the house, a patrol of Rochefort city guard arrived on scene. Lieutenant Pascal confronted the party about a distubance they were causing. Argus explained the situation, however he only served to agitate the guards. Davona made her presence known, and the guard recognized her from her time in the city guard. She explained the situation and who they were, whichdiffused the situation, although Pascal requested that they avoid agitating the locals. Davona offered to have a drink with Pascal to catch up when he is off-duty. The guard left, but not before Pascal silently gesturing at Argus that he'll be watching him.
- Before leaving, Davona noticed that the drapes from the house across the street fluttered again. Already leery of causing trouble, the group opted to ignore the suspicious activity andl leave, albeit more on guard for foul play. However, once away, they got in touch with Cethin, and asked him to discreetly scout out the house. The ninja kitsune noticed that at least one person was inside, but was unable to find out more details.
- During the next few days before the banquet, Davona and Sky met up with Pascal and some of the guard for drinks. Davona bought them drinks, and imparted her suspicions on the house across her father's. Pascal agreed to look into the matter.
- Cethin hanged out in the slummer parts of town, keeping an ear out for any interesting news. He did not uncover much, only learning about the widespread resentment if the lower-class halflings about how they are exploited by the rich upper class.
- Rishoi volunteered at the temple of Kaï-den. While she thought she was doing a normal job of tending to people's needs, whispers and rumours begun spreading of this infernal-looking woman surrounded by a divine, heavenly aura.
- Arina collected Louis, and went to go research on the people attendung the banquet. Her investigation revealed that not only Duloup and Drelevo and their respective retinue will be there, other representatives from the other major factions in Lurindor will be there.
- Jupus also went around to gather some information, but does not learn much other then what Cethin had already learned. However, he did make an effort to be discreet, avoiding certain gnomes in the city.
- Everyone also went to get new clothes. Sky had to emphatically deny Davona's query if her armor was fancy enough. Gnawfang was coming along too, thanks to a modified *cap of human guise* making her appear as a young adult gnome woman. Her faculties had been boosted by her connection to Jupus, and she was capable learning and speaking, although her habits still needed work on acting like a real gnome. Ayaki, having been raised as a princess, helped her brother dress properly for this occasion.
- On the day of the Ball, Davona checked with Pascal about what he had learned. Pascal informed her that a young woman answered the door to his guards on an 'inspection'. This woman claimed to be housesitting for a fun, but other than that, nothing aroused their suspicion.
- At the Ball, the experiences and power that the Evergreen Company have gained have marked them differently from the rest of the attendants. Some look on them disapprovingly, but many other are drawn to them. Both Ayaki and Cethin were in human forms, appearing as exotic Kutai with red hair and blue eyes.
- After an opening speech by Adeline Châteaufoy in which she introduced the three founding parties of the new colonies, everyone was invited to mingle. Ayaki, Theodore, and Rafael updated each other on how things went, though Drelevo was quite snippy with Theodore tried to play peacemaker. Adeline informed them that the Crimson Eagle Company ceased contact some six months prior. But one month ago, Adriano Scarzini, king of Lorthens, sent word that he is claiming the Ursan Highlands. While this runs afoul Rochefort's colonisation plans, the three remaining colonies should not have to worry in the short term. Adeline believes that Scarzini will need time to pacify the region and bring it under his control. Drelevo assured her that his demesne will stand as a bastion against any Scarzini. Ayaki also offered to help, but Drelevo scoffed at her offer.
- Domenico D'onoforio appraoched the Company, stating that he pleased that the Stag Lord was defeated, and thatbthe goods that were loss have been returned. He also said that Enzio Monteri is looking forward to the tripartite trade outpost being built. Sky mentioned that a magical approach is needed to pacify the area, much to Domenico's intrigue. Ayaki quickly stepped in and informed that what entails is a state secret.
- Cethin and Davona both grumble about being out of place and wearing these fancy clothing.
- Aldo Varano approached the Company. He congratulates the Company, and inquires upon their plans for the future. Ayaki replied that they are seeking new opportunities to grow their nation. Varano asked if they had heard of ‘nobilism'. Arina and Cethin actually knew what that was. It was a philosophy and social movement that purport that a special class of people are better than others, and they were called upon to lead the masses to a better life, and ar the same time be better rewarded for their service to the common masses. It was a movement in reaction against the greed and selfishness of the merchant rule, and based on nostalgia of the past before the rise of the trade lords.
- Aldo’s father, the Lord-Executor Cyrano Varano of Blébonté, is the leading proponent of this movement for the betterment of Lurindor. Aldo also believes in this philosophy, and wishes to see the colonies turn towards it. He is willing to invest his personal wealth to see it happen. Cethin determined that his motives are plainly transparent, but his actual adherence to his father’s movement was questionable. The Company told him that they would consider his proposal. After they parted company with Varano, they saw him approach Drelevo.
- Arina, a bit tired of hobnobbing with people, approached the music band, and asked if she could play a solo violin piece. The band quieted down. And the master of ceremonies announced her act. She claimed that she never played one before, and cethin was quite amused. However, Arina drew upon her power, and somehow played an exquisite violin dirge that amazed the crowd, stunning them into silence and moving many to tears. They erupted into thunderous applause upon her finishing. Many of the guests flocked to Arina, complimenting her on performance, and offering her patronage. After the ball, Arina would end up receiving many invitations for more personal and intimate audience. Among the invitations was a single green rose in full bloom. Arina gave thanks to Elyssria.

## Banquet

- Adeline Chateaufoy has invited Theodore Duloup, Rafael Drelevo, and the Evergreen Company for a celebration of their recent successes at Chateaufoy manor.
- Rafael Drelevo is present with his wife Pavetta. Drelevo founded Castello di Drelevo on Nouyan 12th, 108, establishing the colony of Drelevo Demesne.
- Theodore Duloup is present with his one of his party members. Duloup had founded Wolfshold on Minyan 6th, 108, establishing the colony of Domaine-du-Loup.
- All contact with the Crimson Eagles were lost 6 months ago. One month ago, word reached Lurindor that Damiano Scarzini, king of Lorthens of the Tassimar Reach, had claimed the Ursan Highlands.
- Drelevo is smug that he got his settlement founded earlier than the PCs.
- Other representatives of the main Trade Houses are present:
	- **Sovrani Syndicate:** Celia Sovrani is the Syndicate's branch manager in Rochefort. She is Niccolo's cousin, and oversees the Syndicate's trade warehouse.
	- **Biancardi Trade League:** Andrea Biancardi is the daughter of Trade Prince Giovanni. She is in Rochefort on behalf of her father to establish deeper ties between her Trade House and the halfling people in Rochefort.
	- **Dinovio Trade Group:** Lucio Antoni is a successful merchant and senior officer of the Dinovia Trade Group.
	- **Harkûr Trade Guild:** Soberk Harkûr is Angrid's nephew. He is in Rochefort to establish some new contracts between the Trade Guild and Chateaufoy Trade House.
	- **Montari Entreprises:** Domenico Donofrio of the Steel Peacock Company is present on behalf of Monteri Entreprises. He is pleased for the eventual return of many of their loss goods.
	- **Sotillini Cartel:** Gilda Farini, Captain of the Mariporta Marine Corps, and a senior officer of the Cartel.
	- **Teredoro Consortium:** Bartolo Vasilio, a wealthy merchant and senior officer in the Consortium.
	- **Trade Regulation Authority:** Section Chief Dorotea Sordini.
	- **Varano Trade Company:** Aldo Varano, son of Trade Prince Cyrano Varano, has been dilly-dallying in Rochefort. He is interested in becoming a main investor in any of the colonies, hoping to steer them towards nobilism and establish a noble house within their territory.

## Darawesh's House

The Ashaki agents have the house under watch, but they are not meant to ambush Sky and Davona. For one, they know that they are not alone, and two, they know that these people are now diplomatic guests of Lurindor, and they don't want to jeopardize trade relations just yet. They will observe and report back to their superiors, who will then send appropriately powerful agents to take them out.

## The Heart

Wherever the heart is, nature grows wild:
- structures made of wood begin sprouting branches and budding leaves
- flowers grow large and multiply, and their smell becomes super strong
- little fairies start appearing: objects move or are stolen, pranks varying from harmless to dangerous occur routinely
- in the wild, more dangerous creatures ambush the party, drawn to the power of the heart
