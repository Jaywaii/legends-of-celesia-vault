The murdered is an orc by the name of Kundal. Kundal is a young male Rughir orc who came down to Prateria with the refugees led by Attila. During his escape in the wilderness after the Rughir salughter, he had contracted lycanthropy, which he believes is the Ulfeddin's punishment for fleeing while his kin were killed. He has kept to himself, even after when Attila gathered the Rughir survivors. He doesn't know that he has lycanthropy, only that he feels that every few nights a month, he feels drawn to the wilderness and regularly blacks out, only to wake up with with animal blood and remains on his hands and mouth.

The halfling boy's name was Beven. His family's herd of sheep had broken free during the evening, and he was helping his father herd them back.  It was taking them several hours to round them up, and that was when he was killed. Beven's father and mother (Lauvin and Isault) are sobbing and distraught. Like the night before, after the kill, Kundal's bestial side was satiated and he stumbled back into town, finding an abandoned tent to transform. No wind this evening, but witnesses definitely heard wolf howls during the night.

Evidence of Kundal's kills can be found in his tent. A half-chewed up ear with a simple, commoner earring can be found underneath his bunk. The ear belonged to Rosina, and the earring can be identified by hers by her coworkers and landlady. If Kundal is every confronted by the PCs, he tries to run. He only runs because that is what is expected when an orc is confronted by law enforcement.

**Chase:** When Kundal begins running, he is 1 step ahead of the party. The chase ends when the party catches and detains Kundal, Kundal is incapacitated, one side becomes unable to run, or Kundal is lost from sight and manages to steathily leave the area after multiple hours. When the PCs reach 0 steps behind Kundal, they can attempt a standard action.

If the pursuers have double or more the speed of the quarry, then they catch up to 0 steps in the next chase round in a straight race. If their speed have less than double but still more than the quarry, then they catch up in the second next round. If the pursuers have less speed than the quarry, then they fall one step behind each chase round. While running, each quarry can perform 1 Impede action to slow down their pursuers.

Pursuers and quarries may perform skill checks to create and circumvent obstacles during the chase. Each success the pursuers make keeps them on pace with the quarry. If they beat the DC or opposed check by 10 or more, then that is a critical success, and the pursuers may attempt an Overtake action. A natural 20 increases the check result by 10. Each failure increases the chasers steps by 1. If their results is 10 or lower below the DC or opposed check, then that is a critical fumble, and the steps behind increase by 2. A natural 1 reduces the check result by 10.

**Obstacles:**
- *Small Obstruction:* A small obstruction is an impediment in the way of the chase, but still leaves enough room that skilled enough runners can avoid it. Chase participants must make an Acrobatics check to bypass the obstruction, which can be leaping over, rolling underneath, or pivoting around it.
- *Vertical Obstruction:* A vertical obstruction completely prevents the chase participants from going around it, and requires a Climb check or the ability to move vertically to bypass it.

**Impede Actions:**
- *Improvised Obstacle:* The quarry tosses objects behind him to get in the way of the pursuers. The quarry makes an attack roll using his BAB and his Dex modifier. The pursuers must make an Acrobatics check against the quarry's roll to avoid the obstacle.
- *Fake Out:* The quarry makes a Bluff check to throw off his pursuers. The pursuer's must make a Sense Motive to not fall for the trick.
- *Hide:* The quarry hides from his pursuers, which could be in an obscure corner or blending into a crowd. The quarry makes a Stealth check and the pursers make an opposed Perception check.

**Overtake Actions:**
- *Sprint:* Reduce your steps behind the quarry by 1 at the cost of reducing the number of rounds remaining that your can run by 2.
