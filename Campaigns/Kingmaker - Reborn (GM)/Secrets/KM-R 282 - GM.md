# Notes

- Last time we saw the Evergreen Company was Vandarkaï 20th, over 5 months ago.
- The Ashaki resistance group of Aedre and Orok and their hundreds of followers are essentially refugees in Prateria.
- Jester and Salden, 2 prisoners that you also rescued from Sandrock, have thanked you for the rescue. They are currently taking time to recover from their imprisonment and to see what Prateria has to offer. Nonetheless, they say that should you require their assistance, they will answer right away.
- The war between the Ashaki Empire and the group of nations called the Eastern Coalition (comprising of the Free Ashaki Alliance, the Kahadun Calpihate, and other smaller regional forces) is now in a stalemate of attrition after Amonkhaten succeeded in making significant gains against the Alliance. However, his mundane forces are now overstretched against the forces that have united against Ashak.
- News froms Grondeloup speak of the ongoing conflict between Theodore Duloup's people and the centaurs of the Thalamech Uplands. After several major raids, the conflict has descended into localized battles betwen Grondeloup patrols and Thunderhoof war parties. Peace negotiations have not progressed despite efforts on Wolfshold's part.
- To the west, Drelevona has been relatively quiet. Despite Rafael Drelevo's personal animosity towards your group, he has been content to ignore you diplomatically and politically. The only real interaction you have with Drelevona in any manner is through its magister, Ilario Strazza, and his involvement with Sky's Thaumadrasa. He, along with Sky, Erikos Pentras of Wolshold, and Evard Chateaufoy of Rochefort, continue to develop the new academy of magic and share their findings amongst each other while they continue planning how to teach and accomodate students.
- Rochefort continues maintain close relations with you through regular visits by its grand diplomat Johan Lapierre. Rochefort seeks further closeness between the you and the halfling merchant province through trade and cultural exchanges. Through him, you learn that Rochefort is currently focusing their efforts on supporting Duloup and Grondeloup in their struggle against the Thunderhoof centaurs. However, considering what you know of the ongoing situation east of you, you can easily estimate that Rochefort isn't devoting a significant amount of their effort and resources. Lapierre has hinted that ongoing competition and rivalries with the other Lurindor merchant princedoms is a higher priority.
- The rest of Lurindor seems to be similarly preoccupied. Your business dealings with them proceed apace, but neither do they go out of their way to increase their relationships with you, at least for the time being. An exception to that is the Montari Entreprises through their proxy the Steel Peacock company. As you recall, the town of Arbor's Rest is under the Tripartite Agreement between Prateria, Montari, and Duloup, with Steel Peacock's president Domenico D'onoforio as the town governor. Seeing that your development efforts were needed elsewhere, D'onoforio was given leave to bring in Montari resources and personnel to build up the settlement, and continues to do so.
- Your treaty with the fairy court of Queen Hazelea continues to be respected by both sides without issue. The fairies of northern Draketooth Forest are happy to keep to themselves as long as mortal Praterians do not disturb their realm. In fact, the court has been instrumental in tempering the mischief of the fey throughout your realm.
- The Bluecrest sakkadi continue to respect the established borders. A trade relationship is slowly growing between them and the rural inhabitants of your nation.
- The integration of the former Klorgfang inhabitants into your vision of the nation continues, albeit not without some hiccups. The majority of the active adult population were killed during the war, and many amongst those who survived fled the land. Hrondgren had depleted all of his settlements to fill his armies against you, leaving just the elderly and children behind. There was a small boost as the several hundred prisoners you captured were released back into society, but it is a small number relatively. The razing of the temples of Sevrash and Tiamat remain a source of resentment among the former Klorgfang inhabitants, with the vandalism using imagery of those faiths appearing every now and then. Retaliatory attacks from the trolls' former slaves are on the rise in the forms of vandalism, discriminative practices, and assaults on the weaker members of those peoples. However, overall, the annexed people are not actively opposing your reforms and overall rule over them.
- Just recently, the Argent Rose finally ended the threat of the awakened thul-koon city of Ing-gol'zha, which rested underneath the formerly ruins of Hushglow Tower. However, in doing so, a massive wave of outer cosmic energy rippled out of the city, envelopping the village of Hushglow that you established there. The alien energy drove the majority of the inhabitants insane, but a few key members of the Argent Rose retained their minds, and worked hard to stop the rioting. While they saved many, over a hundred people died from self-inflicted wounds, from attacks from other maddened people, or from accidents. Many hundred more were injured, and everyone suffered significant mental trauma. Security forces have been dispatched to secure the village, but the situation is still ongoing.
