# Notes

- The Evergreen Company train themselves to face the coming horde. They also order multiple constructions to build up Prateria. They acquire new lands, establish the new settlement of New Rughir for Attila's orcs, and begin construction of watchtowers. Arina raises up a small army among her followers, while Davona has hundreds of her own retrained into a much more effective combat force.
- There is some changing of positions in Prateria's leadership. With Osla's departure, Louis assumes the role as Pontifex, and Cortez replaces him as the nation's Frumentarius. Tabrir embarked on a mission to speak with the leaders of Lurindor and garner more support. While he officially remains as Prateria's Legatus, he has granted Rialla the authority to act in his stead as she sees fit.
- Cortez embarked on a daring spy mission into the lands of south of the Greenbelt. Through guile and stealth, he manages to acquire the number and locations of all of Klorgfang's armies, and it is not good news. Klorgfang maintains 14 separate armies, 6 of which are north of the Greenbelt. Two have already acquires the old Stag Lord's fort, and the Silent Bastion.
