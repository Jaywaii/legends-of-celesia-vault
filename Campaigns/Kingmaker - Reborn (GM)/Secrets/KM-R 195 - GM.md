# Notes

- Desert break team escapes from Jafarkhet through the sewers. However, in their attempt to scout all eight gates of the city to see if the prison transport exits from it, they are detected by city guards along the walls.
- Domenica sends clones to distract the guards, allowing the team to escape. They make their way to Orok's temporary fall back base. They open a door to Promethea and all four hundred Orok and Adre followers evacuate to Promethea before Jafarkhet's forces reaches them.
