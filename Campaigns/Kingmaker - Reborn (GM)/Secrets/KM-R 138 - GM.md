# Notes

- Now suspecting Louis as their number one candidate to be the leak and forming a plan to catch him in the act, the Evergreen Company at Promethea communicate their new orders to Davona via their *far crystal*. Although they do not reveal the reason why, they tell Davona that she should keep Louis on close watch, and to never let him be alone. Davona does not know why they need her to do this, but she does not mind, and spends the entire time discussing matters of defence with the halfling, and just generally getting to know him better.
- The Evergreen Company rapidly move south on Arina's sunlight shields, and rejoin their armies at Orn Sini. Kign Sootscale grants them one of the more luxurious hovels the kobold village. However, soon after their armies arrive at Orn Sini, their scouts report that Hrondgren has begun crossing the Greenbelt across Jupus' temporary bridge. That night, Arina calls a meeting between all members of the Evergreen Company, King Sootscale, and army commanders, including Colonel Delisle of the 2nd Rochefort Fusiliers, Attila Rakirsson, and especially Louis. Sky protects the room with *mage's private sanctum*, *detect scrying*, and makes himself *invisible* beforehand. Their scouts have just updated them that Hrondgren's forces will have completed their crossing just before midnight, and Davona is chomping at the bit to move south and confront them. It is then that Arina informs everyone of her vision of Norsgurd during their trek to Silverich's Horde, and that she had learned how Hrondgren has been anticipating their moves. "It's treason, then," Arina declares. Knowing that that was the signal, the invisible Sky casts *haste* upon himself and the party, then *dominate person* on Louis while everyone else is still in shock at the news. Despite some strange resistance, the spell takes effect on Louis. Sootscale and Delisle are shocked, and begin reaching for weapons before Arina calms everyone down. The Praterian Consul explains what Nyphadora had told them, and how that led them to realize that Louis was the most likely candidate to be leaking their plans to the trolls, either willingly or unwillingly, thus their current actions. Delisle asks what will they do with him, and Arina explains they plant o keep him secluded and under watch to understand why and how he has been aiding their enemy. Jupus searches Louis' person, but is unable to find any implements to allow him to secretly communicate to Hrondgren. Sky mentally commands Louis to stay close to him at all times.
- Arina explains their further plans to quickly move west and strike down Silverich's Horde. King Sootscale nervously asks about the enemies to the south. Arugs suggests that they turtle here in Orn Sini, and let them crash into them into this defensible position. However, thier initial orders indicated that they would turlte in Promethea instead. Davona informs the group that since receiving those orders, she had remained close to Louis, and he would not have had the chance to relay those orders to Hrondgren yet, as far as she could tell. They alter the plan, then, instead moving north as if to go to Promethea to lure Hrondgren's forces closer to the north sooner than later, then double back to Orn Sini, and face him there. They issue orders to deploy the three legions and the fusiliers immediately.
- A little after 1 am, the Castrum Argentum scouts update the Company that Hrondgren's forces have completed their crossing. They left one harpy unit to occupy the area, while the rest moves north. As they continue moving north, Hrondgren leaves another unit of mounted goblin archers occupy the land they pass through. Seeing their plan working, the Praterian forces immediately double back to Orn Sini. While both armies now require to rest, Prateria has successfully baited Klorgfang into committing an attack on Orn Sini, where the defenders will have the advantage of fortification and hilly terrain.
- At Orn Sini, Jupus begins building defensive trenches with *expeditious excavation*, and Sky begins fortifying a part of the fort with *guards and wards*, still keeping Louis close to him. Speaking of who, Sky attempts a *share memory* spell on Louis of his last time communicating their plans to Hrondgren. However, although the spell successfully takes effect, Sky is unable to find any memory of Louis leaking information to their enemy. Sky asks him directly about it, and Louis tells him that he has not, that he does not know anyone in Hrondgren's camp. Louis is asked about on whose behalf did he appear to Arina, and the halfing also cannot remember it, except that fairies were present. Doubt now sets in among the Company that they suspect the wrong person, or maybe that this was not Louis at all! Nonetheless, with Hrondgren pretty much knocking at their gates, they must move forward on this. The sylph asks his would-be apprentice Carsca to arrange a 24-hour watch on Louis in one of Orn Sini's cells. He also tells her that when all of this is done, that he would like to have her meet with him in Promethea to discuss a new position for her, much to her delight. About an hour later, after the Company turns in to rest, Sky is urgently wakened by a kobold guard stating that Carsca needs him at Louis' cell.
- When Sky arrives, Carsca is closely monitoring the halfling behind the bars. Louis is resting upon a cot, seemingly asleep. His eyes are half-open, and Carsca whispers to Sky, pointing out that the halfling's lips are moving. Sky leans in, and is able to hear Louis whispering in a dull, monotone voice, all of their plans up to now. As Sky leaned forward, he suddenly notices a small pixie hiding behind Louis and listening to him intently. The pixie is confident that no one is able to see through her faerie veil, but is unaware that Sky's sight has been enhanced with *see invisibility*.
- Silently, Sky casts *wall of force* to trap the pixie in the cell. Immediately, he orders Carsca to wake the others, then calls Arina's name. The Consul wakens as her *unspeakable name* is activated, and *scries* on Sky. "This is why we need *see invisibility*!" Sky blurts out. "There's a pixie in Louis' cell! Come quickly!"
- The pixie realizes she has been found out, and casts *jester's jaunt* to appear outside of the cell, but Sky was ready. As soon as she appears, Sky gets off an overclocked *hold monster*, and the pixies is held in place. Meanwhile, Arina suddenly arrives at the cell, having sped her way with *fading leaps*. The rest of the Company is alerted and wakened as word passes through Maalek's collective. After quickly giving herself *haste*, Arina initiates *relativity burst* to slow down time for herself, then rushed to the pixie, grabs it by the wings, and executes a *tornado slam*, throwing it into the wall. As it collpases to the ground, Arina mentally calls out to Nyphadora, asking her to whose court is this pixie loyal to. Arina feels a pulse from the green-hair ring around her finger, and Nyphadora's sweet voice whispers into her ear, *"This one is a member of the Court of Shadows."*
- With that information passed along to the others through the collective, Sky steps up to the paralyzed pixie. "I think I've had enough of this," he says. He touches the pixie with a finger, and *disintegrates* her into a cloud of colourful sparkling dust. As the dust disperses, only Arina hears a faint, ethereal masculine chuckling. "Starting to put the pieces together, Angelcakes! I'm coming for you!" Arina calls out into the air. Sky makes a mental not that Arina's condition is worsening.
- Louis, having been wakened byt he commotion, is now sitting on the cot, rubbing his temples. He asks what happened, the last thing he knew was that he tiredely went to sleep. Sky reassures him that he'll be all right going forward, though the security measures will remain for the time being. As Sky leaves, he gives Carsca a light pat on the head, which leaves her beaming brightly. As the others rush into the room, Arina explains that Louis was being subconciously mentally manipualted to recount the day's events in his sleep to a fairy of the Court of Shadows.
- There are no other incidents afterwards, and everyone is able to a good amount of rest. At 10 am of Vandarkaï 31st, the Argentum scouts report that Hrondgren's forces have begun to move, and will arrive at Orn Sini around noon. At noon, all three Praterian Legions and the Rochefort Fusiliers are fully rested and ready for battle, a combined force of a little over five thousand troops. Hrondgren's forces of seven thousand, combined from his own horde and the diminished horde of Norsgurd, approach Orn Sini. However, they do not engage in battle right away. In dismay, the Praterian forces see that the Klorgfang forces begin encircling the village. After an hour passes, Orn Sini is now under siege.
- The Praterian commander debate on what to do. Orn Sini was small enough, and built underground, that Castrum Argentum was built atop of it, so the enemy will have to smash through the fort to enter the city. The enemy have placed their heavy catapults out of range in the southeast direction. They decide to set up their bombard and cannon in the northeast corner of Castrum Argentum, where they will be in range of the catapults when they approach to attack the walls while being away from immediate threats. They decide that they will make a sorties to the north, to eliminate as many troll-riding trihorn cavalry, and lure the catapults closer for the bombard to hit them.
- At 1 pm of Vandarkaï 31st, 111 6A, the Second Battle of Orn Sini begins.
