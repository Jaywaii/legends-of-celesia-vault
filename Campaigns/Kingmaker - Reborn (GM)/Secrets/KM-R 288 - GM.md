# Notes

## Kaïshan 114

- Cortez returns info:
	- The son of Hrondgren and Hildr (Hroskvir, born 105 6A) was last seen in the company of the kobold Tartuk in the Carpathosi city of Thalmosa. They sought refuge with the Demodokos Trading Company, a cyclopian merchant syndicate with trade links across multiple regions in southern Asteropa and northern Khaliram. Once the pair of refugees entered their offices, Cortez' network could no longer track them. The DTC has a fleet of merchant airships that could have transported them almost anywhere. One note that Cortez did add that might be relevant to your interests is that the DTC was the main trading partner of the Amiatti Conglomerate in Carpathos. Since the Amiatti's bizarre disappearance in 98 6A, the DTC's activities in Lurindor have taken a step back in the grand scheme of general trade between the Lurindor Compact and the Carpathosi League.

## The Coil

- Another case of vandalism against the Argent Rose Prateria Lodge occurs as a brick is thrown through a window during the night.
- The Coil begin secretly importing gangs that it formed in Lurindor and in Tassimar's Reach. The goal is to set them loose on the non-human inhabitants to foment non-human anger against the Praterian government. An arrangement is made with the Fang & Claw Society for the Coil gangs to prey on non-human collaborators, and F&C gangs preying on Praterian loyalist.
- The gangs don't know that they are being funded by the Coil. Only the gang leaders know they are being supplied and directed by an unknown 3rd party (the Coil in disguise) who offer cash, weapons, and opportunities for criminal entreprises.
- No actions take place yet, as Security still remains strong.

## The Fang & Claw Society

- The FCS continue to foment dissent withing Greentusk province.
- The FCS begin forming their own gangs of disaffected youths to attack Praterians. With an understanding in place with the Coil, they will direct their gangs to prey upon businesses loyal to the Evergreen Company and not those under Coil influence.
- No actions take place yet, as Security still remains strong.

## Jupus

- The Coil sabotages Steamflogger Industries supply lines by bribing or buying off their suppliers. Steamflogger's foreman Sebastien Durant reports to Jupus that this month, their suppliers informed him that they would be unable to fulfill Steamflogger's business needs due to shortages and increase demand from elsewhere. The result will be that Steamflogger will lose 10% of the gp they would normally make during this month unless Jupus succeeds a DC 35 Merchant check where he will be able to find additional suppliers at the last minute.
