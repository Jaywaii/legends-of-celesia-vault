- Rishoi, Argus, and Jupus went to the Jade Lounge to speak with Rocco Leonti. The Teredoro champion is already under surveillance by plains clothes guards. The proprietor and staff of the Jade Lounge are taken completely by surprise by the appearance of three of the ruling leaders of Prateria at their doorstep. They readily comply with their request to speak with Rocco in a private room. Rocco is first angry at the interruption of his fun, then taken aback when they Evergreen Company revealed the plot that he is supposedly involved in. Reluctantly, Leonti acquiesces to remain under guard as long as they do not interrupt his activities, and that the Praterian leaders should really be speaking to his masters. He does confirm, however, that he was planning on seeing a woman of certain importance tomorrow evening. Before they leave, the Company orders their guards to keep watch over the champion, and they decide to confer with Sky to have him watch via magic.
- Arina, Osla,and Davona went to the Lurindor Embassy to speak with Elvia Sovrani. The Diplomat enters the reception room, dressed only in a red silk night robe with a a light sheen of sweat, having obviously been interrupted from some late night physical activity. After getting some privacy, the Company explains plot they discovered. Perplexed, but not surprised, Elvia will speak to her security, and agrees to meet in the morning to agree with a plan. Declining an invitation to have a girls' night with Elvia and the young lady athlete she is currently entertaining, the Evergreen Company departs the embassy to come up with a plan to prevent Sovrania's assassination.
- Before going to see the Teredoro diplomat, Rishoi, Argus, and Jupus informed Sky of what is going on. They ask the sylph if there is a better way of keeping an eue on Rocco. Sky suggests that he can *scry* on the athlete, but he will need to get some hair from him. While Sky does that, the others head back to the hotel where the Teredoro delegation is staying. Sky heads down to the Jade Lounge with the aid of a guard who knew the way, enchanting the both of them with *fly* to save time. Again, the staff is surprised to see another leader of Prateria at their door. Fortunately, the plainscloth guard assists the Haruspex, and informs the sylph that Rocco is currently drowning himself in booze and girls to recover from the earlier night's revelation. Along with two other guards, they distract Rocco from his partying. Sky, now *invisible*, cuts a piece of Rocco's long hair. Although a skilled warrior, his inebriated state causes him to fail to find Sky, only knowing something knicked him form behind. Leaving the arguing champion and guards, Sky discreetly leaves.
- Rishoi, Argus, and Jupus arrive at the hotel to already hear Fidenzio Teredoro loudly shouting at the guards, upset with the disturbance and the incident. The Company explained the situation, but Fidenzio remains angered. He agrees to cooperate, but promises that the Consortium's leadership will hear of this debacle.
- Throughout the day, Lumina and Cortez continue their observation on the Sootscale delegation. They observe that King Sootscale seems to be deferential to Gren, the lead champion of the Sootscale athletes, and the athletes themselves more dominant over the Sootscale leaders. Their behaviour becomes less dominant as Lumina and Cortez approach stealthily, making the two think they were caught. They back off, and Cortez leaves to report to the Company. Lumina hands back and continues to observe. She loses sight of the kobolds as they enter a closed off room at dawn of the next day, but she does hear someone chanting. However, she cannot make out the words or the purpose of the chanting.
- The possibility of Rocco being a sleeper agent under magical manipulation came up during discussion. In the morning, Arina visits Rocco at the Jade Lounge. While the athlete snored in the arms of a trio of courtesans, the Consul activates *enchatnment sight*, and sees that he is under the effect of a compulsion spell, a *suggestion* spell to be precise.
- Arina rejoins her companions, and the entire Evergreen Company visits Sovrani at the Embassy right after and share the plan. Elvia agrees to their plan, which involves Sky and Osla examine her room in order to *scry* or *teleport* to it, and to have a room next door reserved for the Company to stay in and watch over Elvia and her nightly companion for this evening.
- After exiting the embassy, Sky and Osla hear, but not see, someone's footsteps on the roof top of the Varano villa. Sky fires a *web*, but fails to catch whoever is there. Varano guards come running out, shouting demands at the Company to explain themselves. Cyrano Varano and the Biancardis are quick to follow. Arina, drawing upon her mythic power, exudes such a powerful and awesome presence, that the Varano patriarch quickly allows the Company to fly up and try to chase their unseen observer. Jupus, unable to fly, remains on the ground beside the delegation. He takes up the opportunity to reassure them and discuss business.
- Atop the roof of the villa, Argus is able to find scuff marks on the tiles, leading them to off the rooftop. Lumina is able to speak with the plants of the courtyard, and the grass informs her that someone unseed dashed through them, about medium height, but heavier than Lumina. The tracks lead to over the villa's walls. Over the wall, Lumina is skillful enough to continue tracking the unseen observer. The Company flies into the sky, and many Promethean, busy with the festivities, look up in awe at their colourful leaders dashing into the air above them. Lumina causes an area to erupt in vines to try to *entangle* the invisible quarry, but only manages to catch hapless citizens. Rishoi descends to take care of the people, while Arina catches up to Lumina. The Consul, through her blindsight, is able to determine the presence of the invisible target, and *shadow pins* him. At Arina's direction, Davona and Argus dive fast at where Arina says the target is. Davona misses, but Argus does not, and is able to trip the person. Sky casts *glitterdust* at the area, causing everyone within, Davona and Argus included, to be covered in magical glitter and blinded. Osla also catches up, and with the target now outlined in glitter, is able to cast *hold person* on him. The glitter-covered figure ceases to move. The Company has caputred their unseen observer.
