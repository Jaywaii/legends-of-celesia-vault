# Notes

- Although the war with Klorgfang has gone surprisingly well for Prateria, with four enemy armies that altogether outnumbered the Praterian four-to-one defeated, a review of all of the battles thus far has revealed to the Evergreen Company that they were ridiculously lucky in their victories. Although their strategies and tactics, coupled with their own incredible individual power, played a significant part in their successes, as well as several tactical blunders on the part of the enemy, there were too many instances where the Praterian forces miraculously escaped damage when they really should have suffered more casualties. In fact, in the three battles thus far, Prateria only suffers two deaths in total. Knowing that Klorgfang still had many more armies in reserve, the Evergreen Company decides to not push their luck. With the enemy forces immediately within reach of their southern border neutralize, they choose to pull back, take on a defensive stance for now, and build up their forces.
- On their way back, Legio II destroys as much Klorgfang infrastructure as they can. The army razes Azrhulig's Fort to the ground, so as to not allow the enemy to retake it. They also destroy one of the bridges over the Greenbelt River. They do not, however, do the same to the Stag Fort, as that location holds significance for the Evergreen Company.
- With the loans provided by various faction in Lurindor, Prateria is able to expand their territorial claims and build up infrastructure. The Evergreen Company focuses on completing the needed administrative work while their forces earn so much deserved rest and train. Sky also completes several magical items for the Company, and acquires a *ring of sustenance* for Bekka. Among the issues the Company has to deal with are a growing number of rumours and dissenting opinions being spread throughout their citizenry, each quite harmful to the stability of the nation and the war effort. Suspecting outside influence, Cortez is provided additional funds to counteract enemy espionage in Prateria, and Attila is formally appointed as the realm's chief enforcer.

- With the defeat of 4 armies, Klorgfang reconsolidates its remaining forces into 2 armies, one led by the Marshal Silverich, and the other by General Norsgurd.
