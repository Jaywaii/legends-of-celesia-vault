# Notes

- After a failed operation that resulted in Aedre's capture, hundreds of their followers killed, and the rest scattered to numerous hideouts, Orok and about thirty of this trusted agents have been hiding out in Jafarket for the past several months. The goal was to recover from the defeat, getting back into contact with their network, and finding out where Aedre could be held if she still lived. Orok and his team found refuge in the cellar of the Sandy Camel tavern which was built atop of the ruins of a temple of Kaï-den. The owners of the tavern are part of Aedre's network of followers. They made efforts to remove all traces that the temple was there, but kept a small shrive in a hidden room in the cellar. The cellar has secret access to the city's sewer system.
- On the street, Asako, Domenica, and Maalek are walking with the Jafarket captain Kasmut and his lieutenant Taia on their way to the nearest gatehouse to get replacement travelers' papers for them. Sky is flying invisibly above them, and attempted to *dominate* the captain. However, the captain resisted, and became alerted of the potential threat. He signals Taia to be on guard as well. The three members of Operation Desert Break feign ignorance and wariness similar to the captain. Quickly, Sky summons several succubi, and orders them to dominate the captain and his lieutenant.  With five succubi, success was assured, and Sky had them order the guards to sit alongside the street. Over Maalek's mental collective, Sky tells the others to play along. Sky then takes the time to properly *dominate* both guards under his own control. Once secured, he has the succubi make a show of entering a planar dungeon by conjuring an illusion of a planar gate, and then ahve the succubi 'enter' through it before dismissing them.
- The group then heads to the gatehouse. The guards greet the captain, and Sky has Kasmut explain that he needs to re-register these three into the log and get papers for them. The guards are surprised at this irregular protocol, but Sky has Kasmut coldly inform them that this is strictly a confidential matter above their pay grades. Still unsure, but not unaccustomed to secret schemes from the higher ups, the guards let the captain do his business without any further interference. With the team's alter egos forged into the records and the needed papers finalized, the party and guards returned to the Sapphire Djinn.
- Before entering, Sky has Taia guard the back of the inn, while the captain enters it. While the rest of the team secures their room with the innkeeper, Sky has Kasmut go to the bar and have a drink. Kasmut's other two drinking companions, the two who remained at the inn while the captain and the lieutenant left to go get the papers, approached their captain to see why he's at the bar drinking alone and where the lieutenant was. Hastily, Sky made the captain wave off the guards and tell them to go home and to leave him alone. The two guards took a long at their captain, then each other, then said their good nights to him before leaving the inn. Sky then had Taia come into the inn from the back door, causing a bit of a raucus in the kitchen before entering the common room. The wizard had the two of them sit at a table and talk idle chatter.

- Orok has been hiding out in Jafarket for the past several months. The goal was to recover from the defeat that resulted in Aedre being captured by Ashaki authorities. Orok and his team found refuge in the cellar of the Sandy Camel tavern which was built atop of the ruins of a temple of Kaï-den. The owners of the tavern are friendly to the Kaïdenites. They made efforts to remove all traces that the temple was there, but kept a small shrive in a hidden room in the cellar. The cellar has secret access to the city's sewer system.
