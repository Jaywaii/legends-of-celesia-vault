# Notes

- Although the Hazel Court was now liberated from the clutches of the Klorgfang Kingdom, it remains in enemy territory. While other fairies can retreat to the realm of Faerie, Hazalea, as a dryad, could not. Sky suggests that it may be possible to help Hazalea bind herself to a new tree. While Hazalea could do that herself, she would be putting her at risk of dying without even coming close to finding an appropriate tree. Sky, however, reveals that he had learned of a way to do so safely. Inquiring where Sky learned such knowledge, as Hazalea admits that only powerful hamadryads are able to accomplish such feats, Sky reveals that they learned it from their dealings with a powerful named Elyssria. Hazalea momentarily drifts back into her memories at hearing the name, and Sky detects a hint of recognition followed by nostalgia in her expression. Hazalea informs them that she knew the name Elyssria, for she was a queen of dryads that was much beloved by all. However, she had long ago gone into seclusion, and had not been seen or heard since. The Evergreen Company shares that they have had dealings with Elyssria, and that she had aided them in the past, displaying their rings of green hair on their fingers. Hazalea tells them that she did sense a powerful blessing of fairy magic upon them, and asks if they are her champions, but Arina hesitantly admits that they are deeply indebted to her for her aid, and though they suspect she acts towards her own goals, she has never acted against them, and only ever given them ways to help them. Hazalea tells them they should consider themselves blessed, for that is likely the best outcome for any partnership with such powerful fey.
- The Company come up with a more simpler way of protecting Hazalea. They would use magic to excavate a portion of the land around her tree, apply magic upon Hazalea to protect her on the journey, and transport the tree to the Gnarlwood. Tyg-Twitter-Twig and Percilash are especially excited at the prospect of their friend being their new neighbour, and having tea parties every day. Hazalea agrees to the move. Sky heads to Rochefort to gain the necessary *move earth* spell. Once done, he returns to the Hazel Court with Percilash and Tyg's guidance, and begins excavating enough land to keep the tree safe. After several hours, the tree and its base becomes free from the ground. Sky then casts *life bubble* upon Hazalea, who then melds with her tree. All of the remaining fairies, the faun Falchos prominent among then, gather around or on the tree. Arina then manifests her sunlight shields, arrayed into a large platform underneath the excavated land and tree. Upon her will, the platform lifts the tree, and everyone present, into the air, then fly towards the Gnarlwood.
- Once at the Gnarlwood, Tyg and Percy quickly lead them to an approriately beautiful glade to replant the tree. Sky uses *move earth* once again to carve a piece of the land to plant the tree. Once the tree has been replanted and the land around them appropriately blended together, Hazalea emerges from her tree, ad informs everyone that the replanting was successful; her connection to her tree informs her that it remains healthy and comfortable in its new home. All of the fairies cheer, and an imprompty party of fairy music, dances, and food erupts among them. Hazalea once again thanks the Evergreen Company for their second gesture of aid.
- The Evergreen Company return to their original plan of occupying enemy territory along their western flank. Sky returns to Promethea, commissioned by Arina to construct *far crystals* to allow them to communicate of long distances. Rishoi also returned to Promethea to take care of local affairs. Arina, Argus, and Davona startoccupying a string of Klorgfang territory from the north of the Draketooth Forest connected to Castrum Aeris, down south to their territory around Castrum Ferro. However, on the evening of the 27th of Vandarkaï, Arina receives dire news from Maalek of Legio I, stationed in the western Castrum Ferro, and Louis of Legio III, stationed in the eastern Castrum Lapis. Two Klorgfang armies, numbering over five thousand troops each, came within the scouting range of each army. Arina realizes that a new offensive has begun. She is able to quickly meet up with Argus and inform him of the situation. They quickly discuss a plan to consolidate their forces, and Arina then heads back to Castrum Aeris to leave a message for Davona, who should be returning any day soon. With her amazing speed and movement technique, the Consul is able to travel to Promethea, Castrum Aes, Castrum Lapis, and Castrum Argentum to issue orders, all withing several hours. Legio I and Rochefort's 2nd Fusiliers 1st Battalion will abandon Castrum Ferro to meet up at Castrum Aurum with 2nd Fusiliers 2nd Battalion from Castrum Aeris. Meanwhile, 2nd Fusiliers 4th Battalion will leave Castrum Aes, and head south to join Legio III and 2nd Fusiliers 3rd Battalion at Castrum Lapis. Legio II at Castrum Argentum in Orn Sini will stand by to aid either the forces at Castrum Aurum to the west, or Castrum Lapis to the east.
