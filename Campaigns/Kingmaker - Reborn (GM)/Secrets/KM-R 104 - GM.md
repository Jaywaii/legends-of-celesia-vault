- Osla continues to meet with the religious representatives, this time with Giovacchin Bellini of Lucial. She gives the church of Lucial an open invitation to build their shrines and temples in Prateria's settlements.
- Arina meets with Adéline Châteaufoy where the leader of Rochefort gives the Praterian Consul her opinions about each Great Trade House, the Trade Regulation Authority, and explains how almost all trade houses in Lurindor are in some way shape or form a subsidiary of one of the Great Trade Houses. She especially advises Arina to be wary of the Sovrani Syndicate who currently control two of Lurindor's trade regions (their original seat of Nuevo Venicar and Lurindor's capital itself, Coracentia) ever since the disappearance of the Amiatti Conglomerate over a decade ago.
- Arina and Jupus later goes to meet with Selene Dinovio, the Santo Venito representative. Jupus leads the discussion, focusing on what trade could happen between Prateria and Santo Venito. Selene explains that the Dinovio Trade Group is always looking for new business ventures, though the two sides should set up an embassy with each other first before establishing economic treaties. Throughout the meeting, Selene is shown to be quite accident prone. Jupus makes sure not to smirk with amusement, though how successful he is in doing so is unknown. In the end, not much comes out of the meeting aside from each side become more familiar with each other.
- At the end of the day, the security forces report to Argus and Davona. They report that more suspicious individuals have been stalking the Lurindor embassy, the Châteaufoy lodging, and the hotels where the Teredoro and Montari delegations are residing.
