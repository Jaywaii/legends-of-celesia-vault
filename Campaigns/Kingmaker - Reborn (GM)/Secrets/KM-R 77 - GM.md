## Expedition to the Northern Heart

### Trial of the Hussars

Green hussars demand payment without words. They are satisfied if the PC let them jab their lance into them, dealing normal damage. The green hussars then dip their bloody lances into the earth, and a small sapling emerges.
- 6 green hussars emerge from the brush and surround you. They lower their lances and point it straight at you. They stand just outside of reach, waiting as still as a real tree.
- DC 25 Sense Motive check reveals that the green hussars have no hostile intent towards the party despite their current posture, although they remain on guard. They appear to be waiting for the party to act.
- DC 25 Knowledge (nature) or DC 20 Lore (Old Arborfane) check recalls that the Old Arborfane allows more freedom to move within her to those who willing feed her their blood.
- Those that willingly pierce themselves on the hussars' lance lose 5 hp which remains loss until they leave the forest. The blood that drips from the hussars' lances hit the earth, and small samplings emerge forth.
- The hussars attack those that try to break through the circle without the blood payment. When the hussars are killed, the Old Arborfane is angry.

### Seeking a Guide

The Erlking of North Arborfane, along with a hunting party of alseid rangers, confronts the PCs. The PCs must bargain with him to gain passage, and can also gain a guide to the Heart of the Forest.
- *There is a commotion throughout the forest. A hunting horn blows in the distance. The sounds of hooves slamming into the ground approach your position. The approaching figures first appear as deers, but as they come into view, they reveal themselves to not be deers, but actually tauric creatures with a lower body of that of a deer, and an upper torso of a wood elf with antlers on their brow. However, the largest figure leading the herd was quite different. A regal elf lord, cloaked in wings of autumn leaves, rides upon a great deer. He, too, bears a pair of large antlers upon his brow. The elf-deers surround you, drawing their bows upon you. The antlered elf lord speaks in an echoey voice: "I, the Erlking of North Arborfane, demand to know where you mortals intend to go. The Old Growth may have allowed to pass her borders, but I guard the path you find yourselves upon. To cross me is to cross the Old Arborfane."*
- The PCs will need to satisfy the Erlking's question, by Diplomacy or Bluffing. Failure results in the Erlking demanding that they leave the Old Arborfane, escorted by the alseid. If the PCs resist, they will be attacked, by both the Erlking's herd and the Old Arborfane. If the PCs answer the Erlking satisfactorily, he nonetheless requires them to complete a task. *"Your answer is satisfactory, but I still cannot let you pass freely. A toll must be paid, one in the form of a task. Old Gnash has recently awoken, and in a terrible mood. In her rage, she is causing unnecessary destruction. Find her and end her swath of destruction, and we shall see if you are worthy of moving further within the Old Arborfane."*
- [[Old Gnash]] is an advanced razor boar, centuries old. She periodically hibernates for years before waking in a terrible mood. However, this time, her mood is further worsened by outside means, caused by the Stag Lord's theft of the central Heart of the Forest recently. If the PCs kill Old Gnash, the Erlking allows them to pass, but is disappointed. He will forewarn the zmey of the PC's approach, and ensures that the PCs path is fraught with dangerb, increasing encounters and the Suvival DC to navigate the forest. If the PCs succeed in subduing Old Gnash and calming her, the Erlking is impressed. He warns the PCs of the zmey [[Drazekmir]] guarding the Heart, and pacifies the path to it, allowing the party to travel at normal forest speed. Old Gnash is affected by a permanent *rage* spell. It can be dispelled as normal with *dispel magic* or countered with *calm emotions* (requires a successful caster level check, otherwise the rage comes back after the spell expires). It can also be removed by performing a ritual, but it will require Old Gnash to remain still for the duration of the casting. The *rage* spell has a 20th-level caster level, and the caster level check DC is 31.
