# Notes

## Hushglow Calamity

- In the aftermath of the outerspawn threats, there is growing concern, and even anger, among the population of Prateria. The past month has seen more casualties in such a small amount of time not seen since the Evergreen War; 97 deaths at Hushglow village plus over two hundred inhabitants suffering from mental trauma, and 148 deaths from the Outerspawns. It was already strongly assumed that the Argent Rose Society was responsible for the madness of Hushglow village, but rumours are now abound about their involvement in the recent disappearances and murders. Anger among the people towards the Society is rising, and there are demands as to what the government is going to do about it.
- The Coil seeks to take advantage of the bad press. Their agents in Prateria are behind much of the rumourmongering.
	- Prateria agrees to finance a support fund for the survivors of the victims of what's being called the 'Hushglow Calamity' for the next 15 years.
	- Rishoi personally goes on a tour to speak with the survivors, and arranges for grief counselors and support network for them.
	- As Chapter-Sage responsible, Asako is technically reduced in rank (from Silver Sage to Rose Scholar). She will be allowed to remain as Prateria Chapter-Sage, but will have to work extra to bring herself back to Silver Sage, and her position will be open for election in 116.
