# Notes

- Prateria recruits 1,500 mercenaries in occupied Nykizou and Grunrahmen, a mix of Bluecrest sakkadi and halflings from the north.
- Rishoi recruits a thousand followers. By swearing fealty to her and taking an oath of poverty, they no longer need to eat, drink, or sleep. Furthermore, they no longer age, and can manifest sunlight energy weapons. These followers become known as 'Rishoi's Immortals'.
