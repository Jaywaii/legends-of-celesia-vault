# Notes

- Evergreen Company only gives the faun to the Wild Hunt.
- Evergreen Company arrives at Duskleaf tea party.

## Trials of the Old Arborfane

### The Wild Hunt

- End of the Hunt
	- If the PCs kills the fawn and hands her his heart, the Queen accepts it it in a mournful manner. *"Your curse has ended, my beautiful child,"* she declares. The next time the PCs look at the fawn, an elvish teen, with white hair, nearly pearly skin lies on the forest floor in its place, his white toga crisply stained with red blood from the hole in his chest. His open eyes stare upward to the forest canopy. With black tears running down her pearly cheeks, the Wild Hunt Queen declares,*"Newcomers, you have succeeded the hunt. As a reward, the path to your goal is now open to you. Depart, and pray we never meet again."* With that, the Wild Hunt gathers the body of the fawn boy, draping it with a cloth. His mother takes him into her arms, and the Wild Hunt gallops into the forest as mist surrounds them, and vanish.
	- The PCs can give her the Heart of the Forest, in which case the Queen's eyes light in delight. *"Your skill and generosity shall be remembered, as will the wisdom of your decisions. The path lies open to you. May you have fortune in what is to come."* Mist surrounds the Wild Hunt as they begin to gallop away before quickly vanishing. The white fawn cautiously approaches the PCs, lowering its head in deference to them. The Old Arborfane once more becomes hostile to the PCs.
	- If the PCs fail the chase, refuse to give her anything, or refuse the Wild Hunt, the Queen curses them, and leaves them to be lost in the Old Arborfane. The curse of unluckiness becomes permanent.
	- In order to give the Queen 'the heart', the PCs can beseech the Queen to accept the fawn's love through a Diplomacy or Perform (oratory) check. Success conveys the fawn's love to the Queen, moving her to weep silver tears. Every successful vision interpretation grants a +2 bonus to the check. *"Your words astound me in their beauty. My chest aches from the feelings you have conveyed to me. You truly have granted me the heart."* She extends her hand to the fawn. *"Come, my child. I now understand your heart. I forgive you, and let us move forward on a new path together, a new way to hunt to soothe your heart."* As the fawn approaches the Wild Hunt Queen, its body shimmers and shifts, and after a few steps, it is no longer a fawn, but a young elf-like boy with pearl-white skin and toga with little stubby antlers protruding from his forehead. With similar silver tears, he embraces the Queen. She looks back to you. *"You have granted a great boon. The path lies open to you, and I offer you this gift."* With a whistle, she extends a finger, and a blue songbird flutters from above out of nowhere and lands on her finger. With another whistle, the songbird lands on the PC's who made the check's shoulder. *"This songbird will provide you with clarity when your choices are blurred."* She lifts the child onto her mount. *"This hunt has come to an end, the prize has been one. I wish you well on the trials to come, and I hope that your mistress may one day receive the same gift you have bestowed me."* With that, mist rises around the Wild Hunt as they gallop away, and then vanish.
