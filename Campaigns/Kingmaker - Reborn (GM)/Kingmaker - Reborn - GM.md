# Setting

In the 98th year of the Age of Reckoning, Lurindor's Crown-Executor Castiella Amiatti and her entire family vanished one night during a storm with green lightning. Since all the shareholders of the Amiatti Trade Company were those very same members, the ruling Trade House of Lurindor had effectively vanished. On that same night, every copy of the succession papers was mysteriously erased, no matter what form of magical protections were laid upon them.

In the midst of the initial chaos, Sovrani quickly acted and initiate a series of hostile takeovers of Amiatti's holdings. Most were bloodless, if coerced, business deals, but Sovrani did not hesitate to use force when necessary. The chief executor of The TRA, seeing the nation about to fall into chaos, recognized the claim. The other Trade Houses disputed the claim, and are currently tied up in the courts trying to remove House Sovrani from power. The nation is now ruled by Interim-Executor Niccolo Sovrani, the man who orchestrated the takeover.

The halfling houses of Châteaufoy, Beauciel, Chamblé, and Terrebonne saw this as an opportunity to push for independence and self governing of the halfling people of Lurindor. However, before they would be able to take steps in that direction, they first had to secure their southern border, and that meant taming the Viridian Marches.

The Viridian Marches is a region of Valendar that has defied colonization. It remains home to a number of wild fey, monstrous beasts, and tribes of ferocious people. Its name comes from the Viridascian Demesne, the territory belonging to a clan of green dragons, established in 56 Age of Starlight and lasted until 2175 Age of Starlight when the Aerlonesti Empire killed many of the Viridascian dragons and scattered the rest. The elves built several outposts and a few settlements in the region, but then the empire fell, the troops were recalled and the wild reclaimed the land.

During the Age of Kings, the League of Stars attempted to colonize the region, however the dominant fey and wild monsters prevented them from fully doing so. The descendants of these colonists eventually moved northward, and became the humans of the Venicar Empire and the halflings of Valderêve.

Today, in addition to its native inhabitants, the Viridian Marches has become the main source of banditry and barbarian raids plaguing Valderêve. It is also vulnerable to conquest from the bandit kingdoms of the Tassimar Reach to the west, another threat to the halflings of Lurindor. In order to fully focus on their northern neighbours, the halflings must have the southern region secure, preferably with an ally state.

To this end, all four clans pooled their resources together. House Châteaufoy petitioned Interim-Executor Niccolo to tame and colonize the Viridian March. Using large bribes, Châteaufoy managed to gain enough support that Sovrani had little choice but to grant the Crown's permission, although he forced Châteaufoy to abide by several conditions:

- Funding for all expedition and colonization will be the sole responsibility of House Châteaufoy.
- In exchange for complete self-governing (any many concessions on the part of the halfling clans), Lurindor will receive 5% of any new colony's monthly income. in addition to whatever return on investment deal Châteaufoy makes with the new colony. This arrangement will last for 10 years after the colony's founding, after which the arrangement can be re-negotiated.
- Any new colony will nominally be a vassal state of Lurindor, and therefore have open borders and free trade between each other. Breaking these treaties will be considered an act of sedition and betrayal. Lurindor will have an embassy and an ambassador in the colony's capital. However, aside from maintaining those treaties, the colony founders may rule their colony however they wish.

The Viridian March is divided into four parts, from west to east:

- Ursan Highlands
- Drakethorn Glades
- Evergreen Plains
- Thalamech Uplands

The Ursan Highlands will be explored by <u>Crimson Falcons</u>, an experienced adventuring company. They were to pacify the region by pushing out the <u>Storm Bears</u> tribe and make diplomatic contact with the city of Lorthens of the Tassimar Reach. Hills and plateaus characterize this region. A month into exploring the Ursan Highlands, the Crimson Falcons were led into an ambush. The Falcons were led to believe that they were to meet with a friendly tribe, but they were attacked in the village where they were supposed to meet. Although the attack was a success and the Crimson Falcons were wiped out, there was one survivor. This survivor was ordered by Falcone to flee when the tide of the battle turned against the mercenaries because they had the best chance to escape. the captain's last word to them were. "It's not just the Storm Bears! Find out who!" Indeed, many of the Storm Bear attackers appeared to have hastily painted on tribal markings.

The Drakethorn Glades will be explored by an aristocrat descended from the old nobles of Lurindor. <u>Rafael Drelevo</u>, a keen, but ruthless, merchant and a skilled diplomat, has made his own fortune and is accompanied by a heavy guard. The southern edge of the Glades touch the northern border of the aggressive cyclops nation of Carpathos. The goal is to secure a permanent trade route between Lurindor and the Issos. This region is dominated by the glades and swamps.

The Evergreen Plains are the least actively hostile of the four regions. It's fertile land and abundant forest is home to many fey, several tribes of kobolds and mites, wandering monsters of all types, wild animals, and the most dangerous predator of all, bandits. While there are many dangers in the Plains, as long as one takes care to avoid them, one can thrive here.

The Thalamech Uplands will be explored by a halfling merchant adventurer. <u>Théodore Duloup</u> is a friendly and jovial character, smart, experienced, and very adventurous. His goal is to do whatever it take to pacify the Thalamech centaurs in the region and establish a trade route with the Holy Radimus Empire. He establishes the town of Wolfshold.

Unbeknown to anyone, an ancient wyrm green dragon has her eyes set on the Viridian Marches. A descendant of the Viridescian Dragons, she seeks to reclaim the ancient demesne of her ancestors. The Rastolis were green dragons in disguised, led by the great wyrm Malachiost. The Trade Wars came about when Malachiost and his loyalist became drunk on power. His younger sister, Viridescia took the guise of Amiatti and overthrew him and his loyalists. In the 98th year of Age of Reckoning, Viridescia and her entire brood (all disguised as Amiatti), retreated to hidden enclaves to sow chaos in Lurindor. Through disguise and agents, she has been manipulating the various factions in Lurindor that will allow her to take over a strong Viridian Marches and Lurindor.

Little does she know that the fey also have plans for the Viridian Marches. The key to defeating the crazed nymph was placed in this region, and she desires to retrieve it and make the land her own.

## The Fairy Plot

Long ago, the nymph Elyssria was chosen by the Fairest Count Calefaidh to be his consort. Elyssria had garnered his attention for her exceptional beauty, artistic talent, and her charisma. Elyssria was well beloved by her followers, and loved them in return. Elyssria was enamoured by Calefaidh through his courting, and their affair was a torrent of passion. During that time, Calefaidh introduced Ellessria a taste of Fairest power which she quickly became addicted to.

However, Calefaidh was selfish and fickle. Although Elyssria remained enamoured with him, Calefaidh had long grown disinterested in their liaison. However, what did interest the fey count was Ellessria's potential. Calefaidh wanted to experiment and see how far he could push this once simple fey through the overly complex machinations of fey courts.

Through subtle manipulations, Calefaidh pushed Elyssria to pursue more and more loftier goals. Elowynna rose through the ranks of the fey, and grew more and more prideful. She eventual caught the interest of Oberon. However, Elyssria became too deluded with her fame and power, and made the deadly mistake of openly courting the Fairest King in the presence of the other Queens of the fey.

Backed by Titania and Mab, the Morrigan cursed Elyssria, and took from the nymph her most potent ability: her capacity to love. The three queens then took that power and refashioned it in the sword *Briar*, and flung it into the Prime, where the sword's nature would obscure it from Elyssria's sight. Elyssria herself was exiled to her realm, the Whispering Vale, that she had crafted at the peak of her power. There, Elyssria changed.

Without her love, she became cold and unfeeling, and the void left in her ached unendingly. Elyssria could still charm and inspire others into following her, but she now felt nothing for them. She did not care for their welfare as she once did, and would now inflict fear and pain upon them at her whim without hesitation or regret.

Elyssria grew obsessed with restoring her status and ending the emptiness within her. She came up with a plan to present a gift to the Fairest so unique and fantastic that they would have no choice but to restore her. She would take the land of the Viridian Marches, long considered by the Fairest to be a jewel of fairykind sullied by the touch of mortals. She would then transform it into the greatest work of fairy art by merging it with the Whispering Vale, bottle it, and present it to Oberon and his Queens.

In order to do so, Elyssria would need to create special seeds that would need to be planted at specific ley lines throughout the Viridian Marches. In addition, these ley lines needed to be purified and prepared by elaborate fairy rituals and architecture. It would take Elyssria centuries to do so, but now that her love was gone from her, the nymph queen had no problems being patient and focused enough to accomplish her goals.

To allow her agents prepare the Marches for her plan, Elyssria would manipulate the mortals from the shadows to prevent them from bringing the land under a unified rule. Over the centuries, Elyssria's manipulations have forced mortal nations to abandon the Viridian Marches because of disasters or war. No one nation was able to control the realm for beyond a short period of time.

During all of this, Elyssria was obsessed with another matter: the sword *Thorn*. When they ripped love out of Elyssria, the Fairy Queens had prophesied that when she comes in contact with the sword, it would be the death of her. Elyssria became obsessed with locating *Thorn*, not only because of the threat to her that it represented, but also because she was subconsciously driven to reunite with the part of her that was stolen. However, the agents of the Fairest did their best to keep it hidden from her thus far.

Elyssria has now reached the point where her seeds are ready to be planted in the Viridian Marches. Once the sites have been prepared and the seeds planted, they will bloom with the power of Faerie, effectively bridging and merging the two realms together. Faerie dust will spread from the blooms, spreading throughout the Viridian Marches to allow Elyssria to pull the land into Faerie and merge it with the Whispering Vale.

However, Elyssria is not unopposed. Due to events in Lurindor, that nation will begin another attempt in securing the Viridian Marches under their rule. The Viridescian dragons also seek to reclaim their stolen land. Other fairy rulers have their own plans, either to oppose Elyssria, or claim the Viridian Marches for themselves.

## Void Singers

The same thinness of reality that makes the Viridian Marches so close to Faerie also makes it vulnerable to the Void Beyond. Long ago, Void cultist established a tower on Wickerlight Isle. They were eventually attacked by their enemies and dispersed, and the tower was destroyed, but the Void Singers never stopped trying to establish a foothold in the region.

Their goal is to secure the release of the Whisper Between Breaths. To this end, they have been conducting vile rituals in various spots in the region to strengthen the bond between the Viridian Marches and the Void Beyond, acts that offend Elyssria and the Old Arborfane.

When the PCs establish their nation, the Void Singers shift tactics to infiltrating the population and corrupt them from within.

## Banditry of the Viridian Marches

<u>The Stag Bloods</u>

In the Evergreen Plains, the bandits are unified under the brutal command of the Stag Lord.

<u>The Red Hoods</u>

The Red Hoods, led by halfling bandit Lerouge, terrorizes the Thalamech Uplands, attacking Lurindor caravans and raiding centaur camps. The gang is composed of many discontent halflings.

<u>The Locarno Gang</u>

Led by Gina Locarno, the Locarno Gang preys on travelers throughout the Draketooth Glades. The gang hires many sakkadi and bogwort warriors as enforcers.

<u>The Blood Bears</u>

The Blood Bears are Storm Bear barbarians that were exiled or fled from the greater tribes for their crimes. Many of them banded together to prey on their former tribes and travelers who pass through the Ursan Highlands.

# Prologue

It is the 108th year of the Age of Reckoning.

Only a little more than a century has passed since Zhao-Khan, God of Power and Tyranny, engulfed the world of Celesia in his Tyrant's World War. The world survived and recovered, but the turmoils if the past have given way to new challenges.

A decade ago, a new power emerged within the Viridian Marches. The bandits of that untamed land unified under the iron grip of the Stag Lord. Other rival badut gangs formed, and all preyed on the southern trade routes of the merchant kingdom of Lurindor.

At the same time, all members of Lurindor's ruling merchant family, the Amiatti Conglomerate, vanished without a trace, leaving Lurindor's leadership in disarray. Niccolo Sovrani, leader of the Sovrani Syndicate, engineered a brilliant takeover of the remaining Amiatti assets, becoming Lurindor's Interim-Crown-Executor, and leaving him as the most powerful and wealthiest man in the nation.

While Niccolo focused on stabilising his new powerbase, Rochefort's rulung halfling clan, the Châteaufoys, sensed an opportunity to remedy many of their problems. In exchange for Rochefort's support to his rule, Sovrani has authorized the Châteaufoys to fund a colonisation effort of the Viridian Marches.

Lurindor's terms for the colonisation efforts are simple:

- Once the colony has been established, it automatically enters into an alliance with Lurindor, meaning open trade between the two sides, and establishing a Lurindor embassy in the colony.
- Rochefort will be responsible for the initial funding of the colony, though the parties may enter into separate agreements with other parties on their own initiative for additional funding.
- For the following 10 years, the colony must render unto Lurindor 5% of its monthly income on a monthly basis. After the 10 year period, this term can be renegotiated. Rochefort herself has graciously declined any additional tribute for her part in organizing this effort.
- In exchange for these terms, the colonies will be considered a fully independent and sovereign nations, and may be ruled however the founders wish.

However, Rochefort's efforts will be closely watched by other nations also eager to claim the Marches.

To the west of the Viridian Marches lies the Tassimar Reach, realm of rogues. Although not unified, it was Tassimar's last attempt to claim the Marches that led to the recent rise in banditry in the region.

To the east lies the Holy Radimus Empire, the theocracy of human pride. A staunch nation dedicated to the humanocentric god St. Radimus, the Holy Empire would be eager to find new lands to convert to their religion.

To the south list the cyclopean city-states of Carpathos. The cyclops have tried time and time again to claim the Viridian Marches, but each attempt ended in failure.The desire for this rich land, combined with the prospect of capture new slave labour, will not doubt draw their attention.

As for Lurindor's latest attempt to tame the Viridian Marches, Rochefort has already enlisted three parties to their cause:

- The Crimson Falcons is one of Lurindor's most famed independent mercenary companies, having won many battles in Valroy's numerous revolutions and defending Lurindor assets from Norgrimm orc raids. Led by Captain Adrianna Falcone, the Crimson Falcons aim to pacify the Storm Bear tribes of the Ursan Highlands.
- The merchant company known as Drelevo Entreprises, whose ownership was recently inherited by Rafael Drelevo, has been contracted to establish a foothold in the Drakethorn Glades. They are tasked with pacifying the local sakkadi and bogwort tribes so that trade routes from Lurindor to Carpathos and neighboring Issos can be established.
- Famed halfling adventure Theodore Duloup has formed his own company to settle the Thalamech Uplands. He will face opposition in the form of the native Thalamech centaur tribes.

All that remains is for one more party to take up the challenge of pacifying the bandit-ridden land of the Evergreen Plains.
