## Fang & Claw Society

The Society of the Fang and the Claw quickly formed soon after Klorgfang's defeat and annexation by Prateria, spurred on especially when Prateria razed all centres of worship to Tiamat and Sevrash in Elsaren 111.

With Vorakios' awakening, the Society has gained financial and magical support from the Cult of the Undying Eye and Carpathosi leadership starting as of Karthoran 113.

**Goals:**

- Restoration of rule of might
- Restoration of slavery
- Restoration of the worship of Tiamat and Sevrash
- Death of the Evergreen Company
- Conquest of the Evegreen Plains

**Leaders:**

- **Ingrahild** (female troll witch): A devout worshiper of Tiamat who spearheads much of fostering discontent and gathering recruits.
- **Cybethaia** (female cyclops oracle): An anointed of Xarios and member of the Cult of the Undying Eye. She has been tasked by the Cult and the Carpathosi leadership to support the Society to cause as much trouble for Prateria from within.
- **Sharguk** (male orc shaman): An anointed of Sevrash.
- **Harzhak** (male hobgoblin rogue): Spymaster in charge of their hidden operations such as intelligence gathering, stealing funds, sabotage, assassinations.
- **Torsgurd** (male troll barbarian): Enforcer in charge of their fighting force, defending their bases, preparing for the day to rise up against Prateria.

**Bases of Operations**

- **Seormaw** is the main base of the Society due to the proximity of Carpathos and the furthest away from Promethea.
- **Greentusk** is the second focus of the Society due to its high troll population and being the former capital of Klorgfang. However, the Society has to limit their activity there because of Prateria using it as the provincial capital, so there is a higher number of Praterian officials present. Nonetheless, Greentusk is vital to the eventual uprising both for its people and defensive location.
- The Society is present in the rest of Klorgfang's settlements, but their numbers are not significant. **Grenflyth** and **Skytalon** would have been other notable bases, but as with Greentusk, Prateria has placed a larger number of loyal officials in charge by making them provincial capitals as well.

**Activities**

- **Subterfuge** is used to portray as the Society as a support network for widows and orphans and veterans on hard times among from Klorgfang citizens due to the war. They pretend to be willing to work with Prateria to ensure a smooth transition of rule.
- **Recruitment** and **training** are primarily their main activities. The war had depleted most of the former Klorgfang citizens of fighting age, leaving only the young and the old. The Society has to fight against Prateria's liberal policies and propaganda to seduce the minds of the young to their cause.
- **Gathering funds and resources** to arm their members, aided by smuggled goods from Carpathos.
- **Smuggling** and **theft** of Praterian goods and wealth are the next step, both to deplete the enemy's capabilities and to increase the Society's supplies. These activities are limited and only done when absolutely certain to not be caught as the Society is not ready to reveal itself.

## The Coil

The Coil's infiltration of Prateria was due mainly to Enzio Montari. The Coil sees Prateria as a ripe target to spread their influence. However, due to the Evergreen Company's close ties to the Argent Rose, they lost their chance to infiltrate the highest levels of government. So for the time being, the Coil is concentrating on controlling businesses and gangs on a street-level.

**Leaders**

- **Master Python** (male human rogue) is the leader of this branch of the Coil. His public persona is "Gaspario Vespari", a wealthy merchant with numerous branches in 5 of the Lurindor trade provinces under the umbrella of the Vespari Conglomerate, dealing in ship building, siege engines, transport, and protection services. He is the only one to report to the mysterious "M" back in Valdargent.
- **Sister Naga** (female human bard) is in charge of intelligence gathering and recruitment. Her public persona is "Magdelena di Caliara", an opera singer and philanthropist.
- **Shadowscale** (male human ninja) is in charge of shadow operations. His public persona is "Cosimo", a wandering peddler.
- **Mistress Asp** (female human wizard) is in charge of funding and resource gathering. Her public persona is "Gemmana di Donado", a wealth merchant.

**Bases of Operations**

- **Arbor's Rest** is the centre of Coil operations thanks to Montari Entreprises involvement. With Prateria allowing Montari to take control of development and staffing, the Coil is able to insert many of its agents through this entry point.
- **Promethea** is a primary target, but it is too secured. The Coil originally tried forming local gangs, but the increased security has ended that plan. Instead, they are now focused on subsuming local businesses.

**Activities**

- **Recruitment/infiltration** focused mainly on a network of businesses. Focus on buying out and sabotaging competition. They are also trying to insert themselves into government positions, but that is proving more difficult due to the abundance of fanatically loyal followers of the Evergreen Company.
- **Funding** and **gathering supplies** through their businesses in order for bribes and acquiring weapons and tools.
