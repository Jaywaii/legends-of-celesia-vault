# Notes on Locations & NPCs

**The Viridian Marches**

A huge swath of uncolonized lands made up of the Ursan Highlands, the Drakethorn Glades, the Evergreen Plains, and the Thlamech Uplands

**The Ursan Highlands**

The westernmost region of the Viridian Marches. Adelaine Chateaufoy of Rochefort sent one of their most famed independent mercenary companies, the Crimson Falcons, to colonize these lands but the expedition ended in failure and they were not heard from again.

·         **Captain Adriana Falcone** – She is the head of the Crimson Eagles sent to colonize the Ursan highlands. They did not show up to the banquet. Adelaine lost contact with them 6 months after we left to colonize (6 months before the banquet) and is presumed dead.

·         **Damiano Scorzini** – The self-proclaimed king of Lorthends. He has claimed the Ursan Highlands. He came to power 10 years ago ousting the families that once ruled Lorthends. He is described as an ambitious man so the expansion is not too surprising. He is also described as a man of great talents and power, and is known to be a strong patron of the arts.

**The Drakethorn Glades**

A region of the Viridian Marches directly west of the Evergreen Planes. Adelaine Chateaufoy of Rochefort sent the merchant company Drelevo Enterprises to colonize these lands. They established the nation of Drelevona.

**Whisperlight**

An island in Lake Iridescence in Prateria where the Whyngard Asylum was located. Nestled on the secluded island of Whisperlight, the imposing Whyngard Manor stood as a beacon of both opulence and obscurity. Originally the ancestral home of the enigmatic Whyngard family, the manor took on a new identity under the stewardship of Dr. Claudius Whyngard, a figure as brilliant as he was eccentric. With his vast wealth and unorthodox methods, Dr. Whyngard transformed the estate into the renowned Whyngard Asylum, a sanctuary for the troubled souls of society. Behind its grand façade, the asylum housed dozens of disturbed minds, offering them respite from the torment of their afflictions. Yet, whispers of strange occurrences echoed within its walls, tales of patients emerging from its confines changed, their once fractured minds now imbued with an eerie clarity and purpose. As quickly as it had appeared, the Whyngard Asylum vanished from Whisperlight, leaving behind a legacy shrouded in mystery and speculation, its true purpose and the fate of its patients forever entwined with the island's enigmatic past. The Manor is adorned with art that is old and very expensive. The manor has been warped and is decrepit and has clearly gone through some conflagration. The paint on the walls is flaking and there is rot all over. There are arcane engravings visible all over the floors, ceilings, and walls. After the defeat of Dr Claudius Whyngard the Manor collapsed into another dimension.

o   The Nobilists are adherent to the ways of nobilisms, seeking to create a ruling class based on responsibility, power, and conduct. They believe that a certain type of person is born with the right to lead the rest of the masses, and should therefore earn special privileges to aid them in their tasks and reward them for their effort.

o   Some historical texts date this organization back to the early days of the Age of Kings (over 15,000 years ago). A few of Asako's experienced instructors dissuaded her from looking into the coil. Whenever mentioned, these nations would either come to ruin or adopt more oppressive policies. EC discovered their interference during the Viridian Games. Maintaining an open and free society will dissuade the coil from recruiting. They generally recruit from the middle and upper class that have a lot of free time and access to resources on their hands and are more likely to disagree with the current status quo. Alternatively, they recruit from dissuaded youth. They are a human supremacist group.

**The Old Arborfane**

An ancient and magic forest that separates the Evergreen Plains from the Thalamech Uplands except for a 12-mile-wide passageway in the North via Arbor’s Rest. It is home to many fey. Time passes erratically in the forest and it is difficult to navigate due to heavy magical interference that can makes one lose their bearings. Without proper rituals, non magical metals will rust after a time. The forest also held the fog of Vorakios at bay, preventing it from entering the passageway from Grand-de-Loup to Prateria. The source of the forest’s magic comes from three “Hearts of the Forest” which EC recovered and replanted using the proper rituals. The locations of the Hearts are kept secret by the EC.
