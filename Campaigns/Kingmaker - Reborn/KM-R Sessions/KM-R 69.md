# KM:R 69

- The Evergreen Company continue planning their assault on the Dancing Lady.
- Sky, Davona, Tabrir, and Argus teleport to Rochefort. They purchase a scroll of commune from the temple of Tierra, and Tabrir uses it to commune with a power aligned with his philosophy. He comes into contact with an entity that inspires dread and finality, yet also comfort, which he suspects might be a deity of death (which is correct for it was Jeshakhet the goddess of death who answered him).

- Sky is unable to find a scroll of teleport to purchase. He is directed to seek out Rochefort's magister, Évard Châteaufoy, at the Rochefort Academy of Arcane Arts. He meets with Évard, who is happy and eager to meet with a fellow magister and person of high intellect. They hash out a deal for Sky to acquire the *teleport* and *shrink item* spells. In exhange, Sky provided a detailed report and analysis of their mythic power and promises to report an analysis on the Dancing Lady and her fairies. The two arcane spellcasters conclude their meeting with hopes of an ongoing professional relationship between the two.
- Davona, Argus, and Tabrir head over to Castle Rochefort, and meet with Johan Lapierre, grand diplomat. They speak to him of their desire to acquire siege weapons' a light catapult and two ballistae, and hope to purchase them from the Rochefort government. Johan is reluctant to part with any of his nation's armaments, but goes to consult with the Rochefort general.
- Johan returns with General Romain Chevalier. Although not initially open to the idea of selling their own weapons to a foreign nation, Tabrir convinces him that it would help develop good relations between the two states. As the amount of 3 siege engines was a relatively modest request, Romain agreed to negotiate, requesting a sum of 4,000 gp for the lot. Tabrir successfully negotiated the price down to 3,500 gp.
- After later acquiring enough cold iron weapons to equip one reinforced platoon and one company of troops, the party regrouped at Asako's shop and teleported back to Promethea. Sky later returned to Rochefort with Lisa and Samara so that they could also learn the *shrink item* spell and help shrink the siege weapons for transport back to Promethea.
