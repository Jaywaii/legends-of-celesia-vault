# Previously

*After defeating the Coil's ambush in the Skelthelbugh University's storage, you took captive the Head Librairan Isolda Greghain and the Dean of Physical Sciences Rhinsley Morroway. It was in fact Rhinsley that struck Isolda from behind near the end of the fight and immediately surrendered to you. She could not give much information due to a geas placed upon her and having been forced to swear an oath of loyalty to the organization that she and Isolda belonged to. However, she could admit that she had long come to regret her youthful choices, and is seeking a way out, mainly you. She also revealed the presence of the Cult of the Mother was present among the University's staff, that they know about Team 13's true allegiance to the Argent Rose, and that her organization is separate from the Cult.*

*Myria was able to maintain her magical charm on Isolda. Despite obviously being upset, Isolda was convinced by Myria to not resist being captive, at least for now. You then proceeded to make her invisible and disguise Rhinsley as one of the students. You then snuck back to the Obscure Collections wing of the Library, and used a key you found on Isolda to open the hidden door to the secret passages of the University. With knowledge of the hidden Marks of the Mother, you were able to make your way past the passageways traps to another hidden door. After unlocking it, it opened up to a descending set of stairs carved into a much older and more natural underground tunnel. This led to another network of much older, more natural set of tunnels with certain passageways marked with the Mark of the Mother. This led you to a constructed set of rooms. On one side was a shallow pit fill with a half-dozen freshly slain bodies. On the other side was a torture rack with another man on it. The man was covered in cuts and blood. When Yujimbo approached, they finally recognize the man as Mardwyn Carth, the caravaneer who transported the team to Skethelburgh, and who was supposed to have left earlier this morning. The bodies in the pit were his teamsters. Waking up for a moment, Mardwyn recognized Yujimbo, and was barely able to whisper "They know who you are... get out of here..." Mardwyn then gasped and gurgled, as his chest pusled, then a verduous slime burst out of him. As if on cue, the pile of bodies started rumbling as more slimes slithered out from beneath them, and even more oozed out of the cracks from the ceiling above.*
