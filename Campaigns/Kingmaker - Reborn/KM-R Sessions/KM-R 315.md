# KM:R 315

# Previously

Under Arina's order, Sky assembles a team composing of Asako, Maalek, and Qu'tari. Needing more allies, Asako quickly called in member of Team 13 who were immediately available, those being Yujimbo, Tao, and Igvald. This team traveled to Viriquia, and offered their service to the Marquis as proof of Prateria's sincerity in helping the Radimites. The Marquis dispatched them to the city's southern marketplace with orders to bolster the defenses until the reinforcements he is assembling can move in there.

At the southern market, the team was informed that they need to help fleeing citizens coming from the easter road. At the same time, a runner comes up from the southern road desperately requesting aid for Capt. Constanis' position. Losing that defensive point would result in the loss of that district to the zombies and the fog.
