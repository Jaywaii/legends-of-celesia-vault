# Previously

On their way to the Northern Heart of the Forest in the Old Arborfane, Tobias was abducted by the mysterious Court of Shadows. There, he was interrogated by Queen Sinmaven and her majordomo. Unsatisfied by his responses, the Queen cursed Tim with a geas to retrieve for her an enchanted locket of hair of his Queen Elyssria. He is returned to the Prime Material plane, just outside the Old Arborfane, and rejoined by the rest of the Evergreen Company. Upon returning to Promethea, Tim suffered the consequences of the curse, and was bedridden. The Company brainstormed ideas as to how to help him. During the discussion, Louis attempted to commune with his patron, and learned that the source of his power is not what he thought it was. In the end, Tim was relocated to the hospital, and Tabrir and Osla traveled all the way to the city of Ferrocitta to meet with the Teredoro Consortium about acquiring a scroll of limited wish. For the rest of the Company, Jupus had sent letters to his family in Rochefort, inviting them to Promethea, but no reply ever came back after several weeks.
