# Previously

Previously on Kingmaker: At the final banquet of the Viridian Games, the Evergreen Company managed to secure promises of loans and some troop reinforcements from allied and neighbouring regions. There was much discussion of the finances needed to equip and maintain future Praterian armies. Attila Rakkirson informed the Company about Klorgfang orcs discreetly approaching his tribesfolk about having them join the troll nation. However, the Company agrees to establish a settlement for the Rughir orcs to secure their loyalty and assistance. Osla had chosen to part company with Prateria in order to lead her Bodaï followers on a journey of self-discovery. And the Silent Bastion has fallen, with all but one of the fifty Ironwatch soldier there slain by hundreds of orcs, goblins, and trolls.
