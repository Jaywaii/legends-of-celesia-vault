# KM:R 24
[[KM-R 23|← Previous: KM:R 23]] · [[KM-R 25|Next: KM:R 25 →]]

- Party rescued Havenford party from 6 perytons. Nearly all guards, Toma, and Johan were felled unconscious.
- Toma tried to get close to Davona.
- Clara tried to get close to Argus.
- Arina's arm was badly mauled by the perytons. Maribelle gave her drugs and treated her wounds in the vardo. During Arina's addled state, Maribelle performed Askulioth brainwashing rituals on her.
- Johan claimed that his family in on a spiritual pilgrimage to his ancestor's home. They traveled from Valroy, where Johan was a successful landowner. His family's traditional pilgrimage was done once a generation to this ancestral site, and doing so would bring good fortune. Due to the monsters and bandits, the last time this pilgrimage was when Johan's grandfather was just a child. Upon hearing that the Evergreen Company defeated several bandit groups and were securing the land, they decided to make the pilgrimage. Good timing too as the family business was steadily declining.
- The Evergreen Company offered to escort the Havenford's to the site, which was several days away at the southern tip of the Cypress Hills.
- They explored along the way. One night, Arina became distubingly enraptured by Johan's recounting of a traditional parable.
- On the night before they would reach the ancestral site, a ruined tower called Hushglow, the Company noticed Maribelle add some extra ingredients in the stew. Ayaki feigned not being hungry and went to tend to the horses, and Arina claimed her wound was disturbing her (which was partly true) and decided to rest in the vardo. When Johan recounted a new parable, one with a grisly ending, Arina's wound flared up. Argus and Davona became sickened by the stew, and Johan declared that their sacrifice would usher in a new age.
- The Havenford party drew weapons and wands. Battle occurred, and Arina turned on her friends as whispers in her mind took over. Every time she took back control, her wounds that Maribelle healed were undone.
- The Havenford party were killed. Arina read Johan's book, realized the hidden meaning and codes in the stories, and was overcome by the Ecstasy of the Nothingness of Existence.
