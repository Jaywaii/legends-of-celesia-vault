# Previously

*Team 13 successfully traveled to the lost world of Nobu-Shugath. You found yourselves within the Breeding Grove of Xin-car'lath's dark young. From one of the alien trees near you, you cut open a birthing sac containing a growing dark young spawn. This awakened a dormant mature dark young that came to life and attack you. In the process, it drove Arjin insane, but the rest of the team succeeded in destroying it. Despite her new madness, Arjin gained a moment of clarity to cleanly cut out the heart of the infant dark young, completely intact and still beating. The team had to quickly teleport back as the dying dark young let out an alien scream that awoke alien yangethe within the grove. The interplanetary teleport was a close thing, as Yujimbo had to shrink themselves in order for everyone to be included in the spell. However, instead of returning to the familiar Argent Rose lodge, the teleport was intercepted and redirected with foul magic back to the summoning platform in Skethelwold, where the 9 main members of the Mother's cult completed their ritual to bring the interlopers of their design before them.*
