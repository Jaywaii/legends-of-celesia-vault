# KM:R 354

# Previously

*Finishing off the last of the vitriol blights, the Evegreen Company could finally take time to recover. After healing all of their wounds, Lonán used her magic to speak with the corpse of the Forgemaster. Although successful in making the body speak, its answers to the Company's questions were cryptic. They concluded that Vorakios had made the heart of the planar dungeon his soul vessel, so in order to destroy him, the dungeon itself must be destroyed. However, the Forgemaster also told them that the dungeon could not be destroyed. He also told them his master have any weaknesses to exploit, and that he wielded death itself. The company was able to extract some information about Vorakios' lieutenants in the other two rooms of the dungeon they had not visited. In the room with the choir chanting held the wisdom of the Deathless Shroud, and the room with the starlit void threatened them with rips and tears in space. Although the Company had gained enough insight into Vorakios and the dungeon to be able to go directly to the dungeon's heart, they decided it would be prudent to go to the other rooms and first eliminated Vorakios' lieutenants there lest they join their master in the final battle.*
