# KM:R 26

- Arina, Ayaki, Argus, and Davona killed a will'o'-wisp at Hushglow Tower after returning to the surface.
- The Company hastily covered up the pit hole with stone debris. They made their way back to Onestrio's trade post.
- Along the way, Arina is attacked by the rest of the party. She is subdued, and she reawakens to find herself strapped down on the altar slab within the Hushglow Tower. Around her, Argus, Ayaki, and Davona each brandish ornate knives. As if in a trance, each of them recite in a monotone voice a strange litany:

"This is long overdue."
"The imprisonment will end."
They seven will be free."
"The Radiant Darkness"
"The Dreamer in the Depths"
"The Defiler From Within"
"The Mother Of All Horrors"
"The Bliss of Worms"
"The Truth That Lies"
"The Whisper Between Breaths"
- The three of them disembowel the Arina with their knives. Her blood flows down the sides of the slab. A light from beneath the slab begins to glow. It then shoots up a beam of light, passing through the stone and Arina's dying body into the sky. It goes up 200 feet, then shoots towards the east. As Arina's vision fades, she notices that the stars are not quite right. Then she wakes up. It was all a dream. She is still at the camp around the vardo, and Argus keeping watch.
- As Arina warily approaches Argus, her hand on the hilt of her sword, a bright light lances up in the far distance, in the direction towards the Hushglow Tower.
- The Evergreen Company decides that they have the means available to them to found their own colony, without the direct sponsorship or aid from Rochefort or Lurindor. With the help of Onestrio, they bring in the resources and people necessary.
- On Sunday, Kaïshan 12th 109 Age of Reckoning, ground is broken at the Great Cypress tree to found the settlement of Promethea, the capital of the newly founded nation of Prateria. Seeking a High Priest, Father Joram recommends Sister Félicité of Deianeira.
- After the ground breaking ceremony, the Company examines the Great Cypress tree more closely. Sky deduces that the tree is thousands of years old, and is unfortunately on its last legs. However, there is potential to revive it. If would require powerful nature of fairy magic to re-ignite the tiny spark of life still within it. Powerful and expensive druidic rituals may be needed. Alternatively, if one could somehow convince a dryad to bond with the tree, although that poses risk to both the tree and the dryad. Another method would be to implant a fresh Heart of the Forest within the tree, one that could be found in the Old Arborfane.
- Bekka is sent off on her first mission to seek out Father Joram in the Gnarlwood, and see if he can contact the fey about a dryad.
- The next day, a new away team formed by Rishoi, Sky, Argus, and Ayaki set out to the Hushglow Tower. Once there, they are forced to fight a will-o'-wisp and defeated it. Sky revealed that he can heal thanks to the vast stores of angelic blood that he extracts on a daily basis from his sister, much to his companions bafflement. He collected some of the will-o'-wisp remains.
- As they approached the tower, Sky was overcome with fear of the unknown. Despite this, the party prepared to once again descend into the depths beneath Hushglow Tower.  There, Sky believes that this city was created by the ancient and malevolent Thul-koon.
