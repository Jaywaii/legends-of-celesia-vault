# KM:R 256

# Previously

*Your team continues your quest in the Dreamlands to acquire impossible components to fulfill the sealing rituals for Ing'gol-zha. On your latest leg of your eerie journey, you find yourselves in the Dreamlands city of Barloth-Keem, a port city on the Sarmassus Shore dreamisle. Seeking shelter at the luxurious inn of Ruby Vortex, you accepted a simple task from its owner, the Ruby Mistress to retrieve the signet ring from a friend of hers, the influential and enigmatic Saffron Prince. Fortunately, the Prince was holding a banquet at his palace, open to any who prove themselves interesting. After proving yourselves and gaining entry, you entered the grand banquet hall to a bizarre sight. Hundreds of guests were in the hall, and strands of black webs would occasionally drop from the ceiling to pluck an unsuspecting guest up into the dark catwalks above. No other guests seem to pay attention to the odd kidnapping, and continued to enjoy the ball, dancing and eating, and conversing amongst themselves. Even the occasional driplets of blood from above failed to gain their attention. What was the center of attention was the rotund Saffron Prince himself, who despite occasionally coughing up black tar onto his gold and yellow raiments, cheerfully conversed with guests and having a good time. You engaged in conversation with the Prince in the guise of Myria as the Prince's long-lost niece, and observed that the Prince did not have his signet ring upon his fingers. You also went up a ladded to the catwalks to figure out what was happening with the kidnapped guests. Above, you discovered a quartet of huge Spiders of Leng, intelligent spiders from the Dreamlands Plateau of Leng, who were in the process of wrapping up their victims in webbed coccoons, and sometimes eating into them. Returning down to the hall, you now ponder on your next move.*
