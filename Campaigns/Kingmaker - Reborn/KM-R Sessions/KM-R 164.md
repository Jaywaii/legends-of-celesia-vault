# Previously

### Argent Rose

Previously on Argent Rose Occult Investigation Team 13: The team defeated the deceased Argent Rose Knight Agatha Sorliu in the depths of the necropolis of Ing-gol'zha. Sorliu had risen after her original death into a psychic stalker, haunting the Gate of the Mother. After defeating her, you uncovered the means to open the Gate of the Mother by having Raggi swallow one of the yellow globes embedded in the layer of creep covering the floor. Raggi then vomitted the globe's contents, a worm like creature that quickly grew into a fully grown sumgola, a spawn of Xin'carlath. However, the spawn was quickly defeated, and its heart served as the key to the gate. The team collected Sorliu's remains, though Igvald took it upon himself to care for her skull.

With the growing dangers that Team 13 are facing, they decide that it more prudent to retreat back to the surface. On their way, they lock the gates to the towers they previously opened. They also seal the entrance to the necropolis at the base of Hushglow Tower. Raggi makes sure his totem is secure, allowing his multiple astral constructs to remain permanently present to guard the tower and the entrance. The team returns to the Argent Rose Rochefort Lodge, and report their current findings to Chapter-Sage Joliette Beauchamp. Team 13 realizes that they still need to do more research, as well as raise the funds and resources for the material components they will need. Unfortunately, Agna learns of events in her personal life have developed, requiring her to leave the team.
