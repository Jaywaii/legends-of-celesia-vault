# Previously

Previously, on Kingmaker: Your prisoner, Puccino, managed to get himself killed by Davona after assaulting Osla. You then met up with Elvia Sovrani to share what you had learned so far. However, upon returning to Sky Emporium, you discover that it was now engulfed in flames. After the flames were extinguished, you found the bodies of three of Sky's staff, with signs on their bodies indicating that they were killed before the fire started. Moreover, the bodies of two of the Coil agents were nowhere to be found. The only other clue was the faint outline of an encircling snake devouring its own tail around the centre of the explosion.
