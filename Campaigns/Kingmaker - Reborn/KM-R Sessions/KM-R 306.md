# KM:R 306

Evergreen Company defeat zombies in Arborfane Gap by 11am.

At 1 pm, Sky wakes up with visions leading to the 2 remaining Cord fragments. He uses a spell to recall information about Anatolkerat.

Team rests, then go to Valenlet to prepare for Vorakio's mind and zombie battle.

# Previously

In the mindscape, the Evergreen Company finally defeated Vorakios and his lieutenants, stopping the flow of new zombies assaulting Valenlet. Once everyone was out, they joined with the Rochefort defenders, and quickly mopped up the remaining undead in the settlement by 2 am. Their tremendous strength and abilities shocked the regular lucklyn soldiers.

While they take some time to recover, Sky made a wish to use the Cord fragment, the part taken by Arina from Valdargent, as a divining rod to the other fragments. Immediately after, he falls unconscious, falling into a coma. Yet there are signs his mind is still active, with his closed eyes shifting rapidly, and his hand tightly gripping the Cord fragment.

Meanwhile, Arina sent a missive to Viriqia informing about the crisis, in hopes to getting a lead to another Cord fragment that might have been looted by that nation. However, a response was expected to return in no fewer than two days later. In the mean time, another threat needed to be dealt with. It was decided that the Evergreen Company will intercept the zombie horde lurching towards Arbor's Rest midway in the Arborfane Gap.

At 5 am of Starday, Asmolan 22nd, the Evergreen Company teleported to Arbor's Rest, then quickly traveled down Arborfane's Gap. At the midway point, they come into contact with the zombies. For the next two hours, the Company slaughters thousands of undead, with only minor wounds suffered thus far. However, thousands more zombies still remain, seeking their flesh, and led by an undead cyclops warrior.
