# KM:R 98

# Previously

Previously, on Kingmaker Reborn. The Rochefort delegation arrived in Promethea, and they had dinner with the Praterian Council at the Pinnacle. During the meal, Arina and Adéline Châteaufoy came to a positive understanding of each other, promising a bright future in relations between Prateria and Rochefort. Sky also shared his idea to secretly leaving a legacy within the halls of Promethea's future magic academy, and invited Erikos Pentras and Évard Châteaufoy to participate as founders, which they both agreed. Sky also plans on asking Carsca of the Sootscales and Illario Strazza of Drelevona to join in as well. The next day, the rest of the Lurindor delegations arrived, with Elvia Sovrani at their head. Arina promised to meet with her privately on the first day of the Games. The Consul also made plans to speak to Alcina Sotillina later that day before having dinner with Enzio Montari and Domenico D'onofrio.
