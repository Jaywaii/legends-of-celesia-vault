# KM:R 357

# Previously

*Having been launched into space by a warping of space, Lonán was able to fly backdown to the central island of the Shatter Observatory, while Gnawfang managed to grab the chain thrown at her by Maalek and be pulled back down. Meanwhile, the Evergreen Company kept Vorakios' pet dragon, the Voidgazer, subdued, allowing Jupus to skillfully repair the Orrery with the pieces they found. However, the Company did too well of a job keeping the dragon alive. Stealthily, it began healing itself while pretending to be unconscious. The Voidgazer only revealed its deception when Lonán examined its wounds more closely, only to see that they were not as bad as before. The dragon attempted to bite her, but the rest of the Company was ready to attack it. Having his patience worn out, Dragormir delivered a fatal finishing blow.*

*Vorakios continued to warp space, sending Jupus flying, but not before he finished completing the Orrery. Gnawfang volunteered to take the backlash from ripping open the exit portal in the Orrery. Unfortunately, she suffered the maximum backlash, becoming a walking dimensional lock until they leave the Fairy Grave. Regardless, the Company left the Shattered Observatory towards the Ossuary Choir. They entered the inside of a cathedral with walls and columns lined with a myriad of skulls, all singing hymns dedicated to the Deathless Shroud. The hymn assaulted the Company's senses, making them sick or confused, although the banners held by Argus and Asoka protected them from the latter. At the far end of the room, behind an altar, was a huge dark figure dressed in priestly regalia of the God of Undeath.*

*"Vicar, show these intruders the truth of our Lord," boomed Vorakios' voice through the skulls. The Vicar of Gloom promised to show the unbelievers the truth beyond life and death. Dragormir strode forward and pinned the Vicar down to the ground. Davona joined him, bouncing off the wall to slam down onto the Vicar. After Adriel sent her swords to join the attack, the Vicar began singing with the skulls, and eight shadows emerged from the surrounding darkness. The Vicar then slipped out of Dragormir's grasp.*

*Half of the shadows worked together to attack Davona, allowing one of them to sink its ghostly claw through her heart and sap much of her strength. The other half helped distract Dragormir, allowing the Vicar to dispel some of the urokhan's enchantments, followed by ripping out his life force with a mythic harm spell.*

*As the chanting reached a crescendo, more shadows emerged.*
