# KM:R 71

- The Evergreen Company away team was ambushed by a patrol of fairies loyal to the Dancing Lady. However, the Company was able to kill them fairly easily.
- Arina, Tobias, Tabrir, and Argus headed back to rendez-vous with the Ironwatch Moondancer Expedition.
- Meanwhile, Sky flew invisibly towards the Dancing Lady's fort to familiarize himself the siege site. He attempted to land and inscribe his arcane mark on the spot, but he made too much noise and did not notice the invisible fairies closing in on him. However, as soon as the first arrows flew towards him and the recaps and quickling appeared visibly, the Arcane Gale quickly teleported back tothe olive patch. He then flies to meet up with the Expedition.
