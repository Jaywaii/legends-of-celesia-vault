# KM:R 105

# Previously

Previously, Argent Rose Occult Investigation Team 13 were joined by two new members, Agna Boulder and Jericho Crowlen, but lost Zen. The team descended into Ing-gol'zha and unlocked the 3rd focal tower, the Gate of Radiance. Shortly thereafter, they were attacked by two ghouls. The ghouls turned out to be two members of the doomed Firesong expedition: "Diamond-eye" Connornack and Garska Drumsong. After a brief battle, the undead Argent Rose agents were destroyed by the living ones.
