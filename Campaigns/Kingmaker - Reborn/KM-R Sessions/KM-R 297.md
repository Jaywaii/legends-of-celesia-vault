# KM:R 297

# Previously

*Thanks to the power of Iolanthe's fragment of the Cord of the Ancients, the Evergreen Company managed to escape the zombie-infested Wolfshold via opening a Doorway to Destiny back to Promethea. Now safe, Iolanthe explains how she acquired the Cord fragment and how Thunderhoof lore spoke of its importance in defending against Vorakios ages ago. Iolanthe needs the Evergreen Company's help to find the remaining fragments of the Cord, lost centuries ago over the many battles the centaurs had to fight against invaders. However, once reunited the Cord has the power to shield an entire city against the evil mist. The Thunderhooves need this to protect themselves as they attempt a ritual to find the ancient bow Pyrouranos. She also informs them that Thunderhood scouts have seen cyclops armies from Anatolkerat seemingly deliver thousands of slaves to the mist.*

*Turning to the immediate threat of the mist, the Company beseeched the aid of Elyssria's representative Nyphadora. the powerful fairy informed the Company that in ages past, even Vorakios avoided directly clashing his powers against the Old Arborfane. Hoping that this remains the same in the present day, the Company prepares Arbor's Rest for evauation, and informs Châteaufoy to do the same to their settlement of Valenlet, the next town in the mist's path. The Company also began thinking of creating a ritual of their own to protect a city, and asked Nyphadora if Elyssria can help.*
