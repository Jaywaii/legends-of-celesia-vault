# KM:R 122

# Previously

Previously on Kingmaker: The Battle of Silvermine continued. The tide was immediately turned in favour of Prateria as they repeatedly decimated enemy units. With the defeat of his own comman unit, the enemy commander, the hill giant Arzhulig, called for a retreat. The retreat caused more Klorgfang units to fall, and Tribune Argus and Praetor Davona themselves pursued the remaining fleeing units and defeated them. In the aftermath of the battle, Prateria took 180 prisoners, among them were several leaders of the attacking horde. As the month of Asmolan rolls around, there are no further attacks from either side. Prateria raises 3 legions, establishes several forts, and expanded their territory. Argus later visited the Old Beldame to check in on her. She was reluctant to leave or move, and suggested that the best way to protect her from the trolls' advance was for Prateria to beat them to it. She also shared that the Old Arborfane is still in pain, a pain older than the ones that the Evergreen Company healed in the past two years, and the source of this pain lies in the south.
