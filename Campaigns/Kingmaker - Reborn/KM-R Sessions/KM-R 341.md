# KM:R 341

# Previously

Entering the Crypt of a Thousand Corpses, the Evergreen Company faced off with its guardian, the Cryptkeeper, an empowered tombstone fairy that woke the undead for Vorakio's armies. Upon seeing the intruders, the Cryptkeeper displayed that ability by dancing. A huge elemental made of gravedirt, tombstone, and bones rose from the ground, while a horde of zombies clawed through the earth underneath the Company's feet. Fortunately, everyone was quick enough to escape their gnarled grasps.

Dragomir dashed across the cemetery to grab the Cryptkeeper while the others dealt with the awoken monsters. However, the Cryptkeeper was a slippery foe, repeatedly escaping the urokhan's grasp, although not unscathed. Meanwhile, Sky flew to the great cyclopean crypt at the centre of the graveyard, and begun analyzing its runes and symbols for insight into Vorakios' story.
