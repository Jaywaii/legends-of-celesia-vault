# KM:R 299

# Previously[/h] Preparations to face the zombie gorde at Arbor's Rest were made, including using the stolen airship crewed with mages to inflict attacks from the air. However, just as the Evergreen Company was ready to travel to Arbor's Rest, horror struck Valenlet.  As soon as night fell, zombies began emerging from the gloom surrounding the Pall. Though the dome of life energy burned the undead upon contact, their numbers grew so much that enough started to break through. Worse, shadowy fey known as grimstalkers had invisibly infiltrated the settlement and started killing the few remaining guards before turning their claws on Rishoi, Louis, and Rialla.  Through the collective, the rest of the Company was alerted, and Aedre teleported who she could to the settlement. As they fought off the grimstalkers, Argus went to hold off the zombies, and Jupus back in Promethea told Colonel Amoullet to get the 2nd Fusiliers ready for battle. After the dark fey were defeated, the company opened a Door of Destiny to Promethea to allow Jupus and the regiment to pass through to fight the zombies. However, the danger had not ended as Sky discovered that Rishoi had been pulled into a psychic battle. The mage opened a mindscape door through the collective, and Rishoi's companions joined her in the mindscape.
