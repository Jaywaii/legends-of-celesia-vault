# Previously

The Carnival of Tears has arrived at Promethea, bringing death to the settlement unsuspecting inhabitants. Now a tool wielded by the would-be fairy queen known as the Dancing Lady, it has ensnared the new Praterians under a powerful enchantment. The Evergreen Company's patron Elyssria had sent her emissary, the leanan sidhe Nyphadora, to explain to Ayaki, Davona, Rishoi, and Jupus that you need to kill the Dancing Lady's chief servant, the Cold Rider, in order to break the artifact enchantment on their people. You may also yet free Namdrin Quinn from the Cold Rider's service if you manage to free the soul of his wife Tessa, trapped within a shard of dark ice. Upon returning to the carnival, your citizens, under the enchantment of the Eye of Rapture, are blissfully unaware that the fun and games have turned into horrible instruments of death, allowing themselves to be killed byt he murderous fey. Thus far, you had managed to defeat the Swinomancer at the Pie-Eating Contest, and then split up to tackle both the Ice Carving Contest, where fairgoers have become trapped in ice, and are slowly being chiseled to pieces by their loved ones, and the Ice Maze, where Gradzaal the Minotaur prowls within, preying on unsuspecting fairgoers, in addition to many deadly traps hidden throughout the maze. When we last left off, Rishoi, Davona, Jupus, and Gnawfang were within the Ice Maze, suddenly attacked by a goat-legged man with icy horns, who was soon joined by Gradzaal charging through the ice walls into Davona. At the Ice Carving, Ayaki, Cortez, and Louis fought against the diminutive frosty chiselers. Ayaki suffered many blows, but managed to direct Cortez into eliminating 2 of the 6, on top of the 2 others that were already defeated.
