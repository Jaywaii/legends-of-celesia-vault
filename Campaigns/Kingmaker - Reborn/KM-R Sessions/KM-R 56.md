# KM:R 56
[[KM-R 55|← Previous: KM:R 55]] · [[KM-R 57|Next: KM:R 57 →]]

- Tyg-twitter-twig and Percilash have accompnied Cortez (much to his discomfort).  They tell the Evergreen Party and very very important guest needs to talk them, right now. Right now! A very big big-wig among fairies. But I think her hair is natural, not a wig?
- The snowfall is getting stronger now. Heavy snowflakes drop frequently and the sky is completely covered in thick, darkening clouds. There is already 2 inches of snow on the ground, audible crunching underneath your feet, and it costs 2 squares of movement to enter a snow-covered square. Perception and Fly checks and ranged attack rolls all have disadvantage. Wind is blowing from North to South, forcing Small creatures to make a DC 10 Strength check to move in the northern direction or a DC 20 Fly skill check if airborne. Tiney creatures on the ground are knocked prone and rolled 1d4 × 10 feet, taking 1d4 points of nonlethal damage per 10 feet, unless they make a DC 15 Strength check. Flying creatures are blown back 2d6 × 10 feet and take 2d6 points of nonlethal damage due to battering and buffeting, unless they succeed on a DC 25 Fly skill check.
- Nyphadora, emissary of Queen Elyssria awaits the Company within their pavilion. She introduces herself, stating that she and they bboth serve the same lady, displaying her green hair ring. She explains to the party that Elyssria attempted to warn them about the coming danger, but her message became shrouded and unclear. Only know that their common foe's power is about to manifest could Nyphadora even manage to make herself present directly.
- Nyphadora explains that an old fairy resident of these parts has suddenly come into great power, and is now making moves to lay claim of the Evergreen Plains, and she means to do so by eliminating the other great power in the regions as her first strike. Specifically, the growing nation of Prateria. The Dancing Lady she is called, and she has long haunted this region, enticing lone hunters and travelers by dancing and singing at night, luring them to her where should could feast on their blood. Not even the Stag Lord's bandits were immune, having lost their lieutenant in the southwest part of the Evergreen Plains. Earlier on, with her new power, she compacted the service of a cold rider, and imbued her own power into his. She no longer feasts on blood, but on souls, and the cold rider excels at doing so. When Vincenzio's Carnival travelled through what she claimed as her domain, she saw an excellent opportunity to sneak her forces into Promethia. The cold rider was given an artifact of Faerie, the Eye of Rapture, which shrouds an area within a mile with a powerful enchantment and illusion magic. Those within range see only what they want to see and are filled with euphoric glee. They ignore anything that happens that does not mesh with their preconceptions. Nyphadora says that they have not activated it yet, but she expects them to do so soon. The Evergreen Company should be powerful enough to resist, but may also call upon Elyssria's boon for aid.
- To aid in the masquerade to get her forces in the carnival close, the Dancing Lady had to keep up appearance. She had already slain Vincenzio, but fortunate for her, she found an excellent replacement in the form of Namdrin Quinn. The half-elf's heart was already filled with despair, easily manipulated by one as powerful as the Dancing Lady, but he was truly bound to her service when she revealed that the cold rider still possessed the soul of his wife, Tessa Kalini. In exchange for her soul, Quinn would take control of the carnival, and lead them to Promethia. Once there, he did not have to do anything but let the Dancing Lady's servants do as they wish. The Cold Rider still possesses Tessa Kalini's soul as a guarantee for his non-interference, being encased in a dark ice crystal. Nyphadora warns the Company to be careful for another skilled swordswoman, one of stealth and shdaow, is in the Dancing Lady's service. Nyphadora paid a dear price learning everythign she knows, and she parts her dress to the side to reveal a razor thin cut along her side, only noticeable due to it a shadowy mark around it.
- She tells the party that they must go now, for she senses the artifact's power activating. The slaughter will soon begin. End this threat, for the good of your people, and defeat the Dancing Lady, for both themselves, and their shared Mistress.
- Upon arriving at the carnival, the sky has darkened, as if night had arrived early. The normally festive lights from the carnival have grown dark. Instead, small globes of light float and twirl through the air above the carnival, lighting the paths and tents of the show. Still, people continue to walk the fair's grounds, still content and excited. Suddenly, a bright light shoots upward, then explodes in a brilliant display of firewarks. Everyone stops to look, and ooh and ahh at the display. With the end of each flahs of light, the whole scene turns dark, only to be lit up again as another firework shoots up. Before you, in the ticket booth, the carny woman looks up at the sky with a wide smile of wonder at the display. Then there is darkness. Another flash of light bursts in the sky. Its light reflects upon a curved blade peeking out from behind the counter, illuminating the top tip of a blood red cap, all behind the oblivious carny who is still looking up in the sky. She cannot hear you over the cacophony of the fireworks. The blade swings, and then there is darkness. Another flash of light. The carny is gone from within the booth, as is the cap and blade. Darkness again. Another flash of light. You see upon the billboard next to the booth the flayed face of the carny woman pinned to it. The fireworks end, and instead of the cheery carnival music, a new, eerie and haunting melodyplays throughout the fair.

---

- The Company decided to make their way to Quinn to confront him and possible stop this disaster from happening. They moved to the Candy Apply tent, and saw the old lady who ran it dead in her wicker chair. Her chest was split wide open, and her organs were stuck with sticks. Around them, children and other attendees were gleefully eating pieces of flesh on sticks, thinking they were candy apples.
- The party ran on ahead to the Pie-Eating Contest. They discovered scores of people eating pies filled with rotting entrails. The head chef announced a winner, and the party saw the winner bbeing transformed into a pig and led into a stable. They also saw another pig halfway into a machine, being churned into pie fillings that were served to the contestants. The party fought the Swinomancer and defeated him (though thanks to his mythic power, stabilized at -60 hp and would have been dead at -75 hp). The pigs returned to human form, but remained under the effects of the Eye of Rapture, and went about to eat more pies, even though they were nude in a winter storm.
- While the rest of the party recovered, Ayaki rushed up ahead on Thuderhoof to Quinn's tent. Just as she arrived, Quinn had completed his dance much to the applause of onlookers. Ayaki confronted Quinn before he entered his tent. However, the Carnival's new master seemed despondent, neither helpful nor hindering either the Company or the Dancing Lady's servants. The rest of the company caught up. Taking a minute to recover, Ayaki searched Quinn's belongings, only finding 2 wands. Quinn let her do what she wanted.
- Ayaki raced ahead pass Gradzaal's cage and towards the ice carving competition area. There, she witnessed happy attendeescarving away at 6-foot high blocks of ice, their tools provided by a half dozen blueish gnomes. Although they seemed like normal iceblocks, Ayaki did witness one being chipped away, releasing a trickle of red blood. To the side, she saw a sleigh arrive with giddy children aboard. The blue gnomes proceeded to dance with them, but that resulted the children to start being encased in ice. The sleigh itself had the limbless corpse of old Mr. Sathelbry tied behind it.
- Ayaki raced back to the entrance the ice maze. The rest of the party arrived, having discovered that Gradzaal's cage was empty, door wide open, and a set of minotaur tracks leading into the ice maze. People were entering the maze, but not coming out.
- The Evergreen Company must now debate on which event to stop next.
