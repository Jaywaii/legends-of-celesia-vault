# Previously

Previously, on Kingmaker Reborn. The Evergreen Company returned to Promethea, unsure as to how to proceed. With the monthly council coming up, as well as preparations for the Viridian Games that need attending, the company decides to take a break. Sky goes with Asako to finish crafting magical items. Osla embarks on a short trip as revealed to her by the stars. Tabrir goes to the Argent Rose's Promethea Lodge research on several devils. Attila is approached by an Ulfeddin shaman of the Rughir, informing him of their tribe's need to form their own settlement. Jupus asks several of his workers to discreetly look in on his family back in Rochefort, and is in turned approached by one of his senior managers, Sebastien Durant, requesting that the Evergreen Company look into forming more official ties with specifically Rochefort. Tobias takes a break from active adventuring to start looking into establishing Prateria's spy network. The rest of the Company makes plans to explore some of the lands west of the Lonely Barrow. Davona convinces Bekka to join them in an attempt to get their Explorator more active.
