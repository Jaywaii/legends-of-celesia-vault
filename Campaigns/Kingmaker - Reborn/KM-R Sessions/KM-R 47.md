# KM:R 47
[[KM-R 46|← Previous: KM:R 46]] · [[KM-R 48|Next: KM:R 48 →]]

- Passing through the Gate of Whispers, Team 13 ventures forward through Ing-gol'zha. Time is warped for them, and the team loses track until they are suddenly overcome with exhaustion. Igvald falls unconscious, and the team halts to make camp in one of the random tunnels of the necropolis. Raggi is able to use Igvald's *wand of cure light wounds* to heal the inquisitor. Upon waking up, he helps heal his companions.
- While the team is resting, they fail to notice a transluscent hand emerge from a nearby wall. It reaches out to clench Zen from the back of his neck. However, just as it makes to grab the half-elf, Zen notices something on the floor and bends down, and the hand grasp nothing but empty air. Everyone else now sees behind the bent over Zen a transluscent, half-decomposed urokhan is adventuring gear whose features are now twisted in rage and pain.
- Team 13 yells out to Zen, who straightens up, wondering what is going on, still not noticing the ghost behind him. Everyone else, however, leaps to their feet, weapons drawn. Zen finally notices the ghost behind him with shock, as the urokhan ghost moans and groans *"Lost... lost...!"*. Judging from his gear, the team surmises that this is the apparition of Olgoth Drumsteel, the Argent Rose scout that was part of the Firesong Expedition. The image of silver pin in the shape of a rose amidst his gear, noticed by Rex, confirms this guess.
- Raggi is first to act. The kobold scholar shouts an ultimatum at the ghost, stating that it has one chance to act peacefully, or he will be forced to inflict pain upon it. The ghost makes no move to acknowledge the threat, and moves towards the tiny kobold. Raggi then leaps and swats at the ghost's leg, his hand charged with his spell, *release death's hold*, but his fatigue caused him to miss. However, Igvald casts *hide from undead* upon himself and his companions, and their presence vaniches from Olgoth's ghostly senses.
- Olgoth moans *"There is no esscaaape... no eessscaaaape...!"* The ghost swings his transluscent arm at where he last saw Raggi, and surprisingly strikes true on the kobold. However, there is a sudden backlash upon contact with Raggi's magical defences. Both Raggi and Olgoth's form appear to age several years, skin cracking. For the first time in centuries, Raggi feels the touch of time. Once more, after hundreds of years, Olgoth is made flesh again. Feeling heartbeats for the first time in centuries, Olgoth is shocked. The teams holds their attacks, and Raggi attempts to communicate with the corporeal half-orc. *"Olgoth, I can help you find peace! Please, all you need to do is to tell me that you want to move on, that you want me to guide you to your deserved rest,"* the wizened kobold pleads with him.
- The former Argent Rose scout turns to Raggi. *"Olgoth... that was my name..."* With tears now forming in his eyes, for the first time in a long time, he begs the kobold, *"Please.. take me home..."*. Raggi invokes *guide the willing* upon him, and Olgoth is engulthed with warm light. He slowly fades away, with a look of peace upon his face. The scout evaporates into a wave of bright motes that fade away, except for one. It bobs up and down, then floats away down the corridor, its light guiding the way. The team follows it, and the lone mote guides them through a maze of twists and turns before finally coming to its goal. Floating at the end of a dead-end alleyway, its light illuminates a crumpled skeletal form before fading away. Upon closer inspection, the team recognizes what gear the skeleton possesses that has not rotted away. It matches the same gear they saw Olgoth's ghostly form wore. A silver Argent Rose pin confirms that this is Olgoth's corporeal remains. The team respectful collects his bones and surviving gear, packing them tightly to return him back home. One other item of note, resting in the skeleton's lap, is a scroll case with Firesong's crest. Within it was another entry from the bard:
	- *"An unfortunate event occurred. Olgoth Drumsteel, renown relic hunter and our main scout for this expedition, has disappeared. He and Connornack were keeping watch while the rest of us slept. Connornack had told us that Olgoth went to go check something out, something only he had heard. Connornack waited a minute for Olgoth to return, then called out, and then wok us when no response came forth. It seems that Olgoth simply walked into the mist, and disappeared. We do not if misfortune fell upon him or if this accursed place rendered him lost. However, the worse outcome would be that he simply ventured and not return of his own free will. Guiding us through this maze seemed to caused Olgoth growing mental pain. Our healer, Xiaowen of Lucial, had tended to his mental trauma, but even after, there was a haunted look in Olgoth's eyes. The normally gregarious half-orc had become more sullen and reserved, but we had all been experiencing this since coming here. Perhaps whatever twists in logic he had to leap in order to continue guiding us got to him, drawing him further into this pit of madness and despair…"*
- The team continues their trek deeper into Ing-gol'zha. After a headache-inducing journey, the team finds themselves in a peculiar chamber. There are numerous large vats lined up along the walls. The vats are made out of thick cloudy panes of crystal, wrapped in bands of calcified mucus, and most are broken. In the middle of the chamber a large table made of stone-like material, covered in chiseled out cavities of odd shapes. Nearby are thick columns with similar cavities carved into them. Rex begins sketching the room while the rest of the team begins examining the area.
- Zen discovers another scroll case, embedded in a nearly hidden crack amidst the debris. As he reaches into the crack to retrieve it, everyone begins hearing a bubbling sound. Looking up, black bubbles start emerging and popping through the cracks of the chamber's ceiling. Then, as if being noticed awakened it, a wave of black, putrid slime erupts from the cracks and crashes onto the floor. It quickly forms up into a huge bubbling mass of carrion slime, a putrid ooze.
- Rex is the first to attack it with his elven curveblade. The jiren strikes deep, but when he pulls his sword back, he sees it badly melted from the ooze's acid. Zen follows up by shooting it with a magically conjured *snowball*. The ooze reacts swiftly, forming a pseudopod and slamming it into Rex, then grabbing him. The jiren begins sizzling as the ooze's acid begins eating away at his metallic body. Raggi tumbles away from the ooze, then grants *battlesense* to his companions. Igvald punches the ooze to free his companion, sending splatters flying, though his gauntleted fists also sizzle from the acid.
- As Zen continues to lob *snowballs* at the ooze, its attempt to crush Rex in its pseudopod. However, thanks to the enhanced insight from Raggi, Rex is able to wriggle free, and the jiren slips from the ooze's grasp. Igvald switches to attacking with his deadly kicks in order to preserve his damaged gauntlets. He delivers a roundhouse kick into the ooze's centre mass, tearing out a huge chunk. The inquisitor continues his momentum while simultaneously kicking himself upwards with his other foot. He spins, then drops a hammer kick into the ooze. The remaining mass is splattered across the floor, and Igvald lands. His boots were melted away and his feet sizzle in pain, but the remaining bits of the ooze are still, then melt away.
