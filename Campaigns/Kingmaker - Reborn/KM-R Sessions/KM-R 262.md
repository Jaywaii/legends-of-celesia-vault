# Previously

*After proving your worthiness to the Hlageth-Kla tribe, your team was provided information on where to capture a whisper of a will-o-wisp. The tribe informed you that no one has approached the temple in many generations for the dangers that lie there. You were given a guide to the temple, who led you to a fog-covered area in the swamp. You made an initial foray into the fog, where you experience on of the first dangers of the temple, a cacophony of mad whispers infiltrating your mind.*
