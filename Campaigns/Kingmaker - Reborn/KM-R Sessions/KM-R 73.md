# KM:R 73

- One by one, the Evergreen company slew the members of the Moondancer court. Among them was the cloaked swordswoman, killed by a perfrct blow from Argus as the giant waded through the enemies. The combined blows from Argus and Davona finally slew the Dancing lady.
- Upon the Dancing Lady's death, her mythic power was released, infusing into the Evergreen Company members that were present, including Tabrir and Tobias.
- The cloaked swordswoman's body soon disappeared right before their eyes. Before any could react, tentacles of shadow emerged from beneath her, wrapping around her, then sunk into the earth, taking the body with them.
- Only 2 satyr guards and Arran the Pixie survived the battle. The satyrs managed to escape, but Sky caught Arran in a *web* spell before she could do the same. The victors decided to spare her life as long as she no longer cause any trouble for Prateria. The Evergreen Company had enough bloodshed, and wished to ensure no more bad blood between Prateria and the fey.
- Separating the Dancing Lady's head from her corpse, Argus took it with him to outside to the courtyard of the keep. He displayed it to all the fairies that still fought, declaring her death at their hands in a loud booming voice. The fariries quickly surrendered and fled the keep. The Ironwatch Moondancer Expedition quickly took control of the keep, cheering victory for the Evergreen Company.
- Maalek reported to Argus that a second, smaller army of fairies were present. They hid until the Ironwatch assaulted the keep, rhen tried to attack their flank. However, thanks to all the preparations made, the Ironwatch suffered no permanent casualties, only injuries. However, Maalek felt they were lucky. Although the army defended itself well, they were not able to influct any serious casualties of their own on the enemy forces. Had the battle gone on longer, he feels that the Ironwatch would be at a disadvantage and taken far more casualties.
- While sweeping the keep, it was discovered that Louis had mysteriously fallen unconscious. Tabrir determined that Louis had fallen into a coma, but he could not find any obvious physical cause. Louis' condition was stable, but non-responsive.
