# KM:R 314

# Previously

The Evergreen Company destroyed the zombie horde in Arborfane Gap at 8 am.

Orok snuck around Ankerata from midnight to 6 am to find a way to free the slaves guarded by the cyclopean mercenary companies. However, when he finally started freeing the slaves at dawn, the rising sun became obscured by a rapidly approaching tendril of mist. Vorakios' Deathmist descended upon Ankerata, consuming its populace, and quickly spread to the mercenary camps on the city's outskirts. Though over a hundred slaves had succeeded in escaping the slave pen, the mist quickly caught up to them as well. Orok, using magical flight, could only rescue an elf girl and a middle-aged luckyn man before being forced to flee west. Behind him, the fog began encroaching after him.

At midnight, Sky received a *sending* from Iolanthe informing him that the Thunderhoof scouts discovered Vorakios' Deathmist around the Greyfang Peaks extended a new tendril southward. Leaving Orok to free the slaves, Sky *teleported* to the Thunderhoof Refuge. After getting the lay of the land from Iolanthe, Sky flew towards the city of Viriquia, the capital of the Radimite barony that bordered the Thalamech Uplands. The city was engulfed by the fog, but unlike Wolfshold, it seemed to be able to resist. Sky made contact with its rulers, and came to an agreement after opening two Doorways to Promethea for their citizens to escape through. By 3 am, Sky convinced the Marquis that Prateria would erect a Pall to protect Viriquia in exchange for the opportunity to negotiate with Holy Radimus Empire for the fragment of the Cord of the Ancients that Sky believes they possess.
