# KM:R 343

# Previously

*In the Cemetery of a Thousand Corpses, an enraged Vorakios repeatedly summoned living graves and spectres to destroy the intruders who slew his Cryptkeeper. Lonán sacrificed her memories of her childhood and where she grew up in order to fuel the magic to erase themselves from Vorakios' memory. The lich immediately returned his attention to assaulting Promethea, letting the Company to finish off the remaining threats around them.*

*Now free to focus on getting out of this "room", Asako flew up in the sky to find an exit. Instead, she saw that the great crypt tomb that Sky was studying was at the centre of a great arcane circle formed by the layout of the graves and paths. Entering the crypt, the Company discovered that its central chamber contained a massive tomb with an effigy of Vorakios carved on its lid. All around the chamber were symbols and inscriptions. Sky, Asako, and Lonán worked together to decipher them. They revealed forbidden knowledge sourced from the Deathless Shroud. Using that information, Asako would be able to bypass the magical lock to the exit portal of this room. The exit's arch was built into the chamber's entrance itself.*

*Learning from their first time entering a portal, te Company quickly applied magical enhancements upon themselves to face the threat of the next room. Once done, Asako concentrated on the arch. Unbeknowst to her, the dark knowledge of the Deathless Shroud would inflict night terrors upon her. However, worst of all, Asako realized the knowledge she and her companions deciphered was inclomplete. The portal only partially opened, allowing only one person to pass through at a time. At the same time, they heard a crack behind them. The effigy began cracking, its eye opening to a red glow. A wave of entropic energy slams into everyone as the effigy of Vorakios came to life, ready to eliminate the intruders.*
