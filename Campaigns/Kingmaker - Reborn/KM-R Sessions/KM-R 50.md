# KM:R 50
[[KM-R 49|← Previous: KM:R 49]] · [[KM-R 51|Next: KM:R 51 →]]

- With the remains of Mick's team safely stored in their vardo, the away team returns to Promethea. A somber Bekka thanks them for retrieving them, and she will arrange for an appropriate plot of land to lay them to rest.
- Jupus is able to identify the strange mushrooms as black rattlecap mushrooms. They have potent uses as ingredients in apothecarical concoctions and witch brews. The Company just so happen to know of a witch, and plan to bring them to her the next time they pass by her hut.
- Davona is alerted by her man Barley. The former bandit informs her of a growing number of unaffiliated gangs hitting the supply convoys coming to Promethea. Food, tools, construction parts are all being stolen. The few witnesesses to the crime report that these gangs seem to be ill-equipped, using improvised weapons and not very organized. Davona takes charge of the investigation, and learns that many of those involved in the thefts are recent settlers who have been having a hard time establishing a life here due to the lack of houses and jobs. A name comes up in her investigation: Lucarno. Whoever this Lucarno is, they are charismatic enough to organized these destitutes and orphans into getting what they need by themselves.
- Davona recruits several children at the lcoal orphanage, dubbing them her 'little cherubs' to seek out information about the the thefts, and they soon return with several leads. Cethin begins stealthily tracking down those leads, and narrows down several locations where the gang operates out of or stores their ill-gotten goods. The next day, Ayaki leads her retinue, and bursts into the likely base. She calls out of the occupants to cease their activities, and to surrender peacefully if they wish to be treated mercifully. The nascent bandits are so convinced and intimidated by Ayaki's presence that they choose to follow her commands, and they are taken into custody. A young human man is identified as Lucarno. It is learned that Lucarno organized the needy, and that he took a good cut from the goods they stole.
- Ayaki discusses with the rest of the Company as to how to rehabilitate these young criminals. Suggestions are made that Argus turns them into soldiers, or Cethin can train them as spies. In the end, Davona volunteers to take charge of rehabilitating Lucarno and his Baker's Dozen, having found the experience of doing so with the Stag Bandits in the past therapeutic.
- With that little criminal activity nipped in the bud and governing duties fulfilled, Arina, Sky, Argus, and Ayaki set out to explore the southeast region of the North Evergreen Plains. They make a stop to talk with the Evergreen Witch at her hut. The Old Beldame assures them that things have been calm thanks to their victory over the Stag Lord and the retern of the Heart to the Old Arborfane. Upon being presented with the black rattlecaps, she tells them about how they make her favorite witch's tea. She's willing to trade a 1st-level potion for each sample. In response to Sky's inquiry, she also tells them that Old Crackjaw, one of the bounties of the Monster Hunters' Association of Lurindor, can be found on the northern shore of Lake Iridescence.
- The exploration team continue to scout the region originally assigned to the late Mick's team, the area around the Mudbowl and some of the west region. They find a patch of lunary herbs that will serve the alchemists of their colony well in the future. Furhter in their travels, they hear the cry of a woman in distress.
- Sky rushes on ahead. However, he finds no woman. Instead, a large, quadruped creature leaps out of the tall grass and bites onto the wizard. In the midst of screaming in pain, Sky recognizes this beast as a leucrotta, a magical beast that can mimic the voices of other creatures in order to ambush prey, exactly as Sky has done. Before Sky is able to react, Arina teleports in and skewers the beast, pinning it down. Ayaki rides in and swings her oversized katana to lop off its head. And the four-armed large form of Argus comes charging in, his giant hammers smashing the body into a fine, red paste. "I had it under control," Sky remarks.
- The Evergreen Company receive an invitation to attend Theodore Duloup's birthday in his new settlement of Wolfshold on Orcsaï 20th.
- Arina, Sky, Cethin, and Argus set out to explore more of the western portion of the North Evergreen Plains. One night, Arina has a dream of being on a beautiful open, green plain. A beautiful woman with long, flowing green hair delicately whispers in her ear, *"The Dancing Lady must be dealt with. Do this for me."* Arina awakens, and feels her ring of green hair pulse with energy before it calms down. Arina remembers the honeyed voice of Elyssria. she recounts the dream and its message to the rest of the team, and Sky casts a spell with the birds about which direction is this 'Dancing Lady'. The birds reply with 'south'. For several days, the team consult the birds on their path to the Dancing Lady, but they would not yet find her as they have to return to Promethea to attend to their duties.
