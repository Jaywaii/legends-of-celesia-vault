# Previously

*Team 13 continues their investigation of Skethelburgh and its famed University. The team met with the Head Librarian a woman named Isolda Greghain and the university's vice-chancellor a man named Almor Bentwick. Between the two, the team acquired permission to work in the university's library during the nightly hours, though only after some very inquisitive discussion from the two university senior staff members. During the night, Tao, using his psychic powers to locate hidden doors, discovers one in the library in the Obscure Collections' wing, from the shelf where Igvald picked up the Pakulkan Astrologues. The team starts investigating the shelf that pinged on Tao's magical detection, and uncovered the mechanism that would shift the shelf and the wall behind it open. Just as you were about to begin unlocking the mechanism, you were interrupted by the arrival of the Librarian Isolda, who began inquiring if she can assist you in any way. You ended up with Raggi, Igvald, and Myria accompanying Isolda to discuss further on your "research", leaving the rest of the team to supposedly continue perusing the Obscure Collections wing.*
