# KM:R 324

# Previously

At the Thunderhoof refuge, under the protective dome of the Cord of the Ancients, Argus, Sky, Davona, Aedre, Orok, and Jupus were pulled into a special set of Dreamlands by a ritual led by Iolanthe. It was hoped that the echoes of the past, and the will of the ancestors that watch over them, will lead the Company to discover what had happened to the Thunderhoof's fabled relic, the boy Pyrouranos. In the old tales, that enchanted bow was key to defeating Vorakios the first time, but was then lost in the millenia after.

The first dreamland the Company found themselves in was the creation of the bow. On a hilltop with centaur shamans, elf mages, and dwarf priests, the ritual of its creation was in progress. But the hill was under assault by the undead forces of Vorakios. This echo of history had been twisted as the forces defending the ritualists were fewer, while those attacking were greater. The Company had to help defend against the undead, and they did so in the nick of time. Their success seemingly met the approval of greater forces watching them, and they were pulled to the next echo of the past.
