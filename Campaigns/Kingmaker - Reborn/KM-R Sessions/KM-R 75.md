# KM:R 75
[[KM-R 74|← Previous: KM:R 74]] · [[KM-R 76|Next: KM:R 76 →]]

- As the Ironwatch Moondancer Expedition force reaches Prateria's borders, many locals flock to the side of the road they travel upon. The locals look on with awe at the parade of victorious troops, cheering and offering their congratulations. Once the force enters Promethea proper, the whole settlement erupts in an improptu celebration, and music blares in the air. The street traveled by the force is lined with crowds who cheer and throw petals and flowers upon the victorious soldiers. The crowd are particularly enthusiastic and vocal at the appearance of the Evergreen Company at the head of the column.
- At the center of town, Ayaki stands up on a makeshift pedestal to address a large group of citizens.
	- *"Citizens of Prateria! It is with great happiness that I announce our nation's victory over the Moondancer Court, the villains behind the tragedy of the Carnival of Tears.Their leader, known as the Dancing Lady, a menace to these lands for decades, was slain in combat, dead by the hands of our very own General Argus and Warden Davona, allowing our forces, the Ironwatch, to easily capture the keep, our nation's first military victory! We proven to the world that Prateria will endure any hardship, and we have the will, and the strength, to defeat any who seek to do harm to our people, and our future! Long live Prateria!"*
- The leanansidhe Nyphadora appears in the Evegreen Company's pavilion upon their return.
	- *A tall, ethereal elf-like woman with long crimson hair stands before you. You recognize her as Nyphadora, Elyssria's emissary.* **"Our lady congratulates you all upon your victory. With the Dancing Lady's demise, the last major threat in this region has been removed. Although there are still many dangers in the land you call the Viridian Marches, none will reach you in the forseeable future."** *She turns to address Argus.* **"I believe you have something to render unto our lady."** *Argus nods, and hands over to Nyphadora the satchel containing the Dancing Lady's heart.* **"Our lady thanks you, Lord Argus for your service."**
	- *She turns to Davona.* **"Our lady hopes that Faerus Sicariah served you well."** *After Davona responds.* **"Our lady has a request in return for granting you this boon. Do you remember the Heart of the Forest of the Old Arborfane? The one you returned with her help was but one of three. In return for her boon, she requests that you venture into the northern part of the old forest, locate the the Heart, and plant these seven seeds around it."** *Davona agrees, and Nyphadora slips out a small bag from her cloak of leaves, and hands it to her.* **"These seeds will help secure and protect the Heart of the Forest, strengthening its ties to Faerie."**
	- **"Once again, you have proven yourselves to be capable champions of our lady. Queen Elyssria is most satisfied with her choice. Remember, you may always call upon our mistress should you ever need aid, and she will be there for you. I now take my leave. Farewell."** *A gust of wind blows through, carrying with it a cloud of leaves. They obscure your vision for a moment, and when it clears, the leanansidhe, leaves and all, is gone.*
- Upon everyone's return to Promethea, Maalek approaches Argus:
	- *"General Argus, I have a suggestion. Although the troops are happy to simply work in your service, I was thinking it would be good to reward them for their victory. Perhaps a medal for all those who participated in the battle, along with a monetary reward. It need not be much; it would be more of a token of recognition.*
	- Argus likes the idea, and agrees to fund the medals and rewards. The Praterian General also discusses with Ayaki to make an official celebratory day on the calendar to commemorate the victory over the Dancing Lady. Ayaki heartily agrees with the idea.
	- Maalek: *"I would also like to bring to you attention the two officers who help led the assault." The aelfkyn looks behind him and signals at two soldiers to come over. Both appear to be of Venici ethnicity. One is a tall, muscular woman with long blonde hair tied in a pony tail. Her eyes are a deep blue, with a faded scar crossed against her right brow. The other is a bald man of below average height, but with a thick, muscular build. He sports a dark goatee, and a silver earring on his left ear. Maalek introduces the woman first. "This is Lieutenant Genevra. She led platoon that led the assault on the walls fo the keep. And this is Lieutenant Orso. He commanded the company of veterans. They were instrumental in countering the surprise attack from the 2nd Moondancer army."*
	- **Genevra & Orso, saluting Argus:** *"General, it is an honor to meet you in person."* Argus dismisses their salute, and with his extra arms,shakes both of their hands at the same time. He thanks them for their work, and Orso reply that it is thanks to his might and leadership. Genevra admits her being impressed at seeing him in his full power, to which results in Argus being somehwat awkward at the praise. Argus exchanges salutes with them, and after Maalek dismisses them, the two of them discuss future developments for the Ironwatch. Argus jokingly reassures him that Maalek is not one that can be replaced.
- Overseeing the more than sixty casualties from the Carnival of Tears, Tabrir makes plans to have the nation build a proper graveyard. It is during this discussion that the veteran members of the Company catch Tabrir and Tim up on their trade deals with the various parties of Lurindor, and to not mess around with the Old Arborfane.
- Ayaki announces she and Cethin plan to abdicate and depart Prateria. She and her brother plan on returning to Iku to depose the changeling fake and reclaim their ancestral home. She nominates Arina to take her place, with the approval of the inner council.
	- *"My friends, it is with a heavy heart that I announce Cethin and my own intention to step down from our roles in Prateria's governing and part ways from our Company. We have learned that events are in motion in homeland of Iku that will eventually require our intervention. We intend to uphold the responsibilities of our current positions for a year's time while we prepare our retinue to retake our homeland. I have long considered who will succeed us, and after speaking with Arina, I would like to nominate her as Prateria's next ruler. However, I leave the final decision to the group."*
- The core members of the Evergreen Company have no objections to Arina succeeding Ayaki. Arina humbly accepts, and thanks them for their confidence in her. Tabrir and Tobias do not feel like they have a place to comment, but offer their support to Arina. Arina will need a replacement as Grand Diplomat, and nominates Tabrir, who she feels has demonstrated the empathy and ability to build bridges to succeed her. Tabrir thanks Arina, and accepts.
- **Cethin:** *"As for me, well, let's be honest, no one can really replace me. But after seeing him in action, I think Tim over there has what it takes to be the next spymaster. You still need a lot of work to prove yourself trustful and useful to the nation, but honestly, when I leave, it's not really my problem anymore, isn't it? I'll leave it to the rest of you to have a proper spymaster."* Tobias ruefully accepts.
	- Arina comments who they'll really miss: Bob the bartender. Jupus mentions they haven't seen that guy in forever. Cethin grins: "Don't worry, Bob's gonna get one hell of a farewell."
- Ayaki informs everyone that as a final gift, she has had her people construct a building to serve as Promethea's town hall. Jupus suggests they name it "The Kashiki Memorial Hall", to which Ayaki exclaims that she's not dead. "Well, you are to me now," Jupus mutters, somewhat jokingly. Rishoi suggests they call it "The Kashiki Pavilion", which everyone can agree is a fine name.
- Cethin also informs everyone that his people also constructed a parting gift. "We got a tavern now. 'Bob's Bar'," he declares with a grin. Arina comments that it's ironic that Bob will never be there. Cethin explains it was going to be a front for the spy team. Tobias says that he is not very good at bartending, to which Cethin tells him, "No one's perfect, kid." "Unlike this guy," Jupus mutters, and Cethin grins in response.
- Plans are made for a festival to celebrate the nation's first military victory. Meanwhile, Arina has comfortable arrangements made for the comatose Louis. Plans are also made to fulfill Elyssria's request to go to the Old Arborfane. While it is mainly Davona's task, other members of the Evergreen Company choose to come along. Tabrir and Tobias also volunteer to help.
