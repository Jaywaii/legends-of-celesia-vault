# Previously

The Evergreen Company continued to battle the fairy forces of the Dancing Lady that had transformed the Carnival of Tears into a playground of death. With snow hammering down on the deluded populace of Promethea, the Evergreen Company took down one attraction after another.  Just recently, they killed the group of redcaps that were operating the Titan's Wheel, where the deadly fey had taken fairgoers and inserted them in the windmill's machinery. They also slew the quickling agent of the Cold Rider, who was trying to lead them on a merry chase. The Quickly foolishly thought to strike at the Company, but that brief moment of exposure proved to be a fatal mistake. The Company recovered the dark ice crystal and broke it open, allowing for Tessa Kalini to regain physical form. Namdrin Quinn, Tessa's husband and the coerced master of the Carnival, recovered his wife. Now that the Cold Rider no longer possessed her, Quinn was free to aid the Company, and provided much needed information on the remaining attractions, and promised to come to the Company's aid when they face the Cold Rider. The Company continued to go around the carnival grounds and end the attractions and save their people. They slew the one-headed ettin Udmoor, who had killed and mutilated the performers at the Menageries of Freaks, and killed Ambrosia, the nymph who was enchanting victims at the Peep Show. Only a few attractions remain active.
