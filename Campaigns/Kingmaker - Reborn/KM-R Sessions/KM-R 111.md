# KM:R 111

# Previously

Previously on Kingmaker Reborn, the Evergreen Company successfully captured their unseen observer. However, when you did so, someone else tried to kill him by firing a poisoned arrow in his throat. They actually succeeded, but Rishoi brought him back to life, and Osla healed the damage from the poison. Sky teleported the captive and Osla to his Emporium while the rest of you searched for the sniper. At the Emporium, the poison passed, and the man, who revealed that his name is Puccino, divested himself of his bonds and attempted to escape. However, Sky cast *dominate* on him, and made him return to his seat. The rest of you gave up on the search and rejoined the others at the Emporium. You began to question the dominated Puccino, and he revealed that he is part of something called the Coil. Sky looked into Puccino's memories and saw that he had seven other companions who disembarked from a ship with him during the night and infiltrated the city. He also saw another memory of Puccino and a female companion infiltrate the Teredoro lodging's. The questioning became too invasive, and Puccino broke the domination. While Sky erected a *mage's private sanctum*, Arina activated her unspeakable name ability. She heard someone speak her title, and she *scried* on them. It was a woman in similar gear as Puccino, talking with three others outfitted the same way in a backalley. They seemed to be spying on the Emporium, waiting for an opportunity to kill Puccino. When they separated, Argus and Osla went out to confront the woman while Arina *projected* her image in the Coil agent's presence to distract her. In her distraction, the Coil agent failed to evade Argus' earth breaker smashing her head into her torso, much to the horror of onlookers.
