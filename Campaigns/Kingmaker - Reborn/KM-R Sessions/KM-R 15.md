# KM:R 15
[[KM-R 14|← Previous: KM:R 14]] · [[KM-R 16|Next: KM:R 16 →]]

Sky had to return to Onestrio's after receving a message from Davona, so it was the group of Arina, Cethin, and Argus that arrived at the Great Cypress tree. On the way there, they were attacked by a group of monstrous centipedes. Their travel was delayed from Argus being overcome by the paralytic poison of the giant instects.

The group did not quite reach the Great Cyrpess as they were ambushed by a group of mites and their giant ticks. The group defeated them, and captured one alive. They interrogated him to gain information on the mite colony and learned that their leader was the mighty King Grabbles and his mighty mount Tickleback.

Back at Onestrio's, Jupus returned from his training trip. Their bandit prisoner Bekka had been keeping herself busy by keeping the other prisoners in line. As the treatment she had been receiving by the Evergreen Company was far more mercifully than she expected, she figured it would be best to stay in their good graces.
