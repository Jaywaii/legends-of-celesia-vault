# Previously

The Evergreen Company continued to stop the attractions of the Carnival of Tears from continuing to murder the people of Promethea who continue to be under the magical sway of the Orb of Rapture. Most recently, they managed to drive off the pixie Arran, who was running the Strong Ale tent, where the ale had been switched to a magical concoction that turned its drinkers into trees. Upon doing so, however, the Cold Rider, the Dancing Lady's chief servant behind this attack, had had enough. The snow storm grew fiercer, but your citizens continue carrying on their day as if it was clear skies, ignoring the beginning of frostbite forming on their body and those that had already fallen onto the ground.  The wind carries the Rider's haunting voice: "Foolish would-be heroes of Promethea, I have deigned to notice your pathetic efforts to interfere with the plans of the Dancing Lady. Should you possess even a modicum of courage, come and face me upon this lake of ice, and we will determine who truly rules this land."

<u>After the battle:</u>
Upon defeat, the cold rider melts into a puddle of cold water, seeping into the earth. The Orb of Rapture cracks, breaking in the pieces, losing its luster. The snow storm died alongside with the rider, and the clouds part to reveal the sky. Quietness falls upon the carnival. Then the first screams pierced the air. The carnival's laughter and merriment gave way to cries of horror and wails of anguish.

Sister Felicite is busy tending to the wounded.
Bekka was entranced, and had been participating in the fair events.
Tessa Kalini, having recovered, is distraught by the actions of her husband, and the two of them and there is now a rift between them.
The people clamour for action against those responsible for this act of terror and death. Some call for Namdrin's head, and begin gathering a posse to go after him unless the Company does something.
The threat of the the true instigator of this horror, the Dancing Lady remains active. Namdrin Quinn knows where she is based: an ancient elven fort recently rebuilt.

- The Evergreen Company, with the aid of Namdrin Quinn, faced off against the Cold Rider on an icy lake in the middle of the carnival. After a fierce battle, the Company defeated the Cold Rider, and broke the enchantment that controlled their people.
- Although 60 people died, and many more injured, the Carnival of Tears Massacre could have been far worse had it not been for the quick actions of the Company. Though the tragedy will remain fresh for years to come, the Company were hailed as heroes.
- Sorrow turned to anger as Prometheans called for action and punishment against those who attacked them. Some called for the head of Namdrin Quinn, as he was the visible carnival master during the incident. However, a speech by Ayaki, aided by others in the Company, calmed the populace. She reassured them that the Company will take action to retaliate against their enemy and protect the security of Prateria. She also convinced them that Namdrin Quinn was also a victim of the Dancing Lady, coerced into aiding the enemy under the threat of his wife, and that his aid in the final battle was invaluable.
- The rest of the Company arrived from their mission to stop the crazed hermit and exploring the Lonely Barrow. The Company focused on rest and recovery of their recent challenges.
