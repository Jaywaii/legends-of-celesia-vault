# KM:R 51
[[KM-R 50|← Previous: KM:R 50]] · [[KM-R 52|Next: KM:R 52 →]]

- Evergreen Company departs for Wolfshold, bringing along 1 aide for Argus, and 5 aides for Arina.
- Sky plays gul bara with Erikos Pentras, magister of Domaine-du-loup, but uses the magical board taken from the Old Arborfane. After Erikos is harmed twice, Erikos examines the board, and is outrage by the deception. He goes to complain to Duloup.

---

Taking the road currently known as the "East road", the Company reaches Ravenna's Pass quite quickly. The road only extends towards midway between Promethea and the Thistledown River, but you the river regardless without issue. There, it takes you some time, but you manage to locate an area along the river where you can ford it to the other side. There are no roads yet created on the east side of the Thistledown, but the remaining pass are simple flat plains. What is of not is that the Pass through the Old Arborfane forest, on the east side of the Thistledown, is about 4 times as longer than the west side. On the eastern side of the Old Arborfane, the land becomes more dominated by rolling green hills.

The town of Wolfshold is the capital of Domaine-du-Loup. It is a small town, but features a town hall, a temple to Tierra, a watchtower, and other services needed by the growing settlers. The town itself is surrounded by a wooden wall. You hear the ringing of bells followed by some shouting as you come into view of Wolfshold. As you approach, you notice a bit of a crowd gathering at the road's entrance into Wolfshold.

At the front of the group is a rugged halfling looking quite jovial. Theodore Duloup welcomes you all with open arms.

Duloup has reserved the entirety of Wolfshold only inn, the Hilltop, to house the Evergreen Company and their entourage. "They may be the best accomodations we have in town at the moment, but I do apologize for not being able to provide anything fancier," Theodore admits.

The celebration takes place in the town hall. The main meeting hall has been rearranged to have seats and long tables arrayed in the shape of a horseshoe, and the central area clear to allow people to move about and dance. Up on the speaker's platform is a small band of hafling musicians playing music. The hall is lit above with chandeliers equipped with *everburning torches*.

Theodore Duloup invites the Evergreen Party to Wolfshold for his 46th birthday.
- Theodore introduces his council to the Party.
- Theodore complains about his council badgering him about getting married and starting a family.
- Theodore and Gredda talks about his conflicts with the Thalamech centaurs, disparaging them, and Corbin and Giovanni talk about recent victories against The Red Hoods, a group of bandits led by Lerouge. He laments that he has yet to capture or kill Lerouge, as the slippery halfling bandit repeatedly slips through Theodore's grasp.
- Theodore inquires as to what challenges that the Evergreen Company has overcome or are currently facing.
