# KM:R 268

# Previously

*Attempting to investigate the Skethelbugh's rumoured secret passages, you Team 13 were interrupted by the arrival of the Head Librarian Isolda Greghain just as you were about to unlock the secret door in the Library. Isolda insisted on talking to you and being helpful, and wanted to show you several relics and artifacts they held in storage. You split up, with Raggi, Igvald, and Myria going with Isolda, and the rest of the team left on their own to once again sneak into the secret passages after Isolda and the others departed. After unlocking the secret door built into a bookshelf, Arjin, Tao, Bur-Jack, and Yujimbo squeezed into the dark tunnels. The hidden passageways were a maze, going left, right, up, down, but the experienced team 13 kept track of their path. During your exploration, you encountered several deadly traps which you handidly avoided. Even more, you discovered that certain passageways were marked with a well-hidden symbol carved into the wall: a headless obese pregnant figure with a fanged maw where the neck is supposed to be, with 7 tendrils rippling out from its bottom, the mark of the Mother of All Horrors. With that much information uncovered, the infiltration made their way back to the library to rejoin the others. Meanwhile, the deception team tried to delay Isolda with many excuses and even Myria magically charming her. Eventually, she successfully led you to the storage room. The charm allowed her to admit that a colleague of hers and a dozen students were preparing a surprise welcome for you which may get a little rough. Walking in prepared, you were able to avoid a dozen crates filled with deadly weapons crashing down upon you. The students, under Isolda's orders, attacked you shouting "In Coil's Embrace!", while Isolda cast spells upon you and her colleague trying to magically support them. Knowing that these students are important children of powerful political families, you opted to nonlethally stop them. It was not a fair fight, the training imposed upon them during their upbrgining was no match for the years of practical experience you have acquired. As the students were being taken down one by one, Isolda still had tricks up her sleeve. She would not get the chance to use them as her colleague began smashing her head from behind with a heavy book before surrendering herself to you.*
