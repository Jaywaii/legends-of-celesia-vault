# KM:R 32

Falgrim Sneeg yawned as he kept watch on the southwestern tower. He hated being on the night shift, but old stiff Acteon insisted that the fort be on guard at all times. Apparently, the Evergreen Company was going on the offensive, but Falgrim thought that would be suicidal, given the new power that the Stag Lord had acquired. The bandit was glad that his leader regularly drinks himself to sleep; he was a terror while awake.

He heard Marge Mcree shout, "Hey! We've got visitors!" Mcree was at the front gate. Could this be them? Since Falgrim was on the other end of the fort, he could not see that a tall, armored man followed by a horse in the field over a hundred feet away from the fort.

He could hear the old stiff shout out, "Identify yourselves!"

Faintly, Falgrim heard a reply. "We come bearing a message for the Stag Lord!"

Falgrim, on watch for intruders on the ground, never saw or heard the five figures gently descending from above. He would never know what the message was, as his whole world was suddenly engulfed in flames, instantly incinerated to ash.

Not far from him, his comrades Jex the Snitch and Carla Slick-Tongue joined him in burning death as Sky's *fireball* engulfed that area of the fort.

The bandits elsewhere in the fort were either stunned by surprise or shocked from sleep. While the bandits' attention was directed towards the fire, Arina, Rishoi, Davona, and Cethin touched ground near the gate, their descent controlled thanks to *featherfall*. Cethin had already vabished from sight. Above them, Sky continued to *fly* over the fort. Even further up, in Jupus' air balloon from where the team had dropped from, Gnawfang positioned herself so that Jupus can aim his oversized rifle to snipe their foes below.

"Davona, Rishoi, hold them off! Cethin and I will open the gate!" Arina cried out to the others. The caelephilim and the malephilim positioned themselves between their comrades and the bandits within the fort. Sky further reinforced the defense by *greasing* part of the ground, causing one charging bandit to slip and fall. "Fat" Norri tried to get up from the greasy ground, but both Rishoi and Davona were quick to put back down in a more permanent manner.

Once the gate opened, Ayaki and Argus could be seen charging towards it. As soon as the *fireball* had hit the fort, Ayaki mounted Thunder, and both she and Argus started charging.

Acteon drew his blade, and moved to a higher position to better view the battlefield. "Form up!" he ordered the bandits. "Act as a group and flank them! Don't attack one at a time!" Privately, the former knight wondered if this was the opportunity he was waiting for, but until he knew for sure, he would lead his men into battle faithfully.

Davona was at the forefront of the battle line. She was cutting bandits left and right with her greatsword, but even so, "Bloody" Mary managed to sink in a lucky blow, stabbing her deeply. Davona only grunted through the pain, and continued swinging.

Topper Red thought to flank the warrior, but suddenly felt intense pain in his back. Looking down, the sharp tip of a wakisashi poked through his chest. The last thing he heard was a fox-like snickering behind him.

Stil, the bandits were not completely helpless; they still had the advantage of being in their home fort. From elevated positions on bridges and parapets, they loosed arows at the Company. Many missed, but some managed to land some hits.

"Bloody" Mary, so far the only one to inflict a serious wound on the Company, exemplified the deadliness of the Stag Bandits. Which is why Rishoi rolled and tumbled to behind her, and executed *Steel Flurry Strike* with fists hardened by *Bronze Knuckle*. Three devastating punches along the bandit's spine smashed her rib cage open. "Bloody" Mary was dead before she hit ground.

Not to be outdone, Cethin stealthily snuck up behind a bandit attacking Davona. Initiating *Red Zephyr Strike*, the ninja emerged into sight, stabbing the bandit in the back before zipping back in a crimson blur. Topper Red fell to the ground, blood pooling beneath his body.

With thr ground cleared, Sky dropped down next to his sister. With a quick incantation, the wizard conjured thick strands of *web* in the center of the fort, catching several bandits in it and blocking the main path to the Company. The Stag Lord's other two lieutenants, Dante and Toro, and a couple of other bandits were cut off from leaving the room they were gambling in.

"Nicely done, little brother!" Davona cheered as she downed a *potion of cure moderate wounds*.

Acteon could see that this battle was bot going to end well. A sudden impact in his chest made him flinched and grunt in pain. He looked up, and saw a gnome aiming a smoking rifle pointed at him. His thoights were interrupted when a deep, booming laughtered echoed above the crackling of fire throughout the fort.

The Stag Lord emerged from his quarters onto one of the raised platforms. He was a towering figure of muscles. his head and face completely covered by a helm made of a stag's skull, grand antlers and all. He dragged forth on a leash a decrepit and filthy old man.

"Sir!" Acteon yelled towards him. "We need your strength!"

The Stag Lord roared in laughter once more. "What's the matter, Acteon? Can't my minions handle rabble?"

"Obviously not!" Argus roared back, as he and Ayaki charged through the open gate, and rejoined their companions. His momentum carried him into smashing the bandits caught in the *web*. Argus' earthbreaker smashed Nero Sam's chest, his back exploding into bits of flesh and bone. The web was splattered red behind his now limp, hanging corpse. Argus' swing continued and slammed into Fredo Stasi's chest.

"Easy with that one! We need him alive!" Davona shouted.

"Sorry! Got caught in the moment!" Argus replied.

Arina looked up to Acteon. Initiating *Fading Strike*, the swordswoman suddenly appeared beside Acteon, and thrust her rapier into him. "Surrender! Clearly the Stag Lord won't help you!" she yells.

"Funny way asking for surrender," the lieutenant grunted. "You haven't yet seen how powerful he is!"

"And you've only just started to see how powerful we are!" Arina retorted as she danced away. As if to answer that statement, Sky conjured a *flaming sphere*, and directed into the web, burning "Dirty" Jeb.

Acteon knew he had to make a decision, one he had wrestled with for months. And yet, now that the time came, the choice came so easy to him. Finally coming face to face with these adventurers had awoken something in him he long thought he buried.

He finally drew his sword. Instead of swinging at Arina, Acteon threw it to the ground below, blade stuck in dirt. "I accept your offer of surrender!" he declared loudly. "And you had all better do the same!" he shouted to the remaining bandits.

But his last order was cut through by the bellowing laughter of the Stag Lord. "Acteon! You may have been an excellent leader and lieutenant, but I always knew you were too soft-bellied for true glory! Allow me to show you how it's done! Old man, take care of them!"

Clicking his heels, the Stag Lord's boots spurt wings, and the bandit lord flew into the air. He drew his powerful longbow, and took aim at the air balloon. The large bow audible creaked as the Stag Lord made an impossibly strong pull. He let go, and the arrow ripped through the air. The Stag Lord's arrow tore completely through the balloon, ripping it apart.

Jupus and Gnawfang began to fall.

The basket crashed into the ground. The night lit up red once more as the three dozen bottles of alchemist's fire stored in it ignites, exploding into a towering ball of fire.

Jupus and Gnawfang gently descend into the fort thanks to the parachutes the rat had squirreled away. Jupus mournfully glanced at all his hard work literally go up in flame.

The fort burned for a third time as a *flame strike* descended into the fort, courtesy of the cackling old man, burning the rest of the company and bandits alike. The bandits who were clear of both the web and fire decided that they would find better fortunes elsewhere.

"You won't escape your punishment!" Ayaki shouted. She reared Thunder up, and directed him to knock one of the fleeing bandits with his hooves.

Arina dashed along the upper levels of the fort, then warped to the old man. She was suddenly overcome with intense nasea. The old man was exuding some malignant miasma around him. Trying to pull herself together, she concentrated on both the old man and the Stag Lord, and felt Mister Angelcakes by her side. "You're both mine," she says, and she stabs the old man her rapier.

Arina cried out in pain. As she thrust her bladr, her arm became lacerated by magical thorns that suddenly appeared. As her blade punctured the old man's *iron skin*, a spray of acidic fluid erupted from his wound and onto Arina. The *caustic blood* burned the young woman.

The Stag Lord laughed. "My old man is quite the nasty fella, ain't he?"

Acteon leapt down onto the ground, and grabbed his sword. He yanked out of the ground, and turned to the Evergreen Company. "The Stag Lord is too strong! We need to work together to take him down!"

"Well said," Ayaki replied, leaping down from Thunder and brandishing her katana. "Together, then!"

"Unite all you want! You might actually be fun to fight, then!" the Stag Lord roared. He fired two more arrows. Sky and Rishoi were barely able to dodge them. The Stag Lord quickly followed up by dropping a kick below at Arina. The young woman narrowly avoided it by dropping and rolling away.

Still cackling, the old man focused at the sky above the fort. A *deadly weather* of dark clouds and strong winds form, and a lightning bolt strikes down into arena. Argus retaliated by marching up to the old man, and slamming his maul into him. Despite his appearance, the old man managed to tank the powerful blow. As for Argus, the old man's miasma had no effect on him.

Arina swapped hands with her sword, her normal hand badly contorted. Grimacing through the pain, she follows up on Argus's attack with one of her own. She delivers a precise strike at the old man, knocking him down. Despite the additional acid spewing out at her, it was a satisfying attack.

"No!" the Stag Lord roared. "He is mine to hurt!" The bandit lord flew down once more, and executed *Iron Axe Kick* onto Arina's skull, knocking her senseless for a few moments.

*Mister Angelcakes, why aren't you protecting me?!* she thought deliriously.

On the ground, the old man cackled, and brought down another bolt of lightning onto Argus. He followed up fading, and wildshaping into an air elemental above the Company.

The Evergreen Company could only wonder what more can these pair of villains do.
