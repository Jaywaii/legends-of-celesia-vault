# Previously

*During their rest and recovery, Lonán and Dragomir each receive a vision, the same for each. In it, they feel a familiar presence, one from long ago but that they could not remember. "You are arriving at the climax of your long mission," a woman's voice spoke to them. "Should you wish it, it is time to renew ancient vows, and remember what was lost." The voice was familiar to the two ancient adventurers, but they no longer remembered why. However, they felt their rings of sustenance throb once with power. It felt like an invitation, a welcome return to an ancient oath of loyalty.*

*Lonán and Dragomir take a moment to look back in their memories, what precious remained after sacrificing so much. They remember the Thunderhoof Champion and the Stellar Sage, and the sacrifices they all had to give in order to bring down Vorakios. The two long-time companions did not need telepathy to know they each shared the same feelings, and the wispy, ghostly remains of their slain friends fluttered around Lonán in similar anticipation. To that ancient, familiar voice, the pair respond the same: they dedicate these final moments to those that were loss, but swear to finally bring the threat of Vorakios once and for all. With these words, they accepted the invitation to renew that ancient bond. "Then once again, let us face this dark threat. Together," spoke the voice.*

*The rings of sustenances, old rings covered in calcified skin that they had long forgotten from which adventure they gained them from, flared up in power. The decrepit covering shed away from them, revealing the rings to be made of green hair, now vibrantly alive once again. The name "Elyssria" whispered into their thoughts like an old memory resurfacing. The pair then remembered that it was she who gifted them the ritual of **Slip Into Oblivion** and protection against Vorakios, though any memory of their shared history remained elusive. At the same time, the green-haired rings worn by the Evergreen Company flared up in power in unison.*

*The Company turned to Lonán and Dragomir in surprise as they now see them wearing Rings of Elyssria as they were. Asking the pair about it, Dragomir told them they were gifted by an old friend. Lonán added that it was an old bond, much to Sky's surprise as did not realize that Elyssria was active even so long ago.*

*After making their preparations, which include Bjorn summoning an angel of the higher order, Dragomir wished to address the others. "Member of the Evergreen Company, you carry the same blessing of Elyssria as Lonán and I. I wish to say that all of us carry the will of the Thunderhoof Champion, the Stellar Sage, and so many others. The catalysts of the universe have aligned today to finally end the evil infestation that is Vorakios. Though some of us may fall, we will not fail here."*

*The spirits around Lonán pulsed and danced around her. "I can still feel the light of Pyrouranos that has brought us here. And I know we will carry it forward to pierce the darkness before us. The lich has taken so much from us, but I will do everything I can to keep us all alive. Regardless of our fate, it has been an honour fighting, and perhaps dying, alongside you all."*

*"Well said," said Sky.*

*"I can think of a dozen other people I would rather not die alongside," Jupus added.*

*"Well... said?" Sky said again.*

*Gnawfang volunteered to sacrifice some of her life force to open the portal to the Heart of the Fairy Grave, and everyone passed through. Upon exiting the portal, the Company suddenly found themselves in the void once more. The Heart of the Fairy Grave was an island in space, with no air or atmosphere for the undead have no need for such trivial environments. Across the floating island were over a dozen undead cyclops. One stood tall amongst them, with a burning red orb lit up like a beacon.*

*"You! I know not by what means you keep slipping from my mind, but you are certainly fools to come and face my wrath instead of fleeing when you had the chance!" Vorakios telepathically roared into their minds. "I shall reward you for your brave decision by feasting upon your fleshing, and binding your souls to my service. Under my command, I will have you as my champions over the land you once sought as your own!"*

*Behind Vorakios was a crystal even larger than he. Shining bright, the crystal displayed images of Promethea under attack by thousands of zombies, banshees, and blighted fey. Yet the Company was not disheartened by the sight, for they saw their comrades bravely fighting back. A new Pall of Shining Life was erected over the city, led by Arina and Rishoi. Acteon and his former bandit companions were rescuing civilians. Iolanthe and her centaurs, and the unicorn Mithratan, charged down zombies in the streets. The gold dragon Halcyathrox flew overhead, assisted by the fairies Tyg-Twitter-Twig, Percilash, and friends from the Hazel Court. The sight brought a tear to Davona's eye. Instead of bringing despair to the Company, the images only reinforced their resolved to end Vorakios once and for all.*
