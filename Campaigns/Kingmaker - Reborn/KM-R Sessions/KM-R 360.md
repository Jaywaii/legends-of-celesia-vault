# KM:R 360

# Previously

*During their rest and recovery, Lonán and Dragormir each receive a vision, the same for each. In it, they feel a familiar presence, one from long ago but that they could not remember. "You are arriving at the climax of your long mission," a woman's voice spoke to them. "Should you wish it, it is time to renew ancient vows, and remember what was lost." The voice was familiar to the two ancient adventurers, but they no longer remembered why. However, they felt their rings of sustenance throb once with power. It felt like an invitation, a welcome return to an ancient oath of loyalty.*

*Lonán and Dragormir take a moment to look back in their memories, what precious remained after sacrificing so much. They remember the Thunderhoof Champion and the Stellar Sage, and the sacrifices they all had to give in order to bring down Vorakios. The two long-time companions did not need telepathy to know they each shared the same feelings, and the wispy, ghostly remains of their slain friends fluttered around Lonán in similar anticipation. To that ancient, familiar voice, the pair respond the same: they dedicate these final moments to those that were loss, but swear to finally bring the threat of Vorakios once and for all. With these words, they accepted the invitation to renew that ancient bond. "Then once again, let us face this dark threat. Together," spoke the voice.*

*The rings of sustenances, old rings covered in calcified skin that they had long forgotten from which adventure they gained them from, flared up in power. The decrepit covering shed away from them, revealing the rings to be made of green hair, now vibrantly alive once again. The name "Elyssria" whispered into their thoughts like an old memory resurfacing. The pair then remembered that it was she who gifted them the ritual of **Slip Into Oblivion** and protection against Vorakios, though any memory of their shared history remained elusive. At the same time, the green-haired rings worn by the Evergreen Company flared up in power in unison.*

*The Company turned to Lonán and Dragormir in surprise as they now see them wearing Rings of Elyssria as they were. Asking the pair about it, Dragormir told them they were gifted by an old friend.*
