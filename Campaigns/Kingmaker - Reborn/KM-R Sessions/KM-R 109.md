# Previously

Previously on Kingmaker Reborn: Day 4 of the Viridian Games comes to a close. The Evergreen Company succeeds in making a deal with the Harkûr representative to help establish a trade route. They follow up with a afternoon meal with the Sootscale delegation where they notice the King being nervous around his tournament champions. Lumina volunteers to stealthily watch over them, and is joined by Cortez. That evening, the guard report cloaked individuals breaking into the Teredoro quarters, specifically the one of Rocco Leonti. The culprits escape through skill and sorcery, but the guard discovers two items that are out-of-place in the room. One was an invitation from Elvia Sovrani for Rocco to come visit her in her room tomorrow evening for some personal time. The other was a suspicious trade deal contract. Jupus confirms that the trade and numbers don't make sense from a business point of view, and everyone is sure that there is a hidden message inside. Tabrir is the one to discover that the double-meaning of the words in the contract allude to a deal between Prateria and the Teredoro Consortium to allow the Consortium to assassinate the Sovrani diplomat during the games!
