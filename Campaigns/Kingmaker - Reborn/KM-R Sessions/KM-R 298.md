# KM:R 298

# Previously

*As preparation were being made, Elyssria came to you in a dream granting you knowledge of the Pall of Shining Life, a ritual demanding significant amount of mythic power to shield a city against the Gloom of Vorakios. And not a moment too soon, for right after, Évard Châteaufoy contacts you with dire news that the fog will soon consume Valenlet, and that they had no means to stop it. Rishoi knew what she had to do, and volunteered to raise the Pall over Valenlet. Quickly, you planned on also having Adriel and Davona also raise their own Palls on either side of Valenlet to try to completely block the Gloom's progress into Rochefort territory. Coordinating with Évard, you used your Doorways to instantly travel to Valenlet and begin its evacuation. Rishoi succeeds in raising the Pall over the settlement, just in time to shield it from the Gloom. However, Davona's and Adriel's attempts fail due to their weakened connection to the Lightbringer. Fortunately, their Palls were not necessary as the Gloom halted its progress northward to instead swarm Rishoi's Pall in an attempt to consume it. Rishoi maintained the Pall and Valenlet's people escaped via the Doorway to Promethea.*

*Leaving Rishoi to maintain the Pall, you returned to Promethea to speak with Iolanthe, whereupon you discover that Elyssria had awoken the mythic power within her that she earned from the Thunderhoof ancestors. Not only that, Elyssria granted her the knowledge to pass on to her elder shamans to perform the ritual to track the journey of Pyrouranos. In exchange, Iolanthe has pledged to be one of Elyssria's champions, just like you. More news came in as a sending from Domenico D'onofrio informs you that the fog's progress halted at the borders fo the Old Arborfane. However, it disgorged a horde of zombies that lurched towards Arbor's Rest, and will reach the town in a day. Although the Steel Peacock has 2,000 of their own mercenaries at Arbor's Rest, you only had a few scout teams. You requested any aid from House Châteaufoy, and they happily sent an old friend, Colonel Amoullet Delisle and the 2nd Rochefort Fusillier Regiment, via the Doorways. Hopefully they and the Steel Peacock forces can hold the line while you raise your own army.*
