# KM:R 30

- After defeating the bandits harrassing the Old Beldame, Ayaki remained to continue talking affairs of the Evergreen with the witch. The rest of the party interrogated their bandit prisoners, gaining information on the layout of the Stag Lord's fort as well as some on the remaining Stag Lord lieutenants. Afterwards, they transported their prisoners back to Promethea, where Ayaki later rejoined them. They put in an order to Onestrio's Trade Post to acquire two *bookplates of recall.*
- They also check in with the kobold refugees. They seemed mostly fine, though Vittoria did mention a few scuffles between kobolds and the settlers. Argus reassures them they will soon end the bandit threat and they will be able to return home soon.
- They also learn that Theodore Duloup has established his own settlement of Wolfshold after many months of fighting against the Thunderhoof centaurs and a bandit group called the Red Hoods. He pushed them both back enough to start settling the Thunderhoof Uplands.
- The party rested in Promethea for about a week, catching up and training themselves. Jupus focused on building his air balloon ship. Meanwhile, Ayaki, Argus, Rishoi, and Davona formed an expedition and continued exploring the Evergreen Plains.
- Domenico D'onofrio returns to Promethea and lets the Evergreen Company know that the Montari Entreprises have agreed to the changes in the proposal. He is also quite happy to receive from the Company some of the goods that were initially lost.
- A messenger from Theodore Duloup, named Armel Duloup and his nephew, arrived in Promethea, bearing a reply to the Company's initial message to him regarding building a trade outpost along their mutual border with the involvement of Monteri Entreprises. Theodore's message is that he wishes that the Company is doing well, and that he is intrigued by the their proposal and wishes to open talks about it. However, the Company decided that it would be prudent to pacify the Arborfane forest before making the meeting. Once that it is done, they can meet with Duloup and Monteri at the site of the proposed outpost.
- The Company also received many reports from Bekka of bandits patrolling the Greenbelt River.
