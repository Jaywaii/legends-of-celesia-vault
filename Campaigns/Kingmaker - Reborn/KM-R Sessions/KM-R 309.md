# KM:R 309

Forged slave report - Sky's Linguistics check result: 85

Day starts at 5am

Gnawfang fails to sneak into a mercenary's command tent within the time limit of 2 hours.

Jupus, Sky, and Orok sneak into the Lyceum. They find a trail of suspicious wards and traps to a lair in the undercroft. They slay the four cyclops cultists who are participating in a ritual to forge the magical container, but not before one of them activates a golem, alerting the cult leader.

# Previously

After ensuring the defense of Valenlet, the Company planned their infiltration of Anatolkerat. Using their mythical *cloaks of the hunt*, Sky, Jupus, Orok, and Gnawfang slip into the cyclopean capital of Ankerata. Meanwhile, Argus distracted the colony's official by presenting himself at their border with a request for diplomatic engagement between Prateria and Anatolkerat. For her part, Arina prepared to meet with the Sotillini Cartel to recruit mercenaries.

Teleporting on the outskirts of Ankerata that Sky previously visited, Gnawfang was assigned to scout the cyclopean mercenary companies camped around the city. She found at least four regiments currently camped, guarding five times their number in slaves. Meanwhile the rest of the team sneaked into the governor's castle, specifically to sort through the offices of both Governor Philankros and his chief councilor Thessamene.

Within the offices, the Company made startling discoveries. In the governor's office, the team found missives from the governing council of the Carpathosi League instructing Philankros to not take any actions towards Prateria, but also to ignore any Praterian attempts of diplomacy. They informed them that should Prateria take any hostile actions towards Anatolkerat, Carpathos has options they can take to dissuade them from doing so, either willingly or forcefully. On a less pertinent note, the team does gain updated map information on the area of the Thalamech Uplands that is being taken over by Anatolkerat. They also find reports on the amount of funding from each of the Carpathosi city-states being poured into their colony here, which coincidentally started a few months before the current crisis began.

Within Thessamene's office, however, the team found clues more pertinent to their current goals. The cyclopean advisor appears to be keeping track of the number and flow of slaves coming from Carpathos being offered to Vorakios' evil fog. They also discover that Thessamene has something of a shopping list of exotic magical components, with some crossed out with dates noted beside them. Sky suspects that if put together properly, these components could be use to build a specialized container to keep powerful magic items hidden from sight, such as a certain sacred relic. Jupus finds some accounting documents that match up with the costs of those components. They seem to be treasury papers as they bear an official mark. Sky, using a bit of mind magic, recognizes the mark as Ankerata's thaumadrasa, the Erixachos Lyceum. Sky slaps his forehead as he recalls that Thessamene is on the Lyceum's board of curators.

It is now 5 am, and the castle's staff and slaves begin to waken.
