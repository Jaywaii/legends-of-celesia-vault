# KM:R 46
[[KM-R 45|← Previous: KM:R 45]] · [[KM-R 47|Next: KM:R 47 →]]

- With two of his companions gibbering on the floor, and another one tied up mostly unconscious, Raggi reports their findings to the Evergreen Company. He asks the Company if they are able to heal his companion's minds, and Sky is able to break Igvald's cannibal curse, but Team 13 will need to go to Rochefort to restore Zen and Rex's mental afflictions.
- They seek that aid at the cathedral of Tierra. The matriarch Jocelyne Labelle is surprised by the group of eclectic individuals. After the team explains their mission and how they became afflicted by these madnesses, the matriarch is happy to aid them. However, she cautions them to be more careful in the future; the afflictions upon Rex and Zen are just within her abilities to heal. Whiel she performs the rites of the Hearthmother, acolytes provide some warm refreshments to Raggi and Igvald that one would expect to get when visiting home. A feeling of warm belonging descends upon Zen and Rex. Both waken with clear minds, with Zen calling out for his family, and Rex suddenly accesses a long-buried data file in his memory that reminds him of how he felt when originally being part of the unity of the great intelligence. The team donates generously to the temple, with Zen giving a little extra.
- After being healed, Team 13 reports in with Sage Joliette Beauchamp of the Rochefort Chapter, and request specialized gear to counter the types of dangers they have encountered so far in Ing-gol'zha. It will take several days for the items to arrive, so Team 13 takes the opportunity to train and hone several new skills. Rex decides to create a journal, a written record describing their venture so far in Ing-gol'zha, with solutions to the puzzles and threats writting in the jiren machine code. The team also begins doing research on the towers and powers they discovered in the necropolis.
- Meanwhile, for the Evergreen Company, their excavators made an astounding discovery in the month of Zhaoren. While clearing ground for a building, they discover ruins of an elf bulding. Workers in Promethea, while working on housing blocks, dig up some well preserved elven scrolls with some information about the green dragons that once ruled the Viridian Marches. It is a report by one Captain Talissa Skywind about the fall of the city of Valimdris by a horde of green dragons and their half-dragon minions. The report dates back to Vandarkaï 19th, 9678 Age of Starlight. The report is quite detailed on the state of Valimdris before its fall, as well as an intelligence report Viridescian Dragon Horde, detailing their composition and tactics. Valimdris was an elven city along the northern shore of the Lake Iridescence, where the Stag Lord Fort is currently located. This spreads the name of Prateria to new regions as elven scholars become interested in these scrolls, and flock to the fledgling nation to study them.
- In the next month of Karthoran, a large influx of immigrants from Lurindor arrive, far more then the current housing allows. Macario Rossi is the most influential and loud spoken of the recent arrivals, and seems to been declared defacto spokesperson for the group. Ayaki successfully learns from Rossi and the immigrants that there was a miscommunication somewhere, and that these pioneers were given the go-ahead far too early to join the growing settlement. Ayaki successfully appeases them with reassurances that they will soon be given housing, and asks that they behave themselves. Rossi is so reassured that he promises to help keep order.
- Back to Team 13, their requests finally arrive and they completed their research. They make their way back to Hushglow Tower, and descend into the necropolis of Ing-gol'zha. Raggi navigates carefully through the maze-like streets of the city, and retrace their steps. They arrive into a new room. It is large and circular, with a large pillar in the middle. There are numerous large stone caskets scattered about the room. Team 13 also finds themselves tired and exhausted, as if they had been traveling for days on end. However, already wary of this place, the team tries to just move on from this place.
- Despite ensuing headaches of trying to figure out this place, they succeed in making it to the Gate of the Defiler. They once again enact the vile ritual to reopen it, although they have to perform it twice as Raggi successfully digested the zombie the first time.
- After another harrowing trek, Raggi is able to lead the team to the Gate of Whispers. Igvald unfurls a *scroll of holy smite*, blasting the gate's pit with positive energy. The team is awashed with a feeling a warmth, and there is a silent explosion, and they swear they could hear a fading wail. The pit is silent but for a quiet whistle of wind. The team is then able to reenact of ritual of opening the gate. Raggi once again willingly takes the mental damage from kissing the corpse and sucking in its breath of death, then exchanging it with the corpse upon the gate's door. The door rolls to the side, opening the way forward.

---

Occult team returned to Promethea. Igvald is cured of his cannibilism curse by Sky. They proceed to Rochefort for additional healing at the Cathedral of Tierra, and report in at the Argent Rose Lodge. They research the Ritual of Severing the Defiler, and the Ritual of Severing the Whispers. They also level up to 7. After 4 weeks, they return to Hushglow Tower to resume their exploration of Ing-gol'zha.
