# KM:R 331

- 3 FATE points to Orok, Aedre, Jupus, Argus, Sky, Davona for completing the vision quest.

# Previously

The Company successfully passed another trial in their dream journey after Pyrouranos. They were able to protect the centaur champion long enough for her to empower Pyrouranos, and deliver a finishing shot from the bow at Vorakios. The Company witnessed how the empowered shot failed to finish the lich, but did damage him enough that his physical form was destroyed, leaving only his dark spirit to flee into towards a distinct mountain range in the distance. With this feat accomplished, the Company was whisked away from another dreamland. They found themselves amidst a battle where centaur tribes were killing each other in order to claim Pyrouranos, which had become a symbol of the Chief of Chiefs among the Thunderhooves. However, the battle was interrupted by the arrival of Sterlingastrax, a great wyrm silver dragon, who admonished the centaurs for their fatal folly. He challenged all who desire Pyrouranos to prove themselves worthy, including the Company. While the centaur leaders fought amongst themselves, the Company attempted to use diplomacy to convince Sterlingastrax of their worthiness. However, the dragon easily rebuffed their appeals. However, once Aedre succeeded in convincing some of the warring centaurs to lay down their arms, then the Company realized how to proceed. Through words of appeal, shaming, and intimidation, the Company succeeded in getting all the centaur leaders to stop fighting before any death occurred. Sterlingastrax expressed his approval, but his testing was not done. The Company proved that they had the heart to hold Pyrouranos, but now, the dragon had to now if they had the might to protect it.
