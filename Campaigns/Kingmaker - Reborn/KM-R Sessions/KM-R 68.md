# Previously

The Evergreen Company took measures to avoid being assassinated by the Dancing Lady's agents. At the same time, plans were made to bring the fight to her. Tabrir and Tobias both agreed to accept the Company's invitation in fighting the Dancing Lady. Sky scried upon them to gain valuable intelligence, and Cethin, Argus, Arina, Davona, Tabrir and Tobias made a public show to leaving the city to explore the Evegreen. On the following night, the group successfully lured out the hidden figures pursuing them.

**Argus Scouting Enemy:**
The night before they left in the caravan, Argus uses his ring to request audience with Elyssria. He apologizes for their silence and absence, as it has been a long time since they spoke, and asks if she has any insight on the Dancing Lady's forces, knowing this is the boon he will owe her.

*During the night, Argus falls asleep, suddenly feeling far more tired than normal. You dream, and sense a female presence beside you, always just out of the corner of your eye. A strand of long, green hair flutters into your sight every now and then. A hand emanating so much power places itself on your shoulder.*

*Suddenly, you are flying through clouds, passing over green fields below you. A ruined keep of elf design appears. Upon its towers and ramparts, you see satyrs with bows and redcaps with scythes patrolling or standing watch. Within the courtyard surrounded by stone walls, many more satyrs and redcaps, along with quicklings, drill in combat.*

*You fly into the keep itself where only the rooms on the first floor are intact. More satyrs, redcaps, and quicklings are seen. You try to fly into the main hall, but its doors slam shut before you, hiding its occupants from your sight. You are shunted back into the sky high above. Your last sight in that hall was that of and elf woman with moon white skin and golden hair, garbed in silver dancer's clothing and crowned with a tiara or intertwining branches of aetherium and platinum with embedded leaves made of emerald.*

*As your vision clouds and fades, there is a chilling whisper beside your ear. "Now you know your foe. Go, my champion, and bring me her heart, and end this threat to us both."*

*You wake, and the sun is just breaking up from the horizon. It is hard to remember clearly your dream, but you are confident in what you had learned.*

Argus will bring his queen her heart, he promises this.
