# Previously

*A rash of disappearances in Promethea becomes known to the Evergreen Company. Immediate suspicion falls on the outerspawns that were accidentally released by Occult Investigation Team 13 of the Argent Rose Society during their quest to seal the city of Ing-gol'zha. This suspicion is immediately confirmed by the Company's guest Aedre of Ashak. One of the disappearances was one of Aedre's freedom fighters, who was gathering supplies in Promethea for their resistance efforts. Aedre tracked her missing goliath Gug to Promethea's aerodrome. In an isolated warehouse, Aedre was attacked by an alien monstrosity, and barely escaped with her life and sanity intact. The Evergreen Company, along with Team 13, quickly rushed to the aerodrome and the warehouse. The monster was gone, but they found its trail slithering into the underground sewer and tunnel network. The trail led back up into another warehouse, where the outerspawn was waiting for them, and as each member of the Company emerged, the monster made its attack.*
