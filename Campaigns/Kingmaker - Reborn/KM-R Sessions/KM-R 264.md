# KM:R 264

# Previously

*Your Team 13 traveled to the city state of Mysthaven in the region of Valendar to speak with its lord, Cyrussilar Ari'luth, a retired adventurer and friend to the Argent Rose. You wanted to learn more of his experience fighting an avatar of Xin-car'lath over a hundred years ago. He informed you that the Mother of all Horrors emerged from its prison within a great mountain in Valendar, and immediately trekked a straight path eastward. Ari'luth's party, the Wolf Mavericks, intercepted and destroyed the entity at the holy city of Archinal, former center of the Asteropan Orthodox Church of Kaï-den. With the clue of Skethelwold as a site of interest to Xin-car'lathan cultists, you and the Mysthaven lord deduced that the avatar was originally heading there. Intel provided by Ari'luth and Mysthaven Chapter-Sage Sumiko informed you that Skethelwold is located within the western mountains of the Duchy of Pendrik. Skethelwold is accessible via the isolated town of Skethelburgh, which also hosts a prestigious university where many of Pendrik's elite, and many across the Aerdwish Empire, attend. You are planning on entering Skethelburgh through the university, which you believe former Team 13 member Raggi will be able to help you with. Meanwhile, Morrigan hopped on board an Argent Rose airship to begin the long trek to the south pole and prepare for the expedition to the lost gnoph-keh city of Roth-teghan.*
