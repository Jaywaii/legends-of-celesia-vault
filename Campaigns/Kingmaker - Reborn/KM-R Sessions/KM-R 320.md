# KM:R 320

Although the Marquis Xemonos acknowledges the PCs service to Viriquia, and the Bishop Basilmon is grateful for their compassionate aid, they are still loathe to let the Cord go. Xemonos considers it a matter of Viriquian pride and property, and the Bishop considers it a rightful relic of the Radimite faith.

Marquis Xemonos suggests that Prateria recognizes any claims made by the HRE into the lands of the Thalamech. His justification is that those lands have proven dangerous to the HRE, thus they were duty-bound to secure it. The PCs know very well that the centaurs would strongly disagree with this option, and would likely resist violently.

Bishop Basilmon's price would be a charter from Prateria to allow a Radimite mission in the nation, officially permitting the evangelizing of the religion throughout Prateria.

An offer of money or resources can help, for every 100 points of capital.

To push the negotiation for the Cord fragment, Arina (11 MP) volunteers to remain in Viriquia to erect the Pall. Salden (11 MP) volunteers to stay to provide her mythic power, but also to serve as bodyguard. Additionally, Domenica (7 MP) joins, and surprisingly the jiren Jester (11 MP) makes an appearance at the Palace. Arina had sent a mental command to her followers to find and recruit the jiren as a bodyguard (16,000 gp a month). Together, they generate 40 MP, enough for the higher cost 35 MP to cast the Pall.

After gaining the Cord fragment, the Thunderhoof centaurs can now protect the refuge, and perform the ritual to trace the path of Pyrouranos. Iolanthe beseeches the PCs to be ones to send through the Dreamlands. Her mother and leader of the centaurs, Andrastreia, will lead the ritual to awaken the power of the Cord of the Ancients to protect the refuge, while Iolanthe will lead the Ritual to send their chosen champions on the Dream Quest. The PCs, through their actions, have proven themselves worthy, and the ideal champions, for this quest.

As soon as the centaurs raised the Cord's protection, storm thunders throughout the land, and the fog flares alive. Tendrils begin to converge on the refuge.

# Previously

After rescuing wounded and retrieving supplies for the Viriquians, the Praterian team managed to get a bit of time to rest, with some of them going to Sky's demiplane in Promethea.. In the interim, Orok returned from Anatolkerat, bringing news that Vorakios' fog had consumed its capital of Ankerata. The urokhan joined the team in Viriquia and used his power to reopen the door between Promethea and the Viriquian palace. Through the collective, the team learned that the other leaders of Prateria were nearly done finishing off the horde of zombies in the Arborfane Gap. After Sky had recovered, he teleported to Viriquia to reopen the door between Promethea and the street in front of the Cathedral of Radiant Mercy.

Just as he finished opening it, the fog went wild. Down the street, dozens of wraiths emerged, and merged together to form a giant whirlwind of wraiths. Meanwhile, the alarm rang out at the palace. Outside, lumbering towards the gate, was a giant undead clyclops abomination composed of dozens of zombies horrifically merged together. The Praterian team now how to split into two teams, and prevent either location from falling to the undead.
