# Previously

Local hunters and Mikmek of the Sootscale Tribe have warned the Evergreen Company of a group of monsters that were killing their way to their nation. In order to protect Prateria, the Evergreen Company had split into three groups to protect their southern border. At the iron mines south west of Promethea, the group of Argus, Arina, and Sky met with the alchemist Moran. Moran and a group of miners traveling on a path to the mines when they were ambushed by centaurs and remoceras. Moran held the assailants off, allowing the miners to flee, before running away himself. He met up with the Evergreen Company, and then led them towards  where he was attacked. Upon approaching the site of attack, the group was ambushed as the fey. Although the party slew two of the remoceras, their injuries are many and severe, and the battle is not over yet.

---

- Tobias Ignatios Malakay (TIM) finally joins the battle after having shadowed the Evergreen Company from afar for many months. Despite a good first attack, his spine is nearly severed from a remacera.
- Moran falls unconscious from his wounds, but a new arrival in the form of Tabrir Cloudgrove who proceeded to heal everyone while imploring everone to cease fighting, but to no avail.
- Sky provided protection by casting *wind wall* around the party which forced the satyr rangers into close combat. Everyone realized he should have done this at the start of the battle...
- After the batttle, the group had to restrain Arina who was afflicted with madness due to her head injuries. She wanted to kill herself in order to reincarnate, but Sky and Tabrir succeeded in stopping her and healing her wounds.
- Moran Desmond slinked off after being healed, unseen while the others argued. After this fight, he had had enough and decided to leave the area before encountering anymore dangers.
