# KM:R 296

# Previously

Having received a fearful warning from the centaur Iolanthe, then losing contact with Theodore Duloup, the Evergreen Company went to visit their capital of Wolfshold. Upon teleporting at its gates, you found the town to be eerily silent and covered by a thick fog. Venturing in, you found no signs of life, and midway to the castle, the fog seemed to come alive, trying to take over your minds, and hordes of zombies began pouring out of the surrounding buildings. During the chaos, you caught glimpse of a blinking light barely penetrating the fog in the distance. You fought your way through the hordes of zombies, and Aedre and Orok were the first to make it to the source of the blinking light. It came from a house with its doors and windows barred from the inside, and surrounded by zombies. It seemed that the light was holding the zombies at bay, and upon knocking on the door, the pair heard voices speaking in a centaurish language within. After you identified yourself, you heard arguing, until a young woman's voice ordered the others to open the door for you. However, upon doing so, the sphere of light protecting the house dissipated, and the zombies began encroaching again. However, the door opened for you, revealing Iolanthe and several centaurs within.
