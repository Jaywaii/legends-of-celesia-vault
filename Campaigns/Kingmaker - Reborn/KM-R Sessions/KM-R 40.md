# KM:R 40
[[KM-R 39|← Previous: KM:R 39]] · [[KM-R 41|Next: KM:R 41 →]]

- In Promethea, the Evergreen Company busy themselves with using their fortune to craft magical items. Sky's expertise and Louis' mystical insight allow them to upgrade Cethin's wakizashi and Ayaki's great katana. They also craft several *sending stones* to share among their contacts such as Onestrio and Joram so they can communicate with them easily.
- At the Argent Rose Society's Rochefort Lodge, Chapter-Sage Joliette Beauchamp meets with Occult Investigation Team 13, one of several teams of Argent Rose agents who specialize in the occult. This team was called in from the Sarkovia Lodge. The team had been adventuring for about five years already. Their members are:
	- Raagi Zugro: a 473 years old kobold professor emeritus of Occult and Historical Sciences of the University of Sarkovia.
	- Igvald Gallevich: martial arts inquisitor who hunts that which should not be.
	- Zen: former carnival cartomancer touched with cursed foresight.
	- RX-817, a.k.a. "Rex": a long-lost early android recovered by Team 13 in a past adventure, and repurposed to become their most recent member.
- Beauchamp infoms the team of why there were dispatched to this region by the Society. South of Rochefort, the expedition known as the Evergreen Company came across the ruins of an underground city with strong ties to ominous otherwordly power while they explored the Evergreen Plains. They have put out a request for any willing explorers who specialize in such topics. As this is right up their alley, the Society immediately accepted the request, and Team 13 were the nearest available team. Team 13 is go down to Promethea and meet with the Evergreen Company to make their presence known and to receive any information about the location before heading their themselves.
- What Beauchamp knows so far is that the underground city that the Company discovered is one known as Ing-gol'zha. Rex had heard of Ing-gol'zha, it was an ancient built by the foul Thul-koon. It was a centre of worship of all known seven cosmic horrors that were present on Celesia during the Age of Horrors. The Argent Rose had records of one of their senior agents, a famed adventure by the name of Talien Firesong, led an expedition of experiences Rose agents over two hundred years ago to investigate Ing-gol'zha and ensure it is never a threat to life. Team 13 is to investigate the city, recover any lore from Firesong, and learn as much as they can of the thul-koon's secrets. More importantly, they must find a way to shut down the power in the city to prevent it from becoming a beacon to all worshipers of outer horrors.
- Upon arriving to Promethea, Team 13 meets with the Evergreen Company in their grand pavilion. They introduce themselves and their affiliation, and their objective to fulfill the Evergreen Company's request for experts to explore the Hushglow Tower and the city beneath it. The Company recounts how they were led there by the Havenford Party, who were a family of Outer Void cultists. They provide the Argent Rose team the cultish trinkets they recovered from them. Rex identifies one of them as the Finger Bone of the Fyodor Drago, a self-proclaim prophet of Askulioth, the Whisper Between Breaths, from two millenia ago. Another of the trinkets is a twisted figurine represensation of Askulioth. The Company also hands over the Firesong note that they found.
- The Evergreen Company grant permission to the Argent Rose team to explore the underground city, and inform them of what they encountered: the will-o'-wisps around the tower and its foreboding aura, the undead they fought in a strange chamber at the base of a glowing tower, and the path that was sealed. Based on the Company's description of the chamber, Zen realizes that he had seen such imagery before in his auguries. He informs everyone that the Company's description matches the imagery associated with Par'manorok, the Defiler From Within. With all the information in hand, Team 13 heads out to Hushglow Tower.
