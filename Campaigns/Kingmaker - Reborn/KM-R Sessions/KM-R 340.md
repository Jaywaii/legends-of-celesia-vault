# KM:R 340

# Previously

Thanks to Dragormir's powers, the Evergreen Company quickly dispatched the banelights guarding the exit gate of the Fairy Garden. Working together, the smart members of the Company figured out how to manipulate the magic of the gate so that they needn't use Vorakios' method of opening it. All it required was for one of them to touch the arch, meditate and focus, and give up some of their life force to the dungeon.

Bjorn, feeling inadequate to the fights taking place in the Fairy Grave, volunteered to give up the needed life force; at least this way he could contribute. However, that would diminish his strength to the point of being unable to cast the needed *plane shift* spell to escape the dungeon. Sky, being the most knowledgeable, performed the task instead. He tried to be clever and instead feed the gate some of his mythic power. Feeling some success, he allowed it to continue, but very quickly, he lost control. The gate absorbed much of his mythic reservoir that Sky was now diminished in tier. However, this did unlock the gate, and an opaque portal open. Inside Sky's mind, five visions appeared.

*Haunting chants evoking religious undertones echoes within an eerie hollowness.*

*The scent of rot mixed with earth accompanied by the sound of tapping against stone repeating over and over.*

*A multicolored void filled with stars, spinning all around.*

*The sound of thick liquids bubbling, accompanied by the sickening scent of chemicals.*

*The sounds heavy hammers crushing bones and striking metal, accompanied by waves of intense heat.*

Consulting with the rest of the Company over the collective, Sky opened the gate to the vision of the rot mixed with earth. The portal shifted, and revealed a dark, vast cemetery. The Company entered the portal. Above them was teh sky covered in dark clouds. All around them were gravestones and tombs of various sizes. Stone paths form concentric circles throughout the cemetery with a giant, ornate crypt at the centre. Flying between each grave was a small gargoyle-like creature dressed as an undertaker. It carried a lamp of eerie blue light in one hand, and a gnarled, metal rod in the other. With the rod, it tapped a gravestone or tomb, and from them rose undead cyclopses and zombie humans, elves, centaurs, and other creatures. Tendrils of dark fog descended from the clouds to engulf the risen dead, and they vanished into it.

The Company only had a moment to take all this in, for before they could act, the Cryptkeeper noticed them. It screeched, and rapidly began tapping fresh graves, releasing a horde of undead, all turning to the Company.
