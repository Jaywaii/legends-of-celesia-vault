# KM:R 39

- The Evergreen Company now stand a clearing with a blasted crater containing the cracked open remains of a great tree. Before them snarls a great dragon's head with a stubby body, a zmey headling. *"Would-be knights of the Arborfane, I once guarded the Heart of the Forest, until that fool of a stag-head slew my body and stole its power. I have feasted upon my own flesh, and that of the remains of the Heart. Now, I shall finish what I began. Hand over the Heart, and in my merciful mood, I shall let you leave alive." Ayaki steps forward, gives the headling a level stare, and simplay states, "No."*
- The Company and the zmey charge at each other. Rishoi leads the way, executes a cartwheel, and slams a downward axe kick, smashing down into the zmey's snout. Bleeding from its nostrils, the zmey snarls and leaps at the malephilim, clawing into her. The zmey's stubby arms belie the deadliness of its claws. Sky draws upon his mythic power to *hasten* his companion, though some of it is drawn into the pit, which briefly glows from the infusion of magical energy. Newly empowered, Davona charges like a comet (*momentum crash*) at the headling, carving out flesh with her many swings of her greatsword. Arina joins in with a *dogpile strike*, hitting a vital spot on the headling to cause it to roll over onto its side. The zmey's blood splashes onto both women, and they discover its danger as it burns their flesh. Some of the blood enters Davona's wounds and weakens her. Argus runs ahead, and swings his earthbreaker into the headling as he passes it. There is a loud crunch as the headling's face is caved in, but Argus is surprise to find a chunk of of his side ripped off. The headling did not accept the blow without paying it back. Argus charges back at the headling for revenge. Cethin draws forth his *ki* to hide himself from sight, and reappears on the other side of the headling, and provides an excellent distraction, but unable to slice the head with his wakizashis.
- It is during this commotion that a small, shadowy figure leaps from behind the trees, and rushes at Ayaki. Whatever it is, it tries to grab at the Heart at Ayaki's side, but the kitsune twists her body keep it out of the figure's reach. "Who are you?!" Ayaki demanded, but the figure slipped back into the shadows. Ayaki's attention returned to the fight at the loud crack from Jupus' rifle. Though the shot misses its mark, it causes the headling to flinch, and Ayaki sees an opening. "Davona! Strike now!" At Ayaki's command, Davona swings her sword at the opening in the headling's defenses, with Ayaki joining in. Though the newly splashed and burned by zmey blood, they both inflict deep wounds. "Excellent call, Ayaki!" Davona shouts.
- Rishoi rushes around the battlefield, quickly attending to everyone's wounds by channeling her inner energies into them. The headling struggles to get back on its feet, but most of the Company's frontliners now surrounded it, laying down blows into it, and ignoring the pain from its burning blood that now spurts out freely. The headling manages to hobble out of the melee. It whips around, opens its maw, and an enormous cone of flame spews out, catching everyone except Sky. Cethin and Gnawfang leap out of the way, but everyone else is burned to varying degrees, with Arina and Jupus getting caught in the full blast. As they burn, both of them focus on the ring of green hair, and call upon the blessing of Elyssria to protect them. Arina also feels a familiar presence embrace her. There is a green flash of light, and Jupus and Arina emerge from the flames black and crisp, but still breathing, if just barely. Magically, the flames continue to burn in the air around them. The zmey headling hacks up blood. *"Fools,"* it spits out. *"If you think that green bitch will succeed at claiming the Heart, you are gravely mistaken!"*
- Davona responds with a *silver fang strike* at the zmey, while Sky floats over to Arina to grant her *bear's endurance*. Davona's swing slices through the zmey's cheek, and Davona's *ki* takes the life taken from the zmey and transfers it to Arina. Arina wakens, and calls upon Mr. Angelcakes to pull more life from the zmey. Argus steps forward at the weakened zmey. He brings down his earthbreaker from overhead, and smashes the headling into the ground, crushing it into a pulp. With the zmey now dead, the Company quickly tend to their wounds, and Jupus beings putting out fires among the vegetation. Seeing that their healing magic seems to make the plantlife more lush around them, Jupus continuously casts several spells to reinvigorate the burnt flora around them. Sky takes the time to collect the magical incendiary glands from the zmey headling's corpse. Ayaki also informs everyone that someone tried to steal the Heart from her during the fight. All that Ayaki could tell was that the figure had a small, feminine form, cloaked and masked in black.
- After recovering, the Company performs Elyssria's ritual to replant the Heart. The small, glowing seed floats from Ayaki's hands to the pit, where roots rise up to englobe it and pull it down into the earth. The ground shifts, and lively roots pull in the corpse of the tree before the pit closes up. A new sapling begins to grow rapidly into a young tree. A ring of large, rainbow mushrooms grow out around it. As the sapling's leaves bloom, each member of the Evergreen Company feels a surge of power within each of them. They are able to sense the once ever present hostility of the forest dissipate, replaced by a sigh of peace. The moment passes, and the imposing presence of the Old Arborfane returns. The trees seem to close in on them except for a single path opening up. After making sure that no other hostile foe remains, the Evergreen Company is able to take the path to exit the forest in peace.
- The Company returns to the Old Beldame. They reassure the old woman that they have safely returned the Heart of the Forest to the Old Arborfane. She informs them that only a day had past since they left, and she thanks them for their labour. The Company departs after formally forming a friendship between their nation and the Old Beldame. In the days to come, peace returns to the lands around the Old Arborfane, and the Company can now focus on building their burgeoning nation of Prateria.

## Heart of the Forest

The Stag Lord killed the zmey that guarded the Central Heart. However, before the beast died, one of its heads separated and hid. After the main body was slain, it lied there, absorbing the remaining ambient mythic energies of where the Heart rested. The remaining headling then devoured its body, becoming a mythic beast.
