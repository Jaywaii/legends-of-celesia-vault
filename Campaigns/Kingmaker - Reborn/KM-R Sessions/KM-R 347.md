# KM:R 347

# Previously

*The Evergreen Company continued battling furnace elementals and forge golems in the Forge of Broken Bones. While Sky and Adriel worked to decipher the aspect of Vorakios locked behind visions on the main furnace's flames and inscriptions inscribed on its base, Aedre, Asako, and Argus worked to unlock the exit portal. The three of them succeeded in opening the exit portal. However, as part of the process, burning metal bands slithered onto Argus, and constricted his limbs. Nonetheless, Argus passed through, focusing on the next room, the one with the sickening bubbling and stench. However, Adriel still had not finished deciphering the aspect while the elementals and golems continued to assault the party.*
