# Previously

Previously on Kingmaker: The Evergreen Company deploys its three brand new legions each to a fort on the southern border of Prateria. The Evergreen Company decides to go on the offensive. After Cortez succeeds in uncovering the location of all of Klorgfang's armies, Arina and Rishoi take Cohors Consul Magica to ambush two forest drake scout teams on the southeastern front, even in spite of a dangerous thunderstorm. Meanwhile, Davona and Jupus lead Legio II out of Silvermine towards the Greenbelt River. They plan on assaulting the Klorgfang army stationed at a fort south of the river. But for that, they must take the time to build a bridge to cross the river.
