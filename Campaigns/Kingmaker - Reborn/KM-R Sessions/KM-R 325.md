# KM:R 325

# Previously

In the next dreamland, the Company found themselves amidst a battlefield between the undead forces of Vorakios against an alliance of centaurs, elves, dwarves, and humans. Among them is a centaur woman wielding the relic Pyrouranos. She told them that they were almost within reach of Vorakios, they just needed to break through the last lines. The Company instinctually knew that they needed to escort the Thunderhoof Champion through enemy lines. However, along the way, the dwarf unit Thunderstone Squad and the elf unit Sunfire Circle were at risk at being swarmed, and the Champion pleaded the Company to save them, as they would be needed in the final push.
