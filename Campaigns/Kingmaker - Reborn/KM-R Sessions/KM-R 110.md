# KM:R 110

# Previously

Previously, on Kingmaker: The Evergreen Company investigated a potential plot to frame both the Teredoro Consortium and Prateria in the assassination of Elvia Sovrani. They discovered that Rocco was under the enchantment of a *suggestion* spell. They came up with a plan to watch over Elvia Sovrani during her night with Rocco to catch the assassins in the act. However, when leaving the embassy after conferring with Sovrani, they succeeded in detecting an invisible person observing them. They gave chase, and thanks to their powers, captured the observer.

Previously on Argent Rose Occult Investigation Team 13: The team defeated the deceased Argent Rose Knight Agath Sorliu in the depths of the necropolis of Ing-gol'zha. Sorliu had risen after her original death into a psychic stalker, haunting the Gate of the Mother. After defaeting her, the team uncovered the means to open the Gate of the Mother by having Raggi swallow one of the yellow globes embedded in the layer of creep covering the floor. Raggi then vomitted the globe's contents, a worm like creature that quickly grew into a fully grown sumgola, a spawn of Xin'carlath. However, the spawn was quickly defeated, and its heart served as the key to the gate. The team collected Sorliu's remains, though Igvald took it upon himself to care for her skull.
