# Previously

Previously, Team 13 returned to Promethea to inter the remains of Connornack, Garska, and Dalaster, the dead Argent Rose agents that were originally part of the Firesong Expedition from over 2 centuries ago. Agna recovered for a week in Promethea while the rest of the time returned to the Argent Rose lodge in Rochefore to report to Chapter-Sage Joliette Beauchamp. Jericho had to be reassigned to another team, but Team 13 were able to order new equipment. After getting those items and once Agna has fully recovered, the team returned to Ing-gol'zha. They made it to the 3rd focal tower. Although similar in dimensions as the previous 2 towers, the floor and lower walls are covered in a semi-solid layer of black, pulsing bile littered with hundreds of marble-size yellowish orbs. An altar stands in the middle of the chamber, and the remains of a small humanoid lies there. However, once the team approached the altar, their exit slammed shut, and a ghostly skull descended from above towards them.
