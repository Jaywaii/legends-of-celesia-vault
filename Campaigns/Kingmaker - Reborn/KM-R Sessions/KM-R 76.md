# KM:R 76

- After 10 days of being in a coma, Louis awakens. Upon seeing the first person in sight, his first words are a cryptic message, delivered in a monotone voice:
	- *"A puppet of blood, dancing and breaking everything within reach."*
	- *"A broken puppet of shadow, dancing around the first."*
	- *"A one-eyed, burnt puppet, coiled in golden thread that wraps around the first two."*
	- *"A pile of discarded broken dolls lies to the side."*
	- *"Atop of it lies one with green hair, and another in dancer's garb."*
	- *"Overhead, the Gentleman in Black whispers and pulls their golden strings."*
- Kal'yne the Mountain Hermit, who was watching over the comatose halfling, quickly records his words, having sensed something ominous about them. The sylph notices that Louis' right eye is darkened by a pale of shadow. Louis then shakes his head to clear his thoughts. He says that he only remembers clearly up to the battle with the Dancing Lady. After that, all he can remember is completely darkness composed of mists of shadows, feeling and in pain. However, shortly thereafter, it is discovered that Louis now has access to higher powers.
- After Louis gets settled, the Evergreen Company discuss his strange words. They suspect that the broken dolls may represent Elyssria and the Dancing Lady. Unfortunately, they are at a loss at the rest of the message, and they table it away for future research.
- Plans are made for the expedition to the northern Heart of the Forest in the Old Arborfane. Beyond Davona, Argus, Jupus, Arina, Sky, Cortez, Tabrir, and Tobias will join the expedition. Before doing so, they plan on speaking with the Old Beldame. During these discussions, Davona reveals a dream she had when she received *Faerus Sicariah*. It was a dream from Elyssria who offered her to take up a quest to seek out and return to her a wooden sword by the name of *Briar*. Elyssria promised that if Davona and her companions do so, they will be rewarded beyond their dreams. Tabrir is somewhat skeptical, but the rest of the group will keep it in mind.
- Plans are made for a festival to celebrate the nation's first military victory. It is planned for Fireday, Tierlan 3rd. As a special treat, one of the premier, Argent Rose-sponsored bardic band, the Magnetar Jones Band, somehow got wind of the party of this up-and-coming nation. They arrive by airship, and put on a fantastic show that will be remembered for years.
	- *A whirlwind of activity descends upon Promethea. Optimism and relief fills the air, finally replacing the worry and fear that permeated for the past several months. Your citizens smile and laughed with sincerity as they hustle and bustle about, preparing the town for the grand festivities. The celebration is being centered around the Great Cypress tree. Sister Félicité works with the ten young dryads to decorate Carnival Memorial Park. Trees bloom and the ground is covered with fantastical fairy flora. With they aid of other fey, the dryads concoct delightful and exotic sweets and drinks, while Sister Félicité oversees and makes sure that they aren't too magical. Stands are set up for musicians to play, playsets are built for children to play, and numerous tables and seats are laid out. A plentiful feast is prepared for all to eat and drink to their hearts content.*
	- *There is a commotion among the folks on the edge of town. "Kobolds!" Shouts a young boy running into town. "Kobolds are coming!" There is indeed a train of two dozen kobolds on giant weasels arriving in Promethea, bearing a black banner with silver dragon emblem. At the head of the group was a tall kobold of dusky, dark grey scale wearing a silver crown. It is King Sootscale, and you recognize that he is accompanied by Mikmek and Samska.*
	- *Although your people are well aware that the Sootscale kobolds are nominally at peace with your nation and that they were invited, their presence nonetheless puts your people on edge. Davona's guard quickly provides an escort for the Sootscale, partly due to honor them, as well as to make sure no ill encounters occur.*
	- *Ayaki welcomes Sootscale and his retinue to Promethea, and invites them to join in on the festivities. The kobold king offers his thanks on behalf of his people for the invitation and for defeating the Dancing Lady. He offers a gift of half a dozen slain boars to help with the feast.*
	- *The celebration is marked by several events. Ayaki provides an encouraging speech to the people to get the festivities started. The Ironwatch forces are rewarded for their victory at the Dancing Lady's keep. The new town hall and taverm are unveiled. During the grand feast, King Sootscale speaks with you all about the future of the Evergreen Plains. "You are now the most powerful force in the region. As fellow monarchs, I must ask what do you intend to do next?"*
	- Ayaki turns to Arina, and gestures her to respond. Arina explains that now that immediate threats to Prateria have been eliminated, they plan to focus on developing their nation as a place where people of all kinds can be happy, and establish good relations with their neighbours.
	- Davona suggests that as an exercise to better relations between Prateria and Sootscale, that they pool their resources to form a joint unit of scouts of Praterians, and kobolds. Both nations will benefit from the knowledge gleaned from the exploration, and it will foster cooperation between the two states. King Sootscale is intrigued with the idea, and will consult with his marshal Kragken.
	- *"We kobolds have never before attained such growth. Thanks to the friendship between our two people, hundreds of kobolds now flock to Sootscale as a beacon of kobold civilization. All thanks to the security you have provided in the region."*
	- *"For now, we have focused on expanding our village. However, the time will soon come where we will need to claim additional land. I hope that each of our ambitions do not come into conflict. Under my kingship, I intend for the Sootscale kobolds to remain as allies with Prateria."*
- This month also sees the new settlement founded in Dragon's Pass in the middle of the Old Arborfane, under the joint management of Prateria, Grondeloup, and Montari Entreprises.
