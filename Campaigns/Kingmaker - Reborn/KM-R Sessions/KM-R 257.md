# KM:R 257

# Previously

*As a simple means to compensate for your lodging at the Ruby Vortex, your team agreed to retrieve the Saffron Prince's signet ring that he owed her. You infiltrated the grand ball being hosted by the Prince, and began investigating where the ring was. Although there were many strange oddities around the ball, you chose to sneak into the royal wing of the palace. You charmed a guard to occupy a free room near the royal quarters, distracted the other guards, pretended to be the Prince himself. However, by the end, you managed to snatch the sign ring from the Prince's secured quarters, and successfully exited the palace without anyone hindering your way.*
