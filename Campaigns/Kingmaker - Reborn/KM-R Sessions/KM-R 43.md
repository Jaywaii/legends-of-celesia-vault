# KM:R 43
[[KM-R 42|← Previous: KM:R 42]] · [[KM-R 44|Next: KM:R 44 →]]

- Davona and Ayaki continue with Dante's rehabilitation. With their words infused with mythic might, combined with the generous treatment provided, the two continue this regimen, and they are able to make Dante really reflect on himself, how he came to this point, who he is and what else could he become. While still arrogant and self-centered, the former Stag Lord lieutenant seems to have a change of heart, and now lacks the bitterness and hatefulness that he had always carried. He starts coming around to Davona's more benign point of view, and changes his mind about doing good for its own sake. Davano removes his constant guards, and much to her satisfaction, Dante responds to this show of good faith by not engaging in any nefarious activities. Instead, he actually becomes helpful in Davona's work, and though he and Acteon continue to butt heads, they are able to work together.
- While all of this was going on, Arina left Prateria to embark on a month-long tour of Lurindor. She travels to every major city, giving amazing violin performances that enrapture every audience. She garners many accolade and much renown for her moving, heart-wrenching dirges. After her last performance before returning home, Arina discovers a shadowy rose left in her room, completely black from petal to stem. She has no idea how it got there, but it feels real to her touch. Yet when sun rises the next day, the rose dissipates in shadowy mist upon being touched by the morning light.
- Team 13 returns to Hushglow Tower. With the Mark of the Veil now inscribed upon them, the aura of fear permeating from the tower no longer affects them. Raggi manifests a totem of the Obelisk at the center of the tower's interior, and beings summoning numerous astral constructs. With dozens of these constructs, the altar is easily moved, and the entrance to the city below is opened. Raggi instructs the constructs to warn away any other visitors to the tower, and to defend it if they attempt to take control of the area. The team descends, retracing the Evergreen Company's steps. They take note of the mural carved in the wall of the hallway leading from the stairs to the vast cavern containing Ing-gol'zha. The mural depict different eras of various peoples abasing themselves before enormous, alien entities, each becoming more primitive as the team moves deeper down the hallway. When they descend into the necropolis, the Argent Rose team aim to reach the first of the seven massive pylons that the Evergreen Company encountered. The twisting paths of the city cause so much confusion that the team begins suffering headaches. Zen and Rex propose going one way, while Raagi and Igvald suggest another. A random card is pulled to determine their path, and the team follows the one proposed by Raagi and Igvald.
- Instead of their intended destination, however, the team finds themselves in a circular chamber. From what remains of the ruins, it appears that this room served as some sort of botanical garden. Amazingly enough, the dried husk of a massive tree still stands at the centre of the chamber within a large basin. Other basins are scattered throughout the chamber, filled with dry dirt. The team begins investing, but upon digging into the dirt of empty basins, the doors to the chamber are slammed shut. Upon closer inspection on the basins, they discover faded inscribings. Using *comprehend languages*, they learn that the engravings are of Thulkesh, but still could not understand it. Raggi's deep learning suspects that this form of writing requires it to be read from the sense of touch. Igvald does so, and with his *comprehend languages* enchantment, he is able to learn that these engravings explain various forms of alien plant life.
- When the team inspected the engravings around the lip of the large central basin, they only have the chance to read the word 'yangethe'. Rex is the only one who recognizes the word. An internal alert goes off in his mind, but before he could vocalize his findings to his companions, the dead tree shudders. Its bark rips open to reveal six glistening black orbs that train on the agents. The agents clutch their heads in pain as the tree shrieks a psychic scream. Rex quickly informs the others that a yangethe is a carnivorous tree, the result of an alien spore from beyond this world merged with a native sapling as a result of thul-kesh experimentation. The alien tree lashes out with its tentacle branches at the team. It manages to grab Igvald, but Rex delivers a precise blow to cause it to let go of his comrade. Raggi manifests an astral construct on the other side of the tree to add to their numbers. but the yangethe delivers devastating swipes at the party. Endruing the alien tree's blows, the Argent Rose team hacked and slashed at it, and finally cut it down, its corpse spewing black ichor instead of sap.

- Davona and Arina continue to wine and dine Dante, and manage to open up his perspective and choose better goals. They removed his guards for a week, and saw his good behaviour, and then made the inal push to turn him CG.
- Arina went on a month-long performance tour in Lurindor. On her last performance she found a rose made of shadowstuff left for her in her room. Arina's fame increased.
- The Occult team returned to Hushglow Tower. Raagi planted a totem and summoned many astral constructs to uncover the stairwell to Ing-gol'zha.
- Zen and Rex want to go one path, but Raagi and Igvald thought another path would be better. In the end, they let Zen's cards decide, which was Raagi's and Igvald's path.
- The team ended up in what seems to be a botanical garden. They learned that the engravings could only be read in Aklo by feeling the engravings. They were then attacked by a yangethe which they quickly slew.
