# KM:R 23

The away team, consisting of Arina, Argus, Cethin, and Sky, contact their home team with their *book of recall*. They needed to rendez-vous at the fangberry thicket so they could get an escort for Sky back to Onestrio's. Sky had suffered several head injuries, and his left arm was left dangling from the fight with the yeth hounds. He needed to go back to Rochefort for healing, but could not do so on his own in his addled state.

A day later, at the fangberry thickets, the away team met up with Davona and Ayaki. Davona was concerned with her brother, and she was loathed to let him be. Ayaki assured her that she'll make she he gets back to the trade post safely. Sky told his sister not to worry, and that they best thing she could do was to pick up his slack. Davona reluctantly acquiesced, and joined the away.

For the next several weeks, the away party of the Evergreen Company continued to explore the Evergreen Plains.

One night, the company heard faint melodious singing at night. It was a woman's voice, a distant echo. Cethin tried to follow it to its source, but got lost. Fortunately, the rest of the team managed to track him down. They returned to camp, and left the singing play until it ended.

Several days later, they met with a hunter named Enrico. He shared with them some news.

Making their way across the Cypress Hills, the party stopped by the Great Cypress. They discovered that the underground lair was infested with giant centipede. It took them a day, but the party managed to clear out the lair of all vermin.

The party made their way back to Onestrio's to retrieve gear for the coming winter. On their way back, they retrieved some belladonna and moon radishes. They tried to get some samples of poppy, but failed to do so. They also stopped by the fangberry thicket. However, they took one look at the swarm of spiders that infested the thicket, and decided that they were one problem they did not need to deal with at the moment.

A few days later, they made it back to Onestrio's.

While the away team was exploring, the home team were not idle. Ayaki had escorted Sky back to the trade post. They arranged for several hgunters returning to Rochefort to help escort him to a temple to get healed.

Meanwhile, Jupus worked on his balloon in his workshop. The others continued to build up resources and funds. They began several projects. They upgraded their garden into a greenhouse, and one of their bunks into a larger lodging. Sky returned after a few days, fully healed, and helped out by crafting alchemical goods. They also talked with Onestrion about using their credit with him to start gathering goods and people to begin building their own, proper nation.

The away team returned. Sky rejoined the away team, eager to get back to exploring. They resumed surveying and mapping the unexplored region of the south west part of the Evergreen Plains. On some nights, they heard the mysterious singing voice, but as they could do nothing about it and it did not seem to be harmful, they simply ignored it.

After a few days of exploring, they saw in the distance a wagon and its people under attack by flying figures. Always willing to protect people, the Evergreen Company charged in.
