# Previously

*Team 13 has arrived on the southern polar continent of Tunakuraq in search for the ecstasy of an ancient creature called a gnoph-keh. You were rejoined by Morrigan. The Chapter-Sage of Lodge Kuvyiak, Amaruqiel Wintersong, arranged to lead the Team to Mikili's Watch, an outpost that the nomadic Nanukhir elves regularly rest at on their wanderings. At this time of year, the Whitewind tribe shold be setting up shop there for several weeks before moving on. Wintersong, a Nanukhir elf herself, had fostered relations with the Whitewinds and their traditional stories might have clues to the location of the lost gnoph-keh city of Roth-Tegan. You arrived at the outpost after 2 days of riding on a Society ski ship but are met with an eerie silence and lack of activity. The vehicles and belongings of the Whitewind elves are present, but all the huts are empty. Only in the central longhouse do you find the elves , but in a horrific state. Many of them have been torn apart and assembled into a grotesque shrine, and upon a long table, more body parts were crudely assembled to create a 6-arm monstrous amalgamation of elf body parts. The eerie silence is broken as the wind suddenly picks up and clouds form above. As a snow storm begins to form, the winds carry the screams of the crew of your ski ship to your ears.*
