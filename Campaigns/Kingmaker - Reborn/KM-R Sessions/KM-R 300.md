# KM:R 300

# Previously

After protecting Rishoi's physical form from deadly grimstalker fey, members of the Evergreen Company joined the malephelim in the psychic battle against their true foe, the legendary cyclops lich Vorakios. Combing their mental strength together, the Company forced the powerful necromancer's psyche on the defensive. These efforts have helped Rishoi maintain the Pall over Valenlet, minimizing the number of zombies able to pass through, allowing the 2nd Fusiliers, Argus, Davona, and Orok to slaughter hundreds of undead with minimal casualties. But the night is still young, and many more hours are still to come before dawn rises.
