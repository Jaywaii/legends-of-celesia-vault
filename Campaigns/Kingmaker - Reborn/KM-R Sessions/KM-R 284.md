# Previously

*You tracked one of the escaped Outer Spawns to a warehouse at Promethea's aerodrome. The Evergreen Company joined together with the Argent Rose Team 13 to fight and eventaully kill the aberration. During the fight, you were assaulted by cultists of the cosmic horrors. After killing the outerspawn, you chased down 2 of the cultists who tried to flee, with Sky dominating one of them. Under compulsion, the cultist informed you that they were under instructions by one "Dr. Whyngard" via dream contact, and that they were supposed to convince the Outerspawn to join them so they can ascend it as an avator of the Whisper Between Breaths. They were supposed to bring it to the northern shore of Lake Iridescence, near the village of Stagfall. Speaking of which, Acteon informed you that as part of going over disappearance reports related to the outerspawn, he discovered that people have also been going missing in and around Stagfall.*
