# KM:R 41
[[KM-R 40|← Previous: KM:R 40]] · [[KM-R 42|Next: KM:R 42 →]]

- Occult Investigation Team 13 make their approach to Hushglow Tower. When it comes within sight, they can see that a dozen carts are parked around it. Faint chanting can be heard within the ruins of the tower, which flickers with lights. The team attempt to stealthily make their approach, but the trepidation of the tower's aura distract them from being successful, even with Raggi's mental protections. A maddening wail can be heard amidst the Thulkesh chanting. *"Until the stars align truly, we beseech the Blessed Elders to accept this morsel to sate your eternal hunger!"* can be heard, and both the chanting and wailing abruptly end with a bloody gurgle.
- "All right, I've heard enough," Igvald says, and the team charges into the tower. Within the ruins of the tower, a group of cultists stand around the circular slab of stone that cover the stairs to the undegroud city, using it as an altar. Upon the altar is a teenage girl, her throat slashed open. Blood, both fresh and dried is sprayed across the surface of the stone. There are cages filled with children and teenagers along the walls, perhaps a dozen in total, all dressed in rags. Their eyes are wide open, and they sway to the hypnotic eldritch chanting coming out of their mouths. The inside of the walls are decorated with eldritch graffiti drawn in blood. Severed human body parts, arms, hands, decapitated heads, feet, are hung all along the walls. Upon seeing the intruders, five of the cultists draw greatswords from beneath their robes. The four remaining cultists draw upon powers from beyond the veil.
- The battle ensues. Igvald nimbly danced between the cultists, avoiding their sword swings. On the other hand, his punches, wrapped in magical thorns, strike true, crushing bones. Rex followed in, protecting his teammates' flank by keeping the cultist at bay with his elven curve blade. Raggi bolsters his team through his psychic connection to them, and Zen tosses his magically empowered cards. In the midst of the battle, the team is lanced by a strike of electricity. The source reveals itself: a will-o'-wisp appears out of thin air above them, then just as quickly vanishes from sight. Unable to attack the will-o'-wisp at the moment, the team focuses on their human enemies. Zen charges a card and whips it at one of the guards. It sticks harmlessly in his armor, but Zen's magic explodes and engulfs the guard in flames and cursing him with weakness.
- The battle against the cultists goes well enough. The cultists are having a hard time landing hits on the Argent Rose agents, while Team 13's synergy avoids that problem. Raggi is able to manifest an astral construct to bolster their numbers. Igvald is even able to bring the fight to the will-o'-wisp when it appears to attack. The task is made easier when Zen casts *glitterdust* on the area to reveal the aberration even while it is invisible. Soon enough, Team 13 kills the cultists, then focuses on the will-o'-wisp. Now exposed, Igvald delivers a powerful flying uppercut at it, and it explodes in a shower of glowing ectoplasm. The sounds of battle end, and the only sounds in the ruined tower are the team's haggard breathing, the whimpering of the children, and the hollow howling of the wind.
