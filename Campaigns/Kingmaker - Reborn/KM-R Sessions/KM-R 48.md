# KM:R 48

---

- Evergreen DT Income (Session 47, Day 35)
- Evergreen DT PCs (Session 47, Day 27)

- Davona's teams: 98 + 84 + 28 = 210 gp. (147 gp after tax)

- Recovering from their fight with the putrid ooze, Team 13 recovers the Firesong scroll case hidden in this chamber:
	- **Entry "Fleshwarping Laboratory:** *"Our first taste of danger was met today. Or was it yesterday? Regardless, we ended up in a strange chamber. Zazghar Farnlorth believes it to be a laboratory of some sort. We found the remains of several bizarre instruments. However, our most convincing clue were a series of destroyed glass containers. They seemed designed to hold a number of people in each. Only one remained, though we could not tell what was within as the glass was obscured by effluvial grime. In our attempts to clean it, we mistakenly triggered its opening mechanism. What was dormant within awoken and attacked us suddenly. More amorpheous oozes seeped into the room. It was a fierce battle, but we destroyed them… or so I hope. In any case we discovered the remains of some very disturing medical instruments among the debris. Abigailla Nurpletock believes, based on the vats and the instruments, that this place was likely one of the thul-koon's fleshwarping laboratory where they experimented on the Younger Races."*
- After Rex sketches the laboratory, the team continues their trek to the next focal tower. After a long and exhausting trek, they enter the base chamber of the third tower. At the centre of the circular slab of stone that blocks the entrance to the next area is an obsidian skull. Of note is its eye sockets that a completely pitch black. No amount of light shines into them when illuminated. It is as if light itself is drawn into them and not out. The script incribed on the door says in Thulkesh *"Light obscures; truth is murkiness; see to enter."*
- Rex attempts to decipher the scripture, combined with the alien symbology engraved upon the walls. However, the complex and bizarre patterns only serves to damage the jiren's cognitive abilities. However, Raggi manages to solve how to open the door. According to the alien imagery and his knowledge of Cosmic Horror lore, the kobold scholar understands that this is the Gate of Radiance, dedicated to the cosmic horror known as F'golthiagga, the Radiance in Darkness. To unlock the gate, one must stare into the void within the skull's eye sockets.
- Argent Rose Occult Team unlocks the Gate of Radiance, but Rex burns out his optics in the process. They return to Promethea to learn that they had spent 5 whole days underground without them realizing it.
- Sister Félicité is concerned with the line of succession, and speaks with Ayaki if she has considered marriage. Alternatively, she asks the council what system of succession does the group have planned in case the worse came to happen. Eventually, the council decides that the next ruler will be elected by the council should the worst come to happen, at least for now.
- Promethean workers unearth another set of elven scrolls: an archeological chronicle of the ancient Halakon Empire, the fabled empire of cyclops that once ruled the lands now known as the Viridian Marches and Lurindor. The chronicle details maps and settlement names, as well as some lore on the empire's politics. It also provides the names of several notable figures, the Emperor Axiotices, the seeress Kalameia, and the archmage Vorakios.
- Cethin learns some information about the province of Iku in distant Xifangsaifu through traveling merchants. The general word is that Iku is quite prosperous under the rule of Lord Yasu. However, the gap in wealth between the nobles and the common folk see to be growing. Business is good, but the number of poor is slowly growing. The only information Cethin is able to glean from the merchants about Yasu is that he seems to be a competent ruler whose only vice seem to be a penchant for frolicking. Ayaki is frustrated upon hearing this when her brothe shares what he has learned with her.
- Davona speaks with Acteon to join her police force. the former bandit lieutenant is hesitant, but Davona shares with him her plan to redeem the former Stag bandits. Acteon confesses that he made many mistakes in the past, costing him his paladinhood and pretty much his whole life, leading him to live in shame and crime. He confessed that he was once a paladin of St. Radimus and was in a political marriage. Though he had affection for the woman and thought she did the same, she ended up engineering his downfall through false accusations. In his rage, he abandoned all oaths and slew his fiancée and her suitor. Davona shares that she too was a fallen paladin like him, but she managed to return to the light in a different way. She tells him that she felt as lost as he was, but in solitude and reflection, found that she could still live in the light, but in her own way. Davona's words move Acteon, and for the first time, he feels hope for his future. The man agrees to join Davona's police force, and promises to not let her down.
