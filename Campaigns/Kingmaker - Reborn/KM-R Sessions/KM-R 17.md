# KM:R 17
[[KM-R 16|← Previous: KM:R 16]] · [[KM-R 18|Next: KM:R 18 →]]

The Battle of Belladonna continued between six members of the Evergreen Company and twice their number in mites over rule of the Great Cypress tree. After a fierce fight, Grabbles laid dead, his crown now in the hands of Cethin. The remaining mites fled, leaving their kobold prisoner behind. The Company freed the kobold and healed him. He introduces himself as Mikmek, the chief fighter of the Sootscale tribe, and he is immensely grateful for their aid. He recognized them from the tales of his friends Rikrak, Zorzik, Rurik, and Drazdik who previously met the party before, though he revealed that they had been sacrificed in a ritual to strengthen the tribe against all the problems they were experiencing.
