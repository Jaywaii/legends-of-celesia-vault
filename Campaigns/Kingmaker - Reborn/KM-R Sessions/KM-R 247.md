# Previously

### Argent Rose

*Previously, Argent Rose Occult Investigation Team 13 opened the passage through the 7th focal tower of Ing-gol'zha, the Gate of Shorok'logoa, granting you access to the dreadful unknown core of the necropolis. However, after harrowing battle with its guardian, an outer spawn, you opted to return back to the surface, resealing the towers behind you. The forbidden lore you have acquired will allow you to figure out how to truly subdue the necropolis.*

*You updated the Rose Scholar Thonra 'Mindshield' of the forewoman Hushglow Expedition of what new dangers you uncovered, allowing her to adjust both the inner and outer defences around the ruined tower accordingly. You returned to Lodge Rochefort, and updated Chapter-Sage Joliette Beauchamp, and proceeded to do some training. A month later, Team 13 is reconvened at Sage Beauchamp's office. However, Team 13 has seen seen another round of personnel changes. The ordeal has taken a toll on the kobold sage Raggi, severing his connection to his zeitgeist that maintained his immortal, so he has chosen to retire from field duties to live out his remaining years in peace. Due to his experience and centuries of great service to the Society, he was offered the seat of Chapter-Sage at Lodge Mirkoveski. "Either way, the faculty of his university will no doubt be relieved."*
