# KM:R 322

After gaining the Cord fragment, the Thunderhoof centaurs can now protect the refuge, and perform the ritual to trace the path of Pyrouranos. Iolanthe beseeches the PCs to be ones to send through the Dreamlands. Her mother and leader of the centaurs, Andrastreia, will lead the ritual to awaken the power of the Cord of the Ancients to protect the refuge, while Iolanthe will lead the Ritual to send their chosen champions on the Dream Quest. The PCs, through their actions, have proven themselves worthy, and the ideal champions, for this quest.

**Andrastreia:** "I cannot thank you all enough for bringing back the Cord of the Ancients back to our people. Our people owe you a great debt. Moreso should we succeed in this next part."

**Iolanthe:** "My friends, we must once more beseech for your aid. My mother and the elders will perform the ritual with the Cord of the Ancients to shield us from the Undying Eye's evil, and I will perform the ritual to send our champions on the dream quest to trace the path of Pyrouranos. That leaves few capable left to actually send on the journey. While our remaining warriors are brave, I know you all to have the experience and the skills needed to survive this quest. Will you face this challenge for us, please?"

As soon as the centaurs raised the Cord's protection, storm thunders throughout the land, and the fog flares alive. Tendrils begin to converge on the refuge.

**Andrastreia:** "Be careful on the Dream Quest. The spirits of that realm hold their secrets tightly. Through this ritual journey, you will be tested by wit or arms, or outright resisted by them. However, should you overcome those challenges, you will be able to glean those secrets from the dreams that remain from those who lived in those times."
