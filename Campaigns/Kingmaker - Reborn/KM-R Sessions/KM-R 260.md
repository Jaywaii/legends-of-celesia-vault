# KM:R 260

# Previously

*After facing the Octhoculoid and retrieving 3 pearls containing the thoughts of incutilis lords, to willed yourselves back to the Nythagoi Trade company compound with the crew of the Moon Siren. You handed over 2 of the pearls to Xura Nythagoi as compensation for their help and the loss of the ship. You took some time to rest and recovery at the Ruby Vortex, and sought out as much information as you can about the Dagorrad Marshes and whispers of will-o-wisps. Once ready, you willed yourselves across the Dreamlands to the Dagorrad Marshes. Upon arriving, you found discovered a village of swamp natives nearby. You carefully approached until the village came into view, but that was when you discovered over a dozen natives surrounding you. You attempted to establish a dialog with them, but they demanded your immediate surrender. Myria attempted to subtly cast a psychic spell, but the natives noticed it and saw it as a threat, and they charged to attack.*
