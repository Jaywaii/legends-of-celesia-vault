# KM:R 317

# Previously

After protecting the fleeing Viriqian in the streets, and defending Capt. Constanis' defense line, the small party of Praterian agents returned to Baron Xemonos. Both he and Bishop Basilmon acknowledged the party's success. They were given some time to recuperate, as Xemonos still wanted them to prove themselves further. Shortly after, the party was assigned the task of escorting a group of laborers to a supply depot in the northern district of the city. Not only does the party need to protect the laborers there, they need to defend them while they gather supplies, then escort them back to the main defense line so they can redistribute the supplies to other defense points in the district. The party has now just reached the supply depot, but a wave of zombies soon swarm the street in front of the laborers.
