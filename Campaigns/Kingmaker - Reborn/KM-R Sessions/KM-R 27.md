- **Necropolis of Ing-gol'zha**
	- *You descend  down the long staircase along the vast cavern wall. The wall itself is decorated with elaborate but alien carved murals. They depict a mix of incomprehensible patterns that are confusing to look at, and images of recognizable body parts that were attached to each other in very not recognizable ways, and being used in some very disturbing ways.*
	- *At the bottom of the staircase is a short paved pathway into the city. Although the beam of light is in the center of the city, its light somehow reaches even to the very edge. The shapes of the buildings and other objects not straight. They bend and curve at odd angles, and seem almost organic. Upon closer inspection, they do not seem to be made of stone. More like some sort of calcified mucus or slime.*
	- The Silver Sage Talien Firesong (male half-elf bard 12) led an expedition of 20 experience Argent Rose knights and scholars into Ing'zha over 200 years ago. He left his notes at key points throughout the city in case he failed to return.
		- *"Tierlan 7th, 5071 Age of Strife. I leave this, and plan to leave several other messages, here as my legacy should the worse come to happen. May my words serve as a guide and warning to those who follow after me. I, Talien Firesong, Silver Sage of the Argent Rose, have traveled to this blasphemous underground necropolis along with an expedition of 20 Argent Rose knights and scholars, each experienced adventurers in their own right. Our clashes with the Void Singers have led us to this lost city of the Thul-koon. I have taken it upon myself to explore and record whatever we can so we can continue to prevent the return of the Ancient Horrors. Know this, you stand at the entrance of the Necropolis of Ing'zha. According to ancient lore, it is believed that the thul-koon abandoned the city in the early years of the Age of Starlight, over twenty thousand years ago. Since then, the Old Cults made pilgrimage to it, and would build and rebuild on top of it. The main hallway to the entrance displays architecture from the Age of Starlight, the lower half of the staircase displays that of the Age of Kings, and the top half from the Age of Strife."*
		- *"From this position, we have a good view of the layout of the city. Ashani the Blue and Dalaster of Westgate concurred with my observation; the thul-koon created their city as a massive ritual circle. We believe that the city could act as a natural receptacle for the proper mix of magical energies to pierce the Veil to the Outer Void. In theory, the thul-koon could have use Ing-gol'zha to summon a Cosmic Old One. We do not know if the thul-koon were able to do so, but the fact that the Age of Horrors ended with our world's survival suggests that they were unsuccessful or did not get the opportunity to do so. Regardless, it is our duty to find out everything we can about this place, and prevent it from being used again."*
		- *"From here, we can see seven towers. According to Darmok, Sage of Dol Baran, they must be the focal points that make the circle functional. We will need to descend into the city, assess area, and find a way to disable or destroy it. Doing so should render the whole city inoperable. We know not what dangers remain in Ing-gol'zha, but should we succeed and survive, and I will return to collect my notes. May the gods guide us through. -Talien Firesong of the Knights of the Quill, Silver Sage of the Argent Rose."*
	- *"Traveling through Ing-gol'zha requires you to navigate through a complex of open pathways and closed rooms. The direction wind, split and rejoin without much sense, at least to you. A perpetual haze of thin mist permeates throughout the city. It does not obscure your vision, but it carries the faint light that shines from the centre of the city."*
	- PCs make progress to the next gate only they fail a Will DC 20 save and suffer 1 point of Wisdom damage. If they succeed, they make no progress and end up in a useless area, unless they succeed by 10 or more. If they do, they get to one of the gates without any Wisdom damage.

Each room has Talien's notes in a leather tube hidden somewhere. Some of the pages and text have been burned off. Only legible parts remaining are Talien's notes about the room and clues about how to get past.

- The party descends down the long staircase along the vast cavern wall. The wall itself is decorated with elaborate but alien carved murals. They depict a mix of incomprehensible patterns that are confusing to look at, and images of recognizable body parts that were attached to each other in very not recognizable ways, and being used in some very disturbing ways.
- At the bottom of the staircase is a short paved pathway into the city. Although the beam of light is in the center of the city, its light somehow reaches even to the very edge. The shapes of the buildings and other objects not straight. They bend and curve at odd angles, and seem almost organic. Upon closer inspection, they do not seem to be made of stone. More like some sort of calcified mucus or slime.
- One of the rooms explored appeared to be some sort of storage facility, with numerous stone casket containing nothing but dust. However, by opening the caskets, the party triggers a trap, and the stone doors of the room slam shut, trapping the party within. Vents open up, and gas begins rushing in. The party holds their breath, and being smashing through one of the doors to make their escape. Returning, they found another Argent Rose scroll case, detailing how the city is taking a toll on the Firesong expeditions mental fortitude. Nonetheless, the expedition continued on.
- After walking through many many tunnels and empty rooms, yet feeling like only minutes had past, Ayaki manages to lead the party to one of the focal towers, though it takes a toll on Ayaki's psyche in order for her to guide them there. The tower is a grand chamber whose ceiling reaches far above them, nearly out of sight. Its architecture is far more elaborate with alien decoration. On the opposite side of the entrance they passed through was an enormous, skeletal torso, headless with six gaunt arms, embedded in the wall. Underneath the torso was a passageway blocked by a stone slab. Along the edges of the circular chambers was a moat filled with glowing green viscous fluid. Sky is blasted by a powerful sense of magic from everything in the chamber, but decides to collect a sample of the fluid. He pours it over a piece of boar meat that Argus provided. The piece of meat rots and contracts, then releases a foul smelling gas. Sky thinks he hears a silent scream.
- Peering closer to the gate, the party discovers a cavity in the stone slab, the size of a fist. There was also Aklo script inscribed along the edges of the slab. Sky reads it, with the others ready to knock him out if he becomes possessed. The text reads "To continue your ascent to the truth, pay tribute to the Defiler From Within. Par'manorok dictates that true sight comes from within." As soon as he reads the text, the giant skeletal statue moves an arm. It reaches down into the trough of slime, and pulls out a rotting corpse, dangling it a few inches above the middle of the chamber's floor. Its eye sockets are open, revealing milky white orbs.
- The party is in a dilemma. They cannot progress, and going through this ritual might activate the focal tower. They debate leaving this place for now. Examining the statue with magical sight only gives Sky an even worse headache. All he got out of it was that it reeked of magical necromancy. The party decided to allow Argus to smash the statue's arm. The earthbreaker bounces off of it harmlessly. It drops the corpse, then all six arms reach into the torse, and rips it open, revealing a dark, endless void of dread. The room shakes, and the door slams shut. A torrent of green, maggoty fluid erupts from the void onto the party, burning them badly. The trough around the room bubbles, and two hordes of plague zombies crawl out from either side of the room.
- Rishoi, in 3 quick blows, blew apart one of the hordes, her fists and feet empowered with healing energies smashing through multiple zombies in single blows. Ayaki got grappled by the other horde, but Rishoi dashed from the other end of the chamber to heal her and cartwheel kicked into the horde. Sky used his *wand of burning hands* to burn through the horde. He also conjured a magical pit beneath them to drop them in. Argus smashed his earthbreaker into the horde, blasting away all the zombies back into the pit.
- After the zombie hordes were destroyed, the massive torso closed and its arms reset back to their original positions, with one of them reaching back into the pit to pull out another corpse. The entrance doors behind them swung back open. The party was fully agreed that whatever was down in this city was well beyond their current preparations. After recording the inscription, they hastily made their way back to the surface. They send a message back to their companions back in Promethea telling them to seek out aid to investigate this horrific city.
