# Previously

*Team 13 explored a network of tunnels underneath Skethelburgh with passageways marked with the Mark of the Mother, a cultist symbol of Xin-car'lath. Within them, you found a torture room with the bodies of Mardwyn Carth and his teamsters, the caravaneers that transported you to Skethelburgh. Your presence triggered the spawning and arrival of a swarm of verdurous slimes that you had to defeat. Once done, you continued traveling through the tunnels and found your way out, exiting into the forest of Skethelwold. Using your tracking skills, you followed a rough trail through the forest. However, the trails were stalked by monsters, one of them being a hodag. While fighting the hodag, more monsters appeared, a yaoguai, a manticore, a chimera, and a cocktrice. You killed them all, but Rhinsley Morroway, the Coil agent who surrendered to you, was killed by the chimera's acid spray. You also saw a figure observing you in the distance before they disappeared back into the forest. You hurried forward, and the trail led you to a sinister clearing with a round platform made of fossilized flesh in the shape of the base of a large tree's trunk. Upon the platform's surface were numerous blasphemous runes and thulkesh scriptures. Your analysis led you to conclude that this was a summoning platform linked to the Gardens of Nobu-Shugath, and required a number of sacrifices in order to complete the summoning ritual. However, with the Pakulkan Astrologues and your own skills in rituals, you figured out a way to change the ritual to allow you to travel to Nobu-Shugath yourselves without needing any sacrifice.*
