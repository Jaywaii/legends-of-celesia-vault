# KM:R 22

- Prisoner morale is stable, but slightly disgruntled. A DC 20 Sense Motive check reveals that the prisoners haven't really reformed. They still have no respect for the law or honest life. Only the combination of the deadly skills of the PCs and their continued mercy keeps them in line. Vittoria tells the PCs that the prisoners responded well to Father Joram's sermons, and seemed to be coming around before he left to rebuild the temple. She says that the prisoners like and respect Ayaki, but suggests that the PCs get someone better trained in rehabilitating them, especially since Ayaki occasionally leaves.

---

The party spoke with Bekka about prisoner morale. From their observation of their behaviour and overhearing snippets of their conversations, they felt that their prisoner will continue to behave, but have not come around to respecting the law or an honest life. The half-orc told them that the former bandits are behaving, but are slightly disgruntled. They have no motivation to rehabilitate, and they've been forced to work every day. They respect the Evergreen Company, especially Ayaki, but only their deadly skills and continued mercy keep them in line.

The Company thought about what to do. They did want to rehabilitate these prisoners. They decided to grant them a week of feasting, and declare that they would have 2 days a week of rest. Bekka mentioned that the prisoners were responding well to Father Joram's sermons, but since he left, there was no one to help minister their spiritual growth. The Company decided to seek out another preacher. They would contact the away team to meet with Joram at the Temple of the Elk and see if they could get a referral.

Vittoria, the leader of the guards the Company hired advised caution about giving the former bandits so much time off with nothing to do. She was concerned that their idle hands might be used for no good. The Company agreed, and ordered multiple games and toys to be brought in. And with their own funds, they prepared a plenty of food for everyone in the trade post for a week. Even the guards and Jupus' workers. When they announced their plans to the prisoners, they cheered and their morale was raised.

The away team met with Joram, and he suggested Sister Felicité from the Four Seasons Temple in Rochefort. They sent the message back to the home team with their book. The home team then sent a messenger back to Rochefort with their request and orders. During that time, some of the hunters started talking about a crazed hermit near the Draketooth forest who would attack anyone they meet. He was accompanied by a large cat, a tiger or a mountain lion. One of the survivors of such of an attack described the old mad man.

Biaggio was at the post when this story was talked about. The hunter actually mentioned that the mad hermit looked like Biaggio. Upon hearing the description of the mad hermit, the old alchemist became agitated, and started cursing. The Company asked him what that was about. Biaggio told them the story of his younger brother who cut off his pinky finger and killed their mother before running off southwards into the wild. Biaggio said his brother had always claimed to hear voices, and was erratic even in their young age. All this happened over forty years ago. Biaggio asked the Company that if they come across his brother, they could do whatever they wanted with him, but he asked them to bring back a locket with their mother's picture that his brother had taken when he fled. He promised his savings, some 300 gold pieces, as a reward, or alternatively, 900 gold pieces worth of potions. Biaggio did not remember his brother's name (he was not even sure that 'Biaggio' was his own name), but he gave a description of what his mother looked like.

The away team arrived back at Onestrio's. At the same time, Sister Felicité arrived. The Company introduced her to the prisoners, and they began the feast. The prisoners' morale jumped up significantly, and they praised the Evergreen Company for their generosity.

The next day, half the party ventured out to continue their exploration. They made good use of Sky's new *rope trick* spell, although the horses were forces to remain on the ground. On their 3rd night, they were attacked back a pack of 6 yeth hounds.

Cethin and Arina hopped down from the extradimensional space. Cethin was immediately tripped, but Arina managed to dash around and stabbed a yeth hound through and through with her rapier. In one blow, she had felled one of the fabled beasts. The hounds bayed, and Cethin and Arina were overcome with unnatural fear.

Argus dropped down and engaged a pack of them. Sky poked his head and hands out of the extradimensional space and fired a *burning arc* against several of them. In return, one of the yeth hounds bit onto the rope and flew up into the extradimensional space. It bit Sky's leg, pulling him out of the *rope trick* and onto the real ground.

Arina kept running. Cethin ran, but managed to draw on his loyalty to his companions to overcome the magical panic in his heart.

Eventually, the party managed to defeat the pack of yeth hounds, but not before Sky receiving many severe injuries.
