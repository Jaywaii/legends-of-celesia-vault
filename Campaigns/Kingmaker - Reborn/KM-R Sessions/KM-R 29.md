# KM:R 29
[[KM-R 28|← Previous: KM:R 28]] · [[KM-R 30|Next: KM:R 30 →]]

- A group of bandits are outside of Old Beldame's hut, just outside the fenced yard around the hut. By the time the PCs come into sight, the bandit group's leader, is calling out to Old Beldame to submit to the Stag Lord's rule. Should she reveal the secrets of the Old Arborfane and use her magicks for his cause, she would be greatly rewarded. In response, Old Beldame's craggly voice shouted out from the inside of the hut that they better all bugger off, and screw their Stag Lord at the same time. That's when the pumpkin head scarecrow comes to life and stands threateningly in the frontyard between the bandits and the hut. Lira shouts back that if she will not submit, they she must be removed. The bandits see the Evergreen Company arrive, and Lira shouts at Beldame to pay attention, as the bandits will demonstrate what happens to those who defy the Stag Lord.
	- The bandit leader is a woman name Lira Kal. She has 5 bandit swordsmen and 4 bandit archers.
	- Should the Evergreen Company have some difficulties, Old Beldame sends the scarecrow to attack the bandits.
	- The Old Beldame is very difficult to deal with, but responds well to people who treat the Arborfane with respect, and perform services to aid it.

---

- During downtime, Domenico returned to Promethea to let them know that his Moneri superiors found their proposal acceptable. However, the Evergreen Company, realizing how difficult it would be for them to fulfill their part of the bargain, wanted to renegotiate.
	- The Company initially offered the whole of Stag Lord's fort over to Monteri, but Domenico refused, stating that this would be too huge of a risky investment for Monteri Entreprises: they would be assuming too much risk for something they had little interest in owning to begin with.
	- After further discussion, a new proposal was drafted. The Evergreen Company would establish a new settlement at Ravenna's old camp. They would create a joint venture between the Evergreen Company and Theodore Duloup and Monteri Entreprises, and set up a trade post between them, with Monteri representative overseeing it. Domenico seemed satisfied by this change, and would return in a week's time with his Monteri superiors reply. Meanwhile, Evergreen will need to contact Duloup and have him agree to this proposal.
- The Company also learned from Bekka about Acteon, Dante, and Toro, the Stag Lord's main lieutenants. From how Bekka described Acteon, the 2nd-in-command,
- Meanwhile, the away team discovered a group of bandits outside of Old Beldame's hut. Their leader, Lira Kal, was threatening the witch to serve the Stag Lord. The witch was having nothing with it, and activated her scarecrow golem. Lira Kal saw the wagon's approached, and had her bandits attack them as an example.
	- To the bandits dismay, they realized that they were facing off with the Evergreen Company, but Lira Kal threatened them onward. Several of them were killed as Ayaki charged through them. At the end of the battle, half of the bandits were dead, two fled, and the rest surrendered. The Company tried to keep Lira Kal alive, but Mr. Angelcakes needed his due. They interrogated the surviving bandits, and learned that the Stag Lord and several hand picked bandits traveled for many months to the monstrous southern Evergreen lands and the Old Arborfane, and returned with immense power. He retrained his bandits with this power, and new lieutenants were installed.
- The Company addressed the Old Beldame diplomatically, and after declaring their intentions by shouting across the yard to her hut, she invited them in. Strega Elga was interested in their sincere declaration that they wanted to heal and appease the forest. She told them that from what the bandits confessed to, that the Stag Lord may have stolen one of the Hearts of the Forest from Old Arborfane. She warned them that although the forest may die eventually without the heart, it will take many, many years, and in the mean time, the forest will lash out violently.
- The Evergreen Company also asked about reviving the Great Cypress. Old Beldame was impressed by their desire to do so. She confirmed their current options in reviving the old tree. The party admitted that they didn't want to sacrifice the Old Arborfane just to revive the tree. Old Beldame admitted that their may be way to use the power of the Heart of the Forest to revive the tree, but it will come with a price. She will tell them what the price would be, and what is needed to to enter the Old Arborfane without it killing them, but only after they defeat the Stag Lord and secure the Heart of the Forest.
