# KM:R 28
[[KM-R 27|← Previous: KM:R 27]] · [[KM-R 29|Next: KM:R 29 →]]

The Stag Lord had been battling and subduing monsters south of the Greenbelt River, then explored the southern region of the Old Arborfane, along with several elite bandits. He had left his 2nd in command, Acteon, in charge in his absence, along with lieutenants Dante and Toro. Acteon is stressed out. He never found any pleasure in banditry, doing it mostly as a means of lashing out against the world that rejected him. However, he had come to form a bond with his men after spending so much time training and leading them in the Stag Lord's stead. The Evergreen Company's successes against the Stag Bandits have demoralized him. His men were getting killed, Dante and Toro are gunning for his position, and the Stag Lord has been gone.

Eventually, the Stag Lord returned, more powerful than before. He and his elites being retraining the Stag Bandits, brutally, but with success. In his quests, the Stag Lord claimed the southern heart of the Old Arborfane, and gained more favour from Elyssria, which she bestowed upon him to empower his bandits. Acteon was resigned that the Stag Lord would kill him for losing 3 quarters of their territory, but the Stag Lord didn't care about the losses, which also infuriated Acteon. In fact, the Stag Lord said that they deserved to die for being so weak, and that he was looking forward to fighting to get his territory back. Acteon depredation of his current situation continued to sink. He believes that the Stag Lord is going to drag he and like-minded bandits into death. Nonetheless, the Stag Lord brutally retrained and invested the power he had gained in his exploration of the Old Arborfane.

As a result, Old Arborfane has awoken and become wrathful. However, the awakening is slow, and it will take it time before it can reach out beyond its borders to wreck its vengeance. However, any who enter the forest will be in danger.

- Meanwhile, the bandits waylaid a caravan of weapons and armor destined for Carpathos. The caravan was owned by the Steel Peacock Trading Coster, a subsidiary of Montari Entreprises, operating out of Valdargent. Only 3 survived the encounter: Domenico Donofrio and two guards. Wounded and exhausted, he arrives in Promethea full of righteous anger against the Evergreen Company. He blames them for false reports of winning against the Stag Bandits, and intends to bring his complaint to Trade Prince Enzio Montari, and impose fines and sanctions against the Evergreen Company and Promethea. However, Domenico can be appeased with a Diplomacy or Bluff check.
- In other news, reports have come in that the Sootscale kobolds have stopped trading silver with Onestrio's Trade post. The Stag Bandits have raided the kobold mines, and are now using them as slave labour. Acteon had his bandits only rough them up, and not killed outright. The kobolds, easily outmatched, surrendered immediately. Acteon personally led the raid. He ensured that the kobolds were beaten, but not killed. He personally stopped Toro from killing Mikmek, saying that a kobold as strong as this one would make an excellent slave.
- The Witch of the Evergreen, the Old Beldame, is concerned with the growing hostility of the Old Arborfane.

---

- The Evergreen company proposed to Domenico that they would retrieve as much of the Monteri weapons and armour from Stag Lord Bandits as much as they can. Domenico said that would not be enough, as some of their losses (wagons and men) could not be compensated. He said that in addition to the retrieval, the Evergreen Company would build a guildhall and a warehouse in their settlement of Promethea within a year, and they reserve it for Monteri Entreprises and Steel Peacock Company to move into them.
- The Company didn't like that idea, so they countered proposed that they'll take over the Stag Lord's fort, build it into a settlement. While part of their nation, its governor will be a representative of the Monteri Entreprises, and both the Evergreen Company and Monteri Entreprises will each pay half for the guildhall and the warehouse in the settlement.
- Thanks to the good treatment from the Evergreen Company during his stay in Promethea, Domenico was amenable to the proposal, and will bring it to his superiors for them to consider.
- Meanwhile, there are reports of dangerous wildlife and plantlife emerging from the Old Arborfane. Something's got them stirred up, and they been extremely hostile to all living things. Many hunters were killed by these things. The Company learned that maybe the Old Beldame, the Witch of the Evegreen, might know a thing about it.
- The Company also learned that the kobolds didn't send their monthly shipment of silver. Onestrio requested the Company to find out why.
- The away team (consisting of Argus, Sky, and Rishoi) arrived at the kobold mine to discover that Stag Lord bandits have taken over. However, the Company killed the bandit lieutenant, Maegar Lorn, and several other bandits while the rest fled. They freed the kobolds, and brought them to Promethea to be safe until the bandit problem was dealt with permanently. There was some heavy concerns from the Promethean workers, but the Company allayed their fears, at least for the time being.
- The away team, consisting this time of Argus, Arina, Ayaki, and Sky set off to continue exploring and meet with the Old Beldame. Along the way, they traveled along the border of the Old Arborfane. Although they encountered no beasts, they saw and heard many eerie things, like giant wolf tracks, dead men killed in magical fashion, and remains of strange plants. The Old Beldame's hut finally comes into view.
