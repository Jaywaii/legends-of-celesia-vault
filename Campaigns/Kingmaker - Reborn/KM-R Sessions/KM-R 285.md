# Previously

*Assembling the team together with Argent Rose Team 13, you traveled to Stagfall to investigate the disappearances in the town and surrounding area. You tracked down to fishermen that were found suspicious by the local town guard. It turned out that they were indeed cultists, and they were just returning on boat from delivering another victim to Whisperlight Isle. After interrogating one of the prisoners via domination, everyone except Yojimbo who left to deliver the prisoners to the lcoal authorities, flew to the island and tracked traces of the latest victim to a decrepit manor. A butler invited you in for dinner with Dr. Whyngard, and the interior seemed normal, but magical sight reveals a more worn and warped reality underneath the visible surface. You were welcomed by Dr. Whyngard in the dining hall, and offered a pleasant meal with equally pleasant conversation. After everyone had their fill of food and talk, Claudius Whyngard thanked you all for coming, and that now is the time to embrace the real truth of reality, and his form begins to shift.*
