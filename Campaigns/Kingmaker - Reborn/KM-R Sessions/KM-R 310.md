# KM:R 310

# Previously

With all clues to the Crod fragment pointing towards the Erixarchos Lyceum, Orok, Jupus, and Sky sneaked into the Ankerata's thaumadrasa. Following a trail of suspicious wards, illusions, and enchantments, the team gained access to a set of isolated hallways and chambers that led to a hidden undercroft. Within it, they found four cyclops mages performing a ritual with the ingredients that was listed in the Thessamene's office. These mages were constructing a container on a pedestal. Suspecting that the Cord fragment was within it, the team rushed in and killed them, but not before one of them touched a sigil of a nearby sarcophagus. The sarcophagus opened to release a brass golem, which the team also defeated quickly. However, the sigil apparently also set out a silent alert as more cyclops teleported in the connecting hallway, just as the team closed the chamber and barred the doors.
