# Previously

*Team 13 is welcomed to Skethelburgh University by its Chancellor, Morgwyn Dellentyre. You have been assigned a guide, a senior student by the name of Aerona of House Lysdragal. Afterwards, you went to one of the local pubs frequented by students, the Gilded Goblet. Bur-Jack engaged in a drinking game with one of the senior students after taunting him, and learned 2 rumours: every year, there occasional student or tourist goes missing and students believe the reason is the forest of Skethelwold. The other rumour is that of students disappearing and reappearing in different locations throughout the university, likely due to the fabled hidden passages within the university's walls. One student mentioned of a noble girl getting up to no good with her servant in the library without ever getting caught. After that, you went to the Mother's Embrace, a high-class hotel and reserved rooms there, and began planning how to proceed with your investigation.*
