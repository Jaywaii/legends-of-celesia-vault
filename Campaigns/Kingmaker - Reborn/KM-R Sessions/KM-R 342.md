# Previously

*Still in the Cemetery of a Thousand Corpses, the Evergreen Company succeed in killing its guardian, with Jupus decapitating the Cryptkeeper. The Eye of Vorakios peered down upon them, and the lich now diverted his attention and efforts upon the intruders. As Sky deciphered the central crypt and uncovered the aspect of Vorakios contained within it, the lich rose more living graves and spectres to assault the party. Now the party must uncover how to exit this part of the planar dungeon, all while dealing with Vorakios' attacks.*
