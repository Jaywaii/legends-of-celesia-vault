# KM:R 327

# Previously

Still within the Thunderhoof dreamscape, the Company pushed through the undead forces only face another of Vorakios' theats. A circle of witchfires cast a pall of despair upon the allied forces, weakening their hold on the passage they cleared for the Thunderhoof champion, allowing zombie hordes to get in your way. Among those defenders is the Champion's lover, a human priest, who is at risk of being overwhelmed by the horde. The Champion immediately went to her lover's side. Meanwhile, blighted fey known as nuckelavees trample the battleground with their vile powers.
