# KM:R 12

After spending some time with Ayaki and some self-reflection, Cethin finally came to accept that he was her long lost brother. However, the result of this realization was that he came to resent the Nine-Tail Coven for taking him from his family. The revelation that he was nobility changed nothing of his whimsical nature, and the kitsune decided then and there to abandon his coven training and embrace the life he was meant to have.

He released Tuck Tootle, his fairy familiar, from his ring. Tuck lamented that they wouldn't make fun of anymore pompous folk, especially that stuck up Davona. However, he enjoyed his time, and wished Cethin luck, and to keep poking holes in inflated egos.

Now that he was ready for a fresh start, Cethin conferred with new sister what to do next. Ayaki suggested that he studied under an old retainer of their family, a skriven master who accompanied her from Xifangsaifu. The former princess thought that Cethin's natural skills and trickster personality would lend naturally to the master's training. Under the tutelage of the elderly Master Hahen, Cethin began his training to become a ninja of Iku.

Joram and several faithful laborers and guards traveled to the fallen temple of Deianeira. Davona opted to escort them, helping them fend off several wild beasts and giant insects along the way. They also suffered many pranks from fairies. Joram found them enjoyable, but knew how to appease them. They had prepared many appropriate gifts for them before they left Onestrio's, so they quickly appeased them. Davona opted to not provide any gifts, but weathered the pranks in good nature.

Argus, Ayaki, Davona, and Rashoi eventually departed Onestrio's to continue exploring. They followed along the edge of the Old Arborfane forest. Along the way, they saw many of things.

Ayaki thought she saw a deer with a human torso and antlers on its head spying on them from within the forest. Rashoi thought she saw little naked children covered in vegetation running through the bushes. She could hear eery giggling coming from them.

The closer the Evergreen Company got to the Old Arborfance, the more disturbing their dreams became. Old women cloaked in wind and lightning battling three-headed dragons. The green encroaching upon them oppressively. Three hearts beating.

The finally found Ravenna's camp. It was empty, and it looked ransacked. They noticed that several bags were dragged away, leaving a trail of tiny, gnarly feet imprints. As they started taking stock of what was left, Argus saw a group of bandits creeping up behind them.

The Evergreen Company battled and defeated them. Five out of the six died. One tried to run, but Davona and Rashoi chased him down by horse. He tried to hide, but Rashoi's keen nose sniffed him out, and they forced him to surrender.
