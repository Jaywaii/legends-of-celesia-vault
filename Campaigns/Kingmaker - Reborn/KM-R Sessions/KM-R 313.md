# KM:R 313

## Ankerata

Sky and Orok arrived at Ankerata around midnight, but Sky had to depart, leaving Orok on his own to perform his sabotage. He decided on freeing the slaves the mercenaries were guarding.

## Viriquia

At midnight, Sky received a *sending* from Iolanthe informing him that the Thunderhoof scouts report that a new tendril of Vorakios' deathmist had emerged, quickly traveling south through the Southern Greyfang Pass. This was towards the nearest city of the Holy Radimus Empire, Viriquia. Orok and Sky agreed that Sky should check it out while Orok remained in Ankerata to free as many slaves as he could.

After teleporting to the Thunderhoof refuge and consulting with Iolanthe and the centaurs there, Sky flew towards Viriquia. He found the city already in the grip of the deathmist. However, unlike Wolfshold, the city was not deathly silent. Instead, even in the air, Sky could hear the cries of the despaired, the clash of battles, and divine chanting. Although the mist surrounded the city and permeated into its streets, Sky could see flashes of sunlight every here and there. There was also a single thin pillar of constant sunlight somewhere in the middle of the city that pierced the dark fog. on his way down, he witnessed Viriquian citizens being consumed by the fog or by the zombies, even as the city guard fought back.

Using the gap in the deathmist caused by that pillar of sunlight, Sky flew down into the city. The light emanated from a great cathedral, and many priests and templars were on the streets around it, defending it and shielding citizens into the building. Sky saw a priest channel energy to dissipate the fog in his range; the holy symbol of St. Radimus the priest wielded seemed to glow with uncharacteristic power.

Sky made his presence known, and using the diplomatic skills he studied and dissected by those far more charismatic than him, succeeded in assuaging the local authorities of his sincere desire to help. He demonstrated his ability to open a Door to Promethea to allow Viriquians escape from the city. Salden was contacted to help usher them on the other side. The Radimite priest then escorted Sky to the Marquis' Castle which was under siege by zombies, defended by their own cadre of priests and its guard.

# Previously

The Company waited in Valenlet for Vorakios to mind battle Rishoi. However, an hour passed with no attack occurring. Even the mist around the shielded town failed to disgorge zombies as before, although the for did appear to be more active. The zombies were still in there, though, as Argus felt when he flew into the mist to scout it out. Now concerned by this change in pattern, most of the team used Doors to go defend Arbor's Rest. Meanwhile, Sky and Orok teleported to Ankerata to begin sabotaging their efforts. They also issued a *sending* to Iolanthe to see if any changes happened near the Thunderhoof refuge.

When the team door'ed into Arbor's Rest, they were met with the sounds of gunfire, screams, and unearthly shrieks. The Steel Peacock mercenaries and the 2nd Rochefort Fusiliers were in the middle of fighting a couple of hundred banshees. The defenders suffered many casualties, but the timely arrival of the Evergreen Company turned the tide, and ended the attack. The 2nd Fusiliers did break from the fight, but Colonel Amoullet will be able to rally them back now that the battle is over. The Company then rushed down the Arborfane's Gap, and began battling the zombie horde. However, the zombies' poison was now in full effect, and Maalek and Asako had to extract themselves from the battle or else be killed. By 2am, nearly half of the zombies have been destroyed.
