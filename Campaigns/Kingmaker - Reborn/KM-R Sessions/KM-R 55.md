# KM:R 55

- Ayaki, Davona, Jupus, Rishoi, along with Gnawfang and Cortez, visit the Carnival of Tears, learning of their new master, Namdrin Quinn. They learn that Vincenzio retired.
- Rishoi confronts the Menagerie of Freaks, but finds no suspicious behaviour.
- At the Titan's Wheel, Davona tries out the game, but only produces one ticket.
- At the Ale Tent, a drunk construction worker confronts the party, thinking they are regular adventurers. He throws a punch at Davona, which lands harmlessly on her cheek, but she lets him go after Ayaki convinces him to walk it off. Cortez takes some notes about the man.
- At the Blind Peep Show, Rishoi investigates it, and after exiting, overhears a girl being slapped around. She confronts Kabran, who attempts to attack her. Rishoi knocks him out in one blow, and Cortez arrests the man and escorts the girl (Rialla) back to town.
- They visit the Modern Engineering and Jupus was glad to see that they still had the velvet security rope. He also noticed that the machines have been upgraded to be more efficient.
- They visit the Pie eating contest, when Rishoi playfully starts a pie fight. Jupus and Gnawfang quietly slip into the background.
- They meet with Namdrin Quinn who is busy practicing for his show in the evening. He says that they could do whatever they wanted with Kabran. They noticed that his act is flawless, but lacks passion. He says he wanted some happy memories of Promethea before leaving.
- The Company hears a scream in the maze. They rush there to find the minotaur loose, having just killed 2 people. They defeat him without killing him, and bring him back to his cage. They tie him up and repair the lock. It is at this moment that Cortez runs up to the Company with the faerie dragon Percilash and the grig Tyg-twitter-twig hanging on his shoulders.

- During the night before, you all receive the same dream. First, you felt a throbbing of power around your finger, the one that bore the Ring of Elyssria. There was a sensation of cold. A dark figure, bearing a pair of great horns of ice, held a woman, encased in ice in one hand, and a heart consumed with shadow in the icy grip of the other. Behind the figure was dancing, just dancing under the moonlight.
- It is now Winterbirth, the first day of winter, and a light snowfall already descends at the strt of the morning. Many children are delighted at the beautiful sight, and a thin layer of snow already covers the ground.
- The carnival amazingly set itself up overnight, now bearing a winter theme. Carnival criers dressed in garish winter themed costumes shout to all "Come one, come all! Now under new management, the Carnival of Tears features never before seen spectacles and activities, alongside old favorites! Come and celebrate this year's Winter's birth with a day spent at the Carnival of Tears!"
