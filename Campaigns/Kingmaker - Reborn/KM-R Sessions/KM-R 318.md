# KM:R 318

# Previously

The Praterian expedition succeeded in escorting the Viriquian laborers to the besieged supply depot. During this time, a call for help came from a neighboring street. An aid station was also under sieged, and the healers needed help to evacuate the patients too maimed to move on their own. The Praterians split up, but as soon as they did, dark wraiths flew out of the fog. They then exploded, filling the previously cleared and sanctified streets with more fog. Undead cyclops warriors soon emerged to join the zombies.
