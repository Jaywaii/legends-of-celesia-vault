# KM:R 303

# Previously

While the defender's of Arbor's Rest fought the horde of zombies within the Old Arborfane gap, the Evergreen Company were busy trying to find the fragments of the Cord of the Ancients. While Aedre and Orok used their mythic power to help open Doors to ferry Rochefort's forces into besieged Valenlent, Arina attempted to arrange a meeting with Enzio Montari who the Company believed now had possession of one of the fragments. Without making any signficantly direct incentive to meet right away, Arina was given the run-around by Montari's staff, so she herself snuck into the Montari Palace in Valdargent to find and steal the fragment of the Cord of the Ancients that was looted from the centaurs centuries ago. Her skills, mythic power and cloak allowed her to easily enter in and out without triggering any mundane or magical alarms, even ones as the highest quality that Montari's vast fortunes could afford.

Meanwhile, Sky flew from the Thunderhoof's hidden refuge to try to find the Cord fragment lost in the Presires Plains, which was now occupied by the Carpathosi colony of Anatolkerat. Sky snuck into Anatolkerat's capital of Ankereta to find the fragment, but was unable to find it before being forced to return to the refuge before his power to maintain his mythic invisibility wore out. Also flying out of the Thunderhoof refuge was Argus. The Praterian general scouted the edges of the cursed fog, mapping its extent from the Greyfang Peaks, revealing that it blankets much of the northern portion of the Thalamech Uplands.

At 6 pm, when night falls, a hundred banshees and and a hundred grave sprites descend upon the *Horizon* and its crew of mages. Worse, Vorakios once more assaulted Rishoi via a psychic assault.
