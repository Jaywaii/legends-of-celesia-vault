# Previously

Previously on Kingmaker: with the assassination attempt on Elvia Sovrani foiled, the Evergreen Company was able to enjoy the rest of the Viridian Games without further incident, even patching up some of their relationship with Cyrano Varano. However, during the closing ceremony, the Sootscale kobolds athletes revealed themselves to trolls in magical guise. Led by Hrondgren, King of Klorgfang, the troll king declared the lands of the Evergreen Plains to be his, and that all Praterians must leave. Effectively declaring war on Prateria by stating his forces have already crossed the Greenbelt, heading towards Praterian lands. The trolls then teleported away, leaving the Praterians and Lurindor dignitaries worried and concerned. Talking with King Sootscale, the kobold leader admitted he was coerced by Hrondgren, and that this way was the only way to get close to the Evergreen Company to warn them without crossing the troll. The kobold asks for Prateria help and pledges to aid them in the fight. The Company also decide to go ahead with the final banquet to demonstrate their resolve.
