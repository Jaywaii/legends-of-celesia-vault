# KM:R 295

# Previously

The Steamflogger investigators arrived at Onestrio's for the meeting between Teffania Revedal and the man who convinced her to foul up Steamflogger's supplies, a merchant named Caisin Berengar. The team allowed Teffania to meet with Caisin at the Gutted Boar Inn & Tavern. Larry went in in disguised as a beggar, while Accursia and Gabriel positioned themselves outside. However, Caisin has his bodyguard take Teffania up to his room. Larry, and Accursia's cat Sgt. Whiskers, followed them in the inside. Thanks to her link with Whiskers, Accursia knew about their movement, and she and Gabriel repositioned themselves underneath the window to Caisin's room. Accursia climbed up to the window, but she was noticed by Caisin's secretary, who alerted the others before breaking the window and stabbing the investigator. Larry broke into the room thanks to his wolf Milo to see Caisin stab Teffania for the betrayal. The investigators are now in the middle of fighting Caisin and his assistant and bodyguard.
