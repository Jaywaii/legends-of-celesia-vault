# KM:R 25
[[KM-R 24|← Previous: KM:R 24]] · [[KM-R 26|Next: KM:R 26 →]]

- Away team heads back to the Temple of the Elk to seek Joram's aid in healing Arina. On the way, Arina was mostly unconscious, her dreams plagued with nightmares and whispers in an alien tongue.
- At the temple, Joram healed Arina's physical wounds, but would require a day to prayer by him and all the acolytes to exorcise the alien influence.
- Arina recovered (mostly), and everyone went back to Onestrio. There, they disarmed all the traps in the vardo, cleaned it out, and prepped it for their own use.
- Rishoi had departed on a soul searching journey.
- Team leveled up.
- Away team consisting of Arina, Ayaki, Davona and Argus depart with vardo, and explore areas up to Hushglow Tower.
- At the tower, they feel fear, and Davona's will is broken and becomes shaken. The horses become shaken as well, but the party manages to handle them.
- They cut through many thorny nettles to the tower. Inside is a circular clab of stone. Arina examined it, unconsciously whispering the blasphemous prayer inscribed on it. Nothing happens, but Ayaki was the only one to hear it.
- Argus broke the slab in two, which releases musty, rotting air. A will-o'-wisp appears and attacks the party. It is killed by Argus and Arina.
- Underneath the slab was a staircase descending downward. The party went down for 10 minutes, but no end in sight. Arina and Davona return upward to watch the horses, while Ayaki and Argus continue to descend.
- Ayaki and Argus arrive in an alien place. Going through a hallway opens up to a vast cavern that Arina and Argus could not see the end. They turn away, but not before hearing their names being groaned out from the nothingness.
- Ayaki and Argus rejoin Arina and Davona, and they decide to cover up the hole. But before they could do so, another will-o'-wisp appeared to attack.
