# KM:R 120

# Previously

Previously on Kingmaker: The Sootscale kobolds willingly, but with sadness, agree to become part of Prateria under the Evergreen Company's rule. Arina led her Cohors Consul Magica to scout the Silent Bastion. After surveying the Klorgfang army there, they ambushed and defeated another squad of frest drakes, taking them prisoner. Their battle caught the attention of the harpy scouts in the area, which then gave chase. The Consul Magica had to ambush and defeat them as well. However, the unit was getting fatigued at this point, having been active for over twenty hours straight and receiving wounds from the battle. Worse, another company of harpies was right on their heels. To make matters even more dire, while this was going one, Arzhulig's Horde, one of the armies attacked by Consul Magica, arrived at the Sootscale's Silvermine, ready to attack it. Thabkfully, the other members of the Evergreen Conpany were still present in the settlement after the annexation ceremony and overseeing the new unit of Sootscale outriders.

*It has been almost a whole week since the ceremony of the Sootscales' incorporation into Prateria. You had remained in their settlement of Silvermine to oversee the transition of rule, as well as inspecting the newly trained reinforced platoon of Sootscale dino-riders. Your planning and discussions with the kobolds are interrupted by sudden shouts of alarms. "It's an army! An army approaches!" From atop of the village's wooden walls, down the slope of the hill the village rests upon, you can see the ragged banners of Klorgfang, a blood red maw and claw mark upon black cloth. They flutter above over nine hundred monstrous soldiers, a mix of orc, harpies, gnolls, goblins, hobgoblins, kobolds, led by a unit of hill giants.*

*The village of Silvermine lies atop the largest hill in the area, which also contains the main deposit of silver. The village is surrounded by a wall of wood logs. There is an upward slope that the enemy army must climb up to reach the walls. They are arrayed to face their center towards the main gate to the village.*
