# KM:R 301

# Previously

The Evergreen Company successfully protected Rishoi from the mental attacks by the mysterious Vorakios. After blasting the lich from the mindscape, the mist around Valenlet ceased spweing out zombies, allowing the 2nd Fusiliers, Argus, Davona, and Orok to wipe out the remaining zombies in the settlement. With only a few hours before another zombie horde reaches Arbor's Rest, the Company coordinated with House Châteaufoy to use Promethea and its Doorways to Destiny to transfer fresh Rochefort troops in and out of Valenlet while the 2nd returns to their original mission of defending Arbor's Rest. During this time, Adriel's team had arrived at Arbor's Rest to raise the Pall of Shining Life. Along with them were two teams of mages from Arina's retinue, the 10-troop elite scout team, and a new unit of 100 novice mages on board of the magical airship. With all the forces in place, Arbor's Rest was now ready to face the thousands of zombies lurching towards it.
