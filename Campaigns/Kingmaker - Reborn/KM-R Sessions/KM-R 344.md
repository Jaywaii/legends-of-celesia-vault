# KM:R 344

# Previously

*The Evergreen Company's attempt to force open the exit portal of the Cemetery of a Thousand Corpses was only partially successful. The portal had opened, but only enough to allow one person to pass through at a time. At the same time, the attempt triggered an effigy of Vorakios to awaken and attack them. After an initial blast of necrotic energy, Dragormir crushed the effigy into dust. The Company quickly reopened the exit portal properly now, and passed through to the Forge of Broken Bones.*

*Waiting to greet them was the Forgemaster, a brontes cyclops. Slamming his hammer on an anvil, he summoned to life two golem made of adamantine weapons and armour. Dragormir and Jupus leapt upon him, but the Forgemaster used his mythical insight to break free and counterattacked. He also slammed his hammer on the anvil once again and summoned two elementals of fire and burning steel from the main furnace. The rest of the Company poured through the portal to join the fight.*
