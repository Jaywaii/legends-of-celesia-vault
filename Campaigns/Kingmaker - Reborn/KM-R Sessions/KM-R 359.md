# KM:R 359

# Previously

*The Evergreen Company worked through the onslaught of cursed hymns and ghostly shadows to bring down the Vicar of Gloom. The team piled onto it, and with a series of strikes, Jupus was able to finish it off. This sent Vorakios into a rage, and the lich summoned another wave of shadows to descend upon the Company. However, Bjorn's summoned angels warded the adventurers from the shadow's deathly touch, rendering them impotent. Lonán recasted the **Slip Into Oblivion** enchantment to erase the Company from Vorakios' mind, with Gnawfang volunteering to use her life force to fuel the spell. While the shadows floated impotently above them, the Company examined the exit portal, and found that the key to forcefully opening it was to allow the Deathless Shroud's whispers into their minds. Sky volunteered to opened the door, and in doing so, opened his mind of the Deathless Shroud's influence. However, instead of being disturbed by what the God of Undeath whispered into his mind, Sky was more distracted in trying to decipher the dark lore. Regardless, Sky opened the portal to the Fairy Garden, where the Company would escape to Lonán and Dragormir's refuge there so they could rest.*
