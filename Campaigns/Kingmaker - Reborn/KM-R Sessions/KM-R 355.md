# KM:R 355

# Previously

*After spending almost half and hour preparing, Adriel volunteered to open the exit of the Well of Shackled Souls. Although the door opened, Adriel felt mycellium creep up the veins of her arm and up into her brain. It felt like her mind was on fire while her body shivered violently. She had become afflicted with the full affects of the Funglebloom Fever. Nonethless,t he Evergreen Company passed through the portal.*

*Horrifically, the Company found themselves floating weightlessly in a starlit void. They could not breath, they felt their internal liquids boild and swell, and radiation washed upon them. As they grappled with being in the atmosphereless void, a draconic roar echoed in their minds. "New guests! The master has sent me new guests to play with!" a manic voice passed through their thoughts.*

*Quickly, Sky managed to cast* life bubble *upon his companions, protecting them from the hazards of the void and allowing them to breathe. Maalek conjured a chain from his ring that others could grab on. Those that had magical flight were able to move. Ahead of them were four islands of ruined floors. The largest of them hosted an enormous orrery that loked broken, while the three smaller carried damaged furniture and arcane tools. Sky was the frist to reach the larger island, and discovered that he became affected by gravity upon touching it. Davona joined him, but also discovered that she lost gravity as soon as she left five feet off the floor. That was when the origin of the mysterious voice in their heads appeared.*

*Before Davona appeared a gargantuan dragon with purple and black scales that scintillated with stars. A void dragon. With its appearance came an overwhelming alien presence that threatened to make them sick with vertigo, although Argus' own indomitable presence inspired those close to him to overcome it. The dragon then dispelled the spell immunity enchantment upon most of the group, and then tried to bite into Davona. Davona mirror dodged the attack, but then saw the dragon warp space to appear next to her again. Adriel swooped in and used her orbiting weapon to tear into the dragon. Argus followed, dragging the others with him with Maalek's chain. He then slammed all his weapons into the dragon as well, and sealed its ability to teleport thanks to the phase-lock enchantment on one of his hammers. The dragon flew upward, and cast a* prismatic spray *down upon the Company. Most of the team were relatively unaffected, but Maalek turned to stone.*
