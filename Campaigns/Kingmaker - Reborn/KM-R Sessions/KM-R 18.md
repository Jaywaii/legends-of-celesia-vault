# KM:R 18

After defeating the mites and claiming their booty, Arina, Cethin, and Argus accompanied Mikmek to the Great Cypress whiel Ayaki, Jupus, and Rishoi returned to the Trade Post with their loot. Mikmek wanted to retrieve a status of Sharptooth stolen by the mites. He explained that the tribe's new shaman ordered the warriors to retrieve the status or else the fury of Old Sharptooth will fall upon them. It was during this mission that Mikmek was captured by the mites. The shaman Tartuk was a strange kobold, foreign to their tribe, but was able to call upon the spirit of Old Sharptooth and subdue it. The Company arrived at the Great Cypress tree and explored the mite caverns underneath it, killing off remaining centipedes and retrieving the status.

They brought the status back to the Sootscale caverns, where they spoke with Chief Sootscale. They also met with another kobold warrior Napnik and a female kobold apprentice shaman by thename of Samska. They confronted Tartuk and fought him, forcing him to flee.
