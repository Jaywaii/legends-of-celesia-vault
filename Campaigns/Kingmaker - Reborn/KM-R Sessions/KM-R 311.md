# KM:R 311

# Previously

Before Jupus, Sky, Davona, and Orok could lay claim on their prize, the Ankeratan councilor Thessamene teleported in with her underlings in the hallway leading to the secret undercroft. Davona and Orok closed the doors while Sky examined the magical wards the slain cyclopean mages were crafting. From outside the room, Thessamene activated the two remaining golems in the chamber while she and her underlings magical forced open the doors with magic. Spells and strikes were exchanged, with the Company gaining the upperhand, with Davona coming within killing reach of the councilor. However, the crafty cyclops had contingencies planned, and she and her surviving minions magically escaped. The Company cleaned up the remaining threats, disabled the magical wards around the half-built container, and retrieved the fragment of the Cord of the Ancients. They then used a Doorway of Destiny to escape back to Promethea with their prize. Now, only one fragment remained.
