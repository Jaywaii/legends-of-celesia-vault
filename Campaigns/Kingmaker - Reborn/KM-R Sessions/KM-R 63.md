# KM:R 63
[[KM-R 62|← Previous: KM:R 62]] · [[KM-R 64|Next: KM:R 64 →]]

---

- The Evergreen Company continue to administer their nation. In Rochefort Sky re-encounters his childhood friend Asako Hino working as an archivist for the Argent Rose.
- In the month on Minyan 109, it was discovered that gremlins had ruined many of the nation's food stores. Through quick action, the Company managed to secure the remaining food supplies and drive off the gremplins by protecting them with circles of shaved iron filings mixed with a smattering of cold iron filings.
- When the month Kaïshan of the new year came around, Mikmek of the Sootscale kobolds appeared before the Evergreen Company. He reported that hunters and trappers that traded with the kobold tribes were being attacked. The kobolds sent patrols with the hunters, and nearly all were killed. Those that survived reported that the monsters were comprised of goatmen and giant caterpillars. They were seen heading northeast, and will reach Prateria borders any day now.
