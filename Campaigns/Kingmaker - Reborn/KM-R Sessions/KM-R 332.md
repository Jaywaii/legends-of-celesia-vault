- Orok scouts path to Argentia's lair.

# Previously

Within the Thunderhoof dream realm, the Company confronted the echo of Sterlingastrax, a great wyrm silver dragon who originally took Pyrouranos away from the warring centaur tribes to end their inter-tribal massacres over the bow. The Company proved themselves worthy of Pyrouranos in Sterlingastrax's eyes by defending themselves against his fearsome attacks, preventing the centaur leaders from being hurt, and risking their lives to save one another. After judging them worthy, Sterlingastrax transformed into a younger, female silver dragon who beseeched the Company to find her lair and avenge her, and she then transformed into a star of silver light that shot across the horizon. The Company's vision was then pulled along with the shooting star, watching it cross over a vast mountain range, before landing amidst a distinct formation of mountains.

The Company then awoke from the dream journey. Andrastreia, chieftain of the Silvermane tribe, greeted them, letting them know only hours have passed in their slumber. During that time, an army of undead and blighted fey reached the refuge. With the aid of the Company's cohorts and followers, the Thunderhoof centaurs have been fighting them off with great success, but it seems their numbers are unending. Iolanthe remains in deep mediation to maintain the Ward of the Ancients against Vorakios' power. After explaining their vision quest to Andrastreia, the centaur matriarch believes they speak of Argentia, a silver dragon who watched over the Thalamech Uplands. She had not been seen in over a decade, but that was normal as the dragon preferred to remain unseen. However, the current crisis should have definitely warranted her presence, especially as she and the Silvermane tribe had a common ancestral silver dragon. Andrastreia identified the specific mountain formation the Company saw in their vision, and provided its location on a map, a place within the Jade Horn Peaks southwest of the refuge.
