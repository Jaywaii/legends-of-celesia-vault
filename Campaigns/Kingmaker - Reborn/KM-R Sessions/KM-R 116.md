# Previously

Previously on Kingmaker: Prateria expands and establishes the settlement of New Rughir, all in preparation for battle against the trolls. Arina has raised a small army of wizards from her followers, while Davona orders 500 of her followers to train for combat. Tabrir embarks on a tour of Lurindor to push for more aid, leaving Rialla in charge of diplomatic affairs. Louis assumes the position of Pontifex and Cortez takes Frumentarius. In his new role, Cortez embarks on an espionage mission in Klorgfang territory, and succeeds in discovering the current location of all of Klorgfang's 14 armies, as well as details on the troll nation's leadership.
