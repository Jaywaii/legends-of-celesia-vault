# KM:R 319

Upon their return to the palace, the party is welcomed back by Bishop Basilmon, who thanks them for rescuing the wounded soldiers.

Marquis Xemonos acknowledge the party's success, informing them that those supplies proved vital in holding the line against the undead. However, he criticizes the party for letting so many brave souls be damned despite the so-called prowess of the party.

Xemonos tells the party they are free to recuperate. The lines have stabilized, but there is still about two hours to go before Sky's vaunted defense against the fog becomes available. He will summon the party back if there is more need of their services.

A little after 6 pm, Orok informs the consumption of Ankerata by the deathmist. If he is able to return to Promethea, he becomes available to join the expedition.

As time draws closer to 8 am, the party receives updates from the main Company that they are nearly done with the zombie horde. However, Xemonos urgently summons the party to him. He tells them that a new threat has appeared at the castle gates. A giant undead abomination has emerged from the fog, stomping through the defence line, and will soon reach the gates. Then, another soldier rushes in, alerting the Bishop that an enormous whirlwind of wraiths is storming towards the Cathedral, ripping through the holy light shielding the area around it. They cannot afford to lose either of them.

# Previously

The Praterian Viriquian team successfully helped the Viriquian laborers escape back to the defence line safely, as well as evacuate the wounded from the besieged aid station. This came at the cost of many Viriquian guards' lives, though the team saved many as well. The aid station almost failed as guards slain by wriaths rose as wraiths themselves, and they passed through the aid station's walls. Fortunately, Sky had just enough mythic power to open a Door of Destiny back to Promethea within the aid station, and Salden charged through to hold off the undead and allow everyone else to evacuate.
