# KM:R 34
[[KM-R 33|← Previous: KM:R 33]] · [[KM-R 35|Next: KM:R 35 →]]

## Summary

- Evergreen Company brings back the bandit prisoners, who have all sworn to work under them and be rehabilitated into honest working people.
- On the first night after defeating the Stag Lord, each of the Evergreen Company's members receive the same dream:

*You find yourself in an open, grassy field colored by many exotic flowers. They smell of fresh sweetness that you can't quite place. A faint mist fills the air, dispersing hazy sunlight throughout the sky, making it seem orange-pink. As you turn around to take in the sight, a woman of otherwordly beauty suddenly comes into view. She was a tall, elf-like woman over six feet tall, slender and graceful, clad in semi-transparent pink lacy fabrics. Her skin is alabaster white with a tinge of blue, and her green hair cascades down her body all the way to her feet, decorated with pink petals. Her captivating eyes are of a golden hue. You feel a powerful, yet undeniable attractive, presence emanating from her. She sitting in the grass, delicately caressing a tall rose. Finally, she turns to you, as if seeing you for the first time.*

*"Oh, hello," the woman replies in a sing-song voice. "I am Elyssria. I know who you are."*

*She tilts her head, and looks at you curiously. "You are touched by ancient power. I wonder... what do you wish to do with it?"*
- Each of the Company responded thusly in their own dreams:
	- Ayaki: To reclaim her homeland from the imposter that exiled her.
	- Sky: To take down the god-king of Ashaki and avenge his parents' execution at his command.
	- Rishoi: Initially hostile to Elyssria as she is fey, she came to believe that she can help her. Rishoi'll bring stability to this land if Elyssria helps her find her mother.
	- Arina: To find Marcelette and uncover her fate.
	- Jupus: To right the wrongs against his family.
	- Argus: To rebuild his mercenary company.
	- Cethin: Recounting his life, and now that his sister opened his eyes, he wants to get back the life he missed out on.

*"You helped defeat the one you called 'Stag Lord'. He had promised me to unify the land you call the Viridian Marches. For that, I bequeathed him my boon." She plays with a lock of her green hair, twirling it around a slender finger.*

*Elyssria looks down, a somber look on her face. "Sadly, he could not do so. That poor boy, he held such promise despite the hurt he felt and the way he was treated. I had hoped he would have overcome his demons with my help, but alas, it was not to be."*

*"Perhaps... would you like to take up this task?"*

*"You may call upon my power to aid, but be warned, doing so would mean incurring a favor to me. By ancient laws of Faerie, failure to fulfill that favor would bring misfortune upon you."*

*"The Viridian Marches touch so closely to the realm of the Fey. I wish to nurture her to her full potential, a haven for fairies and all others who wish to live in beauty and bliss. I am a Queen in search of a worthy cause to support, and I believe I can help you fulfill yours."*

*"You seek to return the Heart of of the Forest. It is good of you to do so. However, re-planting the heart will be no sure thing. Its removal will have no doubt left a scar. Fortunately, I know of a ritual that will ensure the Heart's safe replanting. I would teach this to you, for I too wish for the Arborfane to be healed."*
- The Evergreen Company learn the Ritual of the Heart's Repose. The ritual does exactly what Elyssria's promises: the Heart is embraced into the earth, and reinvigorates the forest.
- Ayaki also tells Elyssria of their desire to revive the Great Cypress, as does Sky in his own dream.

*"Truly, you may be the caretakers this dear land needs. I know how difficult it will be. The Heart would certainly heal the tree, but it would deprive the Arborfane of her life. You may also call upon the aid of a dryad to bond with the tree. There is a powerful one residing in what you mortals call the Draketooth Forest. However, you and your companions have been touched by ancient power. With the right ritual, you may use your own power instead."*

*"If you agree, then let us pledge ourselves to each." The beautiful fairy plucked a locket of green hair from her head that came loose effortlessly. She gently takes your hand into hers. The locket of hair wraps around your finger on its own, forming a ring.*
- Elyssria gently cups their chin, and lays a light kiss upon their lips, swelling them with feelings of exuberance. Everything turns bright, and each of them awakes as dawn breaks ove the horizon. Still feeling euphoric, each saw that a ring of green hair rests on their finger. Inside their mind burned the knowledge to perform the Ritual of the Heart's Repose.
- The Company first begins making their way to see the Old Beldame. After politely greeting her, they inform her that they retrieved the Heart of the Forest and that the Stag Lord is dead. The old witch is astounded and entranced by the power. She is equally relieved that the Old Arborfane will still yet live. They tell her about the dream they each had with Elyssria, and explained the ritual the fairy queen gave them to heal the scar left in the forest by the taking od the Heart. The Old Beldame analyzes the ritual and agrees that this would heal the Heart and the forest. She also tells them that she had heard the name Elyssria whispered throughout the region over the past several years, and that they should be wary of unforeseen consequences with dealing with the fey.
- Moving onto the next topic, the Old Beldame informs them of a rough area she believes where the Heart needs to be replanted. She also teaches them the Oldwood Ritual to ease their entry into the Old Arborfane. She also informs them of some of Old Arborfane's traits, such as rusting normal metals, weakening spells by sucking some of its magic.
- Upon returning to Promethea, the Evergreen Company threw a festival for the people to celebrate their victory over the Stag Lord. The kobold refugees participated as well. After the party, the kobilds returned to the silver mine now that the bandits are no longer threatening them.
- During council, Bekka informed the Company of the tragic news that Mick's scout team was lost in the line of duty while exploring the Mud Bowl. Only the team's runner Colton and one survivor, Guido, remain alive after the team was ambushed by the large plant monsters.
- The Evergreen Company began making their way to Rochefort to heal their severe injuries. On their way, they stopped by Onestrio's Trade Post. There, they received some tragic news:

*The weather was rainy, as it had been the past few days since he left Lurindor. He was avoiding the roads, as he was accustomed to after being on the run for several months. The minions of the God King were closing in on him, and he knew that he could not outrun them much longer He wore his cloak tightly around him, steeling his resolve to survive. If only he could outpace them for a few more days, he might be free…*

*It was with great sadness that Darawesh left Sky and Davona in Lurindor all those months ago, but he knew he would only slow them down. He had already lost his dear Adriel because he was incapable of fighting; he would not lose his children. He wandered from village to village, always keeping to the edges of town, only staying long enough to grab a bite of food from the local tavern and sometimes hear the latest goings on. Every so often he would hear tell of the exploits of the Evergreen Party, of his daughter Davona, and adopted son Sky, and he couldn’t be more proud. They had become fine adults, and he knew they would be able to protect themselves.*

*But last week he heard the most shocking news of all, the Evergreen Party had established a nation! A free nation, and a capital named Promethia – one where he might be able to finally stop running. He wrote to a trusted friend, making arrangements to send his most precious belongings down to the new city, and set off on his journey. But now, hugging the contours of a large tree for shelter from the rain, he realized what an error that had been. The agents of the God King were always watching, and now they knew where he was headed.*

*He saw some lights in the distance, and began making his way towards them. He could just barely make out the sign when he felt a sharp pain in his side. He looked down to see an arrow poking out through his chest. The pain was excruciating, but he knew he could not fall here. He mustered up all his strength and ran towards the lights. He made it another hundred yards before an arrow pierced his thigh, but still he continued. He could feel that these arrows were poisoned, and that he probably would not last much longer. He was mere feet away now, and lay on his back looking up at the sign: Onestrio’s Trading Post.*

*Onestrio heard the commotion of battle and called the guards. They rushed to the gates and found the man lying face up in the mud, his assailants nowhere to be found. The rugged man was on death’s door, and they took him inside to treat him. As they called for the priestess, the man beckoned Onestrio in close. The blood oozed from his wounds as he hoarsely whispered, “Davona, Sky, I’m sorry”, and he was no more. As Onestrio was inspecting the body to find a clue as to who this mysterious man was, he found, clutched tightly in his right hand, a note addressed to Sky and Davona…*
- The bloodstained letter read as such:

*My precious Davona, my dearest Sky,*

*I hope this letter finds you well. I have had much time to think on how I should approach this subject, but if you’re reading this then it means I am no more. I have heard tales of your exploits in the Viridian Marches all the way in Lurindor, and I think you are ready to hear the truth about your mother, and how the God-King found us.*

*A year before you both set out on your journey, an old friend of mine, a wealthy merchant named Samir Baniti, happened to be in Lurindor and our paths crossed ways. As you know I had severed all ties with my past life when I took you in Sky, but I knew I could trust Samir. Samir has eyes and ears in many countries, which helps him to profit in his trade, but I was not ready for what I was about to hear. Davona, your mother is alive. She has been prisoner to the God-King since she sacrificed herself for us all those years ago. I did not believe it at first – I needed proof – I saw the bones, the ashes. Samir understood, and after many months I heard from him again - I received a crate – Adriel’s armor. Her armor was unique – Davona you might have been too young to remember – but it is beautiful. I kept the armor hidden in a secret room in my shop, and I was going to give it to you the night of your graduation, but it was not to be.*

*The best guess I have is that the armor was bait to lure me out of hiding. And it worked. A month later I was warned by some local merchants that some foreign men were asking about me. They didn’t tell them anything, but I knew we had been found out. I came to find you two immediately to get us out of the city. I couldn’t risk giving you the armor with the God-King’s men on our heels – you would be too recognizable. I hope you will forgive me.*

*If I may make one final request, please do not go after your mother just yet – you are not ready to face the might of an entire army. But when you do rescue her, please give her my deepest apologies for not realizing sooner, and my eternal love.*

*Your proud father,*

*Darawesh*
- Although deeply angered by these events, Sky and Davona decide to follow their father's last request, and not rush to rescue Davona's mother. First, they must inspect Darawesh's shop. They made arrangements with Onestrio to have Darawesh's body to build a grave at the Trade Post for their father to be buried in, which Onestrio agreed to.
- Also at Onestrio's, they handed over to Lorenzo Stasi's ring and informed him of his death. While Lorenzo was disappointed that Stasi would not face proper justice, at least he paid for his sins. He rewards the party with a pair of masterwork weapons of their choice.
- Upon arrival at Rochefort, Ayaki led a small group to speak with Lady Adeline Châteaufoy to inform her of the Stag Lord's defeat. Lady Châteaufoy congratulated them, and informed them that Drelevo and Duloup have pacified their respective region and already started their own colonies. In fact, both of them were already in Rochefort, and that this would be an excellent opportunity to celebrate everyone's success. Along with their congratulations came a reward of 5,000 gp for the elimination of the bandit threat. The Evergreen Company will also receive 30 BP from Rochefort, and the terms of the charter will be enacted: Prateria will pay 5% of its income to Lurindor, enter into an alliance with Lurindor, and both nations would establish embassies within each other's capital, all in exchange for complete autonomy rule of their colony.
- Meanwhile, as the temple of Kaï-den, Arina was getting her injuries tended to. A young page was brought in to assist, and much to Arina's shock, she recognized the halfling boy as Louis Montplaisir, her lover's younger brother! However, Louis claimed to not remember her, or indeed anything of his past except for his first name. The temple Sunmother explained that this young man was found wandering around aimlessly in an addled state. Arina explained who he was, and asked to take custody of him. The Sunmother said she would look into this case, and if everything was as Arina said, she did not see an issue with Arina watching over him. Indeed, she thought it might prove good for him to be away from this city.
- The Sunmother also told Arina that Louis showed signs of being touched by an otherworldly power, displaying magical abilities. Arina claimed that Louis showed no signs of such in the past when she lived with him.
- A few days later, the Evergreen Company attends the Grand Colonial Banquet at the Châteaufoy manor.
