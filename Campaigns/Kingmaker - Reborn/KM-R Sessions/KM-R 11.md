# KM:R 11

Armed bandits leaped out of their hiding amidst the foliage on the ground and the shacks built above in the tree branches. "You'll rue the day you crossed swords with Bekka's Blades!" shouted a half-orc woman, firing an arrow into Arina.

"I'm already regretting it!" Arina retorted as she jumped down from the empty shack she was investigating. She willed Mister Angelcakes to claim a nearby bandit, and sapped his blood. As Arina absorbed the blood mist, her bleeding ceased.

Knowing how vulnerable his position was, Sky began dashing back away from the bandits. However, he had no intention of staying out of the fight. "Here's a parting gift from I to you!" yelled Sky to the woman Bekka as he blasted her with an overcharged volley of *magic missiles*.

One of the bandits managed to sink an arrow into Argus, piercing his psionic armor. These bandits seemed to be better trained.  As much as Argus would have loved to return the favour, the mercenary had a job to do. He put himself in-between his allies and the bandits armed with melee weapons, and began exchanging blows with. He managed to smash the ribs of one bandit, but another sliced through his armour and into his chest. That was when Argus noticed the faint, mystical sheen on their weapons.

"Shit, they have enhanced weapons!" Argus warned the others, coughing up blood. Cethin rushed to his back, and applied healing magic, stemming the bleed.

Arina cartwheeled and executed *red zephyr's strike* against a bandit, and formed a line with Argus. She continued to will her old friend Angelcakes to wrack pain upon their foes and absorb their blood.

Spitting out blood, Bekka tried to ignore the pain. The battle was turning, no, had turned against them. These explorers were more powerful than expected. With the condition she was in, and that of her remaining people, there was only one choice if they wanted to survive. She threw down her daggers, and raised her arms, hands open.

"Wait, wait! We surrender! Everyone, stop fighting and throw down your weapons!" she cried out before the Company made another attack. Thankfully, Bekka's two remaining bandits themselves had apparently come to the same conclusion as her, and immediately followed suit.

The Company were wary, but they had no desire for continued bloodshed, and if the bandits making themselves even more weaker was a trick, it was a poor one. Argus kicked their weapons away for good measure. They forced the surrendering bandits together, and stripped them of any other weapons. The bandits offered no resistance. Once they were tied up, the Company questioned them.

Sky: "How many other bandits in this camp?" Bekka: "No, it was just us! We threw everything we had against you." They did have another part of their group who went patrlling north, and another group out in the plains. The Company remembered defeating them.

Argus:"You're not very good at this."
Bekka: "Well, we never really faced much opposition."
Arina: "You're looking at the new power in the Evergreen Plains." *cracks knuckles*.
Argus: "You were wise to surrender when you did."
Bekka: "We heard of newcomers throwing their weight around. I guess you were the guys who took care of Ravenna's group?"
Arina: "Who?"
Argus: "Play along."
Arina: "Yes!  our people took care of her. Ravenna was nothing."
Bekka: "Ok, well, I'll tell you this for free, if you're going to be the new power, you better watch out for the Stag Lord."
Arina: "Pfft, he's nothing."
Bekka: "I don't know about that, he gives me the willies. Heard he killed 20 men with his bare hands in one afternoon."
Sky: "Where did you see him?"
Bekka: "Uh, just for my peace of mind, what happens when I answer you guys?"
Argus: "You play fair, we'll treat you fair."
Arina: "We're just an expeditionary force. If we had our full force, we'll wipe the floor with you."

Cethin went to work on healing Argus. He then proceeded to heal the remaining bandits. Bekka was surprised. "All part of our rehabilitation program," Argus told her. Bekka found herself genuinely curious about this, and asked about it.

Arina explained about how they are teaching their current bandit prisoners how to live honestly by having them help with the chores, the farming and gardening, feeding the livestock, and so on. The Company provides them with a small, regular pay for their work, and while they keep them under guard, the Company keeps an eye on their progress. Argus mentions that if they prove themselves with good behaviour, the reformed bandits will be granted a plot of land in the new nation.

"'Honest living', eh?" Bekka seemed intrigued with the notion, as if encountering the concept for the first time in her life.

"It's far less dangerous than getting your ass kicked out in the wild," Argus remarked.

"Bekka huffed. "Well, for a long while, we were the ones kicking asses!"

"And look where it got you now."

Glancing at the headless corpse nearby, Bekka deflated. "Yeah..."

"Sorry about your friend," Argus offered.

Bekka shrugged. "Not really my friend, and we all knew the risks of our profession."

Nonetheless, the Company buried the bodies in a decent enough grave, after lifting anything of value from them first, of course. They also looted the rest of the camp. The bandits did not seem to mind, 'c'est la vie' as the halfling saying goes. They find crates full of different types of supplies, such as furs. One crat contained a large nubmer of bottles of green herbal liquor. Cethin claimed the liquor.

"I know a friend who'd want some," he said.

"Is that friend you?" Argus remarked with a raised eyebrow. "How about we share one with the prisoners, they seem to be having a shitty day."

The prisoners greatly appreciated the offer, and they gave a toast to their fallen companions. They and Company made camp in this fortified area, and Argus cooked up a field dinner for them.

"By the way, Bekka, this seems important," Arina asked, presenting an amulet of a stag's head shaped in silver. They had taken this off of the half-orc.

"Oh, that? That marks me as one of the Stag Lord's lieutenants. Have to show that when we enter camp." Bekka explained that the Stag Lord is headquartered at an old fort southeast of their current position, on the north shore Iridescent Lake, just west of the creepy Old Arborfane forest. The Company marked the location on their map.

Bekka went on to recount how the Evergreen Plains was broken up into different gang territories. When the Stag Lord appeared, he united them all under his rule, or wiped them out completely. One time, she saw him fire a powerful longbow and hit a target dead on over a thousand feet away, and he was stinking drunk too!

The next day, the away team escorted their new prisoners back to the trade post.

---

After the home defeated Ravenna's bandits, they managed to capture one of them alive. The reforming-bandit Mick identified the seniormost  of the newcomers as 'Taggart'. He was unconscious at the end of the battle, but the Evergreen Company stabilised him. After waking him up, they proceeded to interrogate him.

Davona was unsympathetic and harsh towards their prisoner. She had given up on reforming these criminals, and questioned the rest of the team why bother with him. Rishoi was interested in the questioning, but took a far more intimidating approach, shooting the floor in front of him. She casually mentioned breaking thumbs and other physical forms of persuasion.

Davona: "Why should we heal them? Seems a waste."
Ayaki: "Rishoi, please put your weapon away."
Rishoi: "Fine, whatever."

However, Ayaki took charge, and told the other two to stand down and give them some space, and treated Taggart more mercifully. Giving him a meal and some water, Ayaki managed to convince the bandit to tell them all that he knew, which was not much unfortunately.

Rishoi: "Now, tell us all that you know, or we won't give you the antidote."
Taggart: "Antidote?! What did you give me?"
Ayaki: "Rishoi, please cease your jesting, and leave us."
Rishoi: "Fine. I thought you kitsunes were more fun than that. At least the other one is."

The only thing they learned was that Ravenna had brought all of her remaining bandits with her. Only those three archers that managed to escape were all that remained to Ravenna's Blades. Taggart also pointed out their camp on the Company's map. It was at the crossing between the North and Middle Arborfane, along Thistledown River. Taggart knew that there were other bandit groups under the Stag Lord's command, but only Ravenna knew about them. However, Taggart did know that now with Ravenna's gone, the Stag Lord no longer had the northeast territory of the Evergreen Plains under his control.

Ayaki thanks Taggart for being so forth coming. Ayaki then went on about their plans here, how they hope to rehabilitate the bandits into honest workers. Jupus added the potential rewards for working with them. Taggart agreed to come on board, but mostly not having many good options open to him. Mick came by, and reassured him that things were not so bad here, despite being escorted by the Company's hired guards.

Davona: "Ugh, don't tell me we're letting them stay..."
Ayaki: "Of course, has that not been what we've been doing?"
Jupus: "If they work for us, they won't be bandits anymore."
Davona: *sighs* "Bah, semantics. Bunch of pansies..." *walks off to join Rishoi*

Despite her grousing, Davona kept a close on Taggart and the other prisoners for the next several days. To her surprise, they caused no trouble, and appeared to be adhering to the Company's conditions laid out to them. What she witnessed gave her food for thought.

Rashoi and Ayaki left to go back to Lurindor to seek healing for their mangled hands. Ayaki returned a few days later, but Rashoi stayed to retrain herself. Jupus had the prisoner begin building a reservoir for the trade post as part of the rehabilitation.

---

Bekka and another of the bandits awoke to find cobwebs covering their faces. An amused Cethin told them about the prankster fairies that followed them. More pranks followed on their way back to the trade post, earning the bandits a little more sympathy from the Company.
Upon arrival, they were greeted by Lorenzo. Bekka saw first hand that the Company was telling the truth. The bandits they took prisoners appeared to be treated well, and were hard at work constructing additions to the trade post, such as a hatchery and the aforementioned reservoir.

The Company informed Joram that they discovered the temple he sought. They recounted to the ecstatic priest their encounter their, about the wild bear that transformed into an old man upon death. Joram surmised that that must have been the original priest, and they might have released him from a curse after the Company cleaned the sacred pool. Joram is eager to travel there and clean it up. The company provided clear directions and markings on a map for him to reach there safely. Joram thanks the Company. He pledges his loyalty to the them, and offers his spellcasting for free to the party.

They discuss about what can be done to repair the temple. Joram suggests it could help with the rehabilitation of their prisoners; he has been overseeing their spiritual growth, and he is encouraged. There's not rush, however, and the Company thinks it might be good to recruit additional laborers and guards to help out. Joram says he should be able to call upon the faithful in Deianeira for this, and as it was a sacred duty, the Company will not have to provide a lot of funds.

Cethin transformed back into Rob the bartender persona.
