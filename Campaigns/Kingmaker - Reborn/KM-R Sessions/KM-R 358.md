# KM:R 358

# Previously

*Inside the Ossuary Choir, the Company faced off with a nightwalker known as the Vicar of Gloom. With Dragormir having pulled it to the ground, the Company inflicted blow after blow against it. But the Vicar proved slippery in more ways than one. With the aid of summoned shadows, the Vicar repeatedly slipped out of Dragormir's grasp, allowing it dispel more of Dragormir's magical enhancements. Thankfully, Adriel managed to counterspell one attempt that would have affected many of them. Sky attempted to block off the summoned shadows with a wall of force, but the shadows took advantage of their incorporeal form to simply slip into the ground. They reappeared underneath the prone Vicar, and were absorbed into it, healing its injuries. Bjorn summoned two planetars to help bolster the Comapny, warding them from magical death, and granting Argus' hammer the ability to destroy the undead. The Vicar then negated Adriel's daylight with a deeper darkness of its own, and summoned more shadows around it. The skulls of the cathedral's walls than sang towards another crescendo, blasting Argus, Dragormir, and Jupus with unholy sounds, with Jupus' mech falling over from the impact.*
