# KM:R 49
[[KM-R 48|← Previous: KM:R 48]] · [[KM-R 50|Next: KM:R 50 →]]

- Under Davona's supervision, the former bandit Bekka organized a scout team out of the former Stag bandits the Evergreen Company had captured and rehabilitated over the past year. Led by the former bandit called Mick, the team explored a decent amount of the Evergreen Plains that the Company had not reached yet. However, news reached Promethea of a disaster that fell upon Mick's Team. Colton, who served as the team's runner, was supposed to meet up with Mick's team at the Mud Bowl. When he got there, he found the survivors (Guido) barely alive. Guido told Colton about the giant plant monster and how it ambushed the team as they were exploring and killed them all. Only the two of them managed to return to Promethea to tell the Company of what happened at their pavilion at the base of the Old Cypress tree.
- The Evergreen Company decides to avenge Mick's team and end the threat at the Mud Bowl themselves. The team consists of Arina, Davona, Cortez, Argus, and Maalek. Colton and Guido come along to guide them to their fallen comrades. They take their wagon and take 2 days of travel the Mud Bowl. The area, on the southern tip of the Cypress Hills, is very geothermically active, resulting in numerous large pools of bubbling mud emerging from the ground. The smell of sulfur and other chemicals waft in the uncomfortably warm and humid air for miles away. Arina and Cortez actually become nauseated from it. The wagon also gets stuck in the mud several times, but Argus is able to lift it out thanks to his powerful astral suit and natural strength. Another feature of note in the area are the numerous mounds of fungi and strange mushrooms with black, rattling caps, some that grow as tall at ten feet high. Argus suggests they collect several specimen to see if they have future use.
- They arrive to the area where Mick's team was ambushed. Cortez's keen eyes points out several bodies half-buried in the mud. Argus and Maalek tie ropes around their waists and to the the wagon, and begin carefully making their way to the nearest bodies, taking care to avoid possible sinkholes. Cortez then calls out over Maalek's mental link for them to be wary, for he sees half a dozen of suspicious mounds of mud.
- After agreeing on the next course of action, Cortez begins firing his revolver into the nearest suspicious mound. A moment after the bullets sink into the mud, a strange huge creature resembling thick trunk capped by a crown of wide leaves, whipping vines, mushrooms, and a ravenous purple maw, erupts upward, screaming. Five more of these creatures erupt from the suspicious mud mounds, eqaully screeching. They flail their vines, and begin charging towards the team. Arina informs the team over the mindlink that these are tendriculoses, aggressive plant creatures.
- Arina is first to react. From her seat on the wagon, she disappears from sight, and immediately appears at the nearest tendriculos, stabs it with a *fading strike*, then flashes back onto the wage. Maalek and Cortez assault the enemies with a barrage of fiery *energy missiles* and bullets. Although wounded, the tendriculoses wade through the mud at astounding speeding, coming into reach of the team on the wagon.
- Argus tries to jump over a puddle of mud to meet the oncoming horder, but the weight of his gear does not allow him to get far, and he falls into the pool. The rest of the team look on in horror as they see the Iron Giant disappear from sight into the mud. Davona hops down from the wagon, and reaches into the pool. She is able to pull her companion out of the mud and onto the bank. However, both are assaulted by the plant creatures. Argus is grabbed by one of them, but succeeds to break free.
- Cortez and Maalek continue to provide range support, and are able to bring down several of the monsters. Arina fades in and out of sight, flashing about the battlefield. Each time she appears, she delivers a devasting thrust of her blade, tearing away large chunks of plant matter thanks to her empowered skill. Argus and Davona are surrounded by tendriculoses. They try to get out of their current position, but they are forced to wrestle with the many powerful vines attempting to pin them down.
- Nearby, Arina appears out of nowhere, and completely eviscerates a tendriculos' upper half with a *persecution strike*. She hears a faint, sinister laughter in her ears, a reminder of whose power she had to call upon to execute such a fatal blow. Nonetheless, only five plant monsters remain. Cortez succeeds in killing another one with another volley of bullets from his pair of revolvers, relieving Argus of some of his attackers.
- Arina then vanishes and reappears behind one of the tendriculoses assaulting Davona. Catching sight of the other woman, the caelephilim reacted with well-honed coordination they have developed after all this time of fighting along with each other. As Arina executes her *fading strike*, Davona unleashes her *garnet lance* technique. Both women slash and skewer their foe to pieces at the same time, tearing it apart to pieces.
- With all of the monsters killed, the team recovers the remains of Mick's team. They prepare them for transport in the wagon and return to Promethea. They also gather the strange mushrooms as their prize.
