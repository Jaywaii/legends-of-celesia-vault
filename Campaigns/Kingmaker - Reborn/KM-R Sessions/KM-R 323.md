# Previously

At the Thunderhoof refuge, Andrastreia leads a circle of shamans to use the Cord of the Ancients to raise a dome of holy energy over the refuge. Meanwhile, her daughter Iolanthe with another circle of shamans, performs Elyssria's Dream Quest, sending Davona, Sky, Argus, Jupus, Orok, and Aedre to the Dreamlands crafted based on dreams and memories of those who were involved in the creation and fate of Pyrouranos. The Evergreen Company found themselves atop a hill where centaurs, elves, and dwarves were in the middle of performing a ritual on a legendary bow, while centaur and human warriors fought hordes of undead trying to climb the hill. The Company joined in the defense, but the horde grows ever more oppressing.
