# Previously

Previously, on Kingmaker Reborn. After realizing that a different Heart of the Forest was needed for the ritual to help Arina be rid of Mister Angelcakes, the Evergreen Company explored the southern shores of Lake Iridescence. Finding the mouth of a river leading into a forest land southwards, the Company disembarked from their magical boat. However, upon setting foot on land, the forest immediately attacked them, shooting out large spikes of gnarled branched to impale them, and creeping vines and roots to grab them. The Company made a hasty retreat back onto the boat, and sailed away from the shore and out of harm's reach.
