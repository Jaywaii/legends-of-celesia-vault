# KM:R 52
[[KM-R 51|← Previous: KM:R 51]] · [[KM-R 53|Next: KM:R 53 →]]

- Vincenzio's Carnevale arrives in Promethea, and the Evergreen Company goes to inspect it. They wind up participating in a few of the events. Sky wins the ring toss. Bekka has a lot of fun, but Félicité is unhappy with the distraction of miscreant carnies.
- Rest of the party arrives and meets with Vincenzio to assess his intent. Satisfied that the carnival is legit, Sky buys orphans and beggars 1 gp worth of tickets.
- Jupus visits the Wonders of Modern Engineering, and saves a girl from getting caught in the auto wood chipper machine. He warns the carny running the exhibition to put up more security. He brings back what he learns from the exhibition to his engineering team.
- Ayaki and Cethin participate in the archery contest, where Ayaki wins a clown doll.
- While attending to their governing duties, the Company learns of the recent death of a local ranger by the name of Tess Kalini at the hand of a rider covered in ice, and the death of one Enzo Ragosta, a local hunter, at the hands of an old man with a scythe and a large tiger.
- Cethin, Arina, Sky, and Argus continue exploring the lands west of the Evergreen Hills, first trying to track down weird sounds. Communicating with birds is fruitless as they only bring Sky sightings of a stray feral cat.
- The explorers spot the Mad Hermit, lying in wait amidst the tall grass. The disheveled old man tries to ambush them with his dire tiger, but he is quickly killed in two blows after being disabled by Sky's *stinking cloud*. Sky harvests the tiger for parts.
- The Company find the hermit's home in the North Draketooth Forest, and find a locket with a picture of a woman, along with a faded map.
- The Company continues exploring towards the Lonely Barrow. They awaken the hundreds of bats in the mound's entrance, and Cethin enters it invisibly.

---

- The next day after Duloup's party, the Company has the opportunity to discuss any official business with Duloup.
- The Dancing Lady is making her move, claiming the Forgotten Keep as not only her base of operations, but her new capital of a new fairy nation of which she is queen. Count Calefaigdh has gifted her with shadowy power, which she has used to ensnare a cold rider into her service. Calefaigdh is once again interested in manipulating souls and fates. The Dancing Lady no longer needs to feed on blood, but on souls to grow her power. She sends the cold rider to bring her more souls.
- Upon the Company's return to Promethea, a traveling carnival has set up camp just outside the outskirts of the settlement. Called the Vincenzio Carnevale, it is a proper carnival where the owner Vincenzio has given refuge to many of the malformed outcasts of Lurindor, but along with some other undesireables.
- The Carnival remains in Promethea for a month, unless the Company drives them off. No hostile actions are taken this time, the Carnival behaves as a proper carnival, providing fun and entertainment for all. After it leaves, Company has a chance to learn that some settlers disappeared. These would be orphans or loners, described by others as being enamored with the life of a traveling carnie. These disappearances are attributed to them leaving with the Carnival, which is true and they were welcomes as such.
- The Company is welcome to take part of the festivities in the Carnival. However, sad news arrive of the death of one of their settlers, a woman by the name of Tessa Kalini was reported killed. A former adventurer, she and her husband Namdrin Quinn were exploring the southern reach of the Evergreen Hills when a horse rider cloaked with ice slew her. Her husband managed to escape and return to Promethea. Quinn disappears later. Witnesses say that he joined the carnival.
- The next month, a male halfling messenger from Grondeloup arrives in the Prateria with an offer from Théodore Duloup: despite the little incident in an otherwise great birthday celebration, he would like to further deepen the ties between our two nations by opening a Grondeloup embassy in Promethea. Should the Prateria accept, Josèphine Chansonnais will arrive in Promethea with a small retinue one week later, along with the future ambassador.
- During the 6 months before the Carnival's next visit, they ventured too close to the Dancing Lady's territory. They are attacked by the Cold Rider, but the Dancing Lady has different ideas. She corrupts the carnies with the darkness already in their hearts, especially warping Namdrin to become one of her new champions, promising him to set Tessa free from her icy prison.

## Vincenzio Carnevale

- **Mr. Sathelbry's Wild Rides:** Sathelbry is a one-armed geezer who offers rides around and throughout the carnival grounds. He dislikes children, but although gruff, he stillacts professionally to prospective clients.
- **Menagerie of Freaks:** Vincenzio gathered these pour unfortunates, and although they must display their features to make ends meet, Vincenzio treats them fairly, and ensures they are not mistreated by others.
	- The Dog-Face Girl
	- The Baby in a Jar
	- The Human Fish
	- The Sword Swallower
	- The Fat Woman
	- The Poison Man
- **The Titan's Wheel:** A contest of strength to win tickets for free beer.
- **The Strong Ale Tent**
- **Burlesque Show:** A pavilion reserved for more mature audiences which features entertaining shows performed by scantily clad nubile young male and female dancers wearing garish lingerie. The show is run by Kabran Bloodeye (CN male half-orc rogue 5), whi is stern, but treats his team fairly, although he does have a crude sense of humour.
- **The Wonders of Modern Engineering:**  Several large pavilions set up together. Within features all sorts of new machinery, exotically designed although their practicality is dubious. These machines are designed for logging, farming, production.
	- A young girl risk getting caught into one of the machines.
