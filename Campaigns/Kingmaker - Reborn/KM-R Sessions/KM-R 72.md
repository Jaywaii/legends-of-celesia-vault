# KM:R 72
[[KM-R 71|← Previous: KM:R 71]] · [[KM-R 73|Next: KM:R 73 →]]

- Sky tried once again to stealthily approach the Dancing Lady's keep to mark the area to teleport the siege engine ammunition. This time, he made additional efforts to be stealthy, and successfully snuck between the enemy patrols to place his arcane mark on a stone.
- Days later, the Ironwatch Moondancer Expedition arrived at the Dancing Lady's Keep without resistance. The Dancing Lady was ultimately confident in her superior fairy forces against the mundane soldiers, and allowed them to approach.
- The army could not see the invisible fairies, so the keep looked empty. They began firing their catapult and pair of ballista, not doing a lot of damage. Even fireballs impacted on the doors and walls of the keep did little damage.
- After much discussion, it was decided that the army should engage with the enemy forces. As the army charged the walls and the fairies revealed themselves, Sky and Louis teleported members of the Evergreen Company in waves. The first group ended up in the VIP guest room on the 2nd floor of the keep. Sky's 2nd wave of Ayaki, Cethin, and Tobias ended up within the courtyard of the keep, and had to spend another teleport to get back with the rest of the party in the keep.
- In the keep, most of the party found the stairs to descend to the floor below. Argus, magically enlarge, went into the opposite hallway, and smashed through the floor, dropping down directly in the middle of the Dancing Lady's courtroom.
- After getting over their surprise, the battle between the Moondancer Court and the Evergreen Company ensued. The Dancing Lady's majordomo, Rigg Gigardilly, was the first to be killed by Argus.

## Notes:

- Because the Evergreen Company did not take steps to hide their actions, the Dancing Lady is aware of their assault, and has since reinforced her forces with an addition 75 fairies (split evenly among redcaps, quicklings, and satyrs) since Elyssria's vision provided to Argus. These additional fairies will form another force that will remain hidden outside of the keep, and will assault the Ironwatch army as they lay siege to the keep.
- The Dancing Lady relies on the intelligence provided by Marcelette, known only as the Cloaked Woman. The insight she gained was from her patron, Count Calefaigdh, who is using her brother to spy on the Evergreen Company. Marcelette has orders to assist the Dancing Lady in her fight against the Evergreen Company, but if she looks to be defeated, Marcelette is to take her heart and return it to Calefaigdh. She has access to a *shadow-time bomb* which can slow down time to a crawl except for her in a wide area. She uses this to stop everyone else from killing the Dancing Lady, and to allow her to take the heart and disappear.

## Dancing Lady's Keep

On an open field with the Greenbelt River within sight stands a stone keep of elven design. The stones of its foundations, parts of the wall, and half of its towers are old and worn, but recent construction has been built upon it. A ring wall encircles the keep, over 20 feet high. Six towers over 30 feet high can be seen built into the wall. Within the wall is a two-story small keep. The wall is thick enough to allow troops to patrol atop of it.

The 1st floor of the keep holds the main hall. Its wings house quarters for the important lieutenants of the Dancing Lady. The 2nd floor is reserved for the Dancing Lady's chambers and important guest quarters where Marcelette currently resides. There is a basement for storage for supplie, and cells for prisoners and torture. The courtyard is filled with tents for the general troops and areas for combat practice.
