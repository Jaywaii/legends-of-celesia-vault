# KM:R 44
[[KM-R 43|← Previous: KM:R 43]] · [[KM-R 45|Next: KM:R 45 →]]

- Team 13 recovers their wounds from their fight with the yangethe. They must once against attempt to navigate the twisted maze of paths of Ing-gol'zha, a task which takes a toll on their minds. They notice that the city is not built of stone, but of some form of calcified material. Alien engravings decorate the city's structures, made of symbols and impossible shapes that hurt to examine. Their sense of time is also distorted. However, they eventually make it to the first pylon.
- At the base of the pylon is a circular doorway leading into a large round chamber within the pylon. Across the room is another doorway, sealed with a stone slab. Hovering above it is a massive skeletal torso with arms and skull attached, seemingly overlooking the entire chamber. The walls of the chamber is lined with a large trough of glowing green viscious ooze embedded into the floor. This is the furthest that the Evergreen Company ventured forth.
- The chamber walls are covered in engraved murals of symbols and images, stretching upward all the way to the top of the pylon. Rex is able to analyze them, but in doing so, corruption occurs in his systems. However, the jiren is able to decipher the murals which depict a ritual.
- Raagi takes an hour to manifest *sensitivity to psychic impression*. The ancient kobold observes the past of this chamber. He watches the Evergreen Company strike the massive skeleton arm, and their battle against a zombie horde as a result of it. His next vision flashes back further into the past, where he sees a group of adventurers of various races examining the chamber as they are doing. Another scene flashes, and Raagi sees a halfling bent over gagging and vomits up something glinting. Throughout these visions, Raagi has been experiencing a sense of revulsion, and that revulsion finally hits him strongly enough to knock him out of it and makes him vomit up what few contents of his stomach has. Back in the present, he sees that the massive torso had lifted a dessicated corpse from the pool of ooze, and dangles it by the neck an inch over the centre of the chamber.
- Rex explains that the ritual he read on the walls requires someone to consume the eye of the inert zombie, then vomit up the key to the door. Raagi, ironically being the most hardy of the group, volunteers to ingest the eyeball. As Igvald sinks his fingers into the zombie's eye socket, the zombie begins sreaming and flailing in horrific pain. It continues to do so as Igvald pulls out the eye, after which the zombie suddenly ceases all sound and movement. Raagi takes the slime covered eyeball, and swallows it in one gulp. After a moment, Raagi starts feeling ill in his stomach. There is audible gurgling and churning within him. He doubles over and vomits. Amidst the bile, a glinting opalescent pearl pings off the floor. The pearly fits perfectly in the small central indentation of the stone slab sealing the next doorway. Zen places it in, and the slab rolls to the side, opening the chamber to the next passageway. As it does so, the giant torso dumps the zombie into the pool and returns to its original positioning.
- Before leaving, Rex notices a small, dug out alcove in the wall. In it contains a dusty old metal scroll case bearing the symbol of a musical note surrouded in flames. Within that scroll case is a well preserved parchment, another note from Talien Firesong:
	- *"We finally arrived at one of the focal towers. It is truly a site of worship for the mad adherents of the Elder Horrors. Magical power thrums throughout every inch of this unholy place. The giant, mummified torso of a sight to both behold and unnerve. The green ichor that pools along the chamber glows eerily. It is amazing that it is active for all these millenia. The murals within are a fascinating source of lore regarding the worship of the Defiler From Within. The torturous leaps of logic that we needed to perform gave us headaches, but we managed to figure out how to unlock the gate to reach further into the city. In regards to the proper steps, I shall keep notes on these upon my person as I dare not leave it idle for any to uncover. Suffice to say, Margerite La Sagesse will not be having an easy time eating any time soon. We know not yet what needs to be done to destroy or seal the power of this tower; more research will be needed. For now, we press on."*
	- Taking some time to examine the chamber and drawing upon their learning of secret mythos lore, Team 13 is able determine that kind of ritual would be necessary to seal the power of this focal tower. However, they would need some dream fungus and a special idol of Par'manorok.
- After more headache-inducing exploration, Team 13 finds themselves inside another large chamber where its floor and walls are completely covered in a layer some kind of organic and veiny resin, originating from a large pit at the chamber's centre. Embedded in this resin, on the floor, walls, and ceiling, are numerous large cocoon-like pods, many of which are broken and empty. However, there are still a few that are intact, but their contents are obscured by the fluids within them. Within the pit lies a dessicated, mummified corpse that his horrifically bloated from chest downwards, completely filling pit. Raagi begins to manifest *sensitivity to psychic impressions*, while the rest of the team examines the room. They discover another hidden scroll case from Talien Firesong. In it, the ancient bard recounts:
	- *"We clashed against another of this city's horrors, though this one was far more familiar than others. Only a few of us had never fought ghouls, but none of us had even set foot in one of the Thul-koon's original breeding pits. The broodmother had long since expired; only a mummified husk remained. However, the chamber was covered in ghoul incubation cocoons. Even more bizarrely, we could identify different strains of ghouls being grown, as opposed to a singular strain based upon the original broodmother. This must be an effect of the city or advanced thul-koon experimentation. However, during our investigation, we somehow triggered the birthing process. All of the pods burst, releasing their spawn, each eager to devour us. We were completely swarmed. Only the fact that these ghouls were fresh without equipment or experience allowed us to eliminate them without significant loss, though many of us were badly injured. Afterwards, we surmised that the ghouls reacted to the psychic power of Dormek of the Third-Eye when he used it to aid in our examinations."*
- Upon reading that, the team turned to Raagi to stop him, but it was too late. Raagi completed his psioninc manifestation, and the moment he does so, the contents of the remaining pods become active. Clawed hands start tearing through the pods' film, releasing their fluids. Gaunt, pale humanoid creatures emerge, their snarling maws filled with sharpened teeth. Team 13 recognizes these creatures: ghouls. The doors to the chamber slam shut.
- Battle ensues between the Argent Rose agents and their undead foes. However, the ghouls are newly spawned, and lack any intelligence and skills, operating solely on carnivorous instinct. With their tools and experience, Team 13 is able to dispatch them easily enough. However, if it was not for the Firesong expedition wiping out the majority of the ghouls, Team 13 could have easily been swarmed judging from the numerous cocoons in this chamber. Tearing through the layer of organic creep, the team is able to reach the mechanism to open the door. Before the team leaves this chamber, they notice that some of the empty cocoons are slowly pooling with fluids once more.
