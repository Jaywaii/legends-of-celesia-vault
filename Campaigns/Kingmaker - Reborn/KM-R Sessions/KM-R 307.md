# KM:R 307

Defense of Valenlet begins at 6 pm.

At 8 pm, Vorakios is defeated in the mindscape.

At 11 pm, zombies within Valenlet are wiped out.

# Previously

It took the Company most of the morning, but you ended the zombie horde in the Arborfane Gap, saving Arbor's Rest from another battle. A couple of hours later, Sky awakened from his mysterious coma. While unconscious, the magic of his wish allowed him to see visions with clues to the current locations of the remaining fragments of the Cord of the Ancients. One is held by the servants of St. Radimus in his house of mercy, and the other is in the hands of minions of the undying gaze who are working to distance it from its homeland.

Information was gathered on the political figures of Anatolkerat. An appointment was made with the Sotillini Cartel to recruit mercenaries. Discussion was also held with House Châteaufoy. The lucklyn trade house promised additional aid, and to support Prateria in whatever approach they make towards Anatolkerat and their Carpathosi sponsors. However, little else coudl be done as the Comapny needed to rest before confronting Vorakios once more in the mindscape to protect Valenlet.
