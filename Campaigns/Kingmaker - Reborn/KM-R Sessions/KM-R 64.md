# KM:R 64

---

A day after meeting with Mikmek, Ayaki convened a council to discuss what to do about this imminent monster attack. It was determined that trying to find invisible monsters in an area miles in length would be beyond their means. Cethin suggested that instead of going to to look for them, that they should draw the monsters to them. Ayaki split the group into three. Cethin and Davona would go to the southest of Promethea, Ayaki, Jupus, and Rishoi would go south, and Arina, Argus, and Sky would go to the southwest. Each would be accompanied by a large retinue of guards. Davona further deployed her hundreds of newly formed police force around the nation to keep an eye out for any other hostile activity.

Two days later, the troops set out from Promethea. They made a big show of making their presence known, both to reassure their people and to make themselves targets. Around this time, Moran Desmond found himself wandering the same area.

Having long since departed his homeland of the Holy Radimus Empire, Moran followed whatever direction his feet led him too. On this day, he was walking along a dirt path along with some workers and tradesmen on their way to the nearby iron mine. The travelers were attacked by the sudden appearance of satyrs and remoceras. Although many were wounded and some outright killed, many survived thanks for Moran, who held off the fairies with explosives before escaping himself.

Argus' patrols found Moran, and upon hearing his story, brought him to the Evergreen Company. He recounted the events that just happened, backed by other witnesses that were found by other patrols. Moran was then volunteered by the Company to lead them to where the attack happened.

Nearing the area, only Argus noticed something was amiss before the satyrs appeared and fired arrows at them. Louis purge the veil around him, revealing four remoceras charging at them. Moran and the Company took make severe wounds, but managed to kill two of the remoceras. However, the fight for survival was not over yet...
