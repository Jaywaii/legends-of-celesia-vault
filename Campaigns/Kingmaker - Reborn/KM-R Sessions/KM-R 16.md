# KM:R 16
[[KM-R 15|← Previous: KM:R 15]] · [[KM-R 17|Next: KM:R 17 →]]

The away team consisting of Arina, Cethin, and Argus released the mite prisoner from their most recent attack. They had the mite return to its home with a challenge to their king over who rules over the Great Cypress Tree. Should the Evergreen Company be defeated, they promised to aid the mites in destroying their kobold rivals. However, should the Evergreen Company be triumphant, the mites are to be exiled from the Evergreen Plains towards the Drakethorn Glades. The challenge would take place a week from now, to be fought on the plains of the Belladonna patch east of the Evergreen Hills. They did not have to wait long before an arrow lands before them with the mite's acceptance.

The group returned to Onestrio's. They learned that Sky and Davona had disappeared off to somewhere. There have also been an increase in strangers visiting the trade post, asking around about the Evergreen Company. Aside from that oddity, they prepared for the battle to come.

When the day of the battle arrived, six of the Evegreen Company faced off against the mass of mites that have gathered. King Grabbles even presented a kobold prisoner that would serve as a witness to the inevitable victory over the tall folk. Despite being outnumbered 2 to 1 by the warriors selected by the mite warriors selected to fight them, Ayaki, Arina, Jupus, Cethin, Rishoi, and Argus met them head on in battle.
