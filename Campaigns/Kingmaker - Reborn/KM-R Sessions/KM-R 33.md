# KM:R 33

The rest of the Evergreen Company were dashing and leaping all over the fort to join up with Arina and Argus. Rishoi reached Arina and touched her, transfering her reiki to heal the badly wounded woman. Acid burns caused by the old man's *caustic blood* were slowly healed.

"Thank you, Rishoi," she said with relief. Arina looked up at the Stag Lord, and pointed at him. "Mister Angelcakes, I need you."

A ray from her fingertip struck the Stag Lord true. Arina's *Chronal Aggression* technique warped the Stag Lord's sense of time, sending him mentally reeling.

Jupus and Gnawfang finally touched ground into the fort. Quickly, Gnawfang helped brace the oversized rifle, and the gnome took aim at the Stag Lord. He had that bastard's head dead in his sights. "Shoot down my balloon, will you?" he muttered. He squeezed the trigger.

The shot should have been true. It should have been the perfect kill shot. But the Stag Lord's instinct was on edge. Without even fully realizing it, the bandit lord shifted his head the moment Jupus pulled the trigger. The bullet scraped flakes of bone on the edge of the stag skull. Jupus' curses echoed throughout the fort.

Meanwhile, the Stag Lord whirls around to Rishoi, and repeatedly smashed his fat fist into her face, breaking her arms when she tried to raise them to shield herself. He then spins around, and sent the back of his fist into Argus' face. The tall man takes the blow without a flinch.

The old man reformed back into his true self further away, and unfurled a scroll. He began incanting the spell upon it when Cethin appeared out of thin air, and tried to stop him. However, the ninja was overwhelmed by the old man's nauseating miasma. He missed, and the old man healed some of his wounds. Ayaki joined her younger brother, but she too was overcome by the old man's stench. Her gagging and coughing caused her to miss her swing.

Rishoi struck the Stag Lord with a side kick in the gut, but the bandit was still moving with preternatural anticipation, and her follow-up kicks missed completely. However, all of the Stag Lord's bobbing and weaving led him directly into Arina's path.

Arina executed *Dogpile Strike*, the best technique to use against a foe surrounded by one's allies. Her thrust went directly into the stag skull's eye socket, and directly into the Stag Lord's own eye. The man roared in pain as he pulled his head back, a trail of blood shooting in the air. Some of that blood is absorbed by Arina, and some of her wounds heal.

Cethin cloaked himself from sight once more. He then attakcs the old man with *Sting of the Adder*. Infusing his *ki* into his wakizashi, Cethin struck at the old man's mind, ignoring the pain caused by the mystical thorns lacerating his hand. His life energy corrupted part of the vile druid's mind. The slice at the inside of the leg made the old man fall to the ground screaming. Cethin quickly dodged out of the way of the wound's spray of *caustic blood* in his direction.

Davona came running up behind Cethin. She marshalled her will to overcome the nausea of the old man's miasma. With a powerful thrust, Davona's *Garnet Lance* stabbed down into the old man's chest, piercing through into the wooden floow beneath him. The old man cackled and gurgled out blood as he dies, the last of his enchantments lacerating Davona, and his spraying blood badly burning her.

From his height, the Stag Lord looked at his father's demise, then roared to the heavens. He initiated *Iron Knuckle*, then executed *Shards of Steel Strike* at Argus. The bandit punched through Argus' helm, smashing his face in. Argus had never before known a blow such as this one as he vomited blood. The force of the blow badly damaged one of his eyes, rendering him half-blind. But the Stag Lord was not done.

Drawing upon some unknowning mythical speed, the Stag Lord whipped his fast back at Arina before anyone could react. He smashed into Arina's remaining good arm, breaking her bones, and floowed through downward into her leg, shattering her knee. At the end of his attack, the Stag Lord fell to one knee, and remained still. It was only after a moment of complete stillness that the Company realized the growing pool of blood that had formed beneath him.

Before anyone could react, a green glow began emanating from the Stag Lord's body. Glowing green motes slowly rose into the air. From his wounds, the Stag Lord became enveloped by vices and roots. A burst of emerald burst from the kneeling corpse, striking into each member of the Evergreen Company. In turn, they too began to glow green. Each of them suddnely began feeling a power swelling within them. A moment after, the Stag Lord ceased glowing, now just a kneeling figure of vines and branches. A ring upon his finger unfurls into strands of green hair, floating away by the night's wind.

"What... what was that?" Arina wondered.

"I don't know," Argus offered. "But did you feel that? It did something to us. I've never felt so... alive."

Sky flew down beside them. "We've been touched by a great power. It's amazing! I'll need to study us further to find out what. Wait, what's that?"

The sylph pointed at the Stag Lord's figure. A faint, green flow emanated from a gap between the roots on his chest. The Company approached the kneeling figure, and the roots on his chest rotted away. In the revealed cavity, the Stag Lord's heart still beated, but it and its veins glowed green, and was wrapped in thin roots and vines. The Company were drawn to it, and they could feel the power pulsing from it. Ayaki reached into, and effortlessly pulled it out. "Friends, I believe this is the Heart of the Forest that the Stag Lord stole from the Old Arborfane." The Company quickly wrapped the Heart with a clean cloth, and packed it away.

Acteon, now finally up on the walkways of the fort alongside Ayaki and Davona, looked on with amazement. He hesistated approaching them until the glow dissipated. "You've done it," he whispered. "You killed the Stag Lord."

"I'm sorry that we had to take the lives of your men," Davona said. Even as she said the words, she was surprised that she actually did feel sorrow for the deaths of bandits.

But Acteon shook his head. "It was the life we chose. It was bound to end this way. Speaking of which..."

The former second-in-command of the Stag Bandits leaned over the side of the walkway, and yelled. "Everyone! The Stag Lord is dead! It's time to give yourselves up!"

Sky dismissed his *web*. One by one, the remaining bandits who survived the fight came out, and surrendered, throwing their weapons onto the ground. The Stag Lord's dimwitted lieutenant Toro was about to charge, but Acteon raised his hand up to stop him. "It's over, Toro. We've surrendered." The large brute let out a mournful, depressed sound as he stopped his charge to a slump.

The Stag Lord's other lieutenant, the preening Dante, swaggered up to the Company. "My ladies, sirs, allow me to offer my congratulations on defeating tha villains! Though I had to follow that brute's orders on pain of death, I would have readily turned upon him and aid you in battle had I not been cut off by that web!" Acteon rolled his eyes at the man's expected routine.

Judging from Ayaki's tone of voice, she too was not fooled by the man's demeanour. "No doubt that you would have."

After securing the remaining bandits as prisoners, the Company investigate the fort. They discovered many of the Montari crates that were recently stolen from the Steel Peacock Company. There were also a large amount of supplies. Acteon also provided all the information he knew about the Stag Bandits' operations. He revealed that the Evergreen Company has killed or captured all of the lieutenants except for one, who they sent to the west towards the North Draketooth Forest, but they had not heard back from him since.

As for the bandits, they were offered to swear fealty to the Evergreen Company, and work to be rehabilitated. "Of course! Absolutely, whatever you say, my lords and ladies!" Dante readily replied. Toro looked to Acteon first, then agreed when the older man nodded. All of the bandits's gear were confiscated for the time being, but they were not bound as long as they remained peaceful and made no attempt to escape. Considering the might and power the Evergreen Company displayed, along with Acteon's leadership, none of them were even considering the attempt. Even Dante opted to patiently bid his time for an opportunity to show up.

With their enemy slain, and the spoils of victory in hand, the Evergreen Company returned home triumphant.
