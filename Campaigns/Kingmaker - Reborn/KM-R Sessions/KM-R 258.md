# Previously

*Your team gained the assistance of your recent acquaintance, Xura Nythagoi, to travel to the Sea of Mulemria to find a thought of an incutilis lord. In exchange for the rainbow conch, given to you by the Saffron Prince, or another thought of an incutilis lord, Nythoagoi lent you the services of the Moon Siren, captained by Gaxxio Lethgul. You had a comfortable day-long sail to the Sea of Mulemria, and you spent the time getting to know each other much better, as well as reflecting on your recent activities and actions. Upon arriving at the Sea of Mulemria, you had the crew go below deck and man the ship's cannons, and you then blew the conch. Dark clouds quickly formed above you, and lightning struck the sea. At the first crash of thunder, the conch shattered into a thousand pieces, and the sea became rough. Wild waves and winds battered the ship when eight 100-ft long tentacles emerged from the waters. Then, a wave crashed upon the ship, revealing the appearance of a pair of grotesque fishmen. The tentacles, tipped with massive maws, started to swallow you, while the horrific presence of the fishmen triggered primordial fears in several of you. Detective Ford then jumped overboard and swam deep below. He quickly found what you sought, thousands of glowing pearls each containing a thought of an incutilis lord. He also found the origin of the tentacles, for those pearls were embedded in the shell of a massive, nightmarish primordial incutilis lord.*
