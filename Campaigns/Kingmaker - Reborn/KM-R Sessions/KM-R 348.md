# KM:R 348

# Previously

*As Adriel finished discerning the aspect of Vorakios in the Forge of Broken Bones, the Company each passed through the exit portal to the Well of Shackled Souls. When Argus and Gnawfang first entered the room, they were greeted by a morodea known as the Twisted Apothecary, a fairy of cockroaches who was surrounded by alchemical golems. The morodea's cockroaches hissed loudly in unison, causing two glass vats near Argus and Gnawfag to shatter, spilling out a torrent of vitriol that coalesced into two threatning blights.*

/*As more of the Company entered the room, they fell under assault by the blights and their stench. Maalek proved that melee attacks to be dangerous against them as his one strike to one of them caused it to spray acid on the aelfkyn and his weapon, partially dissolving both. The threat became partially contained when Dragormir grappled both of the blights. The Apothecary proved no less dangerous as she lobbed a bomb of acid at Argus and Davona. In return, Argus started smashing many of the alchemical tools and devices in the room, including one device that churned out alchemical golems. However, that put him in range for some of the golems to throw bombs at him while the others started assaulting the rest of the Company.*
