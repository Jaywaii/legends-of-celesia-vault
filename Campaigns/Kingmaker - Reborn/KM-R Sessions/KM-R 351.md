# KM:R 351

# Previously

*After killing the Twisted Apothecary, the Company battled its remaining minions in the laboratory known as the Well of Shackled Souls. They were not helped by having once more gained Vorakios' attention, who took control of the Well and its apparatuses to spawn more vitriol blights. Sky began deciphering Vorakios' aspect within the primary alchemical vat and the rest of the Company moved to protect him. Bjorn's planetars invoked earthquakes throughout the laboratory, hapering the actions of the enemies while the Company was safe as all began flying. However, the blights spreaded more cloudkills, and took advantage of its cover to deliver deadly attacks. Argus suffered the worst of it, as a cloudkill flowed over him. While the effects of its magical poison was negated by his magical ring, his vision was literally clouded. He tried to move through it, but the blights, hidden within the cloudkill with their blindsight and lengthy reach, fatally struck him several times. The planetars tried to sustain him, but Prateria's supreme general ultimately fell dead.*
