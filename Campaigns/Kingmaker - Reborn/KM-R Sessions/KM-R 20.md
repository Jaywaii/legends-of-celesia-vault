# KM:R 20

- The **<u>Blood Jackals</u>** are one of Amonkhaten's deadliest agents. They serve the Ashaki God-King as hunters and assassins, sent to hunt down and eliminate his enemies.
	- **[[Razeena]] (female kitai stalker 5):** Razeena the Bloodclaw is slender and leopard-like: a thin layer of golden fur with black spots. She is quiet and precise.
	- **[[Menekheb]] (male ifrit magus 5):** Menekheb Flamelord is the Blood Jackals' second in command. He is a long time comrade of Amenaru.
	- **[[Amenaru]] (male human investigator 5):** Leader of the Blood Jackals, Amenaru the Erudite is a tall, thin man with deeply tanned skin, and sports a thin, gold vertical chin strip. His bald head is covered with a wide-brimmed hat, and he wears a pair of glasses. He is always well-spoken and thoughtful, and the one that comes up with the plans.
	- **[[Musaid]] (male human bloodrager 5):** Musaid of the Raging Flame is a tall, bulky man with dark skin covered in arcane tattoos. His head is bald except for a long top-knot, and a long braid of hair on his chin. He is the muscle of the Jackals.
	- **[[Shawi]] (female human inquisitor 5):** Shawi Throne-Speaker is devoted to the will of Amonkhaten II. She is stern and fanatic.

- Downtime events:
	- Unexpected Fees: The Davini Lumber Company has hit you with some unexpected import fees for the lumber purchased for the bunks. You can either pay the tax amount (10% of the bunk = 40 gp) or attempt a DC 20 Bluff check to talk your way out of the taxes. If you succeed, you don't have to pay these taxes. If you fail, the tax owed doubles and you can't talk your way out of it.
	- Day of Rest: It's an unusually relaxing day. Nothing bad happens, and minor events seem to conspire to make all the little things work out perfectly. Your prisoners and guards are well rested and in good spirits. You gain 1d3 points of Labor.
	- Infestation: The hatchery suffers an infestation of rats. As long as the hatchery is infested, it takes a -10 penalty on checks to generate capital. Each day the infestation continues, you lose 1 point of Goods, or Labor To end an infestation, you must succeed at a DC 20 Handle Animal or Survival check; the DC increases by 1 for each day the infestation persists (maximum DC 30). Alternatively, the GM may allow you to resolve the infestation with a combat encounter.
	- Rumormongering: People are talking about the Evergreen Company. Attempt a DC 20 Diplomacy check. On a success, word spreads far that your presence in the region is valuable and welcomed, and you gain 1d4 points of Influence. On a failure, the rumors are not so complimentary (and perhaps even insulting), and you lose 1d3 points of Influence.
	- Famous Visitor: Famed halfling chef Armand Escoffier visits the trade post, seeking fresh herbs. Attempt a DC 20 Diplomacy check. On a success, the famous visitor visits your garden, and you gain 1d2 points of Influence. Otherwise you're snubbed and lose 1d2 points of Influence.

When Davona and Sky fled into the wilderness, they continued training and gaining strength. One day, they saved a family from a wild monster. The family was extremely grateful and provided them shelter. Davona and Sky stayed around to help them out, and continued to hone their skills against the dangers in the surrounding area. However, they became suspicious when the father of the family kept coming home at the end of the day a small amounts of wealth or a fair amount of supplies that one would not normally find in the wilderness. However, they saw how he used his gains to make his family comfortable out here on their own. Nonetheless, Sky and Davona stealthily followed him one day. They discovered that he was a bandit, stealing from caravans and travelers.

They confronted the family, but they explained that this was the only way to survive. The father was disgraced, and could not make a living honestly. Savona, having come to like this family and seeing their sincere family bonds, stayed her hand. This caused a revelation to emerge within her: that not all bandits were unrepentant scum. This paradigm shift in her worldview caused her to go into seclusion to reflect on this.

---

Back at the Trade Post, a representative of the Davini Lumber Company attempted to hit the Company with some unexpected import fees for the lumber purchased by the Company. However, Ayaki saw through the paperwork what loopholes are being exploited, and the Company was able to point out that they do not have to pay a single coin, and that if they insist, the Company would be happy to address the issue with the Trade Regulation Authority.

In other news, the Company learned that the Stag Lord had shifted his focus to the lands south of the Greenbelt river. They learned that a man named Acteon was in charge of the bandits in the meantime, and from what their bandit prisoners told them, Acteon was actually a good leader. The Company also returned Sabina's wedding ring to Onestrio, having retrieved it from the mites' loot.

Davona and Sky finally returned to the trade post. Davona finished a lengthy journey of soul searching and retraining. During that time, she encountered a family on hard times, and stayed with them to help. She only later learned, after getting to know them well, that the father was forced to become a bandit to support his family. This changed her worldview, and it took her some time to reevaluate her choices in life. The family came with her back to the trade post.

Meanwhile, the away team consisting of Arina, Cethin, and Argus where in trouble, having succumbed to paralysis from wasp poison that they had just fought. A grig and a faerie dragon appeared, flying circles around them, and introduced themselves as Tyg-twitter-twig and Percilash respectively. They wanted to express their thanks for the offerings the party made, and for their good sense of humour in face of all the pranks the pair did to them. The fey and faerie dragon collect fruits, berries, and nuts for them. They drip sugar water onto their lips to keep them hydrated. They fly around in circles above them keeping watch. They also have some fun at their expense. The two apply colorful make up to their faces, drawing paintings on their clothing and armor, tied flowers to their gear and make crowns of flowers which they place on their heads. When the party can eventually move and are ready to depart, the two pranksters bid them farewell, and tell them they have friends in the Gnarlwood if they ever need to call upon them.

The away team made a stop at the Temple of the Elk to speak with Father Joram to exchange news about each other's progress. The party also made a stop at Biaggio's hut on their way back to the trade post.

Back at the Trade post, the home team finally encountered the foreign strangers that had been visiting the place and asking about the Evergreen Company. When Davona and Sky finally made their reappearance, these strangers revealed themselves. They were assassins from the Ashaki Empire, the Blood Jackals, perosnally agents of Pharoah Amonkhaten, God-King of Ashaki. They attempted to kill Sky and Davona, but they were not ready to face the full might of the entire Evergreen Company. The Blood Jackals were slain in their entirety. It was in fact that Sky and Davona had received word of the Blood Jackals arrival in the Evergreen that caused them to disappear for a time. Davona and Sky explained their past to the rest of the party, and why Amonkhaten wanted them dead.
