# KM:R 67

# Previously

Having defeated the group of monsters sent by the Dancing Lady to terrorize their nation, the Evergreen company returned to Promethea along with two new allies, Tabrir Cloudgrove and Tobias Ignatios Malakay, as thanks for their aid in the battle. After confirming that there were no other threats, the Company took a well-needed break to recuperate and hone their skills. The Company also signficantly expanded their personal retinue, their growin fame attracting many eager recruits. To accomodate these new recruits, they begun a project to build 5 garrisons. While this was happening, Spymaster Cethin's contacts among the fairies have revealed to him of rumours that the Dancing Lady has recruited a team of assassins and seeks to set them upon the Evergreen Company. However, no one knows when or by what means they will do so...

---

**Summary:**
- Tobias and Tabrir discuss their goals with the Evergreen Company in Promethea. Tobias, aka "Tim", explains that he was part of the Crimson Falcon company, but their company was betrayed and he was the sole survivor. He has been observing the other expedition parties, and came to the conclusion that the Evergreen Company was the least likely to be involved. Tabrir was a bit more obscure about his reasons for being here, but asked to talk to the person in charge of religious matters, and was directed to Sister Félicité. Ayaki welcomes them both to Prateria and invited them to stay as long as they wished as thanks for their aid against the Dancing Lady's satyrs and remaceras.
- Tabrir met with Félicité who was teaching Deianeiran philosophy to the dryads at the Great Cypress tree. Félicité invited him to her home to talk privately, and Tabrir asked her about the Cloven Crown Society. He explained that they were a secret society dedicated to the service of the archdevil Azazel, and that they were after him. Félicité was unfamiliar with them, but promised to investigate them further.
- The Evergreen Company discussed what to do with the Dancing Lady and her assassins. It was decided that they should bring the fight to her. Further discussion resolved around how to do so, and how to deal with the assassins. For the time being, the Company, along with Bekka and Félicité should seclude themselves to Argus' garrison.
- Sky decided to scry on the enemy. Opting to skip the Dancing Lady, he successfully scried the quickling majordomo. He found the fairy in an elven stone structure speaking to a woman off screen. They were discussing about the svartalfar assassins and by how expensive they were. The woman reassured the quickling that they were worth the price. Sky followed the quickling as he departed the room, and right before the spell ended, he caught glimpse of a small, cloaked figure starting to speak to the quickling.
- Sky tried scrying the cloaked figure. He found them inside a room, creating a black rose made of shadow stuff. They were only sitting on the bed, contemplating. Sky felt she seemed familiar, but found his memory of her clouded. However, after much effort, he remembered that he saw this swordswoman during the fight against the zmey headling in the Old Arborfane; she had tried to steal the Heart of the Forest. The swordswoman then looked at his sensor, and then somehow cancelled the scrying. He relayed everything he had learned back to the rest of the Company. Arina also managed to recall a clouded memory. She remembered receiving a rose made of shadows in her room one time after performing in Lurindor.
- The Company decided to invite Tabrir and Tim to the expedition. Both accepted the invitation and took part of the planning. It was decided, at Cethin's suggestion, that when the army reaches the Dancing Lady's fort, most of the party minus Jupus would teleport into the chamber that the quickling was seen in. Sky has to go scribe multiple *teleport* scrolls to make the trip.
- Meanwhile, the rest of the team decided that they should deal with the assassins. Cethin suggested that now that the Company has holed up at the garrison, they should give an opportunity to the assassins to attack them by publicly sending a part of the team out in the field where they should be vulnerable. The assassins can't possibly give up such an opportunity. Cethin will even appear as Ayaki.
- As the army is being formed, Cethin (as Ayaki), Argus, Arina, Davona, Tabrir, and Tim took the Evergreen Company's vardo and made a public show of heading out to continue exploring the region. A day away from Promethea, the part made camp for the night. The party retired into the vardo, while Argus patrolled around it. Thanks to his armor's tremorsense, the General sensed the presence of two individuals. Suspecting they were the assassins, Argus charged forward, and slammed his earthbreaker twice into one of them. The other svartalfar revealed himself and whistled. Two crossbow bolts shot from the darkness into Argus.
