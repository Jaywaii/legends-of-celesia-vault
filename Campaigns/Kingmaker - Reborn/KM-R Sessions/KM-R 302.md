# KM:R 302

# Previously

At 6 am of Fireday, Asmolan 21st, the zombie horde smashed into the Pall of Arbor's Rest. Despite incinerating thousands of zombies upon contact, the culminating mass of undead flesh succeeded in pressing through the curtain of life energy, allowing thousands of zombies to stream through towards the settlement. Asako, Domenica, and Maalek aided the Rochefort 2nd Fusilliers and the Steel Peacock security forces defend against the undead, while Attila defended the vulnerable Adriel from fairy assassins. In the air were Arina's veteran Cohort Consul Magica and her newly formed 1st Consul Magica Company.

The battle lasted 11 hours until 5 pm. Thousands of zombies were destroyed, but at the cost of over a thousand killed among the defenders. In the air, a company of grave sprites killed almost two dozen young mages before being eliminated. However, the relief of victory was soon dimmed as a bit of aerial scouting discovered that as soon as the sun set an hour later, a new wave of zombies began pouring out of the fog, lurching towards Arbor's Rest once more.
