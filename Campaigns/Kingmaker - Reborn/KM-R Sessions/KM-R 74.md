# KM:R 74
[[KM-R 73|← Previous: KM:R 73]] · [[KM-R 75|Next: KM:R 75 →]]

- The Evergreen Company looted the keep and the bodies of the Moondancer court. Argus rushed back in and cut out the Dancing Lady's heart, tossing her head away.
- Asako and Sky remained behind to repair the keep. Argus left a contingent of 50 troops to occupy the keep, with a supply of 9 BP.
- The rest of the Company returns with the rest of the army back to Promethea.
- Tabrir and Tobias each receive a dream from Elyssria, and pledge themselves to her service.
- Argus visits the settlement of Silvermine, the capital of the Sootscale Kingdom. Mikmek greets him and escorts him to King Sootscale. Argus informs him of their victory over the Dancing Lady, and invites him to a future celebration.
- Sky and Asako rejointhe army, and everyone returns to Promethea.

## Notes

- The defeat of the Dancing Lady ushers a year of peace in the northern part of the Evergreen Plains.
- The next time the party falls asleep, Tabrir and Tim receive a dream from Elyssria.

*You find yourself in an open, grassy field colored by many exotic flowers. They smell of fresh sweetness that you can't quite place. A faint mist fills the air, dispersing hazy sunlight throughout the sky, making it seem orange-pink. As you turn around to take in the sight, a woman of otherwordly beauty suddenly comes into view. She was a tall, elf-like woman over six feet tall, slender and graceful, clad in semi-transparent pink lacy fabrics. Her skin is alabaster white with a tinge of blue, and her green hair cascades down her body all the way to her feet, decorated with pink petals. Her captivating eyes are of a golden hue. You feel a powerful, yet undeniable attractive, presence emanating from her. She sitting in the grass, delicately caressing a tall rose. Finally, she turns to you, as if seeing you for the first time.*

*"Oh, hello," the woman replies in a sing-song voice. "I am Elyssria. I know who you are."*

*She tilts her head, and looks at you curiously. "You have been touched by ancient power. I wonder… what do you wish to do with it? You have fought alongside my champions to defeat our common foe, and have proven your prowess and done servier to me. You have seen how I have aided my champions. Should you desire it, I would bequeath you my patronage as well, and grant you boons within my power."*

To Tabrir: *"You are being hunted. I would see you safe in my service."*

To Tobias: *"You seek those that betrayed you. I am able to aid you in your quest."*

*"If you agree, let us pledge ourselves to each other." The beautiful fairy plucked a locket of green hair from her head that came loose effortlessly. She gently takes your hand into hers. The locket of hair wraps around your finger on its own, forming a ring.*
