# Previously

Previously on Kingmaker: The Evergreen Company ambushed the Coil assassins sent to kill Elvia Sovrani. Most of the team waited in the room next door to Elvia's bedchambers, while a few others waited outside. Sky had gathered the aid of Évard Châteaufoy and Erikos Pentras, though in the end, their assistance was not needed. Elvia had suffered grievous wounds, but Rishoi's and Osla's intervention saved her life. In astounding speed, all of the assassins were killed at the hands of Evergreen Company.
