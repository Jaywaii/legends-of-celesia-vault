# KM:R 356

# Previously

*The Company was able to swarm the Voidgazer once Dragormir was able to get his hands upon it. However, the void dragon did not go down without a fight, biting deep into Dragormir and blasting the company with astral forces. After enough battering, and returning Maalek back to flesh, the Company was able to render the Voidgazer unconscious. However, before it closed its eyes, the old dragon roared, and the stars in the void shook. The broken, giant orrery then shook, and its components began to move and spin, locking repeatedly into different configurations. When at last it halted, the central orb opened into a red orb where a glowing eye stares at the company.*

*Earlier in the fight, several members of the Company investigated the smaller ruin islands. Amidst the debris, they found pieces of the central orrery. They would be needed to repair the orrery so the company could use it to force open the exit portal of this room. However, Vorakios' eye pulsed every so often, warping space around them. Gnawfang and Lonán were caught in one of the pulses, and were flung upwards, with the sudden momentum battering their insides.*
