# KM:R 321

# Previously

The ad-hoc team of Praterian officials and Argent Rose team 13 successfully defended the Viriquian Palace and the Cathedral of Radiant Mercy long enough for the the Evergreen Company to arrive. In their capacity as the official leaders of Prateria, the Company opened negotiations with Marquis Xemonos and Bishop Basilmon for their fragment of the Cord of the Ancients. The Company told them there was no point in denying it, and out of respect for the aid they granted, Basilmon admitted the relic was in their possession. He escorted Sky down into the reliquary of the Cathedral where Sky saw the truth.

The Cord fragment rested on a pedestal, surrouned by divine circles and praying priests. Golden light shone from the fragment, flowing to the priests and their divine instruments before gathering together and shooting up through the Cathedral. This was the light was broke through Vorakios' Deathmist, and empowered the Radimite priests to create safe pockets. This was the key to their survival.

The Company had already planned on erecting another Pall of Shining Life to protect Viriquia. However, Xemonos had other demands in order to give up their one means of defense. He demanded that Prateria will officially recognize any future territorial claims by the Radimite Empire in the Thalamech Uplands, from a crusade Xemonos planned on leading as soon as the empire gathered its forces. Moreover, Basilmon requested that Prateria also grant special privileges for the Radimite clergy to evangelize in their territory which also included special legal exemptions.

The Company discussed quickly over the mental collective. They knew the centaurs would never accept further Radimite aggression, and Prateria would be betraying them if they accepted the demands. Instead, Arina offered to raise and anchor the Pall herself, with the aid of Domenica, Salden, and a recently recruited Jester. The Consul of Prateria was essentially offering herself as a hostage. Furthermore, they would offer more wealth then Xemonos could ever imagine, offering nearly half of Prateria's treasury. Sky also offered to craft a magical ring of immense power similar to the ones he crafted for the Company. With all that on the table, along with Arina's amazing negotiating skills, Xemonos and Basilmon grudgingly accepted the terms.

After the agreement had been ratified by both sides, the Evergreen Company now possessed the last fragment of the Cord of the Ancients.
