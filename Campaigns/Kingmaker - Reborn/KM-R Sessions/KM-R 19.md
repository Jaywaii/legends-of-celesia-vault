# KM:R 19

- Sootscale is relieved for Tartuk's removal, partly for his threat to his tribe, but mostly for the fact that he is once more the sole authority of the tribe. He expresses thanks to the Evergreen Company, and he is mostly sincere. However, he is wary of their intent going forward. He rewards the PCs for what they had done for his tribe with Tartuk's possessions. If pushed, he grudgingly allows them to take from the tribes treasure as well (which Tartuk had in his lair).
- Sootscale asks the PCs what do they intend to do next. Soostscale is willing to stop attacking tall folk and halflings (for now; the tribe's numbers are too depleted in any case). They will only fight in self-defense. Sootscale is willing to trade for what they need to survive instead of raiding, but has little to barter with. They could mine the silver, but they lack the tools. If the PCs can provide the tribe enough mining equipment (30 kobolds), they could mine the silver and that would extend the peace.

---

Having forced Tartuk to reveal his true intentions and forcing him to flee, the Evergreen Company learned through Tartuk's journal that the kobold was in actuality a gnome that had been reincarnated as a kobold. The mad gnome was filled with nothing but hate for his former gnomish people and for the kobolds, and had spent years masquerading as a shaman to instigate wars between gnomes and kobolds that would destroy them both. Chief Sootscale is grateful for the Company's aid in the matter, and welcomes them as friends.

The Company negotiated with Sootscale to establish trade between the Company and the Sootscale tribe in order to have them cease any hostile actions.

The Company continued exploring the Evergreen Plains. They fought an ogre that was terrorizing the area, as well as a swarm of giant wasps. During the fight against the wasps, all three members of the away team were afflicted with wasp poison, causing them to be paralyzed. During the fight, they were aided in battle by they sudden appearance of a grig and faerie dragon.
