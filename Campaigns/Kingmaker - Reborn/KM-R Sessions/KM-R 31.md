# KM:R 31
[[KM-R 30|← Previous: KM:R 30]] · [[KM-R 32|Next: KM:R 32 →]]

- The Evergreen Company begins construction of a dump and an orphanage at their new settlement of Promethea. To help fund their new nation, the Company cashed in on all their credit they had with Onestrio and sold him all their construction they had built at his outpost.
- Cethin, as Spymaster, began an investigation into the Steel Peacock company and its parent company, Montari Entreprises. The kitsune did not trust them. Through well placed bribes to some key officials, Cethin managed to gain a significant information. In addition to gaining key trade secrets that will benefit their economy, Cethin learned that Montari Entreprises was the ruling trade house of Valdargent, and that they are very ambitious and treacherous. Montari will use any means to gain an advantage in dealings, and if anyone slights them in any way, they would repay them tenfold. Its current leader is Enzio Montari, a man with a dangerous reputation.
- Bekka sent out her Blades to explore the Evergreen Plains.
- Sky went to Rochefort to buy some spell scrolls.
- After Jupus brought his balloon from Onestrio's to Promethea, a retinue led by Theodore Duloup arrives at the new settlement. His visit causes quite a bit of excitement. The Company proposal regarding the tripartite settlement prompted him to come visit them. He tella them that he is looking forward to doing business with them. He updates them on his own progress in the Thunderhood Uplands. Many hills, but also many problems with the local centaur tribes. He also shares the location of his own settlement of Wolfshold. By the end, the visit of such a famous foreign dignitary was a huge success, becoming an enormous celebration and attracting a significant amount of wealth and productivity.
- With these new resources at their disposal, the company comissioned the building of a vineyard in the hills west of Promethea.
- In the following days, Bekka reports of attacks along the border of the Old Arborfane forest: horsemen made of wood and leaves, small thorny green children, seductive nymphs luring unsuspecting travelers never to be seen again.
- On Tierlan 4th, the whole of the Evergreen Company depart from Promethea on their caravan. Bringing along their deflated balloon. After 6 days of stealthy travel, they arrived in the area of the Stag Lord's fort. The plan was to assault at night, under the cover of darkness, from the air in the balloon.
- Davona briefed the party of the known persons: the Stag Lord and his 3 lieutenants. Of particular note was the Stag Lord's 2nd-in-command Acteon Lucilius, who was an great leader according to Bekka, and had differing opinions than the Stag Lord.
- Plans are made, night falls, and the Evergreen Company begins their assault on the Stag Lord's fort.
