### Horror Beneath the Tower

*When the Evergreen Company defeated the Havenford party and made their way to Hushglow Tower, they knew something was deeply wrong with this place. Arina suffered a bout of madness, and recited blasphemous inscriptions upon a circular stone altar within the ruined tower. A flash of light shot up from the altar into the night sky, then shot eastward. The tower trembled, and the Company discovered that the altar was a cover to a circle of stairs descending deep into the earth. Below, the discovered a hallway with murals depicting the Younger Races of Celesia battling horrific worm like monsters and their disgustingly alien masters. The hallway ended at the mouth of a vast cavern. From the balcony, they beheld an underground city of alien architecture. A column of light shined from within the city's center. Seven imposing towers stood tall above the rest of the city. A voice called out to the Company in their minds, and they knew they were not ready to face this city.*

*Resealing the stairway, the company returned to Promethea where they put out a call to anyone who specialize into delving into the alien and  unknown. The Argent Rose answered the call, and they sent an experienced team of field operatives, Occult Investigation Team 13: Raagi Zugro, a centuries-old kobold medium; Igvald Gallevich, a self-harming human inquisitor-monk; Rex, one of the first jiren who lost much of its memory and recovered, repaired, and recruited by Team 13; and Zen, an erratic card magus. Team 13 delved into the underground city, and discovered that two hundred years ago, another Argent Rose expedition attempted to explore the city. The expedition's leader, renowned bard and Silver Sage Talien Firesong, had left well-preserved notes throughout the city. Team 13 learned that the city was Ing-gol'zha, a fabled city created by the ancient beings known as the thul-koon, and dedicated to their masters, the seven Cosmic Horrors entombed upon Celesia.*

*Team 13 explored Ing-gol'zha, seeking a way to depower the city, and tried to make their way to the seven focal towers. They unlocked the path through two of them: the Gate of the Defiler, and the Gate of Whispers. They encountered several terricle creatures within the city, and put to rest the ghost of Olgoth Drumsteel, the Argent Rose guide to the Firesong Expedition. They researched and uncovered the rituals to sealing both towers, though they lacked the means to do so yet. However, the city took its toll upon their sanity. Attempting to navigate the maddening twists and turns of the city warped their minds. Unlocking the gates required them to take vile, disgusting actions. Igvald's mind was temporarily broken and became a cannibal before he was cured. The rest of the team had bouts of confusion or were mentally drained from the endeavour.*

*The toll was too much for Zen, and his condition worsened. He was brought back to the Rochefort lodge so that the Society can provide him with long-term treatment. Team 13 requested reinforcement, and after a short while, two more field agents were brought in.*
