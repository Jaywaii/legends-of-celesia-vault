# KM:R 45

- Team 13 continues exploring Ing-gol'zha, and eventually makes it to the second focal tower. Although similar in dimensions as the Gate of the Defiler, this chamber differs in that there is a constant flow of air originating from tiny holes as the base of the walls. The flow of air streams upwards, passing through oddly shaped scultures embedded in the walls all the way up to the unseen top of the tower. The protusions are shaped as cylinders of different length and width, as warped seashells, and even in what looks to be crudely shaped humanoid figures. They must have had airholes within them as well, for an eerie, alien hymn can be heard as the wind passes through them, sounding almost like an unending stream of whispers. At the centre of the chamber is a wide, circular pit with no bottom in sight. Upon closer inspection, the team can see that the walls of the pit are lined with mummified corpses hanging by chains. The wind howls within the pit. The other entry way into this chamber is sealed, just as the first tower was, and there is a decayed corpse chained to the stone slab blocking their way.
- An inscription on the stone slab above the chained corpse reads in Thulkesh: *"The secret of progress can only be whispered from one breath to another."* The walls of the tower's chamber again contains engraved murals of alien symbols and imagery. Studying the murals takes a toll on the team's mind, but they come to understand the ritual to unlock this tower, the Gate of Whispers, in tribute of the Whisper Between Breaths.
- Before attempting the ritual, the team examines the chamber to see if Talien Firesong left a note. They manage to find one hidden away in a crack in the floor, underneath a loose slab. It reads: *"Much like the Gate of the Defiler, the Gate of Whispers requires us to perform a vile ritual to continue pass. Ashani volunteered this time. I suspect the lady was curious to see what 'truths' she could extract firsthand. The ritual was fascinating to watch, and I was surprised at how intrigued I was in observing it. So too were the others. And I could swear I heard something when Ashani breathe into the corpse. Something that rung true to me. Perhaps it was simply wishful thinking, my imagination enflamed by the alienness of this place. If there were any such secrets, the Lady Ashani remains quiet about them, entering a contemplative mood after unlocking the way. I look forward to getting the chance to learn more secrets as we move forward."*
- To aid them, Raagi manifests an astral construct. However, the manifestation of psionic energy in this foul place sends a backlash throughout the minds of the entire team. Alien whispers overwhelm their minds. Igvald and Raagi manage to hold onto their wits, but Zen and Rex emerge with a befuddled look. While Zen stares aimlessly into nothingness, Rex suddenly lashes out at Igvald. Igvald manages to avoid the attack, and Rex then proceeds to hit himself in the face. As Igvald attempt to knock out Rex, it is Zen's turn to attack his companions.
- While Igvald holds off their teammates, the astral construct lowers Raagi down a rope into the pit. The kobold scholar, swallowing his disgust, open mouth kisses one of the chained mummified corpses, and breathes in the rotting, nauseous air within it. He is suddenly assaulted by a buzz of whispers and alien lore. He is quickly lifted up, and lifted up again to the corpse chained to the stone slab. Raagi places his mouth on that of the corpse, and breathes out the foul breath he was holding. The corpse comes to life for a brief moment, shrieking and shaking, before falling limp once more. The stone slab then rolls to the side, opening the way forward. Raagi paid little attention as he falls to the floor, gagging. He shakily stands up, swooning as he tries to focus his thoughts through the haze of strange truths that now fill his mind.
- In a brief moment of circuitry aligning properly, Rex declares the corruption of his program. He tosses his weapon away, and kneels to the floor, requesting Igvald tie him up. Igvald does so while Raggi commands his astral construct to grab a babbling Zen. Although they run out of rope, Raggi is able to manifest an ectoplasmic rope out of thin air a few minutes later to tie up Zen with. The two remaining sane members of Team 13 quickly decide to head back to the surface so that their companions can receive treatment. When they arrive back to the first focal tower, the doorway sealed shut behind them. Traveling further, they end up in a new, rectangular chamber with multiple entrances. There are long trenches dug into the ground, coveredin a layer of calcified grime, almost like troughs. The team makes their way through the chambers when they suddenly become overcome by a sudden hunger. Raagi and Zen are able to push it down, but the hungers continues to grow in Igvald and Rex to the point that they feel that they are starving, and that only the flesh of living creatures could satisfy their need. In fact, Raagi's little form is starting to look like a delectable appetizer. Raagi surmises that whatever happened here in the past left it haunted by a psychic impression of intense hunger, and that his companions are now affected by it. Raagi is able to talk Igvald down, and the inquisitor forces himself to resist against the cannibalistic urges within him.
- Hurrying, the team succeeds in reaching the surface without anymore incidents. As they make their way back to Promethea, Igvald turns his head towards Raagi, who in his miniscule form is riding on Igvald's shoulder as usual, and begins apologizing to his teammate for his behaviour earlier. Raagi is immediately put on edge, and quickly jumps off as Igvald tries to take a bite out of him. The kobold knocks out his comrade with a *mind thrust*, and then manifests another rope to tie up Igvald. While Igvald is unconscious, Raagi implants an *aversion* towards eating his companions in mind. Raagi manifests another astral construct to tie up Igvald and to place him on a horse. With that done, Raagi is able to bring his companions back to Promethea.
