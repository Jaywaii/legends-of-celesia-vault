# KM:R 294

# Previously

### The Steamflogger Case

*Captain Acteon summoned you all to investigate a special case. Steamflogger Industries have been experiencing a complete disruption of their supply chains for several months. While this is generally a private merchant matter, the fact that the Industries ownder is Lord Jupus of Prateria's government, plus the fact that ALL supply chains were disrupted in one way or another while other companies are doing mostly fine, Acteon suspects foul play is involved, targeting Jupus Thane's source of income.*

*You investigated 3 supply companies. One company had its lumber sabotaged with termites not native to the Evergreen Plains en route from Onestrio's to Promethea. A 2nd company had to cancel its contract with Steamflogger. And a 3rd was attacked by a griffon, again en route from Onestrio's to Promethea. Interrogating the manager of the 2nd company, the canceled contract, you manage to get a confession from her. Teffania Revedal admitted that she was approached by a merchant named Caisin Berengar to inject some clauses in the Steamflogger contract, then use them to divert their supplies to Caisin's company, in exchange for paying off some large gambling debts.*

*Making a deal with Teffania, you got her to arrange a meeting with Caisin at Onestrio's, under the guise of making another deal. Once the meeting had been confirmed, you traveled by land to Onestrio's. Along the way, you investigated the site of the griffon attack. You could not find its tracks, and questioning the local guard at the nearby fort of Castrum Campis informs you that their patrol lost sight of the griffon when it flew into the clouds.*

*Lawtaumaton was asked to stay back by Captain Acteon in order to follow up on another lead.*

*Larry was able to join you after dealing with another case, and Acteon got him up to speed.*
