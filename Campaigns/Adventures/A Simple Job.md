# A Simple Job

# Goldcross Village

You are all companions, freshly ready for your first adventure. In your search for challenges and excitement, you came across a farming village that just so happened to be in need to brave souls with just a bit of recklessness. Your attire and gear, very exotic among the common farmers, immediately caught the attention of the village's mayor, a human man named Rondall. He is a man with quite a bit of weight on him, balding, but with a fine mustache. Unlike the more drab clothing of the villagers, he obviously makes an effort to wear fine clean clothing.

You accepted Mayor Rondall's invitation to speak with him, and you are alone with him inside the village's assembly hall, a modestly large building where the villagers can gather for discussion.

"Thank you for accepting my invitation, young travelers!" the mayor boomed. "Forgive me for any assumptions, but judging from your attire and gear, but I presume that you are folk willing to face danger for a good sum of coin? If so, I have an offer for you."

After accepting, the mayor explains the following:

- A band of goblins have made camp in the forest neighboring the village over a month ago. Since then, they have stolen some livestock, a pig, a couple of chickens, eggs. However, two days ago, one of the villagers (a man named Darvin) has gone missing, and we fear that the goblins have grown more bolder.
- Kill or drive off the goblins, and you'll be rewards with 50 gold pieces each, and you can claim any loot form the goblins.
- Darvin's farm is on the edge of the village, bordering the forest. "We think the goblins took poor Darvin in his barn. There are signs of struggle there. You might pick up their trail."
- Rondall had his thugs kill Darvin during the day. The thugs used knives.
- DC 20 Sense Motive: Mayor Rondall concern about the death of his villager seems superficial, and he seems impatient to for the PCs to quickly resolve the situation as he requested.
- DC 20 Perception: Notices a door at the back of the hall that is opened just a crack, hears a foot scrape against the wooden floor behind it, with some movement int he shadows.
- If brought up, Mayor Rondall mentions there is some janitorial staff at work cleaning the back of the building.

## Darvin Farm

- There is no movement at the Darvin farm, but you hear some hammering nearby. An older woman with greying hair, tucked underneath a strawhat, is repairing a section of the fence between Darvin's land and another farmland.
- Farmer Jacquelle, an elderly, crass woman with graying hair. "Damn shame about Darvin. His parents' left'im a rich a fertile land. I wonder what Fancy Rondall's gonna do with it."
- "Hmm? Fany Rondall? Well, don't call him that in his face. He ain't from around here, but immigrated here about  5 years ago. Used a lot of his wealth to buy favor from a lot of our folk to get himself elected as mayor. Has a good sense for business, so it's not all bad, but nobody around here believes he likes being in a village. He's always trying to act like some high and mighty cityfolk."
- In the barn, there are signs of struggle. Knocked over tools and buckets, hay straw scattered on the dirt floor, splatters of blood.
- The goblins snuck in later during the night to loot the place and steal some chickens.
- DC 20 Perception: Uncover traces of additional tracks with signs that someone attempted to cover up and wipe away.
- DC 15 Survival: find tracks of 4 goblins (small, claw-like footprints) around in the barn, can be tracked into the forest.
- DC 20 Survival: following the goblin tracks, the tracker discovers additional tracks that appear to be normal size human boots. There are traces of blood on some of the boot marks, and signs of something being dragged.

# Goblins

- **[Goblin](https://www.aonprd.com/MonsterDisplay.aspx?ItemName=Goblin)**: hp 11; AC 16/13/14; Fort +3, Ref +2, Will –1; Melee short sword +2 (1d4/19–20); Ranged short bow +4 (1d4/×3); Perception –1; leather armor, light wooden shield, short sword, short bow with 20 arrows, 1d20 cp, 1d10 sp.
- TO PCs: "How do you want to make your approach?"
- "Hey! What you want?!"
- "Piss off! Leave alone!"
- The goblin camp possesses farm tools, clothing, remains of farm animals. The search finds Darvin's body, not in the camp, but not far outside of it.
- DC 20 Heal: Darvin's wounds appear to be rough like the goblins' weapons, but the cut across his throat was made with a very sharp and long knife. Such a weapon is not found among the goblins' possessions.

# Return to Village
