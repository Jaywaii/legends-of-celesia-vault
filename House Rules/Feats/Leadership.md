# Leadership

*You attract followers to your cause and a companion to join you on your adventures.*

## Prerequisites

CR 7th.

## Benefit

This feat enables you to attract a loyal cohort and a number of devoted subordinates who assist you. A cohort is generally an NPC with class levels, while followers are typically lower level NPCs. Your cohort and followers do not appear immediately; you need to recruit them. However, once recruited, your cohort and followers are completely loyal to you, and require no regular upkeep from you to remain in your service, provided you perform no actions to alienate them.

*Leadership Score:* You have a Leadership Score (LS) regardless if you have the Leadership feat or not. Your LS is equal to your CR + Charisma modifier + mythic ranks (if any). If you occupy a leadership position in a nation, you gain a +1 bonus to your Leadership score. If you occupy the Ruler position, your bonus increases to +2 instead. This bonus remains only as long as you occupy the nation leadership position. By having the Leadership feat, you gain a cohort and a number of followers. See **Table: Leadership** for the maximum cohort CR and number of followers you can recruit based on your Leadership score. In order to take into account negative Charisma modifiers, this table allows for very low Leadership scores, but you must still be CR 7 or higher in order to gain the Leadership feat.

*Cohort CR:* You can attract a cohort of up to this CR. Regardless of your Leadership score, you can only recruit a cohort who is two or more CR lower than yourself. The cohort should be equipped with gear appropriate for its level (see Creating NPCs). A cohort can be of any race or class. The cohort's alignment may not be opposed to your alignment on either the law/chaos or good/evil axis, and you take a –1 penalty to your Leadership score if you recruit a cohort of an alignment different from your own.

A cohort does not count as a party member when determining the party's XP. Instead, divide the cohort's CR by your CR. Multiply this result by the total XP awarded to you, then add that number of experience points to the cohort's total.

If a cohort gains enough XP to bring its CR to one lower than your own, the cohort CR does not increase—its new XP total is 1 less than the amount needed to attain the next CR.

*Number of Followers by Level:* You can lead up to the indicated number of followers of each CR. Followers are similar to cohorts, except they're generally low-CR NPCs. Followers can have a CR no higher than two below than your cohorts. If you have a high enough Leadership Score to have followers of a higher CR than your cohort, the CR of these followers are reduced to your allowable limit. If your LS increases later on, you can level up these followers up to your new limit. Up to 1/3 of your followers upon initial recruitement can be of PC classes; the rest must be of the Civilian class. You can retrain your Civilian class followers to a qualified PC class by using the Retraining rules, and the total number of PC class followers can exceed the 1/3 ratio. However, you can only recruit future followers as Civilians if you currently have 1/3 or more followers with PC classes. Followers are also considered as Downtime hirelings, and you can assign them a hireling role that they qualify for.

The numbers on the table represent the maximum number of followers at that maximum CR. When recruiting followers, you can recruit a follower of a lower CR to fill a higher CR follower slot. You can then spend gold and time to train that follower up to the maximum level for their slot. For example, if you have a leadership score of 10, you might only have the skill and resources necessary to recruit CR 1 followers. You can recruit up to 41 CR 1 followers, then later increase the level of three of them up to CR 2, two of them up to CR 3, and one of them up to CR 4.

Followers don't earn experience like PCs and cohorts. When you gain a new level, consult **Table: Leadership** to determine if you acquire more followers. Some of them may have a CR higher than the existing followers, while others may be more prominent followers who have increased in level. Don't consult the table to see if your cohort gains levels, however, because cohorts earn experience on their own.

## Recruiting Cohorts and Followers

Recruiting requires time and money. You also need to be in a location where these people can be recruited. Your recruiting efforts involve speaking to potential recruits, impressing them with your abilities, exploits, and resources. The money you spend for recruitment serves one or several purposes, such as wages, relocation costs, entertaining potential recruits (food, entertainment, location). You must spend one day of Downtime activity per CR of the recruit. At the end of the required period of time, make either a Diplomacy or Intimidate check, or roll 1d20 + your Leadership Score; a success results in the recruitment of the target, while a failure results in no recruitement, and the day and money being wasted. If you have a Fame score in a sphere of influence, and the recruit falls into that sphere, then you gain a +1 bonus per 10 points of your Fame to your Diplomacy check. You gain the same bonus to Intimidate check based on your Infamy instead. The DC varies on the recruit. If you fail at your recruitment attempt, you can attempt to recruit the same persons again one month later.

*Cohorts:* Cohort recruitment cost equals 100 gp per the potential cohort's CR. The recruitment DC is 10 plus twice the recruit's CR. For every 10 that you beat the DC, reduce the recruitment cost by 100 gp (to a minimum of 0 gp). You gain a +2 bonus on your recruitment check every time you pay additional gp equal to the base cost. If a previous cohort in your employ killed by violence and disease, and remains dead within the past number of years equal to half of the previous cohort's CR, you receive a penalty to your check equal to half of the candidate cohort's CR. This penalty is cumulative for each dead cohort within the overlapping time frame. You also gain a -1 penalty for each step the recruit's alignment is different from yours. If you already have a non-cohort animal companion, familiar, or special mount, you gain an additional -2 penalty to your check.

*Followers:* Followers are recruited like Downtime hirelings (see **[[Downtime|House Rules: Downtime]]**), except for the following modifications. Follower recruitment cost equals the hireling cost in gp divided by 10 multiplied by the recruit's CR. The recruitment DC is 10 plus the recruit's CR if he is a civilian, and 15 plus the recruit's CR if he has a PC class. For every 10 that your check beats the DC, you recruit another hireling of the same CR without having to spend additional time or money. You gain a +2 bonus on your recruitment check every time you pay additional gp equal to the recruitment base cost. You gain a +2 bonus to your check if you own a stronghold or base of operations. You gain a penalty to your check equal to -1 for every follower killed by violence or disease within the past year. Your cohort can recruit followers on your behalf using your Fame or Infamy bonus and dead follower penalty, but using his own Diplomacy or Intimidate modifier.
