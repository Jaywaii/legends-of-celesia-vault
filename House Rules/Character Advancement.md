# Character Advancement

# Advancing Your Character

Upon getting enough experience to increase your level (or when the GM declares that you gained a level), you must spend a number of days equal to your new level to finalize your training before actually gaining your level. Before you can start this training period, you must have spent at least a number of weeks equal to your new level at your current level, which represents the amount of time spent learning and practicing new abilities and skills.

## Ability Scores

You may increase two different ability scores of your choice by 1 at every level after 1st.

## Feats

You gain a new feat at every level after 1st.

## Hit Points

Add the maximum value of the Hit Die of the class you are leveling into to your current and total hit points. Add your Constitution modifier, plus any other relevant modifiers (such as teh bonus hp from the Toughness feat).

## Racial Feats

You gain a new racial feat at 4th-level and every three levels thereafter.

## Skill Increase

You gain a proficiency increase to one skill at every level after 1st.

# Advancing Beyond 20th Level

- No class can progress beyond 20th-level, and no non-epic prestige class can progress beyond its listed level progression. You must select a new class to advance upon leveling. Epic prestige classes can progress infinitely.
- Base attack bonuses continue to stack beyond +20.
- Saving throw bonuses continue to stack beyond +12.
- You continue to gain a feat every level. These may be regular or epic feats.
- You continue to gain a racial feat every three levels after 19th.
- You continue to gain a +1 increase to two different ability scores every level.
- You continue to gain one skill proficiency increase every level.

Your high ability score also increases your number of spells known by the same number as your bonus spells per day.
