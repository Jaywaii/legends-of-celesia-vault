# Psychic Battle WIP

# Basics

**Psychic Integrity (PI)** represents the wholeness of your psyche, and you start with an amount of PI equal to your level + your Intelligence modifier + your Wisdom modifier + your Charisma modifier (minimum 1). This value is also your maximum PI. When your PI reaches 0 or below, you are defeated in the psychic battle and suffer the consequences.

**Manifestation Points (MP)** are your resources in the mindscape, drawn from your mental strength and experience. At the start of the psychic battle, you start with MP equal to your PI. There is no limit to how much MP you can possess. Starting on your second turn, you gain MP equal to your highest mental modifier.

**Mental Resilience (MR)** is your base defense. It is equal to 10 + your highest mental modifier.

**Mental Range** is your reach from your edge of the mindplane. It is a number of squares equal to your highest mental modifier.

## Generating MP

Starting on your second turn, you generate MP equal to your highest mental attribute modifier at the start of your turn. When this occurs, you can generate additional MP once by doing one form of sacrifice. You can also sacrifice to gain MP as a free reaction once per action or reaction. The maximum amount of MP you can gain per sacrifice is equal to your level + your highest mental modifier. The means to gain MP through sacrifice are the following:

- *Sacrifice Psychic Integrity:* For every point of PI sacrificed, you gain 1 MP.
- *Sacrifice Class Resources:* If you possess a pool of points, such as essence, grit, qi, power, spell (and others at GM's discretion), you gain MP equal to your level per point sacrificed. Once sacrificed, these class resource cannot be recovered until you gain a full rest.
- *Sacrifice Class Ability:* If you possess a supernatural ability with daily uses, you gain MP equal to your level per daily use sacrificed. Once sacrificed, these class abilities cannot be recovered until you gain a full rest. Each at-will supernatural ability can be sacrificed to gain MP up to your level + your highest mental modifier once per psychic battle.
- *Sacrifice Formation Channels:* You can sacrifice a formation channel to gain MP equal to your level + the channel's power. The channel can only be recovered after a full rest.
- *Sacrifice Natural Mystic Arts:* If you possess natural mystic arts with daily uses, you can sacrifice one daily use of it to gain MP equal to your level + its power. Each at-will natural mystic art can be sacrificed up a number of times equal to your level + your highest mental modifier per psychic battle. These daily uses can only be recovered after a full rest.

# Combat

## The Mindscape

Upon entering a psychic mindscape, all combatants appear at the edges of a flat mindplane, with each side on opposite ends. Each edge is at least 15 squares from every other (2 sides form a 30 x 30 square grid, 3 sides form a hexagon with 15 square-long edges, and so on). Combatants are not considered to be on the mindplane.

## Initiative

Before the start of the battle, everyone rolls initiative: 1d20 + their highest mental modifier. Until a combatant has acted during the battle, they cannot use reactions.

## Actions

During your turn, you can take as many actions as you wish to spend MP for.

### Bolster Pawn

When your pawn is within your mental range, you can spend an amount of MP to increase its PI by the same amount. You can also spend 2 MP to change your pawn by 1 size category.

### Cast Mystic Art

Cast a mystic art that you know. If cast from as a combatant, this action costs MP equal to 1 + the art's power, and you can only target your pawns and mindwalls on the mindplane; they are always considered to be within reach. Alternatively, you can choose to cast it from one of your pawns of your choice, costing MP equal to the action to cast it. If so, use the pawn as its source and for line of sight. Mystic arts can only affect targets in the mindplane.

### Control Pawn

You can control your pawn as like normal combat, but you can only move or use nonmagical abilites that you know. Each beat used costs 1 MP. Attacks use your highest mental modifier as their attack modifier. Each successful attack and every degree of success deals damage equal to your highest mental modifier. The DC of your abilities uses your highest mental modifier instead your original. You can spend additional MP to boost your pawn. Each time you spend 2 additional MP, choose one of the following:

- +1 bonus to one attack roll
- +1 square of speed of your current or a new movement type

Your pawn can attack other pawns and mindwalls, but can only attack another combatant if they are adjacent to their edge of the mindplane.

### Manifest Pawn

Manifest a pawn on the field with an amount of PI equal to the amount of MP spent. The pawn appears in a square along your edge of the mindplane; you cannot manifest a pawn if all of those squares are occupied. The pawn appears as you, has your movement types and speeds, and has your size. It uses your MR and highest mental save. You can change your pawn's size by spending by spending 2 MP for each size difference when manifesting it. Pawns can be used as the origin of your abilities when you manifest them.

### Mental Recovery

Target yourself, a combatant, a pawn, or a mindwall. Heal the target's PI by 1 for every 2 MP spent. You cannot gain more PI beyond your maximum.

### Mental Strike

A mental strike is a direct attack from you to another combatant, a barrier, or a pawn. You can choose how your mental strike appears in the mindscape. Your strike's damage is equal to a number of d6 per 5 MP spent. Its DC is equal to 10 + 1/2 your level + your highest mental modifier. You can increase the DC by 1 for every 2 additional MP spent. The target uses his highest mental save modifier. Save for half damage, crit save for no damage, crit fail for double damage.

Increase area of effect by spending additional MP. Choose a a line, a sphere, or a snake. Each square of radius or length costs 2 MP.

Inflict a condition by spending the condition's MP cost. If the target receives damage from the mental strike, he must make another save against the mental strike's DC to negate the condition.

*Blinded (3 MP):* If the target is a combatant, he cannot make a mental strike. If the target is a pawn, it has a 50% miss chance on its attacks. This lasts until the start of your next turn.

*Confused (10 MP):* The target loses concentration and cannot make reactions. On his turn, he rolls 1d100. A result of 1-25 allows him to act normally, 26-50 causes him to babble incoherently and do nothing, 51-75 makes him deal damage equal to his level + his highest mental modifier to a pawn or barrier under his control (or to himself if no valid targets are available), and 76-100 makes him deal the same damage to himself. This lasts until the start of your next turn.

*Nauseated (4 MP):* The target cannot take any actions during his turn. He can still make reactions. This lasts until the start of your next turn.

*Shaken (2 MP):* The target gains +1 disadvantage on all d20 rolls for 1d4+1 rounds. He can spend 1 MP as a free action at the end of his turn to attempt to save against this effect, negating it if he succeeds.

### Manifest Mindwall

You manifest a mindwall on the mindplane. You can manifest the mindwall anywhere within your mental range. Each square of the wall costs 5 MP. You can shape the wall as a line or as a snake of contiguous squares. The wall can be targeted by mental strikes and martial attacks. The wall has a PI equal to 10 per square, and uses your MR and mental save for defense. The wall blocks movement and line of sight of pawns on the mindplane.

## Reactions

You can make as many reactions as you have MP to spend for.

### Boost Save

When you need to make an ability save, you can spend an amount of MP to gain a bonus of the same amount on your save. This lasts only for that one save.

### Fortify Resilience

When you are targeted by an attack against your MR, you can increase it by an amount equal to the number of MP you wish to spend. This increase lasts only for that one attack.

### Psychic Redirect

When you receive damage from a mental strike, you can redirect that damage to one pawn or barrier under your control by spending 1 MP per point of damage redirected. You cannot transfer more damage than the pawn or barrier's current PI.

### Psychosomatic Transference

When you receive damage from a martial strike, you can preserve your PI by transfering the damage to your physical body in the real world. Each point of MP spent transfers up to 5 points of damage to your real body.

# Concluding the Psychic Battle

The instigator of the psychic battle can voluntarily withdraw on their turn to end the psychic battle.

A combatant leaves when they are reduced to 0 PI. When this happens, he must make a save with their highest mental modifier against a DC of 10 + the amount of damage that reduced him to 0 or lower. On success, they receive a minor madness. On failure, they receive a major madness. On critical success, they receive no madnesses. On critical failure, they take ability drain to each mental modifier equal to the highest mental modifier of the attacker that reduced them to 0 PI in addition to receiving a major madness.

Casting a *Mindscape Door* art allows all designated targets to leave the psychic battle. The creator of the mindscape can attempt a mental save to prevent you from creating the door if she is within the mindscape.

all reactions are still available

boosting MP same as tactical play

**Blast:** Perform a mental strike against a single target. Damage from this attack can be reacted with the Psychomsomatic Transference reaction.

**Defend:** Gain a bonus equal to the amount of MP spent on this action (up to your highest mental bonus) to your mental saves until the start of your next turn.

**Healing:** Heal a target's PI by 1 per 2 MP spent.

**Buffs:** Select a number of targets, and apply one buff to all of them. Pay an additional 2 MP per ally targeted beyond the first. Buffs last until the start of your next turn.

- *Mindshield:* Grant targets a bonus equal to the amount of 1/2 the MP spent on this action to their mental saves until the start of your next turn. This bonus cannot exceed your highest mental bonus.
- *Sharpen Mind:* Increase the targets' blast DC by 1 per 2 MP spent as a bonus. This bonus cannot exceed your highest mental bonus.

**Debuffs:** Select a number of targets, and apply one debuffs to all of them. Pay additional 2 MP per target beyond the first. The debuff's DC is equal to 10 + 1/2 level + your highest mental modifier. Debuffs last until the start of your next turn.

- *Blind (1 MP):* reduce dmg by 50%
- *Confusion (10 MP):* blast ally for 1d6 dmg (01-18), damage self equal to your highest mental modifier (19-36), debuff allies (37-54), buff opponents (55-72), do nothing (73-90), act normally (91-100). buffs and debuffs are also random. unable to make reaction. target must pay MP cost of actions, do nothing if insufficient MP
- *Nauseated (3 MP):* do nothing
- *Shaken (2 MP):* +1 disadvantage on all d20 rolls
